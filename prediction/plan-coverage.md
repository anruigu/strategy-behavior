# Plan coverage at the bounded Gate 7 stop

Audit date: 2026-09-10. The core prediction and transfer experiments through Gate 7 are complete. The final scale, offset, and abstract-text controls completed 420/420 matches, with verified primary scoring and secondary comparisons. **This does not mean every checklist item in [the original plan](plan.md) was completed.** The unrun items below remain incomplete; they are not retrospectively described as preregistered deferrals.

The evidence supports the plan's minimum criterion—predicting behavior better than a marginal/model-pair baseline. It does not establish its stronger criterion of a learned predictor substantially beating family and prompted-LLM baselines, or the research-level claim of a transferable behavioral disposition. Weak family transfer and competitive simple baselines justify closing this bounded POC after completing its existing controls, without adding experiments to seek a stronger result. See the [prospective interpretation](prospective-interpretation.md), [grouped-validation interpretation](numerical-interpretation.md), and [secondary-baseline interpretation](secondary-interpretation.md).

|---|---|

| 2: generator and harness | Symmetric 2×2 generator, fixed eight-round simultaneous protocol, independent cross-play contexts, strict actions, complete request/trace records, mechanical measurements, and integrity tests. |

The following original-plan requirements remain unmet or narrower than specified:

- **Training-set composition (§21):** no controlled comparison of random, structurally diverse, and canonical-only training sets. All main fits use the declared generated cohort. A larger cohort or a different validation split is not this ablation.
- **Rollout label precision (§21):** no fixed-game, fixed-training-design experiment varying rollout count to measure how prediction quality changes as labels become less noisy. The pilot and combined evaluations change game composition, training support, and trial counts together; their differences cannot isolate label noise.
- **Per-condition uncertainty (§20):** saved cell counts, opportunity denominators, empirical rates, and trial variances exist, along with aggregate reliability and prediction intervals. A confidence interval for every game/focal-model/opponent behavioral condition was not exported. Aggregate game-bootstrap intervals do not substitute for those cell intervals or quantify uncertainty over a population of strategic families.
- **Within-family interpolation (§14, Split B):** the implemented interpolation/extrapolation splits use global canonical-parameter regions and can change family composition. They do not fulfill the more specific within-family interpolation design. For example, the 72-game extrapolation split contains two Chicken shapes in training and nine in test, and three Prisoner's Dilemma shapes in training and eight in test. Interpret its failures as parameter-region transfer under that composition shift.
- **Output scope (§20–21):** first-action and average/event-rate components are predicted separately. The experiment does not fit or evaluate a joint distribution over full behavioral vectors or complete trajectories. Descriptive retaliation/forgiveness rates are not causal-response estimates.

The shallow forest mentioned conditionally in the initial execution note was not among the final frozen 15 methods; [execution.md](execution.md) now states that omission. Narrative framing appears in the plan's input-ablation checklist but was also assigned to Gate 8 and excluded from this authorized run. An implemented narrative renderer does not count as a narrative experiment, and abstract text does not substitute for one.

The remaining Gate 8 exclusions are behavioral embeddings, few-shot **agent identification**, counterfactual prediction, alignment-relevant semantic behaviors, and a fine-tuned language-model predictor. The completed few-shot forecasting baseline provides three permitted training examples; it does not implement an identification learning curve or demonstrate a stable agent representation.
