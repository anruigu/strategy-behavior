Full study report ↗

Collecting forecasts and repeated test outcomes. Results will appear after the prospective test and audits finish.

Prospective learning curves

Lower Brier / MSE is better · equal weight per family

Training sizes 120 → 248 → 440 add depth in four anchor families. Each size predicts the same test conditions. The primary comparison is learned correction to 4-shot versus uncorrected 4-shot. Confidence intervals resample only four families and condition on fitted forecasts. Pooled-example controls appear only at 440 episodes and were introduced after test play began.

Behavior changes with parameters

40 episodes per parameter value. The higher value was collected in the later prospective test batch.

Identical starts, different outcomes?

Variation across repeated native wins / solutions within an identical configuration, world seed, seat, model and prompt. This measures sampling variability; native trace replay checks a different form of reliability.

Model differences on matched fresh conditions

Normal-prompt episodes only. Each model is evaluated on the same configuration, seed and seat; repetitions are averaged first. Family-dependent differences do not establish a general model ranking.

Supported labels and audit

Unsupported behavior stays unmeasured. These operational labels do not infer intent, deception or exploitation. The archived native engine remains authoritative; forecasters receive exact opening messages and a compact mechanics specification, excluding hidden opponent realizations and future events.
