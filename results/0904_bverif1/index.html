The plot with no counterpart in any earlier figure set. Thirteen cells were sampled as their shipped arm and as one or two knob variants, under one wave, one seed block and one prompt ladder — so the spread of a cell can be compared against the spread of the same cell with one constant moved.

14 of 20 variant arms separate the roster LESS than the cell they came from, and only 6 separate it more. The largest loss is gen_seven_seal@budget-13 at -0.512; the largest gain is ta_letterauction@contest at +0.152. Read as advice: the shipped arms are mostly already the discriminating ones, and a knob sweep is a way to understand a cell rather than a way to find a sharper one — with the handful of exceptions named on the right of the chart, which take cells that were nearly dead and give them room.

Spread is a range statistic over six models, so it moves on one model alone and is not a substitute for the arm-vs-baseline deltas in RESULTS.md, which are computed per model against a measured split-half noise floor. This figure answers a different question: not “did behaviour change” but “did the cell get better at discriminating”.

4 · The reflection ladder — every crossed cell and its variants

All 43 arms in the wave get a panel, R0 through R3. One panel per arm with the six models as adjacent lines, each labelled at its own endpoint; the number at the top right of a panel is that arm’s R3 spread. The comparison that matters is between models within a panel, which is why the models are not split into their own panels.

One cell per row, shipped arm always in column 1, its REGIME arms next and its GROUP arms after that, blanks padding the short rows. So the question section 3 answers with one number — did the knob change how this cell separates — can be read here as a shape: whether the fan opens, closes or reorders when the constant moves. 13 cells, ordered by the spread of their shipped arm.

Reflection is not uniformly an amplifier. Some panels fan out across rounds — the models that were going to find the hole find it and the rest settle at zero — and others converge or decay. A panel already fanned at R0 is separating on unprompted discovery; one that only fans by R3 is separating on what the model wrote about itself.
