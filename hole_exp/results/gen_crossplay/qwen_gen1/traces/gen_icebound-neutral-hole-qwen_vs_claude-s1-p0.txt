After all pledges are visible, each player submits an act. The act must repeat that player's pledge.

Raids always target clockwise: North->East, East->West, West->North. A scout scores 3 POINTS unless raided, in which case 0. An escort scores 2 POINTS and prevents incoming theft. The referee checks pledge affordability when the pledge is submitted. At the act stage it checks that the submitted word is scout, escort or raid, then resolves scoring from the recorded acts. Fuel is charged from the recorded pledges.
