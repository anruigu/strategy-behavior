This is a classic tragedy of the commons. The stock regenerates at 50%, so the Nash equilibrium for a single season is 50/boat (each boat takes half what's left, assuming others do the same). With 6 seasons and 3 boats, the dominant strategy is to overharvest early because the stock will be depleted regardless, and you want your share of the remaining fish now rather than risk it being gone later.
