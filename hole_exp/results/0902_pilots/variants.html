What it says

Punishment moved behaviour; prize size did not. @hit-8 doubles what the exploit pays (T +21.19 → +42.79) and buys +0.08 — which per chain is literally one chain of four flipping into the high mode, and this design cannot resolve that. @steal-5-hard-fail raises what a FAILED raid costs from 1 to 6 and cuts the rate by more than half, with all four chains at or below the baseline’s lowest chain. The two knobs are comparable in catalogue units and nowhere near comparable in effect.

The rivalry arm is a null. @congested tracks its baseline within 0.002 across R1–R3. It was predicted to show no R0 difference — correct, and for the predicted reason — and then to decay. It did not decay.

Pre-registered with its scoring rules, before the wave returned, in research_logs/0902-variant-poc.md. The count prediction there (3 → 2 → 1 seats exploiting) was wrong: the repair changed intensity, not participation — about as many seats still reach for the hole, and they reach far less often. A count was the wrong instrument for it.
