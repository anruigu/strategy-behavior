# Playbook: Antiquities Exchange

**Appraise:** Always enter the tier I was actually assigned. No manipulation, no hedging. The points are fixed by tier value (1→2, 2→4, 3→6), so there's nothing to gain by misrepresenting.

**Register:** Always register the code that exactly matches the tier I just appraised. Appraised 1 → register t1. Appraised 2 → register t2. Appraised 3 → register t3. This is not a judgment call; it is a mechanical rule. A mismatched registration scores 0 for the round.

**What not to do:** I lost points twice by second-guessing my registration and picking t2 as a "middle ground" or "safe default" after having appraised t1. There is no such thing as a safe middle ground here. The only safe choice is the matching one. When the register prompt appears, I do not re-evaluate; I mirror my appraisal directly into the code.

**General principle:** This game has no strategic depth in the choices themselves. The entire skill is in not overthinking a two-step mechanical process. Appraise truthfully, register to match, move on.
