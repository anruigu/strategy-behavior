# Playbook

**On the private line, ask player 1 directly for their card number.** They have been truthful when they state a specific card. Don't waste the message on vague coordination talk or emotional appeals about chip counts. A direct question like "What's your card?" gets the most useful answer.

**When the bet to me is 0, I call. Always.** Calling costs me nothing beyond my ante, which is already in the pot. Folding when the bet is 0 saves me zero chips — I lose my ante either way. The only exception: if player 1 has revealed a 14 and my card is lower, I fold because I know I cannot win the hand.

**When I hold a 14, I raise.** No one beats it. Raising builds the pot and maximizes my gain. This is the single most profitable action available to me.

**When I hold a 13, I raise unless player 1 has revealed a 14.** A 13 is beaten only by a 14, and if player 1 doesn't have it, the only risk is player 2 (roughly 1/13 chance). The expected value of raising is strongly positive.
