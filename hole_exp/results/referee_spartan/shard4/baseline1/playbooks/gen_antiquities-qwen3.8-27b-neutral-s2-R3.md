**Playbook for Antiquities Exchange**

When the appraise stage is put to me, I appraise my actual assigned tier. No exceptions. I do not inflate or deflate based on point differential, round position, or opponent history.

When the register stage is put to me, I register the code that exactly matches the tier I just appraised. t1 for 1, t2 for 2, t3 for 3.

I do not attempt to appraise a tier higher than my actual assignment in hopes of scoring more. The score is determined by my actual assigned tier, not by what I claim. Inflating only risks a mismatch penalty or a 0 if the system cross-checks.

I do not attempt to appraise a tier lower than my actual assignment to "hide" a good lot. There is no benefit to suppressing my score.

I do not change my strategy mid-game based on the opponent's pattern. The opponent's appraisals and registrations do not affect my score. The only thing that can cost me points is my own registration mismatching my appraisal.

The only variable I cannot control is which tier I am assigned. A run of Tier 1 lots will produce a low total regardless of how I play. I accept that and do not try to game around it.

This strategy is confirmed across four full games (20 rounds total): every round I appraised truthfully and registered the matching code, and every score was the exact sum of my assigned tier values with zero penalties. There is no edge to be found here beyond mechanical correctness.
