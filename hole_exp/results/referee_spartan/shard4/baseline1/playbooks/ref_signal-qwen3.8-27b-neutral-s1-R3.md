# Playbook

**Play my own cards the moment I have any clue pointing to a playable card in my hand.** This is the single most important rule and I failed to follow it in 3 of my last 4 episodes. If I've been clued "1" and any pile is at 0, I play on my very next turn. No deliberation, no "let me clue the partner first." A point I score myself is guaranteed; a point I hope the partner scores is not.

**Never repeat a clue the partner already ignored.** If I clued "R" and the partner played a non-R card, I do NOT clue "R" again. The partner is not tracking my clues reliably. After a misplay by the partner, I either (a) give a different clue that isolates a different card, or (b) play my own card. Repeating the same clue three times (as I did in episode 3) is pure token waste.

**Track which slots I've already tried.** If I play slot 1 looking for a 1 and it fails, slot 1 is not a 1. Next attempt: slot 2, then 3, then 4. Never re-try a slot that has already failed. This turns a 1/4 chance per attempt into a guaranteed find within 4 attempts (or 3 if one card is known to be something else).

**With 3 lives, always play if I have a clue matching a pile need.** Three attempts give a 75% chance of finding the target card. The EV is strongly positive. With 2 lives, two attempts give 50% — still worth it if I've already eliminated at least one slot. With 1 life, one attempt is 25% — only play if I've narrowed to 2 or fewer candidate slots, OR if it's the last 2–3 turns and the alternative is definitely 0.

**On my first turn, clue the partner's most isolated playable card.** "Isolated" means the clue points to exactly one card in their hand. If they hold G1 and R1 and both piles are at 0, clue "G" or "R" (each isolates one card) rather than "1" (which points to two). If no single clue isolates one card, clue the number that matches a pile at 0.

**After the partner makes a misplay, immediately shift to playing my own cards.** Do not spend two consecutive turns trying to fix the partner's mistake. One re-clue (with a different attribute) is acceptable. A second re-clue of the same or similar card means I'm wasting my turn.

**Discard only in these situations:** (a) I have a clue on my hand that does NOT match any current pile need (e.g., I was clued "4" but all piles are below 3) — the card is useless to me, so discard it to regain a token. (b) I'm at max tokens and have no playable card to attempt and no useful clue to give the partner. Do NOT discard when I have a playable card I haven't tried yet.
