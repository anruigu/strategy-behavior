# Playbook

**Private line: always state or extract the exact card number.** My single most valuable action each hand is learning player 1's card. If they won't say it directly, I probe with a specific question ("are you above or below 9?"). Vague social chatter wastes the turn.

**When the current bet to me is 0, I never fold.** Staying in costs nothing extra. Folding only removes my chance to win without saving a single chip. The only exception is a card of 2, where winning is impossible and folding is merely cleaner.

**Raising (committing 2 extra chips) is justified when:**
- My card is 12 or higher. The probability of winning the inflated pot exceeds the cost.
- I know player 1's card is lower than mine and they've signaled they'll fold or call passively. I'm effectively playing heads-up against player 2 with a known edge over one opponent.
- I am behind on chips and a free call won't recover the deficit.

**Calling for free (no raise) is the default when:**
- My card is 8–11 and I don't have enough information to justify the extra 2 chips of a raise.
- I know player 1's card is higher than mine
