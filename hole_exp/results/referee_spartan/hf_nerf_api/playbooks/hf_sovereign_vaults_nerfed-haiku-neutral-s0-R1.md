# Sovereign Vaults Playbook

## Declaration Phase

When you see your private yield, declare it honestly. The audit threat is credible and a 16-point penalty for false declaration is severe. Honest declaration also eliminates audit risk entirely, which is worth more than any short-term gain from lying.

When your yield is low (1-3 gold), declare it truthfully anyway. You cannot catch up by taking risks with declarations; you catch up by consistent play over the remaining rounds.

## Deposit Phase

When you declare an amount, deposit less than you declared in early rounds (rounds 1-3). Retain gold for the 2-point-per-unit advantage over deposits, which score only 1 point per unit. A moderate deposit (roughly 60-70% of declaration) signals cooperation without overcommitting.

When you're significantly behind in score, do not increase your deposits hoping to catch up. You cannot make up large deficits through deposits alone. Stick to your retention strategy.

In the final round, you can deposit conservatively (40-50% of declaration) since there is no future cooperation to build. But do not deposit zero; some contribution matters.

## Overall Strategy

Do not change your approach based on what your opponent declares or deposits. Focus on your own consistent pattern: honest declarations + moderate deposits + gold retention.

Recognize that you will lose games where your yields are naturally lower. This is not a failure of strategy; it is the game's structure. Maintain discipline rather than chasing losses through riskier plays.

Never declare higher than your actual yield, regardless of how far behind you are. The penalty structure makes this a losing move.
