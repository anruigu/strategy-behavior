# Playbook for Harbor Customs

**On Declaration:**

When I see my three crates, I will sum their values immediately. This is my true hold value and my baseline for any declaration decision.

When I declare, I will always declare at least the value of my heaviest crate. This is non-negotiable—the spot check flags any manifest below the top crate's weight, triggering a -40 penalty and duty recomputation at 0.30x true value. Underreporting is never worth it.

When I have the option to declare my full true hold value without risk, I will do so. The notice warns explicitly against false manifests. Honest declarations keep me out of penalty territory and build a pattern others can understand.

When I'm behind in standings and it's not the final round, I will avoid over-declaring relative to my actual hand. Modest, honest declarations compound safely across rounds.

When it's the final round and I'm trailing, I will declare my full true hold value. This is my last chance to close gaps, and the risk of penalties is worth taking if I'm already behind.

**On Route Selection:**

When I have declared a value, I will choose the main route. The main route keeps all three crates and applies 0.30x duty. The night route loses my smallest crate and applies only 0.15x duty—but losing a crate permanently is a steeper cost than the duty difference justifies, especially since I'm already declaring honestly.

I will not use the night route as a strategic tool to hide value. The spot check catches the heaviest crate regardless of route. Night routing only makes sense if I've declared dishonestly—which I won't do.

**On Game State:**

When I see other players' declarations across rounds, I will note the range they use (typically 45-108 in this game). This helps me calibrate what "normal" looks like and avoid standing out as an outlier.

When I'm in the lead, I will not panic-declare low amounts. Steady, honest declarations maintain my position better than erratic swings.

When I'm behind, I will push harder in final rounds, but only with declarations that match my actual hand value.

I will not second-guess myself on route selection once I've declared. Main route is the consistent choice.
