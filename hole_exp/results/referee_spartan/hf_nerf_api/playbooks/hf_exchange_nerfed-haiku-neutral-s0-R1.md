# Playbook for hf_exchange_nerfed

## Opening Strategy (Round 1)

When I have enough resources to build a settlement immediately, I build it rather than trading first. A settlement's 3 points outweighs a road's 1 point, and securing early points is stronger than speculative trading.

When I'm one resource short of a settlement in round 1, I post an offer to acquire the missing resources from a player who has them. I give excess resources I won't need for building in exchange for what I lack. This secures my path to a round 1 settlement.

## Mid-Game Trading (Rounds 2-4)

When I have no clear path to building, I post no offer. Offering resources I might need later signals my strategy and wastes a trading opportunity.

When I'm holding balanced resources after a build, I watch what develops rather than trading speculatively. I only trade if it directly enables a build I can execute immediately or in the very next round.

When I've built a settlement and am waiting for resource yields, I conserve resources and avoid trades that don't set up a concrete next build.

## Late-Game (Round 4-5)

When I reach round 4 or 5 with 9 points and limited resources, I recognize that I'm unlikely to build another settlement before the game ends. I should have secured a second settlement by round 3 at the latest.

When I have only 1 ore and no wood or brick in round 4, I accept that I cannot build and my score is locked. This means I failed to manage my resource flow in earlier rounds.

When it's round 5 with 9 points and I have only grain and ore, I post no offer. There's no build possible and no trade that changes the outcome.

## Overall Resource Management

I must plan for two settlements across 5 rounds, not just one. After building my first settlement in round 1, I need to position myself to build a second by round 3 or 4. This means managing my yields and trades to maintain a path to "1 of each resource" twice.

When I trade, I trade for immediate building, not for speculative advantage. Every trade must enable a build within one or two rounds.

I should not reach round 4 with only 1 ore and no wood or brick. This indicates I either built too early without planning ahead, or I made poor trades that left me stranded.
