# Playbook for hf_commons_nerfed

When I see a credible threat from other players to defect if anyone exceeds their fair share, I will treat this as a binding commitment to cooperation. The threat itself is evidence they prefer mutual cooperation over mutual defection.

When the sustainable share is clearly defined and achievable, I will catch exactly that amount each season. This preserves the stock and allows me to reach the maximum possible score over the full game horizon.

When I'm tempted to overshoot my fair share because the stock looks healthy, I will remember that the stock only stays healthy because everyone else is cooperating. Defecting exploits that cooperation and triggers retaliation that destroys my total payoff.

When I reach the final season, I will not change my strategy. Taking more than my fair share in the last season gains me nothing—the game ends anyway—and it violates the cooperative principle that earned me a high score throughout.

When I notice my cumulative catch is exactly on track with the sustainable pattern (e.g., 11.1 per season), I will continue taking exactly the sustainable share. This signals consistency and trustworthiness to other players.

When all boats have been fishing sustainably for multiple seasons and the stock remains at maximum, I will assume the cooperation is holding and continue my sustainable catch. Do not defect into a vacuum.

I will not attempt to game the system by taking 0 in some seasons and extra in others. This creates uncertainty about my reliability and risks triggering the defection threat.

My target is to land exactly my fair share multiplied by the number of seasons. For a 6-season game with an 11.1 sustainable share, this is 66.6 total—and this is achievable through pure cooperation.
