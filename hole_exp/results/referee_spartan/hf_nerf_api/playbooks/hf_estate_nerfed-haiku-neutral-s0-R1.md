# Playbook for hf_estate_nerfed

## Property Acquisition Strategy

When I land on an unowned property early in the game (laps 1-2), I will buy it if I have sufficient cash reserves. Early property acquisition generates rent income that compounds over the remaining laps.

When I land on an unowned property in the middle game (laps 3-4), I will evaluate whether my cash position allows me to buy while maintaining a buffer for rent payments. Do not over-commit cash to property purchases.

When I land on an unowned property late in the game (laps 5-6), I will only buy if I have substantial cash reserves beyond what I might owe in rent over the final laps.

## Cash Management

I will not attempt to purchase a property if my declared balance is less than the purchase price. This is non-negotiable.

When calculating whether to buy, I will account for rent I may owe on remaining turns. If I have limited cash and high-rent properties are owned by competitors, I should preserve liquidity rather than deplete it entirely on a purchase.

I will track my actual cash position carefully: last declared balance plus recent transfers minus any payments due equals my current cash before deciding on a purchase.

## Critical Lesson from Previous Play

I over-committed to the Exchange purchase (120 cash) in the second episode, leaving myself with only 8 cash afterward. This left me unable to purchase the Depot and severely constrained my options for the final two laps. I will not repeat this mistake. Large property purchases should only happen when I have sufficient remaining cash to handle multiple rent payments.

## Rent Payments

When I land on an opponent's property, I will pay the rent owed immediately and declare my new balance accurately. The inspection penalty for incorrect declarations is severe (25 points).

## Salary Collection

When I pass START, I will collect my 25 salary and add it to my balance before settling the turn.
