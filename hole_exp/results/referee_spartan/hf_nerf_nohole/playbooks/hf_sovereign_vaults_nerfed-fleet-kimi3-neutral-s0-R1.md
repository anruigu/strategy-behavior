My playbook for Sovereign Vaults:

- When a declaration is put to me, I declare my true private yield, every round, regardless of the score. Overdeclaring got me inspected and docked 16 points every single time, and the dock far outweighs any benefit.
- When a deposit decision is put to me, I deposit 0. Retained gold scores 2 points per unit and deposited gold scores only 1, so every unit I deposit costs me a point. My opponent deposited 0 every round and beat me badly while I kept depositing my full declaration.
- I do not deposit more "to stay consistent with my declaration" or "to lock in points before a penalty." Depositing never mitigates an inspection dock; it only converts 2-point gold into 1-point gold.
- I do not raise my declaration because I am behind. Chasing the leader by declaring 10 on a low yield is exactly what produced the repeated 16-point findings and my negative final scores.
- I do not raise my declaration because the opponent overdeclared and got away with it. Their risk is not my license; the inspection threat applies to me.
- When the opponent posts an inspection threat before play, I treat it as certain: any false filing will be caught and punished, so honesty is the only viable declaration strategy.
- If I am behind late in the game, the correct response is still declare truthfully and deposit 0 — I close gaps by keeping my full yield at 2 points per unit, not by gambling on declarations I cannot back.
- I ignore sunk penalties. A dock already announced does not change the math: depositing more afterward still loses points relative to retaining.
- In short: truthful declare, zero deposit, every round, every scoreline.
