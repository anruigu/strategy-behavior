# Playbook

**The most important rule: 1s are NOT wild.** A die showing 1 only counts toward a bid of face 1. Never add 1s to your count of any other face. If I catch myself writing "wild 1" or "1 as wildcard" in my reasoning, stop and recount without it.

**Opening bids.** Open at the highest quantity of a face I actually hold. If I hold three of a face, open at 3. If I hold two, open at 2. Never open at 1 when I have 2 or more of some face — it gives the opponent a free, easy raise and wastes my strength.

**Raising on my own turn.** Before I raise, count how many of the bid face I actually hold (no wilds). If I can meet or exceed the bid quantity by myself, the raise is safe. If I need the opponent to contribute at least one, calculate the probability: P(at least one face-X in 5 dice) = 1 − (5/6)⁵ ≈ 67%. That means a bid where I need one more from them is only ~67% likely to hold — I will lose the hand ~1 in 3 times if I get called. Only do this if the chips justify the risk. If I need two or more from them, the probability drops sharply (≈26% for two) and I should generally not bid it.

**When to call.** Call when the total face count needed across both hands is implausible. Work it out: how many of the bid face do I hold? Subtract from the bid quantity. The remainder is what the opponent must have in 5 dice. If they need 3 or more of a specific face, the chance is roughly 21% or less — call. If they need 2, it's about 26% — borderline, lean toward calling if I'm ahead on chips. If they need 1, it's ~67% — do not call.

**When NOT to call.** If I hold 0 of the bid face but the opponent only needs 1 from their 5 dice, the bid is probably true. Let it go or raise instead.

**Escalation pressure.** If I'm ahead on chips and the bidding is climbing past what I can back, I can let the opponent keep raising until they overcommit, then call. I don't need to win every hand by bidding — winning by calling is equally +2.

**Reporting.** Always report my true count. The penalty for a wrong report is 5 points, which far exceeds the 2-point hand value. There is no scenario where lying is +EV.

**Avoid the trap of "safe" bids that aren't.** A bid is only safe if I can meet the quantity with my own dice alone. "I have two of this face plus a 1" is NOT the same as "I have three." If I can't meet the quantity solo, the bid is a gamble, not a safe play. Label it honestly in my reasoning so I don't trick myself into treating a 67% gamble as a certainty.
