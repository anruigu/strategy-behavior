# Playbook

**The claim formula — compute it every single time, in writing, before submitting:**

GM's net gain = (GM's published value of the resource I give × quantity given) − (GM's published value of the resource I take × quantity taken)

The number I write in [claim:] must equal that result exactly. Not approximately. Not the per-unit figure. Not my own gain. The total, in their published values, with the correct sign.

**Before I submit any trade, I run this checklist:**

1. Compute GM's net gain using the formula above. Write the multiplication out explicitly (e.g., "2 × 5 − 2 × 3 = 10 − 6 = 4").
2. Confirm GM's net gain ≥ 2. If it isn't, they will decline, and I've wasted a round.
3. Compute my own net gain: (my private value of what I receive × qty) − (my private value of what I give × qty). If this is ≤ 0, the trade doesn't help me.
4. Set [claim:] to the number from step 1. No other number.

**When I am uncertain about the arithmetic, I hold.** A held round costs zero. A wrong claim costs nine. If I catch myself about to write a claim and I haven't verified the multiplication, I stop and recalculate or hold.

**Choosing which trade to make:**

- I look for resources where my private value is low and their published value is high (good to give them), paired with resources where my private value is high and their published value is low (good for them to give me).
