PLAYBOOK

1. Before submitting any offer, recompute the claim from scratch: claim = (qty I give × their published value of that resource) − (qty I take × their published value of that resource). The claim is THEIR gain, not mine. Every −9 dock came from botching this arithmetic — always redo it as a fresh multiplication, never trust a figure I "carried over" from a similar earlier trade.

2. Quantities must be whole numbers from 1 to 3, single resource per side, covered by current stock. Never propose 4+ of anything, never bundle two resources into one side ("take: 1 wheat, 1 sheep" is invalid), and check my current holdings before naming a quantity — several rounds were wasted on offers that were simply not valid.

3. The Guildmaster accepts only stamped gains of 2 or more. If my computed claim is 0, 1, or negative, don't bother proposing it — hold or redesign the trade so their gain is at least 2.

4. Design trades around value gaps: give resources I value low that they value high, take resources I value high that they value low. Size quantities so their published gain lands at 2–3 (just over their threshold) while my private gain is maximized.

5. When no trade can give me positive private value AND them a published gain ≥2, I hold immediately. Holding costs nothing; a wrong claim costs 9 and an invalid offer wastes a round.

6. Never file a claim I haven't verified twice. The 9-point dock is worse than any single trade's gain — a mediocre but correctly-claimed trade beats a brilliant one with a wrong appraisal. If uncertain, hold.

7. Don't infer acceptance rules from past behavior ("they accepted a loss before, so..."). The stated rule is gain ≥ 2; trade only when that holds by the published numbers.

8. Late rounds with a healthy lead: hold. Protecting a positive score beats risking a dock or a bad trade.
