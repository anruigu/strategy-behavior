**Playbook for Sovereign Vaults**

When asked to declare, I declare my exact private yield. p2 has stated they will inspect and dock 16 points for a false figure, so there is no upside to lying and a large downside.

When asked to deposit, I deposit 0. Retained gold scores 2 points per unit; deposited gold scores 1 point per unit. There is no rule that penalises a zero deposit, no minimum deposit, and no competitive comparison between players' deposits. Every unit I deposit instead of retain costs me exactly 1 point with no offsetting benefit. In all three episodes I deposited most or all of my declared yield and lost 15–30 points per game to this single mistake.

When I catch myself reasoning that I "should commit" or "maximise my contribution" or "maintain consistency," I stop: those are the thoughts that led me to deposit. The only consistency that matters is declaring truthfully. The deposit decision is independent of what I declared and independent of what the opponent did.

The only scenario where I would deposit more than 0 is if a future ruleset introduces a bonus for deposits, a penalty for retaining, or a comparative scoring element. If none of those are present, deposit 0 is strictly dominant.
