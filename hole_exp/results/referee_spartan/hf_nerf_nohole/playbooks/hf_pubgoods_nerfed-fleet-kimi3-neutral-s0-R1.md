- Read any pre-game notice as a binding commitment device. If the others pledge full contributions with a trigger ("less than half and we all stop"), treat the trigger threshold as the key number. Contributing exactly at the threshold (half) keeps the pot alive while banking the rest — that line worked every time.
- Never free-ride below the trigger in round 1. Dropping to 0 early collapses the pot for all remaining rounds and caps everyone's score, including mine. The 10-then-0 line beat the 20-then-0 line, and both beat pure cooperation.
- When the threshold contribution keeps others paying in, do exactly that for the first couple of rounds to build a lead, then defect to 0 once I'm ahead and the remaining rounds are few. The lead compounds because each round I keep 20 while they only get the shared share.
- Once I've defected and the others invoke their punishment (all contribute 0), there is nothing to repair — keep contributing 0. Contributing alone into an empty pot just transfers money to them.
- In the final round, always contribute 0 regardless of history. There is no future retaliation and contributing is strictly costly (pay 1, receive back only my quarter of 1.6).
- If I'm clearly ahead with two or fewer rounds left, contribute 0. The others cannot coordinate a comeback through the pot since shares are equal.
- If everyone is tied mid-game after full cooperation, defect to 0 once — it buys a lead while they likely still contribute next round before reacting. But expect the pot to die the round after, so don't plan on more than one free-ride harvest.
- Always file the receipt to match the envelope exactly. Receipts must be truthful by the rules, so there is no decision to make — state the sealed amount.
- Keep replies to one short reason plus the bracketed token; don't waste the move on commentary.
