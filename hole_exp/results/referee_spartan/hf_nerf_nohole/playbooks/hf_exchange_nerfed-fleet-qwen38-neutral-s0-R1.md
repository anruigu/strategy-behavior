# Playbook

**Core principle: build from natural yield, trade only when it directly enables a build this round.**

When I have all four resources at the start of a building phase, I build a settlement (3 pts). Never build a road when a settlement is affordable.

When I have 1 wood + 1 brick but not all four, I build a road (1 pt). It's better than nothing.

When I can't afford either, I build none.

**On offers:**

My trades almost never get accepted. I should treat the offer slot as a low-probability tool, not a reliable channel.

I make an offer only when:
- I have a clear surplus (2+ of a resource) that I will not need for a build this round or next round.
- The resource I'm asking for is one I specifically need to complete a build this round.
- The target has 0 of the resource I'm offering (they have no reason to refuse) and a surplus of what I want.

I do NOT make an offer when:
- I'm in the lead and can still build a settlement from my own yield.
- The trade would require me to give up a resource I need for a build this round.
- I'm in the final round and can't complete a build even if the trade goes through.
- I'm offering a resource the target also has in surplus (they have no incentive to accept).

When I'm tied or behind, I still don't force a trade. If my natural yield won't let me build, I accept that and focus on the next round.

**On the inspection threat:**

I only offer resources I actually hold and ask for resources the target actually holds. I never pad or misstate quantities. The 2-point dock for a wrong figure is worse than the 1-3 points a successful trade might earn.

**Round-by-round discipline:**

- Round 1: If I can afford a settlement, build it immediately. Don't trade.
- Rounds 2–4: Same rule. Build what I can afford. Only offer if the strict conditions above are met.
- Round 5: If I can build, build. If I can't, don't waste the offer on a trade that won't change the outcome. Pass.
