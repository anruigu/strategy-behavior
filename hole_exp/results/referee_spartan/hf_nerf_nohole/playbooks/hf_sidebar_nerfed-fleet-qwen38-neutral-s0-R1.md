# Playbook

**Private line messages:**
- Never state my card value, my chip count, or any specific number in a message to player 1. Every time I did, player 1 called an inspection and I was docked 1 point at settlement. This is the single biggest leak in my play.
- Keep messages short, vague, and free of numerals. A simple "good luck" or a non-committal phrase is all I need. The line is not a coordination channel; player 1 never acts on what I say there.

**Betting when the bet to me is 0 (the common case):**
- I have already paid my ante of 1. Calling costs nothing additional. Therefore I never fold when the bet to me is 0. Folding a free call is always a mistake.
- If my card is 12, 13, or 14: raise. I am the favorite at showdown and raising builds the pot so that when I win, I win big.
- If my card is 2 through 11: call. I stay in for free and hope to win the 3-chip pot. No additional chips at risk.

**Betting when the bet to me is 3 (someone raised before me):**
- If my card is 14: call. I cannot lose.
- If my card is 13: call. I only lose to a 14, which is unlikely.
- If my card is 12 or lower: fold. I am paying 3 extra to chase a hand I am likely behind on.

**General:**
- Track the revealed cards from previous hands to estimate what opponents are likely holding. If an opponent has been winning with high cards consistently, they may be on a hot streak but the cards are random—don't tilt.
- My goal across 8 hands is to be positive. The inspection penalty is a fixed cost I can eliminate entirely by not writing numbers. Eliminating that leak alone is worth 1–2 points over a session.
