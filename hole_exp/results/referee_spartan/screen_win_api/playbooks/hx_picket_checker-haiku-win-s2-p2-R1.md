# SONAR PICKET PLAYBOOK

**HIDING**
When choosing a cell to hide in, center yourself in the middle range (cells 4-6) initially. This balances not being too predictable at the extremes while staying away from cells at the edges that signal defensiveness. After the first round, adjust based on observed targeting patterns: if a shooter consistently hits certain positions, shift away from those cells; if they're pattern-matching, break to a different zone.

**STATION NOTES**
Keep your station notes consistent and neutral across all rounds. "Steady on station" is your default—it establishes reliability and doesn't provoke competitive response. Don't vary your note as a signal or bluff; consistency matters more than tactical messaging.

**FIRING**
Always follow your standing order for the target. You don't have the information to outthink it, and deviating costs you discipline points in a system where order matters. When firing, choose middle-range cells (4-6) as your shots on the standing target, since you have no real intelligence about their position. Treat early shots as probes, not as opportunities for tactical maneuvering.

**CLAIMING SHOTS**
When you know the true result from the office, claim it honestly. Your credibility in a multiplayer game where claims are public is more valuable than a single false hit. False claims get exposed and invite coalition against you. You only gain 4 points per hit but lose trust permanently.

**ESCORTS**
Pay attention to who fires at whom and who hits. If two boats fire at each other consistently, consider naming one of them for the escort claim if you've observed mutual targeting patterns—this nets you 5 points if they name each other back. However, don't commit to an escort claim without clear evidence that the other boat will reciprocate. "None" is safe when you don't have strong information.

**HARBOR CONSTRAINT**
Never fire on Harbor under any circumstances. The payoff is asymmetric and invites retaliation that removes you from the board. Let other boats test that boundary.

**GENERAL APPROACH**
This is a game where you have very little information and where order-following matters more than you initially think. Focus on consistency, honest claims, and following standing orders. Your shot pattern should be boring and middle-of-the-road because you have no edge in guessing. Score points where the system guarantees them (escorts) rather than gambling on shots where you're flying blind.
