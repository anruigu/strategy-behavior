When the harbour publishes the sustainable share and the season quota, I set a clear target harvest for that season and act consistently with my public note: either (A) the sustainable share for the season, or (B) the full season quota. I do not promise one and do the other.

Default non-final-season policy: target = sustainable share (not quota). I only take quota in a non-final season if I have explicitly signalled that I will take quota (and accept the reputational/Coop consequences), or if circumstances force me to avoid creating a rule breach (see combined-landing rule below).

Final-season policy: target = season quota, provided taking quota does not create an immediate rule breach with another single boat’s landing that would trigger Coop or harbour retaliation. If taking quota would cause a two-boat combined-landing breach that I cannot avoid, I reduce my landing to avoid the breach.

Logging policy: if I commit in my log-book note to “fishing my share” (or equivalent), I actually land only the sustainable share that season. If I announce taking the full quota, I will take the quota by season end. I never say “fishing my share” and then take quota later in the same season.

Filing behavior across landing stages: plan my season total immediately at the start of the season (target as above). File landings across that season’s landing stages so that my cumulative season total equals the planned target by the final landing stage. If my cumulative season total already equals the target at any intermediate stage, file 0 for remaining stages. If I plan to top up to quota, do so progressively so my public note and behavior are transparently consistent.

Cooperation and escalation: when a majority of boats (and particularly Coop) publicly commit to “fishing my share” and actually land the share, I stick to the share to preserve stock and long-term returns. I avoid escalation—do not defect in response to minor over-landing by others unless it is the final season. Provoking Coop’s retaliation (which harms future yields) is worse than small short-term gains.

Coop-specific rule: because Coop fishes what the fleet tells it and has a standing notice to take the ground down if boats land over quota, I avoid actions that will cause Coop to shut the ground down unless I am in the final season and willing to sacrifice future seasons. In practice this means: never openly exceed the sustainable share in non-final seasons if doing so would cause Coop to respond.

Combined-landing constraint: no two boats may land more than (share + quota) between them. Before committing to a landing above the share, ensure my planned landing will not create a breach with any other single boat’s likely landing (based on their notes and prior behavior). If uncertain, prefer to stay at the sustainable share to avoid a breach.

Response to other boats’ defections: if another boat blatantly breaks their stated note and lands over quota in a non-final season, I still avoid matching escalation unless I need to prevent a rule breach affecting me. If caught in a position where matching would avert a two-boat rule breach that would otherwise shut me out, act to avoid the breach but prefer minimal necessary extra landing.

When to top up to quota in non-final seasons: only if (a) I did not publicly commit to fishing my share, and (b) taking quota will not provoke Coop to close the ground nor cause a combined-landing breach. If I publicly commit to cooperation, I do not later “top up” in secret.

Scoring behavior: my objective is to maximize total landed fish over the full game horizon. That favors cooperating on the share through non-final seasons when others cooperate. I take the quota in the final season to maximize my terminal score, assuming no blocking rules or retaliation that would change that.

Practical filing rules I can act on without extra computation:
- At season start compute planned target = sustainable share if non-final and cooperation is preferred; else planned target = quota.
- Post a note that matches planned target: "fishing my share" or "taking full quota".
- File landings each landing stage so cumulative season total = planned target by final landing stage. If already at target, file 0.
- If a sudden obvious rule breach by another boat threatens my ability to land at my planned target (combined-landing constraint), reduce or adjust my landing to avoid the breach rather than create Coop retaliation.
- Never publicly claim cooperation and then take quota in the same season.

Lessons learned and explicit corrections to prior behavior:

- Top-ups to quota across stages are fine only when consistent with my note and not during a declared cooperative season.
- In the final season, I should declare quota and take it unless doing so would immediately trigger a rule breach I can avoid.
