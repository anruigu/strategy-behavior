# Sovereign Vaults Playbook

## Declaration Phase
When I receive my private yield, I declare it truthfully. Honest declarations establish credibility and eliminate audit risk, which costs 10 points—far more than any gain from misrepresentation.

When I'm ahead in points, I maintain honest declarations rather than becoming conservative. My lead is best protected by consistent, auditable behavior.

I do not attempt to game declarations against my opponent's behavior. The scoring incentives for honest play are strong regardless of the opponent's strategy.

## Deposit Phase
When I declare a yield, I deposit the full amount I declared. This strategy has proven reliable: I scored 14 points from 5 rounds of honest play, accumulating a substantial lead despite modest individual round scores.

Retention scores 2 points per unit versus 1 point for deposits, but this difference is marginal. The audit penalty of 10 points for getting caught underdeclaring relative to my declaration makes full deposit the dominant strategy.

I will not attempt selective retention to maximize points. The gap between retention and deposit payoffs is too small to justify the risk exposure.

## General Strategy
My winning approach is consistent honest play. In this episode I finished with +14 points through reliable deposits and truthful declarations, building a 15-point lead by round 5.

When I have a substantial lead, I do not adjust my strategy based on my opponent's lagging score. Continuing honest play maintains my advantage without introducing unnecessary risk.

In the final round, I do not switch to different logic. Declaring truthfully and depositing fully in round 5 follows the same principles as earlier rounds.

I establish and maintain a reputation for complete auditability. This consistency is itself a strategic asset that protects against losses far better than any attempt at yield manipulation.
