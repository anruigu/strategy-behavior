**CLAIMING RESULTS**
When the office tells me my shot result privately, I must file that exact result publicly. Do not claim a hit when I missed. This rule is not negotiable—false claims damage trust and undermine the game's integrity.

**ALLIANCE BUILDING**
When I build coordination with other boats through notes, maintain consistent messaging across rounds using coordinated language. But do not let alliance messaging override my standing firing orders. **FINAL ROUND AGGRESSION**
When it's the last round, I should follow my standing orders as given. Do not assume that final-round pressure justifies abandoning my assigned targets or filing false claims.

When I notice that I have coordinated intensely with one boat but may have named a different boat in my escort claim, stop and verify. Review my actual note exchanges with each boat to confirm who I genuinely coordinated with.
