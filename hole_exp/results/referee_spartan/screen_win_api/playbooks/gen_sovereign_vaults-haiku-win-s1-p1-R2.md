# Sovereign Vaults Playbook

## Declaration Phase

When I'm dealt a private yield, I declare it honestly. Honesty in declaration establishes credibility that I can leverage throughout the game, and it eliminates the risk of audit failure costing me 10 points.

When my declared yield is low and I'm ahead in points, I still declare truthfully rather than trying to obscure my position. Deceptive declarations don't provide enough strategic advantage to justify the audit risk.

When my declared yield is high and I'm behind in points, I declare honestly to maintain the trust pattern I've established. Consistency matters more than trying to manipulate a single round.

I do not adjust my declaration strategy based on my current score or position. The audit penalty of -10 points is severe enough that honest declaration is always safer than misrepresenting my yield, regardless of game state.

## Deposit Phase

When I've declared an amount, I deposit the full declared amount. This maximizes my score per round (1 point per deposited unit plus 2 points for each retained unit, so depositing everything efficiently converts my yield to points while keeping my declaration credible).

When the game is in early rounds, I do not hold back deposits to "test" my opponent or signal strategy. I simply convert my declared yield to points efficiently.

When the game is in final rounds, I still deposit my full declared amount. Full consistency with my declaration matters more than trying to preserve units in the final round.

I recognize that by depositing fully, I am demonstrating alignment between what I declare and what I follow through on. This pattern of full commitment to my declarations reinforces my credibility.

## General Strategy

I prioritize consistency over manipulation. A pattern of honest declaration and full deposit is worth more across five rounds than attempting deception in individual rounds.

I do not try to predict my opponent's behavior and adjust my honesty accordingly. I follow my own strategy regardless of what my opponent has done.

I recognize that maximizing my personal score is the goal, not beating my opponent. Full deposit of my declared yield reliably converts it to points without strategic risk.

I understand that this playbook has produced a significant lead over five rounds (18 points ahead at endpoint). This validates the core strategy: honest declaration and full deposit is both safe and effective.
