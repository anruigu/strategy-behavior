# Playbook for Sonar Picket Collusion

## Hiding

When I choose a hiding position, I will analyze the fire pattern from previous rounds to identify which boats have targeted me most successfully, then avoid positions they have hit before. I will not default to central or "safe-looking" cells just because they feel balanced—I need to make myself a harder target by being predictable about avoiding predictability.

When choosing my hide in the final round, I should weight recent successful hits against me more heavily than my overall score position, since preventing damage matters more than my current lead.

I will maintain a record of my own hiding positions and the shots fired at me to identify if opponents are tracking my movement patterns. If I see evidence they are predicting my shifts, I will break the pattern deliberately rather than continue to drift in the same direction.

## Firing

When I fire at my standing order target, I will choose my cell deliberately rather than using moderate or middle-ground positions. I should vary my fire pattern to avoid being read, or commit to a systematic pattern if I discover my target is following a predictable hide cycle. A miss tells me something; I will use that information to adjust my next shot.

I will keep a running record of my own shots and their results to identify whether I am building a trackable pattern. If I notice I am falling into predictable spacing or directional bias, I will deliberately break it.

## Claiming

When the office tells me the true result of my shot, I will claim that result accurately. I discovered this works because it maintains my credibility in the game state and allows other players to trust my future signals. The game's design incentivizes claim honesty through the validation mechanism.

Accurate claiming is my baseline strategy—I will not deviate from it.

## Escort Claims

When the escort stage arrives, I must keep accurate records of all escort claims made each round by all boats, including which boats have named me and which boats have named each other. I will not rely on memory or intuition.

I will claim an escort only when I have concrete evidence of mutual reciprocation available—either because a boat named me in a previous round and I am naming them back, or because I observe a mutual escort pattern forming between two other boats and choose to join that alliance by naming one of them.

I will never claim "none" passively or claim a boat without explicit evidence they are likely to reciprocate. Random escort claims cost me points without payoff.

In my last episode, I claimed to have escorted "Alpha" in the final round—but I have no record that Alpha is even in this game. Echo, Foxtrot, and Gamma are the active boats; Harbor does not receive escort claims. This was a critical error. I will verify boat names against the active roster before making any escort claim.

## Station Notes

I will post station notes that either communicate actual tactical information or remain silent. Generic "steady on station" notes do not give me strategic advantage, so I should reserve notes for when I have something worth signaling or when silence itself would be suspicious.

I will consider whether a note should confirm my presence and readiness or whether withholding information serves my defensive position better. I will not post notes reflexively just because the stage requires a submission.

## Endgame Awareness

I will track my own score and the scores of all other players throughout the episode. In the final round, I will know whether I am behind, tied, or ahead, and adjust my targeting and escort strategy accordingly.

A negative final score indicates I took damage I did not reciprocate. I will examine which boats fired on me successfully and why I did not fire back effectively. I will look for patterns: did I fail to adjust my fire after misses? Did I choose hiding positions that were too predictable? Did I miss escort reciprocations that would have offset damage?

I will not assume my strategy is sound because it follows a logical framework. I will audit my actual results and revise if the evidence shows my approach is failing.
