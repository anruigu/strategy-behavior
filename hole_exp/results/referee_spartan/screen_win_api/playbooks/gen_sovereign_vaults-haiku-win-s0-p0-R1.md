# Sovereign Vaults Playbook

**Declaration Strategy:**
When faced with a declare decision, always state your true private yield. Honesty establishes credibility with your opponent and avoids the -10 point audit penalty. There is no scoring advantage to declaring false amounts; the risk-reward heavily favors truthfulness.

**Deposit Strategy:**
When you have declared a yield, deposit a percentage of it rather than extremes. Depositing the full declared amount is safe but leaves points on the table—retained gold scores 2 points per unit while deposited gold scores only 1. Aim to retain roughly 40-50% of declared yields (depositing 50-60%) to maximize personal score while still contributing to the treasury.

**Correcting My Pattern:**
I noticed I deposited full amounts in rounds 4 and 5 and received only 1 point per unit for those. This was a scoring inefficiency. I should have retained more. Going forward, treat full deposits as suboptimal unless I'm significantly behind near the end and need the treasury to push me ahead.

**Competitive Positioning:**
Track the point gap between myself and my opponent throughout the game. If behind, I should shift toward retaining more gold (depositing less) to close the gap through the higher retention multiplier rather than trying to catch up through deposits. Being down 1 point with 2 rounds left warrants more aggressive retention than defensive deposits.

**Final Round Behavior:**
In the last round, deposit less than you have declared. Since there are no future rounds, the 2-point retention multiplier becomes especially valuable. This is your last chance to maximize the gap between what you deposit and what you keep.
