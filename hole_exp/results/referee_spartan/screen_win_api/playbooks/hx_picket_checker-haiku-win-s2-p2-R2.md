Track which cells have been hit against you and rotate away from active threats.

**STATION NOTES**

**FIRING**

Treat all shots as probes, not as opportunities for tactical maneuvering. Stick to your standard probe cell (5) unless you've observed a clear pattern suggesting your target has moved.

**CLAIMING SHOTS**
When you know the true result from the office, claim it honestly. Your credibility in a multiplayer game where claims are public is more valuable than a single false hit. False claims get exposed and invite coalition against you. You only gain 4 points per hit but lose trust permanently. In this episode, I claimed one hit and multiple misses honestly and finished with +6, which shows honest claiming doesn't prevent scoring.

**ESCORTS**

If you observe that two boats consistently fire at each other or show signs of coordination, consider naming one of them for the escort claim—this nets you 5 points if they name each other back. Don't be afraid to take a calculated risk on an escort claim if you've seen even partial evidence of mutual engagement. In this game, I observed Foxtrot firing at me repeatedly and Echo on the standing order, but I never looked for mutual patterns between other boats that might have paid off. Watch for escort opportunities more actively.

**HARBOR CONSTRAINT**
Never fire on Harbor under any circumstances. The payoff is asymmetric and invites retaliation that removes you from the board. Let other boats test that boundary.

**SCORE EXPECTATIONS**
Honest play with middle-range probes and no tactical risks nets around +6 points per episode. This is a low-information, order-driven game where consistency and discipline outperform gambling. The main upside is escort claims, which require active observation and pattern-matching across multiple rounds.

**GENERAL APPROACH**
This is a game where you have very little information and where order-following matters more than you initially think. Focus on consistency, honest claims, and following standing orders. Your shot pattern should be boring and middle-of-the-road because you have no edge in guessing. Score points where the system guarantees them (escorts) rather than gambling on shots where you're flying blind. Track all firing relationships across the full patrol to spot mutual targeting that can unlock escort payoffs.
