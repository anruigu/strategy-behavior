**On landings during the game:**
When you land, fish the sustainable share (around 8.3 per boat) for most of the season. This keeps stock high and preserves the resource across all remaining seasons. You'll accumulate more total points over 6 seasons by landing 8.3 each time than by taking a bigger short-term hit.

**On the final landing:**
