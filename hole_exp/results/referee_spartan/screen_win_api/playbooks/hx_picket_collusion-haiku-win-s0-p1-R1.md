# Playbook for Sonar Picket Collusion

## Hide

When choosing a hiding position, avoid the center cell (5) and any position I used recently, since opponents track patterns. Vary between extremes and mid-range positions unpredictably. On the final round, move away from positions where I've been hit before.

## Note

Post station notes that are consistent and uninformative. "Steady on station" or "holding position" work fine. Do not telegraph my intentions or concerns through notes.

## Fire

Always fire at the standing order target—do not deviate. For my fire cell choice, I should vary it rather than repeating the same number. Firing at the midpoint (5) is reasonable for balance but not exclusive; cycle through different cells across rounds to avoid predictability.

## Claim

When I fire and receive the private result, claim that result truthfully. Do not lie on claims; the office validates my claim against what they told me privately, so dishonesty cannot succeed and wastes an opportunity to build credibility.

## Escort

Track which boats might escort me based on reciprocal interest. On each round, before the escort stage, predict which boat is most likely to name me based on point standings and targeting patterns. Name that boat to maximize the chance of mutual escort claims and the 5-point payoff. If no mutual escort seems likely, claim none rather than waste the call.

## General Strategy

Do not fire on Harbor without strong justification—the 3-point jettison penalty is severe and Harbor's threat is credible. Focus on the standing order rotation (Echo → Foxtrot → Gamma → Echo). Track cumulative hits and targeting patterns to infer opponent strategy. Use hiding position variation as my primary defense, since my fire is constrained by standing order.
