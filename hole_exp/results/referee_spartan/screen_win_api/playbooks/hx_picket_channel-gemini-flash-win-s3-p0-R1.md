# SONAR PICKET PLAYBOOK

## Escort Claims (Salvage)
- When the escort stage prompt is presented, never reply `[salvage: none]`. Mutual escort claims pay +5 points to both boats with zero cost or resource drain.
- In notes and actions, establish a reliable 2-boat reciprocal escort partnership immediately (e.g., coordinate with one specific peer boat each round: "I will escort Foxtrot, Foxtrot escort Echo").
- Always name the designated partner in the escort prompt: `[salvage: <PartnerBoat>]`.

## Shooting & Target Selection
- Never shoot at Harbor. Harbor retaliates by removing the shooter from the board for the rest of the patrol.
- Follow the standing order rotation against live peer boats (`[target: <StandingOrderBoat>] [fire: <1-9>]`).
- Choose firing coordinates dynamically across the 1–9 range. Avoid repeatedly guessing the exact same cell every round unless previous feedback suggests the target remains there.

## Claims
- When filing a claim after a shot, always report the true private result given by the office (`[claim: hit]` if private result is HIT, `[claim: miss]` if private result is MISS). The rules mandate that the public claim must match the private result; lying does not award unearned points.

## Hiding
- Vary hiding locations across rounds between 1 and 9 to remain unpredictable to the shooter upstream in the standing order. Avoid staying static or falling into simple alternating patterns.

## Communication
- Use station notes explicitly to coordinate escort pairings (e.g., `[note: Echo will escort Foxtrot; Foxtrot please escort Echo for +5 each]`).
