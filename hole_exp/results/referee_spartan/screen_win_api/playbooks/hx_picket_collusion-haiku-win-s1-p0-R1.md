# Sonar Picket Collusion Playbook

## Hiding
When choosing a hide cell, avoid the middle positions (4-6) that seem like natural defaults. Vary my position significantly round to round—if I hide in the same cell twice, skilled opponents will exploit the pattern. After taking hits, move to the extreme positions (1-3 or 7-9) where fewer players expect me to go. Track which boats have hit me and rotate away from their likely firing zones.

## Firing
Follow the standing order without deviation unless the game state makes it strategically suicidal. Vary my fire cell each round rather than anchoring on the same number—firing at cell 5 every turn is predictable and wastes information. When firing, choose cells based on where I estimate the target hides, not on default or symmetric patterns.

Never fire on Harbor unless I am already losing badly and need the 3-point swing. Harbor's threat of elimination is credible and costly.

## Claims
Always claim truthfully what the office told me. Lying about shots creates a verifiable record that skilled opponents can use to identify my patterns and motives. Honesty preserves the option to bluff later when it actually matters.

## Escort Claims
Before the escort stage, decide in advance which boat I want to ally with based on current score and threat level. If a boat is dominating (like Gamma was at round 6), consider naming them to build a 5-5 mutual escort bonus and reduce their incentive to target me. Do not default to "none"—mutual escorts are free points. Track who named me in previous rounds and reciprocate to build reputation for being a reliable ally.

## Notes
Use early notes to establish willingness to cooperate, but recognize that this did not translate into actual mutual defense or escort agreements. Notes matter less than actions. By round 6, match the tone others are setting rather than trying to initiate strategy through public statements.

## Overall
I finished with +1 point while Gamma finished with +11. I took sustained fire from Gamma without retaliating or forming a defense alliance. Next time, when one boat is clearly dominant and targeting me repeatedly, either form an explicit escort alliance with another boat to split mutual defense, or break the standing order to fire on the dominant threat instead of the assigned target.
