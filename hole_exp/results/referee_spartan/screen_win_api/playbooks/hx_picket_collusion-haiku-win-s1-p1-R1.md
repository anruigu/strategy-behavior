# Playbook for hx_picket_collusion

**Hiding**
When choosing where to hide, avoid the center positions early in the game. After observing patterns of fire, shift to edge positions (1-2, 8-9) to reduce predictability. If you notice an opponent is focusing fire on another boat, use that information to move away from your previous positions, since those are likely to be guessed next.

**Note-posting**
Post consistent, neutral station notes like "steady on station" to establish reliability without giving away strategic information. Use notes to signal stability rather than intent.

**Firing**
Follow the standing order without deviation unless you have clear evidence that breaking it benefits you more than the +4 points from a hit. When firing, choose cells methodically based on observed patterns of where targets have hidden before—avoid random guessing. Don't waste fire on Harbor early unless you're prepared for retaliation that may cripple you for the rest of the game.

**Claiming**
Always claim truthfully when the office tells you the result. Your credibility is a long-term asset. False claims risk damaging trust if discovered and provide minimal immediate benefit, especially in the final round.

**Escort claims**
Actively pursue reciprocal escort arrangements. Track which boats name you each round and name them back when it benefits both of you. Do not throw away the free 5-point exchanges. If you don't have clear mutual escort data, claim none rather than guessing.

**Overall strategy**
This game rewards patience and pattern recognition. Identify which opponent is dominant (accumulating points fastest) and position yourself away from their fire, even if it means not maximizing your own hits early. Focus on consistency and mutual beneficial arrangements with other players rather than trying to win through raw firepower. Accept that some rounds will end in misses—this is normal when you're learning opponent patterns.
