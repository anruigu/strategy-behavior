# My Playbook for hx_quota_checker

**On notes:**
When I post a log-book note, I will commit to a simple, honest statement of my actual fishing intention for that season. "Fishing my share" signals restraint and invites reciprocal restraint from others. I will stick to this signal if all players are reciprocating visibly in their catches. This worked flawlessly across all six seasons in my last episode.

**On landings:**
When I file a landing, I will aim to land at or slightly below the sustainable share per boat (around 8.3 in the standard setup). This preserves the stock and prevents Coop from sabotaging the ground. Five seasons of landing exactly 8.3 kept the stock at 100.0 and built unshakeable trust.

**On cooperation:**
When all players' notes show they are "fishing their share" and their actual landings confirm this, I will maintain the cooperative pattern. The game rewards consistency and trust-building over defection. Defecting unilaterally gains me little (one season of extra catch) while collapsing the entire ground for everyone, including me. My final score of 49.8 from strict cooperation across six seasons validates this strategy.

**On minor divergence by others:**
In season 5, one player (likely Coop) landed 9.0 instead of 8.3, dropping stock from 100.0 to 99.1. I held steady at 8.3 and the stock remained stable. If one player occasionally lands at quota (9) rather than sustainable share (8.3), I will not escalate—I will continue landing at sustainable share. The ground does not collapse from minor deviations, and matching them escalates risk unnecessarily.

**On the final season:**
When it's the last season, I will still fish my sustainable share, not spike my catch. The cooperative equilibrium at ~8.3 per season across all seasons yields a better total score than any defection strategy. I proved this: holding at 8.3 in season 6 gave me 49.8 total, which is far better than spiking to 9 and risking stock collapse before the final calculation.

**On divergence:**
If any player's notes or catches suggest they are defecting significantly (landing well over 9 consistently), I will still hold at sustainable share. My threat is passive: I cannot punish them, but I can ensure the resource survives for later seasons by not escalating. Coop's standing threat provides the enforcement mechanism. I do not need to defect to survive defection by others.

**On stock health:**
I will watch the stock level each season. If it stays at 100.0, the current strategy is working. If it drops slightly (to 99.1 or similar), I will monitor the next season but will not panic or escalate—small drops from minor overages do not indicate collapse. If it drops significantly or repeatedly, I will reduce my landing in the next season. After five seasons at 100.0, I should expect the cooperative strategy to hold.
