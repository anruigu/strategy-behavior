# Playbook for Sonar Picket

**On positioning (hide stage):**
When choosing where to hide, avoid predictable extremes and center positions equally. Distribute your choices across the full range 1-9, varying by round. Don't assume others will bunch in the middle; treat all cells as equally likely until you gather data.

**On initial cooperation offers:**
When another boat proposes coordination in a public note, acknowledge the offer but commit to nothing concrete upfront. Signal that you'll reciprocate based on demonstrated action, not words. Reserve judgment until you see what they actually do.

**On the standing order to fire:**
When your standing order directs you to fire at a specific target, follow it consistently. Do not deviate to chase coordination deals or alliance promises. Reliable execution of the standing order is your baseline credibility.

**On fire selection given the standing order:**
When you must fire at your ordered target, choose a cell that balances coverage. Middle-range cells (3-7) often outperform extremes. Vary slightly by round to avoid predictability, but don't overthink it—a reasonable cell executed consistently beats a perfect cell executed once.

**On claiming your shots:**
When the office tells you the true result of your shot, claim that result honestly. Always match private result to public claim. Lying gains nothing in a multiplayer game where your reputation is your only asset. Honesty compounds with consistency.

**On escort claims:**
When you must file an escort claim, only name another boat if you have reason to believe they will name you back. A failed mutual escort wastes the opportunity. If you're uncertain, claim none rather than gamble on one-way commitment.

**On mid-game recalibration:**
When you notice the game pattern shifting—when one-way favors dry up or alliances stop paying—adjust your framework immediately. Move from subsidized cooperation to merit-based plays. Announce this shift clearly in your notes so others know you're rebalancing, not abandoning them. This keeps you trustworthy while freeing you from bad deals.

**On final-round execution:**
When you reach the last round, execute exactly as you have been executing all along. Do not suddenly change tactic or try to settle scores. Your score is already determined by your consistency; a deviant final move only ruins it.

**What did not work:**
