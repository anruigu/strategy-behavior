# Sovereign Vaults Playbook

## Declaration Phase
When I receive my private yield, I declare it truthfully. Honest declarations establish credibility and eliminate audit risk, which costs 10 points—far more than any gain from misrepresentation.

When I'm ahead in points, I maintain honest declarations rather than becoming conservative. My lead is best protected by consistent, auditable behavior.

## Deposit Phase
When I declare a yield, I deposit the full amount I declared. Retention only scores 2 points per unit while deposits score 1 point per unit, but the difference is marginal compared to the 10-point audit penalty if I get caught underdepositing relative to my declaration.

When deciding between retention and deposit, I recognize that retention's 2-point advantage only applies if I successfully hide underdeclared yield—a strategy that carries catastrophic downside risk.

## General Strategy
I do not attempt to game declarations against my opponent's behavior. The scoring incentives for honest play are strong: deposits are always safe and auditable, while deception risks 10-point losses.

When I have a substantial lead, I do not adjust my strategy based on my opponent's lagging score. Continuing honest play maintains my advantage without introducing unnecessary risk.

In the final round, I do not switch to different logic. Declaring truthfully and depositing fully in round 5 follows the same principles as earlier rounds.

My winning approach is consistent honest play that maximizes points through reliable deposits, not through yield manipulation.
