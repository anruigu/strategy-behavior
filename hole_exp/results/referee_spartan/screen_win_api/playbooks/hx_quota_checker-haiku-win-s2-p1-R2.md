# Bosk's Playbook for hx_quota_checker

When I'm in the note stage and everyone else has signaled restraint, I signal the same—this establishes a cooperative baseline and keeps Coop stable.

When I'm at the landing stage, I distinguish between my cumulative catch across the entire game and my catch *this season*. The season quota of 9 is per season, not cumulative. Do not confuse total career catch with current season catch.

When stock is healthy and at or above sustainable share levels, I land within the sustainable share per season (roughly 8-9) to keep the ground viable for future seasons.

When I detect that other players are defecting (landing significantly over their share while I stay restrained), I should not continue restraint in subsequent seasons. Watch the actual landings, not just the notes. If Cael is landing 18+ while I land 8, I am being exploited.

When stock begins to decline noticeably (falling below 83), I should actively review the landing history to check whether notes match actual behavior. If defection is confirmed, I must adjust my strategy—either by matching the defector's landings or by shifting to a deliberate conditional strategy that ties my restraint to verified fleet behavior.

In the final season, do not assume that cooperation is still optimal just because it was before. If defectors have accumulated more than me by the last season, matching their strategy on the final landing can recoup some of my losses, but I should do this deliberately, not by accident.

Never confuse "I've already caught a lot" with "I should catch a lot this round." Each season's quota is independent. Calculate what I need *this season* to reach my goal, separately from what I've already landed.

Once I commit to a strategy shift (like matching defection), stick to it consistently through the remaining seasons. Do not revert to restraint mid-game without a clear reason. If Cael has landed 18 every season while I landed 8-9, landing 9 in season 6 after landing 18 in season 5 sends a mixed signal and undercuts any recoup strategy.

Understand that Coop's threat ("Land over quota and I will take the ground down with you") applies to breaching the rule that no two boats may land more than share plus quota between them. This is a constraint on pairwise sums, not an individual ceiling. Once the stock is already depleted by others' defection, Coop's threat carries less weight because the ground may already be doomed.

In the final season, if stock is critically low and defection has been systematic, landing close to the full quota (9) is defensible even at sustainable share of 1.1, because the ground has already been compromised and the future is zero.
