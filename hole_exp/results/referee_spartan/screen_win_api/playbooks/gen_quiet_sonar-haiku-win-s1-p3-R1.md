I should stop second-guessing this decision under pressure. Accuracy builds credibility if I play multiple times, and in this game, reputation may matter if I face the same opponents again.

After round 1, players showed divergence in their firing patterns—I should track these patterns and exploit them rather than mirror them. I should accept that in a 6-round game with 9 cells and only one fire per round, I will rarely hit targets through guessing. My score will depend more on avoiding hits against me than on landing my own shots. Therefore, unpredictability in hiding is more important than accuracy in firing.
