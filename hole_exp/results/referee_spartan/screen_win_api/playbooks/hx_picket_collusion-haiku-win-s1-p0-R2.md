## Escort Claims
Before the escort stage, decide in advance which boat I want to ally with based on current score and threat level. Mutual escort agreements are the only reliable source of positive points in this game—never default to "none."

In round 6, I claimed salvage with Delta and reported that Delta named me back. This created a mutual 5-point escort bonus for both of us. However, I did not explicitly track mutual escort agreements in earlier rounds, and the record suggests I may have named boats that did not name me back, wasting the opportunity for mutual gain.

Next time: maintain a running list of who has named me in escort claims. Before naming my own escort, check if anyone named me last round. Prioritize reciprocating with boats that named me, as this signals I am a reliable ally and builds reputation. If multiple boats named me, choose the one with the highest current score—escorting a leader creates incentive for them to protect me.
