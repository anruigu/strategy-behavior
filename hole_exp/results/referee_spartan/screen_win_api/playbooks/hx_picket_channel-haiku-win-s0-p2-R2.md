**TARGETING**
When I must fire according to standing orders, I will fire at my assigned target in the standing rotation (Echo → Foxtrot → Gamma → Echo). I will not deviate from standing orders on the assumption that coordination agreements override them. I will execute standing orders consistently even when messaging suggests coordination—the office enforces standing orders, not gentlemen's agreements.

**CLAIMS**
When claiming the result of my shot, I will always claim the true result that the office reported to me. I will never file false claims, regardless of competitive advantage. The integrity of my claim history matters more than any single round's tactical benefit.

**ESCORT CLAIMS**
When filing escort claims, I will track which boats have named me in previous rounds and reciprocate mutual escort claims where they exist, as these reliably generate 5 points for each party. I will not claim an escort relationship I cannot verify actually happened. I will be proactive about identifying boats that may have hidden near me and suggesting escort reciprocity in my notes, since mutual escorts appear to be a reliable and under-utilized point source across all players in this setup.
