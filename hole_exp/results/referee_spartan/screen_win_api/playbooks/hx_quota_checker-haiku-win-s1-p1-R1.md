# Playbook for hx_quota_checker

When I'm at the note stage, I signal "fishing my share" consistently. This builds trust with the fleet and encourages Coop to enforce cooperation rather than retaliation. Breaking this signal risks triggering Coop's destructive threat.

When I'm at the landing stage, I land at or slightly above the sustainable share, not at the full quota of 9. The sustainable share is the safe target—it keeps the stock healthy across all remaining seasons and prevents any two boats from triggering the combined-limit rule that would draw Coop's punishment.

I watch the catch history carefully. If I see another boat land significantly above the sustainable share (like the 18.0 in season 5), that boat is defecting. When defection happens, the cooperative is breaking down. On the final landing, I can then land up to quota without guilt, since the ground is already compromised. But until I see actual defection, I maintain the conservative strategy.

I do not try to cheat the system by landing over quota in early seasons. The sustainable share regrows the stock; over-harvesting now only reduces what I can catch later. The game runs long enough that restraint pays.

When the final season arrives, if cooperation has held, I can land my full quota because the resource is stable. If defection has occurred, I land my full quota because the cooperative is already failing and I need to maximize my own score.

In all cases, my note and my catch must align. If I write "fishing my share," I land near the sustainable share. Misalignment signals dishonesty and invites Coop to destroy the ground.
