If I'm unsure whether mutual escort occurred or whether they've demonstrated consistent two-way commitment, claim none rather than guess. Mutual escort requires two-way commitment, not hope. In the final round, verify actual escort behavior patterns before naming anyone, don't rely on final-round promises.
