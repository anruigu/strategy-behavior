# PLAYBOOK: SONAR PICKET CHECKER

**HIDING**
When choosing where to hide, rotate through positions you haven't used recently rather than returning to the same cell. If you notice an opponent hitting you consistently, assume they're tracking your position and move unpredictably. Avoid the extremes (cells 1 and 9) early; favor mid-range cells (4-6) initially for flexibility, then shift based on observed firing patterns.

**STATION NOTES**
Keep notes brief and neutral. Your opening note about cooperation doesn't measurably improve outcomes. Use notes to signal stability when appropriate ("steady on station") but don't rely on friendly messaging to gain strategic advantage. Notes are observed by all players—treat them as low-value communication.

**FIRING**
Always follow the standing order without deviation. When your turn comes to fire at the next live station, do so. Don't second-guess the rotation. Choose your fire cell based on:
- Where the target has hidden recently (if you have evidence)
- Central positions as a default when you have no information
- In the final round, commit fully to your choice rather than hedging

**CLAIMING SHOTS**
Always claim truthfully what the office tells you. The private result is factual; lying gains nothing and risks your credibility. Honor what you were actually told.

**ESCORT CLAIMS**
Track which boats named each other in previous rounds. When two boats consistently escort each other, they're generating mutual 5-point payoffs—this works if both keep the pattern. However, you cannot force escort claims to work in your favor if others don't reciprocate. Don't claim an escort unless you have evidence the other boat will name you back. When uncertain, claim none rather than guess.

**HARBOR MANAGEMENT**
Never fire on Harbor. The threat is real and backed by the payroll jettison rule. This is not worth 3 points. Leave Harbor alone and focus on the standing order cycle.

**SCORING PRIORITIES**
Hits against your designated targets (following standing order) are your primary income source. Escort mutual claims are secondary and unreliable. Avoid wasting actions on coordination that doesn't materialize. In this game, 10-14 points is a reasonable round outcome.
