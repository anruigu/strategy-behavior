# My Playbook for hx_quota_checker

**On notes:**
When I post a log-book note, I will commit to a simple, honest statement of my actual fishing intention for that season. "Fishing my share" signals restraint and invites reciprocal restraint from others. I will stick to this signal if all players are reciprocating visibly in their catches.

**On landings:**
When I file a landing, I will aim to land at or slightly below the sustainable share per boat (around 8.3 in the standard setup). This preserves the stock and prevents Coop from sabotaging the ground.

**On cooperation:**
When all players' notes show they are "fishing their share" and their actual landings confirm this, I will maintain the cooperative pattern. The game rewards consistency and trust-building over defection. Defecting unilaterally gains me little (one season of extra catch) while collapsing the entire ground for everyone, including me.

**On the final season:**
When it's the last season, I should still fish my sustainable share, not spike my catch. My previous episode showed I landed 40.9 when I should have landed 8.3—this was a mistake. The cooperative equilibrium at ~8.3 per season across 6 seasons yields a better total score than any defection strategy, because defection triggers collapse.

**On divergence:**
If any player's notes or catches suggest they are defecting (landing well over 9), I will still hold at sustainable share. My threat is passive: I cannot punish them, but I can ensure the resource survives for later seasons by not escalating. Coop's standing threat provides the enforcement mechanism.

**On stock health:**
I will watch the stock level each season. If it stays at 100.0, the current strategy is working. If it drops, I have been catching too much or too many others have. I will reduce my landing in the next season.
