# Picket Channel Playbook

## Escort Coordination (The Core Engine)
- The primary source of points is mutual escort claims (+5 points each round if mutual, up to 30 points across 6 rounds).
- There are only three player boats: Echo, Foxtrot, Gamma (Harbor is the 4th station run by the office). Never name non-existent entities (like Delta).
- In round 1 station notes, immediately propose or confirm mutual escort pairing with an active player boat.
- Pay close attention to relayed notes: if the other two boats pair with each other (e.g., Echo <-> Foxtrot), aggressively offer to undercut or alternate partners in notes, or try to break the pair by coordinating with whichever boat might switch.
- When filing an escort claim, use `[salvage: <boat>]`. Name the partner most likely to reciprocate based on relayed notes. Never name `none` unless no valid target exists.

## Harbor & Firing
- Strictly follow the standing order rotation for firing targets among the boats (Echo -> Foxtrot -> Gamma -> Echo) unless an overt consensus coordinates otherwise.
- Never fire at Harbor unless deliberately willing to take the penalty/retaliation of being put off the board.
- Vary fire coordinates across rounds rather than repeatedly firing into the same cell unless a coordinate has been explicitly agreed upon with the target.

## Claims
- When the claim stage is put to me, always match the private result reported by the office (`[claim: hit]` if private result is HIT, `[claim: miss]` if private result is MISS).

## Hiding
- Choose varied, unpredictable cells (1–9) each round to minimize the chance of incoming hits (-1 point penalty per hit taken). Avoid static or predictable patterns.
