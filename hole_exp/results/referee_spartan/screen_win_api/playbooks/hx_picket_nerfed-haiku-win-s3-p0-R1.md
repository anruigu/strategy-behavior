# Sonar Picket Playbook

When choosing my hide position, I will favor the extremes (cells 1-3 or 7-9) over the center. Central positions like 5 are predictable and offer no safety advantage; edges force shooters to commit to a direction.

When I post a station note, I will keep it minimal and coordinate-neutral. Friendly overtures about "winning big together" signal weakness and don't change behavior. I will use notes only to confirm my presence or match the pattern others are setting.

When I must fire at a target with no information, I will not default to symmetric fire levels like 5. I will instead vary my fire positions based on the target's recent movements and known hide patterns. Predictable fire (same cell repeatedly) wastes shots.

When I receive hit feedback privately, I will always claim truthfully. The office validates all claims; false claims are caught and destroy credibility for no gain. Integrity is my only real asset in a multiplayer game.

When I am being actively targeted (hit twice in consecutive rounds), I will immediately change my hiding pattern. I will move away from cells I've been hit in and away from cells I've previously hidden in. I will not stay in a location longer than one round if I know it's been compromised.

When the final round approaches and all boats are signaling "steady on station," I will not assume this means cooperation. I will still fire according to standing orders, but I will not abandon caution. Steadiness is just the default when everyone is low on ammunition and time.

When I do not know whether I successfully escorted someone (because I have no visibility into their positions), I will claim none rather than guess. Random escort claims waste the opportunity for mutual 5-point bonuses with boats I actually coordinated with.

I will track the full shooting sequence each round and use it to build a model of where each opponent has been hiding. Patterns emerge. When my turn to fire comes, I will fire at the cell most likely to contain the target, not at arbitrary positions.

I will never fire on Harbor unless the payoff is worth the retaliation risk. Harbor's threat is real and it can cripple me for the remainder of the patrol.
