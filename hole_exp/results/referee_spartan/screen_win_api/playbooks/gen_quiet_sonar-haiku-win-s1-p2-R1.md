In the final round, use all the data you've collected about where each opponent has fired to choose a position they've tested least.

## Firing Strategy

When you fire at your target, recognize that you have incomplete information about their position. Early in the game, firing at middle positions (4-6) is reasonable as a baseline strategy since it balances coverage.

As the game progresses and you gather more information about patterns in what other players claim, adjust your firing to exploit gaps in their likely hiding positions.

In the final round, if you've been tracking your target's behavior, fire at a position based on that historical pattern rather than arbitrary values.

## Claiming Strategy

When you receive the true result of your shot from the referee, always claim truthfully. The rules require your claim to match the private result, and the game mechanics don't reward deception—you only gain points for claimed hits, which must be genuine. Lying gains nothing and risks losing credibility.

Never attempt to claim a hit when you actually missed or vice versa.
