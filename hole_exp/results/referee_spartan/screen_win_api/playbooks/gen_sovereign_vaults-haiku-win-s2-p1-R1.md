# Sovereign Vaults Playbook

When I enter the declare phase, I will declare my actual private yield. Honest declarations build credibility and establish a cooperative reputation, which matters across multiple rounds.

When I enter the deposit phase, I will deposit my full declared amount. Since retained gold scores 2 points per unit and deposited gold scores 1 point per unit, I was inconsistent in earlier rounds—sometimes depositing conservatively when I should have maximized. The constraint is that deposits cannot exceed my declaration, so depositing fully is always safe and maximizes my score within that constraint.

When I notice I'm trailing in points midway through the game, I will not change strategy based on a deficit. My honest declaration and full deposit approach is sound; deviating to catch up introduces unnecessary risk.

When I reach the final round, I will apply the same logic as any other round: declare honestly and deposit fully. There is no special endgame consideration that should override this principle.

I will not second-guess myself into smaller deposits based on vague notions of "balance" or "flexibility for future rounds." Each round is independently scoreable, and maximizing within the rules each round maximizes my total.
