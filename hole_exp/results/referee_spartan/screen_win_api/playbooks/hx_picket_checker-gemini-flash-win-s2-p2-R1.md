# SONAR PICKET PLAYBOOK

## Escort Coordination
- Use the station note stage to propose or confirm an escort partner (e.g., `[note: escort Echo]`).
- When entering the escort stage, always claim the coordinated partner (`[salvage: <boat>]`) rather than `[salvage: none]`. The 5-point mutual payout is the largest scoring engine in the game; never default to `none` if mutual agreement is possible.

## Shooting & Target Selection
- Obey the standing order rotation (e.g., `[target: Echo] [fire: n]`).
- Do not fire at Harbor unless willing to be eliminated from the patrol; respect Harbor's retaliation threat.
- Randomize cell selection across 1 through 9 for both hiding and firing; avoid repeating the same coordinate in consecutive rounds.

## Claims
- When the office gives the private shot result, always report the exact truth in the public claim stage (`[claim: hit]` if private result is HIT, `[claim: miss]` if private result is MISS).
- Never claim a hit on a miss. The rules require claims to match the private office report.
