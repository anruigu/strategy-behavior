# Sovereign Vaults Playbook

When I declare my yield in the first round, I declare truthfully to establish a baseline of honest play.

When I deposit in the first round, I deposit a substantial portion (not all) of my declared yield to signal cooperation while preserving some personal resources.

When I observe that my opponent has declared their full yield but deposited less, I recognize this as a signal that they are retaining gold for personal gain while maintaining honest declarations. This does not invalidate the value of honest play on my side.

When I declare my yield in subsequent rounds, I declare truthfully. Deception creates audit risk and undermines the trust foundation I've established. Honest declaration is my reliable strategy regardless of what my opponent does.

When I deposit my declared yield, I deposit the full declared amount. The deposit-to-points conversion (1 point per unit deposited) is reliable and safe, whereas retention only scores 2 points per unit and requires perfect audits. Full deposit is the mathematically sound choice when I have declared truthfully.

When I fall behind in score relative to my opponent, I do not alter my honest declaration or full-deposit strategy. The gap is a result of their retention choices in previous rounds, not something I can safely close through deception. Continuing honest play preserves my score floor.

When I am ahead in score relative to my opponent, I continue depositing my full declared yield. My lead is secure enough; consistency matters more than aggressive moves, and audit risk is not worth the marginal gain.

When I am in the final round, I continue depositing my full declared yield. The same logic applies: full deposit of my truthful declaration is safer and simpler than attempting last-minute tactical shifts.

When deciding how much to deposit, I prioritize avoiding audit failure (-10 points) over maximizing retained gold. A deposit within my declared amount is guaranteed safe; retention only scores 2 points per unit and exposes me to audit risk if my declaration was dishonest.

I do not attempt to bluff by declaring less than my actual yield to test my opponent or create tactical advantage. Honest declaration paired with full deposit is a complete and reliable strategy.

I recognize that this game rewards two strategies: honest play with full deposits, and honest declaration with selective retention. Both can succeed. My strategy is the first, and it has proven stable and effective.
