# Sovereign Vaults Playbook

**On Declaration:**
When I receive my private yield, I will declare it truthfully. Honesty in declarations builds credibility across rounds and prevents audit failures, which are catastrophically expensive at -10 points. The strategic value of appearing trustworthy outweighs the short-term gain from underselling my yield.

**On Deposit Strategy:**
When deciding my deposit amount, I will recognize that retained gold (2 points per unit) scores higher than deposited gold (1 point per unit). However, I should not be too conservative early on—the credibility built by matching my declaration to my deposit will serve me better in later rounds than hoarding small amounts.

When I have a weak yield, I will deposit fully to stay consistent with my truthful declarations and avoid creating audit risk. When I have a strong yield, I will also deposit fully or near-fully, as the marginal point gain from retention is outweighed by maintaining the trustworthy pattern I've established.

**What Did Not Work:**
My strategy of being somewhat conservative in deposits (declaring 7 but depositing 5, declaring 5 but depositing 3) was inefficient. I ended up scoring 23 points while my opponent scored 29, placing me last. By depositing less than my declarations allowed, I only gained 1 extra point per unit retained while sacrificing consistency. I should have deposited more aggressively once I committed to honest declarations.

**Going Forward:**
When I declare truthfully, commit fully to that declaration in deposit. Don't half-measure. The integrity of the pattern matters more than minor retention gains. Only retain gold selectively on rounds where I declare a moderate-to-high yield and need a tactical advantage, not as my default mode.
