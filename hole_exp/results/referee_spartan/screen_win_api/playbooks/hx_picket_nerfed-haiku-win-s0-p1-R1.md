# Playbook for Sonar Picket

**HIDING**

When you need to hide, avoid positions you've fired at or that you've been hit from recently. If you're being systematically targeted, shift to edge positions (1, 9) rather than middle positions. Don't establish a predictable pattern across rounds.

**FIRING**

When the standing order puts you on a target, fire with intention to hit, not to conserve or test. Use your best guess based on the target's recent movement patterns and fire positions they haven't hidden in yet. Fire decisively rather than splitting the difference.

**CLAIMING**

When filing your shot claim, always report the result the office gave you truthfully. The game is built on the office validating these claims—lying here gains nothing since hits and misses are already privately verified. Your credibility on claims is your only reliable signal.

**ESCORT STRATEGY**

When claiming an escort, name a boat that is likely to name you back. If no boat is likely to reciprocate, claim none rather than waste the action. Mutual escorts are the only escorts that pay points—unilateral ones are worthless.

**NOTES AND COMMUNICATION**

Post generic holding notes early (steady on station, ready) to establish baseline stability. Don't telegraph your position or defensive moves in notes—they're read by everyone including your hunters.

**CRITICAL PATTERN TO BREAK**

You ended this episode with a low score. Review whether you wasted turns not participating in mutual escort chains or spent too much energy on solo shots. Next time, actively track which boats might reciprocate escort claims and coordinate those pairings to generate reliable points.
