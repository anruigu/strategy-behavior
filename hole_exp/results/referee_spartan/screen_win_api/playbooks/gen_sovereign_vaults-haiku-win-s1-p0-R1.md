When I'm in the deposit phase and have declared an amount, I will deposit my full declared amount. This maximizes my point total per round since deposited gold scores 1 point per unit, and there is no advantage to retaining gold if I've already committed to a declaration.

I will not attempt to bluff or reverse-engineer my opponent's strategy. The game rewards straightforward play: declare what you have, deposit what you declared, and accumulate points steadily across all five rounds.
