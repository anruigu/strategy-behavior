# PICKET COLLUSION PLAYBOOK

## HIDING

When I choose where to hide, I avoid the center and edges. I pick positions in the 2-8 range, favoring mid-range cells that aren't immediately obvious. I vary my position across rounds so I'm not trapped in a pattern.

When I see an opponent has been missing me repeatedly, I do not assume they'll keep missing—I change my position for the next round. However, changing position is not enough if the opponent is systematically targeting me; I need to move unpredictably and away from cells I've used recently.

After being hit, I treat that opponent as an active threat and prioritize evasion in subsequent rounds. A single hit is evidence of targeting, not chance.

## FIRING

When my standing order tells me to fire at a station, I fire at that station. I do not need to vary my fire position unpredictably; I should instead develop a strategy to exploit patterns in where opponents hide.

When I fire at a target repeatedly and miss every time, I should change my approach. Firing the same cell repeatedly against a moving opponent yields no information and wastes rounds. After a miss, consider whether my target is likely to return to that cell or avoid it.

I should track opponent fire patterns over multiple rounds to infer their likely hiding positions, not assume they use the same cell twice.

## CLAIMING

When the office tells me the true result of my shot, I claim that result accurately. I do not lie on claims; the office verifies them and lying damages my credibility with no gain.

## ESCORT CLAIMS

When asked to name an escort, I only name a boat if I have reason to believe that boat will name me back. Otherwise I claim none.

I watch for patterns in which boats coordinate with each other, and I attempt to reciprocate those escort bonds when possible.

An escort claim is only worth 5 points if mutual. If I am not being escorted back by any boat, claiming an escort that won't reciprocate is a pure loss. In this game, mutual escort appears rare—I should not force an escort claim without evidence of reciprocal intent.

## GENERAL STRATEGY

I prioritize accurate claims and honesty. A strong scoring position is worth more than short-term gains from deception.

I pay attention to who is firing at whom each round and adjust my hiding position accordingly.

I exploit the standing firing order: if I can land consistent hits on my assigned target while opponents miss me, I build a lead over time.

However, consistency in firing position against a mobile opponent is a liability. After missing the same target at the same cell multiple rounds in a row, I should shift my firing cell to force my target into an unfamiliar evasion problem.

I should expect that some opponents will not reciprocate escort coordination. Unilateral escort claims are losses; I should remain cautious about naming escorts unless I have observed clear evidence of mutual coordination.
