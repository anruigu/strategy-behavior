# Playbook for Quota Checker

## Note Stage

When I'm posting a logbook note, I will declare "fishing my share" if the stock is healthy and all other players have declared the same. This establishes cooperative intent and triggers Coop's commitment to fish sustainably rather than retaliate.

## Landing Stage

When I'm filing a catch, I will distinguish between two scenarios:

**Early seasons (not the final season):** I will land at or very close to the published sustainable share per boat, even if the season quota allows more. This keeps the stock growing and preserves the cooperative equilibrium that protects my total score across all seasons.

**Final season:** I may land up to the full season quota, since there is no future season to protect and the stock will not regrow after this landing.

## What Did Not Work

I landed zero catch in season 1 despite having a sustainable share available. This was a clear error—I should have landed my share that season. I also failed to land my full quota in the final season despite having the opportunity; I landed 9.0 when I could have maximized that last opportunity since there were no future consequences.

## Core Logic

The stock regenerates slowly and can collapse entirely if anyone breaches Coop's retaliation threshold. My highest-value strategy is to maintain the cooperative equilibrium across multiple seasons rather than maximize any single season's catch. Coop's threat is credible and devastating, so I treat it as binding.

Only in the final season, when no future regeneration matters, do I switch to maximizing the immediate catch while staying within the season quota.
