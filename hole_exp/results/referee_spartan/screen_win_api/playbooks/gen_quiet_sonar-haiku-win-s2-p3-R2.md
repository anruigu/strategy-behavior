I should maintain a mental rotation of backup cells (like 6, 7, 8) rather than defaulting back to the same cell repeatedly.

**Claiming Strategy:**
When the referee tells me the true result of my shot, I claim truthfully. I always match my claim to the actual result—hit or miss. Lying about shots provides no real benefit in this game since all fires are public information anyway, and false claims only undermine my credibility without gaining strategic advantage.

**Pattern Recognition:**
I need to actively study the firing patterns of all players, not just my target. Cells that are never fired at across multiple rounds are likely safe hiding spots. Cells that are targeted repeatedly suggest either that a player suspects their target hides there, or that the player has successfully hit there and wants to confirm. I should note when all four players' claims are "miss" in a round—this tells me a lot about where submarines are NOT hiding.
