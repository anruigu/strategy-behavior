# Quota Checker Playbook

When I post my logbook note, I declare "fishing my share" consistently. This signals cooperative intent and establishes a baseline expectation for the other boats.

When I file a landing, I land at the sustainable share level (8.3 in this case) rather than pushing toward the quota limit (9). This keeps the stock healthy and avoids triggering Coop's sabotage threat.

I trust that other boats will do the same if they've posted "fishing my share." The notes are reliable signals of intent.

I maintain this cooperative strategy across all seasons, not switching to exploitation in later rounds. Consistency builds trust and keeps the ground productive for the full duration.

When the stock remains at or near maximum, I know my conservative landings are working—continue them.

I do not attempt to land more than the sustainable share even in the final season. The temptation to exploit when the end is near does not outweigh the risk of ground collapse or the cooperative equilibrium I've established.

My target score is sustainable share × number of boats × number of seasons. This gives me a reliable floor and a defensible strategy.

If I see other boats exceed their sustainable share in their notes or landings, I still hold my line at the sustainable share. Do not race to the bottom by escalating.
