# Playbook for Sonar Picket

## Hiding

When choosing a hide position, vary it across the board rather than clustering in middle positions. Track which cells you've used recently and avoid repeating them. The predictability of a single favorite spot (like 5) costs you hits. Alternate between different regions of the 1-9 line each round. Don't try to reason about other boats' positions—just make yourself hard to predict by varying genuinely.

## Station Notes

Post "steady on station" consistently. This establishes credibility and doesn't reveal information. Don't experiment with different notes; the standard message is safe and matches what others do.

## Targeting

Follow the standing order without deviation. When it's your turn to fire, you fire at the designated next station. This removes decision paralysis and keeps you predictable to the office (which helps with coordination). Fire at a cell that's different from where you hid this round, since players may correlate their own position with their firing position.

## Shooting Accuracy

After your first hit, treat subsequent shots as having lower confidence. You hit once early, then went cold. Rather than repeating the same cell against the same target, vary your fire systematically. Don't return to cell 5 repeatedly—it wasn't working. Try corners (1, 9) and edges more often; they're less likely to be occupied than middle cells.

## Claiming

Always report the true result that the office tells you. The only payoff from false claiming is short-term, and the downside is permanent. The office knows the truth and validates claims. Honor your claims.

## Escort Claims

Don't claim escorts unless you have good reason to believe they will be reciprocated. Naming "none" is safe and costs you nothing. Only claim a specific escort if you've observed that boat naming you in recent rounds—look for mutual patterns before claiming. Otherwise leave it blank.

## End-of-game decisions

The final round is not special. Play it the same way you've played every other round. Don't change your hiding, claiming, or escort strategy because it's round 6 instead of round 3. Consistency is your advantage, not a liability.
