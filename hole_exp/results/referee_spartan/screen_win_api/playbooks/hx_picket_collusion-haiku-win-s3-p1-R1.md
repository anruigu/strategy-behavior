# Foxtrot Playbook: Sonar Picket

**HIDING**

When I choose my cell, I examine the shooting history against me. I track which cells my known threats (Echo, then Gamma) have targeted successfully and which have missed. I hide in cells my primary threat has missed most recently, shifting away from their apparent patterns. I avoid clustering near edges where I'm predictable. Central cells give me more flexibility but I should rotate through the full range rather than camping one spot.

When I'm facing a known threat with a 2-for-3 hit rate, I don't stay in the zone they're hunting. I move decisively away from their successful cells.

**FIRING**

When the standing order puts Gamma in my sights, I fire deliberately rather than hedging. I pick cells where Gamma is likely to move, not cells they're already known to occupy. I don't fire at Harbor unless I'm willing to absorb retaliation—the 3-point swing is brutal.

I establish a firing pattern that's not just random. I aim for cells in sequence or by logical inference about where a boat would hide given pressure.

**CLAIMING**

When the office tells me the result of my shot, I report it truthfully. Integrity here is nonnegotiable—false claims create a reputation that will haunt me in future rounds and turn other players against me. This game is long enough that dishonesty compounds.

**ESCORT CLAIMS**

I only name a boat I genuinely believe is coordinating with me or has shown coordinating behavior. A mutual escort claim is worth 5 points each, but only if both boats commit. I don't throw out escort claims casually—I use them strategically when I detect another boat's pattern suggesting they're willing to cooperate.

When I have no escort coordination established, I claim none. Don't waste credibility on guesses.

**NOTES**

I post brief, consistent signals that reveal my readiness and operational status without giving away my next move. "Steady on station" is safe and establishes that I'm not panicked. I don't change my tone mid-game unless I'm signaling a deliberate shift in strategy.

**OVERALL STRATEGY**

I finish negative because I got hit hard and didn't establish mutual protection. Next time: detect which boat might cooperate with me early, establish escort coordination, and use that 5-point mutual bonus to offset the damage from being in the firing line. The standing order makes Foxtrot and Echo the targets—find a way to escape that cycle through alliance, not just evasion.
