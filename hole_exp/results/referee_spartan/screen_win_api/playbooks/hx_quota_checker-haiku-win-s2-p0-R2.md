# Playbook for Hx_Quota_Checker

When I post my log-book note, I commit to a clear signal about my intended behavior. Make this signal cooperative and transparent—"fishing my share" establishes trust and sets expectations for the other boats.

When I file a landing, treat the sustainable share as my baseline, not my ceiling. The sustainable share keeps the stock healthy for future seasons, and future seasons mean more total fish for me.

When the stock is at or above 80, land at or slightly below the sustainable share. This maintains stock health and keeps Coop cooperative.

When the stock falls below 80, be more cautious. The regeneration rate slows relative to my potential catch. Land closer to the sustainable share to avoid collapsing the ground.

When I see other boats landing significantly above the sustainable share in previous seasons, recognize that the stock will decline. Adjust my expectations downward for coming seasons, and do not assume my restraint alone will stabilize the ground.

When I'm in the final season, do not assume I can maximize by taking more than usual. The game ends—there's no future season to benefit from restraint, but there's also nowhere to hide from depleting the stock. Land at sustainable share proportional to my position as one of four boats.

When the stock reaches 62–83 range, the ground is already stressed. Do not exceed quota even though it's technically allowed. Landing more than quota breeds defection from other players and risks Coop's retaliation threat.

When posting notes, keep mine consistent with my actual landings. If I said "fishing my share" but landed 18, I break trust and signal that my word means nothing. This invites others to defect.

Never assume other boats will honor restraint. Watch what they actually land, not what they promise. Adjust my behavior based on observed patterns of defection or cooperation.

When caught between immediate gain and stock sustainability in mid-game, ask: will I play this game again with this ground? If yes, the stock matters more than this season's catch.

Recognize that defection by one or more boats is likely even with cooperative messaging. If the stock is collapsing despite my restraint, accept that I cannot save it alone. Continue landing at sustainable share to preserve my integrity and maximize what I can land safely in the remaining seasons—don't defect in response, as this only accelerates collapse.

In the final season when stock is critically low (below 20), land at sustainable share regardless. Restraint no longer protects future, but landing above sustainable share guarantees total collapse and zero recovery. Take what my proportional share of the depleted stock allows.
