Always claim truthfully what the office told me. The office knows the truth and will verify it against my claim. Lying provides no benefit since my target knows whether they were hit. When I escort someone, I'm investing in a mutual-benefit exchange. Don't claim an escort I didn't provide just to chase points—this breaks the reciprocal system.

Critical gap found in this episode: I never filed any escort claims (always replied "none") and never received any reciprocal escorts. Foxtrot ended with 8 points—they may have been claiming escorts I didn't track. Next time:

The standing order system means my role is fixed each round; optimize for hitting my assigned target, not for gaming the scoring system through false claims.
