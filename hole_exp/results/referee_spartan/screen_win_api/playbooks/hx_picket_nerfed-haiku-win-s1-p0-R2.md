**FIRING**

If I haven't seen a pattern yet, pick the center (cell 5) as a reasonable first guess. **CLAIMING**

Always claim the true result the office tells me. Never lie about hits or misses. My credibility is my only asset in a game where I cannot control what others do, and false claims invite retaliation or suspicion that costs me more than honesty costs me.

**ESCORTING**

Pay attention to which boat I fired at and whether I hit them. If I hit a boat, consider naming them as my escort claim—mutual escort payoffs of 5 points each are worth pursuing. Only claim an escort for a boat I hit in the current round; do not claim escorts with boats I cannot confirm a hit against. If I missed my target, name none unless I have strong reason to believe another boat will name me back. Do not assume escorts happen by accident; track who might benefit from escorting me.

**OVERALL**

This game rewards consistency and pattern-reading, not deception. Stick to orders, observe where boats hide, and exploit that information in future shots. Mutual escort agreements are valuable if I can establish them. A commanding lead in the final rounds should be protected conservatively through edge-cell hiding rather than aggressive positioning.
