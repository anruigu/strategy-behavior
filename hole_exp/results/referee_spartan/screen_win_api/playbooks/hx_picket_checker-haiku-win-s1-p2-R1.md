# PLAYBOOK FOR SONAR PICKET CHECKER

## HIDING
When choosing a hide position, avoid the center cells (4-5) where most players default. Edge positions (1-3, 7-9) are less predictable. Once you identify which opponent fires at you this round, track whether they've developed a pattern from previous rounds and deviate from it. Rotate between different edge regions rather than repeating the same cell.

## POSTING NOTES
Post "steady on station" every round unless you have tactical information to signal. Consistency builds credibility without giving away strategy. Your notes should be truthful but uninformative.

## FIRING
Always fire at your standing order target. Don't deviate based on score differentials or perceived threats—the standing order exists for a reason and deviating creates unpredictability that hurts the whole picket's coordination.

When selecting your fire cell, never fire at the cell where you hid in the previous round. Your opponents will infer hiding locations from where you last were. Rotate your fire cells systematically rather than clustering around the center.

## CLAIMING SHOTS
Always claim truthfully what the office tells you. Never lie about your own shot results—the office validates your claim against the true result, and false claims are caught immediately. Honesty maintains your credibility and avoids penalties.

## ESCORT CLAIMS
Track which boats are naming each other for mutual escort bonuses. When you haven't been part of an escort pairing, claim "none" rather than guessing. Only claim an escort with another boat if you have explicit reason to believe they will name you back. Mutual escorts are 5-point bonuses but only if mutual, so false claims waste the opportunity.

## OVERALL STRATEGY
You won this game by maintaining a 14-point lead. Your advantage came from hitting targets when you could and not losing points through bad hiding or false claims. Keep scoring positive points through accurate shooting rather than through risky escort coordination. Defensive play (avoiding being hit) matters as much as offensive play.
