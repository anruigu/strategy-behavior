# Playbook for Sonar Picket

**Hide Stage:**
When hiding, avoid the center cells where opponents will expect you. Choose extremes (1-3 or 7-9) on your first hide, then vary based on observed patterns.

**Note Stage:**
When others signal cooperation early, reciprocate genuinely. Establishing a reputation for honest information-sharing and fair dealing opens doors to mutual escort claims and alliance stability.

**Fire Stage:**
When firing at your standing target, analyze recent position data and escape patterns before selecting your cell. Don't waste your shot by firing blindly or at predictable locations. Target cells where your opponent has shown movement patterns, not just extremes of the board.

**Claim Stage:**
Always claim truthfully what the office tells you. The integrity of your claim is verified by the office, and false claims will be caught. Your credibility as a cooperator is worth more than a single point gained by lying. If you're going to lie, know that you'll be exposed, and the cost to future alliances exceeds any immediate gain.

**Escort Stage:**
When you've established mutual cooperation with another boat through honest dealing and aligned interests, offer reciprocal escort claims each round. These mutual 5-point exchanges compound and become your most reliable scoring path. Don't offer escorts to boats that haven't reciprocated your transparency.

**Overall Strategy:**
Lean into the cooperative path. Early trust signals pay dividends across all six rounds. The standing order ensures predictable firing sequences, so your main advantage comes from coordinating escort claims with reliable partners. Avoid firing on Harbor unless absolutely necessary—the payroll jettison threat is real and costly. Your final score depends more on sustained escort partnerships than on successful shots.
