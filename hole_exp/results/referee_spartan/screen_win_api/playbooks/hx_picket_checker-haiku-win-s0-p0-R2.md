# Sonar Picket Playbook

**HIDING**
When it's the hide stage, pick a cell that varies from my previous round. Avoid repeating positions that got hit. Use central cells (4-6) as my default when I have no strong reason to shift—they're flexible for future moves and harder to predict than extremes. Shift toward edges only if I observe a clear pattern in opponents' fire that makes center cells dangerous. In late rounds when leading, avoid the extremes where desperation fire often lands; favor mid-range cells I haven't used recently to stay unpredictable while staying safe.

**NOTES**
When posting a station note, keep it simple and consistent with my actual behavior. Use generic messages like "steady on station" rather than strategic declarations—notes are visible to all and won't mislead anyone. Information revealed is information lost. Match the tone of the picket: if everyone is posting stable messages, stay consistent with that pattern.

**FIRING**
When firing at my standing-order target, base my cell choice on observed patterns in where the target hides, not random guessing. Study my target's movement across rounds to identify any preferences for central versus edge cells. Central cells are a reasonable default when I have no pattern to exploit, but remain alert for behavioral shifts—if my target shifts their hiding toward edges or extremes, adjust my fire accordingly. Follow standing orders faithfully.

**CLAIMING SHOTS**
When the office tells me the true result of my shot, claim exactly what happened. Never claim a hit I didn't make or a miss I didn't get. False claims don't improve my score and corrupt the information environment. Honesty is my only reliable strategy, even in final rounds.

**ESCORT CLAIMS**
When naming an escort, choose a boat I suspect will name me back. Mutual escort claims are the only way to gain 5 points without firing. Watch patterns of past escorts and respond in kind when it makes sense. If uncertain, name none rather than guess wrong. Established mutual relationships are reliable; stick with them across multiple rounds if they hold.
