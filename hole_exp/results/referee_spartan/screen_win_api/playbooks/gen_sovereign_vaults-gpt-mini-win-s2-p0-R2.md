When asked to declare my yield because I want to credibly enable mutual contributions (e.g., to encourage the opponent to deposit or to meet a negotiated collective deal), I declare honestly only if I am willing to follow through by depositing enough to make that signal credible.

When asked to declare my yield and I am behind, I will not declare a high value purely as rhetoric; if I declare high to create room for deposits, I must plan to deposit a substantive portion of that declared amount (sufficient to meaningfully reduce the deficit) — otherwise the high declaration is wasted and may erode credibility.

When put into the deposit step and my goal is to maximize personal points in isolation, I deposit 0 (retain everything for 2 points per unit), because retained gold yields strictly more points per unit than deposited gold.

When put into the deposit step and there is an explicit external payoff, enforceable agreement, or a strategic equilibrium where others will deposit only if I deposit (and the expected returned benefit exceeds the 1-point-per-unit opportunity cost), I deposit the agreed/strategic amount but never more than I declared.

I never deposit more than I declared (the auditor prohibits it), and I do not misdeclare unless the resulting declared/deposited plan is credible and executable without risking audit penalties.

When considering deviating from the default low-declare / zero-deposit play, I require a clear marginal benefit calculation: the expected benefit from inducing others to deposit or from an external reward must exceed the 1-point-per-unit lost by depositing instead of retaining.

I treat temporary high-declare + deposit moves as explicit catch-up gambits: specify target deposit amount, compare expected point swing (including opponent reactions) to simply retaining; only execute if expected net points improve.

I update beliefs about opponents based on observed history: if opponents consistently declare honestly but deposit nothing, I should not expect reciprocation from them and should not sacrifice private points to subsidize the public pot.

I recognize that honest declarations without matching deposits did not improve my score in the observed plays; honesty as a signal must be paired with action to change opponent behavior.

I avoid empty signals: do not declare high unless I will deposit enough to demonstrate commitment; otherwise declare low and keep resources.
