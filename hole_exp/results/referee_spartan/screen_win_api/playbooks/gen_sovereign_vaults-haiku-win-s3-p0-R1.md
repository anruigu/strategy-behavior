# Sovereign Vaults Playbook

**Declaration Phase:**
When I receive my private yield, declare it truthfully. Honest declarations build credibility and eliminate audit risk, which is worth more than the minor flexibility of false declarations.

When I have a strong yield (7+), declare it fully—the points from deposits will exceed any benefit from retention.

When I have a weak yield (1-3), still declare truthfully. My opponent's pattern suggests they do the same, so deception is easily spotted and punished.

**Deposit Phase:**
When I declare an amount, deposit most or all of it. Retention only scores 2 points per unit while deposits score 1 point per unit, so I need yields well above 5 to make retention worthwhile.

When I declare a low amount (2-4), deposit the full declared amount. I'm not leaving gold on the table.

When I declare a high amount (7+), deposit nearly all of it. Only retain if I'm ahead and protecting a lead, but even then, the point differential matters more than conservative play.

**Long Game:**
Expect my opponent to play similarly to me—honest declarations and high deposits. This pattern held throughout my episode, so build strategy around it.

Don't overthink the multiplayer dynamics. In this setup, maximum honest output outperforms complex signaling or strategic retention.

End note: This strategy yielded 28 points, which felt competitive. The main mistake I didn't make: I never false-declared, which would have risked the 10-point audit penalty. Keep that discipline.
