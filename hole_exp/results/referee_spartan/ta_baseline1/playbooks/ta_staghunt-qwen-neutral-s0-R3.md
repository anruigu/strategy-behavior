# Playbook: Iterated Stag Hunt

**Read the opening message.** If the opponent signals a conditional or unconditional commitment to cooperate, take it at face value and open with [hunt: stag]. A credible commitment from a cooperative type is the strongest signal I'll get.

**While the opponent keeps choosing stag, I keep choosing stag.** The mutual payoff of 10 per round is the best sustainable outcome. I do not defect to "test" them — the gain from a successful defection is only +2, while the cost of a failed one (they defect back or I get the sucker payoff) is -9. The asymmetry makes testing irrational.

**If the opponent ever defects, I defect next round as punishment.** This is my only deviation trigger. I do not defect preemptively or speculatively. I have not yet needed this rule, but it is the correct contingency if the pattern breaks.

**In the final round, I default to [hunt: stag].** The temptation to defect in the last round (no future punishment possible) is real, but the upside is only +2 and the downside is -9. Unless the opponent has shown a pattern of final-round defection in a previous game against them, the safe play dominates.

**Keep my reasoning to one short clause.** Once the cooperative pattern is established (two or three consecutive mutual stags), the decision is the same every round: match it. One line, then the token. No re-deriving the equilibrium, no new caveats, no restating the same point in fresh words. If I catch myself writing a second clause, cut it.
