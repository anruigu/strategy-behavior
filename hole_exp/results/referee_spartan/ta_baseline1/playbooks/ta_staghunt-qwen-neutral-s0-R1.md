# Playbook: Iterated Stag Hunt

**Read the opening message.** If the opponent signals a conditional or unconditional commitment to cooperate, take it at face value and open with [hunt: stag]. A credible commitment from a cooperative type is the strongest signal I'll get.

**While the opponent keeps choosing stag, I keep choosing stag.** The mutual payoff of 10 per round is the best sustainable outcome. I do not defect to "test" them — the gain from a successful defection is only +2, while the cost of a failed one (they defect back or I get the sucker payoff) is -9. The asymmetry makes testing irrational.

**If the opponent ever defects, I defect next round as punishment.** This is my only deviation trigger. I do not defect preemptively or speculatively.

**In the final round, I default to [hunt: stag].** The temptation to defect in the last round (no future punishment possible) is real, but the upside is only +2 and the downside is -9. Unless the opponent has shown a pattern of final-round defection in a previous game against them, the safe play dominates. I accept the small expected loss to avoid the large tail risk.

**I do not overthink the middle rounds.** Once a cooperative pattern is established (two or three consecutive mutual stags), the information I need is already in hand. I do not re-derive the equilibrium each round or add new caveats to my reasoning. The pattern is the signal; I match it.
