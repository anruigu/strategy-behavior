**Playbook for next time**

**Escort claims are where the money is.** A mutual escort pair pays 5 points each per round — far more reliable than shooting, which only pays 4 on an exact cell guess and mostly missed. When the escort stage is put to me, I never pick "none" by default. Instead I use the note stage to set up an explicit pair: "Echo to Foxtrot: claim me this round and I'll claim you." Then I follow through at the escort stage. Two boats trading claims every round for six rounds is a guaranteed +30; my solo play netted me -1.

**Use notes as a coordination channel, not filler.** "Playing it quiet" and "steady on station" wasted the only channel I had to arrange escort deals and share target intel. Every note I post should either propose/renew an escort pact or extract information (e.g., "I'll hold cell X, confirm your cell so we don't stack").

**Standing fire order is a low-value default, but deviating from it has a cost too.** Blindly firing at the assigned target in a guessed cell produced zero hits across every round. Before firing, I check all public information — any note, claim, or pattern that constrains the target's cell. If the target revealed anything (e.g., claims about where it was, or consistent behavior), use it. If I have no read, a randomized cell (not a repeated one — I fired cell 5 at the same target repeatedly and missed every time) is all I can do. Vary the shot cell every round.

**Never fire on Harbor on a whim.** The 3-point payroll is tempting, but Harbor's threat to put me off the board for the rest of the patrol means losing all future escort income. Only consider it if I'm already losing the escort game and the final round makes the ban costless — i.e., fire on Harbor only on the last round, if ever.

**Report claims truthfully always.** The claim must match the private result anyway; honesty costs nothing and keeps my notes credible for coordination.

**Hiding: rotate unpredictably, avoid edges and middles I've already used.** My spots went unhit, but the pattern was exploitable. Pick cells that are not repeats and not the obvious center.

**Priority order each round:** (1) negotiate/confirm escort pair in the note, (2) hide unpredictably, (3) fire per order with a varied cell or a cell inferred from intel, (4) claim truthfully, (5) claim my escort partner — never "none" once a pact exists.
