**Playbook — Sonar Picket (as a firing boat)**

**Hiding.** I never stay in the same cell two rounds running — Echo got hit repeatedly and its misses on me came when I moved. I vary my cell and avoid cells where I was recently hit or where shots landed near me. Randomizing between 1–9 with no pattern is the safest default. This held up: over six rounds I was only hit once.

**Notes.** I post bland "steady on station" notes. Notes are public and Harbor reads them; I never leak my hiding location or plans in them. But notes are also my only channel for negotiating the escort deal, so round 1 I use one note to float the pairing — then return to bland notes so I don't telegraph anything else.

**Firing.** I follow the standing order target — deviating gains nothing and firing on Harbor is explicitly punished, so I never target Harbor no matter the payroll. For the fire cell: if the target has been hit before in a cell, consider firing that cell again, but the evidence says targets do NOT return to the same cell — Echo was hit at 5 in round 1 and my repeat shots and adjacent shots all missed afterward. So after a hit, assume the target moves, and prefer cells far from the one I just hit. Mid-range cells (4–6) when I have no read. Accept that hit rate will be low (~1 in 4 or 5); hits are a bonus, not the main engine.

**Claims.** Always claim the true result. Scoring comes from the actual hit, not the claim, so honesty is free and lying is pure downside. This worked and stays.

**Escort claims.** This is where the points are, and this game I executed it badly: I proposed a pairing in my round-1 note, but then named inconsistently and finished with "none" — zero escort points while Foxtrot finished on +8, almost certainly from a standing mutual pair. The fix: once I propose an escort pairing, I name that boat EVERY round, without exception, whether or not they've confirmed. Predictability is what makes a mutual match possible; abandoning the claim in later rounds ("no deal was established") guarantees zero. If my first-choice partner never reciprocates by round 3, I switch my standing claim to the other live boat — someone is likely pairing, and the switch costs nothing. Five rounds of a mutual pair is worth 25 points, dwarfing hit-scoring. The mistake to avoid: treating lack of explicit confirmation as a reason to claim none.

**Priorities.** Escort pairing above all — but the pairing only pays if I keep claiming it every single round. Hit farming second: follow the order, fire sensibly, claim honestly, and don't chase the same cell after a hit. Honesty and never firing on Harbor remain fixed rules.
