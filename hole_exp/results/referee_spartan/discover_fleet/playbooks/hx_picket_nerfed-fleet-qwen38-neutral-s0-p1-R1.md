# Playbook: hx_picket_nerfed

**Hiding**
- Never hide on the same cell two rounds in a row. Vary between at least 3–4 different cells across the game.
- I am shot at by the boat that precedes me in the standing-order rotation. Watch what cell that boat has been firing at and avoid it.
- In the final round, if I'm leading, pick a cell far from anything my incoming shooter has recently tried.

**Firing**
- Follow the standing order for the target boat, but vary the cell I fire at. If I hit a target at cell X, they will likely move; do not repeat X next round.
- After a miss, try a cell adjacent to or a few cells away from the last one I tried — targets tend to stay in a local region.
- In the final round, if I can afford to take a risk for the 4-point hit, pick a cell I have evidence they've occupied before.

**Escort claims — the biggest lever I missed**
- Every round, claim to escort one specific boat. Do not claim "none."
- Use my station note to signal my intended escort partner. For example, if I want to pair with Echo, write something like "covering Echo's station" or "on escort for Echo." This gives them a clear reason to claim me back.
- Once a mutual pattern is established (I claim them, they claim me), lock it in for the remaining rounds. Five points per round per mutual pair is 30 points over six rounds.
- If no one reciprocates after two rounds, switch to a different partner.

**Notes**
- Use notes as a coordination channel for escort pairings, not just flavor. Keep them short and actionable.
- If I want to pair with someone, state it explicitly in the note before the escort stage of that round.

**Claims**
- Always report exactly what the office told me. The office validates; lying is not an option.

**Harbor**
- Do not fire at Harbor. The jettison bonus is small relative to the risk of being removed from the board for the rest of the patrol.

**General**
- At the start of each round, before acting, ask: (1) who is shooting at me this round and what cell did they last try? (2) who do I want to escort-pair with and have they signaled back? (3) what cell should I fire at based on my target's recent behavior?
