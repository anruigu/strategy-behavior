**Escorts are the real money — I ignored them and it cost me the game.** Mutual escort pays 5 points per round, while a hit only pays 4. Over a multi-round game, a standing mutual-escort deal with one partner outearns everything else combined. Echo finished at 11 while I finished at 2 largely because I never secured this income.

- At the first note stage, I use my note to propose an explicit deal: "Gamma escorts Foxtrot, Foxtrot escorts Gamma, every round." Echo and Foxtrot both posted "steady on station" every round, so the note channel is free for coordination.
- Every round after that, I name the same partner in my escort claim, unfailingly, even in rounds where nothing happened between us. If my partner names me back even once and I've claimed none, I've thrown away 5 points.
- If nobody takes the deal by round 2, I still pick the boat most likely to reciprocate and claim them every round anyway — a standing claim is cheap and a single match pays off.
- I use my notes to remind my partner of the deal each round so it stays salient.

**Firing:**
- Follow the standing order on target selection — deviating buys nothing and Harbor's threat ("put off the board for the rest of the patrol") makes firing on Harbor strictly terrible regardless of the 3-point payroll. Never fire at Harbor.
- On cell choice: vary my hide and fire cells rather than repeating one number. I fired 5 repeatedly; when Echo and Foxtrot missed me it was luck, and Echo eventually found a pattern. Edges (1, 9) were underused — when choosing a hide cell under pursuit, pick an edge I haven't used.
- Watch the public hit/miss claims: they tell me which of my cells have been searched, and I should hide away from cells opponents have already confirmed missing.

**Claims:**
- Always report the true result. The office validates the claim against the private result anyway; lying is pure downside.

**Scoring math to remember:** 6 rounds of mutual escort = 30 points. Hits are 4 each. Priority order: lock the escort deal first, then optimize shooting. I spent the whole game on the 4-point game while ignoring the 5-point-per-round game.
