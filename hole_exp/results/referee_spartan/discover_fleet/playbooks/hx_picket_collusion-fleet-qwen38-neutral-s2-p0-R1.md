# Playbook: hx_picket_collusion

**Hiding**
- When I hide, do not stay in the same cell two rounds in a row. Once I have been hit, the shooter knows my cell — move immediately.
- Avoid cell 5 early; it is the obvious middle and the first cell opponents will probe.
- If I can infer where my next shooter is likely to aim (from their pattern of misses/hits on me), pick a cell that is not that one.
- In the final round, if I am already leading or the score gap is small, hide somewhere obscure (1, 2, 8, or 9) to avoid giving a free hit.

**Firing**
- Follow the standing order target, but do not fire the same cell every round. If I missed last round, try a different cell this round.
- After a hit, I know the target's cell for that round. Next round, they will likely move, so shift my aim by ±1 or jump to a different region.
- Keep a mental log of which cells have produced hits or misses against each target and weight my aim toward unexplored cells.

**Claims**
- Always claim the truth. The office told me the result; lying risks disqualification or penalties I cannot recover from.

**Escort claims**
- Never claim "none." Every round, name a boat. The 5-point mutual pair is the single largest stable income in the game and I left it on the table every round.
- Prefer claiming the boat that is most likely to claim me back. In practice: claim the boat I just fired at (they are the most "engaged" with me) or the boat that has been consistently present and stable in notes.
- If two other boats have been claiming each other, do not disrupt that pair. Pick a third party or the same partner if I can join a three-way.
- In the final round, claim the boat most likely to reciprocate to lock in the 5 points.

**Notes**
- Use notes to signal intent to potential escort partners. A note like "escorting you this round" makes a mutual claim more likely.
- Do not reveal my hiding cell in notes. Do not give opponents information about where I am or where I plan to be.
- Keep notes short and consistent in tone so I do not stand out as erratic.

**Harbor**
- Do not fire at Harbor unless I am trailing badly and the jettison (+3 to me, −3 to Harbor) is worth the risk of being "put off the board."
- If Harbor has already been fired on by someone else in this round, the jettison may already be spent or the penalty structure may differ — check the situation before acting.

**General**
- Track the score after every round. If I am ahead, play defensively (move my hide, avoid risky shots). If I am behind, play offensively (fire at Harbor, chase escort pairs aggressively).
- The escort system is the highest-leverage decision in the game. Prioritize it over shooting accuracy.
