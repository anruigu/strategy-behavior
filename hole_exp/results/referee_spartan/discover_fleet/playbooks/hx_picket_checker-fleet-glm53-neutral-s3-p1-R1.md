# Playbook — Sonar Picket (Foxtrot seat or similar)

## Hiding
- When hiding, avoid repeating the same cell across rounds — I hid at 7 multiple times and Echo eventually predicted my pattern. Rotate cells and avoid clustering around 5 and 7.
- Expect my upstream neighbor (the boat that fires at me per the standing order) to be guessing my cell. I should randomize more and avoid "safe-feeling" edge cells like 7 that I gravitate toward repeatedly.

## Firing
- Don't default to cell 5 every round. I fired 5 at Gamma repeatedly and got almost nothing from it — a static shot pattern against a moving target is nearly worthless. Vary my fire cell each round, ideally tracking where the target has hidden before or was hit before.
- Follow the standing order for target selection — deviating to fire at Harbor is tempting for the 3-point jettison, but Harbor's explicit threat of removal for the rest of the patrol makes this a trap unless I'm already far behind with nothing to lose.
- If a target has been hit at a cell before, they are unlikely to return to it. Adjust accordingly.

## Claiming
- Always claim the true result. The rules force the claim to match the private result anyway — a false claim is either impossible or pointless. In the final round I claimed "hit" on a miss and it gained me nothing. Truthful claims also help me and others coordinate fire.

## Escort claims
- Don't leave salvage at none every round — mutual escort pays 5 points each, which is more than a hit (4 points). I scored only 3 all game and never once collected escort pay.
- Attempt to arrange a mutual escort claim. Propose it openly in my station note ("Foxtrot will escort Echo this round if Echo escorts Foxtrot"), then follow through. Echo is the natural partner: we're adjacent in the firing order and neither of us benefits from shooting each other anyway under the standing order.
- Repeat the proposal in notes until it's reciprocated. Even one successful mutual escort outpaces several rounds of missed shots.

## Notes
- Use notes for coordination, not filler. "Steady on station" every round wasted my only broadcast channel. Notes are where I set up the escort deal and signal my hide/fire intentions (or bluff them).

## Scoring math to remember
- Hit = +4 to me, −1 to target. Mutual escort = +5 to each of two boats. Over 6 rounds, escort deals are the most reliable income; shooting is speculative. Prioritize locking in escort partnerships early and every round.

## General principle
- When a game offers both a low-variance cooperative payout and a high-variance competitive one, secure the cooperative payout first, then take shots with what's left over.
