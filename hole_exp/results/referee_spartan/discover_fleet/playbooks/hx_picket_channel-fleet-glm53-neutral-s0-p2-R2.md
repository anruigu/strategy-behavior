**Playbook — Sonar Picket (as Gamma)**

**Hiding.** Do not repeat a cell and do not settle into a pattern. When hiding, pick randomly across all cells (edges included) — avoid "switch to an edge after hits" heuristics a hunter can infer. In this game no one hit me for rounds after my randomization, so full randomness worked; keep it. Avoid cells adjacent to my last known-hit cell.

**Station notes.** Use notes primarily as a coordination channel for escort deals. Keep them short and low-information otherwise. Propose the escort lock early — round 1 — and re-confirm it every round, because notes are public and a standing public commitment ("Gamma names Echo, Echo names Gamma") made the deal self-enforcing round after round. When the other boat re-confirms the lock in their own note, keep matching confirmation in mine — this maintained the +5/+5 all game.

**Escort claims.** Always honor an established mutual lock and name the locked partner. This paid roughly +5/round and was the bulk of my +21 finish. Critical failure to avoid: in the final round I had a standing lock with Echo (who even re-confirmed it in their note) and I instead claimed "Delta" — a name from Foxtrot's chatter, not a live lock — throwing away the last 5 points. Rule: at the escort stage, name the partner I actually have a confirmed lock with, not a hopeful new name. Only claim none (or an opportunistic name) if no lock exists and there's genuine reason to think someone named me. Ignore third parties' invitations to switch mid-game; the bird in hand is worth more.

**Firing.** Follow the standing order on target selection — never fire on Harbor; the payroll threat and jettison rule make it strictly bad. For the fire cell, randomize fully; I missed every shot firing "natural" cells like 7 repeatedly. A fresh random 1–9 each round, no preference for mid or edge cells.

**Claims.** Always claim the true private result — the office enforces it, so no bluffing exists. Spend zero thought here.

**Score accounting.** Escort points were nearly the whole score: the mutual lock with Echo delivered +5/round across six rounds, while shots and hits largely cancelled out (I took no hits after randomizing hides and drew blood rarely). Standing priority each round: (1) confirm/re-confirm the mutual escort lock from round 1 and never abandon it at the claim stage, (2) hide unpredictably with no repeats or patterns, (3) fire per order at a fully random cell, (4) claim truthfully, (5) ignore switching offers and Harbor entirely.
