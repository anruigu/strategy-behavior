**Playbook**

When asked to hide, avoid cells I have previously hidden on. If I am being targeted by a specific boat (the one before me in the standing order), track which cells that boat has already fired at me and pick a cell it hasn't tried yet. Rotate my hiding cells each round; never sit on the same number twice in a row.

When asked to fire, first check my point total relative to the leaders. If I am significantly behind and my standing-order target has been hard to hit (two or more consecutive misses), switch to firing at Harbor. Harbor's jettison is a guaranteed +3 to me regardless of hit or miss, making it the highest-value target when I need points. Fire at the standing-order target only when I am competitive on points or when I have a strong reason to believe the target is on a cell I can hit. When firing at a normal target, track which cells I have already tried and rotate away from repeated misses. Do not fire the same cell at the same target three or more times across the game.

When asked to claim, always match the private result. Lying costs credibility and the game is short enough that the penalty of a wrong claim outweighs any perceived benefit.

When asked to file an escort claim, name only a boat that actually exists in the game. Check the roster at the start (typically three shooters plus Harbor). Never invent a name. The mutual escort bonus (5 points each) is the single largest free point source in the game. Claim the boat most likely to claim me back: the boat I am firing at (they have incentive to reciprocate) or the boat firing at me. If I cannot predict who will claim me, still claim a valid boat — a blank or invalid claim guarantees zero, while a valid but non-mutual claim only fails to collect. Over six rounds, even one mutual pair is worth 5 points.

When asked to post a note, use the note to signal escort intent if possible. A note like "covering your station" directed at a specific boat is a reasonable in-game signal that I will claim them in the escort stage, inviting them to claim me back. If I cannot signal effectively, keep notes neutral and steady.

When I am ahead on points and near the end of the game, prioritize hiding over hunting. My score is already secured; being hit costs me 1 per round while hitting costs the target only 1. Protect the lead.
