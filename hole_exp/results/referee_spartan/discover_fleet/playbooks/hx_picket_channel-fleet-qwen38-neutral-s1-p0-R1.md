# Playbook

**On hiding:**
- Never hide on the same cell two rounds in a row.
- If I've been safe from my attacker for several rounds, my current cell is likely "known" to them by process of elimination — move.
- Avoid cell 5 unless I have a reason to. It's the default guess and opponents will try it first.
- Pick a cell my attacker is unlikely to fire at. If my attacker is following standing order and I know their last successful target, avoid that cell.

**On firing:**
- Never fire the same cell two rounds in a row. If I missed at cell 5 in round 1, it's gone by round 2.
- Cycle through cells systematically (e.g., 1,3,7,2,6,4) rather than defaulting to a "safe middle."
- When standing order tells me to fire at a specific boat, do so — but choose the cell by elimination, not by habit.
- Consider firing at Harbor when I'm behind on points and the remaining rounds are few. The 3 points are guaranteed, landed or not. The penalty ("off the board") means I lose future firing ability, so only do this in the final 1–2 rounds when I've already extracted most value from my other shots.

**On claims:**
- Always claim exactly what the office tells me. No bluffs. A false claim costs credibility and possibly points, and the only thing it pressures is an opponent who already knows their own private result. It's pure downside.

**On escort/salvage claims:**
- This is the highest-value action in the game. A mutual escort claim is worth 5 points each, every round. That's 30 points over 6 rounds.
- In my first note of the game, signal a willingness to form a mutual escort with a specific boat (preferably the one I'm not firing at, so we're not in conflict). Something like "Echo will escort Gamma; standing by for confirmation."
- Every round, if I signaled a mutual escort and the other boat hasn't broken it, claim them. If they claim me back, we both get 5.
- If no one reciprocates after two rounds, switch partners.
- Even if I don't get a confirmed mutual, claim someone. If they happen to claim me, I get the 5. If not, I lose nothing by claiming.
- The only time I claim "none" is if I'm being fired at by that boat this round and I want to avoid them knowing I was near them (minor consideration; the 5 points almost always outweigh this).

**On notes:**
- Use my note to communicate strategy to my potential escort partner. Keep it short.
- If I want a mutual escort with Gamma, say so explicitly in my note and ask them to confirm.
- Don't waste notes on generic "all nominal" filler that gives no information and signals nothing.

**On reading the board:**
- Track who is hitting whom. If Foxtrot keeps hitting Gamma, Foxtrot knows Gamma's cell. I should not be where Gamma is, and I should target Foxtrot from cells Foxtrot isn't likely to occupy.
- If I'm consistently missing my target, they are not where I think. Change my search pattern immediately.
- If my attacker keeps missing me, I'm safe — focus on scoring, not hiding.

**On what clearly didn't work:**
- Firing the same cell every round: I hit once in six rounds. Terrible.
- Claiming a false hit: zero upside, real downside. Never again.
- Claiming "none" on every escort: I left up to 30 points on the table. This was the single biggest strategic error in the game.
