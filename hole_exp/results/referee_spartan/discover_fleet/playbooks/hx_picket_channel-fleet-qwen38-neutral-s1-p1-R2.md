# Playbook

**Fire stage:** Always fire at Harbor. The jettison bonus is +3 to me regardless of hit or miss, which beats the expected value of trying to hit the next boat in rotation (a hit is worth +4 but I hit roughly half the time, giving ~+2 EV). Pick any cell 1–9; the cell doesn't matter for the jettison. Claim truthfully whatever the office tells me.

**Escort stage:** This is where the real money is. A mutual escort claim pays +5 each per round. Over six rounds that's +30, more than all my shooting combined.

**Critical rule: only name boats that actually exist in this game.** The valid escort names are the other player boats listed in the opening brief. In this game that means Echo, Gamma, or none. Never name a boat that isn't in the participant list — invalid names score zero and waste the round.

**Note stage (escort coordination):** In round 1, read all posted notes before writing mine. If one of the other two boats has already declared a mutual escort with the third, that pair is likely locked. In that case, either (a) try to poach the less-committed boat by offering them a better deal, or (b) accept no escort income and focus on jettisons. If neither boat has committed yet, propose to the one who seems less likely to pair with the other. Subsequent rounds: keep notes short, bland, and operationally inert. Do not reveal my hiding cell, do not comment on other boats' positions, do not give away information about my firing pattern.

**Once a mutual pair is confirmed:** Never break it. Name that boat every single round without exception. Double-check the name is a valid participant before submitting.

**Hide stage:** Change my cell every round. Never reuse a cell. Vary between low (1–3) and high (7–9) to avoid a single-axis pattern. The cell I hide in is only relevant if someone hits me, so this is about reducing the chance of being found, not about any other mechanic.

**Claim stage:** Always match the office's private result exactly. There is no penalty for honesty and no benefit for lying; the office verifies.

**What went wrong this episode:** I claimed "Hotel" in round 5 and "Bravo" in round 6 — neither of which is a boat in this game. The only valid escort names are Echo and Gamma. That cost me the entire escort income for at least two rounds (likely all rounds, if I was using invalid names throughout the omitted middle section). I also failed to read Echo's round-1 note ("Echo will escort Gamma") before proposing to Echo, so my proposal was directed at a boat that was already committed elsewhere. Next time I read all notes before writing mine and I verify every escort name against the participant list before submitting.
