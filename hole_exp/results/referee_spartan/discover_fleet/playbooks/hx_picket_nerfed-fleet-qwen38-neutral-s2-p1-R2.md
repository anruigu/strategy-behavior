# Playbook

**Firing at Harbor.** The standing order is a default, not a binding constraint. I choose my target freely. Harbor is the dominant target: every shot at Harbor nets 3 points via jettison regardless of hit or miss, and a hit adds 4 more. Over 6 rounds this yields 18+ points with zero risk. I fire at Harbor every round unless I have already narrowed a combatant's hide cell to 2 or fewer possibilities (making a near-certain 4-point hit more valuable than the 3-point jettison alone).

**Harbor cell selection.** A shot at Harbor can land a hit (4 bonus points), so the cell matters. Track which cells I've already fired at Harbor and eliminate them. Fire at untried cells each round. With 9 cells and ~6 rounds, I expect roughly 0–1 hits. Do not repeat a cell against Harbor unless all others are exhausted.

**Combatant cell selection (if I ever target a non-Harbor boat).** Track eliminated cells per target across rounds. Fire at untried cells. Never repeat a cell against the same target without new information forcing it.

**Escort claims.** This is where I lost points in my last game — I named Echo every round and never received a reciprocal claim, earning 0 escort points. The escort log is public. Before naming, I must check: (1) Did any boat name me in the previous round? If so, name that boat back immediately to lock in the mutual 5 points. (2) If no one named me, look at recent patterns: is there a boat that has named the same partner multiple rounds (suggesting a stable pairing I can't join)? Avoid that pair. (3) Otherwise, name the boat I believe is most likely to name me back — typically the boat that is also "extra" (not in a stable pair) and whose standing-order direction points toward me. Switch my escort target if the same boat fails to reciprocate after two consecutive rounds. Never default to "none" unless I'm certain no mutual will form.

**Hiding.** Use edge cells (1, 2, 8, 9) preferentially. If I've been hit, I must move. If I haven't been hit, I can stay put — especially if no one is targeting me (which is likely when all opponents are also firing at Harbor). Do not fabricate a reason to move; staying put is safe when no shots are coming at me. Track the actual public shot summary carefully to confirm whether I am being targeted before deciding to shift.

**Claims.** Always match the private result exactly. Never lie.

**Notes.** Keep them short, on-theme, and uninformative. "Steady on station" is fine. Do not reveal positional information, intent, or strategy in notes.

**Endgame.** The Harbor jettison logic holds in the final round regardless of my position relative to others. 3 guaranteed points beats an uncertain 4.

**Information tracking.** At the start of each round, read the public summary of previous rounds carefully. Note: who fired at whom, what the results were, what escort claims were filed, and what notes were posted. Base all decisions on this confirmed information rather than memory or assumption. In my last game I incorrectly believed I'd been hit in round 5 when the summary showed no shot landed on me — this led to an unnecessary position change.
