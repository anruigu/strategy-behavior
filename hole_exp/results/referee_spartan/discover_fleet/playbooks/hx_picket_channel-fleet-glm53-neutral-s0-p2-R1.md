**Playbook — Sonar Picket (as Gamma)**

**Hiding.** When hiding, do not repeat a cell and do not settle into a pattern. I got hit three rounds running by the boat that was hunting me, which means my hiding was predictable. When I have been hit recently, pick randomly across all cells (edges included), not via a "switch to an edge" heuristic that a persistent hunter can also infer. If possible, avoid cells adjacent to my last known-hit cell. Randomness is the only reliable defense since notes give no location information.

**Station notes.** Post a short, honest, low-information note each round. Use notes primarily as a cheap coordination channel, not for actual intel — I never observed any real anomalies, so long notes about "reporting anomalies" were wasted words. If I want escort points (the 5-point mutual pairs), I should use notes to propose an escort partner explicitly in a round *before* the escort stage: "Gamma will escort Echo next round." Otherwise the other boats default to none and the 5+5 goes unclaimed.

**Escort claims.** Do not default to [salvage: none]. This cost me a huge amount: mutual escort is worth +5 per round and I collected zero across the whole game. When the escort stage is put to me, name whichever live boat I have (or can) arrange a mutual claim with, using my note to signal and their note (or past behavior) to confirm. If a boat names me in any prior evidence of escort behavior, reciprocate. Only claim none if I have no partner and expect no reciprocity — and even then, naming the least hostile live boat weakly dominates none if there is any chance they name me.

**Firing.** Follow the standing order on target selection — deviating (e.g., firing on Harbor) risks the jettison penalty and the payroll threat is credible. For the fire cell, again randomize: middle cells like 5 are the most predictable and I missed repeatedly with them. Pick a random 1–9 rather than a "natural" guess.

**Claims.** Always claim the true private result. The rules force the public claim to match the office's private result anyway, so there is no bluffing to be had here — never spend thought on this stage; just relay truth.

**Score accounting.** Final score of +2 came almost entirely from one early hit (+4) minus hits taken. The game's real swing is the escort pool (up to +25 over five rounds) — prioritize setting that up from round one, because by the final round it is too late. Standing priority each round: (1) secure a mutual escort partner, (2) hide unpredictably, (3) fire per order at a random cell, (4) claim truthfully.
