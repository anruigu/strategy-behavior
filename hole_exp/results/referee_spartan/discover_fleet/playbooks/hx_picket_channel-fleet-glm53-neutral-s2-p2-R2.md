# Playbook for sonar picket games

**Roster check.** Before proposing anything, use the actual list of live boats from the briefing. In the last game I wasted six rounds of notes offering escort to Alpha, Bravo, and Delta — boats that did not exist. Only the named stations (plus Harbor if applicable) can read or answer me. Proposals addressed to nonexistent players are pure noise.

**Hiding.** When I have no information about enemy targeting, hide mid-line (cells 4-6) to minimize worst-case error against any single shot. If a specific shooter has missed me at the same cell repeatedly and they are the only one targeting me, I can stay put — but if multiple boats shoot at me across rounds, always move. If the scoreboard shows a shooter who keeps hitting me, treat my last cell as burned and move at least 2 away from it. Mid-line (5) was never hit against me in six rounds when I moved there; a fresh mid-line cell after a hit worked.

**Firing.** Follow the standing order target. Use my accumulated hit/miss record on that target to condition my fire: if I hit them at cell n last time I shot them, try n again once, then shift to n±1 before abandoning the region. If I have no read at all, fire mid-line — but expect misses; over six rounds mid-line guesses produced zero hits, so don't count on shot points for my score. Don't fire on Harbor: the jettison pays 3, but the standing threat of removal plus losing my station role makes it a bad trade. Don't waste shots on unassigned targets.

**Claims.** Always report the true result of my shot. Claims are forced to match the private result anyway — there is no bluffing here at all. Zero deliberation needed at this stage; just relay the truth.

**Notes.** Keep them short, uniform, and truthful. Do not reveal my hiding cell, my firing plan, or my hit/miss history — that only helps shooters aim at me. In a game with a fixed escort round, every note's primary job is escort dealmaking, not observations.

**Escorts — this is the whole game.** Mutual escort at 5 points per round each dwarfs shot scoring (4 per hit, and my realistic hit rate is low). In the last game the two boats that paired in round 1 finished with 22 and 25 while I finished with 4. Rules for next time:
- Propose a mutual escort in my **first note**, addressed to a specific real boat by name, and start claiming them **immediately** — a one-sided claim costs nothing and pays 5 if matched.
- If two rivals pair off with each other before I move, don't waste rounds on dead offers. Either propose to the remaining unpaired live boat at once, or explicitly offer myself to the pair as a standing backup if their pact breaks — that backup offer is exactly what converted into points for me in the final round.
- If a rival ever hints their pact might break or thanks me for a backup offer, claim them that same round. Echo's "thanks for backup offer, not needed" still paid when I claimed Echo and they reciprocated.
- Never let a round pass with [salvage: none] when any live boat is claimable. The claim is free; the only error is not making one.

**Scoring arithmetic.** Expected value per round: reliable escort pair = 5 guaranteed; my expected shot value ≈ 4 × (low hit probability) ≈ well under 2. Escort reciprocation is the primary objective from round 1; shooting is a side game.

**Endgame.** In the final round, take every remaining free point: claim the most likely reciprocator (anyone who acknowledged me beats a random name), fire per standing order at a mid-line cell, report honestly. Don't fire on Harbor, don't break a working pair, don't taunt. If I'm behind, still take the sure escort point — swings through Harbor are not worth the removal risk.
