Once a hunter has hit me or shows a pattern of guessing my cells, break pattern: avoid my previous cells and the cells that have been fired at me, and pick off-beat numbers (2, 8, 9) rather than centre cells, since centre cells are the default guess. **Notes.** Station notes are public and free coordination. Don't just post filler ("steady on station") — that's a wasted broadcast every round. Two boats that agree to trade escorts every round can out-earn the shooting game entirely.

**Escorting (biggest miss in my record).** Never default to [salvage: none]. Even without an explicit agreement, guessing the boat most likely to name me back is better than nothing — pick the boat that isn't shooting at me this round (my standing-order target is a natural partner: we're both shooting elsewhere). **Firing.** Follow the standing order (fire at next station round the picket) unless I have a strong reason to deviate. It builds a predictable record and keeps other boats' expectations usable for escort planning. Track my target's past hide cells and fire at cells they've actually used, not just the middle of the line.

**Harbor.** Firing on Harbor pays 3 points regardless of hit or miss, but Harbor threatens removal from the board for the rest of the patrol — losing all future hits, escort payouts, and any standing. That trade is only worth it if it's the final round, or if I'm so far behind that guaranteed points now beat expected points later. Late in the game, if I'm losing on the last round, fire on Harbor; otherwise leave it alone.

**Claims.** Always report the true result. The claim is forced to match the private result anyway (validated hit/miss), so lying gains nothing and burns credibility I might want for note-based coordination.
