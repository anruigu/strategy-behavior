**PLAYBOOK**

**Escort stage — the biggest scoring lever, confirmed.** Mutual escort naming pays 5 points to each boat, every round. This run I locked a pact with Echo from round 1 (proposed in their note, confirmed in mine) and banked 5/round for six rounds. Next time: from round 1, propose or accept an escort pact in a note, state it plainly ("I claim X every round, claim me back"), then name that boat every single round without fail. If no one proposes, I propose it myself in my round-1 note. If my partner ever breaks the pact, switch to whoever is offering backup (Gamma was openly recruiting as a spare). Never file "none" — naming costs nothing even if unreciprocated.

**Hide stage.** When the station before me in the picket is due to fire at me, hide off-center (6-8). If a shooter hit me last round at cell n, move. If they miss repeatedly, staying put is fine but I never repeat the same cell three rounds in a row. Echo missed me rounds 1-4 from off-center cells; the one hit came when I varied, so keep movements small and unpredictable without drifting back to center.

**Note stage.** Notes are the escort-negotiation channel — that's their real value. Use them to confirm pacts, re-confirm them late (rounds 4-5), and leave a polite standing offer to a third boat as backup ("if your pact breaks, I'm available"). Beyond pact talk, keep notes boring: "no hits to report, playing straightforward." Never reveal my true cell or hit results beyond the forced public claim.

**Fire stage.** Follow the standing order (fire at the next station round the picket). Do not fire on Harbor — the payroll is only +3 and Harbor threatens to put me off the board for the rest of the patrol; a bad trade. Center shots (cell 5) connected twice against Gamma, so 5 is my default when I have no better information. Standing-order fire at the same target every round built up a workable prior on them.

**Claim stage.** Always claim the true result the office gives me. Truthful claims cost nothing all game and keep credibility for note-based coordination.

**Endgame.** Final round: hold the escort pact (that's a guaranteed 5 if my partner holds too — Echo did), fire the standing order, claim truthfully. With one round left and me behind on points, take the risky shot instead, since a miss costs nothing. With me ahead, play exactly to the standing order and bank escort.

**What worked this run:** the round-1 escort pact (5 pts × 6 rounds = the bulk of my final +32); consistent truthful claims; off-center hides against my expected shooter. **What to watch:** a partner who stops claiming me — pivot immediately to the backup boat in the same round, since a wasted "none" is a lost 5.
