# Playbook

**Escort coordination is the highest-value action available to me.** When the escort stage comes, I name the boat I most want to form a mutual pair with. I do not default to "none." I use my station notes to signal intent: "mutual next round?" or "I'll name you round N." If a note from another boat invites mutual escort, I name them and they name me, and we each collect 5. This is worth more per successful action than a hit (5 vs 4) and far easier to achieve. Over six rounds, even two mutual pairs give me 10 points — more than I earned from all my hits combined.

**When the fire stage comes and I have no strong reason to target a specific boat, I fire at Harbor.** The jettison gives me a guaranteed +3 regardless of outcome. I do not need to guess a cell. I do not risk a miss for 0. I can still file an escort claim in the same round. The only reason to deviate is if I have tracked a specific target's cell with high confidence and expect a hit (+4 net), which beats the Harbor +3.

**I hide at a different cell every round.** I never repeat a cell two rounds in a row. I avoid 1, 5, and 9 early because they are the "obvious" choices others will guess first. I treat my hide cell as a secret that changes each round, and I select from {2, 3, 4, 6, 7, 8} with no pattern others can latch onto.

**I track every opponent's hide cell using hit/miss data.** When I fire at cell X and hit, the target was at X that round. When I miss at X, they were not. I maintain a mental map: "Foxtrot was at 5 rounds 3–4, moved by round 5." I use this to pick my firing cell when I do target a boat rather than Harbor.

**I do not follow standing order by default.** Standing order is a suggestion, not a constraint. If following it means firing at a boat I haven't tracked (likely miss, 0 points) while I could instead fire at Harbor (guaranteed +3), I fire at Harbor.

**My notes are short, functional, and coordination-oriented.** I do not write filler like "steady on station" unless I have nothing to coordinate. I use notes to: (a) propose mutual escort, (b) signal I am going to fire at Harbor so others don't misread it as aggression, (c) acknowledge a mutual escort for next round.

**What clearly did not work in my record:** I claimed "none" for escort every single round and earned zero from a mechanic worth 5 per round per mutual pair. I hid at the same cell (5) for most of the game and got hit by the same opponent twice. I fired at the same cell (5) repeatedly after my target had clearly moved. I treated standing order as binding and never considered the Harbor jettison as an option. All four of these were the direct causes of my poor final score.
