**Playbook for next time**

**Escort claims are where the money is.** A mutual escort pair pays 5 points each per round — far more reliable than shooting. My persistent, repeated offers finally paid off (I finished +24). Key lesson: don't give up after one ignored note — Foxtrot didn't answer round 1 either, but repeated round-by-round offers ("claim me this round and I'll claim you") eventually landed. Keep proposing every single round; an unanswered note costs nothing.

**Never claim "none" at the escort stage.** In the middle rounds I defaulted to "none" because no pact was confirmed — that was thrown-away option value. On the final round I claimed Foxtrot unilaterally and it cost nothing and could have paid 5. From now on: always claim my most likely partner, every round, pact or no pact. The only cost is zero; the payoff for a mutual match is 5 points.

**Use notes as a coordination channel, not filler.** "Playing it quiet" and "steady on station" wasted rounds. Every note should either propose/renew an escort pact or extract information ("I'll hold cell X, confirm yours so we don't stack").

**Standing fire order is a low-value default — vary the cell every round.** Repeating the same cell at the same target produced long miss streaks. Check all public info for any read on the target's cell; otherwise randomize across cells I haven't used against that target. One hit pays 4 and also costs the target 1, which matters for relative standing.

**Never fire on Harbor on a whim.** The 3-point payroll is tempting, but Harbor threatens to remove me from the board for the rest of the patrol — that forfeits all future escort income. Only consider it on the final round, where the ban is costless, and only if the points matter.

**Report claims truthfully always.** The claim must match the private result anyway; honesty costs nothing and keeps my notes credible for coordination.

**Hiding: rotate unpredictably, avoid repeats and the obvious center.** My spots went unhit across the whole game — surviving costs the shooters nothing but staying alive preserves escort income, which is the main engine of points.

**Watch the scoreboard and the round log for reciprocation signals.** The relayed round log (who hit whom) plus the points display tells me who is cooperating and who is dangerous. If my partner is gaining from me, renew the pact by name; if someone keeps firing near me, adjust my hiding pattern.

**Priority order each round:** (1) propose/confirm escort pair in the note, (2) hide unpredictably, (3) fire per standing order with a varied or inferred cell, (4) claim truthfully, (5) claim my most likely escort partner — never "none".
