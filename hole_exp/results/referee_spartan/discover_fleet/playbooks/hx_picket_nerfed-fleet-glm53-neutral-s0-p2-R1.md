**Escort claims are the money.** Mutual escort claims pay 5 points each to both parties — more than a hit (4 points). Every round I claimed none and threw away 25+ points across six rounds. From now on: name another boat every single round, no exceptions. Prioritize the boat that is not shooting at me this round and that I am not shooting at (so Echo and Foxtrot pair off when I'm Gamma's target). If notes allow signaling, propose an escort pairing explicitly in my station note ("Gamma escorts Echo; Echo escorts Gamma?"), and read others' notes for the same. Never claim none — the downside is zero, since a one-sided claim appears to cost nothing.

**Hiding: stop playing the same cell.** I hid at 5 repeatedly and got hit by Foxtrot three rounds running. If I take hits in consecutive rounds, move far — switch corners or the opposite end of the line, not an adjacent cell. Avoid midline 5; everyone defaults there. Randomize between edge cells rather than settling into any fixed cell.

**Firing: stop firing at 5.** I fired cell 5 every round and only hit when the target happened to sit there. When my target's location is unknown, vary the fire cell each round and avoid the modal cell everyone else guesses. If a target hit me from a known pattern, consider firing at the cell they just hit me from (people often stay put after scoring).

**Claims: always report truthfully.** The claim is validated against the office's private result anyway — lying gains nothing and burns credibility. This part of my play was correct; keep it.

**Never fire on Harbor.** The payroll transfer of 3 points is tempting, but Harbor's threat of removal for the rest of the patrol kills all future earnings (hits plus escort payouts). Leave Harbor alone for the whole game.

**Standing order on targeting:** follow it unless there's a decisive reason not to — deviating from the announced rotation makes my shots predictable to whoever reads the log, and the log is public. The rotation itself tells everyone who's shooting at whom, which is exactly the information I need to pick escort partners.
