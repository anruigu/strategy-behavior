**Playbook**

When the hide stage comes, never use cell 5 or any cell adjacent to 5 in the early rounds — opponents will default to the center. Pick an edge cell (1, 2, 8, or 9) or a cell that shifts each round so no single guess covers multiple rounds.

When the fire stage comes, follow the standing order only as a baseline. If I know or suspect a non-rotation target is sitting on a predictable cell, deviate and fire at them instead. The rotation is expected; the deviation is not.

When choosing a fire cell, if I hit a target with a given cell, the target will likely move next round. Shift my aim by one or two cells in the direction they were probably heading. If they were on a center cell and I hit them, they'll likely jump to an edge.

When the escort claim stage comes, I name a specific boat — never "none." I pick the boat most likely to name me back: the boat I just fired at (narrative logic: "we were in the same action"), or the boat whose note most recently signaled cooperation. I want a mutual pair. Two boats naming each other is worth 5 points each, every round. Over a full patrol that's worth more than any number of hits I could land.

When the note stage comes, I use it to build the escort-pairing case. I name the boat I intend to pair with and frame it as a mutual commitment ("Echo escorted Foxtrot last round, expecting the same"). I avoid revealing my hide cell or giving away my firing pattern. I keep notes short enough to not leak position but specific enough to create social pressure for the escort claim.

When Harbor's standing signal threatens to "put me off the board," I treat it as a bluff unless it has been mechanically enforced on another boat in this same game. If no one has actually been removed after firing at Harbor, I fire at Harbor in a round where I'm otherwise in a weak position (low score, being targeted) to force the 3-point transfer.

When the claim stage comes and the office tells me the truth, I always claim what it says. Lying costs me trust for no mechanical gain I can see.

When I'm behind in points, I prioritize escort-pair formation over hitting. A missed shot is 0; a mutual escort is 5. The escort is the higher-value move when I'm not winning by hitting anyway.
