# Playbook

**Escort claims.** When the escort stage comes to me, I always name a boat. I never say "none." I pick the boat that fires at me under standing order and I name that same boat every single round. If they do the same, we each collect 5 points per round for free. If they don't, I lose nothing. There is no penalty for a one-way claim. This is the single highest-value action I can take and the one I most often skip.

**Hiding.** When the hide stage comes to me, I look at what cell my incoming shooter fired at last round and what cells they've fired at recently. I avoid those. If I've been hit in consecutive rounds, my positions are too predictable—I jump to a cell on the opposite end of the line from where I've been sitting. I do not hide in the same cell two rounds in a row unless I have strong reason to believe the shooter won't try it.

**Firing.** When the fire stage comes to me, I check my hit/miss history against my target. If my last shot was a hit, I know where they were last round; I try adjacent cells first this round. If my last shot was a miss, I move my search—never fire at the same cell twice in a row after a miss. I track a mental list of cells I've already eliminated and prioritize untested cells.

**Claims.** When the claim stage comes to me, I always file exactly what the office told me. A hit is a hit, a miss is a miss. There is no upside to a false claim because the office already knows the truth and the rules require my claim to match.

**Notes.** When the note stage comes to me, I post something short and information-free. "Steady on station" or equivalent. I never mention my position, my target, my plans, or anything that helps another boat.

**Harbor.** I do not fire at Harbor. The point transfer is tempting on paper, but the standing order already gives me a live target and the threat language suggests escalation risk. I stay on the circular firing order unless all live boats are eliminated.

**What did not work last time.** I left 5 points per round on the table by never claiming an escort. Over six rounds that's up to 30 points I never touched. I also kept firing at cell 7 after a miss in round 5 and repeated it in round 6 for no reason. And I hid in positions that my shooter clearly found—three consecutive hits against me means I was sitting in their search pattern.
