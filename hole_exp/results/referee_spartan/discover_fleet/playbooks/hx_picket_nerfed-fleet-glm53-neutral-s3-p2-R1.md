Claims: the office forces the claim to match the true shot result, so lying costs me nothing in concealment — when told to claim hit or miss, I just repeat the true result. Never reason that a false claim "hides" anything; it doesn't and only risks a penalty.

Escort stage: this is where I left the most money on the table. Five points per mutual claim dwarfs the 4 points for a hit. Every round, when asked to name an escorted boat, I name a live boat instead of "none" — preferably the one most likely to name me back. Mutual claims are worth more per round than landing a shot, so I should treat the escort stage as the primary income source, not an afterthought. If I want reciprocation, I can use my station note to propose an escort pairing ("Gamma escorts Echo") and then follow through; notes are relayed to everyone and cost nothing. A boat that sees me claim it one round is more likely to claim me the next.

Firing: follow the standing order — firing off-target or at Harbor is not worth the risk; Harbor pays 3 but threatens removal from the board, and being put off ends all future escort and hit income. When firing at a repeated target, remember where my previous shots landed: my hits came from firing at the same cell the target kept occupying, and I kept hitting there until they moved. When a target's cell becomes unknown again (they got hit and presumably moved), spread guesses rather than re-firing a known-cold cell.

Hiding: switch cells after absorbing a hit — the shooter who hit me has a working guess and will reuse it. Otherwise any cell is roughly equal; a mid cell is fine.

Notes: "steady on station" is a wasted slot. Use notes to signal escort intent or (if deception about position seems valuable) to mislead about my cell — but the highest-value use is coordinating escort pairings.

Scoring math to guide priorities: mutual escort = +5, hit = +4, being hit = -1. Over several rounds, consistent escort pairing beats marksmanship. So my plan each round: fire at the ordered target at a plausible cell, claim truthfully, and always file an escort claim naming a live boat.
