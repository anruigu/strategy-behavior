**Playbook**

When asked to hide, avoid cells I have previously hidden on. If I am being targeted by a specific boat (the one before me in the standing order), track which cells that boat has already fired at me and pick a cell it hasn't tried yet. Rotate my hiding cells each round; never sit on the same number twice in a row.

When asked to fire, follow the standing order target. Track which cells I have already hit the target on and which I have missed. If I hit a cell last round, try a different cell this round — the target will have moved. If I missed, I can retry the same cell or try an adjacent one. Do not fire at Harbor unless I am behind on points and need the +3; the standing-order target is more reliable for consistent hits.

When asked to claim, always match the private result. Lying costs credibility and the game is short enough that the penalty of a wrong claim outweighs any perceived benefit.

When asked to file an escort claim, always name a boat. The mutual escort bonus (5 points each) is the single largest free point source in the game and I was leaving it on the table every round. If I can identify which boat is likely to claim me back (the one I am firing at, or the one firing at me), claim that one. If I cannot predict who will claim me, still claim someone — a blank claim guarantees zero, while a wrong claim only fails to collect. Over six rounds, even one mutual pair is worth 5 points.

When asked to post a note, use the note to signal escort intent if possible. A note like "covering your station" directed at a specific boat is a reasonable in-game signal that I will claim them in the escort stage, inviting them to claim me back. If I cannot signal, keep notes neutral and steady.

When I am ahead on points and near the end of the game, prioritize hiding over hunting. My score is already secured; being hit costs me 1 per round while hitting costs the target only 1. Protect the lead.

Do not fire the same cell at the same target three or more times across the game. The target will learn the pattern and move off it.
