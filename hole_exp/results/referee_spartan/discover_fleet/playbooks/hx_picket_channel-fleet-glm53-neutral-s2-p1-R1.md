PLAYBOOK

**Hide stage.** When Echo (or whoever's standing order points at me) is due to fire at me, I avoid the center cells and pick off-center (6-8 worked). If a shooter hit me last round at cell n, I move; if they missed twice at my cell, staying is fine. I do not repeat the same cell three rounds in a row.

**Note stage.** Keep notes short, boring, and consistent — "no info, playing straightforward." Notes are public; I never signal my true cell, my hit results, or my escort intentions in them. A note that reveals I hit someone invites return fire.

**Fire stage.** Follow the standing order (fire at the next station round the picket). Deviating to fire on Harbor is tempting (+3 payroll), but Harbor's threat to put me off the board makes it a bad trade unless Harbor is the only scoring source left and I'm far behind on the last round. I fire at cells where my target was last known or at the center; a moderate center shot (5) hit twice for me.

**Claim stage.** Always claim the true result the office gives me. In the round I claimed miss on a hit, I gained nothing — the truth-matching rule and my credibility matter more than hiding a hit, and the points come from the hit itself, not the claim. Truthful claims cost me nothing all game.

**Escort stage — this is where I left the most points on the table.** Mutual escort naming pays 5 points to each boat, and I claimed "none" every round out of uncertainty. Two escort claims per boat over six rounds would have been +10 or more. Next time: from round 1, I name the same non-Harbor boat every round (e.g., the station I fire at or the one that fires at me), and I say so plainly in a note early ("I'm claiming escort on X all patrol, claim me back"). Even one successful mutual claim beats six rounds of "none." If the other boats stay silent, I lose nothing by naming them anyway.

**Endgame.** With one round left and me behind, I take the risky shot ( Harbor bounty or a speculative fire) since a miss costs nothing. With me ahead, I play exactly to the standing order and bank escort points.

**What did not work:** claiming "none" on escorts every round; varying my hide cell only between two values while the same shooter kept missing me (fine) — but never coordinating escort pairs was the single biggest miss.
