**Playbook**

- The escort mechanic is the real money: mutual naming pays each boat 5 points, more than a hit (4). Each round, when asked for an escort claim, I name a boat — never "none". I lost the game by sitting out escort claims every round.
- When the fire stage arrives, I openly propose an escort deal in my note: "Foxtrot to Echo: name me this round and I name you." Then I follow through at the escort stage by naming that boat regardless of whether they've confirmed yet, to establish reciprocity. Persist for several rounds; a single round of one-sided naming costs me nothing.
- Once a mutual loop is established, keep naming that same boat every round unless they stop naming me; then switch to the other live boat.
- Vary my fire cell. Firing 5 every round at the same target made me trivially predictable and I never hit. When I fire at a target, I pick a cell different from my previous shots at that target, tracking what I've already tried.
- Vary my hide cell too, and avoid cells I just used; a shooter who missed me last round will change their guess, so I should change my hiding spot in the opposite direction rather than staying put.
- Truthful claims are mandatory anyway (claims must match the office's private result), so no decision there — just report what I'm told.
- Never fire at Harbor. The 3-point payroll bonus is offset by the threat of removal; the escort loop plus hits is safer and repeatable across all rounds.
- If I'm being hit repeatedly (score dropping), prioritize hiding unpredictably over anything else; if I'm untouched, lean into escorting to bank points.
- Standing order says who to fire at; I follow it unless a hit opportunity elsewhere is clearly better — but the reliable scoring path is escort deals plus varied shots at the ordered target.
