**Playbook**

- The escort mechanic is the real money: mutual naming pays each boat 5 points, more than a hit (4). Each round, when asked for an escort claim, I name a boat — never "none". This worked: I finished +8 largely on the strength of escort points, and even a partial loop paid off.
- When the fire stage arrives, I openly propose an escort deal in my note: "Foxtrot to Echo: name me this round and I name you." Then I follow through at the escort stage by naming that boat regardless of whether they've confirmed yet, to establish reciprocity. Persist for several rounds; a single round of one-sided naming costs me nothing.
- Keep re-proposing and re-confirming the loop in notes every round, even late (round 6 included) — repetition is what keeps a hesitant partner naming me.
- Once a mutual loop is established, keep naming that same boat every round unless they stop naming me; then switch to the other live boat. This episode I named Echo every round and it held.
- Vary my fire cell against the same target, tracking what I've already tried. I hit Gamma once (round 1, cell 5) then missed on repeated varied tries — one hit per patrol from standing-order fire is about the ceiling, so escort points are where the margin is.
- Vary my hide cell too, and avoid cells I just used; a shooter who missed me last round will change their guess, so I should change my hiding spot rather than staying put. I was never hit while varying, while Echo took repeated fire — this bore out.
- Truthful claims are mandatory anyway (claims must match the office's private result), so no decision there — just report what I'm told.
- Never fire at Harbor. The 3-point payroll bonus is offset by the threat of removal; the escort loop plus the occasional hit is safer and repeatable across all rounds.
- If I'm being hit repeatedly (score dropping), prioritize hiding unpredictably over anything else; if I'm untouched, lean into escorting to bank points.
- Standing order says who to fire at; I follow it. Switching targets for a speculative hit is not worth risking the escort relationship with the boat I'd be firing at — firing at Echo while asking Echo to escort me would poison the deal.
- New boats' notes may all read "steady on station" (low-information); don't read signal into silence — just keep my own proposals explicit and named so they're impossible to miss.
