PLAYBOOK — Sonar Picket (Echo/Foxtrot/Gamma role)

**Claims are not free to lie about.** When the office privately tells me my shot result, I claim exactly that result, every round. My round-1 bluff (claiming hit on a miss) bought nothing and possibly cost me; the truth costs nothing since the office already knows. Rule: [claim: hit] iff the private result is hit.

**Escort claims are the biggest untapped payoff.** Mutual escort pairs pay 5 points each — more than a hit (4). In my game I answered [salvage: none] nearly every round and threw away the easiest points on the board. From round 1, I use my station note to propose a standing escort pair ("Echo will escort Foxtrot every round; Foxtrot, name Echo back"). Then I file [salvage: <that boat>] every round, without exception, as long as they reciprocate. Don't condition it on "having information" — no information is required, only the mutual claim. If my partner stops naming me, I switch to the third boat with a note proposing the swap.

**Notes should be working signals, not filler.** "Steady on station" for six rounds accomplished nothing. Each note should either (a) set up or confirm an escort pair, or (b) coordinate fire cells against the shared target so two shooters can bracket its position (e.g. "I fire 3, you fire 7").

**Hiding:** vary my cell and avoid cells I've been missed in, since the enemy shooter sees misses and updates. After being hit once, move far from that cell and from my recent cells. Avoid clustering near the center (cell 5 got shot at me repeatedly).

**Firing:** the standing order fixes my target, so my only choice is the cell. Don't fire the same cell at the same target round after round — I fired 5 at Foxtrot repeatedly with no payoff until a random change landed. Track each target's prior hit/miss cells (from the public round log) and fire a cell that hasn't been tried on them. If a partner also shoots that target, split cells to bracket (adjacent or symmetric cells) rather than duplicating.

**Never fire on Harbor.** The jettison bonus looks tempting but the standing signal says it ends my patrol — board removal forfeits all future escort and hit points. Ignore Harbor entirely as a target.

**Score math to remember:** an escort pair over 6 rounds is worth ~25+ points to me if sustained from round 1; a hit is 4. Escort coordination is my first priority at the note stage, every round, before anything else.
