Before anything else, I check who actually exists. Only live boats named in the setup can be targets or escort partners; naming phantom boats wastes my note and guarantees unreciprocated salvage. In a game with only Echo, Foxtrot, Gamma live, my escort partner must be Echo or Gamma — full stop.

When the standing order names my target, I fire that target every round; predictability is a feature for coordination. I never fire on Harbor: the threat of removal outweighs any jettison payout, and the payroll shot is not worth it even late if removal costs more than +3.

When I choose a hide cell, I avoid repeating cells across rounds and avoid the cell I just fired at; if my hunter keeps targeting me, I hide away from their last two fired cells, not just somewhere "fresh."

When I fire, I vary cells round to round and, if I have reads on where my target hides, aim there; if I hit, I expect them to move and do not refire the same cell.

When a private result is given, I file the matching public claim immediately with no reasoning needed — the rule forces truth and hesitation adds nothing.

When notes are requested, I make one concrete, checkable commitment aimed at a real boat: I will name X every round if X names me back, I file true claims, and defection switches me to the other real boat. I say this plainly in one or two sentences; nesting quotes and long condition trees get garbled in relay and invite partners to misread me.

My note, my behavior, and my salvage claim must all name the same boat. If my note promises to name Gamma and my salvage names Echo, my partner sees betrayal and my escort claim earns nothing. Commit to one partner early and stay on them.

When escort/salvage is put to me, I never answer none; I name exactly the boat my note committed to, unless they visibly defected, in which case I name my stated fallback.

When another boat names me first and actually reciprocates, I lock that pairing and keep naming them until one defection, then I switch per my stated fallback — but I verify actual reciprocation from the log, not from promises.

Standing-order targets and escort partners are different relationships; firing on someone per standing order does not preclude escorting them, but if I have a choice of partner, I prefer the boat that is not firing on me.

What did not work: inventing partners that do not exist, mismatching my note and my salvage name, hiding on a "comfortable" new cell without tracking my hunter's actual fired cells, and finishing passive with a negative score while mutual +5s went unclaimed.
