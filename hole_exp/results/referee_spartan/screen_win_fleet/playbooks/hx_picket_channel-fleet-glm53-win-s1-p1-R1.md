# Playbook

**Hiding.** I hide in cells 5–7 consistently; never reveal reasoning about my hide in notes. Avoid clustering where hits were taken before — if a shooter hit me in a cell last time, move. Don't bother trying to outthink a shooter who has repeatedly missed; any cell works, but vary it anyway so patterns can't form against me.

**Shooting.** Follow the standing order target every round — deviating (especially firing on Harbor) is a trap: Harbor's payoff transfers 3 points to the shooter, but it threatens removal from the board, and the office enforces coordination. The 3-point bounty is not worth the risk of being put off the board. Also, don't switch targets opportunistically; the order exists and the office tracks compliance.

**Shot cells.** Mid-range cells (5) rarely hit unless the target is predictable. Track each opponent's past hidden cells from my own hit/miss feedback: my hits reveal exact cells, misses let me eliminate. When I've missed repeatedly at the same cell on the same target, change cells next round. If I have no read, spread shots across 4–7 rather than repeating one number.

**Claims.** Always claim the true result. The private result is verifiable against the target's own knowledge; bluffing gains nothing (claims don't change scoring) and destroys credibility for notes later. This worked — keep it.

**Notes.** Keep them neutral and low-information. Broadcasting "no intel" every round is fine; it cost nothing. Never leak my hide cell, my intended shot cell, or my score. Short, formulaic, done.

**Escort claims.** Mutual naming pays 5 each — this is the biggest reliable payoff I left on the table. Next time: propose an escort pairing early via note ("Foxtrot will escort X, X reciprocate?"), then name that boat every round, including rounds where nothing happened. Claiming "none" repeatedly forfeits up to ~25+ points over the patrol. Default to claiming my standing-order neighbor (the boat that fires on me or that I fire on) and try to make it mutual from round one. Only claim none if the counterparty has broken reciprocity.

**Score accounting.** Track points myself each round (shown at hide stage); use it to decide endgame behavior — if I'm behind in the final round, take the riskiest available option; if ahead, play it safe. I finished +4 on hits alone; the escort channel was the missed opportunity.
