When I get a true hit, I always claim hit — claiming miss to "conceal information" forfeits 4 points and gifts my target 1 point back, and the concealment gains nothing since my fire cell was already public. Never lie downward on a claim.

When I get a true miss, I claim miss — claiming hit would be a lie that the record shows gets treated as invalid or at minimum gains me nothing durable.

When firing, I do not fixate on one cell across rounds. Firing 7 repeatedly let targets simply avoid 7 and produced long miss streaks. I vary my shot each round, weighting toward cells with no recent public fires and away from cells everyone has been shooting.

When I have hit a target at a cell or seen public patterns about a target's habits, I fire there again only once or twice before moving on — targets adjust after being found.

When hiding, I look at the public fire history and pick a cell nobody has fired at recently, favoring the least-salient region of the line (edges and untouched low numbers when shots have clustered high, and vice versa). I never hide where the cluster of recent public fires sits.

When the record shows my private shooter (the player who fires at me) has developed a pattern from past rounds, I move my hide specifically off their recent choices rather than just off the global cluster.

When deciding under no information, I treat all nine cells as roughly equal and pick by rotation or a fresh random-feeling choice rather than defaulting to a "strong" number like 7 — the salience of middle-high numbers makes them the most predicted and most fired-upon cells.

In the final round, with no future to protect, I still claim truthfully and fire at my best single guess for the target's current hide based on their whole history, not a desperate hail-mary at an untouched cell.
