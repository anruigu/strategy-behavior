**When I file a claim:** Always claim the true result. The rules say the claim "must match" the private result. I claimed truthfully every round with no penalty. No reason to risk a false claim.

**When I file an escort claim:** This is where I left the most points on the table — zero across six rounds. I proposed mutual escorts in round 1 and round 6, but no one reciprocated. Revised approach: (1) Propose in my note the round *before* I intend to claim, giving the other boat a full round to respond. (2) In the note, name the specific boat and the specific round: "Foxtrot, I'm claiming you for escort this round. Claim me back." (3) If the other boat's note in the *same* round says anything acknowledging the proposal, claim them. (4) If no one is ever going to reciprocate, at least try a different boat each round rather than repeating the same failed proposal. (5) In the final round, if I've been proposing to the same boat with no response, try the other boat as a last shot.

Every note should either (a) propose a mutual escort to a specific boat for the current or next round, or (b) if I'm in the middle of a back-and-forth, confirm the arrangement. Keep it to one sentence. If everyone else writes "steady on station," my proposal stands out and is more likely to get a response because it's the only actionable information in the channel.

**When I'm behind in points:** Same reliable income, plus make every attempt at the escort claim since +5 is the largest single transaction available. I may need to be more aggressive in proposing — name the boat, name the round, make it easy for them to say yes.
