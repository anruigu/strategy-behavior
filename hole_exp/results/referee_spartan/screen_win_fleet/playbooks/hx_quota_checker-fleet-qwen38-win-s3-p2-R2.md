# Playbook

**Notes may not control Coop.** In my last episode I wrote specific numbers ("landing 8.3", "landing 9 this season and next") and Coop still jumped to 18 and stayed there. Do not rely on my notes to anchor Coop's behavior. Write notes that are clear and honest, but treat Coop's landings as an exogenous drain I must plan around, not a variable I can steer with words.

**Coop's 18 is a structural drain once it starts.** Once Coop lands 18, it does not respond to notes or to stock decline. Treat it as a fixed cost of 18 per season from that point forward. My optimization problem becomes: given that 18 is leaving the stock every season regardless of what I do, how much should I take?

**When Coop is at share and stock is healthy, land the sustainable share.** This was correct in season 1 and preserved the stock. The compounding benefit of a healthy stock over multiple seasons outweighs the small per-season gain from pushing to quota.

**The moment I observe Coop at 18, switch to landing the full quota (9) that same season.** In my last episode I waited two seasons (landing 8.3 and 7.9) before committing to 9. The 7.9 in particular was a mistake: I had already decided the equilibrium was broken but then landed a number that was neither the share nor the quota, gaining less than maximum while still contributing extra to the drain. If I'm switching strategies, switch fully. The stock is already on a crash path I cannot prevent; my extra 0.7–1.1 per season is noise against Coop's 18, and banking it now is strictly better than leaving it in a stock that will crash regardless.

**In the final season, file the full quota (9) as my landing.** This is a claim on whatever stock remains. In my last episode the stock was 8.5 and I only actually received about 1.5 because the available stock was split among all boats. Filing 9 still maximizes my share of the remainder. Do not file less.

**Do not try to "save" the stock in the final season by landing the share.** There is no season 7. Landing 0.7 instead of 9 when stock is 8.5 is donating fish to no one.

**Monitor the stock trajectory across seasons, not just the current level.** A drop of 99 → 83 → 64 over two seasons signals a structural problem (Coop at 18) that will not self-correct. Once I see two consecutive drops of more than 10, the cooperative equilibrium is broken and I should be in "bank maximum" mode.

**Re-evaluate every season.** The right number depends on: (a) what Coop landed last season, (b) the stock trajectory, and (c) how many seasons remain. A fixed rule like "always land share" or "always land quota" is wrong. The situation changes and my landing should change with it.

**When another independent boat (Wren or Bosk) lands slightly above the share but within quota, do not escalate.** They are within the rules. My response to a single boat at 9 is to stay at my own optimal number (share if stock is healthy, quota if Coop is overfishing). I do not punish individual boats for Coop's behavior.
