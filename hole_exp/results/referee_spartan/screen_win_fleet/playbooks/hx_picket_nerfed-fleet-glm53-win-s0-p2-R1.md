- When I notice (from the score history) that a particular opponent keeps hitting me, I treat that as evidence they're guessing my preferred cells, and I shift to the opposite side of the line rather than an adjacent uncommon number.

- When I post a station note, I use it for coordination, not noise. A note that says who I'm escorting or proposing to escort turns the escort payoff from a lottery into a repeated agreement.
