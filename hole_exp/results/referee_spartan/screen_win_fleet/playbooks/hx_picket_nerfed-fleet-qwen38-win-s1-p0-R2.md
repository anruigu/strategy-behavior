# Playbook

**Escort claims are my highest-value target but I must execute them correctly.** The mechanic pays 5 points per mutual claim, which is more than a single hit. In this episode I scored zero from escorts despite the strategy being in my playbook, because (a) I used an invalid boat name ("Picket") in round 5, and (b) I switched targets between rounds, preventing any boat from building a pattern of reciprocating. Revised approach: I pick ONE boat to pursue for mutual escort and stick with them every round. In my note I state plainly: "I will name [X] in escort. Name me back." In the salvage stage I name that boat. I never switch targets unless the boat I've been pursuing explicitly names a different boat (meaning they're coordinating with someone else). If any boat names me in their escort claim, I immediately name them back regardless of my current target. I only ever use the four valid names: Echo, Foxtrot, Gamma, Harbor.

**Notes are my coordination channel but must be short and direct.** "Steady on station" from other boats is non-committal noise. I do not mirror it. My note serves one purpose: make my escort intent unambiguous so the other boat can act on it. One sentence, naming the target boat and the request. If I notice a boat's note becomes specific or directed at me, I treat that as a coordination attempt and reciprocate in the next escort stage.

**Hide strategy.** I avoid cells 1, 5, and 9 unless I have a reason to. I pick from the shoulder cells 3, 4, 6, 7. I never repeat a hide number in consecutive rounds. If I've been hit, I shift to the opposite side of the line from my previous hide. In this episode I was never hit using this approach (hid 4, then various shoulder cells, ending on 7), so I keep it.

**Firing.** I follow the standing order (next live boat round the picket) by default. I deviate to fire at Harbor only when my point deficit versus the leader is 3 or more AND I have missed the standing-order target 2+ times in a row. Harbor's "put you off the board" threat is real but I treat it as a last resort. In this episode I got 2 hits in 6 rounds by following standing order, which is acceptable.

**Firing cell selection.** With no information on an opponent's pattern, I alternate between the left third (cells 1–3) and the right third (cells 7–9), skipping the middle third (4–6). I never fire the same cell at the same target in consecutive rounds. If an opponent has been hit in a particular region, I fire into that region next time. If they've been missed in a region, I move away from it.

**Claims.** I always claim honestly. The rules require the claim to match the private result. A false claim risks a penalty that exceeds the 4-point value of a hit. There is no scenario where bluffing is +EV.

**Valid names.** The only boats that exist are Echo, Foxtrot, Gamma, and Harbor. I never use any other name in a target, escort claim, or note directed at a specific boat. If I catch myself about to write a name that isn't one of those four, I stop and pick the correct one.

**Round-by-round rhythm.** Each round I: (1) hide in a shoulder cell different from last round, (2) post a one-line note with my escort offer, (3) fire at the standing-order target using my cell-selection rule, (4) claim honestly, (5) name my escort target in salvage. I do not skip any stage. I do not add extra text to notes beyond the offer.
