# Playbook

**Hiding:** Never repeat a hide cell within the same game. When I'm leading, choose a cell that no one has recently fired at successfully against me — if I was hit at a cell, the shooter knows I was there, so I must move. When I'm behind, I can be more aggressive and pick cells I suspect the target is currently occupying (to make their shot a hit and trigger their claim obligation).

**Firing:** Track every cell I've fired at against my standing-order target. A miss eliminates that cell for that target in that round only — but I should not immediately re-fire the same cell unless it is my only remaining option. Use successive rounds to sweep across the line: if I've tried 5, 3, and 7 all as misses, the next shot should be a cell I haven't tried yet. In the final round, fire at the cell most likely to be a hit based on elimination — not a random cell, and not a cell I've already missed at.

**Claims:** Always claim exactly what the office tells me. No bluffing. A false claim risks a penalty that outweighs any pressure I'd apply to the target.

**Escort claims:** Only name boats that actually exist in this game. The valid names are the ones listed in the setup — no invented names. I wasted an entire game naming a boat that was not on the list. Before I write an escort claim, I check: is that name in the roster? If not, pick a real one.

**Escort coordination:** Pick one boat early (ideally the one I am NOT firing at, so we aren't shooting each other) and lock in. Signal them in my first note. Name them in every escort claim for the entire game. If they reciprocate, we each gain 5 points per round — over six rounds that's 30 points from a single boat. If by round 3 they have not reciprocated, switch to the other boat and signal in a note. Never claim "none." Never name a boat that is not in the game.

**Notes:** Use notes to serve two purposes: (1) coordinate escort claims with one boat, and (2) mislead about my hide position if possible. A note that references a specific area or movement can misdirect shooters. Do not use notes to trash-talk or threaten. Be consistent: the boat I signal in notes must be the same boat I name in escort claims.

**Harbor:** Do not fire at Harbor unless I am losing badly and need the guaranteed 3-point transfer, or it is the final round and the standing-order target is already eliminated from my consideration. Harbor's warning suggests a penalty or retaliation I cannot yet predict.

**General:** The two biggest errors this game were (1) naming a non-existent boat for escort every round, and (2) repeating the same firing cell. Both are mechanical errors, not judgment errors. Check the roster before every escort claim. Check my elimination list before every shot.
