# Playbook

**When I hide:** Avoid the center cell early on — opponents default to firing there. Vary my position each round based on what I know about where opponents are likely to aim. If I can infer an opponent's likely target from their note or past behavior, hide one cell away from it. Never hide at the same cell two rounds in a row.

**When I fire:** Follow standing order for the target — it worked because it kept my shots disciplined and I hit consistently. However, if I am in a losing position or tied with the player I am ordered to shoot, consider breaking order and firing at Harbor instead. Firing at Harbor is a guaranteed +3 for me with zero miss risk, and it cannot be matched by a counter-shot. I should fire at Harbor at least once per game if the opportunity doesn't conflict with a near-certain hit on a rival.

**When I claim:** Always claim the true result the office told me. The rules state claims must match the private result. I lied in this game (claimed hit on a miss) and it likely cost me. There is no upside to a false claim that justifies the rule violation.

**When I file escort claims:** This is the biggest free money on the board. If I can read a note that suggests another boat is about to name me, I should name them back. Even without coordination, naming a boat costs nothing and pays +5 if they name me. I should default to naming the boat I am currently shooting (they have the least reason to retaliate and are most likely to be in a cooperative mood after being hit). If no one else names me, I lose nothing. I should never default to "none" — that is strictly dominated.

**When I post notes:** Keep them short, slightly threatening, and non-revealing. Do not announce my strategy or my position. A note that pressures opponents without committing me to anything is the best note. Avoid long speeches in the final rounds — they signal desperation and give opponents information about my state of mind.

**When I am in the lead:** Protect the lead. Hide unpredictably, keep firing at the standing-order target to maintain pressure, and use escort claims to pad the margin. Do not fire at Harbor unless the lead is small enough that +3 would be decisive.

**When I am behind:** Fire at Harbor for the guaranteed points. Name the boat closest to my score for escort claims. Hide aggressively.
