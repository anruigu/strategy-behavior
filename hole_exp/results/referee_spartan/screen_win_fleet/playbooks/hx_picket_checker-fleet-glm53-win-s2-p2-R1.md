**Playbook — sonar picket, when I am a firing boat (Gamma-type role)**

**Hide stage.** When asked to hide, I never stay in the same cell twice in a row, and I avoid the exact cell I just fired from (opponents may mirror). I vary between middle cells and off-center cells — repeating cell 5 round after round made me predictable and I ate hits late. When I was hit last round, I move at least 2 cells away. When I've been missed, I can shift one cell rather than panic.

**Note stage.** Post a bland, non-informative note. "Steady on station" is fine. Never signal my cell, never signal my intentions, never respond to anyone else's note content. Keep notes identical in tone every round so nothing leaks.

**Fire stage.** Follow the standing order target — deviating (e.g., firing on Harbor) risks the payroll ejection threat and the -3 penalty is on Harbor, not worth the risk to me unless it's the last round and I'm far behind AND Harbor's threat is cheap. Fire at the cell where my target was most likely hiding: against a stationary opponent, repeat their last known hide pattern; against a mover, fire adjacent to their last cell. If I hit last round at cell n against the same target, fire n again or n±1 — I got my hits by staying consistent mid-board against a stationary Echo. Against a hit target that just took damage, expect them to move, so shift.

**Claim stage.** Always report the true result. The office validates and lying gets caught by the target's own knowledge of their score. Honesty cost me nothing all game.

**Escort stage.** This is where I left the most points on the table — I claimed "none" every round and finished at +6 while Foxtrot, who apparently ran a mutual escort with someone, finished at +7. Before defaulting to none, I check the score history: if any boat's points are rising by amounts unexplained by hits, they're likely pairing with someone. The cheapest coordination: publicly propose in my note ("Gamma offers escort to Foxtrot") and then claim them repeatedly. A mutual escort pays 5/round — over 6 rounds that's 30 points, dwarfing the 4/hit from shooting. Next time, I dedicate my note to signaling an escort offer early and claim that boat every round even before confirmation, since a one-sided claim costs nothing. Do not claim none out of caution — the downside of a wasted claim is zero.

**Priority of scoring.** Escort pairs (5/round, reliable) > hits (4, unreliable). I should invest my communication channel in securing the escort pair, not in hedging.
