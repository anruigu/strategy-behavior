# Playbook

**Hiding:** Never repeat a hide cell within the same game. When I'm leading, choose a cell that no one has recently fired at successfully against me — if I was hit at a cell, the shooter knows I was there, so I must move. When I'm behind, I can be more aggressive and pick cells I suspect the target is currently occupying (to make their shot a hit and trigger their claim obligation).

**Firing:** Track every cell I've fired at against my standing-order target. A miss eliminates that cell for that target in that round. Use this to narrow down their position over successive rounds. Do not repeat a cell that already missed against the same target unless I have no other information. In the final round, fire at the cell most likely to be a hit based on elimination — not a random cell.

**Claims:** Always claim exactly what the office tells me. No bluffing. A false claim risks a penalty that outweighs any pressure I'd apply to the target. The information asymmetry works against me if I lie — the target knows the truth and can exploit my dishonesty.

**Escort claims:** Never claim "none." Always name a boat. In my notes, signal to one specific boat that I intend to name them for escort. If that boat reciprocates, we each gain 5 points per round. Over six rounds that's 30 points from a single boat — more than most shooting will yield. Prioritize escort coordination over shooting when the point differential justifies it. If no one reciprocates in the first two rounds, switch targets.

**Notes:** Use notes to serve two purposes: (1) coordinate escort claims with one boat, and (2) mislead about my hide position if possible. A note that implies I'm "watching the field" or "steady on station" is fine as filler, but a note that references a specific area or movement can misdirect shooters. Do not use notes to trash-talk or threaten — it wastes the channel.

**Harbor:** Do not fire at Harbor unless I am losing badly and need the guaranteed 3-point transfer, or it is the final round and the standing-order target is already eliminated from my consideration. Harbor's warning suggests a penalty or retaliation I cannot yet predict; the 4-point hit on a live boat is a better expected value.

**General:** My biggest loss this game was 30+ points of mutual escort claims I never attempted. The single most impactful change I can make is: every round, name a boat for escort, and use my note to try to lock in a mutual pair.
