When I post a station note, I use it to set up escort agreements: name a specific boat and propose we claim each other every round (e.g. "Echo<->Gamma escort pact?"). Mutual escort pays 5 points each — far more than any shooting — and notes are the only channel to arrange it. I respond affirmatively in my next note if another boat proposes a pact with me.

When the escort stage comes, I always name a boat, never "none." Filing "none" forfeits the best scoring action in the game. If a pact exists, I name my pact partner. If none exists, I name the boat I most recently proposed one to, on the chance they reciprocate. I break a pact only if my partner failed to reciprocate twice; then I propose a new one with the third boat immediately.

When I choose a hide cell, I pick uniformly at random from 1–9 and never reuse a pattern or rationalize a "safe" cell. Any reasoning I attach is noise; the opponents have no information, so randomness is optimal. I never hide in the cell I was just hit in.

When I fire, I follow the standing order's designated target and never fire on Harbor — jettison points are not worth being put off the board, and its threat is credible. I pick the fire cell uniformly at random each round, independently of my own hide and of previous shots. Firing the same cell or just two cells repeatedly, as I did, wastes rounds; broad random coverage finds a hiding boat faster than any guess about their habits.

When the claim stage comes, I always report the true result. False hit claims are contradicted by the target's own knowledge and buy nothing; honesty costs nothing and keeps my note-channel credibility intact for escort negotiations. I keep claims to a bare token so there's no discrepancy to exploit.

When I'm losing late, I don't change the fundamentals — random hides, ordered fire, truthful claims — but I push harder in notes to close an escort pact, since paired escort income is the only reliable way to close a deficit when my shots aren't landing. I track the running score each round and, if pacts are impossible, accept that shooting variance decides it rather than making desperate Harbor attacks.

In every stage I reply with exactly the bracketed token requested, on one line, with at most one short line of reasoning before it — malformed or invented answers waste the turn.
