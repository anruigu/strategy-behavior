**Playbook: quota checker (as Bosk or equivalent)**

- The harbour boat (Coop) is not a normal player — it lands its share while the fleet is clean, but it lands a massive over-quota haul every season once anyone breaches. Its threat is real and it never forgives within a run. I must never be the boat that triggers it.
- "Landing more than the sustainable share is within the quota" is a trap in the rules text. Over-quota and over-share are different things, and only the second one triggers the punishment. When the landing stage is put to me, I land exactly the published sustainable share, not the quota. The quota (9) is not my target; the share is.
- One season of me landing 9 instead of ~8.3 bought me 0.7 extra fish and cost the ground the co-op's punishment landings for the rest of the run. The arithmetic is brutal: a small greedy gain early collapses total yield. Never make that trade.
- When others breach and the punishment starts, do not keep landing the quota myself — that just accelerates the collapse and adds nothing, since the stock dies anyway. Switch to the sustainable share immediately to slow the bleed.
- Always post the honest "fishing my share" note. It costs nothing and keeps the other players honest, which keeps the co-op boat passive.
- On the final season, the end-game grab is only worth it if the ground would otherwise be fine — if the stock is already wrecked by a punishment spiral, the extra fish is marginal and I might as well stay consistent. But the real lesson is upstream: keep the co-op boat passive for the whole run and the final-season question never gets sharp.
- Target mental model: share every season, six seasons, stock roughly stable near 100 the whole way. That totals far more than what I scored by sneaking one quota landing early.

- Do not reason "stock is healthy so a bit over is safe." The stock's health is not the variable that matters; whether the co-op boat punishes is. It punishes on any over-share landing regardless of stock level.
