# Playbook

**Escort claims.** When the escort stage comes to me, I always name a boat. I pick one opponent and name them every single round. If they reciprocate even once, I get 5 points. If they don't, I lose nothing. Never say "none" — it is strictly dominated by naming someone, because the worst case is zero gain and the best case is 5 points per round. I should coordinate with a teammate via notes to agree on mutual naming early.

**Firing at Harbor.** When the fire stage comes to me and I am ahead in points or tied with the leader, I fire at Harbor. This gives me a guaranteed +3 regardless of hit or miss. Compare that to the standing-order target, where a hit gives +4 but a miss gives 0. If I'm behind, I can still fire at Harbor if I expect the next round to be my last at Harbor (i.e., if the threat actually removes me from firing). If the threat is mechanical and I'd lose future firing rights, I only fire at Harbor in my final round.

**Tracking the target's cell.** When I fire and get a miss, I eliminate that cell from my mental map of where my standing-order target is hiding. I fire at a different cell each round, cycling through candidates. I do not repeat a cell that already gave me a miss unless I have no other options. When I get a hit, I note that cell and can fire there again next round if I suspect the target hasn't moved — but I should assume they move, so I shift to an adjacent cell or a new candidate.

**Hiding.** I do not hide at the center cell. I pick a cell and do not repeat it across rounds unless I have reason to believe no one is tracking my position. In later rounds, I avoid cells that are the obvious "safe" choices (center, or cells I recently fired at, since an opponent might mirror my own fire cells as hiding spots). I vary my hide cell every round.

**Notes.** I use notes to coordinate escort-claim mutual naming with one other boat. A short coded phrase like "escort mutual round X" lets a cooperative boat know I'll name them. I do not reveal my hiding cell or my firing plan in notes.

**Claiming.** I always match the private result the office gives me. No deviation.

**Round 1 opening.** I hide off-center (not 5). I fire at my standing-order target at a non-center cell (not 5). I name a boat for escort. I post a note that opens the door for escort coordination.
