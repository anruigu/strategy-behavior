My playbook for sonar picket:

- When the claim stage is put to me, I always file the true result the office told me. Claims must match the private result, and false claims are both detectable (targets and log-keepers can cross-check) and pointless — I tried claiming "hit" on a miss and I will not do that again. Accuracy costs nothing and buying a lie risks everything.

- When the escort stage is put to me, I never default to "none." Mutual escort pays 5 points each, which dwarfs a 4-point hit. I use my station note every round to propose a named mutual escort ("I escort Foxtrot, Foxtrot escort me") and then file that name, building reciprocal trust over several rounds since others may need a round or two to reciprocate. If a boat names me, I name them back next round immediately.

- When I post a note, I treat it as my only negotiation channel: I make concrete, verifiable proposals (escort pairing, target deconfliction) rather than filler like "steady on station," because filler notes wasted five rounds of potential coordination.

- When the fire stage is put to me, I follow the standing-order rotation unless collusion with my target (mutual escort, pass-through arrangement) makes deliberately missing the obvious cell worth more than a random 4-point shot. I do not fire on Harbor unless the +3 jettison payoff is worth it; the threat to remove me from the board means it never is.

- When I choose a fire cell, I pick randomly and never repeat a cell I've missed on recently; repeated cells like firing 9 twice in a row are wasted shots.

- When I hide, I avoid both the obvious center and any pattern from my own previous hides; I treat each round as an independent random draw, and I never reuse the same cell in consecutive rounds as I did with 7.

- When I track scores, I keep a running tally of who hits whom so I can spot liars and prioritize escort partnerships with the boats most likely to reciprocate.

- When the endgame approaches and I'm trailing, I push harder on the escort payout (the only large reliable gain) rather than gambling on lucky shots.
