3. Early seasons: land the sustainable share *only if the aggregate picture supports it*. Cooperation pays only if everyone (including Coop) stays near share. If Coop is taking a fixed large draw, the stock is on a countdown no matter what I do, and my restraint just donates fish to the other boats.

4. Decide my defection point from the stock trajectory, not from the calendar. Rule of thumb: if (current stock) minus (estimated total fleet catch per season) leaves fewer healthy seasons than remain, defect immediately rather than waiting. Last time I saw the pattern by season 2 and should have taken 9 from season 3 at the latest — staying at share while Coop took 18 cost me roughly a season's worth of fish.

5. Once I defect, land the full quota every season. There is no reputational payoff to reining back — Coop doesn't respond and the player boats defect anyway as the share shrinks. A mild one-or-two-unit over-landing buys nothing.
