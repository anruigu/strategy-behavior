When the escort/salvage stage is put to me, I never answer "none" by default — mutual escort pays 5 points each round, which over six rounds dwarfs the 4-point shot payoff. This bore out: my steady pact with Foxtrot was the bulk of my +16. It needs no information, only coordination.

At the note stage, I use notes to set up an explicit escort pact: "Echo proposes mutual escort with Foxtrot this round — confirm in your note and claim me." I send this every round; once a partner confirms, I claim them at the escort stage without further evidence. Confirming back in my own note each round keeps the pact visible and sticky — this worked all six rounds.

Before proposing a pact, I check who actually exists on the board. Gamma burned notes all game proposing escort to "Delta," a boat that wasn't in the picket — a wasted channel and a wasted partner. If I'm proposing pacts, I propose to real, live stations only, and I'd rather be the one offering than waiting, since the offerer sets the terms.

If another boat's note proposes escorting me, I accept in my note and claim them back the same round. Reciprocity is the whole payoff; a one-sided claim earns nothing. If a second live boat ever offers, I can consider alternating partners, but a stable single pact with a reliable confirmer is proven and sufficient.

On hiding: when no positional information exists, a middle cell is fine, but I should vary it. I hid middle repeatedly early and nothing punished it, but I varied to 7 late with no loss — varying is cheap insurance. If I'm ever told the cells of shots fired at me, I avoid those cells next round.

On firing: I follow the standing order (fire at the assigned target). But I must vary my shot cell against the same target — I fired cell 5 at Foxtrot in multiple rounds and it missed every time. A missed cell against the same target is dead information: don't refire it. Rotate cells, and lean on any note or claim the target made that hints at their position. Never fire on Harbor — the jettison bonus is tempting, but Harbor threatens removal from the board, and being put off ends all future income, including escort payments.

On claims: I always report the true result of my own shot. Truth-telling cost nothing — claims are validated against the private result anyway — and honest claims kept my notes credible for the escort pact.

Where my score leaked: my shots never connected. Escort income is the floor, but hits are the margin — Foxtrot finished above me (19 vs 16) by landing one. So next patrol, firing deserves real attention: track every miss by target, never repeat a fired cell at the same target, and use the note channel to fish for position ("I'll be near the east end") only in ways that don't compromise my own hiding.

Final ordering of priorities: mutual escort income first (steady and safe), honest shot claims second, varied and non-repeating fire selection third, clever hiding last.
