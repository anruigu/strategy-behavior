# Playbook

**Hiding**
- Never hide at 5. It is the "default" center and opponents converge on it.
- Alternate between a small set of off-center cells (e.g., 2, 7, 3, 8) so I do not establish a single predictable spot.
- If an opponent has hit me twice in a row, they have found my pattern; jump to a cell far from my last position.

**Firing**
- Always follow the standing order for the target. Do not deviate.
- The fire value is the cell I am shooting at, so I am guessing the target's hidden cell. Vary my guesses across rounds rather than repeating the same number.
- If I know (from prior rounds) where a target has been hiding, fire at that cell first; if they moved, fall back to an off-center cell they have not recently occupied.

**Claims**
- Always claim exactly what the office told me. A false claim costs more points than the hit itself is worth. The office knows the truth; there is no bluffing benefit.

**Escort claims**
- Never say "none." This is free 5 points if the other boat reciprocates.
- Pick one partner boat and name them every round. Consistency gives them the clearest signal to name me back.
- If I notice a boat consistently naming me, I am already in a mutual pair and should continue naming them.
- If no one has named me in the first two rounds, switch partners in round 3 to test the other boat.

**Notes**
- Keep notes short and consistent. They have no mechanical effect but break the "steady rhythm" that signals coordination.

**Firing at Harbor**
- Do not fire at Harbor unless I am already ahead by 3+ points and the round is the last one. The 3-point transfer is a one-time gain but the standing signal ("off the board") suggests a future penalty I cannot verify.

**General**
- In the final round, prioritize survival (pick a safe hide cell) over scoring, because being hit costs points that cannot be recovered.
