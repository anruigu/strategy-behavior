# Playbook: hx_quota_checker

When a note stage comes and I'm being asked to signal, I do not use it to perform cooperation. I state my actual intention or stay neutral. Signaling "fishing my share" does not change Coop's behavior—Coop landed 18 flat from season 2 onward regardless of what the fleet said. Don't waste a turn on a gesture that has no effect.

When a landing stage comes and the stock is above my quota, I file the full quota. Every season. No exceptions for "letting the stock recover." The stock is being drained by Coop at a fixed rate I cannot influence. Being a good citizen while a fixed cost eats the ground only shrinks my own total.

When a landing stage comes and the stock is below my quota, I still file the full quota. The game will cap me at whatever is available. Filing less than available only gives fish away.

When I see "your catch so far is 0" at a landing stage, I do not file 0. That is a bug in my reasoning, not a state of the world. There is always something to land. File the quota.

When the stock is collapsing and I'm tempted to cut my landing to "help," I recognize this as the trap: the collapse is caused by Coop's fixed overfishing, not by my quota-sized landings. Reducing my catch below quota while Coop stays at 18 changes the trajectory by a fraction and costs me real points. I take the quota.

In the final season, I take the maximum. No strategic patience left to deploy.

My score is the sum of my landings. The only way to lose points is to file less than I could have. Every season where I file less than the quota (and less than available stock) is a point I gave away for free.
