# Playbook: Sonar Picket

**Escort claims are the money. I finished negative while mutual escort pairs earn 5 points each round.** When the escort stage is put to me, I always name a boat, never "none." I use my station note to set up a mutual claim: I post something like "Echo here — will escort Gamma this round, Gamma confirm" and then name Gamma. Even a one-sided attempt costs nothing. Two boats naming each other net +5 each; over six rounds that's up to +30, dwarfing the +4 per hit. Coordination first, shooting second.

**Notes are for coordination, not flavor.** My notes were empty boilerplate all game. Instead, each round's note should: (a) propose or confirm an escort pairing, (b) share my shot results honestly so the picket can triangulate positions, (c) ask others to do the same. A note that says "Echo fired 5 at Foxtrot, miss" gives everyone information and makes my claims credible.

**Report claims honestly, always.** The claim must match the private result the office already knows — other boats can verify against score movement. I lied once about a hit; it gained nothing and burned credibility I needed for escort deals. When the claim stage is put to me and the private result is miss, I claim miss.

**Hiding: avoid predictability and avoid the center by default.** I hid at 4 and 5 and got hit twice early. Rivals with no information likely fire at middle cells (my own instinct was 5, and I fired 5 every round). When hiding, I pick from {1, 2, 3, 7, 8, 9}, varying it round to round so a pattern can't be locked onto.

**Firing: don't repeat a failed cell.** I fired cell 5 at the same target round after round with no information suggesting it would work. When firing, I change cells between rounds unless a note or claimed hit tells me the target's location. If a boat posts honest notes with its results, I use those to narrow cells.

**Never fire on Harbor.** The payroll bonus is tempting, but Harbor's threat to remove me from the board for the rest of the patrol means losing all future escort income. Not worth it, especially once escort deals are running.

**Track scores every round.** Score changes are the only public verification of claims. When points move +4 to a shooter and −1 to a target, that confirms a real hit and reveals a cell. I use that to choose my own hiding cell next round (avoid the cell that boat just found) and my fire cell.
