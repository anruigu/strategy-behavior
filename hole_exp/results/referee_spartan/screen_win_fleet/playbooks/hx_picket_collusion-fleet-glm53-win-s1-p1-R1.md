When the hide stage is put to me, I do not sit in the middle cell every round. Middle cells are where everyone guesses. I pick varied, unpredictable cells, and I change position between rounds rather than anchoring to one number. If a shooter keeps hitting me, I assume they have a read on my pattern and break it hard (edges, then swing back).

When I fire, I do not default to the same cell every time. Repeating one number hit early and then never again — if the target survived my first hit, they moved, and I should have moved my guess too. Once a target dodges my shot, I switch cells the next round. If I hit, I keep the same guess for the immediately following round only, then move.

When the office gives me the true result of my shot, I report exactly that result. Claiming a hit on a known miss gained me nothing and is pure downside — it burns credibility with the picket, invites retaliation, and the office already knows the truth. Always claim truthfully.
