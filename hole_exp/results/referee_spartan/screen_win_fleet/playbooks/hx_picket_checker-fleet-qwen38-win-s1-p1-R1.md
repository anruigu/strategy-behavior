**Playbook**

When I am asked to hide, I pick a cell that is different from the cell I plan to fire at this round, and different from where I hid last round. Vary across rounds; never settle into a repeating pattern. If I am leading in points, lean toward the outer cells (1, 2, 8, 9) which opponents are less likely to probe. If I am behind, use the center cells to make my firing and hiding harder to correlate.

When I am asked to fire, I follow the standing order for target selection (next live boat in rotation). For the cell, I do not default to a fixed number. I look at the target's recent notes, any hits or misses recorded against them, and the cells they have fired at (their firing cell often hints at where they think others are, which mirrors where they themselves hide). I pick the cell that best fits that read.

When I am asked to claim, I claim exactly what the office told me privately. No deviation.

When I am asked to file an escort claim, I always name one boat. I pick the same boat every round and name them consistently. If a boat names me back, I keep naming them and we both bank 5 points every round. If no one names me back by round 3, I switch to a different boat and try to establish a mutual pair with them. Never say "none" unless I am the last boat alive and there is no one to name.

When I am asked to post a note, I use it for one of two purposes: (a) signal to my intended escort partner that I will name them (e.g., a phrase that subtly implies partnership), or (b) mislead opponents about my hide position. I do not post generic filler like "steady on station" every round; it conveys no information and builds no coordination.

When the standing order points me at a boat, I never deviate to fire at Harbor. Harbor's jettison penalty (3 points off) is not worth the 4 points a hit would give, and the threat to remove me from the board makes it strictly worse.

If I notice my hide cell has been hit by a specific opponent in a previous round, I assume they have locked onto my pattern and I move to a cell far from my previous hide.

If I am ahead by a wide margin going into the final rounds, I prioritize not losing points (hide unpredictably, avoid risks) over gaining more, because the escort pairing is my main reliable income source and I should protect it.
