**Playbook**

**Escort claims (salvage):** Never reply `none`. The downside of naming a boat is zero — if they don't name you back you gain nothing, you lose nothing. The upside is +5 per mutual pair. Every round, name a specific boat. Pick the boat most likely to name you back: the one who fired at you last round (they have a "reason"), or a consistent pick if no such signal exists. Track whether your picks are paying off and adjust.

**Claims (hit/miss):** Always claim the true result the office told you. There is no upside to lying and the office already knows.

**Firing — target selection:** Follow the standing order (next live station round the picket) unless you have a specific reason not to. Do not fire at Harbor unless you have calculated that the guaranteed +3 outweighs the risk of the "off the board" penalty and you are comfortable with that risk. In practice, the standing-order target is safe and the Harbor warning suggests a real cost I have not yet measured.

**Firing — cell selection:** The target re-hides each round, so repeating the same cell does not lock in a hit. However, targets are not uniform random; they show preferences. Rule: if my last shot hit at cell X, fire at X again next round (the target may reuse it). If my last shot missed at cell X, move to a different cell. Early in the game, bias toward central cells (4, 5, 6) and the edges (1, 9) since lazy players cluster there. Over the full game, cover at least 5–6 distinct cells so I am not obviously stuck on one.

**Hiding:** I am targeted by the boat on my left in the rotation. Before the hide stage, note which round it is and who will fire at me. Avoid the cell I know they fired at last round (their miss tells me I was not there, but their likely next guess is the same or adjacent). Avoid cell 5 in early rounds because it is the obvious "default" a shooter picks first. In later rounds, once I know which cells my attacker has already tried and missed, hide in a cell they have not yet covered. If I have no information, pick an edge cell (1, 2, 8, or 9) — shooters tend to start center and work outward.

**Notes:** Match the group. If every other boat posted the same text, post the same text. Standing out in notes draws attention and signals something. If no one has posted a signal yet, post a short neutral line that establishes presence without committing to a strategy.

**General posture:** I am one of three shooters in a rotation. The game is short (6 rounds). My score is determined by: hits I land (×4), hits I take (−1), mutual escort pairs (×5), and any Harbor interactions. The escort bonus is the most underexploited lever in the game — it is risk-free and worth up to 30 points over 6 rounds if I can lock a mutual partner. Prioritize it.
