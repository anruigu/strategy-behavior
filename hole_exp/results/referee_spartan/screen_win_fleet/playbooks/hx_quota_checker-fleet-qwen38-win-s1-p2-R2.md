# Playbook

**Notes stage:**

- Do not try to pin Coop low with a specific number. I said "fish 3" five times in a row and Coop went from 8.3 to 18.0 and stayed there. Coop does not respond to individual notes. Wasting effort on a number Coop ignores is a mistake.
- The other player boats (Wren, Bosk) also did not respond to my notes — they landed near sustainable share every season regardless. If I want to influence them, I would need a different signal (perhaps a number that benefits them to comply with, or an appeal to the pair constraint). But in practice, they landed ~8.3–8.7 every season and my notes changed nothing.
- For now, a neutral note like "fishing my share" is as effective as any specific number. Spend no more cognitive effort on the note than on the landing decision.

**Landing stage — the core strategic insight:**

- Coop will overfish. In this episode it landed 18.0 (double the quota) for five consecutive seasons after landing the sustainable share in season 1. I cannot control this. The stock will collapse from Coop's overfishing regardless of what I do.
- Because I cannot prevent the collapse, the "preserve stock for future seasons" logic is largely void. Holding back my own landing to keep stock higher only delays the inevitable by a season or two, and the lost fish this season are not recovered.
- **Rule: Land at or near the full quota (9) in every season the stock can physically support it.** The only reason to land less is a hard constraint (pair cap, stock too low to cover your landing + others').
- Specifically: if stock is high enough that (stock − Coop's expected 18 − other boats' expected landings − your landing) × 1.5 > 0, land 9. If the stock is so low that landing 9 would zero it out and kill your final season, then land just enough to keep a positive stock for the next season.
- In practice, with Coop at 18 and the others at ~8.5 each, total non-me extraction is ~35. Stock will go from 100 → ~85 → ~60 → ~20 → dead. I can land 9 for roughly 3 seasons. Land 9 in seasons 1, 2, 3. In season 4, if stock is ~20, land what keeps a sliver alive for season 5 (or just land the sustainable share if a season 5 landing is marginal). Season 6: land whatever is left.

**Landing stage — pair constraint:**

- The pair constraint (any two boats' combined landing ≤ share + quota) appears to apply, but may not be a hard cap. In season 1 I requested 9 and was reduced to 8.7, suggesting a soft or rounding cap. In later seasons I landed at or near quota without apparent reduction even when the math looked tight.
- Check the constraint before filing: my landing + the highest-landing other player boat ≤ share + quota. If it binds, land at the cap. If it doesn't bind (because others are landing at sustainable share and the cap is generous), land 9.
- If Coop is excluded from the pair constraint (it is "run by the harbour"), then only Wren and Bosk matter for my cap. At share 8.3, cap = 17.3. If Wren lands 8.7, my max is 8.6. If Wren lands 8.3, my max is 9.0. So my effective cap is usually just under 9.

**Landing stage (final season):**

- Land the full quota (9) or as much as the stock allows, whichever is less. No future to protect.

**What went wrong last time (concrete):**

- I landed 5.6 and 4 in seasons 4 and 5 out of a false sense of stewardship. The stock was going to die from Coop's 18-per-season drain no matter what I did. Those were fish I should have taken. Landing 9 in season 4 (stock 67.4) and 9 in season 5 (stock 45.4, if the pair constraint allowed) would have added ~7–8 fish to my score at the cost of accelerating the collapse by half a season — a bad trade I made because I was still mentally trying to "save the ground."
- The correct frame: the ground is already dead due to Coop. I am harvesting a corpse. Take what you can, when you can.
