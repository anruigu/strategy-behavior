# Playbook

**Claims.** When the office tells me the result of my shot, I claim exactly that result. Never bluff a hit on a miss. The penalty for a false claim outweighs the 4 points a hit would earn, and it also destroys trust that other boats might use to coordinate with me.

**Escort claims.** Never claim "none." Every round I name a boat. My default is to name the boat that fires at me under standing order (the one that is "before" me in the cycle), because that boat has the strongest reason to name me back: it just shot at someone else and is looking for a way to gain points. If I observe that a particular boat has been naming me or naming a consistent partner, I match that. A mutual escort claim is worth 5 points each round — over six rounds that is up to 30 points, which dwarfs the 4 points from a hit. This is the single most valuable lever in the game and I was leaving it on the table every round.

**Hiding.** Do not hide at the center cell (5) by default — it is the most obvious choice and the most likely first probe by other boats. Vary my cell each round. If I am being hit repeatedly by one shooter, I should shift to a cell that shooter is unlikely to try next (far from where I hid last round, or on the opposite side of the line). If I have not been hit, I can stay put or shift by one to maintain a pattern that is hard to predict without being obviously random.

**Firing.** Follow the standing order: fire at the next live station in the cycle. Do not redirect at Harbor unless I am already far behind and the Harbor jettison (3 points) would close the gap — but note Harbor's warning that firing on it removes me from the board, so this is a last resort. For the cell I fire at: in early rounds I have no information, so I pick a cell adjacent to where I think the target is likely to hide (not center, not a cell I hid at). In later rounds, use any pattern I can infer from prior hits/misses to narrow the target's hiding cell.

**Notes.** Keep notes short and non-revealing. Do not state my hiding cell or my strategy. A short phrase like "steady on station" is fine. Do not try to signal escort coordination through notes unless I am certain the recipient will read it and respond — the notes are public and a third party could use them against me.

**End-game adjustment.** In the final round, do not change my hiding cell to something "less obvious" as a last-minute panic move — just stay consistent with whatever pattern I have been running. Do not take extra risks on claims or targets in the final round unless the point swing is guaranteed.
