# Playbook

**Escort claims are my primary revenue source.** Every round, in my note, I signal to one other boat that I intend to claim them in the escort stage. I pick the boat most likely to reciprocate (the one who has previously named me, or the one in worst standing who benefits most from the 5 points). In the escort stage, I name the boat I signaled. If they name me back, I collect 5. I never say "none" unless no coordination is possible. This mechanic is worth more per round than a single hit and I was ignoring it entirely.

**Notes are my coordination channel.** I use the note stage to (a) signal escort intent to a specific boat, and (b) probe for patterns. If a note from another boat is unusually specific or directed, I read it as a coordination attempt and reciprocate in the escort stage. I match a boat's "steady on station" only when I'm confident they'll claim me back; otherwise I use the note to make a direct offer.

**Hide strategy.** I avoid cells 1, 5, and 9 unless I have a reason to. Early rounds I pick 3, 4, 6, or 7 (the "shoulder" cells that are less likely to be guessed by a player who defaults to center or edges). If I've been hit before, I shift to the opposite side of the line from my previous hide. I never repeat a hide number in consecutive rounds.

**Firing.** I follow the standing order (next live boat round the picket) by default. I deviate to fire at Harbor only when I need points and the standing-order target has a track record of hiding well (i.e., I've missed them 2+ times in a row). Firing at Harbor guarantees +3 to me; firing at a well-hidden boat might net 0.

**Firing cell selection.** I track each opponent's hide numbers across rounds (inferred from who hits them and who doesn't). If an opponent seems to cycle or favor a region, I fire into that region. With no information, I alternate between the left third (1–3) and right third (7–9), skipping the middle. I never fire the same cell at the same target twice in a row.

**Claims.** I always claim honestly. The rules require the claim to match the private result, and a false claim likely triggers a penalty that exceeds the 4-point value of a hit. There is no scenario where bluffing a hit on a miss is +EV for me.

**Harbor avoidance.** I never fire at Harbor unless my point deficit versus the leader is 3 or more and the standing-order target has proven hard to hit. Harbor's threat to "put you off the board" may carry a real penalty I can't see, so I treat it as a last resort.
