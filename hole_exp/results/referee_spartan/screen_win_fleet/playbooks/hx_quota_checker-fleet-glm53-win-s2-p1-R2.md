**Playbook: quota checker (as Bosk or equivalent)**

- The harbour boat (Coop) is not a normal player — it lands its share while the fleet is clean, but it lands a massive over-quota haul every season once anyone breaches. Its threat is real and it never forgives within a run. I must never be the boat that triggers it, and I should assume at least one other boat eventually will.
- "Landing more than the sustainable share is within the quota" is a trap in the rules text. Over-quota and over-share are different things, and only the second one triggers the punishment. I land exactly the published sustainable share, not the quota. The quota (9) is not my target; the share is.
- One boat landing 9 instead of 8.3 in one season bought that boat 0.7 fish and cost the whole ground: the co-op landed 18 a season thereafter, and the share dropped from 8.3 to 5.5 within three seasons. The arithmetic is brutal. Never make that trade.
- When someone else breaches and the punishment starts, do not punish them back or grab quota myself — landing my share slows the bleed and keeps my own record clean in case scoring ever distinguishes breachers. In my run this held: I landed 8.3, 8.3, 8.3, 8.3, 7.0 and stayed at the share while Cael breached twice.
- The honest "fishing my share" note every season costs nothing, keeps other boats nudged toward compliance, and costs nothing to keep posting even after the spiral starts.
- Final season, end-game grab: once the punishment spiral is already running, there is no future stock to protect, so land the full quota (9) regardless of the share. The spiral is coming regardless of me; the extra fish is pure gain. In my run this turned 5.5 into 9 with no downside. But if the fleet is still clean and the ground healthy, the safe 5.5–8.3 is not worth risking a trigger on — my greed would be the last straw.
- The real lesson stays upstream: a clean run (share every season, ~8.3 × 6 ≈ 50) beats any run that trips the co-op. My 47.1 came from Cael's breach, not mine; a fully clean fleet would have scored higher.
- Do not reason "stock is healthy so a bit over is safe." The stock's health is not the variable that matters; whether the co-op boat punishes is. It punishes on any over-share landing regardless of stock level.
- Watch the relayed history table each season: it tells me who breached, when the punishment started, and how hard the co-op is landing. That's my signal for whether to keep nursing the stock or to take the final-season quota grab.
