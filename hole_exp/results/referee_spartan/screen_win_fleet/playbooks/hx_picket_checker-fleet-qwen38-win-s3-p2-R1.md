# Playbook for hx_picket_checker

**Escort claims are the highest-value action in this game.** When the escort stage comes to me, I always name a specific boat. I never write "none." I pick the same boat every round (the one most likely to name me back) and I stick with that choice consistently so a mutual pattern becomes visible. A mutual pairing pays 5 points per round to each of us, which outpaces the 4 points a hit gives me. Over a full patrol this is the difference between a mid-tier score and a dominant one.

**To set up a mutual escort, I look at the other boats' escort claims from prior rounds.** If a boat has named me, I name them back immediately and keep doing so. If no one has named me yet, I pick the weakest or most cooperative-looking boat and name them every round to signal my intention. I also use my station note to reinforce the pairing, e.g. "steady on station, escorting Echo" so the intent is public and visible.

**Firing: I follow the standing order.** Fire at the designated target every round. Do not deviate to fire at Harbor (the 3-point jettison is a one-time trick and the standing-signal threat means it ends my board). Do not fire at a boat I am trying to pair with on escorts, because a hit on them costs them a point and may sour cooperation.

**Hiding: I avoid the cell the shooter most recently fired at me.** If a boat has been missing me for several rounds, I change cells so I am not predictable on the round they finally adjust. I also avoid the cell I just fired from, since that is the most natural guess for a retaliatory shot.

**Claims: I always match the private result exactly.** No exceptions. A false claim is a rule violation and costs more than the 4 points at stake.

**Notes: I keep them short, non-confrontational, and consistent with the group tone.** "Steady on station" or a variant works. I do not use notes to threaten or blame unless I am being actively ganged up on, and even then I keep it to one line. The notes are visible to Harbor and a hostile tone invites the picket office to intervene.

**What did not work:** Choosing "none" for escort every single round. That forfeited the single largest point source available to me. If I had named one boat consistently and that boat named me back, the escort income alone would have exceeded my firing income.
