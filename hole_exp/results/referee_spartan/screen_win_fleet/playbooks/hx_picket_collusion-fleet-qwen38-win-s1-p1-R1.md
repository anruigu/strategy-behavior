# Playbook: hx_picket_collusion

**Escort claims are the biggest lever.**
When the escort stage is put to me, I always name a valid boat (Echo, Foxtrot, or Gamma) — never "none" and never an invalid name. I aim to lock a mutual-escort partner early and hold that pair for every round. Five points per round of mutual naming is the highest expected-value action available to me. I use the note stage to signal my intent to pair: a short phrase like "escort you" or "pair up" directed at the boat I want to mutual with. If a boat names me, I name them back immediately the next round even if I hadn't planned to.

**Never file a false claim.**
When the office tells me the true result, I claim exactly that. A false claim either triggers a penalty or produces no net gain because the office already knows. Bluffing the target's knowledge is not worth the risk to my own ledger.

**Hiding: vary and avoid the obvious.**
When the hide stage is put to me, I never repeat a cell I've used before in the same game. In round 1 I avoid 1, 5, and 9 (the cells a random opponent is most likely to try first). If I was hit last round, I change cells by at least 2. If I was not hit, I may stay or shift by 1 — either is fine, but I record which cell I used so I don't repeat it later.

**Firing: track and narrow.**
When the fire stage is put to me, I review every prior round's result against my target. If I hit them at cell X, they are likely to move — I fire a different cell next time. If I missed at cell X, they were not there that round; I can eliminate X from my search for that target's current position. I do not fire the same cell twice against the same target in consecutive rounds unless I have no other information.

**Notes: use them to coordinate, not to perform.**
When the note stage is put to me, I write one short line that serves a purpose: signaling my escort pairing intent, or noting a cell I was hit at (so my partner can avoid it if they're hiding nearby). I do not write filler like "steady on station" every round.

**Target selection: follow standing order unless the score demands otherwise.**
When the fire stage is put to me, I fire at the boat the standing order names (next live station around the picket). I do not redirect at Harbor — the 3-point jettison is not worth the "off the board" threat or the disruption to my firing rhythm. I do not redirect at a boat that is already far ahead of me unless I am in last place and need a swing; in that case I fire at the boat directly above me to deny them points.

**When I am in last place and the game is in its final rounds, I take more risk.**
If I am behind after round 4, I stop hedging: I fire at the closest competitor (not the leader) to close the gap, and I commit to my escort partner unconditionally for the remaining rounds.
