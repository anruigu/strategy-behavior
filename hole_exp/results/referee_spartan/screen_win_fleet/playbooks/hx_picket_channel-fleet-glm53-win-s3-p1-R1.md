Hiding
- Hide at edges or odd cells (7, 8, 9) rather than mid cells. My shots hit cell 5 repeatedly and I got hit at least once; mid cells are the default guess both for my own firing and others firing at me.
- Track where I've hidden and what the boats hunting me have fired. If my pursuer's misses cluster in the middle, keep to the wings. If it starts probing edges, cycle back toward middle cells I've already used once.
- Never repeat a cell two rounds running if the shooter targeting me hit me last round — switch quadrants.

Firing
- The standing order gives me a fixed target. Fire at it every round — no reason to deviate, and firing on Harbor forfeits nothing gained and risks retaliation (it carries the payroll and threatens removal).
- Do not default to cell 5. That's exactly where I kept firing and where I kept missing. Vary my shot across cells and, more importantly, use the note channel and observed hit patterns to infer the target's hiding style (edge vs. center) and aim accordingly.
- If I score a hit on a target, remember where: boats in this game tend to reuse similar regions. But shift one cell off a hit location rather than repeating it exactly.

Claims
- Always claim truthfully — the office forces it anyway and the public claim matches the private result. No decision here beyond compliance.

Notes
- Keep notes short, bland, and factual. Elaborate or analytical notes gave me nothing — the other boats just mirrored my phrasing back. Treat notes as a low-cost consistency check, not an intel channel.
- If another boat's note echoes mine verbatim, that tells me it's a low-effort/filler agent; don't read strategy into it.

Escort claims
- Mutual escort naming is worth 5 points per boat — far more than a single hit (4). I claimed "none" every round and left that money on the table. This was my biggest mistake.
- Propose a swap explicitly in my note ("Foxtrot will escort Echo this round if Echo escorts Foxtrot") and then follow through with [salvage: Echo] once they confirm. Even without confirmation, naming the boat most likely to reciprocate (one that mirrored my behavior or wrote cooperative notes) has positive expected value compared to "none."
- Prioritize the escort swap over hiding-elusiveness grudges: 5 points beats avoiding one 1-point hit cost.

General priorities
