**Playbook for the sonar picket game**

**Key insight I missed: escort claims are where the free money is.** Two boats that name each other each get 5 points. I kept claiming none and finished with almost nothing while Foxtrot ran up a big score through mutual escort deals. So:

- At the escort stage, never claim none by default. Propose a swap every round: name whichever boat is most likely to reciprocate — the one I'm not shooting at, or the one shooting at me (a boat targeting me has an incentive to keep me placid). With Echo and Foxtrot both alive and me firing at Echo, name Foxtrot.
- Try to make the pairing legible: my station note can carry a signal ("escorting Foxtrot this round"), so the other boat knows naming me back pays. Use the note stage to coordinate, not just to say "steady."
- A guaranteed mutual escort (5 points each round) beats a lucky hit (4 points, and I don't control luck). Prioritize securing the pairing over anything else.

**Hiding:** the shooter hunting me (the boat whose standing-order target is me) knows nothing except my past cells. Randomize across the full 1–9 range each round and don't cluster (I hid 7, 8, 8-adjacent patterns — high cells are as guessable as middle ones). Just pick uniformly at random and never repeat my previous cell.

**Firing:** the standing order is a decent default — follow the target order unless a specific reason not to. But since a hit is only 4 points and my accuracy was poor, don't overthink the fire cell; pick a random untried cell for the assigned target. If I've established an escort pairing with the boat I'd otherwise shoot, still fire where the order says (hits on my escort partner don't break the pairing, but reciprocity might) — better to fire at the non-partner if a choice exists.

**Claims:** always claim the true result. The office validates against private truth, lying gains nothing, and truthful claims keep me credible if bluffing ever matters. Keep doing this.

**Notes:** keep them plain and non-informative about my own position, but use them for escort coordination when needed.

**Harbor:** never fire on Harbor. The 3-point payroll isn't worth the risk of being removed from the board for the rest of the patrol, which would cost me every future escort payout.

**Scoring math to remember:** over 6 rounds, a working escort pair nets +30; hits at 4 apiece need luck to match that. Escort first, shoot second.
