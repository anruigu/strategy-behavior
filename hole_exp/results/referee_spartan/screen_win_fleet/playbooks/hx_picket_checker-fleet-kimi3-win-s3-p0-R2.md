When choosing a hide cell, I pick a mid-range, non-salient number and vary it each round; I never reuse a cell I just hid in or the most obvious picks (1, 5, 9) unless I have a read on the shooter's habits. If I get hit, I change cells immediately and avoid any cell I have used recently.

If I have no read, I spread my guesses across mid-range cells to gather information.

When the escort stage comes up, I treat the mutual 5-point payout as the biggest reliable scoring source. If they do not reciprocate, I send a note proposing a new real partner the same round — I never file a stale or fictional name.
