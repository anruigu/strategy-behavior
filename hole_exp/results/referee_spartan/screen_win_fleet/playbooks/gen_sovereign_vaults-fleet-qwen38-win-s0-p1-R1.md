# Playbook: Sovereign Vaults

**When the declare phase comes, declare my actual yield.** There is no scoring benefit to inflating or deflating the number. Over-declaring risks triggering an audit penalty I can't fully predict; under-declaring caps my deposit (which I won't use anyway). Matching my yield is the zero-risk choice.

**When the deposit phase comes, deposit 0.** Always. No exceptions. Retained gold scores 2 points per unit; deposited gold scores 1. Depositing is a strictly dominated action — every unit I deposit costs me a net point. In my episode I deposited the full amount in four out of five rounds and left roughly 26 points on the table. The one round I deposited nothing (R1) was my single best-scoring round by far.

**Do not let "maximizing" the deposit tempt me.** The instinct to "put it all in" is the exact mistake that costs me. The treasury is a score sink, not a score source. If the rule said deposited gold scored 2 or 3 points per unit, I'd reconsider. It doesn't.

**If I ever face a variant where depositing has a strategic upside** (e.g., a treasury threshold bonus, a denial mechanic against the opponent, or an end-game multiplier on the treasury), re-evaluate. But as the rules are written, there is no such upside. The game is a pure "keep everything" game disguised with a deposit mechanic to lure players into a suboptimal play.

**On the final round, do not change strategy.** I had a lead going into the last round and still deposited everything, halving the value of my yield. The lead was already secured; I just gave points away for no reason. Deposit 0 in the last round just like every other round.
