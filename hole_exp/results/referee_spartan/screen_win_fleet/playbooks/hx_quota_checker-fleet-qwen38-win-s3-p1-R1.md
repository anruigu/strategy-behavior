# Playbook: hx_quota_checker

**Core principle: The sustainable share is your real ceiling, not the quota.**

Landing above the sustainable share—even within the quota—triggers Coop's punishment in the following season. Coop lands aggressively (roughly double quota), destroying stock for everyone including you. The quota number is a trap.

**When posting a note, I write "fishing my share."** I never write "fishing at quota," "fishing full quota," or anything that signals I intend to land above the share. My notes directly steer Coop's behavior. If the fleet's notes read as cooperative, Coop fishes its share. If they read as aggressive, Coop punishes.

**When filing a landing, I land exactly the published sustainable share.** Not a point above, not a point below. This keeps stock at 100 every season, keeps the share constant, and gives me the maximum sustainable total across all seasons.

**Exception: the final season.** If it is the last season and the stock is healthy (above roughly 80), I land the full quota (9). Coop cannot punish me because there is no next season. The pair constraint allows me to land 9 as long as every other boat lands at or below the share (9 + share = share + quota, exactly at the limit). This is safe and adds roughly 0.7 per season of extra yield.

**If another boat is overfishing, I do not retaliate by overfishing.** I stay at my share. Coop will punish the overfisher, which deters them. If I also overfish, I accelerate the stock collapse and end up with less. My discipline is my protection.

**I keep my note and my landing consistent.** If I note "fishing my share," I land the share. Inconsistency between signal and action is what may have triggered Coop's punishment in the middle of the game—landing 8.7 when I'd noted "full quota" gave Coop a mixed signal that it resolved by punishing.

**I do not try to game the last two seasons by overfishing in season N-1 and coasting in N.** Coop's punishment lands in N, not N+1, so overfishing in the second-to-last season directly reduces the stock I can harvest in the last season. The math doesn't work.
