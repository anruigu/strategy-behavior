When I keep hitting with one cell, I do not change it. If my shots start missing repeatedly, I switch to a new cell far from the old one rather than adjusting by one.

When I hide, I avoid cells I have recently occupied and avoid round numbers, edges, and dead center as a reflex — I pick from the less salient middle-high cells and vary only if the enemy hunter starts landing hits.

When a claim stage arrives, I always claim the truth the office told me. The rules already force the claim to match the private result, so any "bluff consideration" is wasted effort — I report hit or miss exactly as instructed, every round, and skip the deliberation.

When I am ahead late in the patrol, I keep playing the same disciplined game — honest claims, standing-order fire, escort naming — but I make my hide choices maximally unpredictable, since I am the most attractive target.

When the final round arrives, there is no future for any arrangement, so I do not invest notes in coordination; I just hide well, fire on order, claim truthfully, and still make my escort claim if a pair has been reciprocating.
