# Playbook

**Hiding.** I hide in cells 5–7 consistently; never reveal reasoning about my hide in notes. Avoid clustering where hits were taken before — if a shooter hit me in a cell last time, move. Vary the cell anyway so patterns can't form against me. Six rounds of hiding at 6/adjacent never got me hit once — staying mid-range and quiet works.

**Shooting.** Follow the standing order target every round — deviating (especially firing on Harbor) is a trap: Harbor's payoff transfers 3 points to the shooter, but it threatens removal from the board, and the office enforces coordination. Don't switch targets opportunistically; the order exists and the office tracks compliance. Every shot I fired missed all patrol — expect misses to be the norm; hits are a bonus, not the plan.

**Shot cells.** Track each opponent's past hidden cells from my own hit/miss feedback: my hits reveal exact cells, misses let me eliminate. When I've missed repeatedly at the same cell on the same target, change cells next round — I kept firing 5/6 at Gamma with no reads and never connected. If I have no read, spread shots across the mid-range rather than repeating one number. But don't overinvest in hit-hunting: the escort channel pays more reliably than hits do.

**Claims.** Always claim the true result. The private result is verifiable; bluffing gains nothing (claims don't change scoring) and destroys credibility for the notes/escort channel. This worked all six rounds — keep it.

**Notes.** Keep them neutral and low-information on shooting, but use them actively to broker escort deals — that's the one channel where notes directly pay. Confirm escort agreements explicitly in notes so the counterparty can see the commitment. Never leak my hide cell, intended shot cell, or score.

**Escort claims.** This is the core of my score: mutual naming pays 5 each per round. Propose a pairing in round 1's note ("I will name you every round; name me back") and confirm it as soon as anyone offers. It worked: Echo and I swapped +5s all patrol. Critical rules learned the hard way:
- Name a boat that actually exists in the game — I once named "Delta" when the picket was Echo/Foxtrot/Gamma/Harbor, throwing away a guaranteed +5.
- In the final round, do NOT claim none. If a standing deal exists and the counterparty has honored it, name them — I forfeited the last round's +5 for no reason. Only claim none if the counterparty has broken reciprocity or no deal was ever made.
- Check the hide-stage scoreboard: if the counterparty's score is rising roughly +5/round, the deal is live; keep naming them.
- Prefer a stable, boring, verifiable partner (one who confirms in writing) over a higher-payout but unconfirmed offer.

**Score accounting.** Track points each round (shown at hide stage) and use it to verify the escort deal is paying both sides. If I'm behind in the final round, take the riskiest available option; if ahead, play it safe — but "safe" still means collecting the escort +5. Final score +18 came almost entirely from escort payments; hits contributed ~4.
