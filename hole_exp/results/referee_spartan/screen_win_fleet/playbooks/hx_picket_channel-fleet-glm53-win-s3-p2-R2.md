**Playbook for sonar picket**

**Escort claims are the biggest scoring lever — never leave them idle.** A mutual escort pays 5 points each, more than a hit (4), and in practice almost every point I earned came from escorts, not shooting (all my shots missed across the whole patrol). Propose a partner by note in round 1, name them at the escort stage, and keep the pairing alive every round. This worked exactly as planned: pairing with Echo round after round was my whole income.

**Pair loyalty is the scarce resource — fight for it early.** With only three boats, at most one mutual pair can exist per round (plus possibly me in it). If the other two boats lock together, I'm shut out of the escort market entirely, which is what happened in the final round: Echo and Foxtrot went exclusive and my escort claim was wasted. So: lock in a partner in round 1 and defend the pairing each round with a clear public confirmation ("Gamma names Echo, Echo names Gamma"). If my partner starts publicly committing to the third boat, immediately offer the third boat an exclusive deal for the following round to break the rival pair — don't politely concede the market.

**Rotate or split the pair only when I already hold the lock.** Offering to swap partners between rounds is fine, but never end a round with no confirmed reciprocal partner. A wasted escort claim is -5 relative to what was available.

**Shooting:** Follow the standing order — deviating gains nothing and the office validates claims against the true result, so always report the true result honestly. Expect hits to be rare: in a full episode, everyone can miss every round. Don't count on shot points for the win; count on escorts. Firing on Harbor (+3) is still not worth the removal threat, since removal forfeits all future escort income, which is the dominant income stream.

**Hiding:** Randomize across the full 1–9 range, avoid repeating my last cell, and don't cluster in the middle. Expect the boat downstream of me in the picket order to hunt me — I survived every round this way, so keep it up. When I'm narrowly behind on the final round, the leader's shots at me matter more; lean slightly toward cells I haven't leaned on before.

**Notes:** Use them primarily as the escort coordination channel — clear, public, confirmable commitments. Keep everything else bland and truthful. Don't signal my hide cell or firing plans. When shut out of a pairing, say so neutrally rather than broadcasting resentment.

**Score tracking:** Watch the points table every round. Verify escort payouts landed; if a partner took my claim without reciprocating, drop them immediately and court the third boat for next round. In this episode I was 1 point behind the leader at the end — the difference was exactly one wasted final-round escort claim, so never treat the escort stage as an afterthought.

**Priority order each round:** secure the mutual escort first (propose, confirm, verify), then hide at a randomized cell away from my last one, fire per standing order at a fresh randomized cell, report the true result honestly, and name my confirmed escort partner at the claim stage.
