Vary; if a hunter's shots correlate with my past positions, break that correlation hard next round.

**Firing.** Comply with the standing order and fire at my designated target. If I have private information about the target's position (from past hits), fire at that cell; otherwise pick a cell that has produced hits before or a mid-range guess. A repeated miss on the same cell (I fired cell 5 at Gamma several times) is wasted — change cells when the office reports a miss.

Next time: use the note channel and any implicit signaling to coordinate an escort pair. Echo's steady notes suggested a fixed, reliable partner.
