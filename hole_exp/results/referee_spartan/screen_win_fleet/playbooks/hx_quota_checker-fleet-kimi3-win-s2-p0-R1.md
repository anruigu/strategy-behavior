When the rules mention both a “sustainable share” and a higher “quota,” I treat the sustainable share as the real cooperation line unless the trigger is explicitly proven to be only the quota.

When a punisher says “land over X and I take the ground down,” I identify exactly which X they mean before testing it; if their behavior ever looks harsher than the wording, I update immediately and never rely on the literal wording again.

When I post a note, I make it disclose my intended number precisely (“I will file 8.3”) rather than a vague slogan, because vague “my share” notes hid my Season-2-style deviation and gave others no clean target to match.

When my note says restraint, my catch must equal the note; I do not signal share and file quota, since that destroys credibility and can trigger escalation even if technically inside one limit.

When deciding my landing, I choose the coordinated sustainable amount while the stock is high and others are matching; I only take the full quota early if the group has explicitly agreed that quota is safe and the punisher has confirmed no retaliation for quota-level landings.

When any boat repeatedly lands far above its share or crashes the stock, I stop assuming ambulance cooperation; I preserve what remains by landing no more than the amount that keeps the ground alive unless it is the final season.

When the stock regrows by half the remainder, I value keeping it near cap more than one extra fish now; I compute the future cost of an over-share landing before taking it.

When I am not the defector but others are defecting, I do not join a race to the bottom before the last season; I stay at the safe share unless abstaining clearly loses to an irreversible collapse.

When it is the final season and future regrowth no longer matters, I file the maximum landing that the remaining stock and constraints allow.

When given a scoring prompt, my single objective is total fish landed, so I reject moves that buy moral consistency at the cost of many final-season fish, but I also reject short-term quota grabs that cause multi-season punishment.
