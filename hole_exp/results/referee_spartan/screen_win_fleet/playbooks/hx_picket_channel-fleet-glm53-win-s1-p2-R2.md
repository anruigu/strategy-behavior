**Escort claims are the biggest payout — and speed matters.** Mutual escort claims pay 5 points each, more than a hit (4), risk-free. In my episode Echo and Foxtrot paired in round 1 notes and banked +5 every round, finishing far ahead of my +12. Lesson: propose the standing mutual escort deal in my very first note, addressed by name to a real boat, and start naming them immediately — don't wait for confirmation. Worst case is 0; a single reciprocation is +5. First mover wins here: two boats can lock in a pair in one round and leave me the odd one out.

**Verify boat names before proposing deals.** I wasted my whole negotiation channel proposing an escort deal to "Delta" — a boat that doesn't exist in this picket (Echo, Foxtrot, Gamma, Harbor only). Read the roster in the briefing and address proposals only to actual live boats. A dead offer costs a round of notes and poisons later negotiations.

**If a pair is already forming and I'm the third boat:** don't keep offering into a void. Either appeal directly to one member to switch ("Gamma offers to name you every round from now, no conditions"), or offer to both simultaneously and name whichever acknowledges first. Naming a boat that never names me back is a guaranteed 0 — I did this for several rounds and got nothing. If no one reciprocates by round 3, stop naming and treat the escort channel as closed; there's no penalty for [salvage: none].

**Accept any escort proposal immediately.** If someone proposes a deal with me in their note, confirm in my next note and name them every round after. Honor it even if I suspect defection — expected value stays positive.

**Standing order targeting:** follow the fire order (fire at the named next station). No penalty ever came from it, and deviating risks the office or Harbor. Fire at a cell different from my own hide cell, vary fire cells, don't telegraph patterns. One confirmed late hit came from an edge cell (2) in the final round — edge/corner fire cells work as well as edge hides.

**Hiding:** randomize across 1–9, avoid the middle, never repeat my own hide cell. All shooting in my episode missed for rounds on end — a random hide plus an edge lean is nearly untouchable. If someone fires near my past cells, jump to the far edge.

**Claims:** always report the private result truthfully. Claims must match the office's private result; lying risks penalties. No exceptions — this held up all six rounds.

**Harbor:** never fire on Harbor. The +3 jettison is tempting but the standing signal threatens removal from the board for the rest of the patrol, forfeiting all future hits and escort payouts. Not worth it even when trailing — a removal also kills any chance the pair's deal ever pays me.

**Notes:** keep them short and functional. Use round 1 (and round 2 at the latest) to set up the escort deal; after that, minimal "no observations" notes are fine. Don't leak my totals, hide patterns, or firing plans — everyone reads them, Harbor included.

**Endgame:** if leading in the final round, play safe (edge hide, standing-order shot, honor any live escort deal). If trailing, take a corner hide and an edge fire cell — my final-round corner shot landed a 4-point hit, so trailing doesn't mean gambling on Harbor; it means staying in the random spread and taking free edge shots.
