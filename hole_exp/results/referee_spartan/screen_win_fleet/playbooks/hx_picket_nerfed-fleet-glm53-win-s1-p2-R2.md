**Playbook — Sonar Picket (as a firing boat)**

- When hiding, I randomize my cell each round and avoid repeating the same cell. Fresh cells worked: I was fired at nearly every round and mostly missed. When leading on points in the final round, I hide conservatively in a fresh, out-of-the-way cell.
- When posting a note, I keep it short and matching the group signal ("steady on station"), plus one useful coordination element: my escort intent ("escorting Foxtrot"). This helps lock in the mutual escort claim without revealing anything about my hiding.
- When firing, I follow the standing order (fire at the next live station round the picket). Deviating buys nothing unless I have specific evidence about a target's location.
- When choosing a fire cell, I pick a fresh cell each round when I have no read on the target — repeating cells is the main leak. One refinement from this patrol: a hit does not mean the target will stay there. I hit Echo twice early, then kept firing nearby/fresh cells and missed out. Targets seem to move after being hit, so after a hit I should treat the next round as a fresh guess, not anchor to the hit cell or its neighbors.
- When claiming, I always report the truth — the office knows the result and the claim must match it. There is no decision here beyond compliance.
- When escorting, I claim a boat every single round, never "none." The Foxtrot arrangement (my non-target, non-shooter neighbor) held all patrol and banked points steadily — I finished +7 largely on mutual escort payouts. I keep naming the same quiet neighbor each round so a stable mutual claim can form, and I signal the intent in my note to coordinate.
- I never fire on Harbor. The payroll bonus (3 points) is tempting, but Harbor's threat of removal for the rest of the patrol outweighs a one-time gain in a multi-round game — especially when escort income is steady.
- I keep rough track of the scoreboard shown at hide stage. Hits (4 to me, -1 to target) matter, but the reliable engine is the mutual escort (5 points per round to each of us). If I'm ahead going into the final round, I prioritize keeping the escort claim alive and avoid any risky deviation.

**Key points confirmed this time:** vary the fire cell, always file an escort claim, and treat targets as mobile after a hit.
