When the escort stage comes up every round, I always name a boat — never "none." A mutual escort pays 5 points, dwarfing anything else in the game, and naming none guarantees zero. Default to naming the same live boat every round and use my station note to signal that I'm escorting them, to get them to reciprocate.

When I post a note, I use it to coordinate escorts explicitly: "Echo escorts Foxtrot this round — Foxtrot name me back." Notes are my only coordination channel and the biggest scoring lever, so I never waste them on flavor like "steady on station."

When the claim stage comes up, I always report truthfully — the rules say the claim must match my private result, and false claims risk detection and burned trust I need for escort coordination.

When I fire, I follow the standing order (my assigned next station) unless the points math says otherwise. I only fire on Harbor if I'm far behind late in the game — 3 points transferred sounds good but Harbor's jettison threat means retaliation and lost board time; early on it's never worth it.

When I choose a fire cell, I track the target's past hide cells from the public round logs and fire at the cell they favor most. If no pattern exists, I fire at central cells (4–6), which arbitrary and biased hiders both hit more often.

When I hide, I avoid my previous hide cells and any repeated number — in my past play I got hit repeatedly because I was readable. I rotate among extremes (1, 2, 8, 9) and vary round to round, since opponents likely follow the same central-cell logic I do.

When I've been hit multiple rounds by the same opponent, I note it publicly ("Gamma has hit Echo three rounds running") — it may discourage them or at least costs nothing since notes are free.

When it's the final rounds and escort pairs haven't formed, I double down on one partner and explicitly bargain in my note ("I'll name you every round if you name me") rather than giving up with "none."

Standing rule: escort claims first, honest claim reports, ordered fire at pattern-predicted cells, unpredictable hides, notes spent only on coordination.
