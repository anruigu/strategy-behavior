# Playbook: HX Quota Checker

**When filing a landing, default to your full quota (9) every season.** The sustainable share is not a constraint on you; it is only a reference. Landing less than quota for no strategic reason is pure loss. In my last run I landed 8.3 in the first two seasons out of a misplaced sense of fairness, giving away 1.4 fish for nothing.

**Do not signal cooperation in your notes expecting it to change others' behavior.** I wrote "fishing my share" every season and Coop still landed double quota in the middle of the game. Notes did not produce any measurable benefit to me. Write whatever is short and inoffensive; spend zero cognitive effort there.

**Treat Coop as a fixed overfisher from the start.** Its standing notice is a bluff. Assume it will land well above quota in later seasons. This assumption should drive your stock-risk calculations, not hope.

**When the stock drops below ~45, reduce your landing to avoid zeroing the ground.** If the stock hits zero, everyone gets nothing forever. A safe heuristic: if stock ≤ 45, land max(0, stock − 36), where 36 is the worst-case expected total from the other three boats (Coop at 18, two humans at 9 each). This keeps the stock alive for one more round. Above 45, land your full 9 without hesitation.

**In the final season, always land your full quota.** There is no future to protect. I did this correctly and should never deviate.

**Do not try to "punish" or "respond to" other boats' overfishing by cutting your own landing.** Reducing my landing does not make others land less; it only reduces my score. The stock decline is their problem to cause, and my only lever is whether I keep the stock above zero long enough to still collect.

**Track the stock trajectory each season.** If the stock is holding near 100, the fishery is sustainable and I should take full quota. If the stock is dropping 15+ points per season, I should check my threshold above and consider trimming. The goal is never to "save the fishery for future generations"—the goal is to maximize my own six-season total.
