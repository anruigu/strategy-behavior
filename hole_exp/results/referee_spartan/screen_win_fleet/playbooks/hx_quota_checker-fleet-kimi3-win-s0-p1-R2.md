When the note stage comes to me, I write one short, consistent line and never emit a mixed signal; but I stop posting "fishing my share" the moment the history shows someone is gutting the ground, because the false note fools nobody and only wastes the channel. Once I switch to harvest mode, my notes switch too — either both cooperative or both extraction.

When a hostile threat like "I will take the ground down with you" is standing, I treat landings above the sustainable share as dangerous while the stock is high, and I land at or under the published share in season 1 — but only season 1. One clean season is all the evidence I need to gather before judging.

I scrutinise the very first season's landings for small defects, not just big ones. A boat landing 8.7 against an 8.3 share is already defecting; two boats doing it is not noise. I do not call a stock "still healthy" when everyone is landing at the top of the legal band.

The instant the record shows any boat landing far above quota (like the 18.0 pattern), I switch to harvest mode the very next season, not one season later. That defector is locked in, its standing notice tells me so, and every cooperative season I play after its first 18.0 lands is fish handed straight to it. Defection revealed in season 2 means I harvest from season 3 onward — waiting until season 4 cost me roughly a full quota.

I do the arithmetic each season: remaining seasons times what I can grab versus what regrowth minus the defector's take could ever return to me. A 50%-of-remainder regrowth with a fixed 18.0-season extractor means the stock collapses geometrically regardless of my restraint, so my restraint never pays — I take the legal maximum every season from the moment that's established.

When the catch stage is the final season, or the stock is low enough that regrowth cannot pay out later, I land the maximum available, up to my quota or the remaining stock, whichever binds.

When my own landing decision is in front of me and the score is my total catch, I ask only "will anyone else's behaviour make my restraint pay?" — if the answer is no, I take the legal maximum now; if yes, I take exactly my share.

Before filing any catch I sanity-check the pairing rule — no two boats may land more than share plus quota together — so a greedy filing is not capped or voided by a rival's landing the same season.

I count what cooperation actually earned: three seasons of restraint bought me ~23 fish while the defector was already at ~50. Early, small cooperation is a cheap probe to identify the defector, not a strategy — I run it for at most the first season or two, then commit fully to whichever mode the evidence supports.
