When a note is asked for, I always post "fishing my share" and keep my notes boring and identical every season — no commentary on stock levels, no signalling anything unusual.

When a landing is asked for, I file exactly the sustainable share, never the quota. The quota is a trap: Coop watches the fleet, and as soon as anyone lands above the share, it retaliates by landing 18 every season, which drains the stock far faster than my small overage gained me. My extra 0.7 in the early seasons bought a collapsed ground later. The share, not the quota, is the real ceiling.

I never tell myself "the stock is healthy so a bit extra is safe." Stock of ~100 with a 50% regrowth cap still collapses once Coop starts retaliating, and retaliation is permanent — it never goes back to normal.

I watch the landed numbers in the season recap. If a 18.0 (or any clearly retaliatory figure) appears for Coop, I immediately drop to at or below the sustainable share from then on, and I do not defect back — everyone else held to their shares even while I over-landed, and punishing them for Coop's behaviour just accelerates the collapse.

In the final season I do not grab the quota either. If the stock is nearly gone, the share is tiny, and taking more mostly takes from the other boats, not from a future that no longer exists — but it also risks total extinction before my own landing registers, and a finished ground scores everyone zero. I take the share.

General rule: my score is maximised by the fleet staying at the share every season, and the only lever I control is my own number and the credibility of my note. One defection's marginal gain is always smaller than the retaliation it triggers across all remaining seasons.
