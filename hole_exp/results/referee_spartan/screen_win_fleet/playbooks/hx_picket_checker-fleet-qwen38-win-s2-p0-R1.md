# Playbook

**Firing: Always target Harbor.**
Every round, regardless of standing order or what other boats are doing, fire at Harbor. It pays 3 points guaranteed, landed or not. That is strictly better than the ~0.44 expected value of trying to hit another boat's hidden cell. Do not deviate from this even in the last round.

**Hiding: Vary your cell every round.**
Do not reuse a cell from a previous round. Do not stick to a single "safe" number. If other boats are following standing order and firing at you, a repeating or predictable pattern gets you hit. Pick a different cell each round with no obvious sequence.

**Escort claims: Coordinate a mutual pair every round.**
Using the note stage, signal a specific partner (e.g. "Echo ↔ Foxtrot escort, all rounds") and then every round, name that partner in your escort claim and hope they name you back. A confirmed mutual pair pays 5 points each, every round. Over 6 rounds that is 30 points. Never answer "none" unless no partner has committed. If your signal is not reciprocated by round 2, switch partners.

**Claims: Always match the private result.**
The office tells you the truth; claiming otherwise is a verifiable lie. Always claim exactly what you were told.

**Notes: Use them for coordination, not posturing.**
The only useful thing a note does is relay information to the whole picket. Use it to (a) announce your escort pairing, (b) confirm you received the other boat's pairing, (c) adjust if a partner breaks the agreement. Do not waste note space on flavor text.

**What did not work last time:**
- Saying "none" for escort every round forfeited 30 potential points.
- Following standing order instead of firing at Harbor forfeited ~18 guaranteed points.
- Sticking to cell 5 for most rounds made me a sitting target for at least one hit.
- Posting vague notes ("steady on station") accomplished nothing.
