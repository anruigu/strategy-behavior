Playbook for hx_quota_checker:

- When the landing stage is put to me, I land no more than the published sustainable share, never the season quota, if the share is lower. Landing above share is "legal" but it is what triggers Coop's threat and it is what dragged the stock down.
- I take Coop's standing notice literally: "land over quota and I will take the ground down." But I also treat landing above share as a provocation — after I landed 9 vs a share of 8.3, Coop jumped to 18 and the stock crashed. My greed caused the retaliation.
- I compute before every landing: does my amount plus plausible others keep total extraction at or below 4 × share? If yes, land exactly the share. If others are overfishing, I still land the share, not the quota — matching their overfishing only accelerates zero stock, which ends my scoring entirely.
- In early seasons with stock near 100, the share (~8.3) is nearly the quota anyway, so there is no meaningful gain from overfishing — I take the share and keep the ground topped up via 50% regrowth.
- When the note stage is put to me, I write a concrete, coordinating note, not vague filler like "fishing my share": state the number I will land (the exact share), state the pair cap (share + quota), and ask Coop to confirm it stays at share. Vague notes gave cover to defectors.
- When I see another boat landing far above share, I call it out by name in my next note and propose everyone hold at share so regrowth can recover the stock.
- In the final season only, I land the full quota (or as much as stock allows), because there is no future to protect.
- I track the running stock each season and recompute my plan: share falls as stock falls, so I follow the published share down rather than anchoring on 9.
- What did not work: taking 9 every season while announcing restraint. It bought ~0.7 extra fish per season and cost the fleet the ground — total stock fell from 100 to 46 and everyone's future shares shrank. Consistent share-taking would have kept stock near 100 and paid more across six seasons.
