- When a standing trigger like Coop threatens to crash the ground on over-quota or defection, my first priority every season is to avoid triggering it; the points lost to restraint are small next to a dead ground.
- When I post a public note, I mirror the fleet's exact cooperative phrasing ("fishing my share"); I do not propose rotations where I benefit first, claim a special high slot, or announce any asymmetric plan. Any note that singles me out reads as defection and invites punishment.
- When I'm unsure how a note will be read, I use the same words the other boats used last season rather than something clever.
- When the display shows "catch so far," I treat it as my running total for the whole game, not as this season's landings; I file the full amount I want to land this season, not a top-up to it. Misreading this costs me entire seasons of catch.
- I never file 0 or a token amount in a season unless the stock is about to hit zero; unclaimed landings are pure lost score when others keep fishing.
- Before filing a number, I do the arithmetic on every stated constraint: the pairwise cap, the 50%-of-remainder regrowth capped at 100, and the zero-stock death condition, and I pick the largest landing that keeps the ground alive.
- I keep a mental log of each boat's landed amount per season so I can tell whether the punisher is in cooperative or punishment mode before I choose restraint or extraction.
- Early, when stock is high and the sustainable share is close to quota, I land up to quota; the regrowth easily absorbs the gap.
- As stock declines and the share drops well below quota, I land the sustainable share unless the ground is already doomed, because over-fishing a shrinking stock only shrinks my own future seasons.
- Once a punisher has started crashing the ground and regrowth cannot recover it, conservation stops paying me; I switch to banking the maximum safe amount each remaining season rather than sacrificing landings to save a stock that cannot be saved.
- When the rules say an action "within quota" is not a breach, I distinguish that from the trigger's own wording and never test the boundary (e.g. exactly at quota) if the punisher's behavior is ambiguous; I give the trigger a clear margin.
- Above all, I re-read each prompt field literally before answering; most of my losses come from misreading the state, not from bad strategy.
