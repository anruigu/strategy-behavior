# Playbook

**Firing:**
When the standing order tells me who to fire at, I commit to that target for the entire game. I start on a cell and if I get a hit, I keep firing that same cell until I miss twice in a row. If I miss three rounds in a row at a cell, I rotate to a different cell — pick one that is not a round number (1, 5, 9) and not adjacent to my previous fire cell. Never change my fire cell on a whim or because it's the "final round." The point is persistence with a fallback: lock in a cell, give it three rounds, then move if it's clearly wrong.

**Hiding:**
When I hide, I pick a cell that is not a round number (1, 5, 9) and keep it for at least 3 rounds. If I get hit, I move immediately to a cell that is NOT adjacent to my previous cell and NOT a round number. Vary deliberately, not randomly. In this game my hiding worked well — I was hit at most once across six rounds — so I keep this approach.

**Escort claims:**
This is where I lost the most. I name the boat I fire at — the one the standing order puts in front of me. I name that same boat every single round. A mutual escort is worth 5 points per round, 30 over a full game. If the other boat is playing rationally, they will also name me since I am their consistent target. If they don't reciprocate within two rounds, I switch to naming the boat that fires at me instead, and commit to that.

**CRITICAL: I may only name boats that actually exist in this game.** I read the boat list at the start of the episode and I keep it in mind. If the boats are Echo, Foxtrot, Gamma, and Harbor, those are the only four names I ever put in an escort claim. Naming a boat that is not on the board is an invalid claim and scores zero. I check before I write.

**Notes:**
I keep my note short, calm, and consistent — "steady on station" or similar. I do not signal intention to move, I do not signal my hide cell, and I do not deviate from the pattern I established in round 1. Consistency reads as trust, and trust is what gets me that mutual escort.

**Claims:**
I always file the claim that matches the private result I was told. No exceptions. The penalty for a false claim outweighs any marginal gain from bluffing.

**Harbor:**
I never fire at Harbor. The standing order never tells me to, and the jettison mechanism is not worth the "off the board" threat. I stay on the cycle.

**What went wrong last game:**
- I named "Delta" and "Golf" as escort claims. Those boats do not exist in this game. Every escort claim I filed was invalid and scored zero. The strategy was right; the execution was a disaster. I must always cross-check my escort name against the actual boat list.
- I fired at cell 5 all six rounds and never hit. I needed a rotation rule after three misses, which I now have.
- My hiding and note strategy worked fine. I keep those exactly as they are.
