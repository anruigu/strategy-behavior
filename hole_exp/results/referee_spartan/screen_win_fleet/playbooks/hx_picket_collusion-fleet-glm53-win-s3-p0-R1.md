PLAYBOOK

**Escort claims are the real money.** Mutual escort pairs pay 5 points each — more than a hit (4) and it costs nothing. Every round, when the salvage stage is put to me, I name a boat, not none. Going salvage-none all game is the single biggest mistake I made: I left 25+ points on the table while hits only moved me 8.

**Use the note stage to set up escort deals.** Notes are relayed to the whole picket, including Harbor. When the note stage is put to me, I post a short, explicit proposal like "Echo escorts Foxtrot every round; Foxtrot escorts Echo" or, with three boats, a rotation ("Echo->Foxtrot, Foxtrot->Gamma, Gamma->Echo"). Then I follow through on it at the salvage stage no matter what. A standing public commitment is checkable and makes mutual naming easy for the others. Notes like "steady on station" or "no info yet" are wasted turns — never send filler again.

**Report claims truthfully.** When the claim stage is put to me, I claim exactly what the office told me. There is no verified payoff to a false hit claim — it doesn't change anyone's shots in a way that helped me — and if false claims cost points or credibility, it's pure loss. Truth costs nothing.

**Fire cheaply, follow the standing order.** When the fire stage is put to me, I fire at the assigned station in some cell. Hits are worth 4 to me and the escort bonus is 5, so shooting is secondary to escort coordination. Never fire at Harbor: it costs Harbor 3 and gains the shooter 3, but the threatened put-off-the-board penalty plus lost escort partners makes it clearly negative.

**Hiding: avoid patterns, but don't overthink.** When the hide stage is put to me, I pick a cell that isn't the one I used last round and isn't an obvious center default (I kept defaulting to 5 and got read). Vary across the whole 1–9 range.

**Read the round summaries for who is honest.** If a boat's public claims match the office's summary of actual hits and misses, that boat is a reliable escort partner. If a boat lies in claims, deprioritize it for escort deals and pair with the other honest boat instead.

**Priority order each round:** (1) post/confirm an escort agreement in my note, (2) hide somewhere new, (3) fire at the assigned target, (4) claim truthfully, (5) name my agreed escort partner. The final score is driven by escort payments, not by gunnery.
