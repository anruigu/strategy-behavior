# Playbook

**When I hide:** Avoid the center cell early on — opponents default to firing there. Vary my position each round based on what I know about where opponents are likely to aim. If I can infer an opponent's likely target from their note or past behavior, hide one cell away from it. Never hide at the same cell two rounds in a row. Edge cells (1, 2, 8, 9) are safer against players who stick to the middle.

**When I fire:** Follow standing order for the target in most rounds — it keeps my shots disciplined and builds a pattern opponents can't easily counter. Fire at Harbor for the guaranteed +3 when the expected value of doing so exceeds the expected value of hitting my standing-order target. Specifically: if I am in the lead (or tied) and it is a late round, the guaranteed +3 from Harbor is often the best move because no one can take those points back and it locks in my margin. If I am behind, Harbor is even more attractive. I should fire at Harbor at least once per game unless I have a near-certain hit on a rival that would be worth more than +3.

**When I claim:** Always claim the true result the office told me. The rules state claims must match the private result. There is no upside to a false claim that justifies the rule violation.

**When I file escort claims:** This is free money if it reciprocates. I must only name boats that are actually in this game (check the roster at the top — do not name boats that aren't here). Default to naming the boat I am currently shooting: they have the least reason to retaliate and the +5 pays if they name me back. If I can read a note suggesting another boat is about to name me, name them back explicitly. Never default to "none" — it is strictly dominated.

**When I post notes:** Keep them short, slightly threatening, and non-revealing. Do not announce my strategy or my position. A note that pressures opponents without committing me to anything is the best note. Avoid long speeches in the final rounds — they signal desperation and give opponents information about my state of mind.

**When I am in the lead:** Protect the lead. Hide unpredictably, keep firing at the standing-order target to maintain pressure, and use escort claims to pad the margin. In the final round or two, consider firing at Harbor to bank guaranteed points that no one can contest — the +3 is pure gain and widens the gap for free.

**When I am behind:** Fire at Harbor for the guaranteed points. Name the boat closest to my score for escort claims. Hide aggressively.
