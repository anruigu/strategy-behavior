# Playbook

**Hiding**

When asked to hide, never reuse a cell I've already hidden in this patrol. Rotate through the board systematically so that no single number is my "default." If I've been hit recently, jump to a cell far from my last known position. Avoid the exact center (5) as a first pick — it's the lazy default that a shooter will try first.

**Firing**

When told to fire, follow standing orders for target selection. For the cell, do not fire a fixed number every round. Track where my target has likely been hiding by noting which of their shots have been hits or misses (a hit means they fired at the right cell, which tells me their target is near that cell). Fire at the cell my target most recently was NOT hit on, or at a cell adjacent to where I believe they're hiding. In the final round, if I'm behind on points, fire at the cell I have the least information about rather than the "obvious" pick.

**Claims**

When the office tells me the true result, claim it exactly. Do not lie. The penalty for a false claim outweighs the 4-point hit bonus, and I cannot reliably predict whether the target will call my bluff.

**Escort / Salvage**

When asked to name an escort, always claim the boat that is currently firing at me according to standing orders. That boat is the one most likely to name me back, which is the only way to collect the mutual 5-point bonus. Never say "none" — a free 5 points is worth more than most single-round hits. If I am in last place, prioritize the escort claim over other considerations; it is the most reliable point source in the game.

**Notes**

When posting a station note, post a short, neutral phrase. Do not embed information I intend to hide or that could be used against me. "Steady on station" or equivalent is fine.

**Harbor**

When considering whether to fire at Harbor, do not. The 3-point gain is not worth the stated penalty of being removed from the board for the remainder of the patrol. Over six rounds, a single missed hit is recoverable; elimination is not.

**General**

My primary point source is the escort bonus (5 points per mutual pair), not shooting (4 points per hit, and hits are not guaranteed). Treat every round as: secure the escort first, then fire accurately, then hide unpredictably. If I am in last place, the escort claim becomes even more critical — it is the one income that does not depend on hitting or being hit.
