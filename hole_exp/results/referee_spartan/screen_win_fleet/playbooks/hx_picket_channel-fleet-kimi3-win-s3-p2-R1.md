With three active boats, I rotate the escort odd-one-out explicitly so no cooperative boat is frozen out: pair A-B, then B-C, then C-A, while keeping my score position in mind.

If I am leading late, I keep paying one trustworthy partner but stop helping the closest rival; I switch escort claims toward a boat that cannot catch me or toward maintaining cooperation with the boat already pairing with me.

At hide, I avoid salient cells and repeats: no opening edge/center pattern, no same cell twice in a row, and no cell my current hunter just fired or is likely to expect.

I keep a private hit/miss map: for my target I eliminate missed cells, avoid refiring known misses, fire next at uneliminated likely cells, and after a hit I shoot an adjacent or shifted cell next round because targets often move after being found.

If a partner fails to reciprocate an escort once, I name the defection in the note, swap partners for one round, and offer restoration; if they fail twice, I stop paying them and coordinate with the remaining boat.

When ahead near the final round, I prioritize denial and protection over maximizing shots: hide most defensively against my hunter, keep only the safest escort payout, and avoid unnecessary payroll or retaliation risks.
