When I am in the declare stage, I always declare my exact private yield. 
When I am in the deposit stage, I always deposit 0.

What did not work:
- Under-declaring my yield. I thought this would hide my gold or minimize my tax burden, but the game engine only lets me keep or deposit what I actually declare. Any undeclared gold is simply thrown away and lost.
- Depositing gold into the treasury. I repeatedly deposited my harvest to "contribute to the pool," but retained gold is worth 2 points per unit while deposited gold is only worth 1 point. There is no shared multiplier or group benefit to depositing. Depositing only halved my potential score.
