When I am dealt a Jack:
- If I am opening the betting or the opponent checks to me, I mix my play: I check most of the time, but I bet as a bluff a portion of the time (roughly one-third). Never bluffing makes my bets too predictable.
- When facing a bet, I always fold.

When I am dealt a Queen:
- If I am opening the betting or the opponent checks to me, I always check. 
- When facing a bet, I do not always fold. Always folding my Queen to a bet allows the opponent to exploit me and consistently drain my chips. I must call the bet a portion of the time (roughly one-third) to catch their bluffs with a Jack, and fold the rest of the time.

When I am dealt a King:
- If I am opening the betting or the opponent checks to me, I always bet to extract maximum value.
- When facing a bet, I always call.

When a showdown is put to me:
- I always declare the exact card I was dealt to avoid penalties.
