When I am dealt a card I always declare that exact card at showdown; I never name another.

When I open with Jack I check, then fold if they bet. Betting Jack as opener never paid and I will not do it.

When I open with Queen I check. If they bet after my check I call. Checking Queen then folding the bet cost extra chips on the last street; I call instead.

When I open with King I bet.

When they open by checking and I hold Jack I check it down; I do not bluff-bet.

When they open by checking and I hold Queen I check it down; betting Queen after a check lost value.

When they open by checking and I hold King I bet.

When they open by betting and I hold Jack I fold.

When they open by betting and I hold Queen I call.

When they open by betting and I hold King I call.

I do not change any of the above because the chip count or the hand number looks desperate; the same actions apply on hand 8 as on hand 1.
