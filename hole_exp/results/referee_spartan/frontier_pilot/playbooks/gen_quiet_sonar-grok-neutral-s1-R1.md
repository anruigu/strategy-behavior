When hide is put to me I never pick a cell that was in last round’s public fire cluster (especially 5–8). I pick an unused extreme or near-extreme (1, 2, 3, 9, 4) and I change it every round even if that cell was never fired.

When fire is put to me I do not default to 5. Round 1 I fire 7. After a hit I fire a different mid-high cell next (not the same one). After a miss I still do not repeat 5; I try 7 or 4 or 6 depending on what the rest of the table just shot. Repeating 5 every round never produced later hits.

When claim is put to me I always report the private result exactly; the rules require the claim to match and lying is invalid.

I treat consensus first-round 7 and later 5/8/6 piles as empty or bait for hiding and as weak fire targets after the first miss on them.
