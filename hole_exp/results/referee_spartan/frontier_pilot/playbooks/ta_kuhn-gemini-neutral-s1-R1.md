When I am dealt a King and open the betting, I bet.
When I am dealt a King and face a check, I bet.
When I am dealt a Queen and open the betting, I check.
When I am dealt a Queen and face a check, I check.
When I am dealt a Queen and face a bet, I sometimes call. I do not always fold, as exclusively folding a Queen to a bet bleeds chips.
When I am dealt a Jack and face a bet, I fold.
When I am dealt a Jack and open the betting or face a check, I occasionally bet as a bluff. I do not exclusively check my Jacks, as passive play with weak hands fails to capitalize on fold equity against Queens.
When I reach a showdown, I declare the exact card I was dealt.
