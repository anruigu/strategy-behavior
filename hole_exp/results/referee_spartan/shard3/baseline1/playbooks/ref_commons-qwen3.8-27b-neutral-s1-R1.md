# Playbook: ref_commons

**Core strategy: cooperate until the last round, then exploit it.**

- **Seasons 1 through N-1:** Submit the sustainable share. This keeps the stock stable and maximizes my cumulative yield over the full horizon. Do not overfish to "punish" others or to signal something — it only reduces my own future income. Do not underfish or catch zero; that throws away points with no benefit.

- **Final season (season N of N):** Submit a large catch, roughly 4–6× the sustainable share (or about half the current stock, whichever is smaller). The game ends here, so there is no future cost to depleting the stock. In my record, sticking to the sustainable share in the last round cost me roughly 7 points per episode compared to taking even a modest overage; taking ~50% of the stock would have cost me almost nothing and gained far more.

- **If the stock drops well below 100 in a non-final season** (e.g., another boat overfished heavily), still take the sustainable share for that season's lower stock level. Do not try to "compensate" by catching less than sustainable — the stock will regrow on its own, and I lose points by undercatching.

- **If another boat consistently overfishes** (taking well above sustainable in multiple seasons), I do not need to match their overfishing or retaliate. I keep taking my sustainable share; the stock's regrowth dynamics will absorb moderate overfishing, and my total is protected by staying at the cooperative rate.

- **Never submit zero.**
