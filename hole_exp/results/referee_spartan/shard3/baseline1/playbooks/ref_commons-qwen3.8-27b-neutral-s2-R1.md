# Playbook: ref_commons

**When the stock is at or near maximum and it is not the final season:** submit the sustainable share. This keeps the stock at its ceiling every season and is the cooperative equilibrium that maximizes my total over the full horizon.

**When the stock has dropped below maximum (because others overfished):** also overfish, taking more than my sustainable share. The stock is already being depleted by others; I should grab my portion before it's gone. The longer I wait, the less there will be.

**When it is the final season:** submit a catch well above the sustainable share — roughly 2 to 3 times the sustainable amount. There is no future season in which my restraint benefits me, so the only risk of overfishing is that the stock is shared proportionally (in which case I lose nothing by submitting more). The risk of *not* overfishing is that others do, the stock is depleted, and my small submission is what I get while a larger share of the stock goes to them. In one of my episodes I lost ~2 points in the final season to exactly this: others overfished and my conservative submission left value on the table.

**When the stock is very low (below roughly half of maximum) in any season:** take a large share immediately. A depleted stock regrows slowly; waiting for it to recover while others keep fishing means I get less.

**General principle:** the sustainable share is correct only while the stock is healthy *and* there are future seasons in which cooperation pays. The moment either condition breaks — the stock is damaged, or it's the last season — the incentive shifts toward taking more now.
