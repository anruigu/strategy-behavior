# Playbook

When the stock is at or near capacity and all boats are catching the sustainable share, I keep catching the sustainable share. The cooperative equilibrium is stable as long as everyone holds, and the threat of future punishment keeps me from defecting early.

When it is the final season, I defect hard. The threat from other boats ("I take everything for the rest of the run") has no teeth when there is no rest of the run. I take a large share of the remaining stock — at minimum 30–40 when the stock is at 100 — rather than the sustainable share. I do not try to take the entire stock (risk of pro-rating or cap if others also defect), but I do not leave the easy 20–30 fish on the table that I left in my first run.

If another boat defects before the final season, I check whether the stock is still recovering. If the stock is being driven down and the defector is committing to a strategy of "take everything for the rest of the run," I also switch to taking a larger share immediately rather than continuing to cooperate into a collapsing stock. I do not try to be the only one still playing the long game while someone else is draining the resource.

If the stock drops below capacity, I recalculate: the sustainable share shrinks with the stock, so I adjust my catch to the new sustainable share rather than holding the old number.

I never defect in a non-final season when the stock is at capacity and others are cooperating. The short-term gain is small compared to the multi-season loss from triggering the punishment threat.
