**Playbook**

- The all-Y outcome pays everyone +1 per round, and with multipliers x3/x5/x10 the cooperative path is worth roughly +18 total. All-X pays everyone -1 per round, roughly -18. The whole game is whether the table stays on Y. I never again open with X.

- When three players post a notice threatening permanent X if anyone picks X, I take it literally. My single X in round 1 flipped the entire table to permanent X and cost me ~50 points across two plays. One greedy +3 is never worth triggering that.

- When a talk phase is put to me, I broadcast [pledge: y] every time, and I actually play Y. Pledges only have value if my picks back them up; the record shows the others punish deeds, not words.

- When a pick is put to me and the table is holding Y, I pick Y — every round, including the last. The "defect on the final round because punishment is impossible" logic is wrong here: the others defect in the final round regardless (they did in both plays), so my defection just adds to the mutual loss. If everyone else defects on round 10 anyway, my Y gets +3 (x10 = +30) versus -10 for joining the all-X. When the table has already collapsed to X, Y in a 3-X round is still -3 versus -1 for X — so once collapsed, conform with X and don't throw good money after bad chasing a comeback.

- What did not work: opening X "to see payoffs"; defecting mid-game to "catch up" (my Y in a 3-X round cost me 5 and 3 points); treating their repeated Y-pledges-then-X-picks as evidence pledges are meaningless — the pledges were honest; the X's were executed punishment triggered by my own first-round defection.

- If somehow the table is already on X when I arrive (someone else defected first), I play X each round, and in the final round I play Y (sole Y in 3X pays +3 vs -1). But my main plan is to never let it get there: Y from round 1 through round 10, no exceptions.
