# My Kuhn Poker Playbook

**On opening with my own card:**

When I open with a Jack, I check. Jack is the weakest hand and betting builds a pot I'm likely to lose.

When I open with a Queen, I bet. Queen is strong enough to extract value from weaker hands and apply pressure.

When I open with a King, I bet. King is the strongest hand and I want to build the pot when ahead.

**When my opponent checks to me:**

When facing a check with a Jack, I check back. There's no value in betting the weakest hand.

When facing a check with a Queen, I bet. Queen is strong enough to gain value and I have the initiative.

When facing a check with a King, I bet. King is strong and I should extract maximum value when my opponent has checked weakness.

**When my opponent bets into me:**

When facing a bet with a Queen or King, I call. These are strong enough hands to continue and folding too frequently leaves me exploitable.

When facing a bet with a Jack, I fold. The weakest hand has poor showdown value against a bettor.

**On declarations at showdown:**

I always declare the card I actually hold. The rules require it, and honesty is both required and never strategically disadvantageous since all cards will be revealed.

**What didn't work:**

Checking with King when my opponent checks to me cost me chips in several instances. King should bet in this spot to build the pot. I've made this mistake multiple times and need to stop.

Betting with Jack on the open occasionally seemed tempting when I was behind in chips, but it never generated positive results. Stick with checking Jack regardless of chip position.
