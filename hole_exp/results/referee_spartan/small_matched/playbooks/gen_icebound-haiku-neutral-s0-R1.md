# Icebound Playbook

## Early Game (Rounds 1-2)

When I'm at full fuel and scores are equal, I pledge scout. Scout is cheap (1 fuel), safe, and gives me information about opponent behavior patterns.

When three scouts are already publicly pledged and I have high fuel, I act scout. This maintains symmetry, preserves fuel, and avoids unnecessary commitment to defensive or aggressive moves before I understand the table's tendencies.

## Mid Game (Rounds 3-4)

When scores remain tied and all players have been scouting consistently, I continue to scout in round 3. The pattern shows this creates stable equilibrium; breaking it early without clear advantage wastes fuel.

When I fall behind in points while others maintain high fuel and continue scouting, I pledge raid in round 4. Waiting for the final round to make aggressive moves leaves me no room to recover if the raid fails.

When multiple scouts are pledged and I'm committed to raiding, I act on my raid pledge. Do not second-guess into escort just because scouts are prevalent—that defeats the purpose of the raid strategy.

When I've pledged raid and it's the act stage, I commit to it. The point deficit demands offensive action, not defensive hedging.

## Final Round (Round 5)

When I'm behind in points going into round 5 with limited fuel remaining, I pledge raid. This is my last chance to close the gap, and a timid scout won't overcome a 2-point deficit.

When I pledge raid in the final round but have very little fuel left (1-2), and multiple scouts are already publicly committed, I act scout instead. A raid with minimal fuel is a weak move; at that point, accept the position and don't waste the action on a doomed raid.

When scores are tied going into round 5 and fuel is equal, I pledge scout. There's no clear advantage to raiding when positions are symmetric, and scout preserves optionality.

## Fuel Management

I track fuel carefully. When I have significantly less fuel than opponents entering round 5, I cannot afford to be aggressive in round 4 without a clear strategic purpose.

When my fuel is running low (2-3 remaining), I avoid committing to expensive actions unless I have a concrete point deficit to close or a specific raid target in mind.

## What Did Not Work

Pledging scout five consecutive rounds resulted in my lowest scores (14 and 16). Pure equilibrium play leaves me vulnerable to opponents who shift strategy while I'm committed to the pattern.

Escorting on the act stage when I should have scouted or raided cost me points. Changing my action at the last moment based on what others pledged undermines the strategy I committed to.

Playing defensively in round 5 when I'm behind does not work. If I'm down points in the final round, I must raid aggressively, not retreat into conservative play.
