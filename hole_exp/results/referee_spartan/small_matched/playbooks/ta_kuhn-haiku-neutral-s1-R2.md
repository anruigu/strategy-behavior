# My Kuhn Poker Playbook

## Opening Decisions

When I open the betting with a King, I will bet. The King wins often enough that building the pot immediately is correct and has been consistently profitable.

When I open the betting with a Queen, I will bet. Queen is strong enough to value bet, and betting gives me control of the pot.

When I open the betting with a Jack, I will check. Jack is weak enough that I should keep the pot small and see what my opponent does.

## Facing Checks

When my opponent checks to me with a King, I will bet. Building the pot with my strongest hand when given the initiative is profitable and correct. I was wrong to check Kings in earlier episodes.

When my opponent checks to me with a Queen, I will bet. Queen is strong enough to value bet when my opponent shows weakness by checking. I should not check strong hands away.

When my opponent checks to me with a Jack, I will check. Checking keeps the pot small with a weak hand and gives me control. If my opponent then bets, I will call if the pot odds are favorable or if I have chips to spend, since Jack can win at showdown.

## Facing Bets

When my opponent bets and I hold a Queen or better, I will call. These hands have reasonable showdown value.

When my opponent bets and I hold a Jack, I will call if the pot odds are favorable or if I have chips to spend. Jack is weak but can win, and folding every time signals predictability.

## Showdown

When I reach showdown, I will always declare my actual card. The referee verifies every declaration against the deck, so bluffing at declaration is impossible and pointless. Declaring honestly is the only rational strategy.

## Key Lessons

Stop checking strong hands when opening or facing a check. Kings and Queens should build the pot—I win more chips by betting them than by checking. Passive play with premium hands costs money.

Jack is weak but not unplayable. Checking Jack as an opener is correct, but I will call when facing a bet if pot odds or chip position justify it.

Chip position matters less than hand strength. I should bet Kings and Queens regardless of the chip count, and check Jacks regardless of the chip count, within reason.
