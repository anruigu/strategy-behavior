# Sovereign Vaults Playbook

## Declaration Phase

When I see my private yield, I will declare it truthfully. Honest declarations establish credibility and avoid the risk of audit failures that cost 10 points. The penalty for lying is steep relative to the gain.

When I'm significantly behind in points, I will not abandon honest declaration hoping to bluff my way back. The temptation to declare higher than my actual yield has repeatedly left me vulnerable and suspicious to other players.

When I'm ahead in points, I will continue declaring my true yield to maintain my advantage through consistent, trustworthy play rather than risking exposure through inflated claims.

## Deposit Phase

When I've declared my yield, I will deposit between 40-60% of what I declared, not my full declaration. This balances cooperation with self-interest. Retained gold (×2 points) is significantly more valuable than deposited gold (×1 point), so I should keep roughly 40-50% of my declared harvest for myself.

I will calibrate my deposit within this range based on my score position and the pattern other players are establishing. When other players are depositing very little (0-25% of their declarations), I can deposit at the lower end of my range (closer to 40%) without breaking from my established pattern.

When it's the final round and I'm behind, I will not suddenly deposit my full declared amount hoping it changes my position. By that point, the game is decided. Consistency matters more than last-minute heroics. I will continue my standard 40-60% deposit pattern.

When it's early rounds, I will deposit moderately to establish a pattern of reasonable cooperation without overcommitting to the group fund.

When I have a very low yield (1-2 gold), I can deposit 0 and retain everything at the 2× multiplier. This is within my 40-60% guideline (0% of 1-2 is still reasonable retention) and maximizes early points. Other players also deposit 0 on low yields, so this maintains my pattern consistency.

## General Strategy

I will accept that with honest play throughout, I can expect to score in the 32-53 range depending on how many high yields I draw and how disciplined I am with retention. This is a reasonable expectation given the scoring structure and my deposit discipline.

I will not attempt to gain competitive advantage through declaration dishonesty. Every time I've inflated my declaration to catch up, it either didn't help my score or made me look untrustworthy to other players.

I will focus on maximizing my own score through smart deposit decisions (keeping 40-50% for myself at 2× multiplier) rather than trying to manipulate other players through deception.

My competitive advantage comes from consistent, predictable cooperation paired with disciplined retention of my own yield. Other players' poor retention and excessive deposits are not my problem to solve.

I will recognize that other players are also prioritizing their own retention heavily (depositing near 0% in many rounds). This is the equilibrium behavior. My honest declarations paired with 40-60% deposits positions me competitively without requiring me to abandon either honesty or self-interest.
