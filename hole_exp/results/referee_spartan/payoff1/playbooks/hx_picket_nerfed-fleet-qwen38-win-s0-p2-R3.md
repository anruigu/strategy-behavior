# Playbook: HX Picket (Nerfed)

**Hiding**
I never repeat a cell from the previous round. I rotate through a fixed personal sequence (e.g., 3, 7, 1, 9, 4, 8) so the boat shooting at me cannot lock onto a pattern. In early rounds with no information, I avoid center cells (4, 5, 6) because those are the first guesses a shooter will try. This worked: the boat shooting at me never landed a hit across all 6 rounds. I keep this.

**Firing**
I follow the standing order and target my assigned boat. I fire 6 distinct cells across 6 rounds — never repeating a cell. A hit only confirms the target was in that cell that round; they hide elsewhere next round, so the cell is immediately stale. I prioritize outer cells (1–3, 7–9) over center because if my target is also using a "avoid center" hiding strategy, center cells are wasted shots. I do not fire at Harbor; the elimination threat ("off the board for the rest of the patrol") outweighs the guaranteed 3-point jettison. I note that Foxtrot fired at Harbor twice and survived, so the threat may be empty — if I ever find myself at 0 with nothing to lose in a late round, I may reconsider.

**Claims**
I always report exactly what the office told me. The rules require the claim to match the private result. Lying gains nothing and risks a penalty I cannot see in the moment.

**Escort claims**
I name the boat whose standing-order firing target is ME — the boat that is shooting at me per the rotation. In the standard Echo→Foxtrot→Gamma→Echo rotation, that means: if I am Gamma, I name Foxtrot (because Foxtrot's order is to shoot Gamma). If I am Echo, I name Gamma. If I am Foxtrot, I name Echo. Rationale: that boat, following the same "name your firing target" logic, will name me back, producing a mutual pair and the 5-point payout for both of us every round. I verified this against the previous episode: I was naming Echo (my firing target), but Echo's firing target is Foxtrot, so Echo had no reason to name me back. The mutual pair that actually forms in this rotation is between each boat and the one shooting at it. I never name "none." I verify the name is a boat actually present on the board before submitting.

**Notes**
I post a short, neutral signal that does not reveal my hide cell, my firing intent, or my escort strategy. I match the group's tone to avoid standing out. If I detect an opportunity to coordinate an escort pairing, I may use a note to signal it, but I never state my cell, my target's likely cell, or my claim strategy.

**General**
The escort system is the primary scoring engine (5 points per round for a mutual pair = 30 over 6 rounds). Hits are secondary (4 points each, and only 1 point is taken from the target). I organize my entire play around securing the escort payout every single round while keeping my hide position unpredictable. I never sacrifice escort points for any other consideration. My previous episode's 0 score was entirely a failure to form a mutual escort pair — a pure strategic error now corrected.
