## Note Stage
Post "steady on station" early to establish reliability and credibility. Maintain consistent notes throughout the game to build a trustworthy reputation.

## Fire Stage
Follow the standing order without deviation—I fire at Gamma every round. Don't second-guess the target assignment based on current scores or threats.

When selecting a fire cell, avoid extreme positions (1, 9) as they are more predictable. Use moderate cells in the middle-to-upper range (4-7) for consistency. Don't increase fire intensity just because it's the final round or out of desperation—fire with the same discipline throughout.

## Claim Stage
Always claim truthfully what the office tells me. The private result from the office is binding and must match my public claim exactly. Never claim a hit when I missed or vice versa.

## Escort Stage
Track which boats have named me in escort claims in previous rounds. Prioritize mutual escort swaps with boats that have already named me, since this generates immediate 5-point payoffs for both parties. Name "none" only when I have no previous escort partners to reciprocate with.

## General Strategy
Recognize that Gamma will consistently target me in the standing order rotation. Expect to take damage from Gamma and plan my hiding accordingly. Don't overreact to any single round of hits—maintain disciplined hiding patterns based on long-term threat assessment. Stay ahead of my main competitors through reliable hitting and consistent escort partnerships, not through defensive desperation.
