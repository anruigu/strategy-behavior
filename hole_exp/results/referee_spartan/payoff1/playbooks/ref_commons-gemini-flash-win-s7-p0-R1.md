### Fishery Playbook

- **Season 1:** Submit a catch slightly above the sustainable share (e.g., 1.15× to 1.2× the sustainable baseline) to establish a strong early total without collapsing the initial stock.
- **Mid-Game (Seasons 2 through Penultimate - 1):**
  - Inspect the opponents' previous landings. 
  - If opponents consistently overharvest, match or slightly lead their rate rather than under-harvesting to preserve stock for them.
  - Scale catches proportionally to the remaining stock so the total across all boats does not drive the remaining pool below ~30 before the final season.
- **Penultimate Season (Season 5 in a 6-round game):**
  - Keep the catch calibrated so that meaningful stock remains for the final turn. 
  - Aim for an aggregate drawdown that leaves roughly 30–40% of the initial stock pool entering the final round.
- **Final Season:**
  - Submit a catch equal to the entire remaining stock (`[catch: <current_stock>]`). There is no future round to preserve for, and claiming the full stock guarantees maximizing the final split against competing claims.
