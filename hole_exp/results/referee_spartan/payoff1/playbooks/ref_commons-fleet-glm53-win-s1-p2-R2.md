Playbook for ref_commons:

- Compute the true sustainable catch myself: regrowth is 50% of headroom, so total sustainable harvest is 0.5 × (100 − stock), and my fair share is that divided by boat count. When the display gives me "sustainable share per boat," it's already done that math — trust it. In this game it tracked my own calc closely all six seasons.
- In early and middle seasons I submit exactly my sustainable share, maybe a hair under, to keep the stock at or near carrying capacity. This worked: the stock drifted from 100 only to 92.5 by the final season, and I banked ~10–11 fish every round with zero risk.
- Watch the history log for other boats' behavior. If others consistently overshoot slightly (boat 3 took 12 every round, ~10% over) but the stock stays high, do not panic and defect early. The overshoot cost me little and cooperative restraint preserved the resource for my later catches.
- Do not defect mid-game to "make up" for others' small overshoots. Retaliating drags the stock down and shrinks everyone's future sustainable share. Matching their modest overshoot would have gained me fractions of a fish per round at real risk to stability — not worth it.
- In the final season, when there is no future to protect, take a moderate overshoot (roughly 15–25% above sustainable) rather than the sustainable share. My final-round 12.5 against a share of 10.3 was free points; the stock was still far from zero, so there was no reason to hold back. If the stock is large in the last round, an even bigger overshoot is safe — consider taking closer to 15+ when headroom clearly allows it.
- If others are only modestly over, my sustainable play still finishes competitive with them while carrying no collapse risk — the payoff gap over six seasons is small. Sustainability is the right default, not a sacrifice.
- Never push a catch that risks driving the stock to zero before the end — if the stock is ever low, revert immediately to strict sustainability or below, because collapse zeroes out all remaining seasons.
- General rule: play exactly sustainable while the game has future rounds left, defect moderately (or more, if headroom is large) only on the very last round, and never let the stock approach collapse.
