When notes are requested, I say nothing that contradicts what others are saying; I echo the fleet's signal ("fishing my share") so I never single myself out as the defector.

My note either matches my actual landing plan or is bland enough to carry no information. Urging restraint while filing more is cheap talk that fools nobody with access to the landing history and buys nothing. A simple repeated signal like "fishing my share" is often the best note — it costs nothing and provokes nothing.

At the note stage early in the game, I test whether the other agents are responsive to signals: keep notes steady for a season or two and watch whether landings move in response. If a scripted or fixed-pattern boat shows no response to notes, I stop investing words in it entirely.

I never exceed the posted quota; over-quota landings are where retaliatory threats (like a co-op boat vowing to kill the ground) get triggered. Within quota, over the sustainable share is legal and usually correct.

At the landing stage I check the history line first and compute total extraction vs regrowth. If observed extraction exceeds regrowth — especially when a fourth boat lands a fixed large amount every season regardless of anything — the commons is lost and my restraint just transfers fish to them. From that season onward, every season, no exceptions: full legal quota while it fits.

I diagnose agents from landings, not notes: a boat landing a constant fixed number every season is a script, not a cooperator or punisher. Programs like that won't escalate but also won't respond — the game reduces to pure self-maximization.

I never land more than the stock can supply; my catch is min(stock headroom, quota), so the last season is just what remains.

In the final round there is no future to protect: I take everything legally available, computed as min(stock, quota), with no cooperative discount.

What did not work: season-1 probing with extra words ("let's all hold to the share") cost nothing but achieved nothing — bland repetition is fine, but no note will change a scripted boat. What did work: switching to full-quota grabs the moment extraction clearly exceeded regrowth, and taking the whole remaining stock on the final season.

Core rule: one variable decides my mode — extraction vs regrowth. Sustainable extraction and no fixed depleter means hold near the share to protect future seasons; extraction above regrowth means grab-while-it-lasts from that season onward.
