# Playbook: Shared Fishery Commons

- In Season 1, catch slightly above the sustainable share (around 1.2x to 1.4x the sustainable benchmark, e.g. ~14–15 if sustainable is 11.1) to secure strong early yield while probing other boats' strategies.
- In intermediate seasons, observe the other boats' past landings:
  - If other boats consistently over-harvest (taking substantially more than the sustainable share), match their catch levels immediately and aggressively; do not unilaterally reduce catch (such as dropping to 12.5 when others take 15.0), as unilateral conservation simply cedes yield without preserving the stock.
  - If other boats strictly respect the sustainable share, keep catch close to the sustainable benchmark to maximize compounding growth across all 6 seasons.
- When the fishery is entering a terminal depletion spiral (remaining stock is low enough that total expected demand will exhaust it in the current round):
  - Do not bid modestly or cap demand at previous catch levels; request the full remaining stock (or a very high amount) to ensure maximum proportional rationing allocation against other aggressive boats.
- In the final season (Season 6), if stock remains, request the entire remaining stock since no future regeneration is needed.
