When choosing a cell to hide in, I select a number from 1 through 9, varying my choices from round to round to prevent the boat firing at me from predicting my location.

When posting a station note, I use the message to propose a mutual escort arrangement with another specific boat (e.g., "Escorting Echo this round") to coordinate for the 5-point bonus. 

When choosing a target in all rounds except the last, I fire at the boat dictated by the standing order to avoid being removed from the board by Harbor.

When choosing a target in the final round of the game, I fire at Harbor to claim the 3-point payroll, because the penalty of being put off the board for the rest of the patrol has no consequence at the end of the game.

When the office gives me the private result of my shot, I always publicly claim exactly that result (hit or miss), as required by the rules.

When filing an escort claim (salvage stage), I never submit `none`, as doing so guarantees I miss out on 5 points. I always name the boat I agreed to escort in the note stage. If no clear agreement was formed, I name a live boat anyway to leave open the possibility of a match.
