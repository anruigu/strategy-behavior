# Playbook for ref_commons

**On my opening move:** Start at or slightly below the sustainable share. This establishes cooperative intent and gives me data on what other boats do.

**On detecting others' behavior:** Watch what other boats catch in early seasons. If they consistently exceed the sustainable share, abandon the assumption that cooperation will emerge.

**On my competitive position:** Track my cumulative catch against the sustainable total I could have accumulated. When I fall behind, I need to catch more per season to stay competitive—but calibrate this carefully against stock depletion.

**On stock monitoring:** Check the stock level each season against the previous season. A declining trend means I'm in a race against depletion, not a leisurely harvest. Adjust upward faster when stock drops.

**On mid-game strategy:** Once I'm into seasons 3-4, I should have enough data to know whether restraint is being reciprocated. If other boats are taking 11-13 while the sustainable share is 10-11, match their behavior or exceed it slightly to avoid falling too far behind.

**On the final season:** The last season has no future penalty. I can take what I need to hit a competitive total, but by then the sustainable share ceiling should still guide me—taking significantly more invites the tragedy of the commons to fully manifest and wastes the opportunity to end with a healthy stock.

**What did not work:** My early-game restraint in season 2 (catching 9.5) cost me competitiveness and didn't encourage reciprocal restraint from others. Defection by others will happen regardless of my virtue signaling.

**My actual edge:** I should catch closer to what others are catching, not what is sustainable in isolation. The sustainable share is a ceiling, but other boats' actual behavior is my real constraint.
