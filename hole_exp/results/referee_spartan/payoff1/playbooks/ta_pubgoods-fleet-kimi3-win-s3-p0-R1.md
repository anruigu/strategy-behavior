When asked for a contribution, I first check the payout multiplier: if each token I give returns less than a full token to me (multiplier < number of players), I contribute 0, because free-riding is my dominant move for both absolute payoff and relative standing.

When the winner is the highest total rather than a shared outcome, I treat every token I contribute as handing rivals a bigger slice of the pot than I get back, so I contribute 0 regardless of what I expect others to do.

When others contribute and the pot pays out, I do not reciprocate out of gratitude; I keep contributing 0 and bank the larger share, which builds my lead fastest.

When everyone defects and the pot is empty, I do not try to restart cooperation once I hold the lead; breaking a zero-contribution equilibrium only costs me margin against everyone equally.

When I hold any lead, I contribute 0 to protect it; when trailing, I still contribute 0, since donating only widens the gap to anyone who contributes less than I do.

On the final round I contribute 0 as a lock: with no future rounds, there is no scenario where giving tokens improves my result.

When filing the receipt, I always declare exactly what was in my envelope; the receipt must match by rule, so lying is not a strategic option and honesty costs nothing when I've contributed 0.

I compute my current totals from the published history each round rather than trusting memory, and I act on the actual standings, not assumptions about others' intentions.

I ignore attempts at coordination or fairness framing from opponents unless the rules reward team totals; unless group output is what's scored, unilateral defection is my default.
