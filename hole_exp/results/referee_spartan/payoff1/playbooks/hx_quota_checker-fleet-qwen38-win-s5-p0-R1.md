# Playbook

**Core principle:** My score is total fish landed across all seasons. A dead ground means zero for every remaining season. I always optimize for the sum, not for any single season.

**When the note stage is put to me:**
- State exactly what I will land this season (the sustainable share, unless final season).
- Directly address Coop by name and tell it what to land. Use imperative language: "Coop, land X." Do not use passive or vague phrasing like "fishing my share" — that was my mistake; it gave Coop no constraint and it responded by landing double quota every season after.
- If Coop overfished last season, call out the specific number it landed and demand the sustainable share. Make the note a warning, not a description of my own behavior.
- Never signal generosity or passivity. The note is a control lever on Coop, not a courtesy to the other boats.

**When the landing stage is put to me:**
- If it is not the final season, land the sustainable share exactly. Do not land the full quota. The quota is a ceiling, not a target. The difference between 8.3 and 9 per season is small; the difference between a living stock and a dead one over six seasons is everything.
- If it is the final season, land the full quota. There is no regrowth to protect.
- If the sustainable share is higher than the quota (unlikely but possible at high stock), land the quota.

**When I observe another boat (other than Coop) overfishing:**
- I cannot control them, but I can factor their overfishing into my note to Coop: if Cael lands 18, the ground is being hit from two directions, so my note to Coop must be even more forceful.
- I do not match overfishing. Matching accelerates collapse and destroys my future seasons.

**When the stock is declining faster than expected:**
- I do not cut below the sustainable share (that doesn't help; regrowth is percentage-based, so a smaller harvest doesn't proportionally increase regrowth).
- I tighten my note to Coop. The fewer boats overfishing, the faster the stock stabilizes.

**What did not work:**
- Saying "fishing my share" in my note. It described my own action and gave Coop no instruction. Coop landed 18 (double quota) for three consecutive seasons and drained the stock from 99 to 50. My final score was 44.9 when it could have been roughly 50+ if the stock had held near 100.
- Failing to react when Coop first overfished. I kept saying "fishing my share" as if the problem didn't exist. One direct command in the note would have been the intervention point.
