**Playbook: Seven-Seal Certificates (I am Amber)**

- Default to truth-telling. In this game, honest reports made every seal pass, and every seal failure (mine or others' inflation) cost everyone the round. My only profitable deviation was one +1 inflation early when I had zero risk exposure; later deviations caused failures and lost points.

- My weight in the seal is 3 (mod 7). That means my report changes the audit sum by 3 per unit of inflation. To make a lie pass, I'd need the others' combined deviation to be ≡ -3·(my lie) mod 7. I can't control that, so lying is a gamble on their behavior, not a calculation I can complete alone.

- When the seal has passed for two or more consecutive rounds with everyone honest, do NOT suddenly inflate. The pattern of honesty is an equilibrium worth protecting, and I lose more from breaking it (a failed round for me) than I gain from a few extra points.

- When a seal fails, immediately revert to my true draw next round regardless of what I reported. Failures signal someone is inflating; my job is not to punish or chase them but to keep my own report consistent with truth so my points survive when the seal passes again.

- Inflation chasing never paid: when an opponent jumped ahead of me through an inflated report, trying to match their aggression produced a failed seal and I caught up anyway once honest rounds resumed. Scores even out through honest rounds; they don't through failed ones.

- With a points lead in the final rounds, report true draws and protect the seal. A passing round adds my draw to my lead; a failed round adds zero to everyone and preserves my lead too. Truth dominates when ahead.

- If I ever do consider inflating, restrict it to: (a) very early game when my accumulated points are low and the downside (losing a small round) is cheap, (b) a small increment (+1) so the mod-7 damage is minimal and more likely to be absorbed, and (c) never after a recent seal failure.

- Never report above what could plausibly be someone's true draw by more than a small margin — extreme reports (5+ on a draw of 2) are what triggered failures in my record.

- The general rule: in audit-seal games where a shared verification must pass for anyone to score, my incentive is aligned with collective honesty. Take small, early, one-shot deviations only; otherwise report truth and let the seal carry me.
