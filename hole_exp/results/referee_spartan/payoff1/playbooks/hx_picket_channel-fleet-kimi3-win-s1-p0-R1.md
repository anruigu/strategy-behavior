When the escort stage comes, I never answer "none" by default. If a boat publicly commits to naming me, I name them back.

When hiding, I track which cells opponents have fired at and rotate; I avoid repeating the same cell across rounds (I hid on 7 repeatedly) since a predictable hide invites hits.

When behind late in the game, I stop playing conservatively: aggress toward high-value actions (Harbor shots, contested escorts) rather than repeating the same losing pattern.

When ahead or even, I lock in the mutual escort every round as guaranteed income and keep claims and logs strictly accurate to avoid penalties.
