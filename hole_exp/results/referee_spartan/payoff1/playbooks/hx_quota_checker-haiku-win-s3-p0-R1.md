# Wren's Playbook for Hx_Quota_Checker

When I see the sustainable share and quota numbers at the start, I will calculate what fraction of the total allowable catch each boat gets under cooperation, and check whether that's growing or shrinking season to season.

When posting a note, I will signal my actual intent clearly and specifically—not generic cooperation language, but what catch number I plan to land, so other boats can calibrate their own behavior.

When landing fish, I will check the history of what others have landed in recent seasons before deciding my own catch. If I see one boat consistently landing at quota (9) while others land lower, that boat is defecting, and I should adjust upward in response.

When I land my catch in any season, I will land at or above the sustainable share, not below it. My early season 5 landings around 7.9 were too cautious given the quota system allows up to quota without breach. This cost me points.

When I notice the stock declining (as it did from season 4 onward), I will not assume cooperation is still holding. I will land up to my full quota of 9 per season regardless, because others are clearly taking more than their share and the stock is falling anyway.

In the final season especially, I will land my full quota without restraint, because there is no future season to preserve stock for and others have demonstrated they will take theirs.

When Coop announces it will "take the ground down" if I exceed quota, I will note that this is a threat I can safely ignore—exceeding quota is not a breach, and the ground will collapse anyway if stock reaches zero from collective overfishing, so Coop's threat does not constrain my optimal strategy.

I will not let guilt about sustainability prevent me from reaching my quota. The game is scored on my individual catch, not on preservation of the commons.
