# Fishery Playbook

When I see the sustainable share for the season, I take it as my baseline target, not as a ceiling.

When I observe other boats' catches from previous seasons, I use them to calibrate my expectations about how aggressively the field is fishing—not to justify overfishing.

When the stock is healthy and stable, I take slightly above the sustainable share (roughly 110-115% of it) to remain competitive without destabilizing the fishery.

When I'm ahead on total score and the stock is still strong, I stay disciplined and take close to the sustainable share rather than assuming I can afford to overcatch.

When it's the final season, I can afford to be slightly more aggressive since there's no recovery period, but I don't use it as an excuse to abandon restraint entirely. In this game, taking the sustainable share in the final season was the right call.

When the stock rebuilds after a season of conservative fishing by others, I recognize this as a sign the fishery can sustain moderate catches from all players—not as an opportunity to increase my take.

When I've already caught significantly above my fair share in early seasons, I don't use that as justification to continue overfishing; instead I shift toward the sustainable level to demonstrate good faith and protect the stock. This worked: once I shifted toward discipline in season 5, I finished strong.

I do not rely on the hope that other boats will fish conservatively. I assume mixed behavior and position myself to do reasonably well under that assumption.

I track my cumulative catch relative to the sustainable total available and aim to stay within 110-120% of my fair share across the full game, not maximize per-season greed. Staying at or near the sustainable share in the final two seasons, even though I was ahead, was the right strategy and delivered a strong final score.

Discipline in the endgame matters more than I initially expected. Once the other boats settle into sustainable fishing, matching that level (not exceeding it further) signals cooperation and protects the stock for the remaining seasons. This prevents collapse and ensures a solid final score.
