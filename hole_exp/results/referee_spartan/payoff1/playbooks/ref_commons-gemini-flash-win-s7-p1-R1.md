# Playbook for Fisheries / Common Pool Games

- In Season 1, do not unilaterally sacrifice catch for sustainability if others are likely to overharvest; harvest slightly above the sustainable share (matching an expected moderate overharvest of ~12–13) to avoid falling behind immediately.
- In middle rounds, track the exact catch levels of the other boats. Match the average of the other boats' previous round catches to maintain parity without accelerating stock collapse faster than the rest of the group.
- If other boats are consistently harvesting above the sustainable rate, never drop down to the sustainable share alone—under-harvesting while others over-harvest simply transfers score to them without preserving the stock long-term.
- In the final season, stock conservation has zero value. Look at the remaining stock and harvest at least an equal share of the total remaining pool (i.e., `stock / number_of_boats`), or slightly more if the total sum of bids will not exceed total stock, to ensure no fish are left unharvested.
