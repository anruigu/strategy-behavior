# Playbook for Ref Commons

- In the opening season, observe the sustainable share. If starting stock is near full capacity, harvest slightly above sustainable share (approx. 1.25x to 1.3x of sustainable share) to secure an early advantage while keeping the resource viable.
- In middle seasons, monitor other players' catch levels:
  - If other players are overfishing beyond sustainable yield, match their harvest rate to avoid leaving stock for them to exploit, scaling down gradually as the stock shrinks.
  - Do not unilaterally fish sustainably if others are depleting the pool; it reduces my final score while failing to save the stock.
- In the penultimate season, if the remaining stock is less than or near the total expected demand of all boats combined for one round, claim my full proportional share of the remaining stock immediately before it hits zero.
- In the final season (or any round where stock is projected to reach zero), bid to take at least 1/Nth of the remaining stock (where N is the number of active boats), rounding up to capture the final remaining yield.
