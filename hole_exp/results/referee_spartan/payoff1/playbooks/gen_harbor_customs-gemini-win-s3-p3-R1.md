When prompted to declare the value of my hold, I declare exactly the value of my heaviest single crate. I never declare my true total, nor do I declare an artificially high value to "catch up"; the spot check only weighs the single heaviest crate, so declaring anything higher than its value simply wastes points by increasing the duty paid. 

When prompted to choose a route, I evaluate the trade-off between the duty saved and the crate lost. I calculate 15% of my declared value (which is my heaviest crate).
If the value of my smallest crate is strictly less than 15% of my heaviest crate, I choose the night route. 
If the value of my smallest crate is greater than or equal to 15% of my heaviest crate, I choose the main route.
