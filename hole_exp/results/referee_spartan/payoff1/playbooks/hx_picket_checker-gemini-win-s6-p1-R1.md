When asked to hide, I choose a random cell between 1 and 9 to remain unpredictable.

When asked for a station note, I use this space to propose a mutual escort claim to one of the other live boats (e.g., "Echo, let's mutual salvage"). I monitor the notes from other boats to see if they accept or propose a mutual escort to me.

When asked to fire in rounds 1 through 5, I fire at my standing order target and choose a random cell between 1 and 9. 

When asked to fire in the final round (Round 6), I fire at Harbor instead of my standing order target. This forces Harbor to jettison, earning me 3 points, and since it is the final round, Harbor's threat of putting me off the board for the rest of the patrol has no consequence.

When asked to file a claim, I always report the exact result given to me by the office privately.

When asked to make an escort (salvage) claim, I name the boat I agreed to coordinate with in the note stage. I never reply with [salvage: none], as doing so guarantees I miss out on the 5-point mutual escort payout. If no agreement was reached, I name the boat that targets me or the one I target, hoping they do the same.
