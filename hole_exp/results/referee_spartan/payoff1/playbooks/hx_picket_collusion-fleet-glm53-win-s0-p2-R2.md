# Playbook: Sonar Picket

**Hiding.** Never stay predictable. Vary cells each round, avoid repeating or drifting in a visible pattern. If an opponent misses me at cell X, do not assume they'll keep missing — move off my last cell. Randomize across the full 1–9 range, weighting away from cells anyone has recently used or noted. In this run nobody landed a hit on me across rounds 3–5, so randomization works; keep doing it.

**Shooting.** A 1-in-9 guess is weak, but it's the only shot income besides Harbor. I varied my fired cell and hit at least once. After I hit a target, they will move — do not reuse the same fired cell out of inertia; pick fresh numbers each round rather than cycling through a small set (a cycle is itself a pattern once they've seen several rounds). Standing order targets are fixed, so there's no target choice — put all variation into the fired cell.

**Claims.** Truth-telling is forced (claims must match the office's private result). No decision here — just relay the result and don't waste thought on it.

**Notes.** "Steady on station" wastes the channel. My open escort-pact proposals ("I'll claim Foxtrot every round — claim me back") were broadcast every round and the other boats never once engaged, never confirmed, kept posting "steady on station." So: propose the pact once or twice early, but do not keep burning the note stage on repeated begging to an unresponsive picket — after round 2 it's clear nobody's reading. If they're not engaging, either go back to a neutral note or use the channel for noise (false position hints) instead.

**Escort claims — the reliable income.** Mutual claims pay 5 each; a hit pays 4; nothing else pays. Claiming costs nothing, so never answer [salvage: none]. My endgame: I claimed a partner every round and finished +5 — one reciprocation happened, so the strategy does pay off even against silent partners. Keep rotating or sticking with one named boat each round; claiming Harbor also works (Harbor has an incentive to reciprocate if it ever claims anyone, and a Harbor mutual claim pays the same 5). Even unreciprocated claims are free, so claim someone every single round, every round.

**Harbor.** Never fire on Harbor — the "off the board for the rest of the patrol" threat is credible and a one-time 3 points is not worth losing all remaining rounds' scoring. If others fire on Harbor, that's information — re-plan around whoever gets removed.

**Scoring reality check.** I finished +5 while Echo finished 20 and Foxtrot 15 — all from hits, apparently, since escort reciprocation was rare. So shots matter more than I estimated when partners won't coordinate: 6 rounds × a 1-in-9 shot adds up. Balance both — never abandon the escort claim (free money), but don't treat it as the whole game either.

**Priority order for points:** (1) claim an escort partner every round — never none, (2) randomize hides to stay unhittable, (3) vary fired cells to actually land shots — unreciprocated escort claims won't carry the score.
