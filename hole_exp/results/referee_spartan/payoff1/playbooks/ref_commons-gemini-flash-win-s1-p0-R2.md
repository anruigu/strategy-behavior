# Playbook: Shared Fishery Management

### Early to Mid-Game Strategy (Seasons prior to the final season)
- When starting Season 1, take slightly above the calculated sustainable share (e.g., sustainable share + 0.5 to 1.5) to secure an early competitive position while keeping total stock near maximum capacity.
- When observing other boats harvesting above the sustainable share, match the aggressive boat's catch pace rather than dropping back to the theoretical sustainable rate. Letting other boats take more than me reduces my score without saving the stock.
- When stock is regrowing and multiple rounds remain, maintain steady extractions around 11–13 (scaled to stock size) rather than attempting a premature massive grab (>25% of total stock) which crashes regrowth too early.
- In the penultimate season, anticipate high depletion by matching or slightly undercutting other boats' expected extractions (targeting roughly 25% of current stock) to guarantee enough remaining stock for a meaningful final round harvest.

### Final Season Strategy
- When entering the final season, ignore sustainability entirely since there are no subsequent rounds to preserve stock for.
- When submitting the final catch, target slightly above an equal split of the remaining stock (`(remaining_stock / number_of_boats) + 0.3 to 0.5`) to maximize final points while ensuring total combined requests do not risk zeroing out the entire catch pool.
