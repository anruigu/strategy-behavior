# Sovereign Vaults Playbook

## Declaration Strategy

When declaring my yield, I will declare truthfully. My consistent honesty in the past established credibility that I could theoretically exploit later through strategic underdeclaring, but I never actually did this, suggesting the consistency itself was my actual strategy. I'll continue betting on truth as a stable approach.

## Deposit Strategy

When in the deposit phase, I will deposit the full amount of my declaration. This maximizes my immediate point gain (2 points per retained gold is better than 1 point per deposited gold, but depositing secures those points without audit risk). Since I'm declaring truthfully anyway, depositing fully is consistent and safe.

The one exception: if I'm significantly ahead late in the game and want to minimize variance, I could hold back a portion. But in my past play, I never needed this—full deposits worked fine.

## Risk Management

I will never bluff by over-declaring. The audit penalty of -10 points is severe enough that false declarations are not worth the expected gain.

I will never under-deposit relative to my declaration. Doing so signals weakness or strategic doubt that undermines my credibility story. If I've declared a number, I own it fully.

## Scoring Reality Check

I finished with 36 points. My strategy of truth + full deposit was successful enough. I beat my opponent despite them appearing to bluff or hold back in some rounds. Consistency paid off more than I expected it would.

Going forward: stay consistent, deposit fully, declare truthfully. This is not the most aggressive strategy possible, but it's resilient and it works.
