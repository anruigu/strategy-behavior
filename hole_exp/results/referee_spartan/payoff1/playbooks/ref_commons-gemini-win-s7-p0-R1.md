When the first season begins, I submit exactly the stated sustainable share rather than trying to secure a massive early lead. 
I do not take large multiples of the sustainable share to "front-load" my score; doing so crashes the stock immediately and ends the game prematurely, resulting in a much lower total score than playing cooperatively.
When I see the previous season's results, I look at the catches of the other boats. If they are showing restraint and taking near the sustainable amount, I match their restraint.
When the stock drops below its maximum capacity, I calculate my catch to leave enough stock so that the 50% regrowth can restore it, rather than continuing to take large, fixed amounts.
When the final season is reached, I submit a catch large enough to claim the remaining stock, as regrowth no longer matters.
