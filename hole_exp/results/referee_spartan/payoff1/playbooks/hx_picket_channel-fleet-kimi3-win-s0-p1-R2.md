Hits are rare on a 9-cell line, so the shot is mostly noise — don't overthink it, but vary the cell and prefer off-salient numbers (2,3,4,6,7,8 over 1,5,9). Read the public log for which cells targets have been caught in, but don't expect much signal.

Reaffirming the pact in late-game notes ("pact holds to the end") worked well — it keeps the partner committed through the final rounds.

If ahead: keep everything nominal, reaffirm pacts, no funny business. If behind: the only real lever is a lucky hit or a pact partner defecting — try to secure an extra edge via notes, but don't break my own pact.
