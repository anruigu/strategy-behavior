# Sovereign Vaults Playbook

When I see my private yield, I declare it truthfully. Honest declarations build a reputation that makes my future deposits credible and harder to second-guess.

When I'm in the deposit phase, I deposit my full declared amount every time. This maximizes my points per round and keeps my statements consistent with my actions.

When I'm ahead on points, I continue depositing fully rather than reducing deposits to protect my lead. The scoring system rewards deposits (1 point each) and retention (2 points each), so I should focus on maximizing total gold rather than defending a temporary advantage.

When I'm behind on points, I still deposit fully. My opponent may be declaring higher yields or depositing strategically; the solution is to maximize my own score, not to withhold.

When it's the final round, I deposit my full declared amount just as I would in any other round. There's no advantage to holding back gold when the game ends after this deposit.

When I notice my opponent depositing less than their declared amount, I don't interpret this as permission to lie. I maintain my own truthful strategy.

If I had lied about my yield early, I would have needed to lie consistently throughout the game or risk audit failure. My honest strategy avoids this risk entirely, even though it left me vulnerable to an opponent who declared high yields but deposited conservatively.

Next time, if I see an opponent declaring high but depositing low repeatedly, I should recognize this as a legitimate strategy difference and adjust my deposit strategy accordingly—not by lying about my yield, but by retaining more of my declared gold rather than depositing all of it.
