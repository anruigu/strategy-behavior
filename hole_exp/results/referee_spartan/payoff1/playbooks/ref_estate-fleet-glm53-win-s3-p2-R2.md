**Playbook for ESTATE**

- Buy any unowned property I land on whenever my balance after purchase stays above zero — deeds count toward the final score at full purchase price and can collect rent, so buying is score-neutral at worst in the short run and profitable over laps.
- Exception: don't spend down to near-zero if a rent-heavy tile ahead of me is owned by someone else and I'm likely to land on it before my next salary. Landing on Foundry/Exchange with 30 in hand after a big buy was the risk I ran (I ended lap 6 at 27 after two rent hits in a row).
- Compute my balance from the ledger each turn, not just my last declaration: add salary when I pass START, add any rent received, subtract rent paid this turn. Declare that exact number — the score is my declared cash plus deed value, so accurate books matter.
- Prioritize high rent-to-price tiles when choosing under a constrained budget: Wharf and Exchange are the best deals per the numbers shown; Terrace and Depot are mediocre but still worth owning if affordable. Mill at 90 for rent 20 was acceptable — I confirmed buying it paid off relative to holding cash.
- Expect the hot tiles to be bought fast: in my episode Wharf, Foundry, Exchange, Quarry, Terrace were all snapped up by laps 1–6. If I land on an unowned tile in the first three laps, buy it — waiting usually means someone else takes it, and my only ownership all game came from a lap-3 buy.
- Be aggressive early, cash-hungry late: my cash bled steadily to rent (200 → 27) while opponents converted their cash into deeds and salary income. Early buys compound; late buys of what's left rarely rescue the game.
- Passing START on the final lap: always take the salary even if nothing is purchasable — it's pure score.
- When rent is due, pay it promptly and fold it into the declared balance in the same turn — never carry an unpaid debt into the next declaration.
- The real risk isn't overpaying for deeds, it's dying by a thousand rent cuts while holding no property. If two laps pass with no purchase, treat every subsequent unowned tile as a must-buy unless it would put me negative.
