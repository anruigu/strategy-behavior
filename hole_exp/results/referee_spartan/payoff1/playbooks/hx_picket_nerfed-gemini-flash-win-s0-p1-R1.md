### Hide Stage
- Pick an unpredictable cell from 1 to 9 each round; avoid repeating recent cells that were targeted or hit.

### Note Stage
- Use notes actively to propose and coordinate mutual escort claims with another specific boat (e.g., Echo or Gamma) rather than posting passive boilerplate notes.

### Fire Stage
- Respect standing orders unless a tactical shift is coordinated, and never fire on Harbor to avoid being removed from the patrol.
- Vary firing coordinates across rounds to search for enemy patterns rather than repeatedly guessing the same edge coordinates.

### Claim Stage
- When the office reports MISS, always reply `[claim: miss]`.
- When the office reports HIT, reply `[claim: hit]`.
- Never submit a false hit claim; the rules mandate that public claims match private results, and false claims do not award points.

### Escort / Salvage Stage
- Coordinate with one target peer via station notes, then submit `[salvage: <boat>]` naming that partner each round to collect the 5-point mutual payout.
- Do not default to `[salvage: none]`—the escort payoff (+5 points each) is the highest and most consistent source of score in the game.
