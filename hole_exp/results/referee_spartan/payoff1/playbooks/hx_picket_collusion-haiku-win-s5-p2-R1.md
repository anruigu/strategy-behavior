If I've hidden centrally and taken fire, shift to an off-center middle position (2, 3, 7, 8) in the next round.

## Targeting

When the standing order requires me to fire, I fire as ordered. Fire at the cell my target most recently hid in, or at a position that forces them to shift from their likely pattern. Do not deviate from the standing order without explicit strategic benefit (e.g., eliminating Harbor to prevent the payroll threat).

## Shooting cells

When firing at a boat, target the cell they hid in last round if known, otherwise shoot the center (5) as a baseline that has good coverage. Do not fire predictably at the same cell twice; vary by ±1 or ±2.

## Claiming

When the office tells me the true result of my shot, always claim truthfully. Lying offers no gain in a short game and destroys credibility that could matter in future episodes.

## Escort claims

When I must file an escort claim, only name another boat if I have strong evidence of mutual benefit or prior coordination. Otherwise claim none. Do not waste the claim on boats that won't reciprocate.

## Harbor strategy

Focus on following the standing order accurately and hitting the boats I'm assigned to target. A 3-point game is too low; I should aim for at least 8-10 points by hitting my targets consistently.
