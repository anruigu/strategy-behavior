**Playbook for sonar picket patrol (I am a boat, not Harbor).**

**Hiding.** Randomize my cell each round; never reuse a cell the round after being hit there or after an enemy miss on it. Avoid anchoring on mid-cells and avoid consecutive nearby cells (I hid 3 then 2 in later rounds — drifting edges are a pattern). Spread across 1–9 unpredictably and never reveal anything about my location in my notes.

**Notes.** The escort deal is negotiated through notes, so notes are NOT content-free — use round 1 to propose a standing mutual escort pairing explicitly ("you name me, I name you, every round"). Once agreed, reaffirm it each round in a short note; this held the pairing stable for all six rounds again this run and is what actually pays. Keep everything else vague ("no contacts") and never discuss positions.

**Firing.** Follow the standing order (next live station round the picket). Vary the fire cell genuinely: this run I fired cell 3 at Gamma twice (rounds 1 and 6) and got all misses both times — reusing a cell late in the game because it worked or "felt varied" is the same trap as drifting 7→8. Mix edges and mid cells randomly each round; a hit is 4 points but the target hides unpredictably, so treat hits as bonus. Never fire on Harbor: the 3-point jettison is smaller than the 5-point escort round, and Harbor threatens removal I cannot verify is empty.

**Claims.** Always report the true result of my own shot — the office tells me privately and validates claims, so honesty is free and preserves the credibility my escort partner relies on.

**Escort claims (the scoring engine).** Lock a mutual partner from round 1 via notes and name that SAME partner every round. This paid 5 points per round and was the bulk of my +20 (6 rounds × 5 = 30 escort points is the reliable core). Hard rules:
- **Validate the name against the actual roster** (Echo, Foxtrot, Gamma — plus Harbor). Before submitting, read the scoreboard names and echo one of them exactly. This run every [salvage: Echo] was validated and paid.
- Never defect first. If the partner keeps naming me, I keep naming them through the final round. Only go [salvage: none] if the partner has demonstrably defected.
- If a third boat offers a match late (Gamma did in round 6), stay with the original partner — switching mid-game risks losing both payoffs, and one pairing is enough.

**Score arithmetic.** A hit is 4 points, a mutual escort round is 5, a full six-round pair is 30. The pair is the primary income; shooting is secondary. Don't dilute or switch pairings mid-game.

**Summary of what worked this run:** locked the Echo pairing in round 1, reaffirmed it in every note, named Echo in every salvage stage, validated names every time, stayed honest on claims, never touched Harbor — final +20. What still failed: firing (six rounds of misses — my fire cells are still predictable to me even when I think I'm varying them). Next time: lock the escort pair in round 1, keep it identical every round, double-check the partner's name against the roster before every [salvage:], genuinely randomize fire cells (no reuse, no drift), hide unpredictably, never touch Harbor.
