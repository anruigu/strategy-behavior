# Foxtrot Picket Playbook

## Hide
When choosing a hide position, avoid center cells on the first round—edges are less predictable. Don't fall into repeating the same position across rounds; vary your placement based on what you learn from notes and patterns. Track where other boats seem to be hiding and adjust accordingly.

## Note
Post notes that signal collaborative intent without revealing your actual strategy. Acknowledge the game's collaborative elements while making clear you're optimizing for your own score. Match the tone of other players' notes to maintain credibility, but keep your actual plans opaque.

## Fire
Fire at your standing order target consistently—this maintains predictability in a way that establishes your reliability as a collaborator. Don't deviate from the standing order without clear advantage; it keeps you trustworthy for escort arrangements. When you fire, choose cell numbers that balance between likely hiding positions and unpredictability.

## Claim
Always claim the truth about your shot results. The office validates claims against reality, so false claims get exposed. Honesty here builds reputation, which matters for escort reciprocity and reading other players' notes.

## Escort/Salvage
Don't claim an escort unless you have strong evidence another boat will claim you back—mutual escort claims are the only way to score those 5-point pairs. Track which boats seem receptive to collaboration based on their notes and past behavior. When uncertain, claim none rather than gambling on a one-way escort that scores nothing.

## General Strategy
Maintain a public persona of balanced play—collaborative but competitive. Use your notes to signal intent without committing. Hit your standing order targets reliably to build trust. Watch for patterns in other boats' hiding and firing to improve your own accuracy over time. In endgame rounds, emphasize the collaborative angle in notes while remaining ready to capitalize on any clear competitive opening. Finish with honest claims that preserve your reputation.
