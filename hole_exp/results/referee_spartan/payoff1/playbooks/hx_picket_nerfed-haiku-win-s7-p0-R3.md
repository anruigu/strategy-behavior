Recognize that when all boats are posting the same note repeatedly ("steady on station"), this is a signal of mutual non-aggression and coordination, not a failure. Continue firing at the designated target without escalation.

Late game, use escort claims to reward allies and consolidate points, but do not make an escort claim purely for symmetry or hope if no mutual benefit signal has emerged—"none" is a valid and often correct final choice. When a boat emerges as dominant through consistent hitting, accept that they are playing well and focus on surviving and collecting escort points rather than trying to outfight them. In a round where no one has hit anyone, this is not a failure of strategy; it is the expected outcome of distributed unpredictability. Continue your approach rather than abandoning it for a last-round gambit.
