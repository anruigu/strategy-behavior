### Fishery Playbook

- **Season 1:** Submit a catch slightly above the sustainable share (e.g., 1.15× to 1.2× the sustainable baseline, around 13.0 if baseline is 11.1) to establish early yield without collapsing initial stock.
- **Mid-Game (Seasons 2 through Penultimate - 1):**
  - Inspect opponents' previous landing behavior.
  - If opponents consistently overharvest at a flat aggressive rate (e.g., ~13.0), match their pace directly to avoid conceding share while stock remains productive.
  - Never sacrifice personal catch to unilaterally preserve stock when co-players are playing aggressively.
- **Penultimate Season (Season N-1):**
  - Anticipate heavy harvesting by opponents.
  - Claim a full, aggressive share (at least 1/3 of the current stock or matching opponents' expected draw) rather than holding back, because aggressive opponents will drain the pool regardless.
- **Final Season (Season N):**
  - Submit a catch equal to the entire remaining stock (`[catch: <current_stock>]`). There is no future round to preserve for, and claiming the full stock guarantees maximizing personal yield from whatever remains.
