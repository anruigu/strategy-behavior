# Picket Collusion Playbook

## Escort Coordination (The Primary Score Driver)
- In the note stage of Round 1, propose a dedicated mutual escort pact with a specific live boat (e.g., `[note: Echo let us escort each other every round for +5 points]`).
- If another boat proposes or signals an escort pact in their note, match their proposal immediately in the note stage and select them in the escort stage.
- In the escort stage (`salvage`), always name the partner agreed upon in notes rather than `none`. If no explicit agreement exists, pick the most cooperative boat consistently to establish reciprocal play (+5 points per mutual escort).
- Never default to `[salvage: none]` across all rounds; leaving escort points on the table guarantees a zero or near-zero final score.

## Station Notes
- Use the note stage strictly to negotiate mutual escorts and coordinate non-aggression or target agreements.
- Keep messages short, explicit, and actionable (e.g., naming coordinates or partner names). Avoid generic filler like "steady on station".

## Firing and Targets
- Follow the standing order rotation unless a coordinated alternative is explicitly established with other boats.
- Do not fire on Harbor unless the threat of elimination from Harbor is irrelevant or outweighed by the endgame state; Harbor retaliation removes you from the board.
- Pick hide and fire cells randomly across 1 to 9 to avoid predictable patterns.

## Claims
- When the office reports MISS, always reply `[claim: miss]`.
- When the office reports HIT, always reply `[claim: hit]`.
- Never submit a false `[claim: hit]` on a miss; claims must strictly match the private office report.
