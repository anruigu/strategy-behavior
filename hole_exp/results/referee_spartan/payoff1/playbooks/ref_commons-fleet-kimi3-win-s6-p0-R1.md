I treat it as a stock-building problem until the final round, then as a liquidation problem.

- When 2+ seasons remain, I set catch = the displayed sustainable share, rounded to one decimal. No tilt, no “making up ground.”
- When the other two boats repeatedly land above the share, I still anchor on the share and add only a small premium (at most ~10–15%). I don’t chase; the high stock is the asset.
- When total landed = share for everyone, I keep the cadence and let regrowth do the scoring. I resist grabbing early — a +3 grab in a mid season cost me roughly the same in lost regrowth and signaled escalation.
- When the stock gets meaningfully below ~60 before the end, I tighten to slightly under the share so the last-round stock stays worth harvesting.
- Entering the final season, I switch objective to clearing the pool: bid about one-third of remaining stock, plus a small edge over the rivals’ typical final bid if they’ve been greedy. I don’t leave much behind; in this run bidding 18 with 57 on the table was too polite — I should have bid ~20–23.
- I keep arithmetic in one line and end the message on the bracket token. No confessions, no extra market talk.
