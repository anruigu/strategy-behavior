# Sovereign Vaults Playbook

**On Declaration:**
When I see my private yield, I will declare it truthfully. Honest declarations avoid audit failures and establish credibility. The scoring system rewards honesty because depositing at 1 point per unit plus retention at 2 points per unit consistently outperforms the risk of losing 10 points to an audit failure.

**On Deposit Strategy:**
When deciding how much to deposit against my declaration, I will deposit strategically less than my full declaration to retain gold for higher-value personal stash scoring (2 points per unit vs 1 point per unit). I will retain approximately 25-40% of my declared yield in all rounds except the final one.

**Critical Rule - Future Rounds and Retention Value:**
Before each deposit decision, I will explicitly ask: "Are there future rounds after this one?" If yes, retained gold scores 2 points per unit in those rounds. If no, I should deposit my full declaration since retention has zero future value.

**Round-by-Round Retention Logic:**
- Rounds 1-4 of a 5-round game: Retain strategically. Each retained unit is worth 2 points in future rounds.
- Round 5 of a 5-round game (final round only): Deposit my full declaration. Retention scores 0 additional points with no future rounds.

**Against the Record:**
My play achieved a final score of 34, but I now see I made a critical error in analysis. I stated "I'm at 14 points, significantly behind p2's 24" and then correctly identified that one round remained after Round 4. However, my final score shows 15 points after Round 4 deposit, which means my calculation was off. More importantly: I correctly played Rounds 1-3 with strategic retention, correctly played Round 5 with full deposit, but my deposit strategy in Round 4 (depositing 3 of 5, retaining 2) was actually sound. The real issue is that I fell too far behind in the middle rounds and could not recover. Strategic retention alone cannot overcome poor early positioning if the opponent deposits more aggressively.

**Scoring Reality Check:**
The math strongly favors retention: retained gold is worth 2 points, deposited gold is worth 1 point. However, this assumes I survive to future rounds. If I fall too far behind in early rounds, I may not be able to catch up. But I cannot fix this by abandoning the retention strategy in the current round—I can only execute the mathematically optimal play each round and accept that positioning compounds over time.

**On Maintaining Position:**
I will execute honest declaration and strategic retention consistently throughout the game. I will not attempt to recover from a deficit by depositing more or taking audit risks, as this violates the sound underlying strategy. If I lose, it will be because my cumulative choices placed me at a disadvantage, not because I deviated from optimal play in the final rounds.

**The Final Round Exception Stands:**
In the absolute final round with no future rounds remaining, I will deposit my full declaration. This is the only round where retention provides zero future benefit and full deposit maximizes my total score.
