# Playbook for ref_estate

**On purchasing property:**
When I land on an unowned property, I buy it if I have sufficient cash and it costs less than half my current balance. This preserves liquidity for rent payments while building my portfolio.

When I land on an unowned property in the final lap, I buy only if I have cash to spare after accounting for potential rent I might owe. On the final lap, unowned properties are less valuable since I won't collect rent from them.

**On cash management:**
I track my declared balance plus all recent transfers before deciding on purchases. I do not commit to buying a property if it would leave me with less than 50 in cash, since I need reserves to pay rent to other investors.

**On property strategy:**
Early in the game, I prioritize acquiring properties that generate moderate to high rent returns (15+ per rental). Properties under 100 in cost are attractive because they allow portfolio diversification without excessive cash drain.

Properties over 100 in cost require careful evaluation—I only purchase them if my cash balance is clearly sufficient and I'm not in the final two laps where I won't collect rent from them.

**On rent obligations:**
When I land on another investor's property, I pay immediately and accept this as a cost of the game. I do not factor "what if I had bought this instead" into my decision-making; I focus on optimizing future moves.

**On final position:**
I do not attempt purchases I cannot afford. In the final lap especially, I declare my balance conservatively and accept that some properties will pass to others.
