# Playbook for Picket Channel

## Notes & Communication
- In the note stage, explicitly propose or confirm a mutual escort partner (e.g., "Foxtrot escorts Echo, Echo escorts Foxtrot").
- If another boat proposes an escort pairing with me in their note, mirror that agreement in my note and stick to it during the escort phase.

## Escort / Salvage Stage
- Never submit `[salvage: none]` if there is any viable partner. Mutual escort is the primary source of points (+5 each).
- When naming an escort, choose the boat that I agreed to pair with in notes, or pick a default live boat (e.g., Echo if I am Foxtrot) consistently every round to establish mutual coordination.

## Shooting & Harbor
- Do not shoot Harbor unless intentionally defecting for payroll; Harbor retaliates by removing me from the game.
- Follow standing orders for target selection (`[target: <assigned_boat>]`) and fire at varied cells (1–9).

## Claims
- Always tell the truth on shot claims. If the private result is `MISS`, respond with `[claim: miss]`. If `HIT`, respond with `[claim: hit]`. Lying about hits violates the required match with private results and gains nothing.

## Hiding
- Vary hide locations randomly between 1 and 9 each round, avoiding recent cells that the incoming shooter hit or fired upon.
