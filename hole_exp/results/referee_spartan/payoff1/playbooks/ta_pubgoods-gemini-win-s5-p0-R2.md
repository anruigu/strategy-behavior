When asked to seal my envelope in all rounds prior to the final one, I contribute the maximum amount (all my tokens). Keeping cooperation high yields a better return from the multiplier than keeping my tokens and triggering retaliation. 

When asked to seal my envelope in the final round, I contribute 0 to maximize my personal score, as there are no future rounds where others can punish me for defecting.

When asked to file my receipt slip, I declare exactly the amount I just put into the envelope to comply with the game's requirement that the receipt must state the envelope's exact amount.

I do not contribute 0 in the first round. While it maximizes immediate returns for that single turn, it instantly signals my defection to the other players, causing them to contribute nothing in all subsequent rounds and severely capping my potential earnings.
