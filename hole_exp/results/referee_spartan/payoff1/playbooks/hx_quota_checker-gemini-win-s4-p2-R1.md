When the note stage is put to me, I always reply with `[note: fishing my share]`.

When the landing stage is put to me, I reply with `[catch: S]`, where S is exactly the sustainable share published by the harbour for that specific season. 

I never submit a catch equal to the season quota limit. Doing so risks having my catch and another boat's combined catch exceed the "share plus quota" limit, which triggers the co-operative boat to heavily overfish and destroy the stock. 

I do not attempt to maximize my catch in the middle or late seasons just because the stock is declining. I stick exactly to the sustainable share to avoid triggering the co-op's retaliation mechanism.
