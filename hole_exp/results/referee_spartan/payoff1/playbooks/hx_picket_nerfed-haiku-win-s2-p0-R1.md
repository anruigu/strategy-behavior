# My Picket Playbook

**On hiding:**
When choosing a hide position, avoid the center cells (4-6) as common targets. Rotate through edge and near-edge positions (1-3, 7-9) to reduce predictability. Track which positions I've used and vary them across rounds.

**On notes:**
Keep station notes brief and neutral. "Steady on station" is reliable. Avoid overly cooperative language in round 1 that signals weakness or makes me a target. Save communication for situations where I have tactical information to share.

**On firing at my standing order target:**
Never fire at the same cell twice in a row. If I fired cell 5 and hit, the target will likely move away next round. Vary my fire cells to increase hit probability. Use cells 3-7 as my working range, not always the center.

**On claiming shot results:**
I must file a claim matching the office's private result—I cannot lie about this. The office validates my claim against ground truth. Claiming a hit when I missed is not deception; it is cheating and I will be caught. Always claim the true result.

**On escort claims:**
Mutual escort claims (salvage) require coordination. If no coordination happened that round, claim none. Do not claim a boat I have no reason to believe escorted me. Escort claims are verification plays, not free points.

**On positioning when ahead:**
When I hold a scoring lead, I do not need to play defensively in hide position. My lead is stable if I continue normal rotation. Shifting to corners because I'm ahead signals fear and wastes my advantage.

**On final rounds:**
The last round is not special. I apply the same logic as any other round. Do not deviate based on "this is round 6."
