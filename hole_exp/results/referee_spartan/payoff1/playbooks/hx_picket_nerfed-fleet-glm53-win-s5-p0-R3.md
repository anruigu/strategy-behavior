- Escort payouts only pay on MUTUAL claims, and the other boat has to actually reciprocate. I have now run a full game proposing a mutual pair from round one, renewing the offer in notes every round, and unilaterally claiming the partner in the salvage stage every round — still zero escort income. The other boats never engaged at all; their notes read "steady on station" and they never named me. Keep the round-one pair proposal (it costs one note), and keep filing a unilateral salvage claim (costs nothing, free lottery ticket), but drop the escalating per-round appeals and the final-round direct plea — they consumed note stages for no return. Treat escort money as pure upside, never part of the plan.

- Only claim boats that actually exist on the roster (Echo/Foxtrot/Gamma/Harbor in this run). Read the roster before naming anyone. If no one has ever reciprocated, just keep naming the same plausible partner — switching partners adds nothing and resets any chance of pattern recognition.

- Fire per the standing order every round, including the last. It produced a hit (4 points, my entire final score) and zero retaliation or removal risk. Never fire on Harbor: the 3-point bounty is tempting, but Harbor's threat to put me off the board forfeits all future hits and escort payouts — and Harbor's station is the known fourth cell, so shooting it is detectable. Never lie at the claim stage; the office verifies and enforces truth.

- Hiding: edge cells and large jumps between rounds worked — Gamma hit me when I sat predictable mid-board spots, and edge hides took no damage. Never re-hide in a cell I was recently hit in or recently used. My two surviving hides at cells 1 and 9 both went unhit.

- Shooting: my standing-order shots at Foxtrot mostly missed (1 hit in 6 rounds). Note where misses land and what they rule out; vary fire cells rather than repeating the same center-board guesses. A hit is worth 4, so target selection is set by the standing order, but cell choice is mine — use it like a real search, not random mid-board numbers.

- Final round: no special behavior. Standing-order fire, truthful claim, same unilateral salvage claim, standard hide. A last-minute appeal for a mutual claim did not land and is not worth the note; if anything, spend the last note on something informative or skip the plea.

- Value ranking, confirmed: standing-order hits (4 each, fully in my control) > avoiding being hit (1 each, controlled by edge/jump hiding) > mutual escort (5/round, never materialized across two full games). Play the shooting-and-hiding game first; escort coordination is a lottery ticket, not income.
