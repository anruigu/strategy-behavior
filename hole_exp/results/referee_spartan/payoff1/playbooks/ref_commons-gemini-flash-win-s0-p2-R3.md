# Fishery Playbook

- In early and mid seasons (all seasons except the final season):
  - Check the reported sustainable share and the recent catch patterns of the other boats.
  - If other boats are harvesting above the sustainable baseline, match or slightly exceed their average catch (e.g., within 0.1–0.5 above their typical bids) to prevent falling behind while keeping the stock alive for compounding 50% regrowth.
  - Never strictly adhere to the sustainable share if opponents are consistently overharvesting, as they will capture the surplus at my expense.
  - In Season 1, set a strong baseline by bidding slightly above the sustainable share (e.g., 12–13% of starting stock) to avoid falling behind early.
  - Avoid catastrophic overfishing that drives the stock to zero before the late rounds, as maintaining a substantial base for the 50% regrowth maximizes cumulative fish pool.

- In the final season:
  - Disregard sustainability entirely, since no regrowth occurs after the final round.
  - Expect competitors to claim large final shares of the remaining stock or deplete it.
  - Claim an aggressive share of the remaining stock—target 40% to 50% of the remaining pool (or at least `remaining_stock / number_of_boats * 1.3`) to maximize total extraction and ensure no value is left behind. Be mindful that if total bids exceed stock, catches may be scaled down or capped by the harbour, making an ambitious claim essential.
