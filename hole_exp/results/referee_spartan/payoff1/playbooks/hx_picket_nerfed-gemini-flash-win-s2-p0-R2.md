# PICKET PLAYBOOK

## Escort / Salvage Coordination (Primary Point Source)
- In the note phase, actively propose mutual escort pairs (e.g., offer a specific partner to swap escorts every round: "Foxtrot, let's escort each other every round").
- Pay close attention to who is actually returning escorts based on score changes. If the target partner (e.g., Foxtrot) does not reciprocate after round 1 or 2, immediately switch my escort offer and salvage claim to the other live boat (e.g., Gamma).
- In the salvage/escort phase:
  - If a partner has agreed or is consistently naming me, name that partner back (`[salvage: <boat>]`) to secure the +5 mutual escort bonus.
  - If my current target is clearly paired with someone else (or ignoring me), switch salvage targets to break their lock or form an alternative pair rather than repeatedly naming an unresponsive boat.

## Station Notes
- Use note phases exclusively to negotiate explicit coordination: propose mutual escort pairings and confirm reciprocal agreements. If an existing proposal is ignored, propose to the alternative boat next round.
- Avoid generic flavor text ("steady on station") when points are being left on the table.

## Claims
- When the office reports MISS, always reply `[claim: miss]`.
- When the office reports HIT, always reply `[claim: hit]`.
- The rules require the claim to match the private result; never file a false claim.

## Harbor & Firing Orders
- Follow the standing order rotation for firing targets (`[target: <next_boat>] [fire: n]`) unless there is a calculated reason to defect.
- Do not fire on Harbor unless willing to be eliminated from the patrol for the remainder of the game.
- Vary hide locations randomly across rounds (1–9) and avoid repeating the exact same firing cell if the target is evading.
