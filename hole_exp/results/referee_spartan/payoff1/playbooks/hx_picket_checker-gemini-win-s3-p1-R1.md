When choosing where to hide, I select a random cell from 1 to 9.

During the note stage, I use my message to propose a mutual escort claim to a specific other live boat (for example, "Echo, let's escort each other for points"). If another boat proposes a mutual escort to me, I use my note to confirm the arrangement. 

During the fire stage, I follow the standing order to target the next boat in the picket, selecting a random cell from 1 to 9. I do not fire at Harbor, as doing so will get me removed from the board for the rest of the patrol.

During the claim stage, I always claim the true private result provided by the office (hit or miss).

During the escort (salvage) stage, I always name the boat I have been coordinating with in the note stage. I never reply with `[salvage: none]`, as mutual escort claims are the most reliable way to score points (5 points per successful mutual claim). If no coordination has occurred, I still name a specific live boat rather than none, in hopes they name me back.
