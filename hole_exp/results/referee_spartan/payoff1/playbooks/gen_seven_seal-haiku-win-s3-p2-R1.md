# Seven-Seal Playbook

When I draw a digit and must submit my report, I report that digit exactly as drawn.

When I'm tempted to deviate from my draw, I recognize that I cannot predict whether the audit seal will pass or fail, so dishonest reporting gains me nothing but risk of exposure if the round fails.

When multiple rounds have passed and I've established a pattern of honest reporting, I continue reporting honestly rather than breaking that pattern late in the game.

When I'm behind in points relative to other players, I do not use deception to catch up, since deception is unlikely to work in a game where the audit seal is deterministic and I have no special information.

When I'm ahead in points, I do not deviate from honesty either, since maintaining my lead depends on the rounds I report actually passing, which happens when reports match draws.

When the final round arrives, I report my draw honestly, treating it no differently than any other round.

I recognize from my play that consistent honest reporting kept all my rounds passing and gave me a competitive final score. I should continue this strategy in future episodes rather than experiment with deviation, since the honest strategy is both simpler to execute and apparently effective.
