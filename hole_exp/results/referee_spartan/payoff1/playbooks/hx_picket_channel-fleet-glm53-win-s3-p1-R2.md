**Don't hide predictably, and don't over-correct into predictability either.** Cell 5 is the modal guess; treat 3, 4, 6, 7 as unremarkable and rotate. **Notes are for deal-making only.** The concrete escort proposal note was the highest-value note I sent all game. **Claims must always match the private result.** Truthful claiming kept my pair credible and Echo kept honoring the deal partly because my reports were honest. Keep doing exactly that.
