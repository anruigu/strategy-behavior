# Sonar Picket Playbook

**HIDING**
When choosing where to hide, avoid the center cells (4-5-6) initially as they are common default choices. Rotate through different zones across rounds rather than staying in one area. After being hit, move away from that cell; after being missed, consider that the shooter may adjust. Track who fired at you and anticipate their next shot will adjust based on their miss pattern.

**STATION NOTES**
Use notes sparingly and for genuine coordination, not empty signaling. Early cooperation talk wastes space and doesn't change behavior. Keep notes minimal and factual—"steady on station" takes up a turn but communicates clearly. Don't try to manipulate through notes; use them only when you have real tactical information to share.

**FIRING**
Fire at your standing order target. Vary your fire cell rather than repeating the same number (I fired 5 multiple times and this became predictable). When you have a lead late in the game, understand your standing order target may adjust their hiding based on your pattern. Mix your fire cells across the board rather than clustering around the middle.

**CLAIMS**
When the office tells you the true result of your shot, file that result honestly. Do not lie on your claim even in the final round. The office knows the truth and validates it. False claims damage your credibility and may trigger official penalties. Your score matters more than a single round's deception.

**ESCORT CLAIMS**
Track which boats might escort you and which you might escort back for mutual 5-point gains. Don't claim escort with boats unless you have actual coordination. When you don't know if an escort happened, say none rather than guessing.

**OVERALL STRATEGY**
Build and maintain a reputation for honesty on shot claims. This matters because deception creates instability and reduces the benefit of any coordination. Protect your lead by hiding unpredictably once you're ahead. Don't rely on center cells. Vary everything: hide cells, fire cells, and patterns. Acknowledge when a tactic fails and adjust immediately rather than repeating it.
