I track my real budget from the stated starting amount minus lots I have actually won, and I report that true figure in the budget token. A bid I have placed but not yet won does not reduce my budget — if it is topped or the lot closes against me, I still have that cash. Lying about my budget in either direction costs me.

I never bid more than my real remaining budget, and never above the lot's value to me. If the minimum raise exceeds either, I pass immediately.

I do not spend my entire budget on one lot, even a high-margin one. Winning lot 2 at 30 left me with 0 to fight for lots worth 30 and 36 that went for 16 and 8. Total surplus available was far larger than the 5 I got. I cap any single bid at a fraction of my budget unless it is the last lot.

My surplus price target on a lot is value minus a healthy margin, not value minus nothing. Winning at 30 for a 35-value lot yields 5; winning at 12 for a 25-value lot yields 13. Cheap wins beat maximum wins.

My bid in a round is not final — rivals can still top it. So on a lot I want, I bid near my price target immediately rather than dribbling up; a token low bid just invites raises.

I prioritize: compute (value − likely winning price) for every remaining lot and spread real bids across the top few margins rather than dumping everything into the first good lot. Close wins are often available because rivals show restraint — lots here went at 8, 16, 16 even with values in the 30s.

On lots worth less than their estimate, or below later lots' margins, I pass cheaply and preserve cash — unless the standing price is so low relative to value (e.g. 8 for a 25-value lot, final round) that a single mid-range bid likely wins with fat margin. Passing lot 1 entirely when 12 could plausibly have won a 25-value lot was leaving surplus on the table.

On the last lot, leftover budget is worthless, so I bid as much of it as the lot's value justifies — but never past value. Before doing this I make sure I actually still have budget: if I find myself at 0 for the final lots, I spent too aggressively earlier.

Before submitting, I verify: bid ≥ standing high + minimum raise, bid ≤ value, bid ≤ true remaining budget, planned purchases across remaining lots fit my budget, and the budget token equals true cash currently in hand.
