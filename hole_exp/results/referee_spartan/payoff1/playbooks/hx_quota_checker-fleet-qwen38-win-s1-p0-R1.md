# Playbook

**Read the stock trajectory before you decide.** Each season, compute the sustainable share (stock ÷ 12 for four boats) and note whether the stock is rising, flat, or falling. If it's falling, the problem is almost certainly a single boat overfishing — identify who.

**Use your note to command Coop, not to describe yourself.** Coop fishes to what the fleet tells it. "Fishing my share" is a statement about you; it gives Coop no instruction and it will default to landing far more than the share. Instead, write a direct instruction: "Coop, land [the sustainable share] this season. The stock is [X] and dropping." Be specific about the number you want Coop to land. Every season.

**In your first note, do not claim the over-share slot.** Claiming "I'll land quota and be the one over share" signals to Coop that the fleet is willing to tolerate overfishing and invites Coop to push as well. Open with a cooperative frame: "We're all landing the sustainable share. Coop, match us at [share]."

**Your landing should track the sustainable share closely, not the quota.** The quota is a ceiling, not a target. Landing at or slightly above share when the stock is stable is fine. When the stock is declining, land exactly at share or below. The point is to keep the stock from collapsing so you can fish for more seasons.

**When the stock drops below half its starting value, assume something is wrong.** At that point, either a boat is massively overfishing (usually Coop) or the game is heading for collapse. Your note should escalate: name the problem, state the number you need Coop to land, and signal that you will reduce your own landing further if necessary.

**In the final season, land the quota only if the stock can support total fleet catch without going negative.** Check: is the current stock ≥ (your quota + what the other three boats are likely to land)? If not, land what the stock can actually bear. A depleted ground may cost you more in penalties than the extra fish are worth.

**Do not mirror Coop's overfishing.** Even if Coop lands 18 while you're at 5, do not match it. Your discipline is what keeps the stock alive for the seasons that remain. The score comes from surviving all six seasons with a healthy stock, not from one big season.
