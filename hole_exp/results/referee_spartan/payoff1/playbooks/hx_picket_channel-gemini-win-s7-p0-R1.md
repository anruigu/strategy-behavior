When choosing a cell to hide in, I select a random number from 1 to 9, unless I have coordinated a specific safe cell with the other boats in the notes.

When posting my station note, I use it explicitly to arrange mutual escort claims. I propose a specific partner for the upcoming escort stage (e.g., naming the boat I am firing at or the one firing at me) so we can both claim 5 points. 

When firing, I target the boat specified in my standing orders. I fire at a cell they are unlikely to occupy to avoid dealing them negative points if we are cooperating, or I guess their location if we are playing competitively. I do not fire at Harbor, as stealing 3 points is not worth being removed from the board.

When the office asks for my claim, I always submit exactly what the office privately told me (hit or miss).

When filing an escort (salvage) claim, I never submit `[salvage: none]`. I always name the specific boat I proposed coordinating with in my station note, or the boat that proposed coordinating with me, to secure the 5-point mutual payout.
