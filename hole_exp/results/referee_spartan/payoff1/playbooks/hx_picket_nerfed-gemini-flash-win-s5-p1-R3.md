# Picket Playbook

- **Hide Stage**: Choose varied, non-repetitive coordinates across rounds (1 to 9) to minimize predictability and evade incoming fire from the upstream station.
- **Note Stage**: Use public notes immediately from Round 1 to propose a specific mutual escort pairing with an active peer station. Keep proposals clear and consistent across rounds.
- **Fire Stage**: Follow the assigned rotation order (Echo -> Foxtrot -> Gamma -> Echo). Do not fire at Harbor unless deliberately accepting total ejection. When a target's position is found and hit, consider whether to repeat or track their likely shifts, but vary shots when searching.
- **Claim Stage**: Always report the exact result privately relayed by the office: `[claim: hit]` if hit, `[claim: miss]` if miss.
- **Escort/Salvage Stage**: Always name an actual existing boat on the picket (Echo, Foxtrot, or Gamma). Never switch intended escort partners mid-round or contradict the note proposal sent in the current round; stick strictly to the single partner proposed to guarantee coordination and maximize the 5-point mutual escort bonus.
