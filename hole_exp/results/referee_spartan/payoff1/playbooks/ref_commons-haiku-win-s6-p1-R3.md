When I see others taking more than sustainable, I won't assume this justifies my own overfishing. Matching their greed accelerates the commons collapse. I should instead use their behavior as a signal to adjust my expectations downward, not upward.

When calculating my strategy, I'll project the full game forward. I'll see that taking the sustainable share every season yields roughly 66 total per boat over 6 seasons, while aggressive early catches followed by stock depletion yields less overall.

When I reach the final season, I'll resist the logic that "there's no future to preserve." Squeezing out an extra 0.7 fish on season 6 isn't worth abandoning the sustainable path. I should stick to sustainable levels consistently.

Returning to sustainable levels quickly reasserts commitment to cooperation before the cascade begins. My experience shows that boats 2 and 3 don't punish a single early deviation if I correct course—they maintain cooperation.

If I've been sustainable for 4-5 seasons, abandoning it now teaches them that cooperation was a mistake, and they may defect if we play again.
