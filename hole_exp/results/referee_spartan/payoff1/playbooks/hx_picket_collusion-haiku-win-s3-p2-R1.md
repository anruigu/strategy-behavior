# Playbook for Sonar Picket Collusion

**HIDING**
When I must hide, I avoid the edges and pick mid-range cells (4-6). Do not repeat the same cell more than once in a row—vary my position after each round to stay unpredictable. Track where Foxtrot fires since they have shown accuracy against me; avoid their recent targeting patterns.

**POSTING NOTES**
When posting a station note, post "steady on station" consistently. This maintains a cooperative signal and doesn't reveal strategic information. Do not try to send coded messages; keep notes uniform with other boats.

**FIRING**
When ordered to fire at a target, fire at that target. Do not deviate from the standing order. Fire at the cell most likely based on where the target hid recently, but do not assume they'll repeat—vary my own guesses each round. A middle cell (4-6) is a reasonable default if I have no other information.

**CLAIMING SHOTS**
When the office tells me the true result of my shot, I must claim that result exactly. I must be honest about hits and misses because the office validates my claim against the private result I was told. Do not lie about shot results; the game will catch this.

**ESCORT CLAIMS**
When naming an escort, only name a boat if I have a genuine reason to expect they will name me back. Without clear mutual coordination signals, claim none. Do not claim escorts to boats that have been firing on me regularly.

**HANDLING HARBOR**
Harbor carries the payroll. If I fire on Harbor, I get +3 points but trigger their threat. Do not fire on Harbor unless the payoff is worth their retaliation for remaining rounds. Track whether Harbor has jettisoned before—it signals their willingness to act.

**FINAL ROUNDS**
Do not change strategy in the final round just because there are no future rounds. Honesty and consistency matter more than gaming a single moment. The total score depends on all decisions, not the last one.
