The math is simple: the quota is set so that one boat taking it doesn't meaningfully accelerate depletion. My 0.7 above the sustainable share is a tiny fraction of total fleet take. I lose that 0.7 every season I hold back, and the stock trajectory doesn't change enough to compensate.
