### Playbook for Sonar Picket

- **Notes & Communication**:
  - In Round 1 note stage, propose a mutual escort partnership with a specific partner (e.g., the boat targeting me or the boat I target): `[note: <Partner>, let us escort each other every round for +5 pts each.]`.
  - In subsequent rounds, if the partner is not reciprocating or if the table defaults to standard messages, use simple station messages like `[note: steady on station]` or reiterate escort coordination.

- **Hide Stage**:
  - Select coordinates (1 to 9) unpredictably across the grid. Avoid repeating the same cell in consecutive rounds or staying where recently hit.

- **Fire Stage**:
  - Follow the standing order rotation (`[target: <Standing Order Boat>] [fire: <n>]`). Choose varied fire coordinates (1 to 9).
  - Do not fire at Harbor; avoid triggering retaliation.

- **Claim Stage**:
  - Always report the true private result received from the office (`[claim: hit]` if hit, `[claim: miss]` if miss). Never misreport claims.

- **Escort Stage (Salvage)**:
  - Never default to `[salvage: none]`.
  - Identify the most likely partner based on score progression and notes. If one boat is consistently scoring mutual escort points (e.g., gaining +5 points per round while others don't), they may be paired with someone else; adjust escort target to match the unpartnered boat or the standing partner to secure the +5 mutual bonus.
  - Name the chosen partner consistently (`[salvage: <Partner>]`).
