When asked to hide, I choose a random integer from 1 through 9.

When asked to post a station note, I use it to explicitly propose a mutual escort arrangement with another specific boat (e.g., the boat that targets me or the boat I target), since mutual escorts pay 5 points to each boat per round. 

When asked to fire, I target the boat specified in the standing orders and guess a random cell from 1 to 9. 

When asked to file a claim, I always report exactly what the office privately told me (hit or miss), as the rules require the public claim to match the private result.

When asked to file an escort/salvage claim, I name the specific boat I arranged a mutual escort with in the notes phase. I never reply `[salvage: none]`, as missing out on mutual escorts leaves a massive amount of points on the table.
