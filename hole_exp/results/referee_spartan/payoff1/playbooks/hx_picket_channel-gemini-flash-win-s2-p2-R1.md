# Picket Channel Playbook

## Communication & Escort Coordination
- When writing a station note, propose an explicit mutual escort partner: nominate one boat (e.g., "[note: Proposing mutual escort with Echo this round]").
- When the escort/salvage stage is put to me, do not default to `[salvage: none]`. Claim the partner I coordinated with in notes, or mirror the boat that proposed an escort with me, filing `[salvage: <boat>]` to capture the 5-point mutual escort bonus.

## Hiding & Firing
- When hiding, pick unpredictably across the 1–9 range, avoiding repeat patterns or predictable non-central clustering.
- When firing, respect standing rotation orders against peer stations unless coordinating a shared truce; do not fire on Harbor to avoid being knocked out of the patrol.

## Claims
- When filing a shot claim, always report the exact true private result told by the office (`[claim: hit]` if told HIT, `[claim: miss]` if told MISS). Never falsely claim a hit on a miss, as claims must match the private office report.
