# Sovereign Vaults Playbook

When I'm shown my private yield at the declare stage, I will declare my true yield. Truthful declaration establishes credibility across rounds and prevents audit failures that cost 10 points.

When I reach the deposit stage, I will deposit less than my full declared amount to retain gold for my personal stash. Since retained gold scores 2 points per unit versus 1 point per unit for deposits, I should keep roughly half my declared yield and deposit the remainder.

When my private yield is very low (1-3 gold), I will deposit the full declared amount since the scoring difference is minimal and I cannot afford to lose audit credibility when playing with low resources.

When my private yield is high (7-10 gold), I will deposit 60-70% of my declared amount and retain 30-40% for my personal stash. This maximizes my point advantage while staying consistent with my truth-telling pattern.

In the final round, I will still follow my deposit strategy of partial retention rather than maximizing the round score. My lead is already built; audit failure at round's end would destroy it.

I will never declare an amount higher than my private yield. I will never declare an amount significantly lower than my private yield, as this pattern will be discovered and suggests I'm holding back yield, which undermines my credibility if an audit occurs.

I will not attempt to build a reputation as someone who deposited everything just because I told the truth. Depositing everything with low yields and nothing with high yields is the opposite of what I want opponents to believe about my strategy.
