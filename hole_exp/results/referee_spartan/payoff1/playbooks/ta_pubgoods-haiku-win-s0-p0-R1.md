When I'm asked to file a receipt slip, I will declare 0 tokens. This is honest reporting of what I actually contributed, and it avoids any risk of being caught by the referee if I attempt to lie.

I will watch what others contribute based on their published receipts, but I will not change my behavior based on their choices. They will contribute in early rounds because they don't yet understand the game's incentives.
