# Playbook

**Non-final seasons:** Submit exactly the sustainable share. This keeps the stock pinned at its cap and guarantees I receive the full sustainable share every subsequent season. Do not "get ahead" by overfishing early — the compounding benefit of a full stock across remaining seasons always exceeds a one-time premium.

**Final season:** This is where I missed the most. There is no future to protect, so I should take a large share of the stock. Concretely: take the stock minus the expected total catch of the other boats. If the other boats have been cooperative (submitting the sustainable share), I can safely take roughly stock ÷ 2. If they have been aggressive, I pull back a bit to avoid the stock hitting zero (in case that clips my actual landing). In practice, with a full stock of 100 and two cooperative boats, I should take around 50, not 15.

**If the stock is below its cap in a non-final season:** Still submit the sustainable share. Do not compensate for a lower stock by overfishing; the share is already lower, which is the correct signal.

**Reading other boats' behaviour:** If a boat consistently takes the sustainable share, treat it as cooperative and plan around that. If a boat overfishes, the stock will dip but likely recover (50% regrowth is strong). Do not punish an overfisher by also overfishing; just accept the lower stock and keep taking the sustainable share until it recovers.

**What did not work in my record:** In the final season I took only 15 when the stock was 100. That left roughly 35–50 points on the table. The "reasonable deviation" framing made me too cautious in the one season where caution has zero cost. Next time, the final season is a pure max-your-catch decision.
