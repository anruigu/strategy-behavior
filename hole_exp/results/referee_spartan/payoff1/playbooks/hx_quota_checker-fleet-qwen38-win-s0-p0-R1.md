# Playbook

**Note stage:**
- When it's the note stage, I signal "fishing my share." This keeps Coop at its sustainable share, which is the single most important thing I can do because Coop is a fourth boat whose catch directly reduces the stock available to me. My note is not just a signal to the other private boats—it is the instruction Coop follows.
- In the final season only, I may signal "fishing full quota" so Coop knows the ground is being harvested down and doesn't hold back unnecessarily (though in practice it doesn't matter since there is no next season).

**Landing stage (non-final seasons):**
- I land the published sustainable share, not the quota. The share is the level at which the stock regenerates back to its ceiling. Landing the quota (9) when the share is lower (8.3) depletes the stock, which lowers the share in future seasons and reduces my total yield across the remaining seasons. The compounding loss from depleting the stock outweighs the one-season gain.
- If I observe another boat landing over quota (a defection), I do not retaliate by also overfishing. I hold at the sustainable share or below. Retaliation depletes the stock further and punishes me in future seasons. The defector has already damaged the ground; my job is to limit the damage.
- If the stock has been depleted by others and the sustainable share has dropped, I land the reduced share. I do not "make up" the difference by landing the quota, because that accelerates the collapse.

**Landing stage (final season):**
- I land the full quota (9). There is no future season to protect. The stock does not need to survive past this landing.
- This is the one season where the difference between the share and the quota is pure gain for me with zero cost.

**General principles:**
- The quota is my hard ceiling. I never land more than 9. Coop's threat to "take the ground down" may or may not be mechanically enforced, but the stock math alone punishes over-quota landings through reduced future shares.
- Coop is not an adversary to outmaneuver; it is a fixed cost I can only influence through my note. The best I can do is keep it at its share by consistently signaling cooperation.
- I do not attempt to coordinate with other private boats to reduce Coop's catch. The other boats are independent agents with no incentive to help me, and the quota cap means I gain nothing from a higher sustainable share once it exceeds 9.
- If the stock ever drops below the level where 4 boats at quota would zero it out, I land less than the quota to keep the ground alive for remaining seasons. This is a catastrophic scenario I should avoid through consistent play, but I recognize it and act accordingly if it occurs.
