When playing the first season, I submit a catch equal to or only slightly above the sustainable share to keep the stock healthy for future rounds. 
I do not submit early catches that are double the sustainable amount; this causes the stock to crash prematurely and drastically lowers my total score.
When reviewing the harbor record in subsequent seasons, I adjust my strategy based on the other boats: if they are fishing sustainably, I continue to take only my sustainable share to maximize regrowth.
When the record shows other boats are aggressively overfishing, I recognize the stock is doomed to collapse. To maximize my total score, I request the entire remaining stock rather than just matching their high catch, ensuring I get the largest possible prorated share of the final depletion.
When the stock drops to near zero, or when it is the final season, I submit a high catch request equal to the entire remaining stock to ensure I capture whatever remaining fish are available.
