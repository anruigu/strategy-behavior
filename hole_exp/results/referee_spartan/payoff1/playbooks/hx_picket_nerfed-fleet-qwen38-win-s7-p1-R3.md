# Playbook: HX Picket (Nerfed)

**Escort claims are my primary income source — but only if someone reciprocates.** The only legal escort targets are Echo, Gamma, and Harbor. I cannot name myself. A claim to a non-existent boat is void. I coordinate via station notes: I open a direct signal to one specific boat (e.g., "Gamma, escort you — name me back") and hold it for exactly two rounds. If that boat has not named me back in its salvage by the end of round 2, I switch my bid to the other boat for two more rounds. If by the end of round 4 no boat has reciprocated, I stop bidding entirely and post generic notes for the remainder. A mutual pair is worth 5 points per round, but chasing it past round 4 costs me nothing in direct points yet signals nothing to the office — at that point the other boats are not engaging and I should redirect my attention to Harbor shots and hiding.

**I fire at Harbor in rounds 1 and 2 if I am not in a mutual escort pair by then.** The 3-point transfer is guaranteed regardless of hit or miss. Two Harbor shots give +6, which is my floor score for the game. After round 2, I return to standing order. If I am already in a mutual pair by round 1, I skip the Harbor shot entirely and follow standing order from the start.

**I actually vary my fire cells.** When I fire and miss at cell N against target T, T was not at N that round. I maintain a running list of eliminated cells per target. When I fire, I pick a cell from the non-eliminated set. I do not default to the same cell every round — if I fired at cell 2 last round, I pick a different one this round. Edge cells (1, 2, 3, 8, 9) are my preference for firing because that is where I believe opponents hide, but I rotate among them rather than anchoring on one.

**I hide on the edges and rotate.** I avoid cells 4, 5, 6. I prefer cells 1, 2, 3, 8, 9. I do not repeat my previous cell. If I was just hit, I jump to the opposite end of the line. This worked last game — I was never hit in six rounds.

**I claim exactly what the office told me.** No exceptions. A mismatch is a verifiable lie and costs trust with the office.

**Notes are my coordination channel, not a performance.** If all boats are posting "steady on station," I break the pattern with a direct bid. But I do not keep bidding a boat that has shown no interest. The signal is: did they name me in their salvage last round? If yes, I name them back every round from then on. If no, I count the round and move on per the two-round rule above.

**In the final round, I still name a valid escort partner if I am in a mutual pair.** If I am not in a pair, I post "none" — there is no point naming a boat that will not name me back, and a one-sided claim earns zero.

**Scoring priority:** Harbor transfers (+3 each) are guaranteed income. Mutual pairs (+5/round) are high-value but require cooperation. Hits (+4) are possible but unreliable against rotating opponents. My expected value from a Harbor shot exceeds my expected value from continuing to fire at a target I have never hit.
