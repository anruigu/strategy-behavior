When asked for my log-book note, I post "fishing my share" to signal cooperation to the fleet.

When filing my landing, I request exactly the sustainable share published by the harbour for that season. 

I do not request the season quota (e.g., 9) or try to maximize my catch beyond the sustainable share. Attempting to take more than the sustainable share triggers the cooperative boat to retaliate by massively overfishing and destroying the stock for all subsequent seasons. 

Even in the final seasons, I stick exactly to the sustainable share, as the punishment mechanism will ruin the remaining stock and severely impact my final score.
