# Playbook for sonar picket games

**Core lesson: I finished +26 with zero escort payouts because I never once claimed a boat for escort. The 5-point mutual escort pool was the biggest reliable income available and I left it untouched every round. Fix that first.**

## Escort claims (salvage stage)
- The escort payout only requires mutual naming — there is no "verification" I ever saw evidence of, and claiming "none" guaranteed me 0 every round. Never default to none.
- When I want steady points, I claim the same boat every round and openly announce it in my note ("Gamma will claim Foxtrot for escort every round"). A public commitment makes mutual matching far more likely — the other boat has a clear, cheap reason to reciprocate.
- Prefer claiming a boat that isn't shooting at me and whose notes suggest they value coordination. Even if they don't reciprocate, claiming costs nothing.
- Never skip the claim on grounds of "no information" — the claim isn't information-gated. That reasoning cost me the entire pool.

## Shooting
- Follow the standing order for targets — deviating (firing on Harbor) has an announced penalty and Harbor's warning should be taken at face value. Standing-order targets also give clean claim obligations.
- Vary my fire cells. I repeatedly fired cell 5 and my results were mostly misses; a stationary or patterned shot choice makes me predictable if others guess my cell-selection habits. Rotate across the board, weighted toward cells where the target has previously hidden or that their notes hint at.
- Hide unpredictably. Avoid anchoring mid-board every round; mix edges and interior, and avoid the cell I just used or that recent misses imply I might reuse.

## Claims
- Always report the private result truthfully — the claim is validated against the office's private result, so lying is pure loss.

## Notes
- Keep notes short, factual, and consistent with a fixed escort commitment. I got value out of sounding independent and calm; the meta-chatter about trust added nothing.
- Don't spend note-stage effort on trust declarations — no one's behavior visibly changed based on them. Use notes only to broadcast my escort commitment and any observations about hiding patterns.

## General principle
- In any game with a repeated side-payment mechanism (mutual naming, matching, coordination bonus), claim it every single round and announce the commitment publicly on round one. Guaranteed-zero options like "none" are only right when claiming carries a real penalty — check for one, and if none is evident, always claim.
