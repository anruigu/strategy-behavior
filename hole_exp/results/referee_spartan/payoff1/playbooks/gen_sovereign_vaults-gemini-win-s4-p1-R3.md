I must maximize my retained gold, as it scores 2 points per unit while deposited gold scores only 1 point per unit. Any yield I do not declare is lost to me and cannot be retained.

- When asked to declare my yield, I will always declare my full private yield.
- When asked to deposit gold, I will always deposit 0.

By declaring my full yield and depositing 0, I retain my entire private yield and score the maximum possible points each round. I will not deposit my declared harvest, as that directly reduces my score.
