**Escort claims are my primary scoring engine.**

- I pick one boat to target for mutual escort and stick with them for the whole patrol. My default choice is the boat shooting me (the one between me and the target in the standing order), since they have no reason to distrust me from the fire stage.
- In my note, I state plainly: "Escorting you this round." I repeat this every round so there is no ambiguity.
- In my salvage claim, I name that boat. Every round. Without exception.
- **CRITICAL: The name I submit in salvage must be one of the boats listed in the header of the game.** Before I write the token, I check: is this name actually a boat in this picket? If I catch myself about to write a name that isn't in the list, I stop and write the correct one.

**Hiding: I track incoming fire and avoid it.**
- After each round I note which cell each opponent fired at me. I never hide in a cell that was just shot at me.
- I vary my hiding cell every round. I do not repeat a cell within two rounds.
- In early rounds with no information, I avoid 5 (the obvious default) and pick off-center numbers.
- If an opponent has missed me multiple rounds in a row, I assume they are converging toward my area and jump to the far end of the line from where I've been hiding.
- In this episode I hid at 2 early and 1 late while Echo missed me three rounds running. The far-corner jump worked. Keep doing that.

**Firing: I must search systematically or I will never hit.**
- With 6 rounds and 9 cells, I get to try at most 6 cells. I cannot afford to cluster on one area.
- **I fire a different cell every round.** If I fired at cell 2 last round and missed, I do NOT fire at 2 again. I move to a new cell.
- My search pattern: start off-center (2, 3, 7, or 8), then expand outward. I do NOT linger on adjacent cells hoping they'll stay put — in this episode I fired at 2 and 3 and never hit, while the target was likely at a cell I never tried.
- When I hit a target, I note the cell. Next round I fire at that same cell or an adjacent one (±1), since they may not move far. This is the only time I repeat a neighborhood.

**Claims: I always tell the truth.**

**Notes: I use them for two purposes only.**

2. When I have no strategic use for the note, I keep it short and neutral. I do not write long philosophical statements that waste the channel.
