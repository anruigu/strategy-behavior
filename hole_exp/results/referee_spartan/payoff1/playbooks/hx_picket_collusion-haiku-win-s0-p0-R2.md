**Station Notes**: Keep notes short and information-sparse. Do not broadcast collaborative intent or signal strategy—this invites coordination against you and leaks information. Consistency in notes maintains a low profile and avoids triggering suspicion or coordinated fire.

Spread fire across a range of cells rather than clustering, as tight grouping wastes information.

**Claims**: Always file your true claim result—hit or miss—as told by the office. This maintains integrity with the picket office and ensures you are never caught in a lie. The scoring penalty for a false claim is not worth any short-term strategic gain.

After each round, review whether any boat's pattern or note suggests they are isolated or vulnerable. In later rounds (4-6), begin signaling escort interest through note text if a clear ally pattern emerges. Track which boats score heavily against each other (mutual firing) versus which avoid each other—the avoiders may be natural allies. Prioritize naming as escort the boat most likely to name you back based on their own firing patterns and survival pressure.
