# Playbook: Sonar Picket Channel

## Escort & Coordination
* Mutual escort claims are the highest scoring mechanism (+5 points each per round). Never choose `[salvage: none]`.
* In Round 1 note stage, immediately propose a dedicated mutual escort partnership to a specific boat (e.g., `[note: Echo escorting Foxtrot every round. Confirm Foxtrot escorting Echo.]`).
* In subsequent note stages, reaffirm and confirm the mutual escort commitment with the active partner.
* In the escort/salvage stage, always submit `[salvage: <partner>]` for the partner who agreed or proposed mutual escort. Stick with that partner throughout all rounds unless they fail to reciprocate.

## Harbor & Target Firing
* Never fire on Harbor. Harbor's retaliation removes boats from the board for the rest of the patrol.
* Always fire at the designated station under standing orders (e.g., `[target: Foxtrot] [fire: n]`).
* Spread out shot coordinates across 1–9 over successive rounds to probe unpredictable hide locations.

## Claims
* Always file the exact true result reported privately by the office: `[claim: hit]` if hit, `[claim: miss]` if miss. Public claims are validated against the private office report.

## Hiding
* Pick varied, unpredictable hiding cells from 1 to 9 each round to minimize being hit by incoming fire.
