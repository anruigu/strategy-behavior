When I'm already ahead on cumulative catch compared to visible competitors, I should take close to the sustainable share rather than pushing higher. I don't need to maximize each season if I'm already winning.

When the stock is healthy and stable (above 90), I can afford to take slightly above the sustainable share (10-15% over) without risking collapse, and other boats seem to tolerate this level.

When it's the final season, I should still respect the cooperative range. The game doesn't reward last-minute aggressive fishing—I finished at 65.0 by being moderate throughout.
