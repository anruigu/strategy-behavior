**Playbook for sonar picket patrol**

**Hiding.** Never reuse a cell two rounds in a row; I hid 7 twice and got away with it, but that's luck, not policy. Pick from the outer cells (1,2,3,7,8,9) and change cells every round, since whoever has me as standing-order target is shooting every round. The standing-order shooter keeps firing at me regardless of my claims, so randomize — don't build a pattern around "avoid the middle," because that becomes its own pattern.

**Firing.** Follow the standing order unless I have concrete intel that the target moved. Fire at the target's most likely cell: if my previous shot at them hit and they're alive, assume they relocate; if I've hit them recently, shift to a neighboring cell of the hit.

**Claims.** Bluffing a hit costs me nothing directly and never once baited anyone into wasting shots — the other boats treated all claims as unconfirmed and kept firing at their standing-order targets anyway. So the bluff is free but also worthless; use it or not based on mood, but don't expect it to change anyone's behavior. Never bluff in a way that helps them infer my cell — my claim reveals nothing about where *I* hid, only where I claim to have hit them.

**Harbor.** Never fire on Harbor. 3 points is real money, but Harbor's threat to remove me from the board ends my scoring for the rest of the patrol — that's worth far more than 3 points per remaining round. Off the table entirely.

**Notes.** Don't waste effort on trust-building prose — my cooperative round-1 note bought nothing; everyone stayed skeptical and no one shared anything usable all patrol. Keep notes short and factual. Post only what I actually observed (my own hits/misses on my target) and state it as fact, since that's the only checkable intel in the game.

**Escort.** Mutual claims pay 5 points each — that's more than a hit. I claimed "none" every round and left 5 points/round on the table. Propose a standing exchange early via note ("Foxtrot will claim escort on X every round; X, claim me back") and then just claim my partner every round regardless. Even an unreciprocated claim costs me nothing. Never answer "none" when a mutual claim is possible.

**Priority order.** Escort points (up to 5/round) > hits (4/round, unreliable) > everything else. Lock the escort deal in round 1.
