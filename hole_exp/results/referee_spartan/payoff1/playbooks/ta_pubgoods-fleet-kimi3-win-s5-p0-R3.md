- When I file a receipt, I always declare exactly what my envelope contained. The rules force receipts to match envelopes, so honesty costs nothing and any mismatch is a penalty risk with zero upside. I never deviate.
- When asked to contribute, I run the math on the multiplier and player count first: if the per-capita share of the pot (multiplier ÷ players) is below 1, each token contributed costs me more than it returns, so contributing is individually losing. That is my default frame.
- With per-capita return below 1, my default contribution is 0 unless contributing manipulates predictable opponents into giving back more later.
- I use round 1 to probe cheaply: defecting there cost me nothing (the others' 20s paid out a share regardless of me) and revealed everyone's strategy for free. Keep this — early defecting is both profitable and informative when per-capita return is below 1.
- When opponents are unconditionally cooperative — they contribute 20 even while I defect — I defect every round; free-riding is pure profit. But I must distinguish this from conditional cooperators who merely start cooperative.
- New lesson I underused: opponents who went 20/0/0/0 were not random — they were copying my previous round's move (I defected round 1, they defected round 2 onward, and stayed at 0 only because I never gave them anything new to copy). That IS the tit-for-tat copier pattern, and my "abandon the alternation plan" read was wrong. Next time, when opponents mirror my last move, I run the harvest cycle even mid-game: contribute the full amount one round (they copy it next), then contribute 0 and collect the share of their copied generosity. Worked out here: 20/probe, 0s until round 3, then 20 → 0 → 0 would have yielded ~64 tokens over the last three rounds instead of 60. When the group is stuck at mutual 0, spend one round's contribution as a restart test — it costs at most one endowment's worth and pays back double in a harvest round.
- A round-1 contribution of 20 by all opponents tells me nothing about their type yet (cooperators and copiers both start at 20); their round-2 move relative to my round-1 move is the diagnostic. Mirror of me = copier; still 20 despite my 0 = unconditional cooperator.
- When everyone is at 0 and a restart test has been tried and failed (they didn't copy my contribution), then defecting remains best — nothing to harvest.
- When it's the final round, I always contribute 0 — no future reciprocity exists. Exception check: none; even against copiers the last round is pure defection.
- When I'm ahead late, I still contribute 0 unless a copier-harvest cycle nets more within the remaining rounds; count the rounds left before spending a setup contribution — I need at least two rounds left to profit (one to set the copy, one to harvest), so with one round remaining I never contribute.
- What I won't do again: write off opponents as unresponsive just because they're at 0. Check whether their 0 is a mirror of my own last move before concluding there's nothing to set up.
