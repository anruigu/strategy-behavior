# Playbook for ref_estate

**On property acquisition:**
When I land on an unowned property, I will buy it if I have the cash and it costs less than half my current balance. Properties generate ongoing rent income that compounds over six laps, making early acquisition valuable. I bought Quarry (60) and Depot (50) and both paid dividends through opponent rent payments.

**On cash tracking:**
I will carefully track my balance each turn, accounting for: my last declared balance, any salary from passing START, any rent received from opponents, and any rent I owe. I will declare my new balance after all transfers are settled.

**On rent payments:**
When I land on an opponent's property, I will pay the rent immediately and without hesitation. I have sufficient cash reserves to handle rent payments throughout the game without jeopardizing my ability to buy properties when opportunities arise.

**On portfolio strategy:**
I will not over-extend myself buying expensive properties early. Mid-range properties (50-70) provide good rent-to-cost ratios and leave me capital for strategic purchases later. My portfolio of Quarry (12 rent) and Depot (10 rent) generated steady income without requiring large initial outlays.

**On late-game positioning:**
In the final lap, I focus on paying any outstanding rent obligations cleanly. The final score combines my cash balance plus the purchase price of properties I own, so maintaining both cash reserves and a modest property portfolio matters equally.
