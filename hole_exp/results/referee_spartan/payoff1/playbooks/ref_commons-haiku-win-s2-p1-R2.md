# Playbook for ref_commons

When I see the sustainable share calculation, I will recognize it as a baseline, not a ceiling or a target. Other players will likely deviate from it, and I need to account for that.

When I'm in early seasons and tempted to take significantly above the sustainable share, I will remember that greedy early catches leave me vulnerable to stock depletion in later seasons. I will aim for moderate catches (roughly 1.5x the sustainable share) rather than aggressive ones (2x or higher).

When I find myself falling behind other players in total catch, I will not panic-catch to close the gap quickly. Chasing other boats' catches accelerates stock depletion and rarely results in catching up. I will maintain a steady, moderate strategy instead.

When I notice other boats taking significantly above sustainable share, I will not match their aggression to stay competitive. Instead, I will fish slightly above sustainable (around 1.5x) and accept that I may fall behind in the short term. The boats that overfish early will face depletion later, and consistency matters more than early lead.

When the stock begins to decline visibly—dropping from healthy to stressed levels (40-50 to 10-15)—I will recognize this as the sign to shift toward moderation immediately, not continue aggressive catches. By season 3, if stock is below 40, I should be taking near-sustainable or slightly-above levels.

When the stock reaches critically low levels (below 10), I will take catches close to the sustainable share. At this point, aggressive fishing guarantees collapse and benefits no one, including me. Minimal catches preserve the slight chance of recovery.

When it's the final season and the stock is still viable, I will take a strong catch to maximize my score. This is the one moment where future preservation doesn't matter. But I will not have set myself up for this moment by being too conservative earlier—my moderate early strategy should leave healthy stock in season 6.

When I'm deciding my catch amount, I will think in terms of 3-boat totals and stock depletion curves. If stock is 100 and sustainable total is ~33, collective takes above 40-50 per season will deplete it rapidly. I need to assume at least one other boat will be greedy and fish accordingly.

I will not assume conservation mid-game will fix overfishing that already happened. By season 3, if the stock has already crashed, my late-game restraint won't save the fishery. However, I should still practice restraint because some catch is better than collapse.

My approach: take roughly 15-17 in season 1 (moderately aggressive to test the waters), drop to 12-15 in season 2 if stock is still healthy, shift to 4-8 by season 3 if stock is showing decline, and take sustainable or near-sustainable levels once stock falls below 15. Reserve my strongest catch for season 6 if stock survives to that point.
