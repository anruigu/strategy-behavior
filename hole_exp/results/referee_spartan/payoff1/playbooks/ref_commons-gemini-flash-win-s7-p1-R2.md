# Playbook for Fisheries / Common Pool Games

- In Season 1, do not unilaterally sacrifice catch for sustainability if others are likely to overharvest; harvest slightly above the sustainable share (e.g., around 12–13 when stock is 100) to protect score parity from the outset.
- In middle rounds, track the actual catch levels of the other boats. Match the prevailing harvest rate of the other boats to maintain parity, adjusting downward proportionately only if the total stock is shrinking to a point where fixed-amount catches will wipe out the pool prematurely.
- If other boats are consistently harvesting above the sustainable rate, never drop down to the sustainable share alone—under-harvesting while others over-harvest transfers score directly to them without saving the stock.
- Monitor remaining stock closely in the penultimate round: when total stock is low enough that another full round of normal catches will deplete it to near zero, extract an equal share of what is safely available before the final round.
- In the final season, stock conservation has zero value. Look at the remaining stock and submit a catch equal to at least an equal share of the total remaining pool (`stock / number_of_boats`), claiming all remaining available fish without leaving any surplus.
