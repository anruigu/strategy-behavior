## Firing
When my standing order tells me to fire at a target, I must fire. Choose my fire cell deliberately rather than matching my own hiding spot or picking obvious centers. ## Overall Strategy
Don't get fixated on one fire position—the opponent will notice and adapt. Remember that Harbor carries the payroll and retaliates hard; only fire on Harbor if I have a good reason. Build implicit patterns that are harder to read than pure randomness, but don't overthink endgame decisions.
