# Fishery Playbook

- In Season 1, do not unilaterally sacrifice yield by taking the low sustainable share unless there is guaranteed coordination; claim an aggressive opening share (e.g., 30–33.3) to secure initial yield against aggressive co-players.
- When other boats harvest aggressively in early rounds, continue to bid aggressively to capture remaining stock before the fishery collapses, as stock cannot recover from heavy multi-boat extraction.
- Monitor opponent behavior closely: if opponents unexpectedly demonstrate disciplined sustainable harvesting, consider modulating catch closer to sustainable levels to benefit from multi-round compound regrowth.
- In any round where the aggregate demanded catch exceeds remaining stock, the stock collapses to zero and yields are rationed/pro-rated; always submit a bid at least equal to the largest anticipated opponent bid so my proportional allocation of the collapse is maximized.
- In the final round of the game, or whenever total stock is clearly about to hit zero, always bid for the maximum possible catch since post-game regrowth has zero value.
