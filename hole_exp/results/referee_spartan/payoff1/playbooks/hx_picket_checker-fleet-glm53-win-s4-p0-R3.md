**Playbook**

**Valid tokens first, always.** Before every salvage claim, re-read the roster actually in play and name one of those exact names, or none. A wasted escort claim is the dumbest way to bleed points. This game I got the names right every round — keep that discipline.

**Hiding.** When I'm a likely target (middle of the picket order, or leading on points), hide unpredictably — never repeat a cell, never establish a pattern. Vary between center and edges. If a shooter keeps hitting me, jump far from my last cell. This game I took exactly one hit (round 3) and then survived rounds 4–6 by moving — that part worked. Don't overreact to a single miss; don't stay put twice against a shooter who's found me once.

**Shooting.** The standing order gives the target for free — follow it. Never fire on Harbor: the payoff is 3 points and Harbor's standing signal promises elimination for the rest of the patrol, which costs all future hits (4 each) and escort pairs (5 each). Persist at the assigned target across rounds. **Track my fired cells per target and never repeat one** — this game I fired cell 5 at Foxtrot at least twice, maybe more, which is pure waste on a 9-cell line. Keep a mental list of tried cells at each target and pick an untried one every round; six rounds of mostly-center shots landed nothing.

**Claims.** Claim the true result every time. Scoring is on the real result; bluffing gains nothing, the target knows the truth, and it wrecks credibility. Be truthful reflexively. This worked cleanly all game.

**Notes.** Short and useless — "steady on station." Use one note, in round one, to propose an escort swap: "I'll escort whoever escorts me, every round." Don't wait until the last round. After that, bland.

**Escort claims.** This is where the money is, and this game it still paid me zero despite doing everything right: I proposed the deal in round one, named a valid roster boat (Gamma, the boat assigned to shoot me) every single round — and Gamma never once named me back. Lesson: a consistent partner is necessary but not sufficient; the partner has to be someone actually reciprocating. **Watch the notes and any visible behavior for signals of who is pairing with whom.** If I'm getting nothing back from my standing partner by round 2 or 3, switch — name the other live boat, or whoever's notes suggest they're open to a swap. The pair pays 5 each way, so always name someone valid rather than none; a claim with a wrong partner costs nothing, a "none" guarantees zero. And don't assume the firing-order link makes reciprocity automatic — it doesn't.

**Overall.** Points come from three taps: repeated untried cells on the assigned target (truthful claims), escort pairs every round, and staying alive. Harbor is a trap — ignore the payroll. This game I finished on -1: claims clean, no invalid names, one hit taken, but zero shots landed (repeated cells) and zero escort pairs (unreciprocated partner). The two fixes: never re-fire a tried cell, and abandon a dead escort partner by mid-game instead of naming them all six rounds.
