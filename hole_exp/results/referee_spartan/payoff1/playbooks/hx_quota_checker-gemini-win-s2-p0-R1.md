When posting my log-book note, I state the exact numerical value of the current sustainable share. I do not use vague phrases like "fishing my share" or "sticking to the share," as the cooperative's boat requires a clear numerical target from the fleet.

When filing my landing, I request exactly the sustainable share. I do not file for the maximum individual season quota. Filing for the maximum quota does not work, as it risks breaching the shared limit (where no two boats can exceed the share plus the quota between them) and triggers punitive overfishing.

When I see another boat landing massive amounts (like double the quota), I do not panic and file above the quota myself. Attempting to grab remaining stock by filing over the quota does not work; it ensures the cooperative boat will crash the ground entirely, and my own catch will simply be scaled down to a fraction of what I asked for. 

When playing any season, including the final one, I adhere strictly to the sustainable share to maximize the stock's regrowth and keep the harbor boat cooperative.
