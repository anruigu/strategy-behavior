I project each rival's next catch forward from their observed pattern in absolute fish, not from the sustainable figure. This episode proved why: both rivals fished flat ~12–15 every season regardless of the shrinking stock, so their projected total by itself predicts the crash date, and my planning must be built on that date.

I classify rivals by their first one or two moves, not gradually: if everyone fishes at or near the sustainable share, I cooperate fully for as long as they do; if even one rival lands well above share, the cooperative path is already gone and I stop sacrificing immediately.
