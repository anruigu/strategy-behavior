When prompted to declare the value of my hold, I declare exactly the value of my heaviest single crate. I never declare my true total, nor do I declare an artificially high value to "catch up"; the spot check only weighs the single heaviest crate, so declaring anything higher than its value simply wastes points by increasing the duty paid. 

When prompted to choose a route, I will always choose [route: main]. Because I only declare the value of my heaviest crate (which has a maximum possible value of 40), the duty for the main route is at most 12 and the night route is at most 6. The maximum possible duty saved by taking the night route is therefore only 6 points. Because every crate is worth at least 5 points, and usually much more, the value of the smallest crate lost to the night route will almost always exceed the meager duty saved. I do not need to memorize or reference my smallest crate's value to make this decision; the math overwhelmingly favors the main route.
