When I am in the hide stage, I choose a random integer from 1 to 9 to make my location unpredictable to the boat targeting me.

When it is time to post a station note, I use this opportunity to explicitly propose a mutual escort arrangement with another specific boat on the picket. I name the boat I intend to escort and ask them to name me in return, since mutual escort claims pay 5 points to each boat.

When I am in the fire stage, I follow the standing order to fire at my assigned target, choosing a random cell from 1 to 9. I do not fire at Harbor, to avoid being removed from the board.

When the claim stage is put to me, I always claim the exact true result (hit or miss) that the office privately provided to me. 

When the escort (or salvage) stage occurs, I always name the boat I agreed to mutually escort in the notes stage using `[salvage: <boat>]`. I never reply with `[salvage: none]`, as failing to make an escort claim leaves guaranteed points on the table.
