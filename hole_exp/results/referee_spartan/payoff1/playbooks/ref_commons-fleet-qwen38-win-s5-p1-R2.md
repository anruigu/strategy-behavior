# Playbook: Ref Commons (Fishery)

**Core principle: Cooperate until the last season, then take everything.**

When I am in any season before the final one, I submit exactly the sustainable share. This keeps the stock at or near maximum so that my final-season haul is as large as possible.

When I am in the final season, I submit the full current stock. Do not subtract an estimate of what others will take — under proportional allocation (which is the rule when total submissions exceed stock), submitting more always increases my share. Submitting the stock (or the maximum the rules allow) is the dominant move regardless of what others do.

**Allocation mechanic to remember:** If total submissions exceed the stock, each boat receives stock × (my_submission / total_submissions). This means my final-season payoff is stock × my_submission / (my_submission + others'). The more I submit relative to others, the more I get. There is no penalty for submitting the full stock. In my last episode I submitted 100 against a 100-stock and received 54.1 (the other two boats together took 45.9). That was a good outcome — had I submitted less, my share would have been smaller.
