When I see others taking more than sustainable, I won't assume this justifies my own overfishing. Matching their greed accelerates the commons collapse. I should instead use their behavior as a signal to adjust my expectations downward, not upward.

When calculating my strategy, I'll project the full game forward. I'll see that taking the sustainable share every season yields roughly 66 total per boat over 6 seasons, while aggressive early catches followed by stock depletion yields less overall. My score of 57.6 confirms this—I got neither the cooperative payoff nor the defector's windfall.
