Naming an escort costs nothing and forfeiting it pays the same as a mismatch (zero), so I ALWAYS name an escort every round — never "none." Ending the final round on "none" was a pure giveaway; with no confirmed partner I should have named my most recent proposed partner anyway, on the chance they reciprocate.
- When proposing a pairing in notes, I name exactly one specific boat and stick with that same boat for multiple rounds so the other boat has a stable, predictable partner to name back. My misses and hits against me show shooters track patterns, so I pick from cells nobody has fired on at all, preferring cells far from my own history, and I treat "edges" as no safer than anywhere — my edge picks got hit anyway. Real unpredictability beats heuristics.
