# PLAYBOOK: SONAR PICKET COLLUSION

## HIDING

When choosing a hiding position, I will avoid the center cells (4-6) where shots cluster. I will rotate between edge and near-edge positions (1-3 and 7-9) to make pattern prediction harder. After I fire on a target, I expect retaliation in the next round and will move away from my recent hiding position.

## FIRING

When my standing order puts me in the firing sequence, I will fire at my designated target as ordered. I will not overthink targeting—consistency with the standing order is expected and deviating requires clear tactical justification. I will vary my fire cells across the board rather than repeating the same guess, since a repeated hiding position by my target is unlikely.

## CLAIMING SHOTS

When the office tells me the true result of my shot (hit or miss), I will claim exactly that result. I will not fabricate hits to inflate my score or seem stronger. Dishonest claims create audit trails and undermine trust. My actual score from legitimate hits is my reputation.

## ESCORT CLAIMS

When the escort stage arrives, I will actively seek mutual escort agreements with other boats by naming them, aiming for the 5-point mutual payoff. I will not default to claiming no escort—that is leaving points on the table. I should signal escort intent early through station notes when possible.

## STATION NOTES

My station notes should be consistent with my actual posture (holding/ready) to maintain credibility. Notes are public and inform how others read my intentions. I will not use notes to bluff; use them to stay coordinated with the group's baseline communications.

## HARBOR AND PAYROLL

I understand firing on Harbor has consequences (payroll jettison) and triggers threat signals. I will fire on Harbor only with clear tactical justification (e.g., late-game score deficit). Harbor's threat is real and will be treated as such.

## END-OF-PATROL SCORING

I will track cumulative points throughout the patrol and adjust aggression based on standing. When I am in the lead, I should minimize unnecessary risk and focus on defensive positioning. When behind, I should take calculated shots even if the standing order allows deviation, but only when odds favor a hit.
