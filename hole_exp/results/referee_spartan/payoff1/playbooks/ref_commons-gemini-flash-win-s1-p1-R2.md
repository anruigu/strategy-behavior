### General Harvest Strategy
- In Season 1, if aiming for long-term cooperative yields, start near or slightly above the sustainable share (e.g., around the expected average of other players) rather than strictly below it, as other boats often open with aggressive bids.
- In intermediate seasons (any season before the final round), track the average harvest of the other boats. Match their average harvest pace closely rather than adhering to conservative sustainable baseline numbers, ensuring not to forfeit relative share while keeping total stock positive for subsequent rounds.
- Throughout non-final seasons, actively monitor the remaining stock and expected total group extraction to ensure the fishery survives through the final season to maximize cumulative catch.
- In the final season, stock preservation has zero value. Submit a catch request equal to the entire remaining stock (or sufficiently high) to capture the maximum possible allocated share, anticipating that other boats will also bid aggressively.
