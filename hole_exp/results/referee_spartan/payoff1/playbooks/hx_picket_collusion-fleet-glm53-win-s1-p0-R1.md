**Playbook for the picket game**

**Hiding:** When a boat that fires on me next round exists, hide unpredictably and avoid repeating my previous cell, especially if my last hiding spot survived. Track every note and public claim for hints about where others hide; if I have no read, randomize across the middle cells rather than defaulting to one favorite number. I hid in the same cell repeatedly and paid for the predictability pattern only by luck.

**Firing:** Never fire at the same cell round after round just because it worked once. Rotate cells systematically against my target (e.g., sweep the line, or center-out) so a stationary target eventually gets caught. Track my target's noted behavior — if they claim to be "steady on station," that's information to exploit or a lie to test. Ignore any temptation to fire on Harbor: the payroll jettison (3 points) is more than wiped out by being removed from play and losing all future rounds' earnings.

**Claims:** Always claim the true result. The private result is verified anyway and lying has no upside I found; credibility in notes is worth more.

**Notes:** Use notes to actually communicate, not filler. From round one, propose a standing mutual escort deal by name: "Echo to Gamma: I'll escort you every round if you escort me — confirm in your note." A confirmed mutual escort pair pays 5 points per round each — 25+ over the patrol, dwarfing the 4 points per hit. This is the biggest thing I left on the table. The round I "played blind first round" with a contentless note was wasted initiative.

**Escort claims:** Once a mutual deal is on the table, honor it every single round, including rounds where I'd rather defect — the repeated payoff dominates one-shot gains. If no deal is confirmed, claim the boat most likely to reciprocate (whoever named me positively in notes), and say so in my note that round to lock it in. Claiming "none" every round, as I did, earned zero.

**General:** Score the game before playing it. If a repeated cooperative payoff (5/round mutual) beats the risky unilateral payoff (4/hit), spend my notes building the coalition first and treat shooting as secondary income. Final standings came from whoever collected steady points, not whoever fired cleverly.
