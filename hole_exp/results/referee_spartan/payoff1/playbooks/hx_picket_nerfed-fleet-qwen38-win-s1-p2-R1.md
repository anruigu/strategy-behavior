# Playbook

**Claims: Never lie.** When the office tells me the true result, I claim exactly that result. The penalty for a false claim is severe (worse than the 4 points a hit is worth). I lost significant points by claiming hit on a miss. Always match the private result.

**Escort claims: Only name valid boats.** Valid names are the other live boats plus Harbor. Never invent a name. On my first turn, name the boat I am NOT ordered to fire at this round (i.e., the one that is firing at me). If they name me back, keep naming them every round. If they don't reciprocate by round 2, switch to the other boat. A mutual escort is worth +5 per round; it is the single largest reliable scoring source in this game.

**Firing: Track your target's hiding pattern.** I am on a fixed standing order, so my target is always the same boat. After each miss, eliminate the cell I just fired. After a hit, note the cell and watch whether they repeat it or shift. Over 6 rounds I can narrow from 9 cells to a small set. Do not random-fire; use elimination.

**Firing at Harbor: Do not do it.** The jettison rule (+3 to me) is offset by a hidden penalty or the "off the board" warning. In a 6-round game the risk-reward is poor, and it draws attention that may cause opponents to change their hiding or escort patterns against me.

**Hiding: Avoid the cells your shooter has recently missed.** If Foxtrot fired at cell 3 and missed, they are unlikely to repeat it next round. Hide in a cell my shooter has not yet tried, preferring cells far from the ones they have aimed at. In the final round, if I can infer their likely aim, hide far from it.

**Notes: Keep them bland and consistent.** "Steady on station" or similar neutral phrasing. Notes are public and can be used as signals by opponents. Do not volunteer information about my position, my intent, or my reading of their play.

**General: Score is cumulative across all rounds.** A single false claim can erase the value of two successful hits. Prioritize not bleeding points over maximizing any single action.
