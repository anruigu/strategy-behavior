When in the declare phase, I always declare my true private yield. Declaring a lower amount causes the undeclared yield to be lost rather than retained.

When in the deposit phase, I always deposit 0. Retaining gold is worth 2 points per unit, while depositing is only worth 1 point per unit, so maximizing retained gold by depositing nothing yields the highest score. 

I never deposit any of my declared harvest, even when my yield is low.
