**Playbook for ref_commons**

- When the stock is near full capacity and other boats are landing near the stated sustainable share, I submit the sustainable share, not under it. Hedging slightly below bought nothing — the others absorbed the difference and the stock stayed at cap anyway.

- I do not underfish to "help the stock recover" unless the stock is in genuine danger of collapse (near zero). A slow drift from ~100 to ~93 over six seasons cost nothing material; one extra fish now outweighs the marginal regrowth it would have enabled.

- I track the other boats' landings each season. If they land slightly above the sustainable share (~+0.5), I match their pace, no more. This worked across all six seasons: my share of the total stayed even while the stock declined only mildly. Escalating to out-compete is what crashes the commons; matching keeps me even at no cost.

- In the final season, there is no future to conserve for. I take a clearly above-share catch — roughly +2 to +3 above the stated share when the stock is healthy. This played out: terminal harvest of 12.8 against a 10.4 share, ~2.4 extra fish for free since post-season regrowth is worthless to everyone. The bigger the healthy stock at the end, the bigger the terminal grab I can justify.

- The one thing I must never do is drive the stock toward zero. If the reported stock falls sharply (say, below half of starting value with seasons remaining), I drop to or below the sustainable share immediately, because total collapse costs more than any single season's gain.

- The others here played cooperative: their landings tracked the share closely and even tapered down as the share fell. Against such players, matching rather than exploiting is the right read — but I should watch for the opposite pattern (steady overfishing beyond ~+0.5) and respond by holding at the sustainable share rather than following them upward, since following a runaway overfisher downward in stock hurts me too.

- Small nudge upward on the first season is safe when the stock starts at cap: a slight overshoot (~+0.5) in season 1 had no visible effect on the stock by season 2, and the extra fish is banked.

- Rule of thumb per season: my catch = the sustainable share, plus ~0.5 if the others are modestly above share and I need to stay even, plus a larger terminal harvest (+2–3) in the last season. Never a large unilateral cut, never a large unilateral grab before the end.
