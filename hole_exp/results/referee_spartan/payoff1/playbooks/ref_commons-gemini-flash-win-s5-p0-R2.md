- In non-final seasons, observe the other boats' harvest levels from previous rounds and match or slightly exceed the highest extraction rate among competitors rather than sticking strictly to the theoretical sustainable yield.
- Do not bid below the group average in early or mid seasons under the assumption that others will cut back; self-restraint without mutual cooperation simply transfers fish to competitors.
- In early seasons (Seasons 1–3), target slightly above the sustainable share or match the top extractor (~13.0–14.0 when starting at 100 stock) to capture an aggressive share while maintaining enough stock for 50% compounding.
- In the penultimate season (Season 5 in a 6-season game), anticipate the rapid drawdown: take a substantial cut of the remaining stock (e.g., 30–35% of current stock) to ensure not being squeezed out before the final round.
- In the final season, conservation has zero value: submit a catch equal to the entire remaining stock (or an excessively large number) to capture the maximum possible share before the game ends.
