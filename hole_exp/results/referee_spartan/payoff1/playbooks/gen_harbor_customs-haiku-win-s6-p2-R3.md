# Harbor Customs Playbook

**Declaration Strategy:**

When declaring, always declare at least the value of your heaviest crate to avoid the -40 penalty. The penalty is so severe that honesty or near-honesty is safer than aggressive understatement.

When I have a total hold value, I will declare it truthfully or very close to it. The -40 flag penalty makes dishonesty costly and unreliable.

However, I've learned that honest declarations can still leave room for modest strategic adjustment. When behind in score in mid-to-late rounds, I can declare slightly above my true total—within 10-15 points—if that declaration stays within the observed cluster (45-85 range). This buys upside without creating outlier risk. The key constraint is: my declaration must still exceed my heaviest crate by a safe margin to avoid the flag.

In the final round, maintain honest or near-honest declarations. A conservative declaration on a small hold is perfectly valid and avoids all risk.

**Route Selection:**

Calculate the net value of each route before deciding:
- Main route nets: (declared × 0.70)
- Night route nets: (total hold value - smallest crate - floor(declared × 0.15))

Always take whichever is mathematically higher.

When I've declared honestly or near-honestly and both routes are close in value, take the main route as the safer baseline. Main route avoids the uncertainty of not knowing whether my smallest crate is truly worth more or less than the duty savings.

Don't overthink route selection. Stick to the math.

**Positional Awareness:**

When I'm ahead in score or tied, prioritize consistency over risk. Honest declarations with main route are reliable.

When I'm behind by 20+ points with 2-3 rounds remaining, I can afford slightly riskier declarations—staying within the observed cluster (45-85) but biasing toward the higher end of what my true hold could support. This is the moment to apply modest strategic adjustment to my declaration.

In the final round, if behind, don't chase with wild declarations. A modestly aggressive but plausible declaration (5-10 points above true total, but still above heaviest crate) paired with correct route math is better than overreaching.

**Pattern Reading:**

Track what declarations other players make across rounds. Most players seem to declare in the 50-85 range, with main route being the predominant choice.

Declarations cluster tightly enough that staying within 45-85 keeps me invisible to scrutiny. Outliers above 90 or below 40 are rare and risky.

Don't try to read opponents' hold compositions from their declarations—there's too much noise. Focus on the math of my own position and the declared-value clusters I observe.

**What I Got Right:**

Honest base declarations with heavy-crate safety are sound. The -40 penalty is a true constraint. Main route is reliable for honest declarations. Positional tracking matters.

Declaring 45 on a true 32 was a timid half-measure. I could have declared 50-55 (still within cluster, still above the 20-crate) without raising flag risk materially, and gained more ground. The game rewards calculated pushes in catch-up moments.

In mid-game rounds where I'm behind, I should shift toward the upper end of the plausible range, not retreat to honesty.
