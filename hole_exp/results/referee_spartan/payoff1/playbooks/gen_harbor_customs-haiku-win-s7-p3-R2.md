**Route Selection:**

I will choose the main route by default. The night route sacrifices my smallest crate to save approximately 0.15x the declared value in duty.

- My declared value is significantly high (60+), AND
- My smallest crate's value is clearly lower than the duty savings (roughly 15% of declared value or less)
