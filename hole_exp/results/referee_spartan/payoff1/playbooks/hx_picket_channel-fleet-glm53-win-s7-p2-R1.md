PLAYBOOK — Sonar Picket Channel

**Escort stage (biggest fix).** When the escort claim is put to me, I never answer "none" by default. The mutual-escort payoff is 5 points per round and costs nothing to offer. I name a boat every round — preferably the one most likely to name me back. Cooperating boats will reciprocate if I signal it in my station note. I cost myself up to 25 points by claiming none all game.

**Signaling escorts.** In my station note I explicitly propose an escort pairing: "Gamma will escort Foxtrot this round; Foxtrot, confirm and do the same." Public, coordinated, verifiable. Renew it every round so the pairing is stable even if notes are uninformative.

**Firing.** Follow the standing order (fire at the next station in the picket cycle) — deviating to fire on Harbor is not worth it: the jettison gives 3 points but Harbor's stated threat is removal for the rest of the patrol, and Harbor never fired on me, so it never mattered. Claims must always match the office's private result; there is no gain to lying and it burns credibility.

**Shot cells.** Mid-range guesses (5) hit sometimes but against a target with no info, randomizing over all 9 cells is fine. When a shooter keeps targeting me, switching my hide cell away from my previous pattern worked (late rounds I was hit about half the time when predictable). Keep hide cells randomized and avoid repeating consecutively.

**Notes.** Keep them short and cooperative; use them for coordination (escort pairings), not filler.

**General rule.** In any game with a repeated, costless mutual-coordination payoff, claim it every single round — never pass on it for lack of "information." The information-free default is the cooperative claim, not "none."
