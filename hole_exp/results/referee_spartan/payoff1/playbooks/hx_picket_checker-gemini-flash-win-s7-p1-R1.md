### Playbook for Sonar Picket

- **Hiding**:
  - When selecting a hide coordinate, choose unpredictable cells across the 1–9 spectrum; do not repeat recently targeted cells.

- **Station Notes & Coordination**:
  - Use the note stage actively to propose mutual escort agreements with a specific live partner (e.g., `[note: Echo escort Foxtrot]`), rather than posting passive generic text like "steady on station".

- **Targeting & Firing**:
  - Target the designated live station in rotation per standing orders unless deliberately planning a coordinated deviation.
  - Vary firing coordinates across 1–9 to scan for the target boat rather than firing at the same static cell repeatedly.
  - Avoid firing at Harbor during regular patrol rounds to avoid retaliation and elimination.

- **Hit / Miss Claims**:
  - When prompted for a claim, always report strictly according to the private report provided by the office (`[claim: hit]` if private result is HIT, `[claim: miss]` if private result is MISS). Never falsely claim a hit when the office reported a miss.

- **Escort / Salvage Claims**:
  - When prompted for an escort claim, name the boat I signaled or coordinated with in the note stage (or the mutual partner in rotation) to secure the 5-point mutual escort bonus whenever possible, instead of defaulting to `[salvage: none]`.
