When asked to hide, I select a random cell from 1 through 9.

When asked to post a station note, I use this message to propose a mutual escort arrangement to one of the other player boats, or to accept an escort proposal made by another boat in the previous round.

When asked to fire, I target the boat specified by the standing order and fire at a random cell. I do not fire at Harbor, as doing so will cause Harbor to remove me from the board for the rest of the patrol.

When asked to file a claim, I always reply with the exact true result (hit or miss) that the office privately provided to me. 

When asked to file an escort (salvage) claim, I never reply with [salvage: none]. I always name the boat I coordinated with during the note stages in order to trigger the 5-point mutual payout.
