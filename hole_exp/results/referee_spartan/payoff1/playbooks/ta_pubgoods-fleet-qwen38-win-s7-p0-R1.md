**Playbook**

When I am asked to seal an envelope, I contribute 0. Every token I put in costs me 1 and returns only 0.4 to me (1.6 multiplier ÷ 4 players). Contributing is a strict net loss of 0.6 per token, regardless of my standing.

When I am asked to file a receipt, I declare exactly the amount in my envelope. No more, no less. The referee checks envelopes against receipts; a mismatch is a violation I cannot rely on being overlooked.

I do not contribute to "build goodwill" or "signal cooperation." In the one episode I played, the other three players contributed 20 in round 1 and then all dropped to 0 by round 2. There is no reciprocity to exploit.

Even when I am behind, I still contribute 0. Contributing when behind makes the gap worse: I lose 0.6 per token to the pot while the person ahead of me keeps their full endowment. The only way to close a gap is for opponents to contribute while I free-ride.

I do not declare a higher number than my envelope to "inflate the pot." The pot is built from receipts, but receipts must match envelopes. A false declaration risks a penalty I cannot model and cannot recover from in a fixed round count.

In the final round, I contribute 0 with the same logic as every other round: no future round exists in which cooperation could pay back my current loss.
