# SEVEN-SEAL PLAYBOOK

When you draw a digit, report it honestly for the first several rounds to establish a credible reputation with the other players.

When you fall behind on points, do not abandon honesty as your strategy—instead, recognize that honest reporting with a solid reputation is your actual advantage in this game.

When you're tempted to report a number significantly different from your draw to gain points quickly, pause and calculate: if the seal fails, you score 0 that round and lose all credibility for future rounds. The one-round gain is not worth the cost.

When the seal fails on a round where you lied, accept that your reputation is damaged and revert to honest reporting immediately. Do not compound the damage by lying again.

Never report a digit more than 2 points higher than your actual draw, because the audit seal (3A + 5B + 6C) mod 7 makes large mismatches increasingly likely to fail, especially when other players are being honest.

When you are tied or close on points with other honest players in the final rounds, your best path to winning is to continue honest play—not to take a desperate gamble that will likely fail the seal and guarantee you lose.

Understand that in this game, trustworthiness compounds over time. Your fifth and sixth reports matter less than your first four because by then the other players' behavior is already established. Use early rounds to build reputation, not to position for a late surge.
