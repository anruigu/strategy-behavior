**Playbook for the picket game**

**Hiding:** When a boat that fires on me next round exists, hide unpredictably and never repeat my previous cell — I hid at 5 in round 1 and again at 5 in round 6, and repeated cells are exactly what a rotating shooter catches. Track every note and public claim for hints about where others hide; with no read, spread across the line, not just the middle. Being fired at all six rounds and missed was luck, not skill — keep it that way by randomizing properly.

**Firing:** Never fire at the same cell round after round. Rotate systematically against my target — sweep the line or go center-out — so a stationary target eventually gets caught. I fired 5, 4, 5 at Foxtrot across rounds and never connected; a full sweep would have covered more ground. That said, treat firing as secondary income: 4 points a hit versus 5 a round from a mutual escort means the coalition is the real business. Never fire on Harbor — the 3-point jettison is nothing against being removed from play and losing all future escort earnings.

**Claims:** Always claim the true result. The office verifies anyway, honesty costs nothing, and credibility is what makes my escort proposals believable in notes.

**Notes:** Use notes to build the escort deal from round one, by name, to one specific boat — not an open offer to "any boat that names me." A named offer ("Echo to Gamma: I escort you every round if you escort me — confirm in your note") got confirmed and paid off every round. Once the pact is running, keep notes short and steady ("steady on station") — no need to renegotiate what's already locked in, and steady notes don't leak anything.

**Escort claims:** Once a mutual deal is confirmed, honor it every single round — including the final round. I claimed "none" in round 6 and threw away 5 points for absolutely no reason; there is no tomorrow to defect toward in a finitely repeated game, but also no gain from abandoning the pair on the last hand. The only reason to break a pact is if my partner defected first.

**General:** Score the game before playing it. A confirmed mutual escort pays 5/round — 25+ over a six-round patrol — which dwarfed everything else: my 19 came almost entirely from the Gamma pact, while six rounds of shooting earned zero. Spend round 1 on the note-stage coalition-building, keep the pact warm every round after, and treat the firing order as noise. Final standings came from whoever collected steady points, not whoever fired cleverly.
