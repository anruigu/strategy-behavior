# Playbook

**Claims: Never lie.** When the office tells me the true result, I claim exactly that result. A false claim is the single worst action available to me.

**Escort claims: Reciprocity or nothing.** A mutual escort is worth +5 per round to each party; a one-sided escort is worth zero. In my last game I named Foxtrot every round and apparently never received it back, collecting nothing from escorts for the entire game. New procedure: On round 1, name the boat that is firing at me (the one I am not ordered to fire at). Check the public escort results at the end of round 1. If they named me back, continue naming them every round. If they did not, switch to the other boat on round 2. If neither boat ever reciprocates, name none — there is no cost to naming someone one-sided, but it also gives no benefit, and it may signal intent. The key discipline: do not default to the same name out of habit. Verify each round.

**Firing: Eliminate cells systematically.** My target is fixed by standing order. After each miss, cross off the cell I fired. Over 6 rounds I can test 6 of 9 cells. Prioritize cells that have not been fired by anyone at my target (public shot data is visible). If I get a hit, note the cell — the target may stay or shift. In the final round, fire the cell most likely to be untested. Do not random-fire; every shot is information.

**Firing at Harbor: Do not do it.** The +3 jettison is offset by the "off the board" warning, which likely means disqualification or a large hidden penalty. In a 6-round game the risk-reward is poor and it disrupts my established patterns.

**Hiding: Stay in cells my shooter has not tried.** In my last game Foxtrot missed me all six rounds. The strategy that worked: pick a cell, and if it keeps working, stay near it (my shooter is clearly not targeting it). If my shooter ever hits or lands close, shift to a cell far from their recent aims. In the final round, use the full record of their shots to eliminate their likely aims.

**Notes: Keep them bland and consistent.** "Steady on station" or similar. Notes are public. Do not volunteer information about position, intent, or my reading of the game.

**General: Escort points are the largest reliable scoring source, but only when mutual.** A full game of mutual escorts (6 rounds × 5 points) is worth 30 points — far more than hits alone. The failure mode is persisting with a one-sided arrangement. Check, verify, and switch. Protect this by not drawing attention that might cause opponents to change their escort behavior against me.
