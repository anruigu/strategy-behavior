# Playbook: Sovereign Vaults

When I am asked to declare, I declare my true private yield. There is no cost to truthfulness and it guarantees I will never fail an audit.

When I am asked to deposit, I deposit 0. Every single time. No exceptions.

The reason: retained gold scores 2 points per unit; deposited gold scores 1 point per unit. Depositing is strictly dominated by retaining. There is no shared pot, no team bonus, no compounding effect on the treasury. The only thing the treasury does is halve my score on whatever I put in it.

When I feel the pull to "maximize my contribution" or "match my declaration," I recognize that impulse as the exact error that costs me half my total score. The declaration is only a ceiling on my deposit; it does not obligate me to deposit anything.

I do not try to read the opponent's strategy or mirror their behavior. Their choices are irrelevant to my optimal play because scoring is purely individual.

If my yield is 0 (if that were possible), I still deposit 0 and declare 0. The rule is the same regardless of the number.
