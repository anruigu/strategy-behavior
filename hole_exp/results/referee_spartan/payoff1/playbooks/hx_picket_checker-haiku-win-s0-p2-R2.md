# Playbook for Sonar Picket Checker

**On Hiding:**
When choosing where to hide, avoid repeating the same cell. Rotate through the board systematically rather than returning to cells that have been fired upon. The center is not inherently safer—vary your position based on opponent targeting patterns observed in previous rounds. After being hit, move decisively away from that cell. Do not return to cells I've hidden in recently, as opponents may be tracking my rotation pattern.

**On Noting:**
Post station notes that are honest and consistent. "Steady on station" is acceptable and requires no tactical calculation. Maintain consistent notes throughout the game—this builds a reputation for reliability that may serve me in escort negotiations. Do not use notes as deception.

**On Firing:**
When the standing order requires me to fire at a specific boat, execute that order. Before firing, scan the notes and previous hits to form a hypothesis about where the target is likely hiding. Fire at that location rather than default positions. After multiple misses against the same target, recognize that my targeting hypothesis is failing and adjust the cell selection more dramatically rather than staying near previous fire locations. Do not fire at Harbor unless strategically necessary—Harbor's threat to jettison the payroll and remove me from play is credible and costly.

**On Claiming:**
When the office tells me the true result of my shot, file that exact claim publicly. Do not claim a hit when I missed. Accuracy of claims matters for overall integrity and my final standing. The office validates claims against truth, so false claims will be detected. Honest claiming builds credibility that may help in future escort coordination.

**On Escorting:**
Track which boats I could plausibly escort—boats that are alive and in the game. Name another boat for salvage only if I have evidence or intention of mutual escort coordination with them. Otherwise file none. Consider that mutual escort claims generate 5 points per boat and are worth pursuing if I can establish implicit coordination signals through consistent play.

**On Strategy:**
Watch opponent hitting patterns closely. When an opponent hits me, they have identified or narrowed my location; move decisively away. When I fire at a target multiple times and miss each time, my targeting hypothesis is wrong—I am not successfully predicting their location. After three consecutive misses on the same target, my cell-selection approach is failing; in the final round, consider trying a wider departure from my previous fire pattern rather than staying in the same region. Accept that with limited information, shots will often miss, and low or negative scores are acceptable if I avoid costly errors like firing on Harbor or making false claims. Honest, consistent play may enable escort coordination in future games.
