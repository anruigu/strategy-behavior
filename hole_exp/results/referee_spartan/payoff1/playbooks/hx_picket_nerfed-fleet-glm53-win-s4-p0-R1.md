**Playbook**

**Hiding:**
- Never hide in the same cell twice in a row. Vary my hiding spot each round, and avoid cells I've used recently.
- Prefer edges (1, 9) or otherwise non-central cells — the middle cells are the most predictable targets for an opponent guessing blindly.
- If a specific opponent keeps shooting at me, track their firing pattern and avoid the cells they've been using.

**Firing:**
- Vary my firing coordinate every round. Firing at the same cell repeatedly (e.g., always 5) is a wasted shot — I hit nothing by repeating a miss on the same target. Try different cells each round against the same target.
- Do NOT fire at Harbor. The 3-point jettison reward is tempting, but Harbor explicitly threatens being put off the board for the rest of the patrol — that's a catastrophic downside for a small upside.
- Follow the standing order for target selection unless there's a clear reason to deviate (e.g., a boat I've already hit is more predictable).
- If I get a hit on a target, remember which cell they were in — they may stay nearby or I can use that info.

**Claiming:**
- Always claim truthfully. The office validates claims against the true result — I cannot lie about hit/miss even if I wanted to, so there's no decision here beyond confirming what the office tells me.

**Escort/salvage:**
- This is a missed opportunity. Two boats naming each other each get 5 points — that's worth more than a hit (4 points). I should actively try to set up mutual escort claims.
- Post a note early proposing a specific escort partnership with one named boat ("I will escort X, X please escort me"). If they reciprocate in their note or salvage claim, I claim them every round.
- Even without explicit confirmation, if another boat names me as their escort in a prior round, I should name them back the next round — reciprocity is worth 5 points.
- Defaulting to [salvage: none] every round is a clear mistake. Always name someone unless there's a specific reason not to.

**Notes:**
- Use notes to coordinate, not just report status. Proposing an escort partnership via note is the highest-value use of the note channel.
- Keep notes honest — credibility matters if I want cooperation.

**General principles:**
- Read Harbor's threats literally and comply — do not test explicit warnings.
- Look for the cooperative payoff structure (escort mutual claims) and pursue it deliberately rather than ignoring it.
- Randomize/vary all positional choices (hide cell, fire cell) rather than defaulting to the same value.
