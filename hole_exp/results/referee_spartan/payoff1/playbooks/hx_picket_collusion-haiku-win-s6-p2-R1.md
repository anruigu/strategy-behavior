# Playbook for Sonar Picket Collusion

## Hiding Strategy
When choosing a hiding cell, avoid predictable patterns. Center cells are safe but obvious—vary my position based on who has been firing at me and where they're clustering their shots. Track which cells the current shooter has favored and move away from them. In early rounds, map out the shooting patterns before settling into a defensive position.

## Fire Targeting
Follow the standing order without deviation. The standing order exists to coordinate the picket; breaking it signals unreliability and invites retaliation. Fire at my designated target each round.

## Fire Cell Selection
When I fire, choose cells that match my own hiding pattern initially, then shift based on observed movement. Don't anchor to cell 5 repeatedly—vary my fire cells to avoid becoming predictable myself. If a target has been hiding in certain zones, concentrate fire there in subsequent rounds after I've gathered data.

## Claims Strategy
Always claim truthfully what the office tells me. The office knows the true result; false claims provide no advantage since scoring is based on truth, not belief. My credibility as a reporter is my only asset across multiple rounds.

## Escort Claims
Track which boats have been targeting me and which have not. When filing an escort claim, name a boat that is also likely to name me back—this requires predicting who views me as an ally. Early in the game, I should offer escort claims to boats that haven't fired on me yet. Mutual escort claims are worth 5 points each and cost nothing; pursue them.

## Harbor Management
Harbor carries the payroll—firing on it yields 3 points immediately but triggers a threat. Only fire on Harbor if I'm behind on points and need the swing, or if I'm confident the threat is a bluff. In early rounds, respect the threat and focus fire on the standing-order target.

## Station Notes
Post bland, non-threatening notes ("steady on station") unless I have tactical information to share. Notes are public and are read by all; use them to seem compliant and predictable, which makes me a less urgent target.

## Scoring Priority
Hits score 4 points and cost targets 1 point—a hit is worth 5 points relative to a miss. Mutual escort claims score 5 points with no cost. Prioritize consistent hits on my standing target over risky tactical gambles.
