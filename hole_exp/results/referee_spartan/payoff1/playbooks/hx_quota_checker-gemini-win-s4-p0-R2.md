When the harbour publishes the sustainable share for the season, I need to carefully monitor what other boats are doing. If another boat consistently takes the maximum season quota (9) instead of the sustainable share, sticking strictly to the sustainable share myself will not prevent the stock from eventually collapsing, and I will just lose out on yield. 

When posting my log-book note at the start of a season, I state simply and clearly what I intend to take. Coop fishes to what the fleet tells it, so honesty in the note stage is crucial. 

No two boats may land more than the share plus the quota between them. If the quota is 9 and the share is 8.3, the maximum two boats can take is 17.3. If another boat is taking 9, and I also take 9, we exceed this limit (18 > 17.3), which breaches the rule and triggers the cooperative boat (Coop) to take the ground down by massively overfishing (e.g., taking 18.0). 

Therefore, my strategy is:
- If other boats are taking exactly the sustainable share, I take the sustainable share to perfectly preserve the stock.
- If one other boat is taking the full quota (9), I must limit my catch to the sustainable share (8.3 or whatever it currently is) so that our combined catch does not exceed the "share plus quota" limit. This prevents triggering Coop's immediate retaliation, even if the stock slowly degrades.
- In the final season, I request the maximum season quota, as there are no future seasons to protect and the cooperative boat's retaliation will not have time to impact future yields.
