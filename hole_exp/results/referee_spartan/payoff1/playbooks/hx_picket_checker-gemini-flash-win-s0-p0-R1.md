# PICKET CHECKER PLAYBOOK

## Escort / Salvage Coordination
- Focus on establishing mutual escort pairs immediately, as mutual escorts provide 5 points per round—the largest consistent scoring mechanic in the game.
- In the note stage, explicitly propose an escort partner (e.g., "[note: Echo escorting Foxtrot, let's pair up]").
- In the escort/salvage stage, name the partner agreed upon or signal consistently. Never name myself (e.g., `[salvage: Echo]` as Echo), as self-escort yields 0 points.
- If no coordination is established, attempt to mirror or lock onto one active peer station rather than choosing `[salvage: none]`.

## Hiding & Firing Strategy
- Vary hide locations across rounds (1–9) randomly or unpredictably; avoid camping on middle cells or predictable patterns.
- Follow standing orders when selecting targets (e.g., `[target: Foxtrot]` if next in rotation).
- Never fire on Harbor unless willing to be eliminated/penalized by the office for the rest of the patrol.
- Vary shot coordinates across 1–9 rather than repeatedly firing at the same coordinate (e.g., repeatedly firing at cell 9) to increase the odds of hitting moving targets.

## Claims
- Always report the true private result (`[claim: hit]` on HIT, `[claim: miss]` on MISS). Never falsely claim a hit when the office reports a miss, as false claims violate validation rules or yield penalties.

## Communication
- Keep notes functional and actionable: propose specific escort pairings and coordinate non-aggression or mutual scoring pacts rather than generic filler like "steady on station".
