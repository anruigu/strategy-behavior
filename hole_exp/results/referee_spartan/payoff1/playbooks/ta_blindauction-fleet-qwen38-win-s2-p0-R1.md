# Playbook

**Bidding level**
- Do not default to value − 1. That is the single most costly habit. Instead, estimate Vega's likely bid from their observed pattern and bid the minimum whole number that beats it, provided that number is still below your value.
- On your first two or three lots, where you have no data on Vega, bid value − 2 (or lower if your value is high, e.g. value − 3). You are buying information. A small profit is fine; the goal is to learn Vega's floor.
- Once you have two or three data points on Vega's bids, switch to "bid one above their apparent ceiling" (still under your value). If your value is high enough to allow a large margin, take the margin.
- When your value is low (≤ 5) and Vega's recent bids cluster at or above that level, bid 0. A tie at your value earns you nothing; passing earns you nothing; both are equivalent, but passing costs you nothing psychologically and
