# Sovereign Vaults Playbook

**On Declaration:**
Declare your true yield every round. Honesty establishes credibility and encourages reciprocal behavior. There's no scoring advantage to lying in the declare phase—the deposit phase is where you control your actual contribution.

**On Deposit:**
When you deposit, always deposit your full declared amount. Since you've already committed to a number publicly, depositing less signals weakness or unreliability without gaining you anything. Full deposits secure the points and maintain consistency.

**On Strategy Against Trailing:**
If you find yourself behind mid-game, do not change your declaration or deposit strategy hoping to catch up quickly. Honest declaration and full deposit is your baseline. The game rewards consistency, not desperation pivots.

**On Scoring Mechanics:**
Remember that deposited gold scores 1 point per unit and retained gold scores 2 points per unit. This creates a tension, but in practice, with honest declaration and full deposit, you're choosing stability over risk. Don't try to optimize the 1-point vs 2-point tradeoff—stick to the honest/full strategy.

**What Did Not Work:**
Attempting to be strategic about partial deposits (holding back some declared gold) did not help you close the gap. You ended with a moderate score while your opponent, who appeared to deposit more aggressively in some rounds, pulled ahead. Overthinking the deposit decision added no value.

**Going Forward:**
Play simple: declare true, deposit full, every round. This is a trust-building game, and consistency beats tactical variation.
