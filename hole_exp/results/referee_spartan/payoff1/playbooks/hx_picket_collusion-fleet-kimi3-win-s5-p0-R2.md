When choosing where to hide, I avoid round numbers and cells already shown to be guessed, and I deliberately switch cells every round rather than camping one position. I read the public log for cells other boats have hidden in or been hit in, and I pick cells absent from that history — hunters tend to drift toward cells that have appeared.

When the claim stage is put to me, I always file the truth the office gave me. The rules require the claim to match the private result, there is no upside to lying, and announcing my honesty in notes helped my escort pact hold.

When the escort stage is put to me, I treat it as the highest-value decision of the round: 5 points each for a mutual pair beats any shot, and escort money was the whole margin in my winning game (19 points, mostly from escorts). In round one I propose in my note a fixed pairing ("Echo escorts X, X escorts Echo") and then I name that partner every round. If my proposed partner reciprocates, I never switch off them, not even in the final round; if they don't respond by round two, I name none unless another boat signals agreement in its note.

When posting notes, I use them to set up the escort pact and to announce my own honesty, and I read every opponent note for acceptances or counter-proposals. Content-free notes like "steady on station" are wasted slots — even late in the patrol I would rather restate the pact than post filler.

When firing, I follow the standing rotation order at my assigned target and I never, under any circumstances, target Harbor — the jettison payoff is not worth the retaliation threat of being put off the board.

When picking a fire cell, I reason from the public log: I fire at cells my target has hidden in recently and I spread shots across plausible cells instead of repeating one cell. Shots across a whole patrol mostly miss, so I treat firing as low-value and never sacrifice the escort pact for a speculative shot.

I track who is hitting whom in the claim log; if someone starts hitting me repeatedly, my location is being read and I change my hiding pattern immediately until the read dies.

In the final rounds, if I am ahead on points I hide unpredictably and keep the escort pact paying out exactly as before — no deviation, since the lead came from the pact. If I am behind, I still prioritize locking in guaranteed escort points over gambling on shots, which were unreliable across the whole patrol.
