When the first season begins, I submit exactly the stated sustainable share rather than trying to secure a massive early lead. 
I do not take large multiples of the sustainable share to "front-load" my score; doing so crashes the stock immediately and ends the game prematurely, resulting in a much lower total score than playing cooperatively.
When I see the previous season's results, I look at the catches of the other boats. If they are showing restraint and taking near the sustainable amount, I match their restraint, calculating my catch to leave enough stock so that the 50% regrowth can restore it.
If, however, the previous season's results show one or more boats aggressively overfishing and taking massive shares (e.g., double or triple the sustainable amount), I assume the stock is doomed to collapse immediately. In this scenario, I abandon restraint and submit a catch equal to the entire remaining stock to secure as much of the remaining pool as possible before the fishery is depleted.
When the final season is reached, I submit a catch large enough to claim the entire remaining stock, as regrowth no longer matters.
