**Playbook for the shared fishery game**

- Default to submitting exactly the stated sustainable share each season. If all boats do this, the stock stays at cap and I bank the maximum total over the season run.
- Recompute the sustainable share from the current stock each turn (it drops slightly as stock drops); never just repeat my previous number.
- If a rival takes modestly above the sustainable share, do not retaliate — absorb it and keep my own catch at the sustainable level. One boat slightly overfishing costs me little; escalation costs everyone.
- Only deviate upward myself if the stock is genuinely collapsing toward zero and others have abandoned restraint — then grab what I can before the crash.
- In the final season, conservation has no value: take substantially more than the sustainable share, since there is no future to protect. My small overage last time was too timid — with no later rounds, a much larger catch was free.
- In the second-to-last season, still hold at sustainable: the stock regrows and there's a real chance rivals punish or the stock matters for the final round.
- Remember the regrowth rule: 50% of what remains after the season's catch, capped at 100. With a stock near 100 and 3 boats taking ~11 each, the system is stable — this is why patience paid.
