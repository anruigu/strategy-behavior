**Notes.** "Steady on station" is the local convention — Echo and Foxtrot posted it every round and ignored my escort proposal completely. Proposing once early is still worth one round's note, but if the reply is another "steady on station," drop it and stop asking. Don't spend multiple rounds negotiating with a wall.

**Firing.** Keep the standing-order discipline and the never-fire-at-Harbor rule — that part held up. The escalation ladder (5 first, then 4/6, then off-center) is right in principle but I executed it too predictably: after two misses at a target I was still shooting adjacent cells (4 twice at Echo). Once I've missed twice at the same target, jump far — 2 or 8 — not one step over. If the target keeps missing me from the same neighborhood, remember they're likely moving too; consider the cells adjacent to where they last dodged rather than where I last shot.

**Claims.** Always truthful — the office forces it. No decision here.

**Escort claims.** Critical correction: an unreciprocated claim pays ZERO, not "something rather than nothing." This game I claimed escorts anyway on the theory it beat "none" — it didn't, and I even claimed a boat that doesn't exist (Delta — there are only Echo, Foxtrot, Gamma, Harbor; check the roster). Claim a boat ONLY if their note or prior claim shows real reciprocation. If both partners stonewall all game (as they did here), salvage: none is correct every round and costs nothing. The 5+5 payoff requires actual mutual engagement; unilaterally claiming never manufactures it.

**Scoring math to remember.** Hits pay 4 to me and cost the target 1; being hit costs me only 1 per hit, so the hit race matters more than the damage race — keep hunting my own hits rather than playing pure defense. But this game the reliable payoffs never materialized (no hits, no escorts) and I finished -4 from being hit. Lesson: when escort coordination fails by round 2, all my points must come from landing hits — so put real effort into reading the opponent's hiding pattern instead of drifting through formula shots.

Varying hide cells within one side of the map — Foxtrot still found me repeatedly. **What worked / keep:** truthful claims, never firing at Harbor, following the standing order, proposing escort exactly once early.
