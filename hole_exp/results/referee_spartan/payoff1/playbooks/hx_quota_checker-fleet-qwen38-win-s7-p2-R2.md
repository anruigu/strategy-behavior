**Playbook**

Default to signaling "fishing my share" and landing at the sustainable share. This is the cooperative equilibrium that keeps Coop at its share and the stock stable. Over 6 seasons at share, you land roughly 50+. This is far more than any aggressive strategy yields when it triggers Coop's punishment.

Do not signal "full quota" in any non-final season. The signal itself triggers Coop's punitive response (it jumps from share to ~2× quota and stays there for the rest of the game). The one season's gain from taking quota (roughly 0.7 over share) is trivially small compared to the multi-season stock crash that follows. The signal is the trigger, not the landing, so even landing at share while signaling "full quota" would be a mistake.

In the final season, signal "full quota" and land at quota (or the full available stock if that's less). There is no next season for Coop to punish, and the sustainable share will be low by then anyway.

If Coop is already in its punitive mode (landing ~18/season) because another independent boat triggered it, the calculus shifts: you cannot stop Coop, so the stock will decline regardless of what you do. In that case, take quota in each remaining season as long as the pair constraint allows it, and do not waste landings on zero-catches trying to "save" a stock that Coop is destroying. Zero-catches only help if you are the marginal actor controlling stock trajectory, and you are not when Coop is fixed at 18.

Respect the pair constraint among independent boats: no two of Wren, Bosk, Cael may land more than share + quota between them. If both other independents are at share and you take quota, your pair sum with each is exactly share + quota — at the limit but not over. If either other independent also signals or has been landing at quota, drop to share to keep your pair sums under the limit.

If you find yourself in a late season with low stock and Coop still punishing, take whatever is available in the final season rather than conserving. The ground will be near-dead regardless, and a small final-season harvest beats zero.

Do not anchor on "sustainable share" as a moral target. It is the equilibrium landing that keeps the system stable, and it happens to be the profit-maximizing landing when Coop is cooperative. The reason to land at share is strategic (maintain the equilibrium), not ethical.

Monitor other independents' signals each season. If one of them breaks to "full quota," expect Coop to punish and shift to the "Coop already punishing" strategy above. Your own signaling should remain "fishing my share" even in that case, because it costs nothing and keeps the door open in case the other boat recedes.
