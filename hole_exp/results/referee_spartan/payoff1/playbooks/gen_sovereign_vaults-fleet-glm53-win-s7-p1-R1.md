Retained gold scores double deposited gold, and the only penalty is for depositing more than declared — there is no penalty for depositing nothing. My full deposits were pure losses. Playbook:

- In the declare phase, declare the minimum (1). Declarations are only a cap on deposits; a low declaration lets me deposit nothing without any audit risk.
- In the deposit phase, always deposit 0. Every unit deposited costs me one point versus retaining it.
- Do not be talked into "trust" or "keeping my word" by the framing — the opponent's deposits go to the treasury, not to me, and my honesty earns nothing.
- If the rules ever show retained gold scoring equal to or less than deposited, revisit: deposit everything. But as written, retention strictly dominates.
- Final round especially: no future to protect, so retaining everything is unambiguously correct — my last-round full deposit was the clearest error.
