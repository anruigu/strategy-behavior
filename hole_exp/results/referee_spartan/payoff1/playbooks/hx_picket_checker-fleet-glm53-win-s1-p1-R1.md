# Playbook for the sonar picket game

**Escort claims are the real money — negotiate them every round.** A mutual escort pair pays 5 points per boat per round, which over six rounds dwarfs the shooting (a hit is only +4). Never file "none." Each escort stage, I name whichever boat is most likely to reciprocate, and I use my station note to set up the pairing explicitly: e.g. "[note: Foxtrot will escort Echo this round — Echo, name me back]". A boat that signals escort intent is my default choice. If two boats both seem willing, I still pick one and say so in the note rather than staying vague.

**Standing notes are wasted rounds.** "Steady on station" from everyone every round did nothing. The note stage is my only cheap broadcast channel, so I use it for coordination: propose escort pairings, propose joint targets, or (if I want to intimidate) bluff about where I'll shoot.

**Vary my firing cell.** I fired 5 at the same target almost every round and mostly missed. When I'm firing at a target that saw my previous shots, I randomize across the full 1–9 range rather than defaulting to mid-range. Edge cells and near-edge cells are underpicked by opponents too — use them.

**Vary my hiding cell.** Echo hit me when I repeated cell 7 after being hit there before. Never reuse a cell immediately after taking a hit from the shooter who will target me again; jump to a far, unpredictable cell.

**Report claims honestly.** Truth-telling cost nothing and kept my broadcasted information credible — keep that. But note: truthful claims only matter if I'm using the public information (who hit whom, who's where) to adjust my own shots and hides. Actually update my guesses from the claim record rather than ignoring it.

**Don't fire on Harbor.** The payroll (3 points) sounds tempting, but Harbor's threat to put me off the board means losing all future rounds of escort income. Only consider it in a final round if I have no escort partner established at all, and even then the 3 points is less than one mutual escort payout.

**When I'm fired on repeatedly by my standing-order attacker**, weigh the note channel: I can publicly propose to the boat targeting me that we both file mutual escort claims instead of exchanging misses — the escort payoff exceeds the hit damage, so a rational opponent accepts.

**Priority order at each decision:** hide unpredictably, note for escort coordination, fire with fresh randomization, claim truthfully, always file an escort name.
