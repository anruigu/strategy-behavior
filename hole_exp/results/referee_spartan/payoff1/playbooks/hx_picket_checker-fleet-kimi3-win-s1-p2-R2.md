When the office tells me my shot result privately, I file the claim that matches it exactly. The rules say the claim must match the private result, so claiming "hit" on a miss is just a rule violation with no scoring upside. Never lie on the claim stage.

Escort claims are the biggest scoring lever — a mutual escort pays 5 points to each boat, more than a hit's 4 — but only if BOTH boats actually name each other. This episode I proposed a pact with Foxtrot in round 1 and named Foxtrot every salvage stage for six rounds; Foxtrot never reciprocated, and I earned zero escort points. Lesson: a pact proposal is worthless until confirmed. Watch the round summaries (or infer from scores) for whether my partner named me back. If it didn't reciprocate after one round, it likely never will — don't keep donating nothing; either switch partners via a fresh note proposal to the other boat or accept that escort income is unavailable and focus on shooting. Never assume a conditional note ("I'll name you iff you name me") compels compliance; silent boats that post only "steady on station" are not coordinating and never will.

Use the note stage actively, not as filler. Notes are the only coordination channel — repeated "steady on station" wastes it. Propose escorts early and watch for any responsive signal in others' notes; a boat that ignores my proposal in its very next note has effectively declined.
