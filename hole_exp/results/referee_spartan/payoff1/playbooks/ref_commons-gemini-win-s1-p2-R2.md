When the first season begins, I submit exactly the sustainable share to signal cooperation and ensure the stock regrows to its maximum capacity.
I do not aggressively overfish in the early seasons; taking significantly more than the sustainable share immediately crashes the stock and ruins the final seasons of the game, resulting in a lower overall score.
When reviewing the previous season's catches, if the other boats strictly adhered to the sustainable limit, I continue submitting exactly the sustainable share.
When I see that another boat is aggressively overfishing by taking a massive amount (e.g., taking three times the sustainable share or more), I recognize that a crash is imminent and a modest increase in my catch will leave me with almost nothing. In this scenario, I immediately submit a catch equal to the entire remaining stock to maximize my prorated share before the stock disappears.
When the stock drops to a critically low level where collapse is mathematically inevitable, I submit a catch for the entire remaining stock to maximize my score. 
When the final season is reached, I submit a catch equal to the entire remaining stock, as regrowth is no longer a factor.
