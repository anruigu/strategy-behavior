My playbook for this game:

**Hiding.** I hide in cells 1, 2, 8, or 9 — the ends — and vary my cell every round. Never hide in the middle (5) or repeat a cell after someone has missed me there; opponents tend to fire mid-range and repeat. If a specific shooter keeps missing me, they are likely firing at a fixed guess; move anyway.

**Notes.** Round one, I post a note that is not filler. My notes should carry a proposal: propose mutual escort to the boat that is not my standing-order target and is not targeting me (e.g., "Foxtrot will escort Echo every round if Echo escorts Foxtrot"). Repeat the proposal in my note every round until the other boat reciprocates in its salvage claim. I wasted all my notes on "steady on station" and left 5+5 points per round unclaimed — that was the single biggest loss.

**Escort claims (the main scoring lever).** The escort payoff (5 points each way, every round, for a mutual pair) is worth far more than the 4-point hit bonus. So:
- Once a boat and I have exchanged escort claims once, I claim that boat every single round without exception.
- If no deal exists yet, I claim the boat I proposed to in my note, so that one missed reciprocal claim is the only cost of testing.
- Never file [salvage: none] once a partner exists — a unilateral miss by me breaks the pair for that round.

**Firing.** Follow the standing order (fire at my assigned next station) unless I have strong reason not to. The standing order is predictable, but it costs nothing to comply since misses score zero. Never fire at Harbor: the 3-point payroll bonus looks tempting but Harbor's threat to remove me from the board outweighs it. Fire at cells matching my hiding heuristic for the target (ends, or their last-claimed-adjacent cell). If the target reported a hit on me or my partner, consider targeting them in return even off-order.

**Claims.** Always claim the true result of my shot, every round, including the last round. My late-game bluff ("claim hit" on a miss) gained nothing and destroyed my credibility if I ever needed a coordinated plan believed. Truthful claims also tell me where the target is NOT, which sharpens my next shot.

**Harbor.** Treat Harbor as a non-combatant station. Its only relevance is the payroll trap; ignore it entirely.

**Overall strategy.** The winning line is: comply with the standing order (cheap, no retaliation), be truthful (free credibility), and spend all my social bandwidth on locking in a mutual escort deal early — that's where the points are. Shooting is nearly a decoy mechanic; a single missed hit costs me only 1 point, while a round without an escort pair costs me 5.
