When the first season begins, I do not take the suggested sustainable share or just slightly above it; doing so fails because other players will likely be highly aggressive and deplete the resource immediately. 

When deciding my first catch, I immediately request an equal division of the total initial stock (Total Stock divided by Number of Boats) to ensure I secure my absolute share before the stock inevitably collapses.

When I see the previous season's results and notice opponents taking significantly more than their sustainable share, I completely abandon all attempts at conservation.

When the stock is critically low due to aggressive overfishing by others, I request the entire remaining stock balance to secure whatever fraction is left before the fishery is finished.

When opponents actually demonstrate restraint and take only their sustainable share in the first season, I switch to matching their sustainable play in subsequent seasons to maximize the long-term yield.
