# Playbook for hx_picket_channel

**On hiding:** Place yourself in central cells (4-6) rather than extremes. This balances defensive positioning against predictability without overcommitting to any sector.

**On station notes:** Match the communication tenor of the table. When others propose coordination, acknowledge it openly while maintaining realistic skepticism about competitive incentives. Signal that you evaluate proposals on merit, not blind alliance. Move quickly from signaling openness to proposing concrete, specific deals—cell commitments, round ranges, mutual names. Vague willingness to "coordinate" leaves value on the table. Lock escort pacts by round 3 at the latest; post-round-4 offers arrive too late for credible mutual commitment since players cannot plan future hides with confidence.

**On firing:** When you have a standing target, commit to it. Don't second-guess the duty rotation—it exists to prevent collusion spirals. Fire at cells where the target is likely to be based on their previous positions and behavior patterns. Even in the final round, executing your standing duty preserves the norm that makes cooperation possible in future games. A locked mutual escort pact does not override the standing order; you fire on schedule and claim truthfully. The pact governs the escort stage, not the fire stage.

**On shot claims:** Always claim truthfully what the office tells you. Lying to gain a single-round advantage costs more in credibility than it gains in points, especially when other players will learn your deception and adjust their cooperation offers downward.

**On escort claims:** Before the escort stage, track which boats have been acting cooperatively with you and have made explicit, specific commitments. A mutual escort pact locked by name and cell and round in an earlier note stage is a real deal—execute it in the escort stage even if you missed your shot that round or fired on that boat per standing order. The fire stage and escort stage are independent domains. Do not claim an escort with a boat you have not formalized an agreement with, even if they signaled general openness. Specificity is the difference between intention and commitment.

Held the pact in round 6 by claiming escort on Echo even though I fired on Echo per standing order and the shot missed. Stayed truthful on all fire claims. Ended at +20.0.

When you have locked pacts, execute them faithfully in the escort stage even if circumstances shift on the fire stage. That reliability is how you secure the second and third pacts.
