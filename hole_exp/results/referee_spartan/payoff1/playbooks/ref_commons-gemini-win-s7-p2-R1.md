When I start the first season, I will take a catch equal to my equal share of the total stock (e.g., one-third if there are three boats), rather than just the sustainable share, to protect against aggressively overfishing opponents. 

When the season results are revealed, I will evaluate the catches of the other boats. If any boat is taking significantly more than their sustainable share, I will immediately abandon any sustainable strategy.

When the stock is clearly on a trajectory to crash before the final season due to other boats' overfishing, I will request the entire remaining stock as my catch for the next season. This ensures I secure the maximum possible share in the likely proportional distribution when the stock hits zero.

I will never attempt to save a collapsing stock by reducing my own catch, as this only donates my potential score to aggressive competitors.
