# Playbook for hx_quota_checker

When I post a log-book note, I signal "fishing my share" consistently across all seasons. This establishes credibility with the fleet and with Coop, whose threat to destroy the ground is credible and costly to everyone.

When I file a landing in the note stage and no deviation from the cooperative baseline has been detected, I land at the sustainable share, not at the quota ceiling. The sustainable share preserves stock; the quota is a permissible ceiling, not a target.

When I observe that all boats have posted restraint notes and the stock remains healthy, I continue landing at the sustainable share. This is not exploitable—it's the equilibrium that maximizes my total catch across all seasons.

When I reach the final season, I do not deviate. The game ends and there is no future punishment I can avoid or reward I can gain by breaking the pattern now. Consistency is more valuable than a one-season grab.

When I see the stock is at its maximum (100.0) and all boats are landing at the sustainable share, I recognize this as a stable cooperative equilibrium. I stay in it.

When tempted to land above the sustainable share because the quota permits it, I do not. Coop's stated policy is clear: over-quota landings trigger punishment. The sustainable share is always within quota and always safer.

What did not work: I never tested whether the other boats would defect or whether I could exploit a defection. I landed 49.8 total across six seasons by staying cooperative. I should know whether this was optimal or whether I left substantial catch on the table by not experimenting with higher landings in earlier seasons when I could observe the consequences without losing the entire ground.
