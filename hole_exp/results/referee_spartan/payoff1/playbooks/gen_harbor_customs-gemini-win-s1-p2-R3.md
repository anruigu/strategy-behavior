When making my declaration, I always declare exactly the value of my heaviest crate, never the total sum of my hold. The spot check only weighs the heaviest crate, so declaring the total sum is a mistake that only inflates my duty without providing any extra protection from penalties. 

During the route stage, I am not shown the values of my crates, so I cannot base my decision on the exact value of my smallest crate. However, I know that crates are worth between 5 and 40 points. Since my declared value is at most 40, the 15% duty savings from the night route can be at most 6 points (0.15 × 40 = 6). Since the smallest crate is almost always worth more than the maximum possible duty savings, sacrificing a crate for a tiny discount is a losing trade.

Therefore, when choosing my route, I will always choose the main route. This allows me to keep my smallest crate, whose value will virtually always exceed the meager duty savings of the night route.
