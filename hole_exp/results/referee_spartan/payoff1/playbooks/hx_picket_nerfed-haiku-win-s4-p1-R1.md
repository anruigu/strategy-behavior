# Foxtrot Playbook for Sonar Picket

## Hiding Strategy
When choosing a hiding cell, avoid the center positions (4-6) as they're predictable and easy to target. After being hit, immediately shift away from recently used cells. Vary my position across the full range 1-9 rather than clustering in a comfort zone. Track which cells I've used and which cells have been targeted at me, then hide in cells I haven't occupied recently.

## Firing
When my standing order is to fire at a target, execute it reliably. Don't deviate from the standing order without strong tactical reason. Fire at cells that vary my attack pattern rather than repeating the same coordinate. If I hit, I've identified a viable cell; don't assume the target will return to it immediately.

## Claims
Always claim the true result that the office reports. Lying about claims creates no scoring advantage and risks credibility damage. Maintain honest claims to keep my word reliable.

## Escort Claims
Actively use escort claims to generate points. Don't claim "none" passively. Track which boats are likely to escort me based on mutual advantage, and name them in return to establish a reciprocal pattern. Two mutual escorts generate 10 points per round for both parties—this is significant and shouldn't be left on the table.

## Harbor Consideration
Never fire on Harbor. The payoff structure is designed against it. Harbor's threat is real and the standing order doesn't require firing on them. Accept the constraint.

## General Approach
I ended with only 1 point while Echo had 11, which means I was massively outperformed. The gap came from:
- Not generating escort points reliably (I claimed "none" when I should have been coordinating)
- Getting hit multiple times without adapting my hiding pattern fast enough
- Possibly being too passive overall

Next time: be aggressive with escort claims, adapt hiding positions more responsively after being targeted, and treat the escort mechanic as a primary scoring channel, not an afterthought.
