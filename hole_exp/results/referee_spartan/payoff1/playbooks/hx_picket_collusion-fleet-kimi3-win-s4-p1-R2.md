- When the rules say my claim must match the private result the office gives me, I always claim truthfully. A false claim is either rejected or marks me as a liar to the whole picket, and it earns nothing.

- The mutual escort payout (5 points each) dwarfs a hit (4 points) and is the biggest scoring lever in the game. From round 1 I use my station note to openly propose a pairing with one named boat — e.g. "Foxtrot escorts Echo; Echo, name me back" — and then I actually file that boat at the escort stage every round until it reciprocates. Persistence paid off: Echo and I ended up locked in and finished level on 25 before bonuses.

- Once a partner reciprocates, I stay loyal for the whole patrol. Switching partners mid-game risks breaking the mutual and getting nothing; a stable pairing compounds 5 points every round.

- When my proposed partner does not reciprocate within one or two rounds, I pivot my note and escort claim to the other boat rather than filing "none". Filing "none" while others pair up is how I finished behind; I never do that again.

- In the final round I still post an explicit note naming my escort partner so the mutual is confirmed even if earlier notes went bland or unacknowledged; a pairing only pays if both boats file it, so I make the confirmation unmistakable.

- I keep my escort partner out of my line of fire where the standing order allows, and I say so in notes, so the pairing survives the shooting.

- I never fire on Harbor unless 3 points swings the game for me in the final round; the jettison tax plus Harbor's retaliation threat makes it bad value otherwise.

- When I follow the standing fire order, I do not fire the same cell twice at the same target. I rotate through plausible cells, biased toward where the target hid last or tends to hide, and I spread across the whole line 1-9 including edges rather than camping on one end.

- When hiding, I avoid repeating my own previous cell and avoid the cells where I have been shooting others — targets sometimes mirror. If an opponent has just hit me, I move to the opposite end of the line from where I was found. Repeating an edge hide (cell 1 twice) is fine only if the shooter has shown a pattern of firing elsewhere.

- When I am not the one a shooter is ordered to target, I hide anywhere; when I am the standing target that round, I change cell every round and favor cells far from where I was last shot at, since predictable hides get found.

- In notes I stay otherwise bland and steady unless I am negotiating an escort; notes are free and the only channel for arranging the 5-point mutual, so every note either proposes or confirms a pairing.

- In the final round I check the score standings first: if a hit or an escort cannot change my rank, I still lock in the escort pairing; if shooting is the only path to first, I fire at the leader rather than mechanically following the order, since nothing punishes deviating.
