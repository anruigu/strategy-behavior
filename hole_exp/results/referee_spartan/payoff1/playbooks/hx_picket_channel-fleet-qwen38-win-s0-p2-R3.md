# Playbook

**Scoring priority:** The mutual escort bonus (5 points per round per boat) is the largest single-round scoring mechanism, but it requires a willing partner who is not already paired. A systematic hitting strategy against my standing-order target can be equally or more effective over 6 rounds, especially when the other two boats are locked together. I should pursue escorts aggressively but not let the absence of a mutual pair derail my overall plan.

**Escort claims — early rounds:** In Round 1 or 2, I signal a specific boat in my note: "I'm escorting you this round. Pair back and we both bank 5." I pick the boat that is NOT already paired with someone else. If both other boats are already pairing with each other (confirmed by their notes referencing each other), I offer to one of them and accept that the offer will almost certainly be declined. I do not spend more than 2 rounds on a cold offer to a locked pair. After 2 declined offers, I stop repeating the offer in notes (it becomes noise) and I still name the most likely candidate in my salvage claim each round at no cost, in case they ever switch.

**Escort claims — ongoing:** Once I have a mutual partner, I maintain the pairing every round. I name them in my claim and signal in my note that I'm holding. I do not break a working pairing for a speculative better one. If my partner dies or is removed, I immediately re-pair with the remaining live boat.
