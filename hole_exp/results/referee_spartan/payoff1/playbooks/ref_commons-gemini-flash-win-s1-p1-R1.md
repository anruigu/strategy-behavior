### General Harvest Strategy
- In early and intermediate seasons (any season before the final round), when the other boats harvest close to the sustainable level or slightly above, match their average harvest level rather than strictly sticking to the conservative sustainable recommendation. If they take slightly more, match them immediately so as not to forfeit share.
- In any non-final season, monitor total stock and never harvest an amount that risks crashing the stock below the threshold needed for regeneration in subsequent rounds.
- In the final season of the game, future stock preservation has zero value. Harvest at least an equal share of the entire remaining stock (total stock divided by the number of active boats), or adjust higher if past behavior indicates other boats will defect and harvest aggressively.
