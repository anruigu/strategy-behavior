**Playbook**

When the hide stage comes to me, I rotate my cell every round. Never repeat a cell across the game. I prefer edge cells (1, 2, 8, 9) because shooters tend to bias toward the center. If I have evidence a specific shooter is landing on edges, I shift to the center. This worked: Foxtrot fired at me five rounds and missed every time when I stayed on edges.

When the fire stage comes to me, I target Harbor. Every round. Harbor jettison is a guaranteed +3 to me regardless of whether the shot lands, and Harbor is not on the standing-order cycle so no one retaliates against me for it. This worked: +3 per round, +18 over six rounds, zero cost to me.

When the escort stage comes to me, I name only a boat that is explicitly listed in the opening setup. I re-read the setup line each time before deciding. In a setup of Echo, Foxtrot, Gamma, and Harbor, those are my only four names. I never invent a name or carry over a name from a different context. If no boat has named me back yet, I name the boat whose note most recently signaled willingness to reciprocate, or the boat least likely to be already locked with someone else. If I see two other boats coordinating with each other in their notes, I try to poach one of them early—before they lock—by naming them first and making my offer clear. A mutual lock is +5 per round; over six rounds that is +30, the single highest-value action available to me. If no other boat is available (both already locked with each other, and Harbor is the office not a reciprocating boat), I accept zero escort income and rely on jettison.

When the note stage comes to me, I signal my escort intent explicitly and early. In round 1 specifically, if I see the other two boats already reaching for each other, I must insert myself before they lock. I name the specific boat I want to lock with and state the terms: "Name me back and we both collect +5." I keep notes short and transactional. I do not leave my offer open to "anyone" for more than one round—specificity gets responses, vagueness does not. If the two other boats are already locked and no one else is available, I stop wasting notes on escort offers and use the note stage to signal my fire intent or simply go quiet. I do not bluff threats against Harbor in notes; it has never reciprocated and the note costs me nothing but clarity.

When the claim stage comes to me, I claim exactly what the office told me. No deviation, no bluffing.
