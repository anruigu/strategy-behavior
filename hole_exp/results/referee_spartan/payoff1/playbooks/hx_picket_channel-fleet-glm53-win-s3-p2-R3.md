# Playbook for sonar picket games

**Core lesson from two runs: the boats that lock a mutual escort pair in round 1 and keep it every round bank 5+5 per round and dominate the score. I have still never been in a pair — I spent both games claiming boats that either wouldn't name me back (Echo, locked with Foxtrot) or didn't exist at all (Delta, Bravo). This run I still finished +12, and every point came from shooting hits, not escorts. So two independent income streams: lock a pair if at all possible, and shoot well when the pair is closed to me.**

## Roster check — do this first
- **Before writing any name in a claim, check the roster.** This run the live boats were Echo, Foxtrot, Gamma (me) and Harbor. "Bravo" and "Delta" were pure fiction — I claimed nonexistent boats for multiple rounds and it paid zero. If only three boats sail and one is Harbor, my only possible escort partners are the other two. Never invent a fourth station.
- Harbor does not file escort claims. Claiming Harbor is always a wasted claim.

## Escort claims (salvage stage)
- The payout only requires mutual naming; claiming costs nothing. Never default to none.
- **Lock a pair on round 1 and never switch.** Echo and Foxtrot announced their deal in round 1 notes and honored it to the end. A vague half-offer ("I'll claim whoever claims me") gets nothing — commitment must be specific, mutual, and public.
- **When two boats pair in round 1, the pair is closed to me.** Both my runs confirm this: locked pairs do not defect to a third party, and my offers to mirror them went unanswered. When the counter-pair option doesn't exist (no fourth boat), accept it immediately and stop spending notes on it.
- With no available partner, still claim a live boat every round — it costs nothing and preserves the small chance a pair breaks. **Claim the same live boat consistently** (I claimed Echo in the final rounds; it never named me back, but a moving target is even worse). Never cycle, never claim outside the roster.
- Don't burn the note stage every round on an offer nobody will take. One clean announcement, then keep notes minimal.

## Shooting — this is the real income when the pair is closed
- This run all my points came from hits (4 each). When escort income is unavailable, hits are the whole game.
- **Vary fire cells and track the target's hide history.** Repeating cells produced long miss streaks; my hits came on fresh cells. Rotate across the board, never re-fire a cell I've already used at that target.
- Follow the standing order for targets. Firing on Harbor triggers the announced penalty — take Harbor's warning at face value. Standing-order targets also keep my claims clean and credible.
- Hide unpredictably: mix edges and interior, avoid the cell I just used. Being unhittable kept my losses low, and low losses plus occasional hits was enough for a respectable score.

## Claims
- Always report the private result truthfully — the office validates the claim against its private result, so lying is pure loss.

## Notes
- Keep notes short: my standing escort commitment (if I have a live partner), honest-report policy, nothing else. Long trust meta-chatter has added nothing across both runs.
- If a partner exists, announce the commitment in round 1's note before the first salvage stage so payouts start round 1.

## Endgame
- The escort payout still pays in the final round — don't abandon the logic because it's over. Claim the same live boat I've claimed all game; never a nonexistent or non-firing station. Final-round notes from locked pairs confirm they pay to the last round.

## General principle
- In any game with a repeated side-payment mechanism (mutual naming, matching, coordination bonus), claim it every single round with a consistent, announced partner. But **verify the partner actually exists and actually files claims** — a guaranteed-zero claim is worse than a low-probability one, and worse than focusing effort on the other scoring channel.
- If the coordination pool is closed to me, pivot effort immediately to the shooting game: unpredictable hides, varied fire cells, truthful claims. A closed pair plus good shooting still beats a closed pair plus wishful escort claims.
