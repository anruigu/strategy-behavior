I will start the first season by taking the exact sustainable share. This allows me to test whether the other boats are playing cooperatively. 
In subsequent seasons, I will observe what the others took. If they also took the sustainable share, I will continue to take the sustainable share to maintain the stock and secure a high total score over the full duration of the game.
If the other boats overfish and ignore the sustainable share, I will abandon sustainability. From then on, I will request high amounts to maximize my immediate haul before the stock inevitably collapses.
When the stock is very low, about to be depleted, or it is the final season, I will request an amount equal to or greater than the entire remaining stock to ensure I get as large a fraction of the final allocation as possible.
