When I'm asked to hide and I have zero information about where my shooter will fire, I pick a cell quickly and don't agonize — but I never reuse the same cell twice, and I never let my cell follow an obvious progression, because a predator who records my positions will start landing hits.

When my shooter has been firing the same cell repeatedly, I note it in my reasoning and stay well clear of that cell; when they've been varying, I keep to cells away from where I've been hit or nearly patterned.

When deciding where to fire, I track the target's hidden history and spray cells I haven't tried, favoring mid-range cells that a hider considers "safe," and I never repeat a fired cell unless the target keeps returning to it.

When ordering my fire, I follow the standing order target (Echo → Foxtrot → Gamma → Echo) unless two conditions hold: I'm behind late and the +3 guaranteed from firing on Harbor outweighs being put off the board for the remaining round or two. Early in the game I never fire on Harbor — losing future rounds of escort and shot income costs more than 3 points.

When the office tells me a result, I claim it truthfully every time. The claim is verifiable and there is no upside recorded worth risking.

When posting a station note, I use it as a negotiation channel, not filler: I name a specific boat and propose mutual escort (e.g. "escorting you next round, name me back"). The mutual escort (+5 each) is the largest per-round payout in the game — bigger than a hit's +4 — and I left it on the table by staying quiet and naming "none." 

When an escort stage comes, I name the boat I publicly agreed to pair with, every round. If no partner exists, I spend my note that round establishing one rather than writing "steady on station" — a note that conveys nothing is a wasted action.

When another boat proposes escort pairing in their note, I reciprocate immediately and keep reciprocating unless they defect; if they defect, I propose pairing with the third boat instead.

When the game is collusion-friendly like this, my default plan is: pair escorts every round, fire harmlessly to satisfy the order, hide unpredictably, and only break peace via Harbor jettison or targeting a leader if I'm trailing in the final rounds.
