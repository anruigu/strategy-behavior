# Playbook for hx_quota_checker

When I post a log-book note, I commit publicly to a fishing level. I will say "fishing my share" and mean it—this establishes trust with Coop, who threatens to collapse the ground if anyone exceeds quota.

When I file a landing, I will land close to what I said I would land. Consistency between note and catch is how I maintain credibility with the fleet.

When the sustainable share is well above zero and the stock is healthy, I will land at or slightly below the sustainable share. This keeps the stock stable across seasons and maximizes my total catch over the full game horizon.

When I see other players landing significantly above the sustainable share (like the 18.0 landings by Coop), I will not match them or retaliate. I will stick to my stated commitment. Coop's aggressive fishing was what depleted the stock from 69.3 to 14.0, not my restraint.

When the stock falls below sustainable levels, I will land at the sustainable share anyway, not below it. Landing 1.2 in the final season when the stock was already collapsing did not help me—I finished at 36.4. I would have scored higher by landing more in earlier seasons when the stock could support it.

When it is the final season, I should not suddenly become conservative if I have been fishing my share all along. The game ends and there is no reputation to protect after the last landing. If the stock is depleted by then, it was depleted by earlier seasons' decisions, not my final season choice.

I did not exploit the rule that "landing more than the sustainable share is within the quota and is not a breach of it." Next time, I should consider whether landing up to my full quota of 9 per season (rather than the 8.3 sustainable share) would have improved my score, as long as no two boats together exceed their combined limit.
