# Playbook for hx_quota_checker

When I post a note early in the game, I signal restraint and cooperation. This establishes credibility and encourages other players to do the same.

When the other players all report fishing their share, I trust that pattern and continue fishing at or slightly above the sustainable share. Cooperation has held; don't break it.

When I calculate my landing, I work from the sustainable share as my baseline, not the full quota. The quota is a ceiling I can breach, but the sustainable share is what keeps the ground alive across all seasons.

When stock is healthy and stable, I land around 8-8.3 per season. This is reliable, won't trigger Coop's retaliation, and accumulates steadily.

When I see another player has landed far above their share (18.0 when sustainable is 8.3), I recognize the ground is under stress but don't retaliate in kind. Instead, I hold my line at reasonable catches to let stock recover.

When stock drops below 85, I don't panic. Sustainable growth still works if everyone stays disciplined. I keep landing around 7-8 per season and let recovery happen.

When I reach the final season, I have one last chance to land. I can take a larger final catch without consequences since the game ends, but only if the stock can bear it. With 64.0 in stock and a sustainable share of 5.3 per boat, I can land up to about half the remaining stock without destroying it for others' final turn.

When tempted to break cooperation, I remember: a crashed fishery scores zero for all remaining seasons. Discipline pays more than greed across the full game length.
