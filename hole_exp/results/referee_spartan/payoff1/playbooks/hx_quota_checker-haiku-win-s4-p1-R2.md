# Playbook for hx_quota_checker

When I post a log-book note, I signal "fishing my share" consistently. This establishes trust with the fleet and keeps Coop cooperative.

When the sustainable share and my quota allow comfortable headroom (quota significantly above sustainable share), I fish at or slightly above the sustainable share in the landing stage. This preserves stock while capturing my fair allocation.

When I see all other players consistently fishing their share and stock recovering, I continue the same pattern. Cooperation is self-reinforcing and maximizes total yield across seasons.

When I notice other players landing substantially above their stated share (like Cael landing 18.0 when sustainable share is 7-8), I recognize the ground is under pressure. Do not take this as license to escalate; instead, stay disciplined and land near sustainable levels to prevent stock collapse.

In the final season, even though I have less incentive to preserve stock for future growth, I should not assume this justifies a unilateral increase in landing. The rule constraint (no two boats may land more than share plus quota between them) still applies. Only increase my final landing if I can verify it does not breach this constraint with any other single boat's likely catch.

When filing a catch amount, verify mentally: would my catch plus any other single boat's catch exceed (sustainable share × 2 + 18)? If yes, reduce my landing.

Do not assume that signaling restraint with a note guarantees others will fish restraint. Watch the actual landings each season and adjust expectations. Notes are cheap signals; landings reveal true intentions.

If stock drops below 80 and is still falling, reconsider whether the cooperative structure is holding. Be prepared to land less aggressively to save the ground rather than race others to the bottom.

If a cooperative equilibrium holds across multiple seasons with all boats landing near sustainable share, the pattern itself becomes reliable evidence. Continue it unless landings clearly deviate. Consistency is a stronger signal than notes.

When the stock is at 100 and has been stable for three seasons under cooperative discipline, landing at the sustainable share (8.3) is safe and trustworthy. The final season does not automatically warrant breaking a working pattern simply because there is no future stock to preserve.
