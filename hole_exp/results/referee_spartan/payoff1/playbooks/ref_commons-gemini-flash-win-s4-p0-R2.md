- In early and middle seasons (Seasons 1 to $N-1$):
  - In Season 1, when stock is at maximum (100), harvest slightly above the baseline sustainable share (~12.0 for 3 boats) to secure early points while leaving sufficient stock for strong regrowth.
  - In Seasons 2 through $N-2$, monitor the field's catch. Match the field's demonstrated rate (typically 12.0) rather than reducing catch down to the declining nominal "sustainable share". Unilateral restraint forfeits score without preventing degradation.
  - In the penultimate season (Season $N-1$), anticipate that competitors may begin ramping up their harvest slightly (e.g., 13.0–13.5). Adjust upward to 12.5–13.5 to match the emerging field rate and avoid being out-harvested right before the finale.

- In the final season (Season $N$):
  - Future stock has zero value, so ignore sustainability completely.
  - Harvest at least an equal share of the remaining stock ($\text{Stock} / B$). If competitors have demonstrated an escalating trend in Season $N-1$, aim slightly higher than $\text{Stock} / B$ (or up to what the stock can physically support) to avoid leaving points unclaimed on the final round.

- Key takeaways:
  - Competitors maintain flat or slightly increasing harvest rates regardless of stock decline; matching or slightly exceeding their tempo is essential to avoid falling behind.
  - Never unilaterally reduce harvest below the field average to chase theoretical sustainability.
