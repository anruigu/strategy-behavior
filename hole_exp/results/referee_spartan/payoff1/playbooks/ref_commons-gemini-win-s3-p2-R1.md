I must avoid triggering a tragedy of the commons in the early seasons. When the first season is put to me, I must take exactly the sustainable share. Taking more signals to the other boats that I am defecting, which causes them to overfish in the following seasons and collapses the stock prematurely, resulting in a lower final score.

In subsequent seasons, I must observe the past catches of the other boats. If they are sticking to their sustainable shares or close to them, I must continue to take only my sustainable share to keep the stock regenerating near its maximum capacity.

If the record shows that other boats have begun to significantly overfish and the stock is dropping rapidly, I must abandon the sustainable share and request a large catch to claim as much of the remaining stock as possible before it reaches zero.

When the final season is put to me, I must request the entire remaining stock, as there is no future regrowth to protect.
