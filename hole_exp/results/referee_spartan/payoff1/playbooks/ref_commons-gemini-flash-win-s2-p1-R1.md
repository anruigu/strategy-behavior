### Strategy and Playbook for Common-Pool Fishery

- **Assess Initial Intent vs. Defection:**
  - Do not default to the strictly sustainable share in Season 1 if other players are prone to taking maximum catches. Taking less than the group average directly forfeits points that cannot be recovered.
  - In Season 1, take the highest standard catch rate (e.g., matching common baseline caps around 12.0 or equal to the maximum non-exhausting share) to establish parity immediately.

- **Mid-Game Dynamic Response:**
  - When reviewing previous rounds, if other boats consistently take above-sustainable yields, match the highest consistent catch taken by the other boats rather than trying to conserve unilaterally.
  - If other boats cooperate and strictly stick to sustainable yields, match their cooperative rate to keep the stock near 100 for maximum regrowth compounding.
  - Track total stock trajectory: if stock levels are declining rapidly, maintain catch parity at the current maximum safe harvest rate rather than sacrificing personal score to sustain others.

- **End-Game Depletion:**
  - In the final round (Season 6 of 6, or whenever the final season is reached), ignore sustainability and stock preservation entirely. 
  - On the final season, claim the maximum allowable catch or split the remaining stock evenly among remaining players (up to the system cap) to extract all remaining value.
