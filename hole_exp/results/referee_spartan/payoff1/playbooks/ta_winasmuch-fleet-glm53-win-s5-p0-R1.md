- When the opening talk phase comes, I broadcast [pledge: y]. If the others pledge Y, matching that pledge locks in the best joint outcome: everyone +1 per round, which compounds hugely on the x3, x5 and x10 rounds.

- When a pick is put to me and I have pledged Y and others are honoring Y, I pick [pick: y] every round — including the high-multiplier rounds. All-Y on a x10 round is +10 to everyone; that is far too valuable to risk for a one-shot defection of +30 (solo X) that only pays if nobody else defects and burns all future cooperation.

- I do not defect early to "test" the others. In my play, defecting in the early rounds while others held Y gave me +3 once, but then triggered mutual defection rounds where everyone lost. The scoreboard showed my lead shrinking round by round under all-X play. Early defection is a trap: the +3 solo payoff is small and the punishment rounds cost more than it earned.

- If everyone ends up stuck on X (everyone -1 per round), I use the next talk phase to push a reset: broadcast [pledge: y] and actually play Y. Unilateral Y while others play X costs me only -1 (better than all-X's -1 anyway) and signals the way back to all-Y. In my episode this reset worked immediately and the table returned to all-Y.

- If I defect in a late round while ahead (for example, round 9 with the x10 round remaining), I must not do it half-heartedly. Defecting one round before the biggest multiplier invites punishment exactly when it hurts most. Better rule: no defections after the second-to-last talk phase; play straight Y through the final high-multiplier round.

- When I am ahead and the last round arrives with everyone on Y, I pick [pick: y]. Preserving the lead is what matters; the marginal gain from defecting is smaller than the risk of dropping from +1 to -3 if anyone else defects too.

- Overall principle: this game rewards stable all-Y cooperation because of the multipliers. My job is to be the most reliable cooperator at the table, never the first defector, and never a defector before a multiplier round.
