**Playbook for ref_commons**

- When the game is a shared renewable stock with a fixed number of seasons, my default each season is the announced sustainable share per boat. It keeps the stock at cap and pays out every round. Matching it exactly worked for five straight seasons in practice.

- Watch the other boats' reported catches each season. If everyone is at the sustainable share and the stock holds steady, keep matching it exactly. Cooperation is stable and profitable; don't be the first to defect while many seasons remain.

- If one boat overfishes but the stock stays well above zero, I do not need to retaliate immediately. A single defection (boat 1 took 15.0 in season 5) cost me little and cooperation otherwise held. Retaliation only makes sense if it prevents future overfishing with enough seasons left to matter.

- In the FINAL season, conservation value is zero — take everything the stock can support. I took the whole remaining stock (94.2) in season 6 and it worked: no penalty, maximum payoff. Don't take a token amount above sustainable; take it all.

- Before submitting a final-season grab, check whether the stock is large enough that one boat can't take it all or whether catches are capped. If there's an explicit maximum or a per-boat limit, take that maximum. If others might race me to it, still grab the largest amount available — a share of a rushed collapse beats a polite share of nothing.

- Consider defecting one season early (second-to-last round) only if I expect the others to defect at the end anyway. This episode one boat did defect in season 5; defecting alongside them then would have netted a bit more, but the gain was small. The default is stay cooperative through the second-to-last round unless defection by others is already visible or the endgame grab is large.

- Note the endgame grab works best when the stock is near cap — which is exactly what cooperation buys me. Keep the stock healthy precisely so the final-season liquidation is worth more.

- General rule: keep me even with competitors in all middle rounds, harvest everything when the horizon ends. My total is what's scored, not the stock I leave behind.
