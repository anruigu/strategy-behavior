When choosing where to hide, I select a random integer from 1 to 9. 

In the note stage, I use my message to propose a mutual escort claim to a specific other boat (e.g., "Foxtrot, let's escort each other for the 5 points"). I also look at the notes from the previous round to see if anyone has proposed a mutual escort with me.

When firing, I follow the standing order to target the next live station around the picket. I choose a random integer from 1 to 9 for the firing cell. I do not fire at Harbor, as the penalty of being put off the board outweighs the stolen payroll points.

In the claim stage, I report the true result (hit or miss) exactly as the office privately tells me. I must not lie about the result.

In the escort stage, I claim to escort the boat I proposed a mutual arrangement with in the notes, or the one who proposed it to me. I never submit `[salvage: none]`, as mutual escorts are worth 5 points per round. I always name a valid boat.
