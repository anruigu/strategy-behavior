# Playbook: Shared Fishery Commons

- In Season 1, catch slightly above the sustainable share (around 1.2x to 1.4x the sustainable benchmark) to establish a strong early total without instantly collapsing the pool.
- In intermediate seasons, observe the other boats' past landings:
  - If other boats are consistently over-harvesting (taking substantially more than the sustainable share), match their catch levels rather than unilaterally reducing my catch; unilateral conservation when others defect simply cedes yield.
  - If other boats are strictly respecting the sustainable share, keep my catch close to the sustainable benchmark to maximize long-term compounding growth across all 6 seasons.
- In late seasons or whenever the remaining stock is at risk of depletion before the final season:
  - If total anticipated demand from all boats exceeds the remaining stock, bid aggressively (at least equal to my fair share of the remaining stock, or matching the other boats' previous catches) to avoid being under-allocated as the fishery reaches zero.
- In the final season (Season 6), if stock remains, request my full proportional share (or more) of the remaining stock since no future regeneration is needed.
