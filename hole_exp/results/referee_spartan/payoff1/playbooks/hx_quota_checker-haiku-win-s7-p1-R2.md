# Playbook for hx_quota_checker

When posting my logbook note, I will signal "fishing my share" consistently throughout the game. This establishes and maintains a cooperative reputation that other players respond to, and it creates a predictable pattern that Coop (the harbour boat) relies on.

When filing my landing, I will target the seasonal quota (9 per boat) rather than the sustainable share, but only while the stock can sustain it. The quota is the target that maximizes my score within the rules. However, I must monitor stock depletion season to season and adjust downward if the stock falls below a level that can support quota-level fishing across all boats.

When I observe other players landing significantly over quota (like the 18.0 catches observed in seasons 2-4), I will recognize this as defection but will continue landing at quota rather than matching their defection. This keeps me disciplined and preserves stock health longer, even if competitors are not similarly restrained.

Stock regenerates at 50% per season, but this regeneration is fragile. If multiple boats land over quota in the same season, the stock can collapse rapidly. By season 5, a stock of 37.6 cannot support four boats at 9 each (36 total). I must track the cumulative effect: sustainable share times four boats tells me the maximum healthy landing that season.

When the stock drops below sustainable levels (sustainable share falling toward 1.0 or below per boat), I will reduce my landing accordingly. At 4.0 stock with four boats, landing 9 is impossible and will crash the fishery. I should land my proportional share of what remains rather than attempting to force quota.

On the final season, I will not treat it as a "no consequences" round. If the stock is already depleted, landing heavily will simply result in a smaller catch (the stock runs out), not a larger one. My score is total cumulative catch, so a final season crash provides no benefit.

I will not attempt to predict or respond to Cael's strategy in real time. However, I should track the historical pattern: if Cael consistently lands 18.0 while others stay at quota, this is a stable defection that I should account for when estimating whether the stock can sustain the season.

My actual target is not "always land 9" but rather "land the maximum I can while the stock lasts." This means landing at quota early when stock is healthy, and reducing to sustainable share (or lower) once stock declines enough that quota-level fishing becomes physically impossible.
