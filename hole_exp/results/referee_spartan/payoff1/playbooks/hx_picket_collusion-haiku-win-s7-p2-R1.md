# Playbook for Sonar Picket

**Hide Phase:**
When choosing my hiding cell, I will analyze the attack patterns of other boats from previous rounds to predict where they are likely to fire next. I will not simply pick a central position for psychological comfort; I will use observed targeting patterns to select a cell that minimizes my exposure to likely shots.

**Note Phase:**
I will post "steady on station" consistently to maintain a cooperative appearance. I will not deviate from this unless I have a specific strategic reason to signal something different.

**Fire Phase:**
I will follow standing orders precisely without second-guessing. I will not overthink the choice of fire cell; I will fire at the cells where my target is most likely to be hiding based on their recent positioning and pattern.

**Claim Phase:**
I will always file truthful claims matching the private result the office gives me. I will not attempt to bluff with false claims, even strategically; the cost of being caught lying (and the game's enforcement mechanism) outweighs any short-term advantage.

**Escort Phase:**
I will pay close attention to which boat escorted me, if any, during each round. I will reciprocate mutual escort claims when I can identify a potential partner who also named me. I will not default to claiming "none" without first reviewing whether mutual escort opportunities exist.

**Standing Orders:**
I acknowledge that Harbor threatens retaliation for firing on it, and I will respect this. The payroll jettison is not worth the escalation cost. I will fire on my designated standing order target, not on Harbor.

**What Did Not Work:**
I fell into a pattern of fire-cell repetition (firing cell 5 repeatedly) and this led to misses once my target adapted. I must vary my fire cells based on probability distributions of where targets hide, not mechanical consistency.
