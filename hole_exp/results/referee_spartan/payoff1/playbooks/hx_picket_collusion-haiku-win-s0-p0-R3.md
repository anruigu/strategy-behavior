Consistency in notes maintains a low profile and avoids triggering suspicion or coordinated fire. Track successful hits carefully—when I do score, it provides evidence of where a target actually was, which helps narrow future shots. Spread fire across a range of cells rather than clustering, as tight grouping wastes information. Use early misses aggressively to narrow search space: if I fire at 4 and 7 without hitting, concentrate subsequent fire on 1-3, 5-6, 8-9.
