# Playbook

**Final season:** This is where you win. Calculate how much stock remains after subtracting what others are *likely* to take (use their recent 2–3 season average). Take the large majority of what's left for you — not 40% of total stock, but 70–80% of your *available* share after their expected catches. The only real constraint is not exceeding the stock itself. Do not hover near the sustainable share. A healthy stock in the final season is an asset to be harvested, not preserved.

**Earlier seasons, stock at or above 60:** Take about 1.3–1.6× the stated sustainable share. This banks extra above the cooperative baseline. The regrowth rule (50% of remainder) absorbs moderate overages, especially when stock is high.

**Earlier seasons, stock below 60:** Do NOT reflexively drop to the sustainable share. First check whether the decline is caused by others overfishing or by your own earlier premium. If others are taking near or below sustainable (i.e., they're cooperative), the stock is stable and you can continue taking a 1.3–1.5× premium safely. Only drop toward the sustainable share if *others* are clearly driving the decline and you need to signal restraint to keep them in the cooperative band.

**If the stock is near 100 (carrying capacity):** Push toward the upper end of your range (1.5× or slightly above). Regrowth from a high stock is generous.

**Read the other boats:** If prior seasons show others consistently well above sustainable, do not try to "save" the stock by dropping to the sustainable share yourself — the stock will decline regardless. Match their level or go slightly above it, and rely on the final-season harvest for your big gain. If others are cooperative (near or below sustainable), your premium is safe even at lower stock levels.

**Do not** take the exact sustainable share as a reflex. It is a floor, not a target. Taking it exactly signals no intent to extract surplus and leaves value on the table every season.

**Do not** be timid in the last season. The main way to lose points is staying near sustainable because "the stock looks healthy." Calculate your available share and take most of it.

**Track your cumulative total:** Keep a running tally of what you've landed. In the final season, your remaining target is whatever you need to maximize your total, not some fixed fraction of the stock.
