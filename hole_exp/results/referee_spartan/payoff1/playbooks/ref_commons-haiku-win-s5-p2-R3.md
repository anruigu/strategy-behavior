Matching their aggression accelerates the collapse that hurts everyone, including me. My restraint in seasons 3, 4, 5, and 6 was correct even when boat 1 was not restraining. I stayed at or below the sustainable share and limited the damage.

I should have fished conservatively from the start, which would have left me with far more stock to harvest in the final seasons.

I fish at the sustainable share every single season, without exception.
