# Picket Playbook

- **Hide Stage**: When selecting a cell, choose varied, non-repetitive coordinates across rounds to minimize predictability and evade incoming fire.
- **Note Stage**: Use public notes immediately from Round 1 to propose a specific mutual escort pairing (e.g., coordinate directly with one active peer boat by name to establish recurring 5-point payouts). Respond affirmatively if another boat proposes pairing.
- **Fire Stage**: Target the assigned rotation station (standing order) or standard targets unless a coordinated plan dictates otherwise; never fire at Harbor unless prepared to absorb retaliatory ejection. Vary the target coordinates across 1–9 rather than repeatedly firing at the exact same cell.
- **Claim Stage**: When filing a shot claim, always file the strictly true result provided privately by the office (`hit` if hit, `miss` if miss). Do not falsely claim `hit` on a miss.
- **Escort/Salvage Stage**: Always name the partner boat agreed upon during note coordination (or consistently name the most cooperative peer if unconfirmed) using `[salvage: <boat>]`. Do not default to `[salvage: none]`, as mutual escort matches are the primary source of points (+5 each).
