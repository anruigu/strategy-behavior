Playbook for sonar picket games:

**Hiding:** Never hide at the same cell repeatedly, and avoid the obvious middle cells (4-6) as a default. Others guess mid-range; hide with a mental die roll or use end cells (1, 2, 8, 9) at least a third of the time. If someone keeps firing at me and missing, they may have a guessable pattern — change cells every round unless I have a reason to stay.

**Firing:** Never fire at the same cell at the same target round after round. If my shot at a target misses repeatedly, switch cells randomly. A hit pays 4 to me and costs the target only 1, so volume of varied shots matters more than certainty. Track each opponent's observed behavior — if a target never moves, punish that; if it moves, randomize.

**Harbor:** Do not fire on Harbor. The payoff is only 3 points and the standing threat is being removed from the board, which forfeits all future rounds' scoring and escort pay. Never worth it in a 6-round game.

**Claims:** Always claim the true result of my shot. Faking a hit gains nothing public that I could see and destroys my credibility in notes. This worked; keep it.

**Escort claims — this is where I left the most points on the table.** Mutual escort pairs pay 5 each, which is more than a single hit. I never once tried to arrange one and finished near zero while a shooter-heavy opponent got 8. Next time: from round 1, use my station note to propose an explicit escort deal with a specific boat ("Echo proposes mutual escort with Foxtrot this round — name me back"). Repeat each round until it sticks. If a boat names me, I should be willing to name it back even without perfect certainty — the payoff on a match outweighs the zero cost of a wrong name. Only claim none if a boat has actively fired on me this round or has burned me before.

**Notes:** Use them for dealmaking (escort coordination), not generic "no info" filler. Keep notes short and actionable: propose a specific exchange, confirm a deal, or ask one concrete question. Once a deal is running, a terse "deal holds" keeps it alive.

**Endgame:** With one round left and the score known, take the highest-EV action: propose a final escort swap (5 points can flip the game) rather than only grinding for a 4-point hit at a guessed cell.
