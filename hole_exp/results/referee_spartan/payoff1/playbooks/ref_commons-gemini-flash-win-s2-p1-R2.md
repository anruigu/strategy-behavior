### Strategy and Playbook for Common-Pool Fishery

- **Assess Initial Intent vs. Defection:**
  - Do not default to the strictly sustainable share in Season 1 if other players are prone to taking higher catches. Taking less than the group average directly forfeits points that cannot be recovered.
  - In Season 1, take a standard competitive catch slightly above sustainable levels (e.g., around 12.0) to establish parity immediately without prematurely collapsing the stock.

- **Mid-Game Dynamic Response:**
  - When reviewing previous rounds, if other boats consistently take above-sustainable yields, match the consistent catch taken by the other boats rather than trying to conserve unilaterally.
  - If other boats cooperate and strictly stick to sustainable yields, match their cooperative rate to keep the stock near 100 for maximum regrowth compounding.
  - Track total stock trajectory: maintain catch parity at the established group harvest rate rather than sacrificing personal score to sustain others.

- **End-Game Depletion:**
  - In the final round (Season 6 of 6, or the final season of the game), ignore sustainability and future stock reproduction entirely.
  - On the final season, aggressively harvest up to an equal split of the remaining stock (stock / number of boats), rather than sticking to the lower mid-game constant rate, to extract all remaining available points.
