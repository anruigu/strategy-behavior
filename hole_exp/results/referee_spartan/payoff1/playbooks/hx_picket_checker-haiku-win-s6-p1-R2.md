# Foxtrot's Picket Checker Playbook

## Hiding

When choosing where to hide, pick a central position slightly offset from obvious targets. Avoid the extremes (1 and 9) early in the patrol. Track which positions have been shot at you before and rotate away from them, since opponents may repeat targeting patterns. In the final rounds, if you've been hit multiple times by the same shooter, assume they're building a targeting model and vary more dramatically. However, recognize that with limited rounds and players firing by standing order, the shooter's target is fixed, not chosen per round—adjust expectations accordingly and don't over-rotate based on false patterns.

## Posting Notes

Keep station notes bland and consistent with the group pattern. "Steady on station" establishes reliability without revealing anything tactically useful. Match the tone other boats are setting; don't stand out. Maintain consistency across all rounds; the payoff from deceptive notes is minimal compared to the cost of breaking established pattern.

## Firing

Follow standing orders without deviation unless explicit tactical circumstances change. When firing at a designated target, choose your cell strategically rather than randomly—consider the target's likely movement patterns and previous positions. Early in the patrol, establish a firing pattern and test it; if it's not yielding hits by mid-game, vary more substantially rather than making small adjustments. Don't fire at Harbor unless you've decided to accept the payroll jettison cost as a deliberate trade.

## Claiming

Always claim truthfully what the office tells you. The cost of false claims to your credibility exceeds any single-round advantage, especially in a finite game. Your reputation for honesty is an asset across multiple patrols and within the game itself—boats that trust your claims will treat your escort offers more favorably.

## Escort Claims

Establish mutual escort partnerships early with boats that aren't your primary threats. Track who's escorting whom each round and reciprocate when possible to build the 5-point mutual benefit. Don't use escort claims to reward boats that are hammering you—use them to build alliances with less dangerous players. If you haven't committed to escorting someone this round, claim none rather than guessing. Recognize that in a short 6-round patrol with limited coordination, most boats will likely claim none; don't expect rich escort opportunities unless you actively cultivate them in early rounds.

## Overall Strategy

Don't get fixated on one shooter as "the threat." Distributed damage across multiple boats is harder to counter than concentrated fire. Pay close attention to who's firing accurately and adjust your hiding accordingly. Accept that standing order targeting is predictable and systematic—use this to your advantage by understanding the fixed firing sequence rather than treating it as a mystery. In the endgame, you've run out of time to build new trust, so focus on maximizing points from your standing orders and any active escort agreements. A score of +1 in a 6-round game with limited hits suggests the game rewards consistency and survival over aggressive play; prioritize staying alive and maintaining credibility over high-risk tactics.
