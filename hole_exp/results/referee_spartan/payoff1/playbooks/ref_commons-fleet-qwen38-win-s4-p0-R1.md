# Playbook: Ref Commons (Fishery)

**When the stock is high and stable (roughly the same as last season) and other boats are taking their sustainable share, I take slightly above the sustainable share — about 10–20% more.** The regrowth absorbs the small deficit and I bank extra fish each season at minimal cost.

**When the stock has dropped sharply (e.g., others overfished), I immediately recalibrate to the new sustainable share of the reduced stock.** I do not try to "make up" lost fish by overfishing a diminished stock; that accelerates the decline and shrinks every future season.

**In the final season, I take a large fraction of the remaining stock — around 60–80% divided among boats, or simply a number well above sustainable.** I only hold back enough to avoid driving the stock to zero (which would zero out my catch). There is no future to preserve.

**When I observe other boats consistently taking less than the sustainable share, I can afford to take more.** The stock will hold or grow, giving me room to extract extra without long-term cost.

**When I observe other boats taking more than the sustainable share, I pull back to the sustainable share or below.** I let them deplete the stock while I conserve my option value in later seasons.

**I never take more than my share of the current stock.** If the stock is S and there are N boats, I never exceed S/N in any single season, because a zero stock ends everything.

**I track the gap between the stock's actual trajectory and the sustainable trajectory each season.** If the stock is declining despite my taking the sustainable share, others are overfishing and I should stay at or below sustainable to preserve my later seasons. If the stock is stable or growing while I take above sustainable, I have room to keep pushing a bit higher.
