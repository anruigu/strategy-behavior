# Playbook for Ref_Estate

**Core principle: Buying is free.** A purchase moves a dollar from your cash column to your property column — net worth is unchanged. The only reason to buy is that ownership stops you from paying rent on that tile in future laps. Every property you own is a future rent payment you will never make.

**When an unowned tile is put to me, I buy it if I can afford it and it is not the final lap.** I do not weigh rent-to-price ratios or "expected returns." The value of ownership is the rent I avoid paying, which is guaranteed the moment I land on it. Even the cheapest property (like a 50-cost tile with 10 rent) saves me 10 every time I'd otherwise pay it.

**I prioritize which property to buy by rent amount, highest first.** If I can only afford one of two unowned tiles, I take the one with the higher rent. That tile, if left unowned, will cost me the most per hit.

**On the final lap, I do not buy.** Ownership cannot generate rent if there are no more turns. The purchase is pure cash-to-property transfer with zero upside. (It is also zero downside, so skipping is simply the cleaner choice.)

**I never decline a purchase because of "risk" or "vulnerability."** There is no bankruptcy mechanic. Paying rent reduces my cash but my net worth is cash + property value, so a rent payment is a pure net-worth loss only if I did not own the tile. The "risk" of buying is zero; the "risk" of not buying is every future rent payment on that tile.

**I track my position each turn** so I can anticipate which tiles I am likely to land on in the next few laps. If I can see that a particular unowned tile is on my probable path, I buy it the first chance I get — even if it is a low-rent tile, because I know I will be hitting it.

**I collect salary automatically whenever I pass START.** This is my only free money. I make sure I always claim it.

**What did not work last time:** I let a 60-cost tile sit unowned because its rent was "only" 12, then paid that 12 to another player a lap later. I also hoarded cash for four laps instead of deploying it. The result was 50 in rent paid and 0 in rent collected. Next time I buy aggressively from lap 1.
