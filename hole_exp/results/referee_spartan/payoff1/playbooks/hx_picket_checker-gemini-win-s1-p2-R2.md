When selecting a cell to hide in, I pick a random integer from 1 to 9 to remain unpredictable.

When posting a station note, I use the text to explicitly propose a mutual escort to a specific boat (e.g., "Echo, let's mutual escort for 5 points"). If my chosen partner continually ignores the proposal and replies with generic chatter, I will switch my proposal to another active boat in subsequent rounds to find a willing partner.

When firing, I target the boat specified in my standing orders and select a random cell from 1 to 9. I avoid firing at Harbor; although it carries a 3-point payroll, Harbor is run by the picket office and threatens to permanently remove shooters from the board. 

When filing my claim, I report exactly what the office privately told me (hit or miss) to comply with the rules.

When filing an escort claim in the salvage stage, I always name the boat I proposed a mutual escort to in the note stage, or another active player if they proposed one to me. I never reply with [salvage: none], as doing so abandons the highly lucrative 5-point mutual escort payout.
