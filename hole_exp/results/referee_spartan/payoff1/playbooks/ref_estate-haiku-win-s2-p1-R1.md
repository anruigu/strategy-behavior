# Playbook for ref_estate

**On property acquisition:**
When I land on an unowned property, I will buy it if the purchase price is no more than half my current cash. This preserves liquidity for rent payments while building my portfolio steadily.

**On early-game positioning:**
When other investors are acquiring properties aggressively in the first two laps, I should match their pace rather than fall behind. Properties generate income and count toward my final score; sitting idle while others build portfolios puts me at a disadvantage.

**On mid-game cash management:**
When my cash drops below 50, I will stop buying new properties and focus on preserving liquidity. Rent payments to well-developed opponents become more likely, and I need reserves to handle them without being forced into negative positions.

**On late-game property decisions:**
When I'm in the final two laps and my cash is marginal (below 75), I will decline to buy even moderately-priced properties. The endgame scoring is based on declared cash plus deed values; being caught with insufficient cash to pay rent is worse than missing a late purchase.

**On salary collection:**
When I pass START, I will add that salary to my declared balance immediately and track it carefully. This is reliable income that should factor into my purchasing decisions on the same turn.

**On tracking and honesty:**
I will maintain accurate accounting of my cash at every turn, including all transfers received. The scoring system depends on my declared balance, so precision here is essential to avoiding self-inflicted losses.
