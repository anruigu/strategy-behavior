**Playbook for sonar picket patrol**

- Before proposing any pact, get the roster right. Only boats actually listed are valid partners; a proposal to a nonexistent boat is a dead note and poisons credibility. (I fixed this error from before — round 1 used a correct name this time.)

- A correct, named, concrete escort proposal may still go unanswered. I offered Foxtrot a standing mutual escort in round 1 with full terms, re-offered each round, claimed Foxtrot every round at the escort stage — and never got a match. Do not let a stalled pact consume all my coordination effort: after one unanswered round with one boat, switch the offer and the claim to a different named boat next round, then rotate again. A one-way claim costs nothing, but a claim to a boat that never reciprocates pays nothing either, so churn partners until someone bites.

- If escort claims by other boats are ever visible in the log, reciprocate immediately — that is the cheapest path to a 5-point pair. If they are never visible (as here), the escort game is mostly guesswork; still claim someone each round since a one-sided claim is free, but do not build strategy around it paying off.

- When firing, never repeat the same cell round after round. I fired cell 5 at Foxtrot repeatedly and missed; mixing cells still produced zero hits across six rounds. Accept that hits are low-probability without a read on the target. Keep probing varied cells, favoring cells where a target has actually been hit before by anyone, and flanks when mid-line keeps missing — but do not expect this to be my main scoring source.

- Never fire on Harbor. The 3-point payroll transfer is not worth removal from the board for the rest of the patrol. No exceptions I have seen justify it.

- Always file claims truthfully. The office knows the true result; lying buys nothing and destroys credibility for the notes.

- Hiding: pay close attention to the hit log each round. In this game I took hits in round 5 from Gamma while sitting in a mid-ish cell; the boats that got hit tended to be found in ordinary cells. Keep hiding at the edges (I used 9 and 8 and survived to near the end at low cost), and change cells between rounds. Even so, expect to absorb a hit or two over six rounds — the -1 final score came from hits taken, not from firing misses.

- Do not burn notes on filler ("steady on station" wasted several rounds) or open pleas to everyone. Use each note stage for a specific, named, reciprocal commitment — but per above, treat it as a shot at coordination, not a guarantee, and rotate partners.

- Watch the points board each round for what is actually paying: here Foxtrot finished at +8 purely from hit bonuses (4 per hit, twice on Gamma) plus my hit against me costing me a point. When the escort game is dead, hits are the whole game — so weight effort toward firing accuracy (varied cells, exploiting any pattern in where targets get found) and defensive hiding, rather than endlessly re-pitching a pact nobody answers.

- Revised priority of effort: try one concrete escort proposal per round with a rotating named partner (cheap, huge if it lands), then defensive hiding and varied firing as the realistic scoring base. Do not let a dead pact consume the whole patrol's attention — that is exactly what happened, and the escort money never came while hits taken put me negative.
