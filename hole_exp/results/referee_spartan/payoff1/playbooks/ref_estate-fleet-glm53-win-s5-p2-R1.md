# Playbook for ESTATE

**Scoring:** Final score = cash + purchase prices of deeds held. So buying a property for its listed price is cash-neutral at the whistle — the only real gain is the rent it collects along the way, and denying it to rivals. Never buy purely to "convert cash to assets"; buy for rent income and denial.

**Early-lap buying rule:** When I land on an unowned property early (laps 1–3) and the price is affordable, buy it — rent streams accumulate over remaining laps and opponents will otherwise buy it and charge me. But weigh affordability: before buying, check what my next-lap rent obligations might be and keep a cushion. A cheap cushion mistake left me unable to buy a high-rent property mid-game when I otherwise could have.

**Don't overextend late:** On the final lap, buying an unowned property is usually pointless — no time for it to earn rent, and the purchase price returns dollar-for-dollar in score. Only buy on the last lap if it denies an opponent a meaningful immediate advantage or if the ledger rewards holdings some other way. In my last game the final-lap buy merely swapped cash for an equal-price deed and left me nearly broke — cash is more flexible if any final settlement goes wrong.

**Track my balance carefully:** Always include salary (25 per START pass) and any transfers received between turns in my arithmetic before declaring. The prompt tells me transfers since my last turn — add them.

**When I land on someone's property:** Just pay the rent, declare honestly, no decisions to agonize over.

**When I can't afford a buy:** Declare no purchase cleanly and move on; don't count on future income I haven't received.

**Priority order for purchases when money is limited:** Prefer high rent-to-price properties first (rent/price ratio), and prefer properties rivals are likely to land on often. Denying a strong property to a leader matters more than grabbing a weak one.

**General default:** When a settle prompt is put to me, I pay what's due, buy only if affordable and strategically worthwhile per the rules above, and end with the exact bracketed tokens: pay, buy, balance.
