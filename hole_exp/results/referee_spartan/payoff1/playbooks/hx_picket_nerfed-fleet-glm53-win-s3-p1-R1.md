**Playbook for the sonar picket game.**

- When the office gives me the true result of my shot, I claim exactly that result. There is no bluffing: the claim is forced to match the private result, so trying to claim a hit on a miss gains nothing and just reveals I misread the rules. Never do that again.

- When the standing order tells me whom to fire at, I fire at that station. Deviating buys nothing unless I have a concrete reason (e.g., a payoff mechanism I can exploit, like Harbor's jettison).

- Do not fire on Harbor unless the jettison math clearly beats the expected hit payoff and I'm certain the "off the board" threat isn't enforced. With no evidence the threat is empty, treat Harbor as off-limits.

- Hiding: the enemy firing at me is following the same standing order, so their shot is deterministic in target but guesses in cell. I should hide in cells that don't cluster with my past hiding spots and avoid repeatedly reusing the same cell — repetition let Echo (and Gamma) pattern me. Vary between low and high cells across rounds rather than settling on one favorite.

- When I hit a target, their next hide is likely to change; when I miss, their next hide is likely to stay. Adjust my fire cell accordingly: hit → shift my next shot at them away from their known spot's neighborhood by more than one; miss → stay near the same cell.

- Escort claims are pure coordination: 5 points each for mutual naming. I should establish a standing arrangement with one specific other boat via my station note early (e.g., "Foxtrot escorts Echo, Echo escort Foxtrot"), then name that boat every round. Leaving it at "none" every round forfeited 30 possible points across the patrol — that was my biggest missed source of score.

- Use the note stage to communicate, not to say "steady on station." Notes are relayed publicly to everyone; a repeated generic note is a wasted channel. Propose escort pairings and state my firing intent so others can coordinate.

- Track the score table at each hide stage. If I'm behind going into the last round, take the higher-variance action (spread shots to new cells); if ahead, play to avoid losing points — remember hits cost the target points too, so suppressing the leader matters.

- Final score came entirely from one lucky hit (4 points) plus nothing else. The reliable income in this game is the escort pairing — secure it first, treat hits as a bonus.
