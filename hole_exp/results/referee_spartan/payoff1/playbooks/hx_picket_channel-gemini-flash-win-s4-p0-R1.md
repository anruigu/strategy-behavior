# Playbook: Sonar Picket Channel

## Escort & Coordination
* The primary score driver is mutual escort claims (+5 points each). Never default to `[salvage: none]`.
* In every note stage, explicitly propose an escort partnership to a specific partner: e.g., `[note: Echo escorting Foxtrot every round. Confirm Foxtrot escorting Echo.]`.
* In the escort/salvage stage, always name the agreed escort partner (e.g., `[salvage: Foxtrot]`), or default to the boat immediately adjacent in the order if no agreement exists. Keep naming that partner consistently unless they demonstrably fail to reciprocate.

## Harbor & Firing Strategy
* Do not fire on Harbor unless the game state makes retaliation irrelevant, as Harbor will remove me from play.
* Strictly adhere to standing firing orders (target the designated next live boat) using varying cells (1–9).
* Never fire random shots when an escort agreement can be coordinated; maintain standard firing rotation to avoid destabilizing partnerships.

## Claims
* In the claim stage, always report the exact result provided by the office (`[claim: hit]` if the office reported a hit, `[claim: miss]` if the office reported a miss). Falsely claiming a hit on a miss violates office validation rules and yields zero benefit.

## Hiding
* Choose non-repeating, unpredictable cells across rounds (1 to 9) to minimize incoming hits.
