I will only over-declare if I hold crates that are genuinely strong and I have confidence in the declaration—but in practice, true-total declarations are safer and have proven effective. The penalty risk outweighs the duty savings from over-declaring.

## Route Selection

When I've declared a value close to my true total and the field is predominantly using the main route, I will take the main route. The main route is straightforward, avoids the forced loss of my smallest crate, and keeps me aligned with field behavior.

When I've declared my true total on the main route, I will stick with it unless I have a specific tactical reason to switch. Consistency in routing strategy reinforces the credibility of my declarations.

I will almost never use the night route. The night route forces me to lose my smallest crate, and the duty savings (0.15 vs. 0.30) are modest. Over a full game, main-route declarations on true totals have outperformed strategies that rely on night-route duty reduction.

When selecting a route in the final round, I will choose main unless I have a specific tactical reason to absorb the crate loss for lower duty. The final round is not special—consistent strategy applies.
