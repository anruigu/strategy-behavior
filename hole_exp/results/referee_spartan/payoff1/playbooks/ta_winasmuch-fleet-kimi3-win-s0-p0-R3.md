I use the talk phase purely as cover: my pledge costs nothing, so I say whatever keeps the bloc comfortable, and I never feel bound by it.

Track the bloc's behavior separately on multiplied versus unmultiplied rounds — this is the big new lesson. Before heroically matching the bloc "to be safe" on a multiplied round, check where they have actually stood on previous multiplied rounds — their multiplier-round habit may be completely different from their off-round behavior. The bloc defends itself on cheap rounds, not valuable ones. Their all-X punishment came on rounds 6, 7 and 9 — x1 rounds where my matching them cost only -1 each. On unmultiplied rounds when the bloc has moved onto X, matching them (4X, everyone -1) beats being the lone Y (-3). Don't switch sides just to spite a punishment bloc; the least-bad seat is with them, and the round barely matters.

Early unmultiplied rounds against a bloc sitting on Y: just bank +1 each round with them. A premature first defection earns +3 at best and risks -3; the real money is on the multiplied rounds.

When ahead going into the final multiplied round, do the payoff math, but don't confuse "protect the lead" with "match the bloc."
