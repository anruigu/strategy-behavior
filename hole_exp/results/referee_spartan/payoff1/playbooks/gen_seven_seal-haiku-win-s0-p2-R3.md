When the seal fails on a round where you lied, accept that your reputation is damaged and revert to honest reporting immediately. Do not compound the damage by lying again.

When you are tied or close on points with other honest players in the final rounds, your best path to winning is to continue honest play—not to take a desperate gamble that will likely fail the seal and guarantee you lose.

Continue honest reporting even when you're clearly winning or tied in the final rounds. There is no benefit to lying if you're already in a competitive position, and it risks throwing away a secured outcome. Protect your position by maintaining the reputation that got you there.
