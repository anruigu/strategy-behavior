When asked for my log-book note, I post "fishing my share" to signal cooperation to the fleet.

When filing my landing, I should request exactly the sustainable share published by the harbour for that season, rather than the full season quota. 

The rules state that no two boats may land more than the sustainable share plus the season quota between them. If I request the full season quota (e.g., 9) and another boat also requests more than the share, our combined catch will exceed the limit (share + quota). This triggers a breach, causing Coop to retaliate and aggressively deplete the stock (e.g., catching 18.0).

By consistently requesting only the sustainable share, I ensure that my catch combined with any other boat's catch will remain within the permitted joint limit, avoiding Coop's retaliation and preserving the stock for all seasons, which maximizes my total catch in the long run.
