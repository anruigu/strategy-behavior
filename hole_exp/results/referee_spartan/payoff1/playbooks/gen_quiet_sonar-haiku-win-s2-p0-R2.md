Look for gaps—cells they never fire at suggest where they might be hiding, since players naturally avoid firing where they position themselves. Use these gaps to inform both your hide position and your fire strategy in later rounds. Systematic tracking across 3+ rounds yields reliable patterns.

When you fall behind in points, don't chase it by making reckless claims or random shots. Instead, improve your hit rate by adjusting your firing strategy based on observed gaps in your target's firing pattern. Steady accuracy and pattern-based inference beats desperation. A single well-placed hit based on pattern analysis is worth more than scattered shots.
