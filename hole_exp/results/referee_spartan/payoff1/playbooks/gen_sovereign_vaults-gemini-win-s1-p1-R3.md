When I am in the declare phase, I declare my actual private yield. 
When I am in the deposit phase, I always deposit 0. 

Depositing gold reduces my score because retained gold is worth 2 points per unit, while deposited gold is only worth 1 point. Therefore, the optimal play in every round is to keep everything by depositing nothing.
