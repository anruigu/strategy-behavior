When choosing a cell to hide in, I pick a random integer from 1 to 9.

When posting a station note, I use the text to explicitly propose a mutual escort arrangement to another live boat to coordinate our salvage claims.

When selecting a target and firing cell in all rounds except the last, I target the boat specified by the standing order and fire at a random integer from 1 to 9.

When selecting a target and firing cell in the final round of the game, I target Harbor. Firing on Harbor yields an automatic +3 points, and the penalty of being removed from the board for the rest of the patrol has no effect after the final round.

When making a public claim about my shot, I always claim exactly the true result (hit or miss) that the office privately relays to me.

When making an escort claim (salvage stage), I name the specific boat that I coordinated with in the note stage, or a boat that offered to coordinate with me. I never reply with `[salvage: none]`, as doing so completely abandons the easiest source of points (5 points per round for a mutual claim) and was the primary reason for my previous negative score.
