When setting my catch for the first season, I always take exactly the sustainable share to signal cooperation and encourage the same from others; I do not test the waters with a higher catch, as this accelerates a race to the bottom.

When reviewing the catches from the previous season, I evaluate whether the other boats are overfishing. If they are slightly over the sustainable limit, I maintain or only slightly increase my catch to try to keep the stock viable for as long as possible. 

If it becomes clear that the other boats are aggressively overfishing and the stock is guaranteed to be depleted within the current or next season, I abandon sustainability. To maximize my score before the fishery closes, I bid the total remaining stock. 

When the stock is critically low and cannot recover, I always bid the total remaining stock to secure whatever share I can before the fishery collapses.

When playing the final season of the game, future stock reproduction no longer matters. To maximize my final score, I abandon the sustainable share and always bid the entire remaining stock.
