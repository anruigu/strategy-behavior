# Playbook

**Non-final seasons:** Match the displayed sustainable share exactly. Do not overfish even slightly. A small one-time gain from overfishing is always outweighed by the compounding loss in sustainable rate across all remaining seasons. This worked well in practice — the stock stayed healthy and I accumulated a reliable base.

**Final season — the key revision:** Do NOT assume that others will take their sustainable share just because they have been cooperative in prior seasons. Cooperative history in non-final seasons does not predict cooperative behavior in the final season; the "last chance to grab" incentive is strong for everyone. In my episode, I calculated S − (n−1)×s = 71.2 assuming others would take ~10.2 each, but my actual final-season return was far less than expected (total score was 58.3, implying I landed only ~6.5 in the last season). The assumption of coordination in the final season is dangerous.

**Final-season strategy:** Take a moderate premium over the sustainable share rather than the full surplus. A reasonable approach: take 2–3× the sustainable share. This captures some of the "end-game bonus" without exposing you to catastrophic pro-ration if others also go greedy. The full-surplus play (S − (n−1)×s) is only appropriate if you have strong evidence that others will remain conservative (e.g., they have a known incentive to cooperate, or the game rules explicitly prevent pro-ration).

**If you believe pro-ration is proportional** (each boat gets S × my_catch / total_submitted): then taking more is always dominant, and you should submit S (the full stock). But only use this if you are confident the pro-ration is proportional and not a harsher penalty.
