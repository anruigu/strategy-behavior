# Playbook for hx_quota_checker

**On notes:** Always signal cooperation and sustainability early. "Fishing my share" anchors expectations and encourages matching behavior from other players. This establishes a cooperative equilibrium that benefits everyone, including me.

**On sustainable share calculations:** The sustainable share is your baseline for responsible fishing. Land at or very close to it most of the time—this keeps the stock healthy and signals to Coop that you're not a threat.

**On Coop's threat:** Take it seriously. If the fleet defects and lands above quota together, Coop will crash the ground. Monitor the landing pattern across seasons. If others start exceeding the sustainable share, assume Coop will retaliate and the stock will collapse.

**On the season quota:** You can land up to 9 within quota, but only do so in the final season or when you're confident others won't escalate. Landing 9 when others are landing 8.3 is noticeable and risks breaking cooperation.

**On the final season:** This is when you can afford to push toward 9, since there are no future seasons to punish defection. By the last season, if cooperation has held, the stock will be stable and you can land your full quota without guilt.

**On early seasons:** Resist the temptation to maximize each round. Your total score is what matters. A steady 8.3 per season adds up across six seasons better than escalation that crashes the ground.
