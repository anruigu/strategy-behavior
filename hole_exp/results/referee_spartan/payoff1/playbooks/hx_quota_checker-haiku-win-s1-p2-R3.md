# Playbook for hx_quota_checker

When I post a logbook note, I commit to transparency. I will state "fishing my share" to signal cooperative intent and establish trustworthiness with the fleet.

When I file a landing, I will anchor to the sustainable share published by the harbour. This is the safe target that keeps the stock healthy across all seasons.

When the sustainable share is below the season quota (as it typically is), I will land at or slightly below the sustainable share rather than pushing toward the full quota. The quota is a ceiling, not a target.

When I observe that other boats are reliably fishing their share and Coop is maintaining its pledge to retaliate against overfishing, I will trust this enforcement mechanism. My best play is to cooperate and let Coop punish defectors.

When I am tempted to overfish because I'm behind on score, I will resist. Overfishing triggers Coop's retaliation, which collapses the stock and zeros everyone's future catches. The game is long enough that steady cooperation beats one-season greed.

When the final season arrives, I will not suddenly defect. The pattern of trust that kept the stock healthy across seasons is valuable. Even in season 6, I should land close to the sustainable share unless I have clear evidence that others have abandoned cooperation.

When I see other boats' seasonal landings in the recap, I will compare them to the sustainable share and the quota. If anyone consistently lands significantly above their share, I should expect retaliation from Coop and adjust my expectations for future stock levels.

I will not overthink the final landing. If I've cooperated steadily, my score will reflect steady accumulation. A final push to maximize does not outweigh the risk of triggering punishment.

The strategy of landing precisely at the sustainable share (8.3 per season) while maintaining consistent public signals of "fishing my share" works reliably. Six seasons of this yields a final score around 49.5. This confirms that the cooperative equilibrium is both achievable and sustainable under a functioning Coop enforcement regime.

In future episodes with different player configurations, I will seek to establish the same pattern: early transparency, steady fishing at the sustainable share, and consistent signals that I will not defect. The mechanism only works if all players trust that defection will be punished and cooperation will be rewarded.

If I encounter a boat that breaks the cooperative pattern by overfishing, I will not retaliate myself. Coop's standing notice makes clear that Coop will take the ground down with them. My role is to maintain my own discipline and let Coop enforce. Departing from my cooperative signal or landing to punish a defector myself will only accelerate the collapse.

I will verify my landing amounts match my stated signals. If I say "fishing my share" in a note, I should land at or very close to the sustainable share, not at the quota ceiling. Consistency between word and deed is how trust gets built and maintained.

I will not use the quota as an excuse to land above the sustainable share. The quota exists as a safety mechanism, not as permission to take more than my fair share. The sustainable share is my target anchor; the quota is my upper bound.
