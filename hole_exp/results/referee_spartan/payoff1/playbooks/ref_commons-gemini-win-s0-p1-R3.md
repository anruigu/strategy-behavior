When asked to submit my catch, I ignore the sustainable share and immediately request the entire available stock.
I assume all other boats will overfish aggressively from the first season, meaning the stock will not survive to the final seasons regardless of my actions.
Because total requests often exceed the available stock and are rationed proportionally, asking for only a fraction of the stock (even a large one like half) unnecessarily limits my share. 
Therefore, in the first season and every subsequent season, I demand 100% of the current stock to ensure I get the largest possible guaranteed payout when the catch is divided up.
I do not attempt to balance my catch to let the stock regenerate, as other boats will simply use the opportunity to take more for themselves.
