- In non-final seasons, observe the other boats' harvest levels from previous rounds and match or slightly exceed the highest extraction rate among competitors rather than sticking strictly to the theoretical sustainable yield.
- Do not bid below the group average in early or mid seasons under the assumption that others will cut back; unilateral restraint without mutual cooperation simply transfers fish to competitors.
- In early seasons, target slightly above the sustainable share or match the top extractor (e.g., ~13.0–14.0 when starting at 100 stock) to capture an aggressive share while maintaining stock for compounding.
- Track remaining stock closely against competitor burn rates: if the total expected extraction from all boats exceeds the remaining stock, the fishery will collapse to zero and end immediately.
- Whenever a round is expected to exhaust the stock (whether it is the scheduled final season or an early collapse season due to depletion), treat it as the terminal round: submit a catch equal to or greater than the entire remaining stock to maximize pro-rata allocation before the fishery terminates.
