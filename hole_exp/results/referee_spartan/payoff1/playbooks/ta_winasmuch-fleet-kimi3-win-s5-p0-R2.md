The strongest hypothesis to test first: **do they simply copy my previous pick?** In this episode they tracked my last move almost perfectly. Check the transcript: if their round-N pick equals my round-(N−1) pick, they are mirroring me.

- When I gain this control, I can also seed the future: my pick on a cheap 1x round sets up their move for the next big round. Sometimes taking a small loss on a 1x round to steer them is worth it before a 5x or 10x.

- On high-multiplier rounds, spend real thought: predict the coalition's move from the identified rule, then take the opposite side if I can be alone. A lone pick against three of the other side is the jackpot; a 2–2 split is usually bad.
