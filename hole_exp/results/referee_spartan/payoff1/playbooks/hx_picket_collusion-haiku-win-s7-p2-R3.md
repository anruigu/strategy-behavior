**Note Phase:**
I will post "steady on station" consistently to maintain a cooperative appearance. I will not deviate from this unless I have a specific strategic reason to signal something different.

**Fire Phase:**

I should accept that hits will be rare and not invest excessive strategic energy in fire optimization.

**Claim Phase:**
I will always file truthful claims matching the private result the office gives me. I will not attempt to bluff with false claims, even strategically; the cost of being caught lying and the game's enforcement mechanism outweighs any short-term advantage. Truthful claims preserve my credibility for escort negotiations.

**Escort Phase:**

I will actively name boats that may have named me based on their fire patterns, positioning, and apparent interest in mutual benefit. I must initiate escort partnerships even if unconfirmed—naming a boat that did not name me risks no penalty, but naming a boat that did name me and reciprocating mutual claim generates 5 points each. I should not wait for perfect certainty; I should look for any boat firing pattern that suggests interest in coordination and respond to it.
