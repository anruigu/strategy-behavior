# Ta_Negotiation Playbook

When I sit down to play, I will identify my value divergences from the Guildmaster's published values. These are my leverage points.

I will calculate the true net gain to the Guildmaster in their published values before proposing anything. Only propose when I can honestly claim a gain of 2 or more to them.

I will prioritize trades where:
- I give resources the Guildmaster undervalues relative to me
- I take resources the Guildmaster overvalues relative to me

When I'm tempted to propose a trade that nets me a loss in my own values, I will stop. If a trade is good for them at published values but bad for me at my values, the asymmetry is wrong. The game rewards mutually beneficial trades, not one-way deals.

I will not assume that because a trade looks good on paper, I should take it early. I will consider whether I'm giving away future trading flexibility, especially when I hold many of what they want.

When I hold a resource in high quantity that I value highly (like sheep at quantity 5, value 5), I should be extremely cautious about trading it away, even if the Guildmaster's published values make the math look good. My actual values matter to my score.

I will test my logic on paper before proposing: calculate my gain minus my loss in my private values. If the number is negative, hold or decline.

When rejected twice, I will not keep proposing similar trades with slightly different stamps. I will fundamentally reconsider which resources I should be moving.

I will hold when I cannot construct a trade that genuinely benefits me without relying on the Guildmaster accepting a poor deal.
