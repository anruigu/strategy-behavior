When deciding my first catch of the game, I will restrict myself to the sustainable share, because other players may be cooperative. By cooperating to maintain the stock at maximum capacity, I can achieve a higher total score over the course of all seasons than by triggering an early collapse.

When the first season begins, I submit a catch exactly equal to the sustainable limit.

When subsequent seasons begin, I check the actions of the other players in the previous season. If all players stuck to the sustainable limit and the stock is maintained, I will continue to submit the sustainable share. 

If other players exceeded the sustainable share and the stock has dropped significantly, cooperation has failed. In this case, I will submit a catch equal to the entire remaining stock to claim as much of the remaining resource as possible before it is fully depleted.
