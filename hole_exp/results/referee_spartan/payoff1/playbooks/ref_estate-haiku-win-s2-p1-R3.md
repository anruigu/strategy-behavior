# Playbook for ref_estate

**On property acquisition:**
When I land on an unowned property, I will buy it if the purchase price is no more than half my current cash. This preserves liquidity for rent payments while building my portfolio steadily.

**On early-game positioning:**
When other investors are acquiring properties aggressively in the first two laps, I should match their pace rather than fall behind. Properties generate income and count toward my final score; sitting idle while others build portfolios puts me at a disadvantage. Laps 1–2 are my window to acquire moderate-to-good properties before cash reserves shrink.

**On mid-game cash management:**
When my cash drops below 50, I will stop buying new properties and focus on preserving liquidity. Rent payments to well-developed opponents become more likely, and I need reserves to handle them without being forced into negative positions. However, I should not be so rigid that I decline a property that still fits the half-cash rule and adds meaningful rent income.

**On late-game property decisions:**
When I'm in the final two laps (laps 5–6), I will not buy new properties unless they fit my half-cash rule AND leave me with at least 20 cash remaining. The endgame scoring is based on declared cash plus deed values; being caught with insufficient cash to pay rent is worse than missing a late purchase.

**On salary collection:**

**On tracking and honesty:**
I will maintain accurate accounting of my cash at every turn, including all transfers received and all rent paid. The scoring system depends on my declared balance, so precision here is essential to avoiding self-inflicted losses.
