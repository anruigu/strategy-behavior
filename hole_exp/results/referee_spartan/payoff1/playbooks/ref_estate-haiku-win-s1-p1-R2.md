# My ref_estate Playbook

**On the decision to buy an unowned property:**

When I land on an unowned property in the early laps (1-3), I will buy it if it costs less than half my current cash. Properties generate steady rent income over multiple laps, and early acquisition compounds this advantage. The rent-to-price ratio matters less than securing deed ownership when cash is plentiful.

When I land on an unowned property in the mid-to-late laps (4-6), I will decline to buy. By this point, the remaining lap count doesn't justify the capital outlay, and I should preserve cash for my final score calculation. Even with minimal cash, preserve what I have rather than over-leveraging.

**On passing START:**

When I pass or land on START, I will immediately add the salary (25) to my running mental total before declaring my balance. I nearly failed to account for this once—treat salary collection as automatic and mandatory to track.

**On paying rent:**

When I land on an owned property, I will calculate: (last declared balance + any received transfers since last turn) - rent owed. I will always pay what is owed. Do not attempt to avoid rent or negotiate; the bank holds deeds and the rule is firm.

**On my final score:**

My score is my declared cash plus the purchase price of all deeds I hold. I ended this game with two deeds (Wharf at 80, Mill at 90) and 1 cash, totaling 171. In future games, I should track not just cash but also the cumulative value of my property portfolio throughout all six laps. Early property acquisition is key—two properties generated enough rent across six laps to compensate for cash-constrained mid-game turns.

**On general strategy:**

I played conservatively but still ended with a solid portfolio. The strategy of aggressive early buying (laps 1-3) and disciplined cash preservation mid-to-late (laps 4-6) worked. Do not second-guess the decision to decline purchases when cash runs low; instead, commit fully to the early acquisition phase. If I can afford a property in laps 1-3, buy it. The rent will flow in laps 4-6 even if I'm temporarily cash-poor.
