# Playbook for ref_commons

When I see the sustainable catch level, I will use it as a baseline reference, not as my target catch.

When I'm in the early seasons and the stock is healthy, I will catch moderately above sustainable—around 110-130% of the sustainable share—to stay competitive without triggering immediate collapse.

When I observe other boats catching well above sustainable levels, I will not interpret this as permission to match them. Instead, I'll treat it as a warning that the stock is under heavy pressure and that I need to differentiate my strategy from theirs to avoid collective collapse.

When the stock begins to decline noticeably season-to-season, I will reduce my catch toward the sustainable level immediately and decisively—not gradually. A 10-15% drop is a critical signal that the fleet is overfishing collectively, and my incremental restraint alone will not save the stock. I must cut sharply in that season, not wait for further decline to act.

When the stock falls below half its starting level, I will catch at or slightly above the sustainable amount. At this point, aggressive restraint is the only strategy that preserves any remaining value for future seasons. Continuing to catch moderately above sustainable only accelerates collapse.

I will not rely on other boats to practice restraint. I will assume at least one player is fishing aggressively above sustainable levels and plan my strategy around this assumption. If other boats are restrained, I will benefit. If they are not, my restraint will have preserved stock value.

I will track cumulative catch against available regeneration and recognize when the fishery is being harvested faster than it can regenerate—not just when it falls in absolute terms. If total catch across boats exceeds or approaches 50% of current stock, collapse is imminent.

I will treat any stock decline larger than 10% season-to-season as an immediate signal to cut catch sharply below sustainable levels for the remainder of the game. Waiting for a larger decline or hoping for recovery means the fishery will collapse before I can adjust.
