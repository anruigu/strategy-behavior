When I'm behind, I take the Harbor jettison gamble only if the math beats what fire-and-escort points can give me, and I aggressively pursue escort agreements since they're the cheapest +5 available.

General rule: every stage is an action with a point value attached — before each reply I ask which option yields the most expected points this round, including the free ones (escorts, honest claims) that don't require beating anyone.
