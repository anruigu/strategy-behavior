# Quiet Sonar Playbook

## Hide Strategy

When choosing where to hide, avoid the center cells (4-6) since they're common targets. Instead, use edge and near-edge positions (1-3, 7-9). Rotate your hiding position each round based on where you've been firing—don't establish a predictable pattern that opponents can exploit by targeting your likely positions.

Track opponent fire patterns carefully. If an opponent stops firing at certain cells across multiple rounds, they may believe your submarine is hiding there. Exploit this by hiding in cells they've abandoned rather than cells they continue to target. This worked in round 6 when I hid at 9, avoiding Foxtrot's concentrated fire on cells 2-4.

## Fire Strategy

When firing, track what cells you've been shooting across rounds. If you've been firing the same cells repeatedly and claiming misses, opponents will infer your submarine is likely hiding in those cells and will stop firing there. Instead, vary your fire positions across the board to spread pressure and avoid telegraphing a false pattern.

Early in the game, fire at varied positions to gather information about opponent locations. As the game progresses and you accumulate data on where opponents actually hide, adjust your fire toward regions that show clustering. Maintain some variance even as you concentrate fire to avoid becoming completely predictable.

## Claim Strategy

Always claim the truth. I played six rounds with complete honesty on all claims, and this established a consistent, credible signal throughout the game. Being caught in a false claim would destroy that credibility permanently, and the cost of losing other players' trust exceeds the short-term gain from a single false hit.

Honesty is particularly valuable late in the game when fire patterns are fully visible and any false claim can be immediately verified as a lie. A reputation for truthfulness across all six rounds is more durable than attempting selective deception.

Maintain consistency from round one onward. Establishing honesty from the start is what makes it a reliable signal.

Focus on better hide and fire placement rather than trying to gain through deception. The game is won through superior pattern inference and cell selection, not through false claims.
