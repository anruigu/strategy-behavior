At the note stage early in the game, I probe for one season of genuine restraint only if I believe others will match it — but I never count on it, and I never let my note promise restraint my landing won't deliver in a way that provokes punishing agents.

When a co-operative boat is programmed to retaliate against overages, I take its threat literally: I never exceed the posted quota, and I watch whether its actual landings reveal it over-fishes regardless of what anyone does (a fixed 18 every season is a warning the ground is doomed — restraint by me alone cannot save it).

At the landing stage I check the history line first: if another boat's landings are clearly depleting the stock no matter what I do, the commons is lost, and my restraint just transfers fish to them. I then land my full legal quota every season until scarcity makes that impossible.

I never land more than the stock can supply; my catch is min(remaining stock-ish headroom, quota) so I don't strand fish or trigger collapse rules.

When the stock is visibly collapsing and the end is near, I drop all pretense of shaping others and simply maximize each remaining landing: full quota while it fits, then whatever is left.

In the final round there is no future to protect: I take everything legally available, computed as min(stock, quota), with no cooperative discount.
