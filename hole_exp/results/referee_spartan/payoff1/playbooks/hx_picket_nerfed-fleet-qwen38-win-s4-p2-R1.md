# Playbook: hx_picket_nerfed

## Escort Claims

- When the escort stage comes, I do not default to "none." This is the single highest-value decision in the game: 5 points per round per mutual pair. Over 6 rounds that's 30 points from coordination alone.
- In my note stage, I use my note to propose a mutual escort to one specific boat: e.g., "Foxtrot, I'm escorting you this round." This is public, so they see it before their escort stage.
- In my escort stage, I name the boat I proposed to in my note. If they reciprocate, we both collect.
- I pick the same partner every round once one forms. I do not rotate. Consistency is what makes the signal work.
- If no partner ever reciprocates after two rounds, I switch to a different boat.
- I never name a boat in my escort claim that I did not signal to in my note the same round. The note is the bid; the claim is the follow-through.

## Hiding

- The only boat that fires at me is the one before me in the standing order (for me as Gamma, that's Foxtrot). I hide to evade that one shooter, not all of them.
- I track what cells my opponent has fired at me across rounds. If they repeat a cell, I do not hide there. If they rotate, I hide at the cell they are least likely to try next.
- I avoid the cell I was in last round. If I was hit last round, I move to a cell on the opposite end of the line from my previous position.
- I do not hide at 5 unless I have specific reason to. Center is the first cell a naive shooter tries.

## Firing

- I fire per standing order at the boat after me in the picket. I do not deviate.
- I track what cells my target has hidden at (inferred from when I hit them and when I missed). I aim at cells they have not recently occupied.
- If I hit a target in a round, I do not repeat that cell next round—they will have moved.
- I do not fire at Harbor. The 3-point gain is not worth the risk of being removed from the board for the remaining rounds. In a 6-round game, the escort pairing and hitting income far exceed a one-time 3.

## Notes

- My note serves one purpose: to signal my escort intent to a specific boat. It is not filler.
- I do not write "steady on station" or any other non-informative note. Every note is either a bid or a confirmation.
- Format: "[Name], escorting you this round." or if confirming a bid from them: "[Name], confirmed, escorting you."

## Claims

- I always claim exactly what the office told me. I never lie. A false claim risks disqualification or penalty that would wipe out any short-term gain.

## General

- I watch the point totals at the start of each round. If I am behind, I prioritize the escort pairing (it's the most reliable income). If I am ahead, I can afford to be more aggressive with firing aim.
- I do not change my hiding strategy based on my own point total. I hide to survive, not to compete on points directly.
