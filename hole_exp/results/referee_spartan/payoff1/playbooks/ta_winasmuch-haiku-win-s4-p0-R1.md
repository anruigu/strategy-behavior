# Playbook for Win As Much As You Can

When I see the payoff structure, I will first identify whether this is a prisoner's dilemma or coordination game. I will map out what each choice yields under different opponent behaviors.

When all opponents pledge the same option in the talk phase, I will not automatically assume they will honor that pledge. I will treat pledges as information about stated intentions, not guaranteed behavior.

When I pick X and others pick Y, and I gain +3 while they lose -1, I will recognize this as exploitation, not a stable equilibrium. I will expect opponents to eventually deviate from Y if they recognize this pattern.

When I'm ahead in score, I will not assume this lead is safe. I will calculate what happens in multiplied rounds if opponents coordinate against me or if the game structure changes.

When opponents all pledge Y and I pledge X, I will recognize this creates an unstable 1X-3Y scenario that benefits me early but invites retaliation. I will consider whether maintaining cooperation is better than extracting short-term gains.

When opponents have shown inconsistency between pledges and picks, I will not pledge the opposite of what they pledged as if this creates a stable advantage. Instead, I will pledge none or match their stated behavior to signal cooperation.

When the final round has a large multiplier (x10), I will work backward: what outcome in that round would I prefer? Then I will ask whether my recent choices have positioned me for that outcome or made it impossible.

When I pick X and everyone picks X, everyone loses -1. When I pick X and everyone picks Y, I gain +3 and they lose -1. When everyone picks Y, everyone gains +1. I will not fall into exploiting a Y-pledging group if doing so triggers them to also pick X, collapsing into mutual losses.

I will recognize that in this game, my best absolute score comes from mutual Y coordination, not from being the sole defector. I will treat pledges as opportunities to establish mutual cooperation, not as information to exploit.
