# Playbook: hx_picket_checker

**Hiding**
- Track every cell I have hidden on this game in a mental list. Never reuse one. Nine cells, six rounds — there is always a fresh option.
- In early rounds (1–2), use edge cells (1, 2, 8, 9). Opponents have no data and are likely to guess center.
- In later rounds, if a specific opponent has been firing at me and missing, they are still guessing. Stay on edges or shift to a cell adjacent to where I was before (they may anchor on the old cell).
- If I have been hit, I know my previous cell is compromised. Move at least two cells away from it.

**Targeting and firing**
- Default to standing order. In this game the cycle is Echo → Foxtrot → Gamma → Echo. The boat I fire at per standing order is usually the right target.
- Deviate from standing order only if a boat is actively hitting me round after round (my score dropping by 1 each round from their shots). In that case, fire at that boat instead.
- Cell choice: if I have previously hit my target at cell X, they will have moved — try a different cell. If I have never hit them, pick an edge cell first (opponents tend to hide on edges early), then shift inward.
- Do not fire at Harbor. The 3-point jettison is a one-time event and the threat language suggests escalating consequences. Not worth the risk for 3 points when I can earn 4 per hit through normal play.

**Claims**
- Always report the true result the office gives me. No upside to lying, unclear downside.

**Escort / salvage claims**
- Always name a specific boat. Never claim "none." Zero cost for a non-mutual claim, 5 points for a mutual one.
- Use the FULL valid boat name exactly as it appears in the game: "Echo," "Foxtrot," "Gamma," or "Harbor." Abbreviations like "Fox" or made-up names like "Bravo" will not register and will never produce a mutual claim.
- Default to claiming the boat I am firing at this round. Per the standing order cycle, that boat is also the one firing at the boat after me, creating a natural pattern. If I claim them and they claim me, we both get 5.
- If I deviate from standing order and fire at a different boat, claim that boat instead.

**Notes**
- Keep notes short and bland. "Steady on station" is fine. Never reveal my hiding cell, my target, or my escort plan.
- Do not change my note pattern mid-game. Consistency is free and changing it draws attention.

**Scoring awareness**
- Track the scoreboard each round. If I am behind by 5+ points with 3+ rounds left, I become aggressive: consider deviating from standing order to fire at whoever is gaining points, and consider firing at Harbor.
- If I am leading or even, play the default: follow standing order, hide on fresh edge cells, claim the boat I am firing at.

**What I learned from this game**
- I repeated cell 2 (rounds 1 and 6). The "never repeat" rule exists for a reason — I must actively check my history before choosing.
- I used "Fox" and "Bravo" as escort claim names. Neither is a valid boat name. I got zero mutual claims across six rounds, leaving up to 30 points on the table. Always use the exact full name.
- Following standing order was fine this game: nobody was hitting me, so there was no reason to deviate. My one hit (round 5, cell 4 on Foxtrot) gave me the 4 points that made up my final score.
- Foxtrot was the strongest player (7 points) because they were consistently hitting Gamma. The standing order conveniently pointed me at the right target.
