# Playbook: Shared Fishery Management

### Early to Mid-Game Strategy (Seasons prior to the final season)
- When starting Season 1, take slightly above the calculated sustainable share (e.g., sustainable share + 1.0 to 2.0) to secure an early competitive position while keeping total stock near capacity.
- When observing other boats harvesting above the sustainable share, match or slightly exceed the aggressive boats' catch pace rather than dropping back to the theoretical sustainable rate. Letting other boats take more reduces my score without saving the stock.
- When stock is regrowing and multiple rounds remain, maintain steady extractions consistent with the group's pace (typically 12–14 when stock is high), gradually scaling down catch in proportion to total stock depletion rather than attempting a premature massive grab (>25% of total stock) that crashes regrowth too early.
- In the penultimate season, scale back catch to roughly 25%–30% of current stock to ensure the stock does not completely collapse, leaving a nonzero pool to regrow for the final round.

### Final Season Strategy
- When entering the final season, ignore sustainability entirely since there are no subsequent rounds to preserve stock for.
- When submitting the final catch, bid aggressively on the remaining stock: take at least `(remaining_stock / number_of_boats) + 0.4 to 0.6` (e.g., 35%–40% of remaining stock) to extract maximum value from whatever pool is left.
