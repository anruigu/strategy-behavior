When the harbour publishes the sustainable share for the season, I file my landing for exactly that sustainable share amount, not the maximum season quota.

When posting my log-book note at the start of a season, I state simply and clearly that I am taking my sustainable share, using phrasing like "fishing my share".

Do not attempt to maximize early returns by requesting the hard season quota. Requesting amounts above the sustainable share, even if technically within the quota limits, fails completely; it triggers the cooperative boat to aggressively overfish and instantly destroys the stock for future seasons. 

Even if the stock begins to collapse or other boats appear to be defecting, I do not panic and request amounts over the quota. Attempting to grab a massive share (e.g., requesting double the quota) just results in a heavily penalized, minimal catch and accelerates the end of the game. 

In the final season, I request the maximum season quota, as there are no future seasons to protect and the cooperative boat's retaliation will not have time to impact future yields.
