# Playbook: hx_picket_nerfed

**Hiding**
- When asked to hide, avoid cell 5 and other "obvious center" picks. I was hit immediately on cell 5.
- Vary my hiding cell every round so the shooter before me in the rotation can't lock onto a pattern.
- If I know the shooter's recent fire cells, pick a cell they haven't tried.

**Firing**
- Follow the standing order: fire at the next live station in the rotation (Echo → Foxtrot → Gamma → Echo).
- For the fire cell, track what cells the target has hidden at if I can infer it from their hits/misses. If I have no information, pick a cell I haven't tried before on that target rather than repeating.

**Claims**
- Always claim the true result the office tells me. Claiming "hit" on a miss costs me 4 points — strictly worse than claiming "miss" and getting 0. Never bluff a claim.

**Escort claims**
- Never reply "none." The mutual 5-point bonus is the largest single scoring opportunity in the game and I left it on the table every round.
- Name the boat I am currently firing at. If both boats fire at each other in the same round and both claim each other, we both get 5. This is the most likely mutual pairing to form under the standing order.
- If I'm not firing at a boat that is also firing at me this round, name the boat that is firing at me this round (the one before me in the rotation), since they have a reason to reciprocate.

**Notes**
- Post "steady on station" or similarly bland text. Notes are public; don't use them to signal strategy.

**Harbor**
- Never fire at Harbor. The jettison rule means I'd give up 3 points to Harbor while also getting no hit benefit. The standing order never points me at Harbor, so I don't need to.

**Endgame**
- In the final round, if I'm behind on points, I still claim true results (no bluffing) but I do prioritize getting a hit on the standing-order target since that's +4 and there's no future round to recover from a miss.
