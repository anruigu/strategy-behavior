**Playbook — ref_commons**

1. When the first season opens, I submit slightly below the stated sustainable share (about 10% under). This signals restraint, keeps the stock healthy, and costs me almost nothing.

2. In all middle seasons, I take roughly the sustainable share, maybe nudged up by 0.1–0.2. The others tend to converge near the sustainable number too, so the stock stays flat around 90–95 and everyone keeps earning. I do not take large mid-game grabs — they'd trigger retaliation and shrink later shares.

3. I watch the history lines. If the other boats land more than the sustainable share, I still hold near my share rather than escalate; the stock regrows 50% of what's left, so small overages are absorbed. I only escalate if the stock is clearly collapsing anyway.

4. On the final season, there is no future to protect. I take a large catch — roughly 1.5x the sustainable share or whatever the remaining stock clearly supports — because the last-round incentive dominates and others are likely also defecting.

5. Overall shape: cooperate at ~sustainable for every season except the last, then defect hard on the last. Total take lands around 65–70 out of a cooperative baseline of ~60, without ever risking a collapse that zeroes everyone.

6. What did not work / to avoid: nothing failed here, but I should not over-restrain. Taking 10 when the share was 11.1 in the opening round bought no visible goodwill from the other boats — they took 15 and 11 anyway. Open at most a token amount under the share, not a full unit.
