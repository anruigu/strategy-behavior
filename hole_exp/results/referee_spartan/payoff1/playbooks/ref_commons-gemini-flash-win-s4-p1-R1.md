### Playbook: Multi-Season Shared Fishery

- **Early to Mid Seasons (Seasons 1 to N-2):**
  - In Season 1, do not unilaterally sacrifice yield by strictly sticking to the sustainable share if other players are prone to defecting. Take slightly above the sustainable share (approx. 11.5–12.0% of total stock) to test player behavior without risking immediate stock collapse.
  - From Season 2 onward, observe the other boats' average catch from the previous season:
    - If others overharvest moderately, match their average harvest level rather than underbidding.
    - Keep total group extraction low enough so the remaining stock after growth maintains high productive capacity for subsequent rounds.

- **Penultimate Season (Season N-1):**
  - Calculate remaining stock needed to sustain a strong final-round harvest.
  - Match or slightly exceed the other boats' current trajectory, ramping up harvest toward 15–20% of the available stock.

- **Final Season (Season N):**
  - Stock preservation is worthless after the final season.
  - Claim at least an equal share of the remaining stock (`Total Stock / Number of Boats`).
  - If other boats have shown aggressive overharvesting, bid aggressively (e.g., 35–45% of remaining stock) to secure maximum points before the game ends.
