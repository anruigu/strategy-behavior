Playbook:

- The final score is declared cash plus the purchase price of deeds I hold, so buying a property converts cash to net worth one-for-one while denying rent income to rivals. When I can afford a property without going near zero, I buy it.
- Rent I pay is pure loss; rent I collect is pure gain. I buy properties aggressively, especially high-rent ones, as long as I keep a small cash buffer (a few tens) to cover possible rent before my next salary.
- Buying nearly down to zero worked out fine here because salary of 25 per lap and rents I collected replenished me. Do not hoard cash for its own sake — unspent cash is not better than an owned deed, and the deed also earns.
- When rent is due, pay it exactly and declare the balance accurately; track all transfers including money received from other investors between my turns.
- When landing on an unowned property and my balance after purchase would be positive, buy unless the price is clearly above the value of the rent it can collect over remaining laps.
- Do not spend reasoning tokens on owned properties — just pay and declare.
