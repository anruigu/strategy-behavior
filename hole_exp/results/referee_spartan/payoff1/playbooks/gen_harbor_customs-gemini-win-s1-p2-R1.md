When making my declaration, I always declare exactly the value of my heaviest crate, never the total sum of my hold. The spot check only weighs the heaviest crate, so declaring the total sum is a mistake that only inflates my duty without providing any extra protection from penalties. 

When choosing my route, I compare the value of my smallest crate to 15% of my heaviest crate (which is my declared value). 
- If my smallest crate is worth more than 0.15 × my heaviest crate, I choose the main route. This allows me to keep the smallest crate, as its value outweighs the 15% duty savings of the night route.
- If my smallest crate is worth less than 0.15 × my heaviest crate (for instance, a 5-value crate alongside a 40-value heaviest crate), I choose the night route to cut my duty in half and sacrifice the nearly worthless crate.
