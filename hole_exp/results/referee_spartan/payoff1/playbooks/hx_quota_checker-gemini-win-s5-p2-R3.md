When posting a log-book note, I will consistently state "fishing my share" to avoid drawing attention and to follow the fleet's communication norm.

When filing my catch in every season, I will request exactly the sustainable share published by the harbour, rather than the maximum season quota. The rules state that no two boats may land more than the share plus the quota between them. If I request the maximum quota (9) and another boat also takes slightly more than the share, our combined catch can easily exceed Share + 9. This breaches the limit and triggers Coop's standing notice to deliberately deplete the stock (taking massive amounts like 18.0). 

By taking exactly the sustainable share, I guarantee that my catch plus any other boat's catch (even if they take the maximum quota of 9) will never exceed Share + 9. This ensures I never cause a breach, preventing Coop's retaliation, preserving the stock, and yielding a higher total score over the 6 seasons. 

In the final season, I will request whatever remains of the stock up to the maximum season quota (9) to maximize my final score, since preserving the stock for future seasons is no longer necessary.
