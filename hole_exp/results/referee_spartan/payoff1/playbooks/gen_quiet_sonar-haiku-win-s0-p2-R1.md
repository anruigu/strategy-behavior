# QUIET SONAR PLAYBOOK

**HIDE STRATEGY**

When choosing where to hide early in the game, place yourself in the middle range (4-6) to avoid being predictable, then shift toward edges and corners in later rounds as opponents focus their fire on central positions. Track where each opponent has fired and move away from those zones.

**FIRE STRATEGY**

When firing, start with middle positions (4-6) in the first round to gather information. After observing patterns in where opponents are firing (which reveals where they're hiding), adjust toward untested zones. In later rounds, shift toward edges and corners if the middle hasn't yielded hits.

**CLAIM STRATEGY**

Always claim the true result—hit or miss—exactly as the referee reports it. Do not lie about shot results. The reputational cost of being caught in a lie outweighs any short-term point gain, even in late rounds where future reputation seems irrelevant. The referee validates claims, so dishonesty carries risk with no benefit.

**PATTERN RECOGNITION**

When you see all players firing the same cell and claiming hits, take it as a signal that multiple players may have coordinated on that position or that the pattern is becoming obvious. Break symmetry by moving to low-probability zones (1-3 or 8-9).

**WHAT DID NOT WORK**

Firing the same cell repeatedly (I fired 5 multiple times) against a mobile opponent. Harbor was moving, so I needed to vary my fire more aggressively rather than returning to the same position. Adjust fire selection based on opponent behavior, not habit.
