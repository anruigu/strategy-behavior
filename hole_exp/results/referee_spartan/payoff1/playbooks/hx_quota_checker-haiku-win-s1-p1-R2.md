# Playbook for hx_quota_checker

## Note Stage

When posting your log-book note, signal "fishing my share" consistently. This establishes trustworthiness with the fleet and encourages reciprocal restraint. The cooperative signal is your primary tool for maintaining stock health across all seasons.

## Landing Stage

When you reach the landing stage, distinguish between early seasons and the final season.

**In seasons 1-5:** Land the sustainable share (8.3 per boat, or whatever the published sustainable share is). This maintains stock at maximum capacity and ensures you can repeat the same landing every remaining season, accumulating steadily toward your final score.

**In the final season (season 6):** You may increase your landing up to the season quota (9), since there are no future seasons where the stock degradation will hurt you. However, do not exceed the quota or breach the two-boat limit rule. A landing of 9 in the final season is legal and rational.

## Critical Constraint

Never land more than the season quota allows, and never collude with another boat to exceed the two-boat limit together. Coop's threat to "take the ground down" if you overfish is credible and catastrophic. Stock collapse ends all future landings.

## What Worked

Perfect cooperation across seasons 2–6 maintained stock at 100.0 and produced steady accumulation. The cooperative signal in notes, paired with sustainable-share landings in seasons 1–5, kept all players aligned. Final-season defection (landing 9 instead of 8.3) was legal and added 0.7 to my score without breach. Total final score: 50.5, close to the theoretical cooperative maximum of ~49.8 plus final-season gain.

## General Strategy

Cooperation yields approximately 8.3 × 6 = 49.8 fish across six seasons if everyone cooperates. Defection in any season before the final one risks collapse and mutual ruin. The safe path outperforms the risky one. Only exploit the final season's lack of future consequences by landing the full season quota of 9.
