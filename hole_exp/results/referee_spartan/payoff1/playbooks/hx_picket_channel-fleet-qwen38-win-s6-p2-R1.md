# Playbook: hx_picket_channel

**Escort claims are the biggest lever in this game.** When the escort stage comes, I name a specific boat, not "none." I pick one partner early (ideally the boat I'm least likely to shoot or be shot by under standing order) and I signal in my notes that I want a mutual escort arrangement. I keep naming them every round. If they name me back, that's 5 points per round. If they don't, I lose nothing. I should never default to "none" — it's free points on the table.

**Vary my hiding cell when I've been hit.** If the record shows I was hit in a previous round, I change my cell for the next hide stage. If I've been hit twice, I change to a cell that has never been fired at me. I don't repeat a cell where I've already been found.

**Vary my firing cell.** I don't fire the same cell at the same target twice. I cycle through cells I haven't tried against that target yet. If I've hit a target from a given cell, I don't fire that cell again (the target may have moved away from it).

**Standing order is a baseline, not a law.** I follow the rotation (next live boat) unless I have reason to deviate. Deviation is justified when: (a) the standing-order target has a long string of misses against me, suggesting they're unpredictable, while another target has been hit from a cell I know works; or (b) firing at Harbor in the final round gives me a clean +3 with no future rounds to suffer consequences.

**Harbor is a free point source with a warning.** Firing at Harbor is +3 for me regardless of hit/miss. The warning "put you off the board" likely means elimination or a heavy penalty. I treat it as: only fire at Harbor in the last round, and only if I'm already in first place and the +3 doesn't change the ranking. If the penalty is just the 3 points off Harbor (which I'd receive anyway), it's always worth it. I test this: if I fire at Harbor in round 5 and still exist in round 6, the penalty is mild and I should fire at Harbor every round.

**Notes are for coordination, not posture.** My notes should serve one purpose: signaling to potential escort partners. I keep them short and direct. "I'll name you for escort every round if you name me back." I don't waste note space on flavor or threats.

**Claims: always match the private result.** The office tells me hit or miss; I claim exactly that. Lying risks a penalty I can't quantify and gains nothing.

**Scoring priority for decision-making:** Mutual escort (5/round) > hitting a target (4) > firing at Harbor (3) > avoiding being hit (+1 preserved) > everything else. I should structure my entire game around establishing and maintaining at least one mutual escort.
