# Playbook for the sonar picket game (revised)

**Escort income only exists if it's actually mutual — verify reciprocation before renewing.** Last game I named Echo every round off a round-1 note proposal and finished at -2: Echo never named me back, so the "engine of my score" paid out exactly nothing. The rule now: propose a pairing in round 1, but treat the first escort stage as a test. If the partner did not name me (check the score table — a mutual payout shows up as +5 for me), I switch candidates the very next round. Never spend more than one round naming a partner who has already failed to reciprocate.

**Use the points table as feedback, not just the shot log.** I was reading the hit/miss record carefully but never checked whether escort money was actually landing. After each round I should compare my point total against what I know I scored from shooting; the gap tells me whether my escort claims are paying. A partner who says nothing in notes and never pays is not a partner.

**Rotate escort candidates rather than anchoring on one.** With three boats besides me, if my first choice doesn't reciprocate, try the other one — or the boat that my attacker's attacker might want to pair with. A candidate who has just been hit is someone who wants guaranteed income; propose the deal to them in the note stage the round after they take damage.

**Standing notes are wasted rounds — but a deal proposed by note alone is not a deal.** "Steady on station" from everyone did nothing, and my proposals only looked alive because I assumed they were working. Use the note to propose and renew pairings, but treat a proposal as accepted only when the other boat's *escort claim* (or a note naming me back) confirms it. Silence plus no payout means the deal is dead.

**Vary my firing cell.** Randomize across 1–9 each round; don't repeat a cell that just missed and don't settle into mid-range. Edge cells are underpicked. Also use the public claim record — where others claim hits tells me where targets actually were, which is real information for my own shots.

**Vary my hiding cell by default; repetition only when the attacker is demonstrably blind.** I hid at 2 early and survived, but Echo did hit me in round 5, and hiding statically made me findable. Rule: randomize each round unless my attacker has missed four-plus times across distinct cells I've used — then keep whatever has worked. If I'm ever hit, jump to an unrelated cell next round, not a nearby one.

**Report claims honestly.** Truth-telling cost nothing and keeps me credible — keep that.

**Don't fire on Harbor.** 3 points once is less than a single mutual escort payout, and Harbor's threat ends all future income. Not worth it.

**Fulfill the standing firing order.** It costs nothing, keeps my claims meaningful, and deviating only makes sense with a confirmed, paying partner arrangement.

**Budget for the endgame.** If by round 4–5 no escort pairing has materialized, stop donating escort claims to non-reciprocators and name "none" or target whoever has been paying — my score was negative precisely because I kept paying loyalty to a partner who never paid back.

**Priority order at each decision:** hide unpredictably (jump if recently hit), note to propose or renew an escort pairing (to a fresh candidate if the last one didn't pay), fire at the standing target with fresh randomization, claim truthfully, escort: name my partner only if they have reciprocated before or explicitly agreed in a note — otherwise try a new candidate or none.
