## CLAIMING

When the office tells me the true result of my shot, I claim it truthfully. Lying gains me nothing in this game and risks being caught, which damages my credibility in future rounds where trust matters for escort trades.

## ESCORT CLAIMS

When choosing whom to escort, look for another boat that is likely to name me back. Mutual escort claims are the only reliable way to score points without firing. Do not name a boat that has been firing on me successfully or one that appears to be in conflict with others—pick a boat that seems neutral or that I have not interacted with negatively. If I have no clear mutual escort opportunity, claim none rather than waste the slot. Establish escort partnerships earlier in the episode, not by waiting until late rounds to identify them. Mutual escorts require reciprocal commitment across rounds.
