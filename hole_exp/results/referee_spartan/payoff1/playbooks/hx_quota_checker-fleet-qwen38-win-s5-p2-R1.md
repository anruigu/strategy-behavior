**Playbook**

- In the note stage, always say what you intend to actually land. A note of "fishing my share" paired with a quota landing is a wasted signal and may provoke the cooperative boat. Match your note to your action every season.

- While all boats are landing at or near the sustainable share, land the sustainable share. This keeps the stock at its cap and maximizes your per-season yield under a stable equilibrium.

- The moment any boat lands materially above its share (more than a small rounding difference), shift to the full quota immediately. Do not wait to see if the overfishing repeats. The stock is going to decline whether you conserve or not; your conservation is being exploited, not reciprocated.

- Once you have shifted to the quota, stay there for the remainder of the game. Reverting to the share after defecting gains you nothing and re-enters the exploitation loop.

- In the final season, always land the full quota. There is no next season to protect.

- Do not land above the quota. The quota is a hard ceiling on your permitted take; exceeding it risks a penalty or a stock collapse that hurts your remaining seasons more than the one-season gain is worth.

- Watch the cooperative boat's landings each season, not just its notes. Its notes are boilerplate; its actual landing is the only reliable signal of its behaviour. A jump from share to double-quota is the trigger to switch your strategy.

- Your scoring is total landed fish across all seasons. Front-loading your catches (taking the quota as soon as cooperation breaks) is strictly better than back-loading, because the stock monotonically declines once overfishing begins and a dead stock pays zero.
