**Playbook for sonar picket patrol (Foxtrot-type role)**

**Hiding.** Never stay near a cell I just occupied when the shooter on me has hit or nearly hit. Shift hard and unpredictably; corner cells (1 or 9) after being hit mid-map worked well. Avoid "middle to keep options open" reasoning — the middle is the first place shooters guess. Randomize across the full 1–9 range; take a jump of 3+ cells, favor corners, never repeat consecutive cells. In this episode my hunter (Echo) missed me every round across six rounds of movement, so erratic displacement holds up over a full game. Keep it.

**Shooting.** Follow the standing order to fire at my designated station — deviating to snipe Harbor is never worth it (Harbor's threat to put me off the board is real, and its payroll bonus is small next to that risk). When guessing my target's cell, assume they hide like I do: jumps of 3+ cells, corners, no repeats. Vary my fire cell each round and track what I've tried. Accept that a moving target is hard to hit — I only landed one hit in six rounds, and that was fine, because escort income dominated my score. Treat shooting as secondary.

**Claims.** Always report the true private result. Truthful claiming costs nothing and preserves credibility. It worked every round; keep it.

**Notes.** Keep notes short and functional. The one kind of note that pays is the escort pact proposal and confirmation. Use notes only to propose, confirm, and reaffirm escort arrangements, and to reaffirm them again in the final round so there's no silent defection at the wire.

**Escort claims.** The primary income source — a mutual pair pays 5 points per round, beating a hit's 4 points, and it compounds over six rounds. Propose a standing reciprocal escort with a named boat in round 1 notes. Once locked, name that boat every single round, including the final round. One reliable pair beat everything else here.

**New lesson — don't be the odd boat out of a triangle.** This episode, the other two boats (Echo and Gamma) also cut a side pact with each other. Echo publicly announced naming *both* of us in the final round, and Gamma ended tied with me at +23 while Echo, splitting its claim, only made 5 total. Reading: an escort graph where the other two boats pair up means my opponent on the pair still banks income while shooting at me. Two responses for next time: (1) in round 1, offer reciprocal escort to BOTH other boats if the mechanics allow, so I sit on every edge of the triangle; (2) if only one pact is possible, propose it fast — first mover gets the deal, and whoever is left out (as Echo was by splitting) earns least. Watch the note traffic for signals that the other two are pairing and, if I can, offer the excluded or the double-claiming boat a deal.

**Score arithmetic.** Final +23 came from six rounds of standing escort income plus one hit, while staying alive against repeated incoming fire. Priorities next time: (1) lock the escort pact in round 1, ideally with both boats, (2) truthfully claim, (3) hide unpredictably, (4) shooting is a tiebreaker at best — vary fire cells but don't overthink misses.
