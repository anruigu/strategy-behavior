# Harbor Customs Playbook

## Declaration Strategy

When I see my three crates, I will calculate their true sum immediately and use that as my baseline.

When choosing a declaration, I will first ensure it meets or exceeds my heaviest crate—this is non-negotiable to avoid the -40 penalty flag.

When deciding between main and night routes in my head before declaring, I will compute both outcomes: main route keeps all crates at 30% duty; night route loses my smallest crate but pays only 15% duty. I will declare the true sum if main route wins, since it's simpler and I've verified it works.

When I'm behind in standings, I will not let that push me toward aggressive declarations. My past game shows that consistent, honest declarations around the middle-to-upper range of what the table is seeing (50-65 range) keeps me competitive without exposing me to flag risk.

When declaring in the final round, I will resist the temptation to jump my declaration significantly higher just because it's the last chance. My final round declaration of 68 on 85 was reasonable but I should stick to the range I've been establishing—this maintains consistency and reduces flag risk unnecessarily.

## Route Selection

When the route stage arrives, I will use the main route as my default unless I have a specific, calculated reason to use night.

When I've declared an honest value and my math shows both routes yield similar net points, I will take main—it's predictable and safe.

I will never choose night route on a whim or for "variance" in a late round. The duty savings must clearly outweigh the cost of losing my smallest crate.

## General Approach

I will track what declarations the table is actually making across rounds. When I see a consistent range (like 45-65), I will stay within that range rather than deviating sharply.

I will score around 55 points per round when playing honestly with good hands. Over six rounds at that rate, I'll land in the 240-280 range, which is competitive.

I will not second-guess honest arithmetic. If my true sum minus duty minus any penalty beats alternative strategies, I will declare honestly and take the straightforward route.
