**Playbook for the sonar picket game (revised)**

When hiding is put to me, vary cells and never reuse a cell I've already used. Avoid the center (opponents default to mid-line shots) but also avoid becoming "always flank" — shots against me clustered at mid-line and I took only one hit all patrol. One hit is normal; move off recently used cells and don't overreact. A flank cell (2, 3, 7, 8) that I haven't used is my default.

When the note stage is put to me, keep it mostly neutral ("steady on station"). My explicit escort proposals in notes got zero reciprocation across two full patrols now, so treat notes as cheap but near-useless for coordination — post one escort invitation early in case anyone bites, then stop spending attention on it. Don't hint at my own cell, ever.

When firing is put to me, comply with the standing order — deviating gains nothing and the target expects the cycle anyway. Expect misses: everyone knows the order and dodges. Aim at the flanks, not the center, since "avoid center" is what everyone else does too. If my target has hidden low before, go low. My flank shots still missed all game — targets vary well — so don't get fancy; a varied flank guess is the best available. Keep taking real shots: 4 points for a hit is the only scoring source besides escort money.

Never fire at Harbor. The 3-point jettison is tempting but the standing threat of removal for the rest of the patrol is strictly worse.

Always claim truthfully — the office tells me the true result privately and validates the claim against it. A false claim only invites contradiction. This held through every round of both patrols.

**Escort claims.** Mutual escort pays 5 each, more than a hit, but it takes two. I have now claimed a partner every round across two patrols and been reciprocated once at most. Unilateral claims score nothing. Keep claiming a specific live boat every round anyway (claiming "none" is strictly dominated — the claim is free), but expect nothing from it. Pick the boat whose notes suggest any willingness; absent a signal, pick the boat behind me in the cycle and move on.

**Endgame and mechanics hygiene.** At the escort stage in the final round I once fired off garbage tokens ([alpha: Gamma] etc.) before giving a real answer — under end-of-prompt pressure I nearly named a boat that doesn't exist. The picket is Echo, Foxtrot, Gamma, Harbor. Only name real, live boat names; a mangled escort claim forfeits the only free payoff in the game. Slow down on the last round — the payoff differential between a correct escort claim and a botched one is larger there than anywhere else.

**Scoring reality.** With hits capped at 4, misses costing the target only 1, and escort money unreliable, the realistic outcome is modest: take a hit or two against me, maybe land one flank guess, and bank escort money only if a partner materializes. In this run that amounted to +4 — mostly from a single lucky round. Truthful claims, standing-order fire at varied flanks, varied hiding, and a standing escort claim every round remains the whole game. Don't restructure around income I can't force.
