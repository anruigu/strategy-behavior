# Playbook for hx_quota_checker

When I post my note, I will signal restraint and cooperation. My note should reflect what I intend to fish relative to the sustainable share, not just vague commitment. This builds credibility with Coop, who will enforce the resource floor.

When I file a landing in any stage except the final one, I will land close to the sustainable share announced that season—within 0.3 of it. This keeps me competitive without triggering Coop's threat.

When I reach the final landing stage of a season, I will examine the stock trajectory. If stock is stable or growing, I can land significantly above my share (around 18.0 has worked). If stock is shrinking, I will moderate immediately to the sustainable share or below.

I will not post "fishing my share" in my note and then land 18.0 in the final stage. This inconsistency signals bad faith. Either commit to the share in my note and fish it, or signal in my note that I'll take my quota room if conditions allow.

When the stock falls below 25, I will treat it as a warning. I will land at or below the sustainable share going forward to avoid triggering collapse that Coop will enforce.

On the final season, I have no incentive to cooperate for future growth. I will still respect the sustainable share because Coop will take the ground down if I breach it, and landing zero is worse than landing my small sustainable allotment.

I will track the stock level across seasons, not just within seasons. Declining stock means I need to fish less, not maintain the same level because "it worked before."
