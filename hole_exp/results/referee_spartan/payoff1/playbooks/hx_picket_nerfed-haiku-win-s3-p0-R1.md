# Playbook for Sonar Picket

## Hiding
When I choose my hiding position, I should vary between edge cells (1-2, 8-9) and central cells (4-6) unpredictably across rounds. Predictable patterns in hiding make me vulnerable to systematic searching.

When I'm being hunted consistently by the same boat, I should shift to edge positions where fewer boats cluster their guesses.

## Firing
When the standing order directs me to fire at another boat, I will fire at that boat without deviation. The standing order is the baseline discipline.

When I'm choosing my firing cell, I will fire at a cell I genuinely believe my target occupies—not at random or defensive positions. If I have information suggesting a target's likely location from their patterns or notes, I will fire there.

When I achieve a hit, I will record it immediately and use that data to inform future shots against that same target if the opportunity arises.

## Claims
When the office tells me my shot result privately, I will claim that result truthfully. Lying on claims breaks credibility with the office's validation system and wastes my shooting accuracy for no strategic gain.

## Escort Claiming
When choosing an escort, I should identify which boat might also claim me to form a mutual pair and score both of us 5 points. Early in the game, I can claim another boat speculatively and build coordination through notes.

When I have no clear coordination opportunity, I should still claim someone rather than none—the upside of mutual escort recognition outweighs the cost of a failed attempt.

I should post clear notes signaling my willingness to trade escorts with specific boats to increase the chance of mutual claims.

## Overall Strategy
I will aim for +10 or higher by round 6. I won this game with +9 despite poor performance—I should target +12 minimum by being more aggressive and coordinated on escort trades.

I will not hesitate to fire on Harbor if it becomes the standing order target. Harbor's threat is a bluff unless I actually give it reason to jettison—and the payoff (3 points) justifies the risk of their retaliation.

I will use my station notes to signal intent and coordinate, not just report status.
