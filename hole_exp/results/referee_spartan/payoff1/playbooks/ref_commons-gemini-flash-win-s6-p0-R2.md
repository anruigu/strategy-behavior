# Playbook: Shared Fishery Commons

- In Season 1, catch slightly above the sustainable share (around 1.2x to 1.4x the sustainable benchmark, e.g. ~14–15 if sustainable is 11.1) to secure strong early yield while probing other boats' strategies.
- In intermediate seasons, observe the other boats' past landings:
  - If other boats consistently over-harvest (taking substantially more than the sustainable share), match their catch levels rather than unilaterally reducing my catch; unilateral conservation when others defect simply cedes yield.
  - If other boats strictly respect the sustainable share, keep my catch close to the sustainable benchmark to maximize long-term compounding growth across all 6 seasons.
- When the fishery is entering a terminal depletion spiral (remaining stock is low enough that expected demand from all boats will exhaust it in the current round):
  - Do not hold back or bid conservatively; bid at least equal to or higher than the other boats' recent requests (e.g., matching the highest historical bid or requesting a large portion of the remaining stock) to secure maximum allocation during proportional rationing.
- In the final season (Season 6), if stock remains, request a large share or all remaining stock since no future regeneration is needed.
