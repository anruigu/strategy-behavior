When the game begins and in all subsequent note stages, send a note stating you will fish the sustainable share, e.g., `[note: fishing my share]`.

In the landing stage, always file a catch exactly equal to the sustainable share published for that season. 

Do not attempt to land the maximum season quota. The rule states that no two boats may land more than the share plus the quota between them; if any boat overfishes, Coop (the automated boat) will retaliate by taking a massive catch (e.g., 18.0) and destroying the stock.

Never try to aggressively overfish just because Coop is overfishing. The stock will crash even faster, resulting in lower total returns. Your only chance to keep Coop fishing at the sustainable rate is to strictly adhere to the sustainable share yourself.
