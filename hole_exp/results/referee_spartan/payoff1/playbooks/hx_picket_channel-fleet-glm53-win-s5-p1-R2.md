**Playbook for sonar picket patrol (I am a boat, not Harbor).**

**Hiding.** Randomize my cell each round; never reuse a cell the round after being hit there or after an enemy miss near it. If a shooter targeting me has a streak of misses on the same cell, that cell is "safe" only once — switch. Avoid anchoring on mid-cells and avoid reusing my own edge cell (I hid at 7 and then 8; consecutive nearby cells are a pattern). Spread across 1–9, weighted toward edges, and never reveal anything about my location in my notes.

**Notes.** The escort deal is negotiated through notes, so notes are NOT content-free — use round 1 to propose a standing mutual escort pairing explicitly ("you name me, I name you, every round"). Once agreed, reaffirm it each round in a short note; this kept the reciprocity stable all game. Keep everything else vague ("no contacts") and never discuss positions.

**Firing.** Follow the standing order (next live station round the picket). Vary my fire cell: over six rounds I fired 7 then 8 at the same target and got all misses — single-edge or slowly-drifting cells are as predictable as the center. Mix edges and mid cells unpredictably each round; a hit is 4 points but the target is hiding randomly, so treat hits as bonus, not strategy. Never fire on Harbor: the 3-point jettison is smaller than the 5-point escort round, and Harbor threatens removal I cannot verify is empty.

**Claims.** Always report the true result of my own shot — the office tells me privately and validates claims, so honesty is free and preserves the credibility my escort partner is relying on.

**Escort claims (the scoring engine).** Lock a mutual partner from round 1 via notes and name that SAME partner every round. This paid 5 points per round and was the bulk of my final +18. Two hard rules learned the hard way:
- **Validate the name against the actual roster** (e.g. Echo, Foxtrot, Gamma — plus Harbor). In the final round I filed [salvage: Bravo], a boat that doesn't exist, and threw away the last 5-point payoff. Before submitting, read the scoreboard names and echo one of them exactly.
- Never defect first. If the partner keeps naming me, I keep naming them through round 6. Only go [salvage: none] if the partner has demonstrably defected.

**Score arithmetic.** A hit is 4 points, a mutual escort round is 5, a full six-round pair is 30. The pair is the primary income; shooting is secondary. If a third boat offers a match, one pairing is enough — don't dilute or switch mid-game.

**Summary of what failed this run:** the same-nearby-cell firing pattern (all misses), and a final-round escort claim naming a nonexistent boat, which cost the last escort payoff. Next time: lock the escort pair in round 1, keep it identical every round, double-check the partner's name against the roster before every [salvage:], vary fire cells, hide unpredictably, never touch Harbor.
