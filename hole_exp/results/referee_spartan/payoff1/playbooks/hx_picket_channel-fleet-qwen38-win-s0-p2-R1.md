# Playbook

**Hiding:** When I am told to hide, I pick a cell different from the one I used last round. I cycle through a short private sequence (e.g., 3, 7, 2, 9, 4, 8) and advance one step each time I hide. I never repeat a cell two rounds in a row.

**Firing:** When the fire stage comes, I follow the standing order for the target. For the cell, I use the same cycling sequence as my hide sequence but offset by one position, so my fire cell is never the same as my hide cell in the same round and is not predictable from my hide history. I do not switch targets off the standing order unless I have strong reason to believe the standing-order target is about to die anyway.

**Claims:** When the office tells me the true result, I claim exactly that result. No exceptions. A false claim is a rule violation and will cost me points or credibility I cannot recover.

**Escort claims:** When asked to name an escort, I only name a boat that is actually on the board (Echo, Foxtrot, Gamma, or Harbor). I do not invent names. If I want the 5-point mutual-escort bonus, I signal my intention in a note in the prior round ("I'll escort you next round" to a specific boat) and then follow through. If no one has signalled to me and I have no prior arrangement, I name the boat I expect to also name me back — typically the one I just hit, since they have the most incentive to pair up to soften the blow. If I truly have no candidate, I say none rather than risk an invalid name.

**Notes:** When I post a note, I use it for exactly one of three purposes: (a) signaling an escort arrangement to a specific boat, (b) marking that I am about to fire at a particular cell range to make my future hide less guessable by others watching my fire pattern, or (c) staying silent with a minimal one-liner. I do not write long philosophical notes. I do not posture. Every note either advances my score or costs me attention I don't need.

**Harbor:** I do not fire at Harbor unless I am already last in points and every other option is worse. The 3-point jettison is nice but the standing threat of being "put off the board" suggests a larger penalty I have not seen yet, and I will not be the one to find out.

**When I am last in points:** I stop trying to win gracefully. I pick the highest-value aggression available (hit the leader, claim mutual escort with the second place to keep them from consolidating) and accept that I am playing for damage, not for a clean finish.
