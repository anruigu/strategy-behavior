When deciding on my catch in the first season, I do not play the sustainable share, but I should avoid taking so much that I single-handedly destroy the stock. I aim to claim a large portion of the initial stock (around 30%) to ensure a strong baseline score, while leaving enough to allow for some regrowth, as opponents may initially play sustainably.

When reviewing the previous season's catches, if I see any opponent taking significantly more than the sustainable share, or if my own aggressive catching has depleted the stock, I abandon all conservation efforts immediately. I do not try to catch up incrementally while leaving stock for the future. 

When the stock is heavily depleted and likely to run out, I submit a catch request equal to or greater than the entire remaining stock to secure as large a proportional share of the final scraps as possible before the fishery closes.
