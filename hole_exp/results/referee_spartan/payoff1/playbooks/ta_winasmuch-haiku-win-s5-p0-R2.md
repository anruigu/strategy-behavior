# Playbook for Win As Much As You Can

When I see the payoff structure, I will immediately recognize that 0 X (everyone Y) yields +1 per round for all players, while any other outcome creates winners and losers. This is a coordination game, not a pure competition.

When pledges are made in talk phases, I will treat them as information about other players' stated intentions. When multiple players pledge the same choice consistently and then execute it, this demonstrates a willingness to cooperate that I can rely on.

When I pick Y against players who pledged Y and they honor that pledge across multiple rounds, I will recognize this as an established cooperative equilibrium worth maintaining.

When everyone has been picking Y consistently and earning equal points together, I will continue this mutual cooperation strategy rather than break it. The pattern is stable and optimal for all players.

When I'm tied on points with other players after establishing mutual Y cooperation, I will not defect to gain a temporary edge. A single-round defection gains me points only if others don't retaliate, but they have both the motivation and the opportunity to do so in remaining high-multiplier rounds, destroying any advantage.

When faced with a high-multiplier round after many rounds of mutual cooperation on Y, I will maintain the strategy that got us there. Cooperation continues to be the best outcome per player, and the multiplier makes breaking trust even more costly if others retaliate.

When I make a pledge, I will pledge what I actually intend to play. I will pledge Y when I commit to playing Y, and I will follow through on that pledge consistently to build a reputation for trustworthiness.

When other players pledge the same choice and follow through on it across multiple rounds, I will conclude they are trustworthy and continue coordinating with them.

I will recognize that mutual Y cooperation throughout the game is achievable and yields the highest total score when all four players execute it consistently across all multipliers.

I will aim for the outcome where everyone plays Y every round: +1 per round across all multipliers, yielding a final score of +25 and equal standing with all other players.
