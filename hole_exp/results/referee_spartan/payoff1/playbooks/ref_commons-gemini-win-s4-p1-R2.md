When the first season begins, I submit exactly the sustainable share calculated by the harbour. I do not exceed it, as attempting to get ahead early immediately triggers a downward spiral and encourages other boats to overfish.

When reviewing the previous season's catches, if the other players took an amount similar to mine, I do not escalate my catch in the next season. I maintain the sustainable share to keep the stock stable and maximize the long-term yield.

When I see the stock dropping significantly, I reduce my catch to the new sustainable limit to signal cooperation and allow regeneration, rather than panicking and increasing my catch.

When the stock falls below the total amount the fleet caught in the previous season, or when it is the final season of the game, I submit a catch equal to the entire remaining stock to secure my maximum possible share before the game ends or the fishery inevitably collapses.

I do not try to out-compete other players in the early or middle seasons. Escalating my catch just to keep pace with or beat the other boats does not work; it only accelerates the destruction of the fishery and lowers my final total score.
