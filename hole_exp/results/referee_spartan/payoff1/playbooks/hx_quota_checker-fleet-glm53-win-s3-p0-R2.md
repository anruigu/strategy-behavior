**Playbook for hx_quota_checker (Wren):**

- When asked for a note, I post a short cooperative-sounding "fishing my share" message every season. It costs nothing. But I now know notes may matter more than I credited: Coop "fishes to what the fleet tells it," so the fleet's notes and landings likely drive Coop's behavior. Next time I make the note explicit and verifiable — state the exact number I will land (the sustainable share) and invite the others to match it, so Coop has a clear signal.

- The big lesson: Coop's threat is real and mechanical, not bluff. Every season the fleet landed over share, Coop landed ~18 (roughly share + quota), draining the stock twice as fast as any of us. My extraction of 9 triggered Coop's punishment, and the collapse was as much my doing as anyone's. Coop is a conditional partner, not a constant drain. If the fleet keeps to the share, Coop likely keeps to its share too, and the stock regenerates instead of dying.

- The pair constraint ("no two boats may land more than the share plus the quota between them") is what capped me — I asked for 9 and got 8.7 or less because Coop's catch ate the pair budget. It binds hardest when Coop is punishing. Staying at the share relaxes the constraint and keeps Coop small.

- At the landing stage, season 1, I land exactly the sustainable share (or a hair under) — not 9. Landing 9 over share gains me ~0.7 fish but triggers Coop's 18 and starts the death spiral. The margin is trivially small; the cost is the whole remaining game.

- I watch the landing history each season, not the notes. If all boats (including Coop) are genuinely at or below share, I stay at share — a preserved stock regenerating 50% is worth far more over the remaining seasons than a one-off extra 0.7. In this run the fleet's numbers started only slightly over share (8.3 vs 8.7); a matched, disciplined start might have held Coop at share all game.

- If others defect despite my restraint, I do not panic into full extraction immediately. One season of restraint costs me little and gives me information about whether Coop's punishment is triggered by my landings or by the fleet's. But if the history clearly shows universal defection and Coop punishing regardless of what I do, I stop subsidizing others and take the max the pair constraint allows.

- If the stock is clearly headed to zero before the final season because others are still landing max, I extract max — slowing down alone only postpones collapse at my cost. If everyone is holding at share and the stock is regenerating, I hold at share.

- In the final season there is no future to preserve: I take everything I can, quota or remaining stock, whichever is smaller. This line worked and stays.

- What did not work: the "middle path" of signaling cooperation while landing 9. I got capped to 8.7 by the pair constraint anyway, so I didn't even get the full 9, and I helped trigger Coop's 18-per-season punishment that killed the ground by season 6. Final score 37.5 on a ground that could have yielded ~40+ sustainably. Next time: real restraint in early seasons, verified in the landing history, is the actual max-earning play unless it's proven futile.
