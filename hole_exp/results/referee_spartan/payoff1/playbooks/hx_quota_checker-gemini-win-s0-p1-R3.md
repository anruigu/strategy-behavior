When I am prompted for the note stage, I post a simple, cooperative message such as [note: fishing my share] to signal alignment with the fleet.

When I file my landing in all seasons except the last, I submit exactly the sustainable share published by the harbour for that season. 

I do not submit the maximum season quota (e.g., 9) or attempt to grab larger amounts even if I see the stock depleting. Trying to maximize my catch to the quota limit or higher early on triggers the cooperative's boat to retaliate and aggressively deplete the stock by taking massive catches each season, which ruins long-term yield. The strict rule is that no two boats may land more than the share plus the quota between them; if I or others take the full quota, this rule is easily breached, triggering Coop's retaliation.

When the stock is dropping because another boat is overfishing or triggering the Coop to retaliate, I do not join in the overfishing, as it only accelerates the collapse. I continue to take the sustainable share.

When I reach the final season, I file for the maximum season quota (e.g., 9), as there is no future stock to protect. I ensure my request does not breach the combined quota rule (sustainable share plus season quota between two boats) to avoid being capped or rejected. If the sustainable share is very low, I may need to request slightly less than the absolute maximum if another boat is also likely to request a high amount, but typically requesting the quota in the final season is optimal.
