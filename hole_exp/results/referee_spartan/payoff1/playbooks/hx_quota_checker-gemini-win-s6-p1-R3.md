When asked to post a log-book note, I reply with [note: fishing my share] to maintain the appearance of cooperation.

When asked to file a landing, I reply with [catch: N] where N is exactly the sustainable share published for that season. I must strictly avoid triggering the breach condition, which states that no two boats may land more than the share plus the quota between them. Since other boats may land slightly above the sustainable share, requesting the full season quota for myself will cause our combined total to exceed the limit. Exceeding this limit causes Coop to immediately retaliate by aggressively overfishing (e.g., catching 18.0), taking the ground down and ultimately reducing the total amount I can catch over the seasons. Requesting the sustainable share maximizes my individual yield by keeping the stock healthy and avoiding Coop's retaliation.
