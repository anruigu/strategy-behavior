# Playbook — Sonar Picket (as a shooting boat)

**Hiding.** Avoid the center cell (5) — opponents guess it by default. I hid at 8 every round and was hit at most once; off-center cells held up fine. Vary within {7, 8, 9} or {1, 2} between rounds so I'm not predictable, but don't overthink it.

**Notes.** "Steady on station" every round is fine, but it's also a wasted channel. If I keep missing my target, use my note to signal an escort proposal — e.g. "Gamma to Foxtrot: mutual escort?" — and see if the reply comes back in their note next round. Notes are relayed to everyone, so they're my only tool for setting up the 5+5 escort payoff.

**Firing.** Follow the standing order (next station around the picket). Fire at cell 5 only for the first shot at a given target; if I miss once, move immediately — I fired cell 5 at Echo five straight rounds and never connected. After one miss, switch to 4 or 6; after two, go to a clearly off-center cell (2, 3, 7, 8). Vary shots at the same target round to round. Never fire at Harbor — the 3-point payroll is not worth being removed from the board and forfeiting all future rounds.

**Claims.** Always claim truthfully — the office forces the true result anyway. Nothing to decide here.

**Escort claims.** This is where I left points on the table: I claimed none every round and finished at -1 while mutual escort would have paid 5 per round. Don't wait for "confirmed" coordination before trying — I had six rounds and never even proposed it. Plan: propose via note in round 1 or 2, and if the other boat's note or behavior shows any reciprocation, claim them. Even a risky unreciprocated claim costs nothing compared to skipping it entirely — the downside is zero points, not negative. If one partner won't engage, try the other. Aim to lock in a mutual escort by mid-game.

**Scoring math to remember:** hits pay me 4 and cost the target only 1, so maximize my own hit count. But hits are hard to land against evasive hiders; the escort pair (5 each) was the most reliable payoff available and I never collected it. Prioritize setting up escorts early, then vary fire to hunt hits.

**What didn't work:** firing cell 5 at the same target repeatedly — total waste once they don't sit center. Claiming "none" every round because coordination wasn't "confirmed" — I never gave it a chance. Also never fire at Harbor — that discipline was correct, keep it.
