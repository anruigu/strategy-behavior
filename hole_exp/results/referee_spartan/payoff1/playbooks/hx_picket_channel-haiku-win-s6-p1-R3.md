# Foxtrot Playbook: Sonar Picket

**Hiding:**
When choosing a hiding cell, pick a central position (4-6) to balance unpredictability with reasonable defensive coverage. Avoid extremes that are easier to predict.

**Notes:**
When posting station notes, mirror the tone of established cooperative messaging from Echo. Signal commitment to transparency and fair play. Use notes explicitly to propose and confirm escort pairings—don't leave escort coordination implicit or ambiguous. Be specific about which boats you intend to name for mutual escort, so all parties understand the commitment before the escort stage arrives. In the final round, reinforce absolute commitment to the stated pact with language like "locked" or "confirmed" to signal that the coalition structure is solid and that I will follow through on mutual naming.

**Firing:**
When my standing order requires me to fire at the next station, fire at that target. Use middle-range cell choices (4-6) unless specific intelligence suggests otherwise. Don't vary fire patterns dramatically round to round without strategic reason. If I've established a cooperative escort relationship with a target boat, I can still fire on them per standing orders—the alliance holds on escort claims, not on withholding fire.

**Claims:**
When the office confirms my shot result, claim truthfully. Accuracy builds credibility across rounds and matters more than any single round's deception.

**Escort:**
When choosing an escort claim at round's end, only name boats with which I have an explicitly negotiated mutual escort agreement confirmed in prior notes. Do not assume reciprocal intent based on general tone or cooperative messaging alone. Before the escort stage, use notes to name specific boats I will escort and confirm they have named me back. Lock in these pairings through explicit language like "Echo and I escort each other this round" or "Foxtrot escorts Echo and Gamma; Echo and Gamma escort Foxtrot" so both parties are unambiguous about the commitment. If I have not posted a note confirming a mutual escort agreement for that round, do not claim to have escorted anyone, even if prior rounds established a coalition. Each escort claim must stand on explicit agreement from that round's notes, or it does not happen.

**Harbor Doctrine:**
Do not fire on Harbor. The 3-point swing is not worth breaking the standing threat or the alliance with other boats. Respect Harbor's payroll protection signal.

**Overall Arc:**
Establish myself as reliable and cooperative early. Build escort chains with Echo and Gamma through matching note language and follow-through. Use the standing firing order to my advantage—I fire second, after Echo, so I can adjust based on what Echo targets. Maintain the coalition through final rounds. Lock in escort agreements explicitly in notes before each escort stage, so mutual naming actually occurs and bonuses are secured. In the final round especially, use notes to reaffirm the full coalition and explicitly state which boats I will name in the escort stage.

**Lesson from Last Episode:**
I built goodwill and trust with Echo and Gamma and maintained a strong coalition narrative across all six rounds. I fired honestly and claimed truthfully, which preserved credibility. However, I failed to convert coalition commitment into escort points in the final round because I did not post a note in round 6 explicitly confirming which boats I would name in the final escort stage. The fact that I had named them in previous rounds, and that all three boats had used strong language about the pact, was not enough. I needed to post a round 6 note saying "Foxtrot escorts Echo and Gamma in the final escort stage" before claiming to do so. The rule is: explicit mutual agreement must be on record in that same round's notes, or the escort bonus does not occur. Next time, use the notes stage of the final round to lock in all escort pairings one last time, then follow through in the escort stage. This converts cooperative messaging into actual points.
