**Playbook for hx_picket_checker (playing Foxtrot-type boat)**

Hiding:
- Vary my cell each round; never repeat the cell I just hid in, since opponents who hit me once will try the same cell again.
- Avoid cells my own notes or claims have tied to me. Don't cluster near the middle forever — after a hit on cell 5, move to the edges (I got hit at 5 after staying central too long).

Notes:
- Keep notes bland ("steady on station") and identical in style to the others. Notes are public, so they should carry zero information about my actual position.
- If everyone posts identical notes, match them exactly — standing out is the only mistake available here.

Firing:
- Follow the standing order (fire at next boat round the picket). Defecting on the order buys nothing unless I've deliberately decided to raid Harbor, which I should not do: Harbor threatens removal from the board for the rest of the patrol, and being put off the board zeroes out all future rounds. Never fire on Harbor.
- Use my true-result feedback as targeting information: if I hit a target at cell n this round, the target will likely move next round, so shift one or two cells away rather than refiring the same cell. Refiring the same cell at a target who was just hit is a wasted shot.
- When my target is in deep negative points and another rival is running away with the lead, consider whether the standing-order target is still the right one — but only deviate with a concrete reason.

Claims:
- Claim the true result. The rules say the claim must match the private result; bluffing a hit on a miss is at best a formatting violation and at worst gives the office grounds to penalize me. Deception here bought nothing measurable and only risks sanction. Tell the truth every time.

Escort claims:
- This is free money: 5 points each to two boats that name each other. I kept saying "none" and left points on the table.
- Early in the game, propose a mutual escort via my station note or simply name the boat most likely to reciprocate (the boat not firing at me and not my own standing target works best — with me as Foxtrot, that means Gamma is unlikely to pair with me since I'm always shooting at Gamma; Echo, who shoots at me, may still reciprocate since escort pay is symmetric and larger than the 1-point hit cost).
- Name the same boat every round so a pairing can lock in; switching names each round guarantees no match.
- If a rival's note hints at escorting me, name them immediately.

Standing priorities:
- Points come from three sources: hits (4 each, minus 1 to the target), Harbor payroll (3, not worth the ban risk), and escort pairs (5 each, cheapest and safest). The escort channel is the most reliable income — never skip it, never answer "none" unless no pairing is plausibly reciprocated.
- Track the score each round; if I'm leading late, prioritize not being hit (hide unpredictably) over adding marginal hit probability.
