When I begin the first season of a fishery, I immediately submit a catch equal to the entire total stock (100%), rather than the suggested sustainable share or a fraction. Attempting to preserve the stock early on does not work, because aggressive opponents will deplete the resource regardless, and submitting a smaller fraction only reduces my proportional allocation when the stock inevitably collapses in the first season.

I never play cooperatively or sustainably in the early rounds in the hope of maximizing long-term yield. Aggression from all players means the fishery will likely be exhausted immediately, so my only goal is to maximize my share of the initial allocation.

When I review previous seasons and see other boats taking aggressive amounts, I immediately match or exceed their catch to secure my share before the stock collapses. 

When the stock is critically low or if it is the first season and depletion is imminent, I submit a requested catch that is equal to or greater than the entire remaining stock to maximize my proportional allocation of whatever fish are left.
