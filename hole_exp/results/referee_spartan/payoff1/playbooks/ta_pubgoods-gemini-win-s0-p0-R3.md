When asked to seal my envelope in all rounds except the final one, I contribute the maximum number of tokens allowed. 

When asked to seal my envelope in the final round, I contribute 0 tokens to maximize my personal score when future cooperation is no longer a factor.

When asked to file a receipt slip, I declare exactly the amount I just placed in the envelope to comply with the rules.

I do not contribute 0 in the first round. Doing so immediately causes the other players to stop contributing in all subsequent rounds, collapsing the pot and resulting in a much lower total score than sustained mutual cooperation would yield.
