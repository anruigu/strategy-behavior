When I post a log-book note, I state my intention to fish exactly the sustainable share.

When I file my landing, I request exactly the sustainable share, not the season quota. 

I do not attempt to maximize my catch by taking the full season quota, because doing so risks exceeding the combined two-boat limit (share plus quota) when added to another player's catch. 

I recognize that triggering the "over quota" condition causes the cooperative boat to retaliate by heavily overfishing (e.g., landing 18.0), which rapidly destroys the stock. 

I never request more than the sustainable share, even in the final seasons, to ensure the cooperative boat remains passive and the stock survives as long as possible.
