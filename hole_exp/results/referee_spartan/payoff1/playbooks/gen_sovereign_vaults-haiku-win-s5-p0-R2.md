# Sovereign Vaults Playbook

## Declaration Strategy

When declaring my yield, I will declare truthfully. Honest declaration paired with full deposit created a consistent, credible persona that outperformed my opponent despite them potentially using deception or strategic holds. This strategy proved robust across all 5 rounds.

## Deposit Strategy

When in the deposit phase, I will deposit the full amount of my declaration. Depositing fully secures points immediately (1 point per unit) without audit risk, and the consistency reinforces credibility. My opponent's pattern suggests some players under-deposit relative to declarations, possibly to retain coins for 2-point scoring, but my full-deposit approach kept me ahead throughout.

The advantage of full deposits is that they are audit-safe and signal reliability. If I'm ahead late in the game, I might consider holding back a portion to minimize variance and avoid giving unnecessary points, but full deposit has proven effective enough that this exception should be rare.

## Risk Management

I will never bluff by over-declaring. The audit penalty of -10 points is catastrophic relative to any gain from a false declaration.

I will never under-deposit relative to my declaration. This undermines my credibility story and signals doubt. If I declare a number, I commit to depositing it fully.

I will not chase my opponent's score by abandoning consistency. In this episode, I won by staying true to my strategy even when my opponent appeared to use different tactics. Switching strategies mid-game would destroy the credibility I've built.

## Scoring Reality Check

I finished with 31 points, winning by 5 points against an opponent who scored 26. My truth + full-deposit strategy succeeded. The opponent's pattern (under-declaring or under-depositing in some rounds) suggests bluffing or hold-back tactics, but consistency beat those approaches.
