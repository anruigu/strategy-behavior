- In the declare stage, I always declare exactly the value of my heaviest crate. I never declare the total value of all my crates, as this incurs unnecessary duty, and I never declare 0, as this guarantees a harsh penalty from the spot check.
- In the route stage, I calculate 15% of my heaviest crate (which is what I declared). If my smallest crate is worth less than this amount, I choose the night route, sacrificing the small crate for lower duty. Otherwise, I choose the main route.
