# Playbook: hx_quota_checker

**When filing a landing, always file the full season quota (9).** Filing less than 9 is never optimal: the system will clamp your actual landing down to whatever the constraints allow, but it will never clamp it up. Filing 9 guarantees you receive the maximum the system will permit. A filing of 0 is pure lost score with no strategic benefit — never do it.

**Understand what actually caps your landing.** The binding constraint is the pairwise rule: no two boats may land more than (sustainable share + quota) between them. Since the cooperative boats (Wren, Bosk) land close to the share, my effective cap is approximately (share + 9 − their landing) ≈ 9. This means I will almost always land just under 9 (typically 8.7–8.9) regardless of stock level.
