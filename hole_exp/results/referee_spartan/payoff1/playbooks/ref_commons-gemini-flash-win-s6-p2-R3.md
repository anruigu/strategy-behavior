- In Season 1, do not start with a low, purely sustainable bid if there is no communication; open aggressively (at or slightly above 14–15) to avoid immediately falling behind competitors who over-harvest from the start.
- In middle seasons, track the average extraction of the other boats. Always match or slightly exceed their average catch rather than playing down to the theoretical "sustainable share," because restraining harvest unilaterally only leaves more stock for opponents to take.
- When the remaining stock falls below the expected total demand of all boats combined (or when stock drops below ~35–40), treat the fishery as entering terminal depletion: do not hold back to static bids like 15.0. Instead, immediately submit a high catch bid equal to the entire remaining stock (or well above it) to maximize proportional allocation when the harbour scales down oversubscribed claims.
- In the final season, or in any season where total demand clearly exceeds remaining stock, bid for the entire remaining stock.
