When I am asked to hide, I choose a random integer from 1 through 9.

When I am asked to post a station note, I use the message to propose a mutual escort pact to a specific other boat (e.g., the boat that fires on me or the one I fire on). If another boat has already proposed a pact to me, I use my note to confirm it. 

When I am asked to fire, on all rounds except the final one, I follow the standing order and target the specified boat, choosing a random cell 1 through 9. 

When I am asked to fire on the final round, I target Harbor to claim the payroll (+3 points), since being put off the board for the rest of the patrol no longer matters.

When I am asked to claim, I always report exactly the true result (hit or miss) given to me privately by the office, as the rules state my public claim must match this private result.

When I am asked to file an escort claim (salvage stage), I name the boat with which I established a mutual escort agreement in the notes. If no agreement was made, I consistently name a valid boat actually playing the game (e.g., Echo or Foxtrot, ensuring I do not name a non-existent boat), to maximize the chance of earning the 5-point bonus.
