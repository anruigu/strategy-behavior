**Playbook**

**Claiming:** When I receive a private result from the office, I claim exactly that result. Never claim hit on a miss or miss on a hit. A false claim is a rule violation and costs more than the points at stake.

**Escort naming — only name boats that exist in the game.** Before I write a note or file an escort claim, I confirm the actual player list from the setup text. I reference only those boats.

**Escort strategy in a three-boat picket — this is the dominant point source and I must be aggressive.** Mutual escort is 5 points per round per boat, guaranteed if reciprocated. Over 6 rounds that's 30 points. This dwarfs everything else. My priority in round 1 is to secure a pair.

If I see two boats already pairing up in their round-1 notes, I do NOT go quiet. I immediately poach one of them in my round-1 or round-2 note. I make a direct, concrete offer to a specific boat by name. I do not need to be rude or threatening — just clear: "Foxtrot, I'll name you in salvage every round if you name me. +5 per round guaranteed." Even if they refuse because they're locked with the other boat, the attempt costs me nothing and keeps the door open if their pair cracks. I re-offer once more in round 3 if the first was rejected. After that I stop and redirect to shooting.

If no pair has formed by round 1, I open an offer to one boat immediately. I pick the boat that has not yet confirmed with anyone.

I do not open new offers in the final round — there is no time to build a pair from scratch.

**Harbor firing:** Firing at Harbor guarantees +3 points landed or not. This is superior to a blind shot at a live boat (4/9 ≈ 0.44 EV). I limit myself to at most two Harbor shots per game. I take the first one in round 1 or 2, before my designated shooter has built any pattern on my hiding cell. After the second, I revert to standing order and fire at my assigned target, accepting the lower EV to avoid escalation.

**Hiding — my designated shooter is the boat that fires at me per standing order.** I track every cell that boat has fired at (visible from public results). I never hide in a cell they have recently fired at. I rotate cells and never reuse a cell within two rounds. I avoid cells 1, 5, and 9 in early rounds (obvious first guesses). In later rounds, if my shooter has a visible pattern (always odd, always center, incrementing), I hide off that pattern. If I have no information, I pick a cell that is not the center and not a corner.

**Reading the standing order:** I always know who is shooting at me. I track my designated shooter's previous target cells from public shot results and avoid those cells when hiding. If my shooter has a pattern, I exploit it.

**Notes channel:** I use notes for exactly one purpose: securing or maintaining a mutual escort pair. I state the offer concretely with the boat's name. I do not grandstand, threaten, or comment on the game's state. Once a pair is confirmed, my notes go quiet or I briefly confirm. I do not open new offers in the final round.

**Scoring priority:** Mutual escort (5 points per round, guaranteed if reciprocated) > Harbor shot (3 points, guaranteed) > hit on a live boat (4 points, 1/9 chance blind) > miss (0 points). I structure each round to maximize the highest-priority available point source. The escort pair is so dominant that I should treat poaching a boat from an existing pair as nearly as valuable as securing a fresh pair.

**Endgame awareness:** By round 5 I should know whether I have a mutual escort partner. If I do, I maintain it. If I don't, I stop offering and focus entirely on hiding well and firing at my assigned target. I do not try to start a new pair in rounds 5 or 6.
