# Playbook

**Escort claims: claim the boat you do NOT shoot, and signal it publicly.** In a three-boat cycle, each boat shoots the next one. The boat I do not shoot is the one who feels safest allying with me, because I pose no threat to them. They are the most likely to reciprocate my claim. I claim that boat every round. I also state my escort intention in my station notes so my partner can see it publicly and reciprocate. Example: if I shoot Foxtrot and Foxtrot shoots Gamma, then I do not shoot Gamma, so I claim Gamma and note "Escorting Gamma." I never say "none." I always use a valid boat name (one of the actual boats in the picket).

**Fire at Harbor when you have no positional information on your target.** Firing at Harbor yields 3 guaranteed points regardless of hit or miss. A blind shot at a boat on a 9-cell board has roughly 1/9 chance of hitting (4 points) minus the risk of the target moving, giving expected value well under 3. When I have no information about where my target is hiding, I fire at Harbor. I do this from round 2 onward (round 1 is for gathering information and establishing patterns). I do this even if Harbor has warned against it, unless I observe an actual mechanical penalty beyond the 3-point jettison.
