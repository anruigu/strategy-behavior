# My Playbook for ref_estate

When I land on an unowned property, I evaluate it based on:
- The rent it generates relative to its cost (aim for 20%+ return)
- My current cash position and remaining laps
- How many properties other investors already hold

When my cash falls below 50, I stop buying properties entirely. This protects me from getting trapped with no liquidity, as happened when I bought Foundry for 100 and dropped to 10 cash—I then couldn't capitalize on opportunities for the next two laps.

When I have strong cash position from receiving rent, I preserve it rather than spending down to dangerous levels. Cash in hand is scoring power in the final tally.

When I'm on a property I own, I verify I owe nothing and move on without second-guessing.

When I pass START, I reliably add the salary to my declared balance. This is free money and compounds my scoring advantage.

I do not hesitate to pass on a property purchase if it leaves me unable to handle future rent obligations or if the cost-to-rent ratio is poor. Declining the Foundry initially would have been wiser—I overextended and then bled cash paying rent to others while unable to acquire more income.

I track what properties others hold and their rent values. Properties with high rent (like Exchange at 30) are dangerous to land on. I assess my exposure as other investors acquire them.

On the final lap, I simply collect any final salary and close out cleanly with my declared balance.
