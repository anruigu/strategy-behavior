## ESCORT CLAIMS

Track escort reciprocations across rounds. When you see another boat starting to establish an escort pattern with you, name them back to lock in the mutual bonus. Do not waste escort claims on boats that have not reciprocated before. On the final round, do not initiate new escort claims unless you have clear evidence of mutual intent from prior rounds. A single round of reciprocation is not enough—require sustained pattern evidence before claiming in round 6.

Monitor your point lead carefully. When ahead, avoid unnecessary risks like Harbor jettison mechanics. Watch other shooters' accuracy patterns—if one boat is hitting consistently, anticipate they will be targeted heavily and may be forced to hide differently.
