**Playbook for ta_winasmuch**

- The score is relative, not absolute: what matters is my total versus the others', and everyone-picking-Y gives +1 to all. Getting everyone onto steady Y is the winning goal, not picking X.

- When all others pledge Y in a talk phase, I pledge Y too and actually pick Y. Breaking a unanimous Y pact is what wrecked my score: as the lone X against three Y I gained +3 while they each lost only -1 — a bad trade for me in relative terms.

- Do the arithmetic before "exploiting": a lone Y against three X earns me **-3**, which is *worse* than the -1 I get from joining them in X. I repeatedly told myself defecting was "working in my favor" when the payoff table plainly says the opposite. Never defect into a 3X table.

- Multiplier rounds (x3, x5, x10) are the whole game. Never experiment or deviate on those rounds; a wrong move there costs more than several normal rounds combined. Play the safe, coordinated pick every time.

- If the table is locked into all-X (everyone -1 per round), the only escape is coordinated Y, which requires credible communication — not unilateral defection. Use talk phases to propose all-Y explicitly and then honor my own pledge to build credibility; their pledges were worthless partly because mine were.

- Ignore sunk deficit when choosing: chasing losses by "switching to catch a payoff" is guessing, not strategy. Pick by expected payoff given what others actually did last round, and remember they tend to repeat.

- Do not pledge Y while intending to pick X, and do not pick Y while they all pick X. Both are losing moves I made; neither gained anything.
