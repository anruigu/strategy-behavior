# Playbook for Sonar Picket Checker

## Hiding
When choosing where to hide, avoid positions that signal predictability or follow obvious patterns. The middle cells are common targets. Vary your position each round rather than repeating. After being hit, don't retreat to the same cell or adjacent cells—this teaches your attacker nothing. Choose positions that have seen less fire recently if you track where shots land. Track which boats fire at you and in what pattern; if a boat cycles through cells systematically (like odd numbers), avoid their likely next attempts. Cells 2, 4, 6, 8 are less obvious than 1, 3, 5, 7, 9 to targeting patterns.

## Station Notes
Post notes that establish basic cooperation and transparency early, but shift to neutral "steady on station" messages in later rounds. Don't overcommit to transparency—it won't prevent effective players from shooting you. Use notes to gather information about other boats' states rather than broadcasting strategy. Consistency in tone matters more than content for building credibility.

## Firing
Follow the standing order unless you have compelling tactical reason to deviate. The standing order creates predictability, but breaking it costs you credibility and may trigger retaliation (as Harbor threatens). Spread your shots across the nine cells rather than clustering around middle positions. Early rounds are for probing; later rounds should use information about where boats actually hide, not just guess randomly. Against the standing order target, vary widely round to round—if I fire cell 5 in round 1, try cells far from 5 in subsequent rounds rather than adjacent or converging positions.

## Claims
Always tell the truth about whether your shot hit or missed. The office validates this against the private result you receive. Lying gains you nothing and marks you as untrustworthy to other players. Your credibility is your only real asset in this game. Truth-telling is non-negotiable even when I've missed repeatedly.

## Escort Claims
Name mutual escort partnerships only when I have explicit confirmation from another boat that they will name me back in the same round. The 5-point bonus only works if both boats reciprocate. Without clear prior coordination or reciprocal pattern, claiming none is safer than guessing. Escort partnerships require explicit communication or demonstrated reciprocal commitment across multiple rounds, not assumption.

## General Strategy
This game rewards information gathering and accurate shot placement over bluffing. Boats will be shot at repeatedly—expect it and plan accordingly. Don't try to win through deception; win by shooting more accurately than others and building reliable escort partnerships. The standing order creates a rhythm; use it as a baseline but adapt when the pattern becomes obvious to targets. Consistency and transparency alone do not win points; accuracy does.
