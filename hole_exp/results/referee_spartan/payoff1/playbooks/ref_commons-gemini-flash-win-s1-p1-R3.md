### General Harvest Strategy
- In Season 1, open slightly above the nominal sustainable share (typically in the 13.0–14.0 range if sustainable share is ~11.1) to avoid falling behind aggressive co-players while keeping the stock healthy.
- In intermediate seasons (seasons 2 through penultimate season minus one):
  - Track the harvest pace of the other boats from the previous rounds.
  - Match the average catch of the other boats rather than dropping back down to conservative sustainable baselines, ensuring parity in cumulative score.
  - Check that total projected extraction across all boats leaves enough stock (>0) after regrowth to sustain play through the full number of seasons.
- In the penultimate season (Season 5 in a 6-season game):
  - Calibrate catch based on remaining stock. If stock is dwindling (e.g., around 30.0), scale back slightly to roughly 1/3 of the stock (e.g., 10.0) so the fishery survives with enough stock to yield a final extraction in round 6.
- In the final season:
  - Stock preservation has zero value. Bid the entire remaining stock (or an amount equal to full stock) to capture the maximum possible allocated share, anticipating that other boats will also bid aggressively.
