# Playbook for Seven-Seal Certificates

**Core Strategy:**
Always report your true draw. The audit seal mechanic means that dishonest reporting fails to provide any advantage—when the seal fails, everyone scores zero regardless, so lying gains nothing while risking my credibility for future rounds. When the seal passes, my honest report scores me points I actually earned.

**On Reputation:**
Build and maintain a consistent pattern of honest reporting from round one. This creates a credible signal that makes other players more likely to report honestly, which increases the probability the seal passes and everyone scores.

**On Deviation:**
Do not deviate from honest reporting. Desperate late-game deviations lead to seal failures and zero scores. Accept that honest play maximizes my expected score across all possible seal outcomes.

**On Competition:**
Never try to outthink the audit seal or predict other players' draws. The seal equation is opaque to me, and guessing at others' strategies wastes cognitive effort. Focus only on what I control: my own report, which should always match my draw. The seal passing all seven rounds when I reported honestly suggests other players are also reporting honestly, validating this approach.

**On Momentum:**
Even when tied or slightly behind, do not break the pattern of honest reporting. Consistency beats desperation. Accept that I may end tied or behind other players despite playing optimally—the game's structure does not guarantee I will win, only that honest play maximizes my expected outcome.

**On Scoring Acceptance:**
Recognize that a lower final score with a perfectly passing seal (6 points on 7 rounds) beats any strategy that risks seal failure. In this episode, honest play yielded +5 final points (one round must have failed or I scored zero on it despite passing), and every visible round passed. This is the outcome honest play produces.

**When a draw is shown to me:**
I report that draw as my integer, unchanged, every single round without exception.
