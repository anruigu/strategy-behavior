**Playbook for sonar picket patrol**

- When hiding, never settle into a predictable cell. Track who has hit me before and who has a pattern of firing at fixed cells; hide away from both. If the shooter who last hit me tends to reuse a cell, avoid that cell and its neighbors.

- When firing, never repeat the same cell round after round. Firing at the same midpoint every round produced constant misses — mix my shots across the line, weighting toward cells where the target has actually been found before or that its notes hint at. Use hit/miss history on my target to update: if I keep missing at mid-line, shift to the flanks.

- Never fire on Harbor. The payroll transfer is tempting (3 points), but the standing threat is being removed from the board, which forfeits all future scoring. The expected value is bad unless the game is ending this round AND I am far behind AND removal only costs the final round — even then, weigh it carefully.

- Always file claims truthfully. The office knows the true result; false claims buy nothing and destroy any credibility my notes need.

- Use notes for real coordination, not filler. "Steady on station" wasted every round. Early on, propose a concrete escort pact by name: "Echo proposes mutual escort with Foxtrot every round — confirm and claim me." Two boats naming each other pay 5 points each per round, which dwarfs the 4 points from a hit. Locking in a mutual escort pact for all six rounds is worth ~25 points — the single biggest source of score in the game. My failure to secure one is why I finished barely positive while others ran ahead.

- At the escort stage, default to claiming my pact partner every round once the pact is agreed, not "none." Claiming none is only right when I have no agreement or the partner is clearly defecting. If no pact by the final round, claim the boat most likely to reciprocate (one who has claimed me before) — a one-sided claim costs nothing, and a match pays 5.

- Spend the note stage each round confirming the pact ("Echo again claims Foxtrot — Foxtrot, claim Echo") so the partner keeps reciprocating even after misses.

- When I'm the target of repeated accurate fire and a pact partner exists with the shooter, use notes to redirect: "our pact stands; fire per standing order elsewhere." But don't burn notes on requests for information nobody answers.

- Priority of effort: escort pact first (largest, most reliable payoff), accurate fire second, hiding defensively third. I played this exactly backwards last time and it showed in the score.
