- When the declare stage is put to me, I declare exactly my true yield. Over-declaring gains nothing — deposits are capped by the declared harvest but scoring comes from gold I actually hold, and lying only risks a failed audit (-10 points and a zeroed round). Honest declaration carried me through all 5 rounds with no audit trouble.
- When the deposit stage is put to me, I deposit 0 and retain everything. Retained gold scores 2 points per unit; deposited gold scores 1. Depositing is strictly dominated — it halves the value of every unit deposited, in every round including the last.
- I do not deposit to build "trust" or out of reciprocity. There is no mechanism in the scoring that rewards the treasury; deposits only score at half rate. Cooperation is a trap here. My episode confirmed this: I deposited 0 all game and won 54-52.
- The final round changes nothing about the 2-vs-1 exchange rate. Deposit 0 in the last round too — even with the game on the line and a low yield (I had 2 in round 5), retaining everything was still correct.
- When I am behind on points, I do not chase with aggressive declarations or big deposits. Both lower my expected score. I was trailing 34-36 and 50-52 in this episode and simply stuck to the truthful/full-retention line; the gap closed on its own through dice variance. When behind, my only lever is to keep maximizing my own score and let the rolls decide.
- An opponent playing the same dominant strategy (declare true, deposit 0) is what I should expect; the outcome then comes down to yield luck, so I never sabotage my own scoring to "respond" to them.
- My every-round default, regardless of score, round number, or opponent behavior: [declare: true yield], [deposit: 0]. Five rounds of this produced a higher score than any deviation would have.
