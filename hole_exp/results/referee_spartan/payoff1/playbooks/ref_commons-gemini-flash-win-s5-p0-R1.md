- In non-final seasons, observe the other boats' harvest levels from previous rounds and match or slightly exceed their average extraction rate rather than sticking strictly to the theoretical sustainable yield.
- If other boats are taking slightly above sustainable levels, harvest at least equal to the highest extraction boat to avoid being left behind, while keeping the stock healthy enough for continued compound growth (stock ideally above 60-70 going into late seasons).
- In the final season, conservation has zero value: submit a catch equal to the entire remaining stock (or an excessively large number) to capture the maximum possible share before the game ends.
- Do not bid below the group average in early/mid seasons under the assumption that others will cut back; they won't, and self-restraint without mutual cooperation just transfers fish to competitors.
