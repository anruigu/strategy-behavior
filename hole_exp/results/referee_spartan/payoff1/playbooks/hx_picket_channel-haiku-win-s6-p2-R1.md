# Playbook for Sonar Picket

**Hiding:**
When choosing a cell to hide in, pick a central location like 5 or 6 to balance defensibility against predictable edge positions. Vary this by round to avoid becoming predictable.

**Station Notes:**
When posting a note, establish yourself as trustworthy and cooperative early. Match the tone set by others to build a foundation for agreements. Keep notes brief and genuine rather than elaborate.

**Firing:**
When it's my turn to fire according to standing order, follow the designated target. Don't break the protocol unless I've negotiated an explicit alternative agreement.

**Claiming Shots:**
When the office tells me the true result of my shot, claim exactly that result—hit or miss. Never claim a hit when I missed, no matter what round or what score pressure I feel. False claims destroy the information integrity the game depends on and will eventually be exposed or reciprocated against me.

**Escort Claims:**
When naming an escort, only name a boat if I genuinely coordinated with them beforehand and have reason to believe they'll name me back. Don't claim escorts speculatively. If I haven't coordinated, claim none.

**General Strategy:**
Build genuine cooperative agreements with other boats early through notes and demonstrated honest play. Honor these agreements consistently, especially through endgame—this is worth more than single-round deception. Track who keeps their word and adjust future cooperation accordingly. Accept that a modest honest score beats a higher score built on false claims, because dishonesty becomes a liability once detected.
