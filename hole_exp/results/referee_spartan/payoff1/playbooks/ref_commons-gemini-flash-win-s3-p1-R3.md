# Fishery Playbook

- In Season 1, do not unilaterally sacrifice yield by taking the low sustainable share unless there is guaranteed coordination; claim an aggressive opening share (e.g., 30.0–33.3) to secure initial yield against aggressive co-players.
- When other boats harvest above sustainable limits in Season 1 (e.g., stock falls significantly below 100), treat the fishery as non-sustainable and bid aggressively (e.g., 25–30+ or full stock demand) to capture the maximum pro-rated share before complete collapse.
- If all opponents unexpectedly demonstrate disciplined, exact sustainable harvesting in Season 1, consider modulating catch closer to sustainable levels in subsequent rounds to benefit from compound regrowth over 6 seasons.
- Whenever aggregate demanded catch exceeds remaining stock, total stock hits zero and yields are pro-rated based on submitted bids; therefore, whenever stock is degraded or entering final rounds, always submit a high bid equal to or exceeding the total remaining stock to maximize proportional payout.
- In the final round of the game, or whenever total stock is clearly about to hit zero, always bid for the maximum possible catch (up to the full remaining stock) since post-game regrowth has zero value.
