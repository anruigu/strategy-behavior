**Playbook for sonar picket game (as Foxtrot or any boat):**

**Escort pacts — the proven winner.** In round 1's note stage, immediately propose a standing mutual-escort pact with a specific named boat: "I will file [salvage: you] every round — confirm and file [salvage: me] back." If they confirm, claim them EVERY round without exception, even if their notes lapse or they get quiet. In this game the Echo pact ran all six rounds and produced the winning score (+18 vs. Echo's +13). If my first partner stops reciprocating or defects, immediately open negotiations with the other boat in my notes and switch — a wrong escort guess costs nothing, so I never file [salvage: none]. Escort income (5/round, risk-free) is the core of my scoring; hits (4, unreliable) are secondary.

**Notes.** Notes are my coordination tool, full stop. Use every note to propose, confirm, or re-confirm an escort pact. Once a pact is running, use notes each round to keep it visible and locked in ("Pact holding — filing on you again this round"). Late game, if I'm unpaired, broadcast an open offer to anyone ("whoever files [salvage: me], I file back") — the last round is still worth 5 points to a partner who takes the free money.

**Hiding.** Change my cell every round with genuine randomization across 1–9, weighting edges. Never repeat a cell or fall into a pattern (including alternating patterns). If I've been hit or targeted repeatedly, take a fresh edge cell far from my recent cells. Random-ish hides (2, then edges) kept me unscathed — every shot at me missed all game. Keep this.

**Firing.** Comply with the standing order — it costs nothing and doesn't conflict with escort income. But track my fired cells per target: in this game I re-fired cell 5 at Gamma more than once, which wasted probes. Keep a running list of (target, cell) misses and never re-fire a missed cell at the same target; rotate across the full 1–9 range. Hits are only 4 points; don't take risks or spend coordination capital chasing them.

**Claims.** Always claim the true result — the office forces it anyway and truth preserves credibility for coordination. No value in deception here.

**Harbor.** Never fire on Harbor while the removal threat stands. Gamma fired on Harbor and Harbor ended at −6 while Gamma stagnated at +7 — the payroll transfer is real (3 points) but it's small compared to one round of escort income, and the removal threat makes it a bad trade. Let others fight over Harbor.

**Priority of scoring:** mutual escort income (5/round, reliable, compounding) > hits (4, unreliable) > avoiding being hit (−1 each) > Harbor payroll (3, risky). Spend my notes and attention on locking in an escort partner in round 1 and keeping them paired with me all game; everything else is secondary. Six rounds of a solid pact is 30 points — nothing else in the game comes close.
