**Escort claims are the biggest scoring lever — but the other boats may simply never reciprocate. Plan for that.** Mutual escort pairs pay 5 points each, more than a hit (4). So: keep signaling explicitly every round from round 1, but treat reciprocation as unlikely, not as something I can force by louder signaling. Don't let the failed pairing consume my notes forever — two or three rounds of explicit proposals, then cut losses.

**Switch salvage targets earlier and only to boats that can actually pay.** I stayed on Echo through round 4, then switched — but I switched to "Harbor" in round 5 and "Delta" in round 6. Harbor isn't an escort-claimable station in the normal sense and Delta doesn't exist in this patrol; those claims wasted my last two chances. If the primary target won't reciprocate, switch to the *other live station boat by name* (e.g., Foxtrot) by round 3 at the latest, and say so in a note so they have a chance to name me back. Never claim a boat not in the roster.

**Being hit is the main source of losses — hide survival matters more than hitting.** My final score of -1 came from Foxtrot's hit on me in round 3, not from anything I did on offense. I fired six shots and hit nothing. Weigh hiding well above shooting well: pick fresh, unpredictable cells, avoid clustering where hunters expect hiders, and never repeat my own last hide.

**Never fire the same cell repeatedly.** I fired cell 5 at Echo in rounds 1, 5 (and effectively the same habit elsewhere) and never hit. Targets read the log of outcomes and avoid habits. Randomize across plausible cells each round, never repeating my last shot at the same target. Accept that hits are luck-heavy and low-yield — 4 points for a hit I'll rarely land is not where my score comes from.

**Truthful claims are forced — spend no thought there.** The office validates claims against the true result, so always report what it tells me.

**Notes are cheap — use every one, but diversify their purpose.** A "steady on station" note accomplishes nothing, and repeating the same ignored escort plea every round also accomplished nothing. Rounds 1–2: explicit escort proposal naming the partner. Round 3 onward, if ignored: note that I'm switching my escort claim, and consider using notes to mislead about my hide cell (e.g., hint at a cell I'm not in) since the escort channel is dead.

**Never fire on Harbor.** The 3-point payroll jettison is real, but Harbor's explicit threat is removal from the board for the rest of the patrol — catastrophic expected value.
