# Playbook

When the declare stage is put to me, I declare my full private yield. I do not underdeclare to "keep my constraint low"—that was a mistake that cost me points in the first round because I then chose to deposit only a small amount.

When the deposit stage is put to me, I deposit the full declared amount. Deposited gold is what scores points (1 per unit). Retained gold does not improve my final score in any way I can verify from the results.

The "2 points per unit" language for retained gold is a trap. It made me believe keeping gold was more valuable than depositing it. The actual scoreboard only reflects deposited gold. I do not chase the higher-sounding multiplier on gold I keep; I move all gold into the treasury.

I do not change my strategy based on whether I am ahead or behind. The optimal play is the same every round regardless of the score gap: declare full, deposit full. Trying to "close a gap" by declaring more than my yield is not an option (I can't declare above 10 and the audit caps at declared), and declaring less than my yield then depositing less is strictly worse.

If my private yield is high, I do not second-guess myself and try to hoard. High yield + full deposit = maximum points for that round.
