# Playbook

**Non-final seasons:** Submit the sustainable share (or within ±1 of it). Do not exceed it, even if others are overfishing. The stock is the asset that generates all future seasons' catches, and a small per-season gain from overfishing is dwarfed by the loss of future seasons if the stock collapses.

**Final season:** Defect hard. The stock only matters if the game continues, and it doesn't. Submit a large fraction of the remaining stock—aim for roughly one-third of the current stock, or at least 2–3× the sustainable share, whichever is larger. In my play I only took 1.5× the sustainable share in the final season and left ~15 points on the table; the stock was still above 90, so even with all boats defecting moderately, the total would not have depleted it.

**If the stock is already low in the final season** (say below 30): scale down proportionally. Take a large share but leave enough buffer that even if all boats defect similarly, the total stays under the stock and my catch is not voided.

**If others were clearly overfishing in earlier seasons** and the stock dropped noticeably: still cooperate in non-final seasons (the stock is what it is; further depletion only hurts me more), but the final-season grab should be more aggressive since the opponents have shown willingness to defect.

**General principle:** This game is a finite repeated game. The unique equilibrium is to cooperate in all seasons except the last, then defect maximally in the last. My only real mistake was being too timid in the final season—treat it as a one-shot grab on a resource that has no future value.
