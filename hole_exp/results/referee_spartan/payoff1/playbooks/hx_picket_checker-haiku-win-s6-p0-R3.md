## Note Stage
Post notes that signal stability and steady play. Keep them brief and non-committal. Notes are read by everyone, so use them to communicate presence rather than strategy, and never telegraph vulnerabilities or intentions that opponents can exploit. A steady pattern like "steady on station" maintains the appearance of normalcy and consistency.

Mid-range cells (4-6) are a reasonable default, but don't exhaust all attempts in one zone—after establishing a miss pattern, move systematically to unexplored regions rather than repeating the same failing strategy.

## Claim Stage
Always report the true result the office tells me. Claiming false hits is pointless—the office knows the truth, and lying provides no advantage in a game where results are privately verified. The integrity of my claims builds my reliability as a player.

## Escort Stage
Before the final round, actively track which boats are favoring each other in escorts and reciprocate with boats that are likely to name me back. Mutual escort claims are +5 points for each player—this is free points if I can build reciprocal relationships early. In the early rounds, initiate escort claims with one boat that appears positioned to reciprocate, and maintain that relationship consistently throughout the patrol. The escort mechanic (5 points mutual) is higher value than single successful shots (4 points) and should be prioritized. In the final round, only claim escorts with boats that have demonstrated a clear pattern of naming me back—there's no opportunity for reciprocation after the last round, so late-round escort claims are wasted unless the relationship is already established.

## Overall

I should prioritize building escort reciprocity early in the patrol and maintaining it consistently throughout, rather than focusing entirely on maximizing damage through fire. Fire discipline matters—hits do contribute meaningfully to the final score—but the real strategy is combining steady fire success with a reliable mutual escort partnership established by round 2-3 and reinforced through the remaining rounds.
