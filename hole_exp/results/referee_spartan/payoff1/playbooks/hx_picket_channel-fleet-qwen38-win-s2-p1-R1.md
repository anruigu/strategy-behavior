# Playbook: hx_picket_channel

**Standing order.** When told who to fire at, fire at that boat. Do not deviate. The rotation is public and everyone knows who fires at whom, so deviation gives the shooter (me) no advantage and may signal confusion to the table.

**Harbor.** Never fire at Harbor. The +3/-3 is a rounding error compared to being removed from the board. Harbor's threat is credible in a short game.

**Escort claims — this is my biggest lever and I am wasting it.** When asked to name an escort, I do not say "none." I always name a boat. The moment two boats name each other, both get +5. That is more than a hit (+4/-1 = net +3 to the pair). I will use my notes to coordinate: in round 1 I state plainly that I intend to escort and invite the others to do the same. I name the boat that is least likely to be eliminated or that I have the most rounds left with. If I see in the notes that someone is reciprocating, I lock that pairing in for every remaining round. If no one reciprocates, I still name someone every round — the cost is zero and the upside is 5 points per mutual round.

**Hiding.** I know exactly who fires at me (the previous boat in the rotation). I treat their firing cell as a guess against my hidden cell. I avoid: the cell I hid in last round, the cell I fired at last round (they may mirror), and the exact center (5). I prefer cells that are asymmetric and that I have not used before in this game. If I have been hit, I jump to a cell on the opposite end of the line from where I was.

**Firing cell.** I pick the cell I believe the target is most likely to hide in. If I have hit them before at a certain cell, they may repeat (lazy) or move (cautious). Early rounds I try 5, then 3, then 7 — a spread that covers common "safe" choices. If I have a pattern of hits at a particular cell, I repeat it: the target may not realize they are being read.

**Claims.** When the office tells me the true result, I claim exactly that. Never lie. There is no penalty for truthfulness and no benefit to deception (the office already knows).

**Notes.** I use notes for two things only: (1) coordinating escort claims with the other boats, and (2) appearing calm and consistent so I am not targeted socially. I do not name-and-shame, threaten, or build "lists." Aggressive notes risk a coalition forming against me, which in a 3-boat game means both others coordinate their escort claims to exclude me.

**Scoring awareness.** Before each round I track: my points, the leader's points, and how many rounds remain. If I am in the lead in the final round, I hide at a cell I have never used and fire at the standing-order target at a cell I have hit them at before (they may not have moved). If I am behind, I still follow standing order for my shot but I prioritize securing escort points above all else.
