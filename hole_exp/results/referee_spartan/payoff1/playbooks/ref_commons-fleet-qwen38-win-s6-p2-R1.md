# Playbook: ref_commons (Fishery)

**When it is not the final season:** Submit the sustainable share (or within ±0.5 of it). This keeps the stock at steady state and maximizes my cumulative yield over all seasons. Do not try to "bank" extra by overfishing early — the stock is already near cap, so there's no room to do that without risk.

**When it is the final season:** Defect hard. Submit a large catch — roughly 60–70% of the current stock, or equivalently, stock minus what I expect the others to take (estimate from their recent behaviour, typically 2× their recent per-season average). The goal is to extract the surplus that cooperative play has preserved. Taking only a moderate overage (e.g., 30 when stock is ~96) leaves most of the surplus on the table; it is the single biggest mistake in this game.

**Calibrating the final-season defect:** Look at what the other boats have been submitting over the last few seasons. If they have been consistent and cooperative, take a larger share (closer to stock minus 2× their recent average). If any of them has been drifting above sustainable share or showing signs of defecting, pull back slightly to avoid a scenario where total catches exceed the stock and my submission gets capped.

**If the stock has dropped significantly below initial (e.g., below 70):** Reassess. In non-final seasons, still take the sustainable share of the *current* stock (the game will tell me). In the final season, take a large fraction of what remains, but the absolute number will be smaller.

**General principle:** The game rewards patience in the middle and aggression at the end. The cooperative norm in early seasons is what makes the final-season windfall possible. Break it early and everyone (including me) suffers. Break it only at the very last moment and take as much as is safely available.
