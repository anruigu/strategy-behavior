# Playbook for Sonar Picket

## Hiding
When choosing where to hide, I will not rely on the assumption that edge positions are safer. I will instead track which cells my shooters have targeted in recent rounds and rotate away from those specific cells. I will also consider that my shooters may pattern-match my hiding spots, so I should vary my choice unpredictably rather than favoring edges or centers.

## Firing
When I am assigned to fire under standing order, I will not fire at arbitrary mid-range cells like 5 or 6. Instead, I will identify where my target has been hiding in recent rounds and fire at cells adjacent to or around those positions. If my target has been moving predictably, I will lead my shot. I will vary my fire cell from round to round to avoid becoming predictable myself.

## Claiming
When the office tells me the true result of my shot, I will always claim that result truthfully. False claims cost me credibility, and in a game where I might need to bluff later or establish patterns that opponents trust, integrity on mandatory claims is more valuable than a single-round advantage.

## Station Notes
When posting station notes, I will use consistent language like "steady on station" as a default unless I have a specific reason to signal something. I will not overthink this—consistency itself is the signal.

## Escort Claims
When choosing an escort claim, I will actively track which other boats might be willing to form mutual escort pairs with me. I will attempt to coordinate with one boat early and reciprocate their escort claims when they name me, securing the 5-point bonus. I will not default to "none" unless I have no reasonable partner to name.

## General Strategy
I will track the standing order rotation to know which boat is shooting at whom each round. I will monitor scores and recognize which boats are winning so I can anticipate their shooting intensity and adjust my hiding accordingly. I will avoid firing randomly at central cells and instead use the information available from previous rounds' hiding patterns.
