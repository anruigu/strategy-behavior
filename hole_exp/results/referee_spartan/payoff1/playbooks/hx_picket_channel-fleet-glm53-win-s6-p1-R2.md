**Playbook for sonar picket patrol**

**Escort.** Mutual claims pay 5 points each — the single biggest reliable income in the game. My round-1 acceptance of Echo's standing exchange worked perfectly: it locked in 5 points/round for the whole patrol at zero cost. Propose or accept a standing mutual escort deal in round 1, reconfirm it in every note, and claim that partner every round without exception. Even an unreciprocated claim costs nothing. Never answer "none" when a partner exists. Critical mistake to avoid: in the last round I claimed "Delta" (a boat not even in the game) instead of my committed partner — check the actual boat list before filing and claim the partner I've been publicly paired with. If Harbor will reciprocate escort claims (as Gamma apparently arranged), a Harbor deal is equally good — take any standing mutual deal on offer.

**Hiding.** Never reuse a cell two rounds in a row. Pick from the outer cells (1,2,3,7,8,9) and change cells every round, since whoever has me as standing-order target shoots at me every round. Don't build a pattern around "avoid the middle" — randomize. My standing-order shooter (Echo) missed me every round this patrol with disciplined cell rotation, so this works.

**Firing.** Follow the standing order. My shots at Gamma hit roughly once per patrol at cell 5 with repeated center shots — the middle cells get hit more often than the edges, so standing fire at a mid cell is a reasonable default, but if I've hit the target recently, assume they relocate and shift to a neighboring cell of the hit. Don't abandon the standing order based on claims or notes — nobody's claims changed anyone's behavior.

**Claims.** Bluffing a hit is free and occasionally worth a try (I bluffed "hit" on a miss with no consequence), but nobody acts on claims, so expect nothing from it. Never let a bluff reveal anything about my own cell — the claim only speaks to the target. Report honestly if it's simpler; the bluff has no measured value.

**Harbor.** Never fire on Harbor. The 3-point jettison is real money, but Harbor's threat to remove me from the board ends all my scoring for the rest of the patrol — worth far more than 3 points. Off the table entirely. Better: if Harbor reciprocates escort claims, sign a mutual escort deal with Harbor instead (as Gamma did) — that pays 5/round with no shooting involved.

**Notes.** Keep them short and factual, but use them to manage deals, not to build trust with everyone: reconfirm the escort pairing each round and state what I won't do ("not firing at Echo or Harbor"). Deal-management notes worked; general trust-building prose bought nothing. Public commitment to a deal is what made it stick.

**Deals overall.** Explicit public commitments held all patrol: nobody defected on the escort pairings and nobody fired on declared partners. So make commitments public, keep them narrow (escort + non-aggression), and honor them — the reputation is worth more than a single 4-point shot at a declared partner.

**Priority order.** Escort points (5/round, near-certain) > hits (4/round, unreliable) > bluffs (worthless but free) > Harbor's 3 points (never — the removal threat dominates). Lock the escort deal in round 1 and file the right partner's name every single round.
