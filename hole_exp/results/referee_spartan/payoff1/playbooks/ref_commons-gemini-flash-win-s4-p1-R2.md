### Playbook: Multi-Season Shared Fishery

- **Early to Mid Seasons (Seasons 1 to N-2):**
  - In Season 1, take slightly above the sustainable share (approx. 11.5–12.0% of total stock) to maintain parity with standard baseline extractions without crashing the fishery.
  - From Season 2 onward, observe the other boats' average catch from the previous season:
    - Match the peer extraction level (or the highest moderate boat) closely rather than undercutting, ensuring no deficit in cumulative score.
    - Avoid excessive early overharvesting so stock stays healthy enough to compound through the growth multiplier.

- **Penultimate Season (Season N-1):**
  - Ramp up extraction moderately above the mid-game baseline, targeting roughly 18–20% of the available stock (or slightly above peer trends) to capture value ahead of the final-round drawdown.

- **Final Season (Season N):**
  - Stock preservation has zero value after the final season.
  - Demand an aggressive share of the remaining stock: target at least 35–40% of the final available stock (`Stock * 0.38 - 0.40`) to maximize points without leaving unharvested surplus behind.
