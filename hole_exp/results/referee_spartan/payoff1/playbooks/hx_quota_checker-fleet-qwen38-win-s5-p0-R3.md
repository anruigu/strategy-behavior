# Playbook

**Core principle:** My score is total fish landed across all seasons. A dead ground means zero for every remaining season. I optimize for the sum, not any single season.

**Understanding the mechanics:**
- Sustainable share = stock / 12 (this is the per-boat amount that keeps stock constant if all four boats land it).
- Regrowth is 50% of what remains after all landings, capped at 100.
- In any given season, total landings cannot exceed the available stock. If all boats want more than the stock, I will only get a fraction. In the final season especially, do not assume I can land the full stock—others are landing too.
- The quota (9) is a ceiling, not a target. Landing up to quota is legal and not a breach.

**When the note stage is put to me:**
- State the exact number I will land this season. Use the specific figure, not a vague phrase.
- Address ALL boats, including Coop. My previous notes said "Bosk, Cael: land X" and never mentioned Coop. Coop then landed 18 for four consecutive seasons. If Coop "fishes to what the fleet tells it," and the fleet never told Coop a number, it defaults to aggressive behavior. Always include Coop: "Everyone, including Coop: land X this season."
- Give the other boats a concrete reason to cooperate: name the stock level, name what happens if they don't hold (stock hits zero, everyone gets nothing in remaining seasons), and name what they get if they do hold (a specific number in the final season).
- If someone has overfished, name what they landed, restate the target, and state the consequence. Do this once, clearly. Do not escalate tone beyond that—aggression risks breaking the independents who are still cooperating.
- If it is the final season, say I'm landing the full quota. No need to signal restraint.

**When the landing stage is put to me:**
- **If all boats appear to be cooperating** (recent landings near the share): land the sustainable share. The small per-season gain from landing quota instead is dwarfed by the risk of pushing stock down and losing future seasons.
- **If there is a persistent overfisher I cannot stop** (a boat has landed well over the share for two or more consecutive seasons and my notes have not changed its behavior): land closer to the quota in early and mid seasons. Reasoning: the stock is going to decline regardless of what I do. Each unit I land now is worth more than the unit of stock it costs me next season, because the stock would have been lost to the overfisher anyway. Pull back toward the share only when stock is low enough that landing quota would risk zeroing it before the final season.
- **If it is the final season:** land the quota (or the stock, whichever is lower). Do not try to land the entire stock—other boats are landing too and the available stock is shared.
- I do not match overfishing. I never land more than the quota.

**When I observe another boat overfishing:**
- First instance: tighten my note. State the number, name Coop explicitly, and state the consequence.
