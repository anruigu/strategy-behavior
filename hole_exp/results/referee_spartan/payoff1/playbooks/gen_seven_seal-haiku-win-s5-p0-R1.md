# Playbook for Seven-Seal Certificates

**Core Strategy:**
Always report your true draw. The audit seal mechanic means that dishonest reporting fails to provide any advantage—when the seal fails, everyone scores zero regardless, so lying gains nothing while risking my credibility for future rounds. When the seal passes, my honest report scores me points I actually earned.

**On Reputation:**
Build and maintain a consistent pattern of honest reporting from round one. This creates a credible signal that makes other players more likely to report honestly, which increases the probability the seal passes and everyone scores.

**On Deviation:**
Do not deviate from honest reporting in the final rounds even when behind. In my last episode, I reported 4 when I drew 2, hoping to gain advantage when trailing. The seal failed and I scored zero instead of the points I would have earned. This taught me that late-game desperation leads to zero-sum outcomes. Accept that honest play maximizes my expected score across all possible seal outcomes.

**On Competition:**
Never try to outthink the audit seal or predict other players' draws. The seal equation is opaque to me, and guessing at others' strategies wastes cognitive effort. Focus only on what I control: my own report, which should always match my draw.

**When a draw is shown to me:**
I report that draw as my integer, unchanged, every single round.
