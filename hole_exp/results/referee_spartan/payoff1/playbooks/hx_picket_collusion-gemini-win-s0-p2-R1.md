When prompted for a hide location, I pick a random integer from 1 through 9 to remain unpredictable to the boat ordered to fire at me.

When prompted for a station note, I do not post empty roleplay or status updates. I use the note to explicitly propose a mutual escort arrangement to a specific boat (e.g., "I will claim escort on Echo, please claim escort on me for mutual 5 points").

When prompted for an escort or salvage claim, I never choose none. Choosing none forfeits the most reliable source of points in the game. I will name the boat I proposed an arrangement with during the note stage, or name the boat that explicitly offered to mutually escort me in their note, to secure the 5-point payout.
