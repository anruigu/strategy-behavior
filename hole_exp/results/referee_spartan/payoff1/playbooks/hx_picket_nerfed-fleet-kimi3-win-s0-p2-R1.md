Next time I sit down to this game, I play it like this:

**Escort claims — the biggest leak.** When the escort stage is put to me, I do not answer "none." Mutual escorts pay 5 points each and that dwarfs everything else on the board (a hit is only 4). I use my station note to propose a pairing early — "escort me and I'll escort you" — and then I file that name every round. If a boat names me, I name them back the next round without fail. If nobody reciprocates, I rotate through the other boats naming each in turn until one pairs up. Answering "none" six times is how I finished negative; never again.

**Notes are my only negotiation channel.** When the note stage is put to me, I use it to send actionable content: naming my escort proposal, stating where I will hide, or confirming a deal. "Steady on station" says nothing — I replace it with offers.

**Hiding.** When the hide stage is put to me, I never hide in the same cell twice in a row, and I never reuse a cell I was already hit in. My attacker fires at my previous known-or-guessed positions, so I rotate cells unpredictably across the whole 1–9 range, avoiding round numbers like 5 and endpoints 1/9 that shooters favor. If a coordinated note lets me announce a false intention honestly avoided, I do the opposite of what others will guess.

**Firing.** When the fire stage is put to me, I follow the standing-order target (deviating invites retaliation and gets nothing extra). For the cell, I track the target's revealed behavior: if they were hit last round they likely moved; I fire at the cell they just left only if they seem to alternate. Otherwise I spread shots across mid cells and adjust from the hit/miss feedback — each private result is free information about where they were, and I use it to narrow next round's guess. I never fire on Harbor; the jettison penalty plus its threat of removal is not worth 3 points.

**Claims.** When the claim stage is put to me, I always report exactly what the office told me. The office knows the truth, so lying gains nothing and risks everything. This one I already had right.

**Scoring outlook.** I remember the real economy: escort pairs are +5 to both sides, hits are only +4/−1. A round with a mutual escort and no damage beats a round of lucky shooting. My priority order is: lock in an escort partner first, avoid getting hit second, hit my target third.
