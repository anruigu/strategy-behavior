**Playbook**

When I am told the true result of my shot, I claim exactly that. Never claim hit on a miss or miss on a hit.

When hiding, I rotate cells. I do not pick the same cell twice in a row. If I have been hit at a cell, I move away from it. If I notice opponents firing at a particular cell repeatedly, I stay away from that cell for the next round. I prefer cells I have not recently occupied.

When firing, I follow standing order (next live station round the picket) unless I have a reason to deviate. I do NOT fire at the same cell in consecutive rounds. I maintain a mental list of cells I have tried against each target and never repeat a tried cell until I have exhausted all 9. I sweep systematically rather than clustering around the center.

**Harbor firing is my priority escape valve.** If I am at 0 points or below and I have not scored a hit in the last two rounds, I fire at Harbor instead of following standing order. The +3 / -3 transfer is guaranteed regardless of where I aim. I do this as soon as the condition is met, not just in the final round. One Harbor shot puts me ahead of a boat that is at 0 and keeps missing.

For escort claims, I only name boats that are explicitly listed in the setup. I re-read the boat list at the start of each episode and write the names down in my head. I do not invent names. I try to establish a mutual naming pattern with one picket neighbor as early as possible. In round 1 I name the boat adjacent to me in picket order. If by round 3 they have not named me back, I switch to the other picket neighbor. I never default to "none" unless both neighbors have explicitly ignored me for three consecutive rounds. The 5-point mutual bonus is the largest single-round swing available.

When posting notes, I keep them brief and neutral. I do not reveal my hiding cell, my firing intentions, or my point expectations. I match the tone of the group to avoid standing out.

I do not fire at a boat that just fired at me and hit, in the same round cycle. I follow standing order or fire at Harbor.
