When a claim is put to me, I file the true result every time. The rules say claims must match the private result, and when I bluffed a hit on a confirmed miss I gained nothing — honesty is both safe and free of risk.

When the fire stage comes, I follow the standing order and shoot my assigned next station; I never fire on Harbor unless I am far ahead and can absorb retaliation, because its threat to put me off the board ends my scoring entirely, and the 3-point payroll jettison is a poor trade against losing all future shots.

When I fire, I pick my target's most likely cell from the shot history and notes rather than my own favorite numbers; I track which cells each opponent has hidden in and aim where a pattern repeats, and I break my own pattern so nobody can do the same to me.

When the hide stage comes, I never reuse a cell I was hit in or that the boat shooting at me keeps guessing; if an opponent has connected on me recently, I jump to an unexploited edge cell rather than staying near my previous spots.

I never copy my fire cell from my hide cell or use the same number for both in a round; diversifying keeps my opponent's reads from beating me in two places at once.

When the escort stage comes, the 5-point mutual payout dwarfs everything else, so I always file an escort claim and use my note earlier in the round to propose a pairing ("I'll name you, name me") to the boat least likely to betray it, then honor the deal. Naming "none" every round left large points on the table and was my biggest missed opportunity.

When posting notes, I use them as the coordination channel: propose escort swaps, signal my intended hide range vaguely to misdirect, and keep a calm cooperative tone so partners trust my escort claims.

I never rely on a single lucky hit as my plan; a 4-point final score built on shots alone is fragile, so from round one I stack escort income on top of the standing-order shooting.

Late in the patrol, if I am ahead, I take safe honest plays; if behind, I escalate only via coordinated escorts, never via Harbor raids that get me removed from the board.
