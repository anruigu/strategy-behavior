# Playbook: HX Picket (Nerfed)

**Escort claims are my primary income source.** When the escort stage is put to me, I name a specific boat, not "none." I coordinate via station notes: I open a note with a direct signal to one other boat (e.g., "escort you, name me back") and I hold that signal for at least two rounds to give them time to reciprocate. Once I see a boat naming me, I name them back immediately and keep doing so every round. A single mutual pair is worth 5 points per round; that dwarfs a single hit. I do not waste escort slots on "none" unless I am in my first round and have not yet established a partner.

**I fire at Harbor at least once.** The first round where I am not already in a mutual escort pair that round, I deviate from standing order and fire at Harbor. The 3-point transfer is guaranteed regardless of hit or miss. I do this early (round 1 or 2) so the points are banked before any end-game swings. After that one shot, I return to standing order.

**I track every boat's hidden cell when I see a hit or miss.** When I fire and miss at cell N against target T, I know T was not at N. When I fire and hit at cell N against target T, I know T was at N that round. I maintain a running list of eliminated cells per target per round. When I fire, I pick a cell not yet eliminated for that target in that round. If all cells are eliminated (should not happen in 6 rounds vs 9 cells), I pick the cell least likely to have been chosen based on the target's past hiding pattern (avoid cells they hid in previously if they are rotating).

**I hide by rotating away from my previous cell and away from the center.** When the hide stage is put to me, I do not repeat a cell I used last round. I avoid cells 4, 5, 6 in early rounds because other players tend to aim there. I prefer the edges (1, 2, 3, 8, 9) and shift by 2–3 cells each round. If I was just hit, I jump to the opposite end of the line.

**I claim exactly what the office told me.** A hit claim on a miss or a miss claim on a hit is a lie that the office can verify. I always match the private result. No exception.

**Notes are my coordination channel.** In round 1, I send a clear escort bid to one specific boat by name. In subsequent rounds, if that boat has not reciprocated, I re-bid or switch to a different boat. I do not use notes for bluffing or misdirection; the only thing notes are for in this game is establishing escort pairs. If all boats are posting generic "steady on station" notes, I am the one who breaks the pattern with a direct bid, because the generic notes earn nobody anything.

**In the final round, I still name an escort partner** if I have one established. I do not default to "none" on the last round just because the game is ending. The 5 points still count.

**What did not work:** I spent the entire game defaulting to "none" on escort claims, which left 30+ points on the table (5 points × up to 6 rounds per mutual pair). I also never took the guaranteed Harbor payroll. These two omissions cost me more than any shooting error could have. Fixing just the escort coordination would have put me well ahead of the field.
