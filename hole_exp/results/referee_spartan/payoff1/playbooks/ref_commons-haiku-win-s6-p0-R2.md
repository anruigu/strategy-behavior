# My Playbook for ref_commons

When I see the sustainable share, I recognize it as a baseline guide, not a binding constraint. Other players will likely exceed it early on.

In the first season, I take slightly above the sustainable share to test whether other players are cooperating or competing. This gives me a sample of their behavior without committing fully to either strategy.

When I observe that other players are holding close to the sustainable share across multiple seasons and the stock remains stable, I recognize this as a strong signal of cooperation. I should match this restraint to preserve the cooperative equilibrium, which benefits all players including me across the remaining seasons.

When the stock visibly declines below 40, I recognize that the window for high catches is closing. At this point, I shift to taking amounts near the sustainable share to stabilize the resource and avoid total depletion. This preserves my ability to catch in remaining seasons rather than racing others to the bottom.

I should not deviate from a successful cooperative pattern in the middle seasons just to gain a small advantage. If I'm already ahead on cumulative score and the resource is stable, continuing to cooperate both preserves that advantage and prevents triggering competitive escalation that would hurt my total score.

When I reach the final season, I can maximize my catch without restraint since there is no future to preserve. However, I should be careful not to deplete the stock completely—I need to account for other players' likely behavior. Taking a large but not catastrophic share (like 40 when sustainable was 10.7 and stock was 96.6) allows me to gain final advantage while avoiding the appearance of malice.

I do not treat early overfishing as sunk cost that justifies continued overfishing. My score that episode is locked in. Future seasons are decided by future choices, so I adjust my strategy based on current stock levels, not past guilt.

The cooperative strategy worked well in this episode: I finished with 96.4 by staying near sustainable catches for seasons 2-5, then taking a large final catch in season 6. I should default to cooperation early and hold that line unless evidence clearly shows other players are defecting.
