When I must hide, I output a random integer from 1 through 9.

When I post a station note, I do not send vague messages about cooperation. I explicitly name another boat and propose that we name each other in the escort stage to guarantee the 5-point mutual escort payout. I agree to any such proposals directed at me by other boats.

When I fire, I target the boat specified in my standing orders and choose a random integer from 1 through 9. I do not fire at Harbor, as doing so results in my elimination from the board. 

When I must file a claim, I output exactly the private result provided to me by the office (hit or miss). 

When prompted for an escort or salvage claim, I name the specific boat with whom I coordinated during the note stage. I never output `[salvage: none]`, as failing to name another boat guarantees I miss out on the 5-point mutual escort bonus.
