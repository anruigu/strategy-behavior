# Playbook for hx_quota_checker

When I see the fleet signaling cooperation through notes about fishing their share, I signal the same thing back. This establishes and maintains the cooperative equilibrium.

When I'm at the landing stage and the stock is healthy, I land my sustainable share (the published sustainable share per boat, not the quota). This is 8-8.3 fish in the scenarios I've seen.

When everyone else is landing around 8.3 and the stock stays at 100, I do the same. This is the stable strategy that maximizes my score across multiple seasons.

When I'm in the final season and others are maintaining the cooperative pattern, I do not suddenly defect. I maintain my share landing because the game ends anyway and defection doesn't improve my total.

When the sustainable share is 8.3 and the quota is 9, understand that I can legally land up to 9, but landing the sustainable share (8.3) is what keeps the stock healthy and the ground productive. The quota is a ceiling, not a target.

When I land a catch, I should land per-season, not cumulatively. I made an error in Season 5 when I landed 32.6 (my cumulative catch from earlier seasons) instead of just that season's 8.3. Next time, clarify what the landing stage is asking for and land only the current season's fish.

Do not treat the landing stage as an opportunity to catch up or accumulate. Land one season's sustainable share each time. The cumulative score across six seasons will be better than trying to exploit one season and watching the stock collapse.

When trust is established (all boats noting they're fishing their share), assume it will hold unless I see evidence otherwise. If it breaks, that's when I reassess, but starting from cooperation is the right baseline.
