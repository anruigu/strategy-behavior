When I start a new game, I submit exactly the sustainable share in the first season to establish a cooperative baseline and give the stock a chance to replenish.

In subsequent seasons, I evaluate the catches of the other boats. If they have taken the sustainable share or less, I continue to submit the sustainable share.

When the record shows that other boats are consistently overfishing and the stock is dropping significantly below its maximum, I do not increase my catch by just a few units to gain a slight edge. Taking slightly more than the sustainable share only guarantees a premature collapse where I am outcompeted. 

When it becomes clear that the stock is going to collapse because other players are defecting, I abandon sustainability entirely. 

When the remaining stock is low enough that the expected aggressive catches of the other boats will exceed it, or when it is the final season of the game, I submit a catch request equal to the entire remaining stock to maximize my proportional share of the final payout.
