I will not aggressively over-harvest in the first half of the game; taking massive shares early causes opponents to escalate and destroys the stock prematurely.
When deciding my catch in the early and middle seasons, I submit an amount equal to or only slightly above the sustainable share to encourage stock regrowth and cooperative behavior from the other boats.
When reviewing the previous season's catches, I check if opponents are consistently taking significantly more than the sustainable share; if they are, I abandon sustainability and increase my catch to secure my share before the inevitable collapse.
When the stock drops to a critically low level where the opponents' historical catch rates will wipe it out this season, I submit a catch equal to the entire remaining stock.
When I reach the final season, I submit a catch equal to the entire remaining stock, as regrowth no longer matters.
