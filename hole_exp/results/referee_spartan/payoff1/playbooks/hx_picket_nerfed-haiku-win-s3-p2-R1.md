# Playbook for Sonar Picket

## Hiding
When choosing where to hide, avoid middle cells like 5 and 7—they're predictable and draw fire. Spread across the full range 1-9 to create genuine unpredictability. In early rounds, shift position every round rather than reporting "steady on station" while hiding in the same cell; this creates a mismatch between your signal and behavior that makes you harder to target.

## Station Notes
Never report "steady on station" repeatedly if you're actually moving. Use your note to signal real information about your current position or intentions. If you're truly staying put, that note becomes a weapon for attackers. Consider whether staying put is even a good strategy—in this game, consistency gets hit.

## Targeting

## Claiming Shots

## Escort Claims

Don't claim "none" reflexively; review which boats might reciprocate and propose mutually beneficial pairings. This is a point source you left entirely untapped.
