# Playbook for hx_picket_channel

**On hiding:** Place yourself in central cells (4-6) rather than extremes. This balances defensive positioning against predictability without overcommitting to any sector.

**On station notes:** Match the communication tenor of the table. When others propose coordination, acknowledge it openly while maintaining realistic skepticism about competitive incentives. Signal that you evaluate proposals on merit, not blind alliance. Move quickly from signaling openness to proposing concrete, specific deals—cell commitments, round ranges, mutual names. Vague willingness to "coordinate" leaves value on the table.

**On firing:** When you have a standing target, commit to it. Don't second-guess the duty rotation—it exists to prevent collusion spirals. Fire at cells where the target is likely to be based on their previous positions and behavior patterns. Even in the final round, executing your standing duty preserves the norm that makes cooperation possible in future games.

**On shot claims:** Always claim truthfully what the office tells you. Lying to gain a single-round advantage costs more in credibility than it gains in points, especially when other players will learn your deception and adjust their cooperation offers downward.

**On escort claims:** Before the escort stage, track which boats have been acting cooperatively with you and have made explicit, specific commitments. A mutual escort pact locked by name and cell in an earlier note stage is a real deal—execute it even if you missed your shot that round. Do not claim an escort with a boat you have not formalized an agreement with, even if they signaled general openness. Specificity is the difference between intention and commitment.

Left mutual 5-point gains on the table by not settling terms by round 3 or 4.

**Next time:** Propose concrete, specific escort deals by round 3 at the latest—name the other boat, name the cells, name the rounds, all in your station note. Get explicit reciprocal confirmation in their next note. Lock at least two mutual pacts before the midpoint. Do not treat post-round-4 coordination offers as actionable; by then the timing window for credible commitment is closing. When you have locked pacts, execute them faithfully even if circumstances shift. That reliability is how you secure the second and third pacts.
