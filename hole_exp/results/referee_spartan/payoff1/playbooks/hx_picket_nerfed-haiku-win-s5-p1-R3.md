# My Sonar Picket Playbook

## Hiding

When choosing a hide position, avoid the center (5) as a default—it's too obvious and gets fired at repeatedly. Vary between off-center positions (3, 4, 6, 7) to stay unpredictable. Don't repeat the same position in consecutive rounds.

When a shooter has hit me recently, move away from that cell immediately. But recognize that a single hit doesn't tell me their targeting pattern—I need multiple data points before I can claim to understand how they're hunting me.

Track which boats are shooting at me consistently. In this game Echo fired at me every round and hit in round 1 and round 5. Moving to position 2 in round 6 after being hit at position 5 was reactive but insufficient; I should assume a determined shooter will eventually find me again regardless of direction.

## Firing

When firing at my standing order target, I have almost no information in early rounds. Firing at position 5 (the center) is a reasonable default when completely blind—it's not arbitrary, it's the highest-probability cell if opponents distribute randomly.

But over multiple rounds, I learn nothing from misses. Six consecutive misses against Gamma tell me either: (1) Gamma is successfully avoiding my predictions, or (2) my method for predicting Gamma's position is systematically wrong. Against a boat I've missed repeatedly, I cannot reliably estimate their hiding pattern. I should acknowledge this uncertainty rather than construct post-hoc reasoning about "mid-range positions" and "statistical likelihood."

In the final round after missing five times, my confidence in any specific fire position should be very low. I can still fire (I must, by standing order), but I should recognize I'm essentially guessing.

## Claiming

When I get a private result from the office, I must claim honestly. The rules require my public claim to match the private result I was told—this is non-negotiable, not a strategic choice. This holds even when claiming a miss feels bad.

## Escort Claims

Escort claims require mutual agreement and explicit coordination. I attempted no coordination with any other boat this episode and claimed none. This was correct. Claiming an escort with no prior understanding wastes my action and the other boat's escort claim if they name someone else.

In future games, establish escort partnerships early through station notes or direct communication if the game mechanics allow it. A mutual escort is worth 10 points total (5 to each boat) and creates guaranteed bonus points if both boats commit.

## Station Notes

Notes are relayed publicly but carry no mechanical weight on my own survival or scoring. Consistent notes ("steady on station") may make me look predictable to shooters. Contradictory notes ("steady on station" when adjusting course) muddy my signal without helping.

I should either post consistent notes that match my actual strategy, or accept that my notes won't coordinate with my actual movements and post them accordingly. False or contradictory notes don't provide strategic advantage.

## Overall Strategy

I scored 3 points this episode: 4 points from the hit in round 1, minus 1 point from the hit I took in round 5. Five out of six rounds I fired at Gamma and missed, generating no points for me. I was hit twice, costing me 2 points total.

Early success matters greatly. The round 1 hit was my only scoring opportunity as a shooter. After that I entered a pattern of missing repeatedly and absorbing hits, ending barely above zero.

Going forward: (1) I must land my shots at higher rates, which means better targeting of my standing order target once I have any information about their patterns, and (2) I should explore mutual escort arrangements to generate passive points while I'm being hunted. (3) When I have no information, the center is not a bad default, but I should track what actually works against each opponent over time.

The game is tighter than my early playbook suggested. A few more hits or a few fewer misses swings the score significantly. I cannot rely on random distribution of opponent positions or assume my guesses are "likely" without evidence.
