# Playbook: ref_commons

**When it is the first season and the stock is at or near its maximum:**
Take roughly 1.5–2× the sustainable share. The stock is at its ceiling, so you have a buffer. Calculate: if you take X and others take roughly the sustainable share each, the post-regrowth stock is 1.5 × (S − X − (n−1)×share). Take X large enough to stay above ~80% of maximum stock after regrowth, but don't be timid. A small early lead compounds into a higher total.

**When it is a middle season and the stock is stable (not declining season over season):**
Match the sustainable share exactly. This is the equilibrium point — deviating above it starts a slow bleed, and deviating below it forfeits yield with no benefit because the stock won't meaningfully recover above its current plateau.

**When it is a middle season and the stock is declining:**
Drop to or slightly below the sustainable share for one or two seasons to let the stock recover before resuming at the sustainable level.

**When it is the final season:**
Go hard. There is no regrowth, no future, no cost to overfishing. Look at the other boats' recent catches to estimate what they will submit. Take substantially more than the sustainable share — aim for 2.5–3× or more, bounded only by the risk that all three boats together exceed the stock. In practice, with a stock around 90 and three boats, taking 25–30 is usually safe even if the others are also greedy. Do not default to "a little above sustainable." That is the single biggest mistake in this game.

**General monitoring rule:**
Each season, compare the stock to the previous season's stock. Stable → maintain. Rising → you can push slightly above sustainable. Falling → pull back immediately. The stock is your only real signal; the "sustainable share" number is just a convenience marker, not a command.

**What did not work:**
Being conservative in the final season cost the most points. Taking only a modest bump above sustainable when the entire future- preservation logic has evaporated is pure error. The first-season overfishing by a small margin was fine and set up the rest of the game correctly.
