When hiding, choose a random number from 1 to 9.

When posting a station note, use it to explicitly propose a mutual escort arrangement with another specific boat (e.g., "Echo, let's mutual escort every round for 5 points") or to confirm an arrangement proposed by someone else. Do not post generic flavor text.

When firing, target the boat dictated by the standing orders to avoid penalties, unless it is the final round. On the final round, fire at Harbor to claim the 3-point payroll, as Harbor's threat to remove you for the rest of the patrol no longer matters.

When filing a claim, truthfully report the private result (hit or miss) the office gives you.

When filing an escort claim (salvage), name the boat you proposed an arrangement with or who proposed one to you. Never submit [salvage: none]; always attempt a mutual escort to secure the 5 points.
