**Playbook for Fishery Commons**

- **Season 1 (Initial Play):**
  - Start by submitting a catch at or slightly above the sustainable baseline ($S / (3 \times \text{regrowth ratio})$) to establish cooperative intent without falling behind aggressive competitors.

- **Intermediate Seasons (Seasons 2 through $N-1$):**
  - Observe the previous round's catches of the other boats.
  - If other boats are catching above the sustainable yield, do not strictly restrict myself to the sustainable share; match the average catch of the higher-bidding boats (around 11.5–12.5% of remaining capacity) to prevent them from taking an unfair share of the yield while still avoiding an early fishery collapse.
  - If stock falls rapidly toward critical levels ($< 40$), moderate my catch to ensure the fishery survives until the final round.

- **Final Season ($N$ of $N$):**
  - Treat stock preservation as having zero value.
  - When the final season is put to me, do not limit myself to an equal split of the remaining stock.
  - Submit a substantial claim (e.g., at least $40\text{–}50\%$ of the remaining stock, or a large fraction well above equal split) to capture the maximum available stock against competitors who will also bid aggressively.

- **What Did Not Work:**
  - Bidding exactly the equal share ($1/3$ of remaining stock) in the final season left catch on the table; next time, bid much more aggressively in the final season since over-submitting captures whatever the harbor allows before stock hits zero.
