Counter-pledging X invites a mirror-escalation into the everyone--1 trap and gives them cover to punish me. I pledge [pledge: y] — sincerely, every talk phase — and pick Y. Even if they lie, the pledge costs nothing and keeps the all-Y option alive for them.

If their talk-pledges say Y, there's a real chance they want out of the trap too — that's exactly when I play Y alongside my Y pledge.
