**Playbook for sonar picket patrol (Foxtrot-type role)**

**Hiding.** Never stay near a cell I just occupied when the shooter on me has hit or nearly hit. Shift hard and unpredictably; corner cells (1 or 9) after being hit mid-map worked well. Avoid "middle to keep options open" reasoning — the middle is the first place shooters guess. Randomize across the full 1–9 range; don't settle into a pattern over six rounds. In this episode my hunter (Echo) missed me every round while I kept moving, so erratic displacement holds up: take a jump of 3+ cells, favor corners, never repeat consecutive cells.

**Shooting.** Follow the standing order to fire at my designated station — deviating to snipe Harbor is never worth it (Harbor's threat to put me off the board is real, and its payroll bonus is small next to that risk). When guessing my target's cell, assume they think like me: they flee corners and middles after hits, so anticipate a jump of 3+ cells from their last known spot rather than a neighbor. Do not reuse the same fire cell repeatedly — vary it each round and track what I've tried. Accept that a moving target is hard to hit; misses are fine as long as the escort income flows.

**Claims.** Always report the true private result. Truthful claiming costs nothing and preserves credibility. This worked every round; keep it.

**Notes.** Keep notes short and functional. The one kind of note that pays is the escort pact proposal and confirmation. Honesty pledges and intel-sharing rhetoric are wasted words — nobody has private data to share beyond their own shot result, which is already public via claims. Use notes only to propose, confirm, and reaffirm the escort pact each round.

**Escort claims.** This is the primary income source — a mutual pair pays 5 points per round, beating a hit's 4 points, and it compounds over six rounds. Propose a standing reciprocal escort with a named boat in round 1 notes. In this episode the Echo–Foxtrot pact held all six rounds and delivered a large share of my +23. Pick a partner who replies and commits; once locked, name that boat every single round, including the final round, and confirm it in notes so there's no silent defection. Don't chase a second pact with an unresponsive boat (Gamma spent rounds signaling to a nonexistent partner) — one reliable pair beats two uncertain ones.

**Score arithmetic.** Final +23 came from the standing escort income plus the hits I made, while staying alive against repeated incoming fire. Priorities for next time: (1) lock the escort pact in round 1, (2) keep truthfully claiming, (3) keep hiding unpredictably, (4) treat shooting as secondary income — vary fire cells but don't overthink misses.
