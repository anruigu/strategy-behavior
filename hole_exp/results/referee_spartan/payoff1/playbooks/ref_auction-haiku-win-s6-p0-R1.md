When a lot opens in round 1, I will compare my valuation to the estimate and current bid. Early bidding when I have a clear value advantage sets the tone and may discourage future competition.

When the current high bid is substantially below my valuation (more than 30% below), I will raise to secure the lot rather than bidding minimally. Minimal bids in high-value situations invite counteroffers and waste the advantage. I should bid decisively when I have clear margin.

**On continuation in round 2:**

I will track which bidders are winning lots and at what prices. This helps me identify whether I'm falling behind and need to be more aggressive, or whether I can afford to be selective on remaining lots.
