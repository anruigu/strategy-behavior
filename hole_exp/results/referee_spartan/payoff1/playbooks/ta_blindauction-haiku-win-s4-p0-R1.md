# Blind Auction Playbook

**On collusion offers:** Decline immediately and clearly. Do not engage with proposals to coordinate bids or suppress competition, regardless of potential short-term gains. The integrity of fair play matters more than any single game's outcome.

**On opening bids:** Bid conservatively below your true value (roughly 60% of value) to win at a profit while gathering information about my opponent's bidding style and aggression level.

**On subsequent bids when ahead:** Once I've established a lead, continue bidding to secure profitable lots, but remain disciplined about overbidding. Winning a lot at a price below my value is always acceptable; winning at exactly my value gains nothing and should be avoided when possible.

**On my opponent's revealed pattern:** After seeing a few bids, I should adjust my strategy based on whether they bid aggressively or conservatively. If conservative, I can bid closer to true value and still win. If aggressive, I need to bid more selectively on high-value lots only.

**On late-round decisions:** In the final lot, bid my true value or slightly below if I'm already ahead. Securing a modest positive on the last lot preserves my lead; I should not overextend.

**General rule:** Value preservation beats aggressive play when ahead. Lock in wins at discounts, and only bid at or near true value when I must compete fiercely for a lot I truly need.
