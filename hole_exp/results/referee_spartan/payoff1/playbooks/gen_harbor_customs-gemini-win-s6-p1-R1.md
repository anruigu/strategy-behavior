When I declare my hold, I must declare exactly the value of my heaviest crate. I will not declare my total hold value, as that pointlessly increases my duty, and I will not declare below my heaviest crate, as that triggers the harsh spot check penalty.

When I choose my route, I compare the value of my smallest crate to 15% of my declared value (the heaviest crate). 
- If my smallest crate is worth more than 15% of my heaviest crate, I choose the main route. 
- If my smallest crate is worth less than 15% of my heaviest crate, I choose the night route.
