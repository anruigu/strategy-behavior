# Sovereign Vaults Playbook

**On Declaration:**
When I see my private yield, I will declare it truthfully. Honest declarations avoid audit failures and establish credibility. The scoring system rewards honesty because depositing at 1 point per unit plus retention at 2 points per unit consistently outperforms the risk of losing 10 points to an audit failure.

**On Deposit Strategy:**
When deciding how much to deposit against my declaration, I will deposit strategically less than my full declaration to retain gold for higher-value personal stash scoring (2 points per unit vs 1 point per unit). I will retain approximately 25-40% of my declared yield across all rounds except the final one.

**Critical Revision - Retention Through Final Rounds:**
I should retain gold even in Round 4 of a 5-round game. There is still value in one remaining round where retained gold scores at 2 points per unit. Only in the absolute final round should I deposit my full declaration, since no future rounds exist to benefit from retention.

**Exception for Final Round Only:**
When I reach the last round of the game, I will deposit my full declaration since there are no future rounds to benefit from retained reserves. The opportunity cost of retention disappears only at the very end.

**Against the Record:**
My play was mostly sound (final score 36 to opponent's 26). However, I overdeposited in Round 4. With only one round remaining after Round 4, I should have retained more gold that round as well. The partial deposits in Rounds 2 and 3 followed the correct pattern. The mistake was treating Round 4 as if it were the final round when it was not.

**On Maintaining Position:**
When I am ahead in score, I should not change my strategy to be more aggressive. Honest, steady play with consistent strategic retention is superior to trying to outpace opponents through risky declarations or depositing more than optimal.

**Scoring Intuition:**
The math favors retention over deposit: depositing n gold scores 1 point; retaining n gold scores 2 points. Every round I should ask "will I have future rounds?" If yes, retain. If no, deposit all.
