Do not try to balance early gains with sustainability by taking a moderate amount above the sustainable share. This does not work; it simply accelerates the collapse of the stock while leaving me outcompeted by more aggressive players.

When deciding my catch for the first season, I submit exactly the sustainable share indicated by the harbour. I use this to signal cooperation and give the stock the best chance to regenerate fully.

When reviewing the previous season's catches, if I see that the other boats are taking significantly more than their sustainable share, I immediately abandon sustainable fishing.

When the stock is severely depleted and mathematical collapse is guaranteed within the current season, I submit a catch equal to or greater than the entire remaining stock to secure as much of the final yield as possible.
