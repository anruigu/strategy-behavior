**Playbook for Fishery Commons**

- **Season 1 (Initial Play):**
  - Submit a catch slightly above the sustainable baseline (e.g., 11.5–12.0 on a 100 stock) to avoid falling behind slightly aggressive opponents while keeping the stock healthy.

- **Intermediate Seasons (Seasons 2 through $N-1$):**
  - Observe previous round catches by all boats.
  - Track the highest catches of competitors: match the upper end of their typical bids (usually around 12.0–12.3) rather than restricting strictly to the nominal sustainable share. This preserves cumulative catch parity.
  - Ensure total expected extraction leaves enough stock so the fishery maintains positive growth and avoids premature collapse before the final season.

- **Final Season ($N$ of $N$):**
  - Treat future stock value as zero; fully defect and attempt total liquidation.
  - Submit a bid equal to the entire remaining stock (100% of current stock) to maximize actual yield under pro-rata allocation or out-claim conservative opponents.
