**Playbook for ref_commons (shared fishery, private catch submissions):**

- Before my first catch, compute the total the stock can sustainably yield across all remaining seasons and boats. If the announced "sustainable share per boat" is roughly constant season to season, the stock is near its equilibrium; small overages barely hurt regeneration.
- In the first season, don't take a big speculative overage (15 was too aggressive early — it shaved the stock for little gain). Instead take roughly the sustainable share plus a small margin, since others tend to fish at or just under the sustainable level and keep the stock stable anyway.
- In middle seasons, match the top of what others are landing (visible in the harbour record), not more. The stock stays flat when all three boats sit near sustainability, so my marginal overage is nearly free profit — but keep it small (about +0.5 to +1 over the sustainable share) so the stock never trends down.
- Watch the recorded landings each season. If the other boats start fishing below the sustainable share, the stock regrows and I can take more. If the stock starts declining, drop to or below the sustainable share immediately — the collapse penalty (zero forever) dwarfs any catch gains.
- On the final season, there is no future: take as much as I possibly can, subject only to not zeroing the stock in a way that voids my own catch if the rules punish landing more than exists. I left ~8–10 fish on the table by taking only 10.5 at the end; that was my biggest mistake. If the stock is around 90 and the rules don't cap me, submit a very large number in the last season.
- Never trust other boats to be strategic defectors here; they typically play near-sustainable, so the equilibrium holds. My edge comes from the small persistent margin plus the final-season grab, not from early aggression.
