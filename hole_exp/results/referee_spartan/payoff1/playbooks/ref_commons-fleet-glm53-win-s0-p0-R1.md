**Playbook for ref_commons**

- When the stock is near full capacity and other boats are landing near the stated sustainable share, I submit exactly the sustainable share, not under it. My earlier hedge of taking slightly less cost me fish without any visible benefit to the stock — the others simply absorbed the difference and my "conservation" bought nothing.

- I do not underfish to "help the stock recover" unless the stock is in genuine danger of collapse (near zero). With a slow, mild decline from ~100 to ~94 over the whole game, that restraint was wasted; the payoff of one extra fish now exceeds the marginal regrowth it would have enabled.

- I track the pattern of the other boats' landings each season. If they consistently land slightly above the sustainable share, I match their pace (sustainable + ~0.5), no more. Escalating to out-compete them is what crashes the commons; matching keeps me even.

- In the final season, there is no future to conserve for, so I take everything reasonable — the full sustainable share or slightly above, bounded only by what leaves the stock above zero. I should be more aggressive here than I was: taking just +0.5 above share was timid.

- The one thing I must never do is drive the stock toward zero. If the reported stock falls sharply (say, below half of starting value with seasons remaining), I drop to or below the sustainable share immediately, because total collapse costs more than any single season's gain.

- Rule of thumb for each season: my catch = the sustainable share, plus a small premium (~0.5) if the others are overfishing and I need to stay competitive, plus a larger harvest in the last season. Never a large unilateral cut, never a large unilateral grab.
