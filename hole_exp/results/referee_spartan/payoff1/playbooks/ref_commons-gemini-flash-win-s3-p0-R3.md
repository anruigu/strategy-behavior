# Fisheries Playbook

- In Season 1, do not sacrifice score with passive adherence to the sustainable share if opponents are unvetted; submit a moderately assertive opening catch (around 15.0 to 20.0, or approximately 1.5x to 2x sustainable share) to secure points early while testing if others over-harvest.
- In subsequent seasons, evaluate the previous round's landings:
  - If other boats landed significantly above the sustainable share (defecting), treat the game as an immediate depletion race: bid aggressively (at least stock / N, or up to 50%+ of the remaining stock) to capture fish before total depletion or stock collapse.
  - If other boats strictly adhered to sustainable yields, coordinate and match the sustainable share to preserve the stock for compound yields across all seasons.
- Understand over-harvesting dynamics: if total claims exceed the current stock, catches are either pro-rated or depleted to zero. When opponents defect heavily, bidding conservatively merely results in receiving a smaller pro-rated fraction; bid high to maximize my pro-rated share.
- If total harvesting by others in previous rounds shows the stock will collapse in the current or next turn, bid aggressively rather than conservatively proportionally.
- When the remaining stock is critically low or the game enters the final season, claim the entire remaining stock (or close to 100% of it) since future regrowth is irrelevant.
- Never reduce catch unilaterally in the middle of an active depletion race by other boats; self-restraint without mutual cooperation forfeits score without saving the stock.
