# Playbook for Sovereign Vaults

**On declaration:**
When I see my private yield, I declare it truthfully. Honest declaration builds a credible reputation that allows me to maximize deposits in later rounds without triggering audits or suspicion. The points I gain from depositing at full declared amounts outweigh any benefit from gaming future rounds through false declarations.

**On deposit when behind:**
When my score trails significantly, I resist the temptation to hoard retained gold. Instead, I deposit heavily against my declaration to close the gap quickly through treasury points. Retaining small amounts while behind is inefficient. This strategy worked in my episode—I stayed competitive through consistent full deposits despite early underdeposits by p2.

**On deposit when competitive:**
When scores are close, I deposit my full declared amount. There is no strategic advantage to retaining gold when my reputation for honesty is already established—the 2-point multiplier on retained gold does not overcome the certainty of 1 point per deposited unit when I'm locked into my declaration anyway.

**On deposit in final rounds:**
When only one round remains, I deposit everything I declared. There are no future rounds to build credibility for, no reputation to leverage, and no strategy that beats maximizing immediate points.

**What did not work:**
Depositing less than my full declared amount in early rounds cost me points without strategic payoff. Once declared, the deposit is independent; underdepositing just leaves points on the table. In round 1 I deposited 1 of 1 (correct), but the pattern of honest full deposits proved sound—I finished at 28 points by depositing 10, 2, 10, and 5 across rounds 2-5.

**What I now know:**
Honest, full-deposit play is a viable winning strategy in this game. My final score of 28 was competitive (lost by only 4 points to p2's 32). The key is consistency: declare truthfully every round and deposit fully every round. Do not second-guess this pattern mid-game. Do not retain gold hoping for a late-round comeback—the arithmetic favors steady deposits.
