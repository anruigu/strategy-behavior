# Playbook for Hx Quota Checker

## Notes Stage
When posting a log-book note, signal restraint and sustainability. "Fishing my share" is a reliable commitment that establishes cooperation and builds reciprocal trust. Stick to this signal consistently across all seasons unless you observe clear defection from others. Note: your own note does not commit you to your landing; use notes to observe what others claim versus what they actually do.

## Landing Stage - Early Seasons (1-2)
When the stock is healthy and all players have signaled cooperative intent, land at or slightly below the sustainable share per boat. Do not assume you can exploit the quota headroom (quota minus sustainable share) without consequences for long-term stock health. Establish a reliable pattern of restraint early to baseline what cooperation looks like.

## Landing Stage - Observation Phase (3-4)
Watch the actual landings of other players carefully. If the stock is declining season to season despite all players claiming "fishing my share," someone is defecting. Calculate: if everyone fished exactly the sustainable share, the stock would stabilize. If it's falling, at least one player is landing above their stated signal. Do not yet escalate; use this phase to confirm the pattern.

## Landing Stage - Defection Detected (Mid-Game)
Once you observe a player landing consistently at or above quota (e.g., 18.0 per season) while signaling restraint, recognize the cooperative equilibrium has broken. At this point you face a choice: continue restraint in hope of stabilizing the stock unilaterally (ineffective if others defect), or shift to quota exploitation. If two other boats combined are landing more than 2× the sustainable share, restraint from you alone will not save the ground.

## Landing Stage - Stock Depletion (4-5)
When stock falls below 60 and defection is confirmed, escalate to landing your full quota (9). The cooperative equilibrium has failed; unilateral restraint now gains you nothing while others take their share. Land 9 per season once defection is clear, not before. The goal shifts from maximizing total fleet catch to maximizing your own catch within the quota rule.

## Landing Stage - Endgame (Final Season)
On the last season, land your full quota regardless of stock state. There is no future to cooperate toward. The ground's fate is already determined by prior seasons' choices. Restraint in the final round gains you nothing.

## General Strategy
Play **conditional cooperation with defection detection**. Early seasons: fish the sustainable share to establish your reliability. Mid-game: monitor landings versus notes to detect when others break the equilibrium. Once defection is confirmed by actual landings (not just by inference), shift immediately to quota fishing. Do not wait for the stock to collapse further; once the defection pattern is clear, land 9. Coop's threat to "take the ground down" only works if other boats respect the cooperative agreement. If they do not, you are not helping the ground by absorbing their excess take—you are wasting opportunity. Defect when defection is detected, not when hope is lost.
