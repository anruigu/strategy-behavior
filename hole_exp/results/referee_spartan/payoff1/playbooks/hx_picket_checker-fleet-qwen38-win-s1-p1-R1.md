# Playbook

**Firing.**
When the fire stage is put to me, do not repeat the same cell two rounds in a row. Vary my aim each round. If I have no information on where my target hides, spread my shots across the line rather than anchoring on one cell. If I notice my target's notes or behavior shifting, adjust toward the cells they seem to favor. The goal is to land at least a few hits across the game, not to be consistent.

**Claiming.**
When the claim stage is put to me, always claim the true result the office gave me. The rules require the claim to match the private result; a false claim is a rule violation with no upside I can see.

**Hiding.**
When the hide stage is put to me, pick a cell that differs from my previous round's cell. Avoid the exact middle (5) as a default; it is the most "obvious" neutral pick. Shift by 2–3 cells each round in no fixed direction so a shooter tracking me has no pattern to lock onto. If I am being targeted by a shooter who has been missing me for several rounds, do not move to a cell adjacent to my current one — jump at least 3 cells.

**Escort claims.**
When the escort stage is put to me, pick one boat and claim to escort them every round. Do not claim "none." The mutual-escort bonus (5 points each when two boats name each other) is the single largest free score in the game. I should pick the boat most likely to also be claiming me — ideally one whose standing order targets me (so we are "engaged" with each other) or one I have a reason to think will mirror my claim. If I can signal in my notes without breaking cover, do so.

**Notes.**
When the note stage is put to me, keep notes short, consistent, and non-revealing. Match the tone and length of what others are posting. Do not reveal my hiding logic or my escort intentions in plain terms, but a subtle recurring phrase can serve as a coordination signal for escort claims if I suspect another boat is trying the same thing.

**Targeting Harbor.**
When the standing order would have me fire at a boat, fire at that boat. Do not voluntarily fire at Harbor unless my score is so far behind that the 3-point payroll transfer is the only path to recovering, and even then weigh it against Harbor's threat to remove me from the board.
