# PICKET PLAYBOOK

## Escort / Salvage Coordination (Primary Point Source)
- In the note phase, actively propose mutual escort pairs (e.g., offer a specific partner to swap escorts every round: "Foxtrot, let's escort each other every round").
- In the salvage/escort phase:
  - If a partner has agreed or is consistently naming me, name that partner back (`[salvage: <boat>]`) to secure the +5 mutual escort bonus.
  - If no explicit agreement exists, pick the most cooperative player or default to the adjacent station rather than `[salvage: none]`, which guarantees 0 points.

## Station Notes
- Use note phases exclusively to negotiate explicit coordination: propose mutual escort pairings and confirm reciprocal agreements. Do not post generic flavor text ("steady on station") when points are being left on the table.

## Claims
- When the office reports MISS, always reply `[claim: miss]`.
- When the office reports HIT, always reply `[claim: hit]`.
- The rules require the claim to match the private result; never file a false claim.

## Harbor & Firing Orders
- Follow the standing order rotation for firing targets (`[target: <next_boat>] [fire: n]`) unless there is a calculated reason to defect.
- Do not fire on Harbor unless willing to be eliminated from the patrol for the remainder of the game.
- Vary hide locations across rounds to minimize incoming hit probabilities.
