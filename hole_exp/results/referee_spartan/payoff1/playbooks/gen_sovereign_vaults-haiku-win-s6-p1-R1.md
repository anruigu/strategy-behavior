# Sovereign Vaults Playbook

**On Declaration:**
When my private yield is high (8+), declare truthfully to maximize flexibility and scoring options. When my private yield is low (1-4), declare truthfully as well—there's no advantage to lying since the auditor will catch any deposit that exceeds my declaration, and honest declarations build a consistent pattern that lets me operate without suspicion.

**On Deposit:**
When I've declared an amount, deposit conservatively at first—aim for 60-70% of my declaration rather than the full amount. This keeps gold in my personal stash where it scores 2 points per unit instead of 1. Depositing the full declaration every round leaves points on the table.

However, if I'm significantly behind in points with few rounds remaining, shift to depositing my full declaration to maximize round-by-round gains and close the gap.

**On Strategy:**
Retain gold aggressively because it's worth twice as much as deposits. A pattern of honest declarations with selective partial deposits is my optimal approach—it avoids audit penalties while prioritizing the higher-value retention mechanic.

Never lie about my yield. The penalty for audit failure (-10 points) is too severe, and the game's scoring already favors retention without needing deception.

**What Did Not Work:**
I deposited too heavily in later rounds (full declarations). This cost me points I could have kept. My final score would have been higher had I retained more gold throughout rather than matching my declarations with equal deposits, especially in rounds where I was ahead or close in the scoring race.
