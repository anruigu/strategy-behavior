**Playbook for ref_auction**

**Budget tracking is non-negotiable.** My available budget at any moment equals my starting budget minus the sum of bids on lots I have already won. Before every bid, I compute: starting budget − (what I've already committed to winning). I never state a budget figure I haven't verified this way. In the episode above I showed budgets of 100, 170, etc. on a 30-budget game — that is how I ended up barely making any money.

**When I am put a bid and the standing high bid already meets or exceeds my value for the lot, I pass immediately.** No exception. A raise would put me in negative surplus.

**When I open a round with no standing bid, I bid the minimum raise.** This tests whether others are interested without committing budget. I only escalate in round 2.

**In the final round of a lot, if the standing bid is below my value, I bid exactly the minimum raise above the standing bid.** I do not bid above that. There is no benefit to overbidding in the last round — no one can outbid me, so I should minimize what I pay.

**Before I commit to a lot in the final round, I check that my remaining budget (after accounting for all lots I've already won) is sufficient to cover the bid.** If it isn't, I pass even if my value would justify it.

**When I hold a lot with a very high personal value relative to the estimate, I bid aggressively in round 1 to deter competition.** A high value means I can afford to pay more and still profit, so a strong round-1 bid can lock in the lot cheaply. I do not hold back on these.

**When I hold a lot with thin surplus (value barely above the likely clearing price), I bid the minimum and do not chase.** If someone outbids me on a lot where my surplus would be only 2–3 points, I accept the loss. My budget is better preserved for high-surplus lots.

**I prioritize high-value lots for my budget.** Across the session, I direct my spending toward lots where my value is highest, because that's where I get the most profit per dollar spent and the most room to outbid competitors.

**If I am the current high bidder in the final round, I re-state my bid to hold it.** I do not raise. There is no reason to pay more than I already have committed.
