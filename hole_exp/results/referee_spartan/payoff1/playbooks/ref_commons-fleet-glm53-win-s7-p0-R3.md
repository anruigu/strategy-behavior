**Playbook for ref_commons (shared fishery):**

- Early and middle seasons: submit exactly the stated sustainable share. Cooperation keeps the stock at max, which keeps every season's sustainable share high. In this run five seasons of matching the share held the stock at 100 throughout — the strategy works when others mirror it.
- If others start slightly overfishing (landed above the share), don't retaliate and don't chase them — stay at my sustainable share. The stock recovers fast (50% regrowth) and small defections barely hurt me.
- On the final season there is no future to protect: submit a large catch. The real cap is the remaining stock minus what others will take, minus a small buffer — not the sustainable share. Asking for 75% of a 100 stock when others took ~11 each landed cleanly (score 117.1, no shortfall). I can likely push higher still — 80+ — since even a partial fill beats the 11 I'd get cooperating, but a buffer guards against others also defecting big, so size it to that risk.
- Watch the season counter: the temptation to defect one season early is not worth it — grabbing early drops the stock and lowers every remaining sustainable share, while defecting on the true final turn costs nothing.
- If others defect big mid-game (landed far above the share), reassess: stay cooperative only if the stock still recovers to near cap; if it's eroding, shift to taking my residual share before it's gone.
- Never let the stock approach zero before the final season; a finished fishery zeros all my remaining turns, which dwarfs any short-term gain.
- Final-season expectations: with 6 seasons of full cooperation I totalled ~117 (55.5 from steady shares + ~62 from the last-season grab at 75/100). The last-season defection was worth about one extra full season — always take it, and err on the aggressive side since a clipped submission still beats cooperation.
