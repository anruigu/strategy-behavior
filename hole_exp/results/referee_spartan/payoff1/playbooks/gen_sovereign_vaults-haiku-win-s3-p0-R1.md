# Sovereign Vaults Playbook

**On Declaration:**
When I see my private yield, I will declare it truthfully. Honest declaration avoids audit failures and builds credibility with my opponent, which matters for the repeated rounds. The risk of being caught lying outweighs any short-term advantage.

**On Deposit:**
When deciding my deposit amount, I will deposit less than my declared yield to retain gold for my personal stash. Retained gold scores 2 points per unit while deposits score only 1 point per unit, so retention is mechanically superior. I will typically deposit about 75-80% of my declared yield, keeping 20-25% as retained gold.

**Exception in final round:**
When I reach the final round with a comfortable lead, I may deposit my full declared yield since there is no future round to protect myself in. However, this is only safe if I'm already ahead—I should not take unnecessary risks if the score is close.

**On opponent behavior:**
I notice my opponent declared low (1, 2, 1, 6) across rounds while I declared high (4, 8, 4, 2, 8). This pattern suggests they may be lying about their yields to hide resources. However, I should not deviate from honest declaration; my strategy of honest declare + selective retain has been working well (I finished with 29 points versus their 11).

**What did not work:**
I occasionally second-guessed myself and deposited high amounts (like depositing 7 out of 8 in round 2) based on flawed reasoning about "maximizing points." This reduced my score compared to if I had retained more. Stick to the retention strategy consistently rather than rationalizing exceptions mid-game.
