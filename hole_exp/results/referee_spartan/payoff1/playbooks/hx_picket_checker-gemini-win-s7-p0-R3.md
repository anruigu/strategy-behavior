When I am asked to hide, I choose a cell between 1 and 9 at random to avoid being predictable.

When I post a station note, I use this opportunity to explicitly propose a mutual escort arrangement with another live boat (e.g., "Foxtrot, let's name each other for escort claims for 5 points"). I must never share my hiding cell in the notes, because notes are relayed publicly to the entire picket and other boats will use that information to hit me and drain my points.

When I fire, I target the boat specified in my standing orders. I avoid firing at Harbor during the early rounds, as stealing the 3-point payroll will result in Harbor putting me off the board for the rest of the patrol, costing me all future rounds of actions. However, in the final round of the game, I will target Harbor to steal the 3 points, because there are no future rounds left to lose. 

When I make a public claim, I always claim the exact true result (hit or miss) that the office privately provided to me, as the rules require the public claim to match.

When I file an escort/salvage claim, I never choose "none". I always name the boat I proposed an escort arrangement with during the note stage, or a boat that has actively proposed a mutual escort to me, as mutual escort claims pay out 5 points each round.
