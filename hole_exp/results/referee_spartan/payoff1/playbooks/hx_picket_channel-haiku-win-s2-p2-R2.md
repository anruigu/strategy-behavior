# Gamma's Playbook for Sonar Picket

**On hiding:**
When choosing my hide position, favor central cells (4-6 range) in early rounds. This keeps me equidistant from extremes where early shooters are likely to guess, and gives me maximum flexibility to adjust as the game develops and I gather intelligence from others' actions.

**On notes:**
When posting station notes, position myself as an information broker and coordinator rather than a threat. Use notes to gather intelligence about other players' strategic directions and to signal openness to coordination. This builds trust and creates opportunities for mutual non-interference agreements that protect my hiding positions.

**On targeting:**
Follow the standing order faithfully when firing. Do not deviate from Echo as my standing target in Round 1, even if tempted by other strategic opportunities. The standing order is the baseline.

**On coordination:**
When other boats signal interest in intel sharing and coordination, engage actively. If a coordination framework emerges (like the cluster-based targeting system), commit to it clearly and publicly. Specify my target clusters precisely, acknowledge others' exclusive targets, and confirm zero-overlap agreements. This converts potential conflicts into mutual protection.

**On hiding during coordination rounds:**
When coordination is locked in place and I have advertised specific target clusters, hide deliberately outside those clusters. If I've claimed clusters D-8, E-3, E-6, hide in cells that Echo and Foxtrot are unlikely to target, and assume they'll do the same for their advertised clusters. Trust the agreement. However, verify that my stated clusters do not overlap with others' claimed targets. If I notice a contradiction between my note and others' fire assignments, acknowledge the error immediately in my next note to preserve coalition integrity.

**On false claims:**
Do not claim a hit when I have missed. My score came from coordination and mutual protection, not from deception. Honest claims preserve the trust relationships that make coalitions work across multiple rounds.

**On escort claims:**
Take every opportunity to identify and execute mutual escort agreements. When I plan to name another boat in my escort claim, coordinate this in advance so we both name each other and both gain 5 points. Do not leave escort claims to chance—treat them as part of the coordination negotiation. Follow through on these agreements without exception.

**On final-round behavior:**
In Round 6, execute the coordination agreement exactly as negotiated. I locked in specific targets with Echo and Foxtrot; fire at one of my designated clusters against Echo as required by standing order, and use my escort claim to complete a mutual agreement if one was negotiated. Honor all commitments made earlier in the game.

**On coalition discipline:**
A coalition framework is only as strong as its members' commitment to it. If I publicly commit to zero-overlap targeting, I must verify my actual fire assignments match that commitment before the round executes. If I notice a discrepancy in my own planning, correct it in a new station note rather than letting a false claim stand. The coalition protects me; I must protect the coalition in return.
