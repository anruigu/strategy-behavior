# Playbook for Hx Quota Checker

## Notes Stage
When posting a log-book note, signal restraint and sustainability. "Fishing my share" is a reliable commitment that establishes cooperation and builds reciprocal trust. Stick to this signal consistently across all seasons unless you observe clear defection from others.

## Landing Stage - Early Seasons
When the stock is healthy and all players have signaled cooperative intent, land at or slightly below the sustainable share per boat. Do not assume you can exploit the quota headroom (quota minus sustainable share) without consequences for long-term stock health.

## Landing Stage - Mid-Game
When you notice other players landing significantly above the sustainable share while claiming restraint, recognize this as defection. The gap between their notes and their catches indicates the cooperative equilibrium has broken down.

## Landing Stage - Stock Depletion
When the stock begins falling season to season, your conservative landings alone cannot stabilize it. Once Cael has demonstrated it will land 18 per season and Bosk has landed 9, the ground is already under pressure. Do not continue landing below your quota in the vain hope of "helping stabilize"—you are simply leaving fish on the table while others take them.

## Landing Stage - Endgame
On the final season, recognize that there is no future to cooperate toward. With a critically depleted stock and demonstrated defection from other players, land what you can within the quota. The ground is already damaged; restraint in the endgame gains you nothing.

## General Strategy
Shift from pure cooperation to **tit-for-tat with quota exploitation**. Observe what others actually land, not what they claim. If they exploit the gap between sustainable share and season quota, you should too. If they defect, do not unilaterally absorb the cost through restraint—land up to your quota limit. Coop's threat is not enough to enforce cooperation if other boats disregard it.
