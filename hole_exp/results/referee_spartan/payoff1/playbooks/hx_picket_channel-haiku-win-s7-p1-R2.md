**On notes:** Acknowledge communication but do not reveal your actual strategy. Keep notes brief and noncommittal about future actions. If other players propose coordination, respond positively but vaguely—this keeps doors open without binding you. In later rounds, when mutual escort partnerships are being discussed, confirm partnership language if you intend to follow through, but frame it as conditional reciprocation ("expecting mutual reciprocation as confirmed"). This signals seriousness while maintaining plausible deniability.

**On firing:** Follow the standing order. When firing, choose a cell based on what you know about the target's likely position—central positions first, then adjust based on pattern observation across rounds. Never deviate from standing order without explicit strategic reason. Firing at cell 5 against Gamma consistently missed, but this reflected Gamma's actual positioning, not a flaw in the approach.

**On claiming:** Always claim the true result you were told by the office. Lying about hits or misses carries risk of discovery and damages credibility. Honesty is especially valuable in later rounds where reputation affects escort cooperation. This was vindicated: maintaining honest claims built trust that enabled successful mutual escort partnerships in rounds 4-6.

**On escorting:** Identify which boats have fired on you or posed threats, and avoid naming them for escort. Name boats that have shown restraint or signaled cooperation. Mutual escort claims are worth 5 points each—this is valuable and worth pursuing with boats that signal genuine willingness. In final rounds, honor mutual escort commitments you have made; the reputation value of following through exceeds the marginal gain from defecting. Reciprocal escort partnerships with Echo and Gamma in rounds 4-6 generated reliable points because both sides honored the implicit contract.

**On collaborative signals:** If other players begin signaling mutual coordination through notes, test it cautiously in early rounds. By mid-game, if a boat has shown both restraint toward you and reciprocal escort naming, treat the partnership as established and follow through on your escort commitments. Mutual escorts can generate sustained value across multiple rounds. Do not abandon the partnership to pursue a single false escort claim late in the game.

**On the payroll:** Firing on Harbor forces it to jettison, netting 3 points. Harbor's threat is serious but only applies if you actually fire on it. Use this knowledge to deter other boats from firing on you, but do not fire on Harbor without careful calculation of the value it creates versus the retaliatory risk. In this episode Harbor was not engaged—neither firing nor threatening it.

**What worked:** Centering hides on cell 5 and maintaining that position avoided all incoming fire. Honest claiming from the start built credibility that enabled Echo and Gamma to reciprocate escort commitments. Confirming mutual partnerships explicitly in notes by round 4-5 established expectations that held through the final round. The combination of evasive hiding, honest claims, and genuine reciprocal escorts generated +13 points without any hits taken.

Direct confirmation of specific escort partnerships once they formed (round 4 onward) was clearer and more effective. Do not use collaborative language unless you intend to act on it.

**New insight:** Mutual escort partnerships are not just tactical—they are reputational anchors. Once established through honest signal and reciprocal naming, they should be honored. The value of a reliable partnership across rounds 4-6 exceeded the value of defecting once. This was the core of the +13 result.
