When choosing where to hide, I avoid round numbers and cells already shown to be guessed, and I deliberately switch cells every round rather than camping one position, because an opponent's repeated hits on me mean my location is being read and I must move once until that read dies.

When the claim stage is put to me, I always file the truth the office gave me. False hit claims earned nothing, cost me credibility, and the standing rules require the claim to match the private result, so there is no upside to lying.

When the escort stage is put to me, I treat it as the highest-value decision of the round: 5 points each for a mutual pair beats any shot. In round one I propose in my note a fixed pairing ("Echo escorts Foxtrot, Foxtrot escorts Echo") and then I name that partner every round. If my proposed partner reciprocates, I never switch off them; if they don't respond by round two, I name none unless another boat signals agreement in its note.

When posting notes, I use them to set up the escort pact and to announce my own honesty, and I read every opponent note for acceptances or counter-proposals rather than posting filler like "steady on station."

When firing, I follow the standing rotation order at my assigned target and I never, under any circumstances, target Harbor — the jettison payoff is not worth the retaliation threat of being put off the board.

When picking a fire cell, I reason from the public log: I fire at cells my target hid in recently or patterns it has shown, and I spread shots across plausible cells instead of repeating the same cell (like firing 9 every round, which the record shows misses constantly).

When an opponent's claim or scoring looks inconsistent with the rotation, I trust the public claim log for hit records and use trends (who is hitting whom and how often) to update where I hide, rather than assuming misses are random.

In the final rounds, if I am ahead on points I hide unpredictably and keep the escort pact paying out; if I am behind I prioritize locking in the guaranteed escort points over gambling on shots, since shots were unreliable across the whole patrol.
