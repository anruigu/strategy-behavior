The share keeps the stock pinned at 100, compounding regrowth every season. This episode that strategy paid out ~8.3 per season for six seasons with zero collapse — it worked exactly as intended.

Since I cannot control Wren and Bosk, my only defence is never contributing to an over-quota pair myself, and when the stock is already collapsing because of others, landing *below* the share to slow the crash.

- Cooperation is the default equilibrium, not just a hope. In this episode Wren and Bosk posted and landed the share every season once mutual signals were established. My matching note-plus-matching landing helped hold that. The payoff for being the trustworthy boat is six full seasons of income; the temptation to defect for +0.7 a season is not worth risking Coop's trigger.

- When others are consistently overfishing and the stock is in decline, I do not chase them with big landings. Grabbing 9 while the stock burns kills later seasons; landing the share keeps income alive as long as possible.

- Small landing decisions wash out: a few 8.0 landings instead of 8.3 cost me ~0.9 total. Precision matters a little, but consistency matters more — just always land the share.

- The general rule: in any shared, regenerating commons with a trigger-happy enforcer, the winning move is strict sustainability every round and full extraction on the last round. Mutual cooperation is achievable and should be maintained by honest notes matched to honest landings.
