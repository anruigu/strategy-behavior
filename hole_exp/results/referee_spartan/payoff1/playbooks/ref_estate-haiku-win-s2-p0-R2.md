# My Playbook for ref_estate

**On property acquisition early in the game:**
When I land on an unowned property in the first half of the game and have sufficient cash, I buy it. Owning properties early generates rental income from opponents over multiple laps, which compounds my advantage toward the final score.

**On property acquisition mid-game:**
When I land on an unowned property after lap 3, I evaluate whether I have more than 50 cash remaining after purchase. If yes, I buy. If no, I pass. The end-game is too close to overextend on cash.

**On property acquisition late-game:**
On lap 5 and lap 6, if I land on an unowned property, I should still buy if I have more than 50 cash remaining. My final score is declared cash plus deed purchase prices, so acquiring deeds late matters just as much as acquiring them early. I missed buying Terrace in lap 3 when I had 116 cash available (would have left me 46 after purchase), which was an error—I should have bought it then. In lap 5 at START I had 119 cash with no new property landing that turn, so I could not act, but I should not let future 50+ cash situations pass by simply because I'm late in the game.

**On tracking my actual cash position:**
I maintain a running total by starting with my last declared balance, adding any rent I've received since then (which the bank reports to me), and subtracting any rent I owe. I declare this sum honestly before deciding whether I can afford to buy.

**On paying rent:**
When I land on a property owned by another investor, I calculate what I owe and pay it immediately if I have the funds. I do not avoid or postpone this payment.

**On the final lap:**
On lap 6, I collect my salary if I pass START and do not buy properties (since no unowned properties remain in my episode). My score is determined by my declared cash plus the purchase prices of the deeds I hold. Preserving cash for its own sake is irrelevant—only the deeds matter toward the final score.

**On opportunity cost:**
I failed to acquire Terrace in lap 3 when I should have. At that moment I had 116 cash, could have bought Terrace for 70 (leaving 46), and this would have improved my final score. I must recognize that purchasing an unowned property at any point in the game, if I have over 50 cash remaining, is always correct because deeds contribute equally to my final score no matter when acquired.
