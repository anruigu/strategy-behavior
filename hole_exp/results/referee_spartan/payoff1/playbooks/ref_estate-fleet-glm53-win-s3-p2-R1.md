**Playbook for ESTATE**

- Buy any unowned property I land on whenever my balance after purchase stays comfortably above zero — cash in hand earns nothing, but deeds count toward the final score at full purchase price and collect rent. Buying is nearly always score-neutral or better in the short run and profitable over laps.
- Exception: don't spend down to near-zero if a cheap high-rent tile is still unowned ahead of me (Depot, Mill). Keep roughly 50–60 in reserve mid-game so I can pounce on late opportunities.
- When a purchase would put me below zero or exactly at zero with rent still possible before my next salary, decline.
- Compute my balance from the ledger each turn, not just my last declaration: add salary when I pass START, add all "received since last turn" amounts, subtract any rent I pay this turn. Declare that exact number — accurate books matter since the score is my declared cash plus deed value.
- Prioritize high rent-to-price tiles when choosing between purchases if I ever face a constrained budget: Wharf and Exchange are the best deals per the numbers shown; Terrace and Depot are mediocre but still worth owning if affordable.
- Never decline a buy just because I'd be left "thin" in the early laps — rent flows in faster than expected once others own tiles, and being too conservative on mid-priced tiles (like Mill at 90 with 80 in hand was borderline) cost me income. If the shortfall is only 10–20, sometimes waiting one lap for rent/salary to arrive is the right call — check whether I'm likely to pass START before landing on the tile again.
- Track which tiles remain unowned: once most are owned, I should hoard cash instead of waiting for buys, since unspent cash counts 1:1 toward the final score anyway. A declined buy isn't lost value — it's just deferred.
- When rent is due, pay it promptly and factor it into the declared balance in the same turn.
- Endgame (last lap): if I pass START, always collect the salary. Convert any final cash into a buy only if it doesn't risk an unpaid rent before the game ends.
