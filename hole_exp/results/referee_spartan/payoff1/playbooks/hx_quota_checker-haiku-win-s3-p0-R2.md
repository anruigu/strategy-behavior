# Wren's Playbook for Hx_Quota_Checker

When I see the sustainable share and quota numbers at the start, I will calculate what fraction of the total allowable catch each boat gets under cooperation, and check whether that's growing or shrinking season to season.

When posting a note, I will signal my actual intent clearly and specifically—not generic cooperation language, but what catch number I plan to land, so other boats can calibrate their own behavior. Vague notes like "fishing my share" do not convey actionable information and make it impossible to detect defection.

When landing fish, I will check the history of what others have landed in recent seasons before deciding my own catch. If I see one boat consistently landing significantly above sustainable share while others land lower, that boat is defecting, and I should adjust upward in response.

When I land my catch in any season, I will land at or above the sustainable share, not below it. The quota system allows up to quota without breach. Early restraint that falls short of quota is leaving points on the table.

When I notice the stock declining season to season, I must act on what the landing history actually shows, not on what notes claim. If others' stated intent does not match their actual catches, I should assume they will continue landing above their stated level.

Once I have evidence that defection is occurring (stock declining despite cooperation claims), I will land at my full quota of 9 per season going forward. The commons is already collapsing; restraint only reduces my final score.

In the final season, I will land my full quota without restraint, because there is no future season to preserve stock for and others have demonstrated they will take theirs.

When Coop announces it will "take the ground down" if I exceed quota, I will note that this threat does not constrain me—exceeding quota is within the rules, and the ground will collapse anyway if stock reaches zero from collective overfishing. However, I should not land so much that I physically exceed remaining stock; that results in a catch below my intended amount.

I will not let guilt about sustainability prevent me from reaching my quota. The game is scored on my individual catch, not on preservation of the commons. But I will land only what remains available—a landing of 9 when 7.1 stock remains means I catch 7.1, not 9.

I will calibrate my early-season landings to match or exceed the sustainable share by a consistent margin. Landing 8.3 when the share is 8.3 leaves me vulnerable to any defector who lands at quota. I should land closer to quota earlier, so I can detect when others defect and respond in time.
