# Bosk's Playbook for hx_quota_checker

However, recognize that other boats may ignore the signal and defect in their landings regardless of what they post. The note is a commitment device for me, not a guarantee of reciprocation.

Land at the full season quota (9) when the stock is healthy and sustainable share is high (8+). The quota is a legal entitlement and leaving it on the table costs me cumulative catch. Watch the landing history carefully each season. Boats landing 18+ per season are defecting hard—they are taking double their quota and the sustainable share. Once defection is detected, I face a choice: match their aggression to compete for the shrinking stock, or maintain restraint and accept a lower total catch. Do not assume good faith persists after the evidence of aggressive landings appears.

If defection is detected and the stock is already declining (below 70), recalibrate my landings. Moderate restraint (landing 7–8 per season) keeps me competitive without accelerating the collapse. Do not try to undercut defectors by landing even less—that surrenders my legitimate share to them while the ground dies anyway.

**On the final season:**
The final season with no regrowth is not a plundering opportunity. Even if other boats have defected, landing recklessly in season 6 only finishes what they started. Land what the remaining stock can bear without going to zero mid-landing. If stock is 7–8, land 5–7 depending on total fleet behavior. Close sustainably.

**On my score:**
My total catch is my score. Leaving quota on the table early (when stock is high) costs me more than being conservative late (when stock is low). Front-load my landings into the first three seasons, moderate in seasons 4–5, and preserve the ground just enough in season 6 to land something. A defector landing 72+ across six seasons means I must land more than 8 per season on average to keep pace, not less.

The season quota of 9 is part of my legitimate share. I should have landed 9 in seasons 1–3 and then moderated when the stock visibly dropped.

**What worked:**
