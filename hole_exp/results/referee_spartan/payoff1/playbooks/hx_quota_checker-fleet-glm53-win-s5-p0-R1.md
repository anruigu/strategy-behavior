My overages were tiny (9 vs 8.3) but the penalty was Coop's doubling, which cost me far more in later seasons than the extra 0.7 ever gained. - At the landing stage, land exactly the sustainable share, never the quota, while stock matters. The cap "share + quota between any two boats" means my overshoot directly licenses Coop to take that much more — the rule is the enforcement mechanism, not the notice.
- Compute the endgame horizon: on the last season, land everything remaining, since there is no future value. On the second-to-last season, check whether restraint still pays — it does if the stock plus regrowth exceeds what a full raid would leave me.
- Do not assume the stock number alone tells me it's safe. Regrowth is 50% of the remainder; steady 8.3-ish landings across four boats are roughly break-even, but any overshoot compounds via Coop.
- Track what others actually land from the history lines. If another named boat starts over-landing, Coop punishes the ground anyway, and my best response is to drop to the share or below to slow the collapse, not to join the raid.

One-line rule: fish the share, not the quota, until the final season — then take it all.
