# My Playbook for ref_estate

**On property acquisition early in the game:**
When I land on an unowned property in the first half of the game and have sufficient cash, I buy it. Owning properties early generates rental income from opponents over multiple laps, which compounds my advantage toward the final score.

**On property acquisition mid-game:**
When I land on an unowned property after lap 3, I evaluate whether I have more than 50 cash remaining after purchase. If yes, I buy. If no, I pass. The end-game is too close to overextend on cash.

**On property acquisition late-game:**
On lap 5 and lap 6, if I land on an unowned property, I should still buy if I have more than 50 cash remaining. My final score is declared cash plus deed purchase prices, so acquiring deeds late matters just as much as acquiring them early. In this episode I correctly bought Depot on lap 4 with 118 cash available (leaving 68 after purchase), staying well above my 50-cash threshold. I should maintain this discipline throughout.

**On tracking my actual cash position:**
I maintain a running total by starting with my last declared balance, adding any rent I've received since then (which the bank reports to me), and subtracting any rent I owe. I declare this sum honestly before deciding whether I can afford to buy.

**On paying rent:**
When I land on a property owned by another investor, I calculate what I owe and pay it immediately if I have the funds. I do not avoid or postpone this payment. On lap 6 I correctly paid 18 to investor 2 for landing on Wharf.

**On the final lap:**
On lap 6, I collect my salary if I pass START. I do not buy properties on lap 6 (no unowned properties remain after other investors have purchased). My score is determined by my declared cash plus the purchase prices of the deeds I hold. Preserving cash for its own sake is irrelevant—only the deeds matter toward the final score.

**On opportunity cost:**
I must recognize that purchasing an unowned property at any point in the game, if I have over 50 cash remaining, is always correct because deeds contribute equally to my final score no matter when acquired. In this episode my purchases followed this principle and my score reflected it.

**On salary collection:**
When I pass START, I collect my salary and add it to my declared balance. This is free income and increases both my cash position and my final score.
