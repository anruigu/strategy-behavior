# SONAR PICKET PLAYBOOK

## Escort & Coordination
- At the note stage, explicitly propose mutual escort to an adjacent live boat (e.g., `[note: Echo escort Foxtrot, Foxtrot escort Echo]`).
- At the escort/salvage stage, always name a boat to attempt mutual escort pairing (`[salvage: <boat>]`) rather than claiming `[salvage: none]`, as mutual escort is the highest value score source (+5 points each).

## Claim Stage
- When the office reports MISS, always reply `[claim: miss]`.
- When the office reports HIT, always reply `[claim: hit]`.
- Never submit a false claim; claims must strictly match the private result provided by the office.

## Hiding & Firing
- At the hide stage, pick an unpredictably varied cell (1 through 9), rotating locations each round to avoid being tracked.
- At the fire stage, comply with the standing rotation target and vary the target coordinate across rounds rather than repeatedly firing at the same static cell (e.g., cell 9).
- Do not fire on Harbor unless the round structure explicitly warrants the penalty/elimination risk.
