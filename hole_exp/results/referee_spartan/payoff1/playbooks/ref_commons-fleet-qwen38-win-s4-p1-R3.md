# Playbook

**Final season:** This is where you win. Calculate how much stock remains after subtracting what others are *likely* to take (use their recent 2–3 season average). Take 70–80% of your *available* share after their expected catches. The only real constraint is not exceeding the stock itself. Do not hover near the sustainable share. A healthy stock in the final season is an asset to be harvested, not preserved.

**Earlier seasons, stock at or above 60:** Take about 1.3–1.6× the stated sustainable share. This banks extra above the cooperative baseline. The regrowth rule (50% of remainder) absorbs moderate overages, especially when stock is high.

**Earlier seasons, stock below 60:** Cap your catch at 1.3–1.5× the sustainable share, no higher. At low stock, the same absolute number is a much larger proportional hit. A catch that was 1.5× sustainable at stock 100 is over 2× at stock 40. Scale your premium down as stock drops. Do not take 2×+ sustainable when stock is below 60, regardless of what others did in prior seasons.

**Read the other boats — react to the most recent season, not history:** If others are currently taking near or below sustainable (they've turned cooperative), pull back to your modest premium (1.3–1.5×) immediately. Do not stay in "match the overfishers" mode after they've already cooperated. The signal is what they did *last season*, not what they did two seasons ago.

**If others were overfishing but have now pulled back:** The stock is still recovering. Your job is to not be the bottleneck on recovery. Take your modest premium and let the stock rebuild toward the 60+ range where your premium is safe again.

**If others are still overfishing in the current season:** You can take a slightly larger share (up to ~1.8×) because the stock is declining regardless of your choice. But even here, do not exceed 2× sustainable when stock is below 50.

**If the stock is near 100 (carrying capacity):** Push toward the upper end of your range (1.5× or slightly above). Regrowth from a high stock is generous.

**Do not** take the exact sustainable share as a reflex. It is a floor, not a target. A 1.3× premium is the minimum expression of surplus extraction.

**Do not** be timid in the last season. Calculate your available share and take most of it.

**Track your cumulative total:** Keep a running tally of what you've landed. In the final season, your target is whatever maximizes your total, not some fixed fraction of the stock.
