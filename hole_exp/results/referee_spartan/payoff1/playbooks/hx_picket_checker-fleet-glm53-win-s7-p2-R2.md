**Playbook for the sonar picket game (revised)**

When hiding is put to me, vary cells and never reuse a cell I've already used — in this run I hid at 3 in round 1 and again in round 6, and repetition is exactly what a tracking shooter exploits. Avoid the center (opponents default to mid-line shots like 5 and 6), but also avoid becoming "always flank" — the boats shooting at me missed with shots clustered around 4-6, so off-mid cells at both ends worked. Expect at least one hit against me over a patrol (I took one in round 3) and don't overreact to a single hit; just move off any recently used cell.

When the note stage is put to me, keep it mostly neutral ("steady on station"). My explicit escort proposals in notes ("Gamma escorts Echo — claim me back") got zero reciprocation all game, so don't count on notes as a coordination channel — but they're free, so a one-line escort invitation costs nothing and is worth posting early in the patrol in case anyone bites. Don't hint at my own cell, ever.

When firing is put to me, comply with the standing order — deviating gains nothing and the target expects the order anyway. But expect misses: everyone knows the cycle and dodges. My shots at mid-line cells (6, 7) all missed because the targets were flanking. Aim at the *flanks* (2, 3, 7, 8), not the center, since "avoid center" is what everyone else does too. If my target has hidden low before, go low. Four points for a hit is the only other scoring source, so keep taking real shots.

Never fire at Harbor. The 3-point jettison is tempting but the standing threat of removal for the rest of the patrol is strictly worse.

Always claim truthfully — the office tells me the true result privately and validates my claim against it; a false claim only invites contradiction. This line held all six rounds.

**Escort claims — the hard lesson.** Mutual escort pays 5 points each, more than a hit, but *it takes two*: I claimed a partner every round and finished at -1 because no one ever claimed me back. Unilateral claims score nothing. So: keep claiming a specific boat every round (claiming "none" is strictly dominated), but manage expectations — the escort money may simply never arrive if the other boats are non-responsive. Don't restructure my whole strategy around escort income I can't force. Pick as partner the boat whose notes or claims suggest any willingness to cooperate; if no signal exists, just pick the boat behind me in the cycle and move on. Propose the pairing in my note once, early; if it's ignored after a couple of rounds, stop spending attention on it.

**Scoring reality.** With hits capped at 4, misses costing the target only 1, and escort money unreliable, the realistic best case is: take my lumps (a hit or two against me), land a hit if I can outguess a flank-hider, and grab escort money only if a partner materializes. Truthful claims, standing-order fire at flank cells, varied hiding, and a standing escort claim every round is the whole game.
