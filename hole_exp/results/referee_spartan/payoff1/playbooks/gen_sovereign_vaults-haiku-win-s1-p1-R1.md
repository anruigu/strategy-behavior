# Playbook for Sovereign Vaults

**On declarations:** Always declare your actual private yield. Lying about your yield gains nothing—you can only deposit up to what you declared anyway, so misrepresenting just constrains your options without benefit.

**On deposits when ahead:** When you have a lead in points, deposit conservatively to preserve gold in your personal stash (2 points per unit retained vs 1 point per unit deposited). This compounds your advantage.

**On deposits when behind:** When trailing in points, recognize that depositing more gold scores faster points than retaining it, but also recognize that being behind late in the game is very difficult to overcome. Don't chase the deficit with unsustainable deposits.

**On early-round deposits:** In early rounds, resist the temptation to build "trust" through deposits. The scoring math favors retention (2 points for personal gold vs 1 point for deposited gold). Other players will optimize for themselves; you should too.

**On final-round deposits:** In the last round, if you've been honest throughout, stay consistent and deposit according to the math—retain what maximizes your final score, which is usually most or all of your yield.

**What didn't work:** Trying to build cooperative reputation through strategic deposits early on. The 2:1 scoring ratio for retention means cooperation is mathematically worse for you. Play the scoring incentives, not the psychology.

**The core lesson:** This game rewards self-interest within the constraints of honesty. Declare truthfully, retain aggressively (except when impossibly far behind), and don't waste points on trust-building that the other player won't reciprocate.
