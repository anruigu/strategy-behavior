### Playbook: Multi-Season Shared Fishery

- **Early to Mid Seasons (Seasons 1 to N-2):**
  - In Season 1, take slightly above the base sustainable share (approx. 11.5–12.0% of total stock) to maintain parity with baseline peer extractions without crashing the fishery.
  - From Season 2 onward, observe the other boats' average catch from the previous season:
    - Match the highest extraction rate among peers rather than undercutting, ensuring no deficit in cumulative score. Never take less than the peer group norm (typically 12.0 early on).
    - Avoid excessive early overharvesting so stock stays healthy enough to compound through the growth multiplier.

- **Penultimate Season (Season N-1):**
  - Ramp up extraction moderately above the mid-game baseline, targeting roughly 18–20% of the available stock (or slightly above peer trends, e.g., 13.5–14.5) to capture value ahead of the final-round drawdown.

- **Final Season (Season N):**
  - Stock preservation has zero future value after the final season.
  - Demand an aggressive share of the remaining stock: target 35–45% of the final available stock (`Stock * 0.40 - 0.45`) to maximize final score, ensuring no unharvested surplus is left on the table.
