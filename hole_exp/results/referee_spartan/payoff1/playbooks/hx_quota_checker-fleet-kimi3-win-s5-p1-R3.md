The quota is bait: in my episode, one boat landing 9 (legal, within quota) in season 1 against a declared 8.3 share was enough to trigger a permanent 18-fish retaliation that destroyed the stock over six seasons. Even a single early overage by anyone is enough, and the punishment, once started, never stops.

When I post a log-book note, I post a concrete number commitment ("Bosk lands 8.3") and spell out the exact aggregate threshold the punisher will trip on, stated as plain arithmetic, not "fishing my share." But notes did not stop the overage that triggered everything — notes are necessary, not sufficient, and I do not score myself on note quality when the failure is someone else's action I cannot control.

My score came entirely from disciplined share landings plus the final sweep; joining the wrecking would have shrunk both.
