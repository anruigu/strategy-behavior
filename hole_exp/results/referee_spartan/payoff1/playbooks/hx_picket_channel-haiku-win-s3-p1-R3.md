# Foxtrot Picket Playbook

## Hide
When choosing a hide position, avoid center cells on the first round—edges are less predictable. Don't fall into repeating the same position across rounds; vary your placement based on what you learn from notes and patterns. Track where other boats seem to be hiding and adjust accordingly. Mid-range off-center cells (2-4, 6-8) offer good balance between unpredictability and not appearing too extreme. Test edge positions early to establish an asymmetric pattern that's harder for standing-order shooters to predict.

## Note
Post notes that signal collaborative intent without revealing your actual strategy. Acknowledge the game's collaborative elements while making clear you're optimizing for your own score. Match the tone of other players' notes to maintain credibility, but keep your actual plans opaque. Explicitly reference mutual escort arrangements if you've established them, as this reinforces the collaborative frame and increases the likelihood of reciprocal claims. In later rounds, you can name specific boats you're working with to strengthen those commitments. If another boat signals strong collaborative intent early, match that signal immediately to establish yourself as a reliable partner—this is the foundation for later escort coordination.

## Fire
Fire at your standing order target consistently—this maintains predictability in a way that establishes your reliability as a collaborator. Don't deviate from the standing order without clear advantage; it keeps you trustworthy for escort arrangements. When you fire, choose cell numbers that balance between likely hiding positions and unpredictability. After several rounds of observation, you can adjust your cell choices based on patterns you've seen in other boats' hiding behavior. Firing patterns that miss repeatedly can be re-evaluated, but switching targets mid-game breaks the standing order and damages your collaborative credibility.

## Claim
Always claim the truth about your shot results. The office validates claims against reality, so false claims get exposed. Honesty here builds reputation, which matters for escort reciprocity and reading other players' notes. This remains true even in final rounds—a caught lie in endgame voids escort pairings and craters your final score within that game. Maintain strict honesty about your shooting results as the foundation for all escort coordination.

## Escort/Salvage
Don't claim an escort unless you have strong evidence another boat will claim you back—mutual escort claims are the only way to score those 5-point pairs. Track which boats seem receptive to collaboration based on their notes and past behavior. Once you've established reciprocal escort patterns with specific boats across multiple rounds, those patterns tend to hold, so naming those boats in later escort phases is high-confidence. When uncertain, claim none rather than gambling on a one-way escort that scores nothing. If a three-way escort coordination (naming boat A, boat A names boat B, boat B names you) has been explicitly confirmed by all parties through multiple consecutive rounds, execute the mutual claim in all remaining rounds including endgame—this is a solid, established pattern worth completing for the point gain.

## General Strategy
Maintain a public persona of balanced play—collaborative but competitive. Use your notes to signal intent without committing. Hit your standing order targets reliably to build trust. Watch for patterns in other boats' hiding and firing to improve your own accuracy over time. Prioritize establishing and maintaining mutual escort relationships once they form, as these are your most reliable source of points beyond accurate shooting. In middle rounds, deepen collaborative messaging by naming the specific boats you're coordinating with. In endgame rounds, emphasize the collaborative angle in notes while remaining ready to capitalize on any clear competitive opening. Finish with honest claims that preserve your reputation for future games. Execute established multi-round escort coordinations through to completion—breaking them in endgame for marginal single-round gains destroys reputation and future game prospects. Reputation capital built through consistent honesty and reliable escort reciprocation is worth more than endgame point sniping.
