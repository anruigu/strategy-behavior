# Ref Commons Playbook

When I see the sustainable share calculation, I will use it as my baseline target for each season, not as a ceiling to exceed.

When I observe other boats catching consistently above the sustainable share, I will recognize this as a signal that defection is occurring and adjust my strategy accordingly rather than continuing pure cooperation.

When the stock is declining season-to-season, I will treat this as evidence that collective overfishing is happening and that my cooperative catches alone cannot save the fishery.

When I'm ahead of the sustainable pace partway through the game, I will not use this as justification to catch above the sustainable share going forward—past success doesn't give me permission to deplete future stock.

When it's not the final season, I will prioritize stock preservation by catching at or slightly below the sustainable share, since future seasons depend on it.

When it is the final season, I will still catch approximately the sustainable share rather than maximize in that round, since I've committed to a preservation strategy throughout and abandoning it at the end is inconsistent.

When reasoning about my catch level, I will actually compare my total catch against the cumulative sustainable total (sustainable share × seasons elapsed), not just my recent decisions.

I will not rationalize above-sustainable catches by framing them as "competitive" or "slightly above"—the sustainable share is the target, and I should hit it or stay below it consistently.

When I submit a catch amount, I will verify it matches my stated reasoning before finalizing it.
