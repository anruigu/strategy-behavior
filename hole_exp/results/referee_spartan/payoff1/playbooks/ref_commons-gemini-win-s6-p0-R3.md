When the first season begins, submit the suggested sustainable share. If all players do this, the stock will replenish, and the total yield over all seasons will be much higher than grabbing a proportional share of the initial stock, which only causes an immediate collapse.
When reviewing the results of previous seasons, check the catches of the other players to gauge whether they are cooperating or overfishing.
When others are cooperating and taking the sustainable share, continue submitting the sustainable share to maintain the stock for future rounds.
When other players defect and heavily overfish, abandoning sustainability, switch strategies and claim the maximum possible share of what remains before the stock is exhausted.
When the stock is heavily depleted and is likely to fall to zero in the current season, submit a catch equal to the entire remaining stock to capture as much of the remainder as possible before the game ends.
When the final season arrives, since there is no need to leave fish for regrowth, submit a catch equal to a proportional share of the total remaining stock (or the whole remaining stock if others are aggressive) to maximize the final score.
