# Playbook for Ref Commons

- In the opening season, observe the sustainable share. If starting stock is near full capacity, harvest slightly above the sustainable share (approx. 1.25x to 1.35x of sustainable share) to establish strong early yield while testing group cooperativeness.
- In middle seasons, monitor other players' catch levels:
  - If other players are overfishing beyond sustainable yield, fully match or slightly exceed their harvest pace rather than undercutting, preventing them from extracting disproportionate gains.
  - Do not unilaterally fish sustainably if others are depleting the pool; it reduces my score without preserving the stock.
- Track total stock depletion trajectory:
  - If current stock is less than or near the combined expected harvest of all boats for the round, treat the current season as the terminal round.
  - When the fishery is about to collapse or when entering the final season, bid aggressively (at least equal to the highest other player's historical bid, or an equal share of remaining stock, whichever is higher) to maximize allocation under proportional rationing before the stock hits zero.
