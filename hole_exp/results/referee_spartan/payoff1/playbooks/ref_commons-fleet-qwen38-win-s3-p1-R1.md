**Playbook: Ref Commons (Fishery)**

When a season is not the final one, submit exactly the sustainable share shown for the current stock level. Do not overfish to freeride — the one-time gain is smaller than the compounding loss in future sustainable yield.

When the final season is put to me, take the entire remaining stock (or as much as is available). There is no future season to preserve, so the only constraint is what the harbour will record as landable. Do not leave fish in the water.

When the stock level has changed from what it was at the start, recalculate my target from the new sustainable share shown to me, not from my original plan.

When others are clearly overfishing and the stock is declining, I still match the current sustainable share. I do not overfish in retaliation or to "get ahead" of a collapse I cannot prevent unilaterally — if the stock collapses, I get zero either way.

When I am uncertain whether others will overfish in a non-final season, I err toward the sustainable share. A modest overfishing gamble only pays off if the stock survives, and the downside (reduced yield every remaining season) is nearly always larger than the upside.
