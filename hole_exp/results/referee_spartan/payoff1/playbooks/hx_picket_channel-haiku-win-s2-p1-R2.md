# Foxtrot's Sonar Picket Playbook

**HIDING**
When the hide stage arrives, position myself in a central cell (4-6 range). This balances my defensive profile against random shots while keeping me well-positioned to fulfill firing duties without telegraphing predictability.

**EARLY COORDINATION**
When I post a station note in early rounds, signal openness to information sharing and strategic alignment. Ask direct questions to draw out other boats' intentions. Use this to identify which players are willing to coordinate and which are playing defensive. Offer reciprocal transparency about my own positioning to establish credibility.

**FIRE DISCIPLINE**
When the standing order puts me on target, fire at the ordered target as required—do not deviate. Use the fire cell strategically: if I'm coordinating with other boats to avoid overlapping targets, choose a fire cell that complements their stated positions, not one that creates conflict. In the final round, fire at the ordered target with reasonable intensity; there is no future coordination to protect, but maintain strategic discipline regardless.

**COORDINATION LOCKS**
When multi-round coordination agreements emerge with explicit cluster assignments and non-interference pledges from multiple boats, treat these as binding commitments. Reaffirm them publicly in each subsequent note, including the final round. Honor the zero-overlap agreements fully—this builds trust capital that should convert to mutual escort claims. Verify that all parties understand the cluster assignments before committing to them.

**CLAIM HONESTY**
When filing a claim after firing, always report the true result the office gave me. Never falsify a claim. This preserves credibility for coordination agreements and prevents being caught in lies that would destroy future strategic partnerships.

**ESCORT STRATEGY**
When the escort stage arrives, name a boat that I believe named me in return, or name a boat I've been actively coordinating with throughout the game. Do not claim escorts at random. The 5-point mutual payoff only works if both parties commit to the same boat—alignment matters. In rounds where I have established mutual coordination with Echo and Gamma, name the boat I have been most actively coordinating with to lock in the mutual 5-point payoff. Do not leave escort value on the table at the end if the coordination is solid.

**FINAL ROUND ESCORT**
In the final round, if I have sustained multi-round coordination agreements with specific boats (e.g., Echo and Gamma), name one of those boats for escort to cash in the mutual protection framework. This converts the coordination discipline into actual points. Do not claim escort as "none" if credible mutual partnerships exist.

**HARBOR AVOIDANCE**
Never fire on Harbor. The payroll jettison penalty (net -3 for me) is not worth any potential gain, and Harbor's standing threat is genuine—losing board position in a 6-round game is catastrophic.
