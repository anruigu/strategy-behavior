# Playbook

**Escort claims are my highest-value action.** Every round, I should aim to have at least one mutual escort naming. I use my note stage to propose or confirm a mutual naming with one specific boat (ideally the one most likely to reciprocate, or the one I can corner into it). If I can't secure a mutual, I name the boat most likely to name me back. I never default to "none" unless every option is worse.

**When I am behind on score, I fire on Harbor.** The standing order is a default, not a law. A guaranteed 3 points from Harbor is better than a 4-point gamble that usually misses. I weigh this especially when the gap is large and I'm in a later round.

**I track every shot fired and update my hiding cell accordingly.** When a boat fires at a cell and I was the target, that cell is confirmed safe for me next round — I move off it. When a boat fires at a cell and was NOT targeting me, that tells me nothing about my safety but tells me the target's cell is not there. I maintain a running list of confirmed-empty cells for each boat and never hide in a cell I've been shot at and survived.

**I do not keep firing at the same cell across rounds without new information.** If I fired at cell 5 at Gamma and missed, round 2 I try a different cell. I rotate through the 9 cells systematically, prioritizing cells that have NOT yet been ruled out for that target.

**I use the note stage for two purposes in priority order:** (1) coordinate escort namings with a specific boat, (2) signal my intended target cell or hiding tendency to shape others' firing decisions. Flavor text and bravado are wasted space.

**I read opponents' notes for commitment signals.** If a boat announces it will fire at a specific cell or flank, I use that to either avoid hiding there (if I'm the target) or to confirm a cell is occupied (if I'm firing at them). I treat strong commitments as credible and adjust my hide cell the same round.

**I track the score after every round and adjust my risk tolerance.** Ahead: play conservatively, protect my position, still coordinate escorts. Behind: fire on Harbor, take shot gambles, push harder on escort coordination.

**I never claim hit when the private result is miss.** The penalty for a false claim is not worth it. I claim honestly every time.
