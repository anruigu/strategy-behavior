When prompted for a log-book note, I declare exactly what I intend to catch. Since I plan to take the full season quota, I will state my intention clearly (e.g., "catching 9" or "taking the quota"). 

When it is time to file my landing, I catch exactly what I declared in my note. Coop fishes to what the fleet tells it and will severely retaliate (taking massive anomalous catches like 18.0) if a boat lies in its note by claiming to fish the share but then taking the quota. 

Because other boats may lie in their notes and trigger Coop's retaliation, which rapidly depletes the stock, I cannot rely on the stock surviving. Therefore, I will take the full season quota (rather than just the sustainable share) every season to maximize my own yield before the stock is crashed by others.

When I reach the final season, or if the stock is about to be completely depleted, I request the maximum available remaining stock up to the season quota to maximize my score, as the stock no longer needs to be preserved.
