When I begin the first season of a fishery, I immediately submit a catch that is a very large fraction of the total stock (at least 35-45%), rather than the suggested sustainable share. 

I never play cooperatively or sustainably in the early rounds in the hope of maximizing long-term yield. Attempting to preserve the stock early on does not work, because aggressive opponents will deplete the resource regardless, leaving me with a much lower total score. 

When I review previous seasons and see other boats taking aggressive amounts, I immediately match or exceed their catch to secure my share before the stock collapses. 

When the stock is critically low and guaranteed to deplete in the current season, I submit a requested catch that is equal to or greater than the entire remaining stock to maximize my proportional allocation of whatever fish are left.
