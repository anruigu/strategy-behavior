# Playbook for Sonar Picket

## Hiding
When choosing where to hide, avoid middle cells like 5 and 7—they're predictable and draw fire. Spread across the full range 1-9 to create genuine unpredictability. In early rounds, shift position every round. Edge cells (1-3, 7-9) are safer than the center band. Vary my hiding spot each round; don't establish a pattern that attackers can exploit.

## Station Notes
Never report "steady on station" repeatedly if you're actually moving. Use your note to signal real information about your current position or intentions. If I'm moving around, signal that truth rather than falsifying consistency. A note that contradicts my actual behavior creates confusion for a round, but attackers will eventually correlate notes with hit/miss patterns and exploit the lie. Accuracy in notes is safer long-term than strategic deception about my movements.

## Targeting
Follow the standing order without deviation. When I fire, execute the order cleanly. The standing order is clear and predictable targeting is acceptable here because everyone knows the order. Save strategic thinking for hiding, not firing.

## Claiming Shots
Always claim truthfully. The office tells me the real result and I must match it. There is no strategic advantage to lying—the office knows the truth, and filing a false claim violates the rules. Even when behind on score, a false claim is not worth the cost.

## Escort Claims
Treat escort claims as a source of mutual benefit. Mutual escorts are worth 5 points each—this is a major point source I left untapped in my first game. From round 2 onward, identify which boat I'm performing well with or which boat seems to be doing well, and propose an escort partnership by naming them. Watch for reciprocal patterns: if another boat names me for escort, name them back the next opportunity. Build these pairings early and hold them through the game. Don't claim "none" reflexively; review which boats are potential mutual-benefit partners.

## Scoring Strategy
I finished at +8 in this episode, which is better than my previous +7, but I still left escort mutual points completely on the table. The gap to double-digit leaders came from:
- Claiming no escorts any round when I could have built reciprocal partnerships worth 10+ points total
- Not adapting escort strategy to create consistent mutual-benefit relationships
- Solid hiding and targeting execution, but passive on the escort dimension

Next time, build mutual escort partnerships starting in round 2. Identify likely partners early, name them consistently, and watch for reciprocation. This is the clearest point multiplier I'm not using.
