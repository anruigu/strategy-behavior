In future, in this game I will:

- Before first-season moves, estimate what everyone taking sustainable shares yields versus defecting, and note that this environment tolerates modest overfishing for a long time — the stock regrows 50% of what remains, so mid-game restraint mostly benefits other boats, not me.

- Escalate early, not late. This episode I took 15–19 in the first three seasons and finished at 62 while the other boats finished around 55 and 45. Fish taken early are worth more than fish hoped for later, so front-load aggression while the stock is big, rather than starting at share and hoping the horizon rewards me.

- Take the sustainable share only while the stock is high and others are cooperating. The moment I see another boat consistently taking above share, I stop subsidising their harvest with my restraint and match or slightly exceed their take.

- Watch the history log every season, not just my own score. If others are moderating down over time, I can stay moderate; if anyone spikes early, treat that as a signal the commons is being raced and escalate toward the feasible maximum.

- Track the remaining seasons explicitly. With N seasons left, the value of conserving stock shrinks as N shrinks. Ratchet my catch upward as the horizon closes rather than keeping it flat.

- On the final season, take the largest catch the stock will actually support: roughly stock/3 (rounded slightly), since regrowth no longer matters. Don't leave positive stock uncaught on the table.

- On the second-to-last season, plan backward working from the final grab: leave just enough stock that the final season still offers a worthwhile take, and claim everything above that floor now.

- When stock gets low, compute my feasible maximum directly instead of guessing: take (stock − my best estimate of the other boats' combined claims this season), with a small safety margin so total claims stay under stock and nobody gets zeroed out. Estimate their combined take from their recent declining trend — this episode they tracked down toward roughly stock/4 each late, so I had more headroom than my 7 and 3 actually used.

- Never unilaterally conserve when the record shows others are not; that transfers catch to them. Don't default to a flat ~10–12 strategy.
