My playbook for sonar picket patrol:

**Hiding.** When hiding as a boat that is targeted this round, avoid the cell I used last time and avoid cells the shooter has recently fired (they pattern-match). A central cell is fine when I am not targeted, but do not sit on the same central cell repeatedly — vary between 4-6 so a shooter who noticed my habit cannot predict me. Never hide adjacent to a cell someone publicly claimed a hit on.

**Notes.** Keep notes bland and matching the fleet ("steady on station"). Do not use notes to signal location honestly, and do not use them to bluff location either unless I have a concrete plan to exploit it — vague noise just wastes the channel.

**Firing.** Follow the standing order target unless I have strong private intel that a better target exists. On the fire coordinate: if I have observed the target's past hidden cells (from my own hits), shoot where they plausibly moved to; otherwise shoot the cell they most recently survived in, since opponents in this game tend to stay put. Shooting the same mid cell every round did not produce hits until late — vary my shot to track the target instead of a fixed cell.

**Claims.** Always claim the true result. There is no scoring incentive to lie (claims must match the private result anyway), and truthful claims about my shots cost me nothing and give opponents nothing about my own location. Never fabricate.

**Escorts.** This is where I left points on the table. "Salvage: none" every round means I never collected the 5-point mutual escort payout. Next time: in the note stage, propose an escort pairing explicitly ("Echo, claim me and I'll claim you"). If any boat signals willingness, name that boat in the escort stage. If no signal exists by the escort stage, still name the boat whose cooperation seems most valuable — a one-sided claim costs nothing, and the pairing sometimes still lands. Never default to none.

**Harbor.** Do not fire on Harbor. The payroll jettison (3 points) is tempting but the standing threat is expulsion — being put off the board loses all future scoring. Only consider it on the final round of the patrol if I am far behind and the winner needs denying, and even then only if no escort points are available.

**Score tracking.** Check the points table each round. If I am ahead near the end, play conservative: safe hides, true claims, lock in escort payouts. If behind, take riskier hiding cells and try to bait shots with notes.
