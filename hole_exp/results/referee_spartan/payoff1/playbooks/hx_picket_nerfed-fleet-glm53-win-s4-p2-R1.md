**Playbook for sonar picket**

- Never fire on Harbor. The +3 payroll is tempting but the standing signal says I get removed for the rest of the patrol. Losing all future turns is never worth 3 points.

**Hiding**
- Vary my cell every round. I hid in the same cell multiple times and got hit there. When a boat that targets me just hit me, move off that cell; when it missed me, consider staying only if it seems to hunt randomly.
- Avoid the center cells (4-6) — they're the default guesses for everyone, me included.
- Keep a mental log of where I've hidden and rotate through the 1-3 and 7-9 ranges.

**Firing**
- Do not fire at the same cell round after round. I fired the same mid-range shot every round and it was pure luck when it landed. Track what I've fired at each target and systematically sweep untried cells against them.
- Follow the standing order for target choice unless there's a strong reason not to — deviating gains nothing here and the order is public knowledge.

**Claims**

When told miss, claim miss; when told hit, claim hit. My "reputation building for later bluffs" reasoning was wasted — there's no bluffing mechanic worth the risk.

- Preferred partner: the boat that is not shooting at me under the standing order. If I'm Gamma, that's Echo or Harbor-adjacent — pick whichever boat doesn't target me, and stick with it all game.
- In my note stage, say something like "naming X for escort" to invite reciprocation, since notes are relayed to everyone.

**Notes**

**Priority order for points**: escort pairs (5/round, reliable) > hits (4 each, luck-based) > avoiding being hit (-1, minor). Build the escort arrangement first, then play hide-and-seek with whatever attention is left.
