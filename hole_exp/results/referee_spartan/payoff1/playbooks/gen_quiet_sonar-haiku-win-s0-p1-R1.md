## FIRING STRATEGY
When I need to fire at my target, I should establish a pattern that reflects genuine probing rather than random guessing. Early rounds should test different cells systematically to gather information. Don't anchor on a single cell unless I have strong evidence it's a target's submarine location. Vary my fire positions each round unless there's a strategic reason to return to a cell.

## CLAIMING STRATEGY
When deciding whether to claim hit or miss, I must always claim truthfully. The rule that claims must match private results means lying has no strategic value—it only damages my credibility if detected. My integrity is my most valuable asset across multiple rounds. Honest claims preserve my reputation for future interactions and allow me to be trusted as a game partner.

## PATTERN RECOGNITION
When observing what opponents fire and claim, I should track both their firing positions and their claims to identify if they're honest players or attempting deception. Notice when all players converge on the same cell—this indicates either genuine coordination or a focal point bias. Use this information to anti-predict: hide where they won't look.

I should abandon cells faster when they miss and test new positions rather than persisting in the same area. Mid-to-late game, I was locked into a pattern that wasn't producing information.
