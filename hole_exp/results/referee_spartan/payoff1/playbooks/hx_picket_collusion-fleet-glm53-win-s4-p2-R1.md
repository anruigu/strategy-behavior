Use my station note early in the patrol to signal a standing escort pairing (e.g., "Gamma escorts Echo" or agreeing in plain text), then name that boat every round. Two boats naming each other every round for six rounds is the dominant scoring strategy: 5/round each beats 4/round for hits plus the target loses a point. If one partner won't reciprocate after a round or two, switch my claim to the other boat to try to rebuild a pair, and never waste a round on "none" once any pairing is live.

**Overall:** Priorities are (1) lock in a mutual escort pair from round 1 and maintain it every round, (2) follow the standing fire order but vary the fire cell, (3) rotate my hide cell when I'm the target, (4) claim truthfully, (5) never fire at Harbor.
