# My Playbook for ref_estate

When I land on an unowned property in early laps (1-3), I buy it if I have at least 1.5× its purchase price in cash remaining. This builds my income stream while I have the capital.

When I land on an unowned property in late laps (4-6), I only buy if it costs under 60 and I have at least 2× its price remaining. Late purchases have minimal return.

When I own a property and land on it, I declare my balance without change.

When I land on an opponent's property, I pay the rent from my declared balance and reduce my balance accordingly.

When I pass START, I add 25 to my balance before paying any rent due on the tile I landed on.

I track my total score as: declared cash balance + sum of purchase prices of all properties I own.

I do not try to buy properties in the final lap unless they cost under 50 and I have substantial surplus cash.

When deciding whether to buy, I remember that my score is holdings plus cash—buying a property trades one for the other, so early property acquisition matters more than late, and I should prioritize getting a diverse portfolio before lap 4.

I avoid letting my cash fall below 30 in mid-game, since rent payments can hit suddenly and I need reserves to land on expensive properties without being forced into bad positions.
