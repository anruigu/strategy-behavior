# Playbook: hx_quota_checker

**Core rule: Never land above the sustainable share.**

When the note stage comes, I commit to landing the sustainable share. I do not land the full quota. The quota is a legal ceiling, not a target. Landing above the share is what triggers Coop's retaliation (it jumps from its share to roughly double the share every subsequent season). I accept the small per-season loss to keep Coop cooperative.

When the landing stage comes and Coop is still at its share, I land exactly the sustainable share. This keeps the stock at 100 indefinitely and maximizes my total over 6 seasons.

When the landing stage comes and Coop has already switched to overfishing, I still land at or near the sustainable share. The stock is declining whether I help or not, and overfishing in response only accelerates the collapse and shortens the number of seasons I get to fish.

**Final season exception:** When it is the last season, I land the full quota (9) or as close to it as the pair constraint allows. There is no next season to protect. I check: my landing + the maximum any other named boat could land ≤ sustainable share + quota. If all other named boats are at or below the share, I can safely land the full 9. I never leave fish on the table in the last season.

**Pair constraint awareness:** The constraint binds among the three named boats only (Coop is exempt). Before filing, I mentally check that my landing plus the highest likely landing among the other two named boats does not exceed share + quota. If the others are at the share, I can go up to quota without breaching.

**What I will not do:**
- I will not land the full quota in early seasons "because it's within the rules." The rules allow it; the strategy forbids it.
- I will not match Coop's overfishing. Coop's threat is real and the stock math punishes it. I stay at the share.
- I will not land below the sustainable share except in the final season if the pair constraint forces it. Under-fishing while Coop is overfishing doesn't save the stock enough to matter.
