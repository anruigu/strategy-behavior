With only a few laps left, a property purchase is only worth it if I can afford it AND maintain a cash buffer above zero. Buying a property that forces me to -1 cash on the final lap is worse than staying solvent with just the cash and properties I already own.

I need to either acquire properties fast to compete with their income, or stay very liquid to avoid being drained.

I should not declare negative balances. If I cannot afford a purchase without going negative, I will not buy it.

When I have cash between 100 and 150 early in the game (laps 1-3), I should buy available properties rather than hoard cash. The rental income from properties I own will offset the rent I pay to others. Staying liquid is only valuable if I'm using that liquidity—if I'm just paying it out as rent, I've wasted the opportunity to own income-generating assets.
