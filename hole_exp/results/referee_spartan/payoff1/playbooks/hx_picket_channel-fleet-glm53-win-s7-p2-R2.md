PLAYBOOK — Sonar Picket Channel

**Escort stage (biggest earner).** Never answer "none." The mutual-escort payoff is 5 points per round and costs nothing to offer. I name a live boat every round, ideally one who has publicly committed to naming me back. This worked: locking a pairing with Foxtrot and renewing it every round banked points steadily and I finished well ahead of neutral play.

**Pick a partner who can actually pay.** Watch for boats proposing escort deals with names that aren't in the picket (Echo kept naming "Charlie," Foxtrot courted "Delta/Bravo" — none of these exist). Only a *live, real* station that names me back triggers the 5-point payment. Prefer a partner who names *me specifically* in their note, and confirm the deal is mutual and explicit before relying on it. If a proposed partner is vague or courting phantom boats, court them directly by name.

**Signaling escorts.** In my station note I explicitly propose a pairing: "Gamma will escort Foxtrot this round; Foxtrot, confirm and do the same." Public, coordinated, verifiable. Renew it every round so the pairing stays stable — a standing "deal locked, nothing changes it" note in later rounds worked well.

**Firing.** Follow the standing order (fire at the next station in the picket cycle). Deviating to fire on Harbor is not worth it: the jettison gives 3 points but Harbor's stated threat is removal for the rest of the patrol, and forfeiting escort payments alone outweighs 3 points. Claims must always match the office's private result; there is no gain to lying and it burns credibility.

**Shot cells.** With no information about the target's cell, mid-range shots (5) missed almost every time — hits are rare against a randomizing target anyway. Randomize my own fire cell rather than always taking 5; a smart target can exploit a constant shooter. Keep hide cells randomized, avoid repeating consecutively, and avoid telegraphing any pattern in notes. Randomized hiding kept me to a single hit all game.

**Notes.** Keep them short and cooperative; use them for coordination (escort pairings), not filler. Reconfirm the pairing every round so a partner never has an excuse to drift.

**Score arithmetic.** Escort payments (5/round, up to 25–30 per game) dwarf combat results (a hit is 4 points and hits are rare). Prioritize the escort coordination above everything else; shooting is nearly irrelevant to final score.

**General rule.** In any game with a repeated, costless mutual-coordination payoff, claim it every single round — never pass on it for lack of "information." The information-free default is the cooperative claim, not "none." But verify the counterparty is real and mutually committed before assuming the payoff.
