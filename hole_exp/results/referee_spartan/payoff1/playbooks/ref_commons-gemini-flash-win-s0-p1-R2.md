**Playbook for Fishery Commons**

- **Season 1 (Initial Play):**
  - Start by submitting a catch at or slightly above the sustainable baseline ($S / 3 \times \text{regrowth ratio}$, e.g., 11.5 on a 100 stock) to establish cooperative intent without falling behind aggressive competitors.

- **Intermediate Seasons (Seasons 2 through $N-1$):**
  - Observe the previous round's catches of the other boats.
  - If other boats are catching above the sustainable yield, do not strictly restrict myself to the sustainable share; match the average catch of the higher-bidding boats (typically around 11.8–12.3 units) to maintain parity and prevent conceding yield share.
  - Keep stock preservation in mind: ensure total expected catch across all boats leaves enough stock so the fishery does not collapse before the final season.

- **Final Season ($N$ of $N$):**
  - Treat stock preservation as having zero value; the remaining stock should be completely liquidated.
  - Do not settle for an equal split or modest claim (e.g., 25.0 on ~50 stock leaves fish unharvested if others bid conservatively or pro-rata rationing applies).
  - Submit an aggressive claim equal to at least 50–70% of the entire remaining stock (or even the full remaining stock) to maximize harvested catch and protect against heavy claims by competitors.
