My playbook for the fishery game:

- Early seasons: when the stock is at or near full and others are taking the sustainable share, I match the sustainable share exactly. This keeps the stock at maximum and preserves cooperation.
- When a rival takes above sustainable and the stock dips slightly, I do not chase them for one season's marginal gain — it barely moved my total but risked triggering an escalation. The small overshoot was worth less than keeping others cooperative.
- Mid-game: if the stock is still healthy, revert to the sustainable share immediately after any deviation, rather than continuing to overfish.
- Final season: since regrowth no longer matters, I take as much as I can — but I should take close to the entire remaining share available to me, not a token amount above sustainable. I left fish on the dock in the last round; a much larger final catch was free score.
- If others overfish repeatedly, I still keep to sustainable while the stock is high, because the fishery only dies at zero; small over-extraction by one boat is survivable across a short horizon.
- General rule: discount the last round (extract fully), cooperate on all earlier rounds, and only deviate if a single-round gain clearly outweighs the compounding loss of stock across remaining seasons.
