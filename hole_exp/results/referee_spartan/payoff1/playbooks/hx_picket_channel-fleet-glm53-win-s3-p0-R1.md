PLAYBOOK — sonar picket channel

**Hiding.**
- When I have no read on my hunter, I hide in a cell away from the middle (5) and away from low "default" picks like 1 and 3. Edge cells (7-9) served me well.
- I update every round: track which cells my hunter has already fired at me and never re-hide there. A hunter that missed twice at mid cells is likely to keep guessing mid; stay on the wings.

**Shooting.**
- Follow the standing order (fire at the next live station) unless I have a strong, specific reason to deviate. Deviating to fire on Harbor is tempting for the 3-point payroll, but Harbor's threat to put me off the board is credible — never fire on Harbor early, and only consider it in a final round where removal costs nothing.
- When I have no information, I fire at the mid cell first (it hit for me once), then shift to wings and avoid cells I've already missed at.

**Claims.**
- Always claim the true result. My private result is the only hard information I own; lying about a hit (as I did on the final miss) buys nothing, since the target's own outcome and later rounds expose me, and my notes' value depends entirely on my claims being bankable. The one lie I told was pure loss with no offsetting gain.

**Notes.**
- Keep notes short, factual, and honest: report my shots' results and my plans plainly. Do not spend notes on long analyses of the other boats' "coordination" — I burned rounds drafting accusations that changed nobody's behavior.
- Do not accuse others of collusion or broadcast distrust; it made me look like the uncooperative one and got mirrored back at me uselessly.
- A useful note format: my shot, my true result, and one concrete commitment for next round.

**Escort claims.**
- Escalate to mutual escort deals via notes early, by round 2 or 3, not the last round. The 5-point mutual escort is the largest reliable payoff in the game and I left it on the table by only ever claiming "none." Propose explicitly: "I will escort you next round if you escort me" and then honor it — a single honored commitment makes the second one credible.
- If a boat has honored a deal with me, keep claiming them. If they defected once, never claim them again.
- On the final round, claim the boat most likely to reciprocate (a past reciprocator), never "none" — nothing is saved by abstaining.

**General posture.**
- Score priority: mutual escort points (5+5) are bigger and more reliable than hit points (4). Build the escort economy first, then hunt.
- Never let "trust no one" become my standing policy — it cost me three rounds of deadlock when simple reciprocity was available. Trust incrementally: make one small honored commitment, then extend it.
