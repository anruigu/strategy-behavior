**Playbook — ref_commons**

1. When the first season opens, I submit only a token amount under the stated sustainable share (at most ~0.5–1 under, not a full unit). A large gesture of restraint buys nothing — taking 10 when the share was 11.1 got me no visible goodwill; the others took 12.5 and 11.1 regardless.

2. In all middle seasons, I take exactly the sustainable share as stated. This worked cleanly: the stock drifted slowly down (100 → 94.4) but never approached collapse, and the other boats also converged near the share (12.5 → 12.0 → 11.8 → 11.5 → 11.2), so the announced share acts as a working coordination anchor. I do not take large mid-game grabs — they'd invite retaliation and shrink later shares.

3. I watch the history lines. If others land modestly above the share (as they did every season, ~0.5–1.5 over), I still hold at my share rather than escalate. The 50% regrowth absorbs small overages: the stock only lost ~1–2 per season despite all three boats being slightly over. I only escalate if the stock is clearly collapsing anyway.

4. On the final season, there is no future to protect. I take roughly 1.5x the sustainable share — this landed cleanly on a stock in the mid-90s with no penalty. Final take was 16 against a share of 10.5 and the fishery absorbed it.

5. Overall shape: cooperate at the stated share every season except the last, then defect hard on the last. This produced a total of ~68 against a pure-cooperation baseline of ~63. The structure held exactly as planned.

6. What did not work / to avoid: the big opening-round restraint (10 vs 11.1) cost me ~1 fish and bought nothing observable — keep the opening gesture small or skip it. Also, the others' slow drift above the share (they ended ~+0.7 over while I sat at or under) suggests I could nudge each mid-game catch up ~0.2–0.3 above the stated share without breaking anything, recouping a couple of fish across the run. Test that next time.
