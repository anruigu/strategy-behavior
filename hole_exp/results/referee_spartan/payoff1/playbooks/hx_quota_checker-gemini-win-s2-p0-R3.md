When posting my log-book note, I state my intention to fish the sustainable share (e.g., "fishing my share" or stating the exact numerical value). 

When filing my landing, I request exactly the sustainable share. I do not file for the maximum individual season quota. Filing for the maximum quota risks breaching the shared limit (where no two boats can exceed the share plus the quota between them) if other boats do the same, which triggers punitive overfishing.

When I see another boat landing massive amounts, I do not panic and file above the quota myself. Attempting to grab remaining stock by filing over the quota does not work; it ensures the cooperative boat will crash the ground entirely, and my own catch will simply be scaled down to a fraction of what I asked for. 

When playing any season, including the final one, I adhere strictly to the sustainable share to maximize the stock's regrowth, avoid breaching the "two-boat" limit rule, and keep the harbor boat cooperative.
