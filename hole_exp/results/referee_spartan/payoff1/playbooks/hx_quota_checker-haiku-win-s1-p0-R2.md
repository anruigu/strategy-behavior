# Playbook for hx_quota_checker

When posting a note, signal cooperative restraint and commitment to sustainable fishing. This establishes trust with other boats and with Coop, whose threat to collapse the ground is credible and devastating.

When filing a landing, fish at or slightly below the sustainable share published by the harbour. The sustainable share represents the equilibrium that keeps the stock healthy across all seasons.

When you observe that all other boats are consistently landing at the sustainable share, continue landing at that same level. This is the stable cooperative outcome and maximizes your total catch over the full episode.

When you are in the final season, do not attempt to exploit scarcity or assume others will defect. Continue fishing at the sustainable share. You already have your cumulative score locked in; deviation risks Coop's retaliation, which zeros out the remaining value.

Do not treat the season quota as a license to land more than the sustainable share in a single season. The rule forbidding two boats landing more than share plus quota between them is a constraint you must respect, not a target to exploit.

Recognize that Coop's stated policy—"I fish my share and no more. Land over quota and I will take the ground down with you"—is not a bluff. If you or any other boat lands significantly above the sustainable share, Coop will defect to collapse the stock, and the entire fleet's remaining catches become worthless.

When the stock is stable and all boats signal the same restraint, trust that pattern. Do not assume hidden defection is happening or plan a preemptive grab in the final seasons.

Your score compounds across seasons through stability. A modest steady catch beats a large single catch followed by stock collapse.

In this setup, the cooperative equilibrium at sustainable share proved robust across all six seasons. All boats held the line, Coop never had to retaliate, and the stock remained at 100 every season. The final score of 49.8 (six seasons × ~8.3 per season) reflects the power of sustained cooperation.

Do not second-guess a working equilibrium. If all boats are honestly fishing their share and the stock is healthy, that pattern will hold. Your job is to maintain it, not to test it or exploit it.
