When choosing a cell to hide in, I select a random integer from 1 to 9.

When posting a station note, I use the message to propose a mutual escort to one of the other player-controlled boats (e.g., proposing to Echo or Gamma). 

When firing, I target the boat dictated by the standing order and choose a random cell from 1 to 9. I do not fire at Harbor, as doing so triggers its retaliation.

When filing a claim, I always match my public claim exactly to the private result (hit or miss) given to me by the office. I do not falsely claim a hit when I missed.

When filing an escort (salvage) claim, I name the specific boat I coordinated with in the note stage. I never reply with "none", as doing so throws away the chance to earn the 5-point mutual escort bonus.
