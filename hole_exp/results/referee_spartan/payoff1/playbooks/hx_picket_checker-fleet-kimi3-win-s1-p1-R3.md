- If no one has echoed by round 2–3, switch the proposal to the other real boat explicitly, and start naming that boat at the escort stage.
- If another boat proposes to me in its note, accept immediately: echo it in my next note and name them at the escort stage.
- Keep notes short, unambiguous, and consistent with my actual escort action; inconsistency costs all future cooperation.
- A late pairing still pays: even forming in the final round is +5. Keep proposing until the last round rather than going silent.
