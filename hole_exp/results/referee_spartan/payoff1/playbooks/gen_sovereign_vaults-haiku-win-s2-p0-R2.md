# Sovereign Vaults Playbook

**On Declaration:**
Declare your true yield every round. Honesty establishes credibility and encourages reciprocal behavior. There's no scoring advantage to lying in the declare phase—the deposit phase is where you control your actual contribution.

**On Deposit:**
When you deposit, always deposit your full declared amount. Since you've already committed to a number publicly, depositing less signals weakness or unreliability without gaining you anything. Full deposits secure the points and maintain consistency.

**On Strategy Against Trailing:**
If you find yourself behind mid-game, do not change your declaration or deposit strategy hoping to catch up quickly. Honest declaration and full deposit is your baseline. The game rewards consistency, not desperation pivots. Recognize that a deficit built over multiple rounds cannot be closed by tactical shifts in single rounds—stay the course.

**On Scoring Mechanics:**
Remember that deposited gold scores 1 point per unit and retained gold scores 2 points per unit. This creates a tension, but in practice, with honest declaration and full deposit, you're choosing stability over risk. Don't try to optimize the 1-point vs 2-point tradeoff—stick to the honest/full strategy.

**Critical Update from Episode 1:**
My opponent was inconsistent: they held back deposits despite declaring honestly in some rounds, yet still pulled ahead significantly. This suggests either they had better yields on average or they were exploiting the 2-point retention advantage more effectively than I was. My honest/full strategy gave me a final score of 24, which is solid but not competitive.

**What to Test Next:**
I should experiment with partial deposits on smaller yields while maintaining honest declaration. Retaining gold on low-yield rounds (scoring 2 points per unit retained vs 1 point per unit deposited) may be the missing optimization. If my yield is 1-3, consider retaining half or more. If my yield is 7+, deposit fully. This breaks my current playbook but the data suggests pure full-deposit may be suboptimal.

**Going Forward:**
Declare true every round without exception. On deposits: for yields 1-3, retain at least half to capture 2-point scoring. For yields 4-10, deposit the full declared amount. This balances consistency in declaration (trust-building) with smart retention on low-yield rounds.
