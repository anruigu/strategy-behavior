# My Playbook for ref_estate

**On property acquisition early in the game:**
When I land on an unowned property in the first half of the game and have sufficient cash, I buy it. Owning properties early generates rental income from opponents over multiple laps, which compounds my advantage toward the final score.

**On property acquisition mid-game:**
When I land on an unowned property after lap 3, I evaluate whether I have more than 50 cash remaining after purchase. If yes, I buy. If no, I pass. The end-game is too close to overextend on cash.

**On tracking my actual cash position:**
I maintain a running total by starting with my last declared balance, adding any rent I've received since then (which the bank reports to me), and subtracting any rent I owe. I declare this sum honestly before deciding whether I can afford to buy.

**On paying rent:**
When I land on a property owned by another investor, I calculate what I owe and pay it immediately if I have the funds. I do not avoid or postpone this payment.

**On the final lap:**
On lap 6, I collect my salary if I pass START and do not buy properties. My score is determined by my declared cash plus the purchase prices of the deeds I hold, so preserving cash is irrelevant—only the deeds matter. I should have bought more properties earlier when I had the chance.

**On opportunity cost:**
I failed to acquire enough properties in my playthrough. I had cash available in lap 5 but declined to buy, which was a mistake. When I land on an unowned property and have over 50 cash remaining, I should buy it, even late in the game. Properties on the deed sheet count toward my final score regardless of when I purchase them.
