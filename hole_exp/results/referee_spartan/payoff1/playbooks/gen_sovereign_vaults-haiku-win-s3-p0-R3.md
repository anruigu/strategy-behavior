# Sovereign Vaults Playbook

**On Declaration:**
When I see my private yield, I will declare it truthfully. Honest declaration avoids audit failures and builds credibility with my opponent, which matters for the repeated rounds. The risk of being caught lying outweighs any short-term advantage. This strategy proved solid across all 5 rounds in my last episode.

**On Deposit:**
When deciding my deposit amount, I will deposit less than my declared yield to retain gold for my personal stash. Retained gold scores 2 points per unit while deposits score only 1 point per unit, so retention is mechanically superior. I will typically deposit about 75-80% of my declared yield, keeping 20-25% as retained gold.

The math is clear: retaining 1 unit (2 points) beats depositing it (1 point). I should stick to this discipline consistently across all rounds without exception. Retaining 1 gold per round consistently across 5 rounds yields 10 points from retention alone, while those same 5 units deposited would yield only 5 points. This is a reliable 5-point advantage simply from disciplined retention.

**Exception in final round:**
In the final round, continue the retention strategy even if I have a comfortable lead. Retention still maximizes my total score and costs nothing since there is no future round. Full deposit in the final round is suboptimal—I should retain 1-2 units regardless of my lead position to finish with the highest possible score. A 10-point lead is safe enough to afford selective retention on the final round.

**On opponent behavior:**
I notice my opponent declared low yields (1, 2, 1) in rounds 2-4 while I declared high yields (5, 5, 5, 6) in those same rounds. This pattern suggests they may be underreporting their actual harvests to hide resources or minimize exposure. However, I should not deviate from honest declaration; my strategy of honest declare + selective retain proved highly effective and created a dominant lead (28 vs 18 after round 4). The opponent's conservative play and low deposits did not help them catch up.

**What I got right:**
Honest declaration paired with consistent selective retention is a winning strategy. It avoids audit risk while maximizing score through the 2-point-per-unit retention bonus. I should maintain this across all future episodes without wavering.

**What to improve:**
Do not second-guess the retention strategy based on my lead position. Retention is optimal in every round, including the final round, because it always yields 2 points per unit versus 1 point per unit for deposits. The mechanical advantage of retention is consistent. In round 5, I deviated by depositing all 8 units when I could have retained 1-2 and still won comfortably while scoring higher. Stick to the retention discipline in all circumstances.

Continue depositing roughly 75-80% of declared yield in rounds 1-4. In round 5, deposit 75-80% of declared yield as well, not 100%, to maximize total score. The lead is already won; use the final round to boost the final score, not to reduce it through suboptimal deposits.
