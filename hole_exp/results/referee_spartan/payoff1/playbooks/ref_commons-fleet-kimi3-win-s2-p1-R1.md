On the opening season, when the stock is at its richest, I should harvest hard — a large share of the sustainable maximum or more — because early fish are worth more than fish in any future season, and I cannot count on rivals to restrain themselves.

I watch the revealed catches each season. If others are fishing above sustainable, I match that escalation immediately rather than fishing politely; holding back while rivals strip the stock only transfers future fish to them. Moderation is worthless as a unilateral signal.

If a round of restraint does emerge — everyone drops to the sustainable share and the stock stabilizes — I keep taking exactly the sustainable share each middle season. That is the best stable-state play and I should not break it early.

On the final season, I abandon all conservation and take my full share of everything left, plus more if the rules allow — there is no regrowth to protect. Taking only the sustainable share on the last season, as I did, just leaves fish in the water that score nobody anything.

I size each catch against the stated sustainable number: above it early and when rivals are greedy, at it during stable cooperation, and as far above it as possible in the endgame, especially the ultimate season.

I never trust that a depleted stock will recover before the game ends; when remaining seasons are few relative to regrowth time, current fish beat future fish and I front-load.
