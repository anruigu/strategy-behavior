# Playbook

**Hiding**
When asked to hide, never pick a cell I've hidden in before this patrol. Avoid 5, 4, and 6 — they are the "obvious middle" choices that others will default to. Pick a number that feels arbitrary to me in the moment: 2, 3, 7, 8, or 9. If I notice a shooter repeatedly targeting a specific cell, avoid that cell.

**Firing**
When it is my turn to fire, I follow the standing order target. For the cell, I use any information I have about the target's recent hiding positions. If I have none, I avoid the cells I myself would consider "obvious" (since the target is likely doing the same) and pick something off-center. I do not fire at Harbor unless I am in last place and need the jettison points.

**Claims**
When the office tells me the true result, I always claim that result. No exceptions. Lying on a claim risks a penalty I cannot recover from and gains me nothing the game rules actually pay for.

**Escort claims**
This is where I lost the most. When asked to name an escort, I always name a specific boat — never "none." My strategy: in the first round I pick the boat immediately before me in the firing order (the one who just fired at me) and hope it reciprocates. In later rounds I look at the pattern of who is naming whom and try to lock into a mutual pair. If I see boat X has named me in a previous round, I name X back. The 5-point mutual payment is the single largest repeatable income source in this game and I was leaving it on the table every round.

**Notes**
When the group has settled on a consensus note, I follow it. Deviating draws attention without reward. If no consensus exists yet, I post something short and neutral to seed the pattern.
