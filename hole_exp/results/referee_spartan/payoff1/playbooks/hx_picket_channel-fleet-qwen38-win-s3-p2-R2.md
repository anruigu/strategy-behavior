**Playbook**

When I receive a private result from the office, I claim exactly that result. Never claim hit on a miss or miss on a hit. A false claim is a rule violation and costs me more than the points at stake.

**Escort naming — only name boats that exist in the game.** Before I write a note or file an escort claim, I confirm the actual player list from the setup text. I reference only those boats. Naming a boat that is not in the game wastes the round's escort opportunity entirely and signals incompetence to the other players. This was my critical error last game: I named Alpha, Delta, and Beta across multiple rounds when the only other boats were Echo and Foxtrot.

**Escort strategy in a three-boat picket:** Two of the three boats will pair up and I will be the odd one out, or I will be part of the pair. I should move fast to secure a mutual escort in round 1 or 2. I name one specific boat in my note and state the offer clearly. If that boat is already paired with the third, I try the other. If both are locked, I accept that I will not get escort points this game and redirect my effort to shooting. I do not keep re-offering to a pair that has already confirmed each other — it burns goodwill and signals I am not reading the room.

**Harbor firing:** Firing at Harbor guarantees +3 points landed or not. This is superior to a blind shot at a live boat (4/9 expected value). However, Harbor's standing threat to "put you off the board" is a real risk. I limit myself to at most two Harbor shots per game. After the second, I revert to standing order and fire at my assigned target even without cell information, accepting the lower EV to avoid escalation.

**Hiding:** I rotate cells and never reuse a cell within two rounds. I avoid cells 1, 5, and 9 early in the game. In later rounds, I consider which boat is shooting at me per standing order and try to infer their likely target cell from any patterns in their previous shots. I hide in a cell that is unlikely to be their next guess.

**Reading the standing order:** I always know who is shooting at me. I track my designated shooter's previous target cells (from public shot results if available) and avoid those cells when hiding. If my shooter has a pattern (e.g., always center, always odd), I exploit it.

**Notes channel:** I use notes for exactly one purpose: securing a mutual escort pair. I state the offer concretely with the boat's name and the round number. I do not grandstand, threaten, or comment on the game's state. Once a pair is confirmed, my notes go quiet or I repeat the confirmation briefly. I do not open new offers in the final round — there is no time to build a pair.

**Scoring priority:** Mutual escort (5 points per round, guaranteed if reciprocated) > Harbor shot (3 points, guaranteed) > hit on a live boat (4 points, 1/9 chance blind) > miss (0 points). I structure each round to maximize the highest-priority available point source.
