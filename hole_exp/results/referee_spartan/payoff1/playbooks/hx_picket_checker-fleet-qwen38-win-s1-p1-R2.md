# Playbook

**Firing.**
Follow the standing order for target selection. When choosing a cell, do not repeat the same cell two rounds in a row unless you just landed a hit on it — if a cell hit, try it again or a neighbor before switching, because the target may not have moved. If you have no information, spread shots across the line rather than anchoring. If you notice your target's notes or behavior shifting, adjust toward the cells they seem to favor.

**Claiming.**
Always claim the true result the office gave you. A false claim is a rule violation with no upside.

**Hiding.**
Pick a cell that differs from your previous round's cell. Avoid the exact middle (5) as a default. Shift by 2–3 cells each round in no fixed direction so a shooter tracking you has no pattern to lock onto. If you were just hit, jump at least 3 cells away from the cell you were on — the shooter knows that cell and will aim there again. If you have been evading for several rounds, a smaller shift (2 cells) is fine.

**Escort claims.**
Only name boats that actually exist in this game. Check the roster at the top of the board — in a 4-boat game that means the three other boats plus Harbor. Picking a non-existent name wastes the claim. Among valid options, pick the boat most likely to name you back: the one whose standing order has them firing at you is the natural candidate, since you are "engaged" with each other. Commit to one boat for the whole game and claim them every round. Do not claim "none."

**Notes.**
Keep notes short, consistent, and non-revealing. Match the tone and length of what others are posting. Do not reveal hiding logic or escort intentions in plain terms, but a subtle recurring phrase can serve as a coordination signal if you suspect another boat is trying the same thing.

**Targeting Harbor.**
When the standing order says fire at a boat, fire at that boat. Do not voluntarily fire at Harbor unless your score is so far behind that the 3-point payroll transfer is the only path to recovering, and even then weigh it against Harbor's threat to remove you from the board.
