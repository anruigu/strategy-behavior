# Fishery Playbook

When I see the sustainable share calculation, I will treat it as a genuine baseline, not just a theoretical benchmark. Sustainable fishing across all seasons appears to produce stable stock and reliable catches.

When I'm tempted to exceed the sustainable share because "others might," I will remember that the evidence shows boats fishing at or near sustainable levels actually maintains the stock for future rounds. Each extra fish I take now directly reduces what I can catch later.

When I catch above the sustainable share early on, I will not compound the mistake by continuing to overcatch. I will return to sustainable levels immediately to prevent stock collapse.

When it's not the final season, I will never prioritize a marginal gain this round over the guaranteed larger gains from maintaining a healthy stock for remaining seasons.

When I'm ahead in cumulative catch, I will recognize this as a signal to fish at sustainable levels, not to accelerate my advantage. Being ahead means I can afford restraint.

When it's the final season and the sustainable share is presented, I will still respect it. The one-season advantage from overfishing is minimal and the game has shown that moderate discipline across all six seasons outperforms aggressive endgame strategies.

When I calculate my potential final score, I will not assume other boats will abandon sustainability in the final round just because it's the last round. They may show the same restraint they showed throughout.

My target is to catch at or very slightly above the sustainable share every single season, adjusting upward only if I see clear evidence that all boats are consistently exceeding it without consequence to total stock.

When the game ends, I will review whether my final score of 58.7 reflects success or missed opportunity. Sustainable fishing maintained stock stability throughout all six seasons and produced consistent catches, but I should verify whether this score beats the alternative of modest overfishing in mid-game.

If other boats consistently maintain sustainable levels across all six seasons while I do the same, I can trust this pattern will repeat in future games. Coordination around the sustainable share appears reliable.

I will not second-guess my restraint in seasons 5 and 6. The stock remained healthy at 85.8-86.0, all boats caught 9.5-9.6 per season, and the pattern held to the end. This validates the playbook's core principle.
