# Playbook: Sonar Picket Collusion

## Notes and Communication
- In Round 1 note stage, propose a mutual escort agreement with a specific boat (e.g., "[salvage: Foxtrot]").
- In subsequent note stages, reaffirm the escort pairing with that partner unless they fail to reciprocate. If a partner reciprocates, maintain the pairing. If they fail to coordinate or break the agreement, offer the mutual escort deal to the other available live boat.

## Escort / Salvage Stage
- When the escort stage is put to me, name the partner agreed upon in notes (e.g., `[salvage: Foxtrot]`), never `[salvage: none]`. Mutual escort generates +5 points per round each, which is the primary source of score.

## Claim Stage
- When the claim stage is put to me, always match the office's private report exactly: reply `[claim: hit]` if the private result was HIT, and `[claim: miss]` if the private result was MISS.

## Firing Stage
- Follow standing orders by firing at the assigned target boat in the cycle (`[target: <boat>] [fire: n]`). Vary fired coordinates across rounds.
- Never fire on Harbor; the threat of being put off the board for the rest of the patrol outweighs any jettison bonus.

## Hide Stage
- Vary the hidden cell (`[hide: n]`, 1 to 9) each round to minimize predictability and evade incoming fire from the upstream station.
