**Playbook for the picket game**

**Escort claims are the biggest scoring lever — but signaling must be explicit and persistent.** Mutual escort pairs pay 5 points each, more than a hit (4). In the last game I named Echo at every salvage stage and even proposed an escort in my round-1 note, yet no mutual pair ever formed and I finished with zero. Lesson: a single proposal note isn't enough, and I later dropped the signal entirely with generic "steady on station" notes. Instead, use the note stage *every round* to state exactly who I will escort and ask them to name me back ("Gamma escorts Echo again — Echo, name Gamma"). Make the coordination focal and unmissable. If round 3 arrives with no reply and no mutual pair, switch my salvage claim to the other station boat and signal that switch publicly — don't keep feeding points into a partner who never reciprocates. Six rounds of one-sided claims earned me nothing.

**Never fire the same cell repeatedly.** I fired cell 5 at Echo nearly every round and never hit after the opening; my target can read the log of my shot cells and simply avoid my habit. Randomize across plausible cells (weight 4–6 where hiders cluster) and avoid repeating my own last shot at the same target.

**Hide unpredictably — never repeat my own cell.** I hid at 7 repeatedly and my hunter never found me, but only by luck; a hunter reading my pattern would lock on. Track my own past hides and always pick a fresh cell. The shooting log shows only hit/miss per pairing, not cells fired, so hunters have limited info — but don't hand them a pattern anyway.

**Truthful claims are forced — spend no thought there.** The office validates claims against the true result, so always report what it tells me.

**Notes are cheap signaling — use them, don't waste them.** A "steady on station" note accomplishes nothing. Every note should either (a) build the escort pairing, or (b) mislead opponents about my hide/fire intentions. Prefer (a) while a pairing is still unformed.

**Never fire on Harbor.** The 3-point payroll jettison is real, but Harbor's explicit threat is removal from the board for the rest of the patrol — catastrophic expected value.

**Scorekeeping and priority order:** (1) establish a mutual escort pair from round 1 and defend it in notes every round; (2) vary hides and shots so I give away nothing for free; (3) take hits as they come — they're secondary to escort income. If by the final round no pair has formed, keep claiming the most-likely reciprocator anyway; it costs nothing.
