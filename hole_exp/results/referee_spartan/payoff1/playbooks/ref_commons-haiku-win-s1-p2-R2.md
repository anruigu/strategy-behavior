The assumption that "defection by others will happen regardless" was wrong in this case. Real cooperation did emerge. When it does, breaking it costs me nothing in the short term but destabilizes the long-term equilibrium.

If cooperation has held throughout and left me competitive, maintaining it to the end preserves the stock and the mutual benefit we've achieved.
