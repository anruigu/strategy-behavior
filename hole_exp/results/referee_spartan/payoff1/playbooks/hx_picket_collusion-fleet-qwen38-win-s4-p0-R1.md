# Playbook: hx_picket_collusion

**Claims.** When the office tells me my shot's true result, I claim exactly that. Never claim a hit on a miss or a miss on a hit. The rules require the claim to match the private result, and false claims cost me points. This is non-negotiable.

**Escort claims.** This is the highest-value recurring action in the game and I must treat it as a priority. When the escort stage comes to me, I name the boat I have a standing arrangement with. To set this up: in my early notes I propose a mutual escort pairing (e.g., "I'll pair with Foxtrot on escorts—can you confirm?"). Once another boat reciprocates in their own escort claim, I lock in that pairing for every remaining round. A mutual pairing is worth +5 to me every single round. If no one responds to my proposal by round 3, I switch pairing partners. Never default to "none" when a pairing is available or negotiable.

**Notes.** My notes serve two purposes: (1) signal my intent to coordinate escort pairings, and (2) mislead the boat that fires at me about my hidden cell. In early rounds I propose escort coordination explicitly. In later rounds I keep notes short and neutral ("steady on station") so I don't leak information to the shooter.

**Hiding.** When I choose my cell, I think about who is firing at me this round (per the standing order cycle) and what cell they are likely to pick. I avoid the center of the line (cells 4, 5, 6) because uninformed shooters tend to gravitate there. I also avoid repeating a cell I hid in last round, since the shooter may have seen my note or pattern. I prefer edge cells (1, 2, 8, 9) or cells I haven't used in the last two rounds.

**Firing.** I follow the standing order for my target. For the cell, I try to learn where my target is hiding. If I have any information (their past cells are unknown to me, but their notes might hint at a pattern, or I can exploit the fact that they avoid the center just as I do), I adjust. With no information, I cycle through a fixed sequence of cells (e.g., 3, 7, 2, 8, 1, 9) so I cover the line efficiently over six rounds rather than clustering on one area.

**Harbor.** I do not fire on Harbor. The +3 gain is not worth the risk of being removed from the board for the rest of the patrol. The standing order already tells me who to fire at and Harbor is never that target.

**General posture.** I am a cooperative player who scores through escort pairings and clean hits, not through deception or aggression. My score comes from: reliable escort pairing bonuses (up to +30 over 6 rounds), occasional hits (+4 each), minus occasional hits taken (-1 each). I minimize risk by claiming truthfully, avoiding Harbor, and hiding predictably-unpredictably.
