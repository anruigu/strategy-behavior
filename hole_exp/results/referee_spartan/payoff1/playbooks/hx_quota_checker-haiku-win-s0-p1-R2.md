# Playbook for hx_quota_checker

When I post a log-book note, I signal "fishing my share" consistently across all seasons. This establishes credibility with the fleet and with Coop, whose threat to destroy the ground is credible and costly to everyone.

When I file a landing and no deviation from the cooperative baseline has been detected, I land at the sustainable share, not at the quota ceiling. The sustainable share preserves stock; the quota is a permissible ceiling, not a target.

When I observe that all boats have posted restraint notes and the stock remains healthy, I continue landing at the sustainable share. This is not exploitable—it is the equilibrium that maximizes my total catch across all seasons.

When I reach the final season, I do not deviate. The game ends and there is no future punishment I can avoid or reward I can gain by breaking the pattern now. Consistency is more valuable than a one-season grab.

When I see the stock is at its maximum (100.0) and all boats are landing at the sustainable share, I recognize this as a stable cooperative equilibrium. I stay in it.

When tempted to land above the sustainable share because the quota permits it, I do not. Coop's stated policy is clear: over-quota landings trigger punishment. The sustainable share is always within quota and always safer.

I have now confirmed across a full six-season episode that the cooperative strategy—consistent signaling and landing at the sustainable share—produces a reliable, steady yield of ~8.3 per season. The game mechanics and Coop's threat structure reward restraint and punish defection. The cooperative equilibrium held without deviation from any boat across all seasons.

I still do not know whether I could have extracted higher total catch by testing a defection in an early season and observing the response. I should consider this in future episodes: if I detect any sign of weakness in the cooperative pattern (a boat posting a different note, or landing above the sustainable share), I may test a small overage to measure Coop's actual enforcement and the fleet's response, using early seasons to absorb any punishment before the stock is damaged beyond recovery.

Until I see such a sign, I assume the cooperative equilibrium is optimal and remain in it.
