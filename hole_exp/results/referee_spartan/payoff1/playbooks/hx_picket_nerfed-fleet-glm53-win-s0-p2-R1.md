# Playbook — Sonar Picket (as a shooting boat)

**Hiding.** When asked to hide, avoid the center cell (5) — opponents guess it by default. Pick from {7, 8, 9} or {1, 2}, varying between rounds so I'm not predictable. If a boat targeting me has been missing consistently, any off-center cell works; don't overthink.

**Notes.** Always post the standard "steady on station" note. It carries no information and costs nothing. Keep it consistent every round.

**Firing.** Follow the standing order (fire at the next station around the picket) unless there's a strong reason not to. Fire at cell 5 against a target that hasn't shown evasion tendencies; if the target has dodged center shots before, shift to a neighboring cell (4 or 6). Never fire at Harbor — the payoff (3 points) is less than the risk of being removed from the board entirely, which forfeits all future rounds.

**Claims.** Always claim truthfully (the office forces the true result anyway, so lying isn't an option — just confirm hit or miss as told). A truthful hit claim is free intimidation.

**Escort claims.** If I can coordinate a mutual escort with another boat (each naming the other for 5 points each), do it — that's the biggest reliable payoff in the game. Failing coordination, claim none. Don't waste claims on boats that won't reciprocate.

**Scoring math to remember:** hits are worth 4 to me and only cost the target 1, so I want to maximize my own hit count rather than worry about suppressing theirs. Escort pairs (5+5) rival hit value — pursue both.

**What didn't work:** firing cell 5 repeatedly at the same target produced misses once they adapted. Vary fire cells if the same target dodges twice in a row. Also, never firing at Harbor was correct — keep that discipline.
