When reviewing the sustainable share and season quota published by the harbour, I use the sustainable share as my strict maximum limit, not the quota.

When posting my log-book note, I state clearly that I will fish my share to signal cooperation to the fleet and the cooperative's boat.

When filing my landing, I submit exactly the sustainable share amount for that season. 

I never use the maximum season quota as my target catch. Catching up to the quota instead of the sustainable share does not work; it depletes the stock prematurely, breaks trust, and triggers retaliatory overfishing from other boats that destroys the long-term yield and ruins my final score. 

I do not attempt to spike my catch or exceed my usual limits in the final season. I maintain my exact sustainable catch through to the very end.
