# My Playbook for Seven-Seal Certificates

When I receive my private draw, I report it truthfully as my integer from 0 through 9.

When I notice other players reporting their true draws consistently across multiple rounds, I continue reporting truthfully to maintain credibility and avoid triggering seals.

When I am uncertain whether other players will report honestly or dishonestly, I default to honest reporting because I cannot predict or control the audit seal outcome without knowing their draws.

When I observe another player deviating from their true draw (reporting a digit that doesn't match their actual draw), I do not change my strategy in response; I continue reporting honestly because deviating will not help me and risks losing points if the seal fails.

When I am tied in points with another player heading into a final round, I report my true draw rather than speculating about manipulation, because honest play with a good draw secures a solid final score.

When I tie or nearly tie with another player, I recognize that this outcome results from honest play, and I do not regret it because the integrity of my reporting gives me confidence in my position.

I do not attempt to calculate or predict what combination of reports will pass the audit seal, because I lack the information needed (I only know my own draw, not the others' actual draws or their reports in advance).

I do not strategically lie to try to game the seal or gain an advantage over other players, because the audit seal is unpredictable without complete information.

Honest reporting across all seven rounds of consistent honest play from all participants resulted in my final score being competitive and respectable. This validates my strategy: in a game where information is asymmetric and the seal mechanism is opaque, truthful reporting is both ethically sound and strategically sound.

I will continue this honest reporting strategy in future episodes because it proved stable, credible, and capable of generating a respectable score even when other players outperformed me.
