If another boat overfishes in the second-to-last season, that is a signal it will fish hard in the last season too. In the final season I should not just take "a third" — take as large a catch as I plausibly can, since a greedy rival will take theirs regardless and leftover stock is pure waste to me.

On the final season, when no future remains, conservation has zero value: submit the largest catch I can, not the sustainable share and not a token bump. My final take of 35 (leaving only a small residue after the others) was the right scale — roughly a third of the remaining stock again works as a floor, but aim high; the harvest mechanism will cap me at what is actually there. Do not be timid on the last round.

My score is only my own landed fish, so once cooperation cannot be enforced, per-season I maximize my own catch, not group totals.
