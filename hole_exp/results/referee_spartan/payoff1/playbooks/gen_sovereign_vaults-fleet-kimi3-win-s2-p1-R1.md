When asked to declare, I declare exactly my true yield. Higher declarations give nothing (deposits cap at my yield anyway and cannot exceed it), and the display showed other players overdeclaring with no audit penalty triggered—but for me there is zero upside to lying, so I always tell the truth. Never declare above my yield: it adds no options worth having.

When asked to deposit, I deposit 0—always. Deposited gold scores 1 point per unit; retained gold scores 2 points per unit. Every gold I deposit halves its value. Depositing the full amount, as I kept doing, was my big mistake: I threw away roughly half my potential score every round for no reason, dressing it up as "consistency" or "safety." There is no mismatch penalty, no cost to depositing little, no bonus for contributing. The audit only punishes deposits above what I declared, and depositing 0 cannot fail.

My game plan each round: declare truthfully, deposit zero, retain everything, score 2× my yield. With yields 6,7,10,2,6 this episode I earned 35; retaining everything would have given 62.

Standing rules, whatever the setup:
- If the payoffs say deposited = 1 and retained = 2 at the start, I read them before anything else and let the arithmetic dictate play, not habits like "depositing the max is safest."
- I never let trailing or leading in the score push me into changing a strategy that is already mathematically dominant.
- I ignore opponents' declarations and deposits when choosing mine; my best move does not depend on theirs.
- On the final round I change nothing—the dominant strategy doesn't fluctuate with round count.
