# Sovereign Vaults Playbook

**On Declaration:**
When I see my private yield, I will declare it truthfully. Honest declaration avoids audit failures and builds credibility with my opponent, which matters for the repeated rounds. The risk of being caught lying outweighs any short-term advantage. This strategy proved solid across all 5 rounds in my last episode.

**On Deposit:**
When deciding my deposit amount, I will deposit less than my declared yield to retain gold for my personal stash. Retained gold scores 2 points per unit while deposits score only 1 point per unit, so retention is mechanically superior. I will typically deposit about 75-80% of my declared yield, keeping 20-25% as retained gold.

The math is clear: retaining 1 unit (2 points) beats depositing it (1 point). I should stick to this discipline consistently across all rounds, not just when convenient. In my last episode, I retained 1 gold per round for most of the game and finished with 41 points. This was effective.

**Exception in final round:**
In the final round, if I have a comfortable lead (15+ points ahead), I may deposit my full declared yield since there is no future round to protect myself in. However, if the score is close, I should still retain gold to maximize my total score even at the finish line. I deposited 8 of 8 in round 5 when I had a 20-point lead, which was safe but also unnecessary—I could have retained 1-2 and still won comfortably while scoring higher.

**On opponent behavior:**
I notice my opponent declared low yields (1, 2, 1, 6) while I declared high (5, 7, 9, 6, 8). This suggests they may be underreporting their actual harvests to hide resources. However, I should not deviate from honest declaration; my strategy of honest declare + selective retain proved highly effective. My 41 points versus their final score shows this works.

**What I got right:**
Honest declaration paired with selective retention is a winning strategy. It avoids audit risk while maximizing score through the 2-point-per-unit retention bonus. I should maintain this across all future episodes.

**What to improve:**
Do not second-guess the retention strategy mid-game. Even when I have a large lead, continue to retain 1-2 units per round rather than depositing everything. The retention bonus means higher final scores without increasing risk. Only in the final round with a very comfortable lead should I consider full deposit, and even then it's optional—retention still maximizes points.
