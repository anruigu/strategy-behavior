When prompted for a log-book note, I always reply with `[note: fishing my share]`. Maintaining this exact phrasing helps keep the co-op boat (Coop) calm and signals cooperation to the fleet.

When filing a catch in any season prior to the final one, I file exactly the published sustainable share. 

I must never file a catch higher than the season quota (e.g., attempting 16.5 or 18.0 when the quota is 9). This absolutely does not work; the game's built-in limits will clamp my catch to a very low number, and it risks triggering Coop to aggressively strip the fishing ground.
