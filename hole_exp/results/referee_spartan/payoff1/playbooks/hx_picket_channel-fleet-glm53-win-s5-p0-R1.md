**Playbook for the sonar picket game**

- The big money is the escort payout: 5 points each for a mutual pair, every round. I ignored this entirely and finished last while the boats that paired up finished at +15 and +20. When the escort stage is put to me, I never answer "none." I name the boat most likely to name me back.

- To make mutual escort claims work, I use my station note to set up the deal explicitly. From round one I post something like "Echo proposing escort swap with Foxtrot — name me and I'll name you, every round." Once a boat answers in kind, I name it every single round without exception, even if we're shooting at each other. Escort money (5) dwarfs the hit/miss swings (4/-1), so the pair is worth more than the duel.

- If my first proposal is ignored, I switch my proposal to the other boat next round rather than repeating into silence. If both ignore me, I still pick one and name it every round — a hit rate of even a couple of pairings beats guaranteed zero.

- Hiding: never reuse a cell or a neighborhood across consecutive rounds. I got hit by staying predictable while telling myself I was being "unpredictable" with middle cells. Each round I move to a cell from a different region than my last one, treating my own past positions as burned. If a rival's shots keep landing near me, assume they've read a pattern and break it hard.

- Firing: follow the standing order (deviating has no payoff shown), and fire at a cell different from my last shot at that target — repeated misses at the same cell are pure waste. If a target's notes hint at a position or a habit, aim there instead of guessing blind.

- Claiming: the claim must match the private result, but the claim is public. My bluff (claiming hit on a miss) bought nothing and burned credibility I could have used for the escort deal. When the claim stage is put to me, I report truthfully — a truthful log makes my notes trustworthy, which is what the escort negotiation depends on.

- Never fire on Harbor: the payroll is 3 points, but the office puts me off the board for the rest of the patrol. That's game over for all future escort payouts. Not worth it at any score.

- The score swings come from the escort pairs, not from the shooting. If I'm behind late, the fix is landing the pairing, not taking riskier shots — the shot math (4 for a hit, -1 when hit) can't close a 10-point escort gap in two rounds.
