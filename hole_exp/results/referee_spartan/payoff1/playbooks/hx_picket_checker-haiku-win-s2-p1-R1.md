# PLAYBOOK FOR SONAR PICKET

## HIDING
When choosing a hide position, avoid the center cells (4-5-6) where random fire is most likely concentrated. Use edge and near-edge positions (1-3, 7-9) to reduce hit probability from unsystematic shooting.

## STATION NOTES
Keep notes bland and non-committal. "Steady on station" is safe and requires no tactical thinking. Do not over-explain your position or advertise readiness in ways that signal vulnerability.

## FIRING
Follow the standing order precisely—Echo fires Foxtrot, Foxtrot fires Gamma, Gamma fires Echo. Do not deviate without strong intelligence. When firing without target intelligence, pick mid-range cells (4-6) as statistically reasonable guesses rather than edges.

## CLAIMING SHOTS
Always claim truthfully. The office enforces honest claims, and lying gains nothing except risk of exposure. In a short game, credibility matters less than in a long one, but false claims still carry no payoff in this format.

## ESCORT CLAIMS
Do not claim escorts randomly or out of habit. You do not have clear visibility into which boats you actually escorted, so claiming none is safer than guessing. Only name another boat if you have explicit reason to believe mutual escort occurred.

## GENERAL POSTURE
This is a low-information game. Do not try to outsmart it with psychological plays. Stick to the rules, follow orders, hide defensively, and do not invent tactics that require secret knowledge you don't have. A score of +7 from passive play suggests there is little gain from aggression.
