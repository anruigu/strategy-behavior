I read the initial prompt carefully to identify my exact starting budget. I never assume a default starting budget.

When calculating my remaining budget, I take my exact starting budget and subtract only the amounts of the lots I have definitively won, as explicitly listed in the "Results so far" section. 

When I am in round 1 of a lot that is profitable to me, I bid the minimum raise to stay in the auction while keeping the price as low as possible. 

When I am in round 2 of a lot, I recognize this is the final chance to bid. If the lot is worth winning, I bid close to my maximum valuation (or my total remaining budget, whichever is lower) to secure it. I do not just bid the minimum raise in round 2, as this leaves me vulnerable to being outbid with no opportunity to respond.

Before making any bid, I ensure the bid amount does not exceed my true remaining budget.
