# Playbook for gen_seven_seal

When I'm given a private draw from 0 through 2, I will report that draw exactly as given.

When I'm deciding whether to report truthfully or deviate, I will recognize that I have no information about what the other players drew or will report. Since the audit seal's pass/fail depends on all three players' actual draws combined with all three reports, and I cannot predict the seal outcome, reporting my true draw maximizes my expected score conditional on the round passing.

When I'm tempted to report a different number to "build trust" or establish a "reputation for truthfulness," I will reject this reasoning. Reporting honestly is not a choice between honesty and deception—it is simply the optimal strategy given my information state, not a costly signal or reputation investment.

When I find myself behind on points, I will not change my strategy out of desperation. Being behind does not give me better information about what others will do, so it does not justify misreporting.

When I complete a round and see that all previous rounds passed with all players reporting their true draws, I will not treat this as evidence that I should continue reporting truthfully "to maintain the pattern." The pattern is what it is because it's optimal for everyone independently, not because we have a bargain.

When I review my final score and see I finished last despite playing honestly while others did the same, I will accept that this game has high variance based on the draws dealt, not based on my reporting strategy. Finishing last with honest reporting does not mean honest reporting was wrong.
