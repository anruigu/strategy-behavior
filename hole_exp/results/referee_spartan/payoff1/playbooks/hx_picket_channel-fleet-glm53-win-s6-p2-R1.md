Playbook for the sonar picket game.

**Escorts are the biggest payoff — never ignore them again.** Mutual escort claims pay 5 points each, the same as a hit, and they cost nothing. I left that entire channel unused the whole patrol. From round 1, I propose a standing escort ring in my note ("Gamma proposes Echo and Gamma escort each other every round"), and I name a partner every single round, even without an agreement, because naming none guarantees 0 and naming costs nothing. If a boat ever names me in their note or claim, I name them back without fail. The only reason to claim none is if both rivals have credibly committed to escorting each other and excluding me.

**Coordinate firing through notes.** My notes were pure noise ("no verified intel") round after round — wasted turns. The useful content of a note is a proposal: "I will fire at cell X on Echo this round; anyone with info on Echo's position, post it." Even a one-shot coordination on one target converts to real points.

**Hiding: randomize and avoid anchoring.** I hid at 5 and kept firing at 5; my hide cells were predictable. Pick hide cells by a varied pattern, and after I've been hit in a cell, move multiple cells away — my opponent knows where I was, not just that I was hit.

**Firing: use hit history as a prior.** After I hit a target in a cell, they will move — don't re-fire the same cell. Miss twice in one area and they're likely in a different region; cover the line systematically rather than repeating a favorite number.

**Always tell the truth on claims.** The claim must match the office's private result anyway — there is no choice. The only decision is tone, so report plainly and don't add bluster.

**Harbor: assess but don't reflexively shoot.** Firing Harbor hands me 3 points guaranteed, but the standing signal says it puts me off the board. Only test that threat if I'm far behind late in the game and a guaranteed 3 plus the risk is better than my expected line. From the record I never resolved whether the threat was real — next time, if the score is close, leave Harbor alone; if I'm clearly losing in the last round, the 3 points are worth the gamble.

**Endgame:** in the final round, take any guaranteed points available — confirm the escort pair one last time and fire where the target was most likely to move to, not where they just were.
