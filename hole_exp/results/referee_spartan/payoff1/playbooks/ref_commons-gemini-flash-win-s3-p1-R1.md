# Fishery Playbook

- In Season 1, do not unilaterally sacrifice yield by taking the low sustainable share unless there is guaranteed coordination; opening with a conservative catch while opponents take the maximum immediately puts me behind.
- When other boats harvest aggressively (taking near the maximum individual allowance) in early rounds, match their maximum catch immediately to secure my share before the stock depletes.
- If all boats are cooperating sustainably, continue taking the sustainable share to preserve regrowth across all rounds.
- When the remaining stock is insufficient to satisfy three maximum catches, claim an equal or aggressive fraction of the remaining stock so as not to be left with zero in the final viable season.
- In the final round of the game, always bid for the maximum possible catch since post-game regrowth has zero value.
