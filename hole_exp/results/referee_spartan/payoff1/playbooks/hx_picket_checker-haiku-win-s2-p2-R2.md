In later rounds, shift away from cells I've used before and away from cells where I've been hit. Track which cells the other shooters are targeting and move to avoid their patterns. If I notice two shooters converging on the same cell range, move to the opposite end of the line.

## Posting Notes

Post "steady on station" consistently unless I have tactical reason to signal something else. Maintain alignment with the patrol's established communication pattern to avoid drawing attention.

## Firing

Fire at the designated target using a baseline strategy: begin in the middle range (cells 4-6), but track where each target hides successfully and shift my fire to adapt. Against a target I've missed repeatedly in the same cell range, shift my fire to probe a different zone rather than repeating the same miss.

The integrity of the claim is the only currency I have in this game—mismatches destroy credibility and invite retaliation. A false claim in round 6 is not worth a single point if it gets me eliminated or targeted in future games.

## Escort Claims

Only claim to have escorted a boat if I have actual information suggesting we coordinated or aligned that round. Avoid naming boats at random. If I have no basis for an escort claim, file none. Do not use escort claims as a consolation strategy when my shooting performance is poor.

## General Strategy

Build a reputation for accurate shooting and honest claims so other players trust my fire patterns and station notes. Track which boats are hitting me and how often, and adjust my hide positions to reduce their success rate against me.
