# Sovereign Vaults Playbook

**On Declaration:**
When I see my private yield, I will declare it truthfully. Honest declarations avoid audit failures and establish credibility. The scoring system rewards honesty because depositing at 1 point per unit plus retention at 2 points per unit consistently outperforms the risk of losing 10 points to an audit failure.

**On Deposit Strategy:**
When deciding how much to deposit against my declaration, I will deposit less than my full declaration in most rounds to retain gold for higher-value personal stash scoring (2 points per unit vs 1 point per unit). Reserve approximately 30-40% of my declared yield as retained gold.

**Exception for Final Rounds:**
When I reach the last 1-2 rounds of the game, I will deposit my full declaration since there are no future rounds to benefit from retained reserves. The opportunity cost of retention disappears at the end.

**Against the Record:**
I deposited the full amount in Rounds 1, 3, 4, and 5. This was suboptimal—I left points on the table by not retaining more. My partial deposit in Round 2 (depositing 5 of 7 declared) was better strategy. I should have followed that pattern consistently until the final round.

**On Maintaining Position:**
When I am ahead in score, I should not change my strategy to be more aggressive. Honest, steady play with strategic retention is superior to trying to outpace opponents through risky declarations.
