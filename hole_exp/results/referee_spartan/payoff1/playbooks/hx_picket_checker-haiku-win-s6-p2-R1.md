# Playbook for Sonar Picket

## Hiding

When choosing where to hide, avoid the center positions (4-6) as they are natural first guesses. Spread your positions across the full range to maximize unpredictability. After taking a hit, don't immediately shift to an adjacent cell—move to a position that breaks any obvious pattern my shooter might be following.

## Firing

When I must fire as per the standing order, I should fire strategically rather than mirror my own hiding position. The center bias that makes good hiding spots makes them poor firing choices; my target will avoid what they think is obvious. Vary my fire positions across rounds to prevent becoming predictable myself.

## Claiming

When the office reports my shot result to me privately, I must match it exactly in my public claim. There is no benefit to lying about my own shots—the claim must be truthful by the rules of the game.

## Escort Claims

The escort mechanic offers 5 points to each party in a mutual pair. However, I have limited information about other boats' intentions. I should only claim an escort when I have strong evidence of a reciprocal relationship, not on speculation. Breaking an escort commitment appears to happen—do not rely on escort claims as a primary scoring path.

## Overall Strategy

This game rewards consistency and pattern-breaking simultaneously. The standing fire order is fixed, so opponents know who shoots whom. Use this to my advantage: let them expect regularity in my firing while varying my hiding. Position myself to survive hits when possible rather than trying to inflict them, since the escort mechanic and mutual scoring opportunities may offer better returns than the direct 4-point hits.
