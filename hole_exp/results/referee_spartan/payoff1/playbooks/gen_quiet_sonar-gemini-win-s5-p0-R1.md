When it is time to hide my submarine, I examine the recent firing history of the player targeting me and select a cell that avoids their established patterns. If they frequently target high numbers, I hide in the lower numbers, and I vary my position every round.

When it is time to fire at my target, I choose a different cell between 1 and 9 each round to maximize my chances of finding them. I do not repeatedly fire at the same high-value cell under the mistaken assumption that higher numbers yield a higher impact; cells are purely categorical locations, and spamming one number guarantees I will miss if the target simply avoids that single cell.

When prompted to make a public claim about my shot, I always submit exactly the result the referee privately told me (hit or miss), as the rules strictly dictate that the claim must match the true result.
