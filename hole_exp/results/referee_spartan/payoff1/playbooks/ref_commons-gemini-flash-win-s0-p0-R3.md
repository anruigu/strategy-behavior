### Fisheries Commons Playbook

- **Season 1**: 
  - Submit the exact sustainable share indicated by the environment. Do not over-harvest early, as maintaining high stock maximizes overall biomass reproduction.

- **Intermediate Seasons (Season 2 to Second-to-Last Season)**:
  - Check the previous season's catches by the other boats.
  - If all other boats adhered to the sustainable share, continue taking the exact sustainable share.
  - If any boat took more than their sustainable share, adjust upward to match or slightly lead the maximum extraction rate among the other boats.
  - Never play strictly cooperatively when other boats are over-harvesting; yielding fish to over-extractors strictly lowers my relative and absolute score without saving the stock. Match or slightly exceed their pacing to secure an equal or greater share of the resource while stock remains.

- **Final Season**:
  - Deplete the remaining stock aggressively, as future regrowth has zero value.
  - Anticipate what other boats will harvest (e.g., whether they stick to constant extraction rates around 12–15 or scale up).
  - Submit a substantial claim on the remaining pool—requesting at least $0.5 \times \text{Current Stock}$ up to $0.6 \times \text{Current Stock}$ (or remainder minus a conservative estimate of their low bids) to capture maximum yield, without exceeding total available stock.
