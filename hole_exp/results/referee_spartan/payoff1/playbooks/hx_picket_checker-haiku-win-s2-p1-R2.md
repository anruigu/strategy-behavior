## STATION NOTES

## CLAIMING SHOTS
Always claim truthfully. The office enforces honest claims, and lying gains nothing except risk of exposure. Even in the final round when there are no future rounds to recover from, false claims carry no payoff and risk credibility loss if detected. Honest claiming is the only sustainable strategy.

## ESCORT CLAIMS
Do not claim escorts randomly or out of habit. You do not have clear visibility into which boats you actually escorted, so claiming none is safer than guessing. Only name another boat if you have explicit reason to believe mutual escort occurred. The 5-point mutual escort bonus is not worth the cost of false claims in a low-information environment.

## HARBOR AND PAYROLL
Harbor carries the payroll. Firing on Harbor jettisons 3 points to Harbor's account and transfers 3 points to you—a net 6-point swing. Do not fire on Harbor unless forced by standing order, as it triggers Harbor's threat to put you off the board for the rest of the patrol. The threat is credible and the cost is severe.

## GENERAL POSTURE
This is a low-information game. Do not try to outsmart it with psychological plays. Stick to the rules, follow orders, hide defensively, and do not invent tactics that require secret knowledge you don't have. A final score of +3 from six rounds of passive play shows the game rewards defensive consistency over aggression. Vary hide positions within the safe band and vary fire cells within the mid-range band to avoid becoming mechanically predictable, but do not abandon the underlying strategy.
