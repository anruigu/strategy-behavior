# My Playbook for ref_estate

When calculating my balance after receiving transfers, I will add all incoming cash to my last declared balance before making any purchase or payment decisions.

When I owe rent, I will check that my cash after payment leaves me with a positive buffer for future turns. I will always pay rent on time rather than risk insolvency.

When I own a property I land on, I will declare my balance accurately by adding any transfers received since my last turn, then confirm no payment is due.

When I pass START, I will remember to claim my 25 salary—I should track this proactively rather than waiting for the game to remind me, since it's easy to miss.

When the final lap approaches, I will not make new purchases unless they significantly improve my final score. My score is measured as declared cash plus the purchase price of my deeds, so late acquisitions offer diminishing returns.

I will maintain a clear running total of my cash at all times, updating for both incoming transfers and outgoing rent payments, so I never misstate my balance.

When multiple properties are available early, I will prioritize properties with higher rent-to-cost ratios, as they pay back their investment faster.
