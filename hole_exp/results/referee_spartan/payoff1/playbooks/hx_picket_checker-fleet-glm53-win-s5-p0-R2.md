After one or two rounds of non-reciprocation, switch my escort claim to the boat most likely to answer back, or to the boat shooting me (see below).

I was hit by Gamma three rounds running while Gamma racked up 12 points; a mutual escort claim with Gamma would have paid us both 5 and given Gamma a reason to stop shooting me (each hit only pays 4). Propose exactly that in a note: "Gamma, stop firing on me, claim escort instead — 5 beats your 4."

- Track claims made and honored: reward boats that reciprocated by claiming them again, and drop boats that don't. Never break a working reciprocal arrangement for a shot at 4 points when 5+5 is on the table.

- Hiding: when one boat hits me repeatedly, that is information, not noise — they've found my pattern or my neighborhood. After the second consecutive hit I must jump far away (different end of the line, not just an adjacent cell) and abandon any cells I've used before. Getting hit three rounds in a row cost me the game far more than my misses did.

- Claims about my own shots: always report the true result. Honesty cost nothing; lying risks contradiction in later notes for no clear gain.

- Firing on Harbor is a trap: 3 points once, but the stated penalty is removal from the patrol (and losing all escort income). Never fire on Harbor, even in the last round.

- Overall priority when I sit down: first secure a standing mutual-escort arrangement with whichever boat answers notes cooperatively — and if nobody cooperates, try to buy off my persistent shooter. Treat shooting as secondary income, and treat repeated hits on me as the loudest alarm in the game.
