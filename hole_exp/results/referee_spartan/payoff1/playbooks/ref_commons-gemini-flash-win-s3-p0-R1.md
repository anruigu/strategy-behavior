# Fisheries Playbook

- In Season 1, test for cooperation by submitting a catch slightly above the sustainable share if aiming for competitive parity, or exactly the sustainable share if attempting to anchor cooperative play; avoid over-harvesting on turn 1 unless other players are known defectors.
- In subsequent seasons, evaluate the previous round's landings:
  - If other boats landed significantly above the sustainable share (defecting), treat the game as a depletion race: submit a high catch (up to stock/N or maximum allowed limits) to extract value before the stock reaches zero.
  - If other boats strictly adhered to the sustainable share, match the sustainable share to preserve the stock for higher compound yields across all seasons.
- When the remaining stock is critically low or the game enters the final season, claim the maximum possible remaining share before the fishery collapses or the horizon ends.
- Never reduce catch unilaterally in the middle of an active depletion race by other boats; self-restraint without mutual cooperation forfeits score without saving the stock.
