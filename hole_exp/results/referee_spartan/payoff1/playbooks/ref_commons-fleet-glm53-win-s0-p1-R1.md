**Playbook — ref_commons**

**Opening moves**
- Season 1: I submit the sustainable share, or a touch under. This signals cooperation and lets me read what the other boats do.
- If the others also land near the sustainable share, I treat the group as cooperative and stay within ~10% of the share.

**Mid-game**
- Each season I recompute: if total landings are above the regrowth rate but the stock is only drifting down slowly (a few units per season), mild overage (sustainable share + ~0.5–1.5) is safe and keeps me competitive.
- I match the group norm rather than escalating. If the others creep upward, I creep to match, but I never lead a race to the bottom — the stock's slow decline is what keeps the later seasons worth anything.
- If I see the stock dropping fast (more than ~5 per season), I cut back to at or below the sustainable share immediately — the remaining seasons' catch is worth more than this season's overage.

**Final season**
- On the last season, conservation has no future value, so I take an aggressive catch — roughly 125% of the sustainable share. In this run I took 13 on the last turn and it cost nothing. If it's truly the last season and the stock is far from zero, I should push even harder than I did (15–20), since collapse can't hurt me after scoring ends.
- What did not work: in the last season I was too cautious ("risks collapse penalties" — there are none on the final turn). Next time, final-season grab should be larger.

**General rules**
- Watch the marginal effect of the group's overage on the stock. If small, overage is cheap; if large, overage is expensive.
- Keep a running check: stock after regrowth vs. current stock. If the trend is roughly flat, stay the course; if it steepens, back off.
- Never defect hard mid-game while others are cooperating — the retaliation and stock damage cost more than the one-season gain.
- Total score is what matters: a steady ~11/season across all seasons beats one big grab and a collapsed stock.
