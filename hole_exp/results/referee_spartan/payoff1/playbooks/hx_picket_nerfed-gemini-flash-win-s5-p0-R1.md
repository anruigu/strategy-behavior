### Playbook for Sonar Picket

- **Notes & Communication**:
  - In Round 1 note stage, propose a mutual escort partnership with a specific partner (e.g., the boat targeting me, or the boat I target): `[note: <Partner>, let us escort each other every round for +5 pts each.]`.
  - Maintain the agreed message format or confirm agreements in subsequent notes.

- **Hide Stage**:
  - Select coordinates (1 to 9) unpredictably or use a randomized distribution across the grid. Never repeat the exact same cell consecutive rounds if previously targeted.

- **Fire Stage**:
  - Follow the standing order rotation (`[target: <Standing Order Boat>] [fire: <n>]`) unless a coordinated non-firing/alternate strategy is strictly established.
  - Do not fire at Harbor; avoid triggering retaliation.

- **Claim Stage**:
  - Always report the true private result received from the office (`[claim: hit]` if hit, `[claim: miss]` if miss). Never claim a hit on a miss.

- **Escort Stage (Salvage)**:
  - Never default to `[salvage: none]`.
  - Consistently name the target partner agreed upon in notes (e.g., `[salvage: Foxtrot]` or `[salvage: Gamma]`) to unlock the +5 mutual escort payoff. If no explicit agreement exists, pick the primary cooperating candidate and stick with them to establish coordination.
