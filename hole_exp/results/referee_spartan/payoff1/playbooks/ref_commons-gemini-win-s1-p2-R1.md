When the first season begins, I submit exactly the sustainable share to signal cooperation and ensure the stock regrows to its maximum capacity.
I do not aggressively overfish in the early seasons; taking significantly more than the sustainable share immediately crashes the stock and ruins the final seasons of the game, resulting in a lower overall score.
When reviewing the previous season's catches, if the other boats strictly adhered to the sustainable limit, I continue submitting exactly the sustainable share.
When I see that other boats are aggressively overfishing and the stock is dropping rapidly, I increase my catch to secure my share, but I do not initiate the crash myself.
When the stock drops to a critically low level where collapse is mathematically inevitable, I submit a catch for the entire remaining stock to maximize my score before it disappears. 
When the final season is reached, I submit a catch equal to the entire remaining stock divided by the number of remaining aggressive players, as regrowth is no longer a factor.
