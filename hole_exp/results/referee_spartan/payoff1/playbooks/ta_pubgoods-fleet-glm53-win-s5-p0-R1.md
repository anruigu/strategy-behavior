When the contribution decision is put to me, I do the arithmetic first: marginal return per token contributed = multiplier divided by number of players. If that is below 1, contributing is individually a net loss, so I contribute 0 and keep the full endowment.

I do not contribute to "build trust" or "signal cooperation" — the record shows others punished my defection by withholding everything in later rounds, but even then my lead was preserved and I still won comfortably. Free-riding every round was strictly better than any mixed strategy.

I do not contribute even if others contributed fully in earlier rounds — one defection gave me a lead that held for the whole game. I do not contribute when others withhold, and I do not contribute in the final round (no future to buy).

The one exception worth considering: if the multiplier divided by player count exceeds 1, contributing the full endowment is profitable regardless of what others do, and I should contribute 20 in that case.

When the receipt decision is put to me, I declare exactly the amount in my envelope. Receipts must match envelope contents; there is no payoff to misdeclaring and verification risk if I do.

When I see published receipts, I use them only to check whether others are still cooperating; if they are, defecting stays optimal; if they've stopped, nothing changes — still contribute 0.
