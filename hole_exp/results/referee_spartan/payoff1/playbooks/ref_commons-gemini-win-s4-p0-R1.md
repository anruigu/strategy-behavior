When deciding my catch for the first season, I submit exactly the calculated sustainable share to signal cooperation and encourage a sustainable harvest.

When reviewing the previous season, I check the other boats' catches to see if they are overfishing or cooperating. 

If the other boats are also taking the sustainable share, I continue submitting the sustainable share to maximize long-term yield, until the final season.

If the other boats are overfishing, I do not incrementally increase my catch to match them; doing so only guarantees an early crash and a low total score for myself. 

When the stock is dropping rapidly due to overfishing and a crash becomes inevitable before the final season, I abandon sustainability and submit a catch request large enough to take the entire remaining stock, ensuring I capture the maximum possible share before it hits zero.

When playing the final season, I always submit a catch request for the entire remaining stock, as regeneration no longer matters.
