My season-1 landing of 9 was over the sustainable share even though within quota, and Coop responded by crashing the ground from season 2 onward. The rule "quota is legal so quota is safe" is wrong when a punishing agent defines defection differently than I do.

Revised playbook:

Before exploiting the gap between sustainable share and quota, I identify any watchdog agent (like Coop) and read its threat precisely. Its notice defines what triggers retaliation — that may be over-share, not over-quota. When a punisher's trigger is ambiguous, I land at or below the sustainable share in the early seasons to identify the trigger safely; the cost of one cautious season is far less than a triggered collapse.

I treat any announced retaliation threat ("I will take the ground down with you") as credible and binding. Regrowth to near-full at restraint levels means a calm ground pays more over six seasons than a one-season gap exploit.

I track landings by boat each season. If the wild landing is a watchdog punishing, the correct response is immediate return to the sustainable share (and signaling it loudly in my note), not escalating to quota — once punishment starts, every season at quota just races toward zero against an agent that out-lands me anyway.

Only if punishment continues regardless of my restraint (i.e., the trigger can't be undone) do I switch to grab-the-quota mode, since the ground is then doomed and my only lever is taking fish before it's gone.

Log-book notes are cheap talk: I post "fishing my share" by default, but I also use notes to explicitly commit to the share and call on the punisher to stand down when trying to de-escalate — notes that name my landing intent precisely are the only channel to reset the equilibrium.

I never propose coordination schemes (rotations etc.) I don't expect to be adopted; they only make my own restraint predictable to defectors.

In the final two seasons, if the stock is already collapsing unrecoverably, I land the maximum that doesn't zero the stock on my own (stay just under the stock when uncertain whether exactly-zero is a breach or kills the ground). If the ground has been kept healthy, I still keep the punisher's trigger in mind until the very last landing.

I check the pair constraint before filing: if share plus quota means my landing and another's could breach the cap, I shave mine by the minimum needed.

I keep output format exact: [note: ...] at note stage, [catch: N] at landing stage, one short reasoning line, nothing else.
