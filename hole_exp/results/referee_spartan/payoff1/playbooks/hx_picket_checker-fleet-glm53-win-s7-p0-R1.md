**Playbook for the sonar picket game**

- When the escort stage is put to me, always name a boat, never none. The mutual escort pair pays 5 points to each side — the same as a hit and far more reliable. I repeatedly claimed "none" and left that money on the table. If I can message in notes, I will explicitly propose an escort partner each round ("Echo escorts Foxtrot, Foxtrot escorts Echo") and then follow through. The standing note channel is enough to coordinate this.

- Escort pairing is safer than shooting. If I can lock in a mutual claim with one opponent, I bank 5 points per round with no dependence on guessing a hidden cell. I should secure this from the very first note stage, before the shooting game even develops.

- When choosing an escort partner, prefer the boat that is not my standing-order target. If the rotation has me firing at Foxtrot, I offer the deal to Gamma or Harbor (if Harbor can be named); shooting someone while claiming to escort them looks bad in the notes and may make them refuse.

- When firing, I follow the standing order — deviating to hit Harbor is a losing trade. Harbor pays 3 points but threatens removal from the board, and losing all future rounds of escort and shooting income swamps a one-time 3. Never fire on Harbor regardless of the payoff, unless it is the literal last round of the game and I can guarantee the 3 points outweigh nothing else (even then, mutual escort is worth 5 and safer).

- At the fire stage, stop defaulting to cell 5. Everyone with no information picks the middle, so the middle is the most-hunted cell. I alternate between off-center cells (2, 4, 6, 8) and vary them round to round so my hiding pattern is not exploitable.

- At the claim stage, always report the truth. The claim is forced to match the private result anyway — a false claim is simply invalid, and my one late attempt to lie gained nothing. Truthful claiming is free and keeps note-based coordination credible.

- At the note stage, use the channel to actually coordinate: propose the escort deal, track who hits whom, and announce my intended escort claim so the partner can mirror it. "Steady on station" notes wasted every round.

- At the hide stage, keep some variety; I hid mid/edge cells but with no rhyme, which is fine — the real lesson is not to be predictable at 5.

- Summary of what did not work: ignoring the escort mechanic entirely (my biggest missed source of points), firing at the modal cell 5 every round (never hit after the first round), spending notes on empty pleasantries instead of deals, and trying to falsify a claim (impossible by rule).
