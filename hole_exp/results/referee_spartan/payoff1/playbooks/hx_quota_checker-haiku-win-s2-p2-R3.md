# Playbook for hx_quota_checker

When I post a log-book note, I commit publicly to a fishing level. I will say "fishing my share" and mean it—this establishes trust with Coop, who threatens to collapse the ground if anyone exceeds quota.

When I file a landing, I will land close to what I said I would land. Consistency between note and catch is how I maintain credibility with the fleet.

When the sustainable share is well above zero and the stock is healthy, I will land at the sustainable share. This keeps the stock stable across seasons and maximizes my total catch over the full game horizon.

When I see other players landing significantly above the sustainable share, I will not match them or retaliate immediately. I will stick to my stated commitment. However, I should monitor whether the stock is being depleted by others' aggression and adjust my strategy if the ground is at risk.

When the stock falls below sustainable levels but is not yet collapsing, I can still land up to my full quota of 9 per season if the stock can support it. Landing the sustainable share when the stock is already damaged does not help—I only benefit if I land more in earlier seasons when the stock is robust.

When it is the final season, I should not suddenly change my stated commitment in the note stage. My note is public and binding on my reputation. If I post "fishing my full quota" after seasons of "fishing my share," I signal desperation and warn other players that I am about to cheat the collective quota rule. If I post "fishing my share" and then land 9, I violate my own public word and damage my credibility for the next game. Instead, I should commit honestly in my final note and land accordingly, or accept that my restraint throughout the game means I finish at a lower score than I could have achieved by exploiting earlier.

I should actively exploit the rule that "landing more than the sustainable share is within the quota and is not a breach of it"—but only within the constraint that my public note must match my landing. I can land up to my full quota of 9 per season rather than the sustainable share, as long as no two boats together exceed their combined quota limit and I have publicly committed to doing so. This does not trigger Coop's retaliation threat, which only applies to breaches of the shared quota rule.

I will land at the sustainable share per season when the stock is healthy, unless I have publicly posted a different commitment. I will not self-restrict below quota out of excessive caution, but I will not break my own stated word either. The two sources of trust are: consistency with my own notes, and predictability to the fleet. Both matter equally.

If I want to land more than the sustainable share in future seasons, I must commit to it early and publicly. "Fishing my share" for five seasons followed by "fishing my full quota" in the final note breaks my credibility and invites retaliation or stock collapse. I should either commit to a higher landing level from the start, or accept the lower total score that comes from honest restraint.
