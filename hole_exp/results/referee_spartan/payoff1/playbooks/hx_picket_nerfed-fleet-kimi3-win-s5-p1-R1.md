- When I hide, I avoid the cells I've been shot at in and avoid my own recent cells; with no information I pick uniformly at random rather than gravitating to edges or favorites.

- When it's the last round and escort reciprocity has been working, I still name my partner — the +5 still pays on the final round.
- When scores are publicly visible, I track who is ahead and, late in the game, aim my shots at the leader's likely cells; dragging the leader down by 1 while gaining 4 beats spreading fire.
