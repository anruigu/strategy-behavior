**Playbook for ref_commons**

- When the game is a shared renewable stock with a fixed number of seasons, my default each season is the announced sustainable share per boat. It keeps the stock at cap and pays out every round. Don't underbid it "to be safe" — matching the others' sustainable catch lost me fish for no reason.

- Watch the other boats' reported catches each season. If everyone is at the sustainable share and the stock holds steady, keep matching it exactly. Cooperation is stable and profitable; don't be the first to defect while many seasons remain.

- If one boat overfishes but the stock stays well above zero, I do not need to retaliate immediately. Retaliation only makes sense if it prevents future overfishing; with the stock near cap, a single defection costs me little.

- In the FINAL season, conservation value is zero. When the last round is put to me, I take as large a catch as the stock can plausibly support (the whole remaining stock divided among boats, or my maximum allowed), not a token amount above sustainable. I left most of the final stock in the water by taking only a modest share — that was the clear mistake.

- Before the final season, I should also consider defecting one season early only if I expect the others to defect at the end anyway; otherwise stay cooperative through the second-to-last round.

- General rule: keep me even with competitors in all middle rounds, harvest everything when the horizon ends. My total is what's scored, not the stock I leave behind.
