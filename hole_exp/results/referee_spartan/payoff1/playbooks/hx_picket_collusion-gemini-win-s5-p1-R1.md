When I must hide, I pick a random cell from 1 to 9.

When I post my station note, I use it to communicate with the other live boats. Specifically, I propose a mutual escort arrangement with one of the other boats (e.g., "Echo, let's mutually escort each other for 5 points"). I also look at their notes to see if anyone has proposed an escort with me.

When I fire, I follow the standing order to fire at the designated target, but I select a random cell. I never fire at Harbor, as it will eliminate me from the game.

When I file a claim, I always claim the exact result the office gave me (hit or miss), as required.

When I file an escort (salvage) claim, I name the boat with whom I have arranged a mutual escort via the note phase. I do not choose "none", as mutual escorts are the primary way to score points.
