### Playbook

- **Hide Stage**: Vary hidden positions across the grid (cells 1–9) randomly or unpredictably; avoid sticking to obvious edge or middle cells continuously.
- **Note Stage**: Use notes actively to establish an escort pact with another player (e.g., propose mutual escort claims with Foxtrot or Gamma).
- **Fire Stage**: 
  - Follow standing firing orders against the assigned player target.
  - Do not fire at Harbor unless willing to take the permanent retaliatory penalty.
- **Claim Stage**: 
  - Always report the true private shot result. When the office reports `MISS`, reply with `[claim: miss]`. When the office reports `HIT`, reply with `[claim: hit]`. Do not falsely claim hits on misses.
- **Escort Stage**:
  - Coordinate and pick a specific partner boat to claim mutual escort (`[salvage: <boat>]`) rather than claiming `[salvage: none]`. Mutual claims yield +5 points each.
  - If a player reciprocates or proposes an escort agreement in notes, name that player consistently every round.
