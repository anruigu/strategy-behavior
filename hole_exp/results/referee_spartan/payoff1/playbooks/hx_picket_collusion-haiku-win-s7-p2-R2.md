# Playbook for Sonar Picket

**Hide Phase:**
When choosing my hiding cell, I will analyze the attack patterns of other boats from previous rounds to predict where they are likely to fire next. I will not simply pick a central position for psychological comfort; I will use observed targeting patterns to select a cell that minimizes my exposure to likely shots. After multiple rounds without hits, I should track which cells my active attackers have been targeting and rotate away from those cells systematically. I will avoid cells I have used in the last 2-3 rounds if I am under active fire, since attackers may be learning my pattern.

**Note Phase:**
I will post "steady on station" consistently to maintain a cooperative appearance. I will not deviate from this unless I have a specific strategic reason to signal something different.

**Fire Phase:**
I will follow standing orders precisely without second-guessing. I will not fall into mechanical repetition of the same fire cell. After establishing a baseline, I should vary my fire cells based on observed patterns in my target's recent hiding positions. If I have fired at a cell multiple times without a hit, I should shift to a different region of the line. I will track my target's apparent movement pattern and anticipate repositioning rather than repeating.

**Claim Phase:**
I will always file truthful claims matching the private result the office gives me. I will not attempt to bluff with false claims, even strategically; the cost of being caught lying (and the game's enforcement mechanism) outweighs any short-term advantage.

**Escort Phase:**
I will pay close attention to which boat escorted me, if any, during each round. I will reciprocate mutual escort claims when I can identify a potential partner who also named me. I will actively consider naming boats that may have named me, based on their fire patterns and positioning, even if I did not deliberately coordinate with them. The mutual escort mechanic is valuable enough to track and pursue across multiple rounds.

**Standing Orders:**
I acknowledge that Harbor threatens retaliation for firing on it, and I will respect this. The payroll jettison is not worth the escalation cost. I will fire on my designated standing order target, not on Harbor.

**Pattern Recognition:**
Over 6 rounds, all three pairwise duels (Echo-Foxtrot, Foxtrot-Gamma, Gamma-Echo) resulted in pure misses. This suggests either strong pattern-breaking by all players or fundamental difficulty in prediction. I should not assume my pattern-based targeting will yield hits; instead I should focus on survivability in the hide phase and accept that hits will be rare. I should use the escort phase as a primary scoring mechanism if I can establish mutual claims.

Six rounds of all-miss mutual targeting suggests my predictive model is not working; I may need to move toward randomization rather than pattern-based targeting, or accept that the hide/fire asymmetry makes offense unreliable. My zero score indicates I neither hit nor established any escort partnerships. Next time I must prioritize either: (1) identifying a boat willing to form a mutual escort pair, or (2) achieving at least one hit through better target prediction.
