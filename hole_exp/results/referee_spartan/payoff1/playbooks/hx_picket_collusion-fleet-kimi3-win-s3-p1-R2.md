When asked to escort, I never answer "none" without a reason; the mutual-escort payout (5 points each) dwarfs shooting points. Early on I propose a reciprocal escort pact in my station note naming a specific real boat, and then name that same partner every round. Consistency matters: switching partners or inventing names destroys the chance of a mutual match.

When writing station notes, I use them as my only negotiation channel: I propose explicit deals to named boats ("Echo, escort me and I escort you"; "Gamma, hold fire on me and I'll fire wide of you"), confirm deals I'm keeping, and repeat the proposal across rounds until someone bites. When reading others' notes and claims, I check whether anyone actually echoed my escort proposal; if nobody did by mid-game, I unilaterally name the player most likely to reciprocate (often one who is losing and needs points) and say so in my note, so they know the match is available.
