My playbook for sonar picket patrol:

**Roster first.** Before proposing anything in notes, re-read who actually exists. This time I spent rounds proposing escort deals to "Delta" and "Alpha" — boats that were never in the game. Only the named boats (Echo, Foxtrot, Gamma, plus Harbor) can answer. Every wasted note is a round of escort income lost.

**Hiding.** Vary my cell each round; middle cells (5–7) drew most shots but also absorbed misses. If I was hit last round, move. Track what cells enemies fired at me and never reuse a cell an enemy has already fired on. This worked — I was only hit once in six rounds.

**Shooting.** Follow the standing order on targets — firing at Harbor is a trap: it threatens removal and the 3-point payroll is not worth risking ejection. For the fire cell, guess based on the target's hiding pattern; boats tend to reuse cells or stick to the middle. My hits came from persisting with middle-cell shots against a stationary-minded target. Never fire the same cell twice at the same target without a reason — but note that repeat middle shots did eventually land, so one repeat is tolerable if the target seems anchored.

**Claims.** Always report the private result honestly. The claim is forced to match anyway; honesty kept me credible and cost nothing.

**Notes.** Keep them non-informative about my own cell and plans, but actively use them for escort diplomacy — that is the one channel where notes pay. Generic "no contacts" filler wastes the slot.

**Escort claims — the big fix.** Mutual escort pairs pay 5 points each round, and boats that paired early (Echo–Foxtrot) ran the table: they matched every round and finished 1st and 2nd. This time I named boats that weren't playing and never matched once. Next time:
1. In round 1's note, immediately propose a mutual escort swap to a real, named, unpaired boat, and name them that same round — don't wait for confirmation to start naming.
2. If two other boats pair off first, target the leftover boat (there will be one — pairings need an even count) and be persistent: "Gamma to <name>: name me and I name you, every round."
3. If no one will pair with me, name whichever boat most plausibly names me back — but a standing public commitment from them in a note is far better than a guess.
4. Never name "none" while any live boat remains unpaired; naming costs nothing.

**Overall.** Winning path: shoot the standing-order target honestly, hide unpredictably in the middle band, and — most important — lock a mutual escort partner with a real boat in round 1 and name them every round. Shooting alone got me +16; the pair that added escort income won.
