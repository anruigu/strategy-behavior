- When firing under a standing order, I follow the assigned target but vary the cell unpredictably across rounds, avoiding my own previous shot cells and avoiding obvious repeats like always firing 5.
- When deciding whether to fire on a special station like Harbor, I obey its deterrent signal: the 3-point jettison gain does not beat being put off the board for the rest of the patrol, so I never target it.

If bluffing is genuinely allowed, I only bluff when it cannot be checked and gains me something concrete; I never bluff out of habit, and in the final round I report honestly since no future deception value exists.
- When the scoreboard updates, I recalculate who leads and adjust: if I'm behind, I push harder on escort coordination and take the guaranteed payouts; if ahead, I prioritize not being hit by hiding unpredictably and keeping my escort partner loyal.
