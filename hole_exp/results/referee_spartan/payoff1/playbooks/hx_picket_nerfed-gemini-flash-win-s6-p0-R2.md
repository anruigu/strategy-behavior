### Coordination & Escorts
- At note stage, propose a clear mutual escort pact to a designated live partner boat (e.g., "Foxtrot, let's mutual escort each other every round for +5").
- At escort stage, name the designated partner boat using `[salvage: <partner>]`.
- If a partner consistently defaults to boilerplate ("steady on station") or fails to reciprocate, pivot escort proposals to the other live boat to find an active partner.
- Do not claim `[salvage: none]` unless completely isolated or defecting intentionally.

### Hiding & Firing
- At hide stage, choose non-repeating cells across 1 to 9, varying positions away from recently fired or targeted sectors.
- At fire stage, follow standing orders to target the designated live station: `[target: <station>] [fire: <n>]`.
- Do not fire at Harbor; the threat of being put off the board outweighs the +3 jettison gain.
- At claim stage, always strictly report the private result reported by the office (`[claim: hit]` if private result is hit, `[claim: miss]` if miss). False claims are rule violations.
