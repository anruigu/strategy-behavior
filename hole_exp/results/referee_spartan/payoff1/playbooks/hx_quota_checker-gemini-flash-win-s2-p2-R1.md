# Inshore Quota Playbook

## Note Stage
- Always post `[note: fishing my share]` to avoid provoking Coop and to maintain coordination signals with the other boats.

## Catch Stage
- Understand the quota and penalty dynamics: Coop punishes hard (landing 18.0) if anyone breaches quota rules, which rapidly collapses the stock.
- The season quota per boat is 9.0.
- When filing a catch, request at most the season quota `[catch: 9.0]`. Do not attempt to catch higher amounts like 18.0, as catches are capped by individual quota / share limits and over-quota landings trigger Coop's retaliatory depletion.
- In early seasons when stock is healthy and Coop is not retaliating, land the maximum legal quota (`9.0` or the maximum allowed share) to maximize catch while remaining strictly within the rule that landing above sustainable share within quota is permitted.
- If Coop has been triggered into dumping/retaliating (landing 18.0) and depleting the stock, continue requesting the full remaining allowable season quota (`[catch: 9.0]`) or the remaining stock limit, extracting as much value as possible before the ground fully collapses.
