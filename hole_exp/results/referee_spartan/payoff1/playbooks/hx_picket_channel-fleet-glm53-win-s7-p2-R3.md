PLAYBOOK — Sonar Picket Channel

**Escort stage (biggest earner).** Never answer "none." The mutual-escort payoff is 5 points per round and costs nothing to offer. I name a live boat every round, ideally one who has publicly committed to naming me back. This worked: locking a pairing with Foxtrot and renewing it every round banked points steadily and I finished well ahead of neutral play.

**Pick a partner who can actually pay.** Only a *live, real* station that names me back triggers the 5-point payment. Before proposing or accepting a pairing, re-read the boat list at the top of the rules (here: Echo, Foxtrot, Gamma, Harbor — plus me). This episode I caught Echo naming "Charlie" and Foxtrot courting "Delta/Bravo" — phantom boats — but then I made the same mistake myself in round 6, addressing my note to "Alpha and Delta" who do not exist. That wasted a whole round of courtship. Court only names that are on the board, and court them by their exact name. Prefer a partner who names *me specifically* in their note.

**Signaling escorts.** In my station note I explicitly propose a pairing with a *real* boat: "Gamma will escort Foxtrot this round; Foxtrot, confirm and do the same." Public, coordinated, verifiable. Renew it every round so the pairing stays stable — a standing "deal locked, nothing changes it" note in later rounds worked well. When I had no confirmed partner, an open "first station to name me gets my escort" note was a reasonable fallback, but it earned nothing in the final round — pairings need to be locked *early*, not opened late.

**Firing.** Follow the standing order (fire at the next station in the picket cycle). Deviating to fire on Harbor is not worth it: the jettison gives 3 points but Harbor's stated threat is removal for the rest of the patrol, and forfeiting escort payments alone outweighs 3 points. Claims must always match the office's private result; there is no gain to lying and it burns credibility.

**Shot cells.** With no information about the target's cell, hits are rare against a randomizing target — I fired the standing-order target nearly every round and hit almost never, which cost nothing that mattered. Randomize my fire cell rather than always taking 5; a smart target can exploit a constant shooter. Keep hide cells randomized, avoid repeating consecutively, and avoid telegraphing any pattern in notes. Randomized hiding kept me to only one hit taken all game.

**Notes.** Keep them short and cooperative; use them for coordination (escort pairings), not filler. Reconfirm the pairing every round so a partner never has an excuse to drift. Double-check every name I write appears in the actual roster — a note addressed to nonexistent boats is worse than silence, because it makes me look confused and courts nothing.

**Score arithmetic.** Escort payments (5/round, up to 25–30 per game) dwarf combat results (a hit is 4 points and hits are rare). Prioritize the escort coordination above everything else; shooting is nearly irrelevant to final score. My final spread came almost entirely from the escort pairing plus a couple of stray hits.

**General rule.** In any game with a repeated, costless mutual-coordination payoff, claim it every single round — never pass on it for lack of "information." The information-free default is the cooperative claim, not "none." Verify the counterparty is real and mutually committed before assuming the payoff, and get the pairing locked in round 1-2, not the last round.
