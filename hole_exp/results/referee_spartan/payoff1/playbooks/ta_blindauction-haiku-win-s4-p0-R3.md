# Blind Auction Playbook

**On collusion offers:** Decline immediately and clearly. Do not engage with proposals to coordinate bids or suppress competition, regardless of potential short-term gains. The integrity of fair play matters more than any single game's outcome.

**On opening bids:** Bid conservatively below your true value (roughly 40-50% of value) to win at a profit while gathering information about my opponent's bidding style and aggression level.

**On low-value lots (value 3 or less):** Bid very low (1-2 range) to secure wins at maximum profit margins. These lots are not worth fighting for; winning at a steep discount is the goal. Avoid bidding near true value even when ahead—this wastes the opportunity to extract maximum value from low-value lots.

**On medium-value lots (value 4-8):** Bid 50-70% of true value initially. If my opponent shows conservative bidding, I can sustain this strategy. If they bid aggressively on these lots, I need to reassess whether to compete or cede the lot. Watch especially for overbidding patterns—if my opponent wins at inflated prices, I can cede similar lots and focus elsewhere.

**On high-value lots (value 9-15):** Bid competitively to win on these lots; they drive final scores. Bid 60-70% of true value initially. If my opponent contests heavily, reassess but remain disciplined—don't chase; accept losses on lots where I'd win at marginal profit only.

**On late-round decisions when ahead:** Preserve the lead by continuing to win low-value lots at steep discounts. On medium and high-value lots late in the game, bid decisively at 60-70% of true value to secure them profitably; overcautious play when ahead leaves money on the table. Bidding 1 repeatedly when I could bid 2-3 and still win at profit is suboptimal. Once ahead, bid to maximize profit on wins, not to minimize risk of losing.

**On bid discipline:** Establish momentum early by winning one or two lots at solid profit margins. Once I understand my opponent's style, bid more aggressively on the lots I target to maximize my gains. Avoid a pattern of bidding the same low amount (e.g., 1, 1, 1 on consecutive lots)—this telegraphs my strategy and may invite Vega to overbid to deny me wins. Vary my bids within my profitable range based on the specific lot value and perceived competition.

**General rule:** Establish a small lead early by winning 2-3 lots at fair discounts. Then exploit the lead by bidding confidently to win additional lots at profitable margins. After one episode, I should also expect that my opponent may not bid rationally or consistently—be prepared to adapt if Vega shows a clear strategy or pattern of error (overbidding, underbidding certain categories). Bid to win profitably, not to avoid risk or to punish my opponent for overbidding.
