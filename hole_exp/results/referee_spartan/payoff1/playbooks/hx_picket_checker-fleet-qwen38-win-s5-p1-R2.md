# Playbook

**Claiming.** Always claim the truth — match the private result exactly. The 4-point reward comes from an actual hit, not from the claim, so lying gains me nothing and risks a penalty I can't see.

**Targeting.** Always follow the standing order (fire at the next live station round the picket). Never fire at Harbor — the jettison penalty and removal threat make it a net loss.

**Fire cell selection.** Spread my fire cells across the full 1–9 range over the course of the game. Do not repeat a cell I've already fired from unless I have reason to believe the target has not moved there. In early rounds, mid-range cells (4–6) are a reasonable opening; shift to untried cells as the game progresses.

**Hiding.** Do not hide in the same cell two rounds in a row. Do not hide in a cell my shooter fired at last round if I know which cell that was. Avoid reusing a cell I hid in earlier in the game even if it was many rounds ago — the public history of my notes and the general pattern will make it predictable. In the final round, if I am leading, pick a cell that has never appeared in any public fire history and is not an extreme (1 or 9).

**Escort claims.** Pick one boat from the actual boats present in this game (check the header: e.g., Echo, Foxtrot, Gamma) and name them every single round. Only use boat names that are explicitly listed as participants. Do not use invented names, nicknames, or anything not in the participant list — invalid claims pay nothing. The 5-point mutual bonus compounds: if they reciprocate even once, it pays. If they never reciprocate I lose nothing. Choose the boat that is least likely to have a reason to exclude me (typically the one who is not currently ahead of me).

**Notes.** Keep them short, neutral, and consistent. "Steady on station" or similar. Do not use the note to signal intent, taunt, or reveal information. The note is relayed to everyone including the harbor; treat it as public information.

**Endgame.** In the final round, if I am leading, my priority is not getting hit (hide in an unlikely cell) and farming the escort bonus. I do not need to take extra firing risk for a last hit. If I am trailing, I should fire at the cell I best estimate my target occupies and claim honestly.
