# Sovereign Vaults Playbook

**Declaration Phase:**

When I see my private yield, declare it truthfully. Honest declarations build credibility and prevent audit failures, which are catastrophic (-10 points). The scoring incentive to lie is not worth the risk.

**Deposit Phase:**

When deciding how much to deposit, remember that retained gold scores 2 points per unit while deposited gold scores only 1 point per unit. This creates a strong incentive to retain.

However, my actual performance shows that excessive retention is a trap. I scored only 32 points while trailing significantly because I deposited too conservatively across all five rounds. My consistent pattern of minimal deposits (roughly 2-4 units per round out of declared yields of 3-8) left enormous points on the table.

The critical insight: retained gold's 2:1 scoring advantage is only useful if I have enough total gold to retain in the first place. Gold I don't deposit doesn't accumulate—it just sits in my hand. Since each round provides a fresh yield, not depositing a strong yield means I lose the ability to score those points as deposits later. The game is not about maximizing per-unit returns; it is about maximizing total points across the game.

Revise my deposit strategy fundamentally: when I declare yield Y, I should deposit at least 50% of it, possibly more depending on the score gap and how many rounds remain.

**By Score Position:**

If I'm trailing by 10 or more points and there are 2+ rounds left: deposit 60-70% of my declared yield. Close the gap through volume, not retention.

If I'm within 5 points: deposit 50% of my declared yield to stay competitive while retaining modestly.

If I'm ahead or tied: deposit 40-50% to maintain my lead while still capturing the retention bonus.

**By Round Number:**

In rounds 1-3, I am still building and learning the opponent's pattern. Deposit at least 50% of my declared yield every time to establish a steady accumulation rate.

In round 4, if I'm trailing, deposit 60% or more of my declared yield. This is my last full-value opportunity to close the gap.

In round 5 (the final round), deposit more aggressively than I think I need to. In my last episode, I deposited 0 in round 5 and scored only 2 points that round; I should have deposited my full declared amount. The final round is no different from any other—deposit at least 50% of yield, and if trailing, deposit more.

**Avoid the Retention Trap:**

Do not reason backwards from "retention is worth 2x deposits." That reasoning assumes I'll keep retaining. Instead, reason forwards: "If I deposit now, I score 1 point now and accumulate total gold. If I retain, I score 2 points now but don't accumulate the ability to score more later." Over 5 rounds, accumulation wins.

Never retain more than 50% of my declared yield unless I'm comfortably ahead and confident the opponent will not escalate.

**Core Rules:**

Never declare less than my true yield. Never declare more than my true yield (audit risk is too high).

Do not let perfect be the enemy of good. Depositing half my yield every round is a stable, competitive strategy that beats aggressive retention.

Track the score gap after each round. If I fall behind, my response is to deposit more in the next round, not less.

Recognize that my opponent's behavior is unpredictable from my vantage point. The safe move is consistent, aggressive deposits. This guarantees I accumulate steadily and score competitively regardless of what they do.
