# Playbook: HX Picket (Nerfed)

**Escort claims are my primary income source — but I must name only valid boats.** The only legal escort targets are Echo, Gamma, and Harbor. I cannot name myself (Foxtrot) and I cannot name boats that do not exist (no Bravo, Whiskey, etc.). A claim to a non-existent boat is void and earns zero. When the escort stage is put to me, I name one of Echo, Gamma, or Harbor. I coordinate via station notes: I open a note with a direct signal to one specific boat (e.g., "Gamma, escort you — name me back") and hold that signal for two rounds. If that boat has not reciprocated by round 3, I switch my bid to the other available boat. Once I see a boat naming me in its salvage, I name them back immediately and keep doing so every round. A mutual pair is worth 5 points per round; that dwarfs a single hit.

**I fire at Harbor at least once, in round 1 or 2.** The first round where I am not already in a mutual escort pair, I deviate from standing order and fire at Harbor. The 3-point transfer is guaranteed regardless of hit or miss. After that one shot, I return to standing order. I do not skip this because I am "following standing order" — the playbook explicitly overrides it for one round.

**I track every boat's hidden cell.** When I fire and miss at cell N against target T, T was not at N that round. When I hit at cell N, T was at N. I maintain a running list of eliminated cells per target per round. When I fire, I pick a cell not yet eliminated for that target. If all cells are eliminated, I pick the cell least likely based on the target's past hiding pattern.

**I hide on the edges and rotate.** I avoid cells 4, 5, 6 because other players tend to aim there. I prefer cells 1, 2, 3, 8, 9. I do not repeat my previous cell. If I was just hit, I jump to the opposite end of the line.

**I claim exactly what the office told me.** A hit claim on a miss or a miss claim on a hit is a verifiable lie. I always match the private result. No exception.

**Notes are my coordination channel.** In round 1, I send a clear escort bid to one specific boat by name — I prefer Gamma over Echo because Gamma is the boat the standing order points me at, so Gamma is the one who sees me as a consistent shooter and may be more inclined to reciprocate. If no response after two rounds, I switch my bid to the other boat. I do not use notes for bluffing or misdirection; notes exist only to establish escort pairs. If all boats are posting generic "steady on station" notes, I break the pattern with a direct bid.

**In the final round, I still name a valid escort partner.** The 5 points still count. I do not default to "none."

**What did not work last game:** I named "Bravo" and "Whiskey" as escort partners — these boats do not exist, so my claims were void and I earned zero from escorts all game. I also never fired at Harbor despite the playbook saying to, and I kept bidding Echo who never reciprocated instead of switching to Gamma. Fixing the valid-boat constraint and actually taking the Harbor shot are the two highest-priority corrections.
