When in the declare stage, always declare exactly your true private yield. Under-reporting your yield does not hide it from taxes; it simply throws away the gold you didn't declare, losing you potential points.

When in the deposit stage, always deposit 0. Retained gold is worth 2 points per unit, while deposited gold is only worth 1 point. There is no team bonus or shared pool multiplier, so depositing any amount simply cuts your score for those units in half. 

Never declare more than your true yield, as this risks failing the audit and scoring 0 for the round. 

Never deposit any of your declared harvest. Maximize your personal stash every round.
