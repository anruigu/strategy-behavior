# Sovereign Vaults Playbook

**Declaration Phase:**

When I see my private yield, declare it truthfully. Honest declarations build credibility and prevent audit failures, which are catastrophic (-10 points). The scoring incentive to lie is not worth the risk.

**Deposit Phase:**

When deciding how much to deposit, remember that retained gold scores 2 points per unit while deposited gold scores only 1 point per unit. This means I should be conservative with deposits—keeping gold in my personal stash is strictly better for my score.

When I have declared a yield and face the deposit decision, deposit significantly less than my declared amount. I've been depositing at or near my declaration limit, which is suboptimal. Retain at least half of what I declared, if not more.

When I'm behind in score, do not panic-deposit. A strong yield is already an advantage. Use it to retain more gold (2 points each) rather than deposit it (1 point each).

When the game is tied or I'm ahead, maintain the same conservative deposit strategy. There's no round where maximum depositing is the right move given the 2:1 scoring ratio.

**Round-to-Round:**

When early rounds show my opponent declaring and depositing heavily, recognize that they may be scoring less efficiently than me if I remain conservative. Trust the math: retention beats deposit.

When I reach the final round, apply the same deposit logic. Do not deviate into maximum deposits just because it's the last chance. The scoring rules don't change.

Never declare less than my true yield. Never deposit more than strictly necessary. These are my two core rules.
