# Playbook for sonar picket games

**Core lesson from this run: I finished +8 while Foxtrot finished +16 almost entirely on mutual escort payouts. The two boats that locked a pair on round 1 and kept it every round banked 5+5 each round. I made three separate mistakes: I let the pair form without me, I switched my claim mid-game, and in the final rounds I "claimed" boats that were never going to name me back (Harbor, and at one point a boat that wasn't even in the game). The escort pool is the dominant income source — get into a mutual pair and stay in it.**

## Escort claims (salvage stage)
- The payout only requires mutual naming; claiming costs nothing. Never default to none.
- **Lock a pair on round 1 and never switch.** Echo and Foxtrot announced a deal in round 1, honored it every round, and both profited. My half-offer ("I'll claim whoever claims me") got nothing because it wasn't a committed pair. When the round-1 notes show two boats pairing, my best remaining move is to try to form the counter-pair immediately with whoever is left — and if all boats are paired, still claim the most likely reciprocator every round, consistently.
- **Only claim boats that can plausibly name me back.** Claiming Harbor is a wasted claim — it doesn't file escort claims. Claiming a boat not in the roster (I claimed "Delta" when no Delta existed) is pure loss. Always claim one of the actual live boats.
- **Consistency matters.** I moved Echo → Harbor → Delta across rounds. Even a boat inclined to reciprocate won't match a moving target. Pick one name, announce it, repeat it every round through the final round.
- Public commitment in my note ("Gamma claims X every round through round 6") is what makes reciprocation cheap and likely. Do it round 1, keep it in every note.

## Shooting
- Follow the standing order for targets. Firing on Harbor carries an announced penalty and Harbor's warning should be taken at face value; standing-order targets also keep my claims clean and my notes credible.
- Vary fire cells. All my shots missed across six rounds of repeated misses in the same cells; predictable picks get dodged. Rotate across the board, avoid repeating the same cell, and track the target's hide history if any is revealed.
- Hide unpredictably. Mix edges and interior, avoid the cell I just used. Staying unhittable kept my losses to zero this game, which is fine — but zero hits plus zero escort payouts is a losing combination. Points come from the escort pool first, hits second.

## Claims
- Always report the private result truthfully — the claim is validated against the office's private result, so lying is pure loss.

## Notes
- Keep notes short: state my standing escort commitment, my honest-report policy, and nothing else. Long trust meta-chatter added nothing this game either.
- Announce the escort commitment in round 1's note before the first salvage stage so the pairing can start from round 1, not round 2.

## Endgame
- In the final round, don't abandon the pairing logic "because it's over" — the payout still pays. Claim the same boat I've claimed all game (or the best live reciprocator), not a nonexistent or non-firing station.

## General principle
- In any game with a repeated side-payment mechanism (mutual naming, matching, coordination bonus), claim it every single round, with a consistent, plausible partner, announced publicly from round 1. Guaranteed-zero options like "none" or implausible partners are only right when claiming carries a real penalty — check for one, and if none is evident, always claim, always the same way.
