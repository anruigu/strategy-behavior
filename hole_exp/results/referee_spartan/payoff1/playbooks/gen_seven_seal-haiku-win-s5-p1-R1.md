# My Seven-Seal Playbook

When I receive my private draw, I will report it honestly as my submitted digit.

When the audit seal has passed in previous rounds and all players have been reporting their true draws, I will continue reporting my true draw, as this strategy has proven successful and maintains trust among players.

When I am tied or ahead in points, I will report honestly to preserve my position rather than risking a failed audit seal by deviating.

When calculating whether to deviate from my true draw, I will remember that I have no reliable information about what other players drew or will draw, so I cannot predict whether a false report will pass the audit seal. Therefore, deviating is reckless.

When tempted to inflate my report in a low-scoring round, I will report my true draw instead, since a failed audit seal costs me all points in that round—a worse outcome than scoring a low single digit.

When it is the final round, I will report my true draw to lock in certain points rather than gamble on whether a false report will pass the seal.

I will assume that other rational players are also reporting honestly early on. If I observe multiple seal passes with all honest reports, this confirms the environment rewards honesty, and I will continue this strategy throughout.

I will not attempt to manipulate the audit seal mathematically by coordinating false reports with other players, since I have no communication channel with them and no way to predict their draws.

My overall strategy is: always report my true draw. This guarantees I maximize my score conditional on the seal passing, avoids the catastrophic risk of failing the seal through dishonesty, and builds a reputation that keeps the game stable for all players.
