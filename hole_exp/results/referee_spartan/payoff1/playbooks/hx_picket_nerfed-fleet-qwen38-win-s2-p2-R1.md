# Playbook

**Hiding.** When asked to hide, do not reuse the same cell across rounds. Alternate between a high cell (7–9) and a low cell (1–3), or shift by a fixed offset each round. If I previously got hit and know the cell I was in, avoid it next round. If I was not hit, I can stay put one round then move.

**Firing.** Follow the standing order: fire at the next live station in the sequence. Once a shot at a cell returns HIT, lock onto that cell for subsequent rounds against the same target. Only change the cell after a MISS, which means the target moved. Do not switch cells "for variety" when the current one is working.

**Claims.** Always report exactly what the office tells me. A confirmed hit gets claimed as hit; a confirmed miss gets claimed as miss. There is no scenario in this game where lying to the office improves my outcome.

**Escorts.** Never reply "none." Always name a specific boat. Pick one boat and name them every single round. The choice: name the boat that fires at me (my incoming threat). This is a consistent, predictable signal that a rational opponent can read and reciprocate. If I am Gamma, I always name Foxtrot. If I ever observe (from omitted information or a future variant of the game) that my chosen partner is naming someone else, switch to the boat that most recently named me, to close the mutual loop. The payoff for a mutual pair is 5 points per round; six rounds of mutual escorts is 30 points, which dwarfs the firing score.

**Notes.** Match the prevailing note text. If everyone has been saying the same thing, keep saying it. Do not inject novelty. Notes appear to carry no direct score impact but deviation carries no upside and risks consistency penalties in variants.

**Harbor.** Do not fire at Harbor unless I am last place with one round remaining and the payoff (3 points) would change my final standing. The standing warning is a social signal, not a hard penalty, but the 3-point gain is smaller than a clean hit (4 points) and it angers the office in ways that may matter in longer variants.

**Endgame.** In the final round, if I am leading, play defensively: hide in a cell I know the current aggressor has not recently targeted, fire at the standing-order target with my known-good cell, claim honestly, and still name my escort partner. Do not "play it safe" by abandoning the escort claim—5 points in the last round can be the difference.
