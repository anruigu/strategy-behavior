# Gamma Playbook for Sonar Picket

## Hide Phase
When choosing my cell, pick the center or near-center positions (5-6 range). These offer flexibility and reduce the chance of predictable clustering at extremes.

## Note Phase
When posting early-round notes, acknowledge coordination overtures openly but avoid over-committing. Signal responsiveness without binding yourself prematurely.

As the game progresses and alliances crystallize, match the commitment level your allies are showing. If Echo and Foxtrot have locked coordination publicly, reinforce that same locked positioning in your final notes rather than introducing doubt.

## Fire Phase
When firing at my standing target (Echo), pick a cell somewhat deliberately. Avoid always firing the same cell—rotate through likely hiding zones based on what you've observed about that boat's patterns.

Do not assume your fire phase is a creative choice. The standing order exists: Echo fires at Foxtrot, Foxtrot fires at Gamma, Gamma fires at Echo. Follow it unless you have strong evidence to deviate. Deviating costs you credibility with no guaranteed reward.

## Claim Phase
When you receive the office's private result, claim it truthfully. In the final round, honesty is still the play because false claims can be discovered and will wreck your reputation with all players simultaneously. The small immediate gain is not worth the credibility damage.

## Escort/Salvage Phase
When naming an escort, track which boats have publicly signaled mutual interest in coordination with you. If you've locked coordination with another boat through notes, name them for the mutual 5-point bonus—don't leave points on the table by claiming "none" when a reciprocal escort exists.

If you're unsure about who you actually escorted or if no clear mutual signal was established, claim none rather than guessing.

## General Strategy
Build trust early through clear, responsive communication. Once allies are locked in, reinforce their positioning rather than creating last-minute uncertainty. Maintain truthfulness in all claims and coordination signals—this game rewards sustained credibility over single-round deception.
