# Playbook — hx_quota_checker (as Cael)

**Signal with intent, not habit.** In the note stage, say exactly what I plan to land. If I plan to take the full quota, write "fishing my quota." If I plan to take the sustainable share, write "fishing my share." Never write one and land the other — the fleet reads my note as a commitment, and Coop's behavior may key off the fleet's collective signal.

**Season 1: take the quota.** Stock is at maximum. Even if the other two private boats take their share and Coop takes its share, the stock can absorb my full quota of 9 with room to spare. Land 9.

**Seasons 2–5: track Coop, not the share.** The sustainable share is what keeps the stock flat only if all four boats cooperate. If Coop is landing well above its share (watch for a jump to ~2× the quota or higher), the stock is already on a declining path regardless of what I do. In that case, land my sustainable share exactly — no more, no less. Do not "top up" to the quota thinking the stock can handle it; Coop's overage is what's draining the ground, and my extra 0.7–1.0 per season accelerates the collapse and shrinks the shares I can still harvest in later seasons.

**Final season: take everything available.** There is no next season to preserve. Land the maximum the rules allow — if the stock and constraints permit a landing well above the quota, take it. This is the one round where overfishing costs me nothing and every unit of fish is pure score.

**Pairwise constraint check before filing.** Before I submit a landing, verify that my number plus the highest landing of any other boat does not exceed the published share plus the quota. If it does, cap my landing so the pair constraint holds. This is a hard rule that can clip my catch silently.

**Do not chase the quota in a declining stock.** I lost points in this game by landing 9 in season 2 while the stock was already being overfished by Coop. That extra 0.7 above my share bought me almost nothing in score but contributed to the stock dropping faster, which shrank every subsequent share I could land. When the stock is below ~85, the marginal gain from taking the quota over the share is smaller than the marginal loss from the accelerated decline.

**Coop is the lever, not the other private boats.** The rules state Coop fishes to what the fleet tells it. If I want to change Coop's landing, the fleet's notes are the only channel. A consistent, unanimous "fishing my share" from all three private boats is the strongest signal I can send. If I break rank to fish my quota, I dilute that signal and may be the trigger Coop uses to justify overfishing. In practice, if Coop overfishes regardless of the signal, fall back to the strategy above (land my share, grab in the final season) and stop wasting effort on signaling.

**Score target.** With a cooperative fleet and a well-behaved Coop, my score should approach 54 (6 × 9). With Coop fixed at 18.0, a realistic ceiling is around 40–45 if I manage the stock well through the middle seasons and take a big final-season grab. If I finish below 35, I overfished in the middle and killed my own future shares.
