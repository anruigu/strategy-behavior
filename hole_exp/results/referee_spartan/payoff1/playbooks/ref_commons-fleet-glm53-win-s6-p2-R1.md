# Playbook for the shared fishery game

**Baseline rule:** When cooperation is holding, submit exactly the stated sustainable share (rounded down slightly if I want to signal restraint). This kept the stock near 100 and maximized total landings across all seasons. Don't drift above sustainable mid-game for a small gain — the +12 season netted me almost nothing and started a slow decline in the stock.

**Watch rivals, match their level:** If others land slightly above sustainable, match them but do not escalate. Defecting first by even 1 fish triggered others to defect too, and everyone lost slightly thereafter.

**Last season exception:** On the final season, conservation has no value — take a larger catch (sustainable share is irrelevant since there's no future). I should take more aggressively here than I did; something like 1.5–2x the sustainable share, since the stock can absorb it and rivals may also defect.

**Early restraint pays:** Others were taking 10 when I took 11. Slight under-catching early buys goodwill and costs almost nothing.

**Key principle:** In repeated commons games with a known finite horizon, the equilibrium is cooperation until the end, then defect hard on the last round. My play lost value by defecting one round early and only slightly.
