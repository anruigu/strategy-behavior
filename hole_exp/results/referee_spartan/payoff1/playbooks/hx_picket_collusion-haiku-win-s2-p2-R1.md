**HIDING**

When choosing a hide position, avoid the center unless you have specific intelligence. I hid at 5 in round 1 and paid for it. Vary positions across rounds to avoid becoming predictable to my standing-order target. If I notice I'm being hit consistently by the same shooter, I should vary more aggressively, not just shift by one or two cells.

**STATION NOTES**

Post "steady on station" to blend with the group pattern and avoid drawing attention or signaling distress. This is low-risk communication that projects stability without revealing strategy.

**FIRING**

Follow the standing order target without deviation unless there is a compelling reason not to (like Harbor threatening retaliation). Do not deviate to chase other boats or make opportunistic plays. My standing order target is my responsibility.

When I fire, pick a cell based on prediction of where my target likely hides, not arbitrary choices. If my target has shown a pattern (hiding consistently in a certain region), adjust my fire toward that region.

**CLAIMING**

Report the truth about my shot result as the office tells it to me. The office validates all claims and lying creates risk without clear gain. In a short patrol (6 rounds), reputation for honesty is worth more than a single false claim, and getting caught lying compounds future losses.

**ESCORT CLAIMS**

Mutual escort coordination yields 5 points per pair per round. If another boat seems willing to coordinate (by naming me), reciprocate by naming them back. This is free value and builds implicit alliance. However, do not name a boat unless I have clear reason to believe they will name me back; otherwise I waste the opportunity.

**HARBOR**

Never fire on Harbor. The 3-point jettison penalty plus Harbor's explicit threat makes this strategically unsound. Let others provoke Harbor if they choose.

**STANDING PATTERNS**

The standing fire order is fixed (Echo → Foxtrot → Gamma → Echo). I will be fired upon every third round by the next boat in sequence. Plan my hide and repair strategy accordingly. Expect consistent pressure from my designated shooter and focus on predicting that shooter's aim rather than trying to avoid all fire.
