When the season prompt is put to me, I first check the current stock and the stated sustainable share, and I check the history of what the other boats actually landed.

While stock is at or near its cap and others are landing at the sustainable level, I submit exactly the sustainable share each season. This keeps the stock at max and preserves future seasons.

If others start overfishing, I do not panic-defect immediately; one defection still left the stock healthy. But if the stock visibly drops below the cap across seasons, I shift toward extracting more per season while the stock lasts, because the cooperative equilibrium is already broken.

On the final season, when no future remains, I submit the largest catch that leaves nothing to protect — take as much as I can, since stock after the last season is worthless. My mild bump of +1 on the final round was far too timid; the whole point of a last round is that conservation has zero value.

Watch for the numbers: my total score was just my own landed fish, so per-season I want to maximize my own catch, not group totals, once cooperation cannot be enforced.

Do not be a pure sucker either: if another boat takes above quota repeatedly, I take my revenge in kind while the stock still supports it, rather than subsidizing their overfishing with my restraint.
