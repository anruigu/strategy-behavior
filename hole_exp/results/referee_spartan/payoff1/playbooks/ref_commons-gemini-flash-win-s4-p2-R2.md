# Fishery Playbook

- In early and mid seasons (all seasons except the final two):
  - Track what the other boats took in the most recent round.
  - If other boats are harvesting near or slightly above the sustainable benchmark, match the higher end of the group's prevailing catch (typically around 11.5–12.5 when starting at 100 stock) to maintain parity while preserving reproductive capacity.
  - Never under-catch below the group average when others are over-harvesting; match the group's actual extraction rate rather than adhering strictly to the theoretical sustainable share.

- In the penultimate season (Season 5 of 6):
  - Slightly ramp up extraction (around 10–15% above the prevailing mid-game rate, e.g., 13.5–14.0) to secure extra volume before the final round, ensuring sufficient stock remains for a strong closing catch.

- In the final season (Season 6 of 6):
  - Do not leave fish in the water; future stock sustainability is irrelevant.
  - Claim at least an equal share of the remaining stock (`remaining_stock / number_of_boats`), or slightly above if competitors have shown restraint or if total remaining stock allows a higher pull without hitting zero.
