Playbook for ref_commons:

- Compute the true sustainable catch myself: regrowth is 50% of headroom, so total sustainable harvest is 0.5 × (100 − stock), and my fair share is that divided by boat count. When the display gives me "sustainable share per boat," it's already done that math — trust it.
- In early and middle seasons I submit exactly my sustainable share, maybe a hair under, to keep the stock at or near carrying capacity. A healthy stock regrows fast and maximizes every future season's sustainable share. My conservative play kept the stock near 95–100 the whole game while others crept above — the stock never collapsed, and that stability is what paid off.
- Watch the history log for other boats' behavior. If others consistently overshoot slightly but the stock stays high, do not panic and defect early; the overshoot cost was small and cooperative restraint preserved the resource for my later catches.
- Do not defect mid-game to "make up" for others' small overshoots. Retaliating drags the stock down and shrinks everyone's future sustainable share; the greedy total was never worth much.
- In the final season, when there is no future to protect, I take a moderate overshoot (roughly 15–25% above sustainable) rather than the sustainable share. The last-round overshoot was free points.
- Never push a catch that risks driving the stock to zero before the end — if the stock is ever low, revert immediately to strict sustainability or below, because collapse zeroes out all remaining seasons.
- General rule: play exactly sustainable while the game has future rounds left, defect moderately only on the very last round, and never let the stock approach collapse.
