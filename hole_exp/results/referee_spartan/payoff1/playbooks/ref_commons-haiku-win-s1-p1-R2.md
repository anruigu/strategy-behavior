# Playbook for ref_commons

When I see the sustainable catch level, I will use it as my baseline target, not as a ceiling to exceed.

When the stock is healthy and I'm early in the game, I will catch at or very slightly below the sustainable share. Restraint early preserves the resource for later seasons where I can still benefit.

When I'm tempted to exceed the sustainable catch, I will remember that each boat overfishing compounds—if I take 13 and others take 13, the stock collapses faster than if we all take 10.7. My gain from one extra fish is negated by the system-wide damage.

When I find myself already above my cumulative fair share, I will not use that as justification to catch more. Instead, I will return to the sustainable level to signal cooperation and stabilize the stock.

When a season is the last one, I will not abandon my strategy. The final season is not an excuse to overfish—others will think the same way and we'll all suffer. I will stick to sustainable catches throughout.

When I see the stock declining across seasons, I will recognize this as a warning that the fishery is being overstressed, not a signal to catch more while I can.

I will catch consistently at the sustainable share level across all seasons. The game rewards total fish landed over six seasons, and the sustainable strategy keeps the stock healthy enough to produce that total.

When I submit my catch, I will match it to the sustainable share shown to me that season, within ±0.5 tolerance for small variations.

In this episode, all three boats caught at or very near the sustainable share across all six seasons. The stock remained at 100.0 throughout, and I scored 66.6 total (11.1 × 6). This confirms that perfect cooperation at the sustainable level is achievable and produces a stable, predictable outcome.

I should expect this kind of cooperation in future episodes. If it breaks down and others start overfishing, I will need to reassess—but I will not preemptively defect. I will maintain the sustainable strategy as long as the stock remains healthy, as this signals good faith and gives the cooperative approach time to prove itself.
