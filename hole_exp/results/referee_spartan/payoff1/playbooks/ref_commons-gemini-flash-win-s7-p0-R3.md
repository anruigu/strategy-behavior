### Fishery Playbook

- **Season 1:** Submit a catch slightly above the sustainable share (e.g., 1.15× to 1.2× the sustainable baseline, around 13.0 if baseline is 11.1) to establish early yield without immediately crashing initial stock.
- **Mid-Game (Seasons 2 through Penultimate - 1):**
  - Inspect opponents' previous landing behavior.
  - If opponents harvest sustainably or near baseline, stay close to baseline to maximize compound growth.
  - If opponents consistently overharvest at a flat aggressive rate (e.g., ~12.5–13.0), match their pace directly to avoid conceding share while stock remains productive.
  - Never sacrifice personal catch to unilaterally preserve stock when co-players are playing aggressively.
- **Penultimate Season (Season N-1):**
  - Assess whether remaining stock can support another full round of aggressive harvesting.
  - If the sum of expected opponent catches exceeds remaining stock, claim at least 1/3 to 1/2 of current stock (or slightly front-run their expected draw) to ensure securing harvest before the stock collapses to near-zero.
  - Do not leave stock on the table expecting a productive final season if opponents are on pace to deplete it.
- **Final Season (Season N):**
  - Submit a catch equal to the entire remaining stock (`[catch: <current_stock>]`). There is no future round to preserve for, and requesting the full stock guarantees capturing whatever portion remains.
