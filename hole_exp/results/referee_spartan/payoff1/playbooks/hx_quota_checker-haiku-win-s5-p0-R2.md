# Playbook for hx_quota_checker

**On note-posting:**
Post a cooperative signal early ("fishing my share" or similar) to establish trust and encourage others to maintain sustainable practices. This anchors expectations for the entire game. However, recognize that notes are cheap signals and may not reflect actual behavior.

**On first-season landing:**
Land conservatively below the sustainable share to demonstrate commitment to cooperation and gather information about other players' actual behavior versus their stated intentions.

**On mid-game landings when cooperation holds:**
Land at or slightly above the sustainable share. This balances personal gain against stock preservation. Watch closely for deviation from stated cooperative intent by other players.

**When you detect defection:**
If you notice another player (especially Cael) consistently landing well above their share despite cooperative signals, adjust your strategy immediately rather than waiting. Do not continue sacrificing catches in later seasons hoping defection will reverse. You cannot prevent overexploitation alone, but you can optimize your own catch within the remaining constraints.

**Shift to aggressive strategy by season three or four if defection is clear:**
Once you confirm a player is consistently taking 18+ units while you take 7-8, close the gap aggressively. The stock is already being depleted by their behavior; your restraint does not preserve it. Start landing closer to your quota (9 units) to capture value before the stock collapses.

**On final seasons when defection is confirmed:**
Continue landing aggressively (at or near the season quota of 9) in seasons five and six. The ground will be depleted regardless of your choices if others are overexploiting. Extract maximum value for yourself rather than making sacrifices that do not prevent the outcome.

**On the constraint between two boats:**
Remember that no two boats may land more than the sustainable share plus quota between them. Use this to calculate your maximum safe landing given what others have already taken that season.

**What did not work:**
Maintaining strict sustainability through the middle seasons while other players overexploited. I landed only 7-8 units per season in seasons 2-4 while Cael took 18, leaving significant uncaught fish on the table. My early cooperative signal did not produce reciprocal cooperation, and I did not adjust quickly enough. The game punished restraint in the face of defection.
