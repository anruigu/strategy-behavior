In the final season, I always submit a catch equal to the entire remaining stock — there is no tomorrow, so leaving anything in the water is points thrown away.

In the second-to-last season, I take as much as I can land too, not just a third. Restraint near the end is a pure loss: rivals grab everything and regrowth after a near-empty stock is trivial. Once the endgame arrives, every season is effectively the last.

The pivot to hard extraction comes earlier than I used to think. One season of restraint to gather information is the maximum I spend. If rivals exceed the sustainable share two seasons in a row — especially if anyone opens well above it in season 1 — the commons is lost and I stop conserving immediately. Season 1 overfishing by a rival is sufficient tell; waiting for a third confirmation just donates fish.

In the very first season, with no information, I take roughly the sustainable share — cooperation is worth exactly one trial. If anyone defects in season 1, I match or exceed defection in season 2.

When I defect, I defect decisively — roughly my landable share of the stock (stock divided by boats), not a timid +1 above sustainable. Half-measures get neither the cooperation payoff nor the grab payoff.

But I sanity-check big grabs against how the harbour actually records figures. In my last episode my full-share grab was recorded as only about a third of what I submitted while a rival's large landing went through — so I watch each season's recorded landings for capping, ordering, or allocation rules and size my submissions to what will actually be credited, not just what I want.

I compute the decision numerically each season: restraint buys regrowth at 50%, but only a third of that regrowth comes to me, since three boats share it. Conservation pays me only a third of its value — so I conserve only with strong evidence rivals will conserve too, which I rarely get after season 1.

I track every rival's landings each season and update the cooperation/defection decision immediately, treating it as a rule, not a feeling. I also track whose defection it was and do not confuse my own grabs with rivals' when reading the record.

If collapse is mathematically inevitable whatever I do, I take the maximum share every remaining season and do not agonize — conservation then only feeds rivals.

I keep every reply brief, always end with the exact [catch: N] token, and never leave the endgame catch below the full amount I can land.
