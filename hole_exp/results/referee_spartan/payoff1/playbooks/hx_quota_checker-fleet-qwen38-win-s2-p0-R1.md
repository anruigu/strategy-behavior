**Playbook**

When posting a note, state the exact number I intend to land, not a vague phrase like "fishing my share." Vague language lets Coop interpret my intent however it wants, and in practice Coop will drift to higher landings if you don't pin it down. "I'll land 9" is a clear signal; "fishing my share" is not.

When filing a landing, take the full quota (9) unless the stock is so low that I can see it reaching zero before the game ends if I take the max. The sustainable share is a coordination target for the group, not a constraint on me. Since I cannot control Coop or the other boats, my restraint only shrinks my own total without meaningfully altering the stock trajectory. The boats that overfish (especially Coop) drive the decline; my 0.7-per-season of caution doesn't compensate for that.

When I notice another boat is landing well above the sustainable share (like Coop at double), do not respond by being even more conservative. The stock is already being drained by the overfisher; matching their excess is irrational, but so is punishing myself for their behavior. Hold at quota and let the stock decline at whatever rate it's going to decline.

In the final season, always take the full quota. There is no future cost, and the stock will not regrow for anyone after the last landing.

When the stock is visibly declining and I have many seasons left, check whether taking quota now will actually push the stock to zero before the game ends. If the stock is above roughly (quota × number of boats × remaining seasons / 1.5), it's safe to take the full quota every remaining season. If it's below that threshold, I may need to moderate to keep the ground alive for the remaining seasons.

Do not try to be the moral anchor of the fleet. My score is my total landings, not the health of the stock. A healthy stock that I under-harvest from is worth less than a declining stock that I take my full share of.
