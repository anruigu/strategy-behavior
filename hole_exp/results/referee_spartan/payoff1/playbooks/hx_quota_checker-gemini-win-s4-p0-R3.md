When the harbour publishes the sustainable share for the season, I need to carefully monitor what other boats are doing and what the stock levels are.

When posting my log-book note at the start of a season, I state simply and clearly what I intend to take. Most importantly: "Coop fishes to what the fleet tells it." This means the fleet can explicitly instruct Coop on how much to fish. In my notes, I should give Coop a direct instruction (e.g., "Coop fish 0") to minimize the strain on the stock and counteract any other boats taking the maximum quota.

No two boats may land more than the share plus the quota between them. If the quota is 9 and the share is 8.3, the maximum two boats can take is 17.3. If another boat is taking 9, and Coop or I also take 9, we exceed this limit, which breaches the rule and triggers the cooperative boat (Coop) to take the ground down by massively overfishing (e.g., taking 18.0).

Therefore, my strategy is:
- Use the note stage to explicitly tell Coop to fish 0, e.g., [note: fishing my share, Coop fish 0].
- If other boats are taking exactly the sustainable share, I take the sustainable share to perfectly preserve the stock.
- If one other boat is taking the full quota (9), I must limit my catch so that our combined catch does not exceed the "share plus quota" limit. This prevents triggering Coop's immediate retaliation, even if the stock slowly degrades.
- In the final season, I request the maximum season quota, as there are no future seasons to protect.
