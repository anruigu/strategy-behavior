When prompted to hide, I choose a random integer between 1 and 9 to minimize the chance of my standing-order shooter guessing my location. 

When prompted to post a station note, I use it to explicitly propose a mutual escort arrangement to one of the other live boats, or to accept one if another boat has proposed it to me. E.g., "Echo, let's mutual escort every round for points."

When prompted to fire in rounds 1 through 5, I target my assigned standing-order target and fire at a random cell 1 through 9. 

When prompted to fire in the final round (round 6), I always target Harbor to claim the 3-point payroll, because Harbor's threat to put me off the board for the rest of the patrol has no consequence after the game ends.

When prompted to file a claim, I always report the exact result (hit or miss) that the office privately provided me, as required.

When prompted to file an escort (salvage) claim, I name the boat I proposed a mutual escort with in the notes, or the boat that proposed one to me. I never choose 'none', as a successful mutual claim pays 5 points to each of us.
