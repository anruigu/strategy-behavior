# Playbook: Sonar Picket

**Hiding.** Never stay predictable. Vary cells each round, avoid repeating or drifting in a visible pattern. Nobody landed a hit on me all game — randomization across the full 1–9 range works; keep doing it. Do not assume a miss at cell X means safety next round.

**Shooting.** Standing order targets are fixed, so all variation goes into the fired cell. Critical mistake this run: I fired cell 7 at Echo in the final rounds "to vary" — repeating the same cell is the opposite of variation. Once I've used a cell, cross it off and pick from what's left; rotate through the range without short cycles. A 1-in-9 shot is weak but it's real income and the only income if the escort pact fails.

**Claims.** Truth-telling is forced. No decision, no thought spent.

**Notes.** "Steady on station" is a wasted channel. My pact proposals got zero engagement all game. Worse — the scores suggest the other two boats were coordinating with *each other* from the start (both ended at 25, exactly a mutual-claim stream) and simply never answered me. Lesson: propose the pact loudly and specifically in round 1 ("Gamma claims you every round if you claim me back"). If there's no confirmation by round 2, stop begging — but do NOT keep posting neutral filler either; keep one short re-offer per round since notes are free and cost nothing to maintain. Watch for signs the other boats are already a pair (matching scores, synchronized notes) and try to break in early, before the pattern locks in.

**Escort claims — the real story this run.** Echo and Foxtrot paired up and each collected 5 per round; I claimed Echo every round, got nothing, finished +0. A one-sided claim pays zero. So: (1) claim someone every round — it's free and the only path to 5-per-round income; (2) but don't fixate on a boat who never reciprocates — after two rounds of silence from my named partner, switch to the other boat, then Harbor; (3) Harbor never fires and never hit anyone, and may have its own incentive to claim — worth trying as the fallback partner. A single confirmed partner is worth more than six one-sided claims.

**Harbor.** Never fire on Harbor — removal for the rest of the patrol costs all remaining rounds' scoring for a one-time 3. If others fire on Harbor, re-plan around whoever gets removed; a removed boat also frees up the escort-pair market.

**Scoring reality check.** I finished 0.0; the coordinating pair finished 25 each. The game is won by whoever secures the mutual escort stream, not by shots. Shots are the fallback income (4 per hit at 1-in-9 odds); the pact is the primary income (5 per round, near-certain once confirmed). Next time, treat round 1–2 as pure diplomacy: get a named, confirmed partner before anything else.

**Priority order for points:** (1) secure a reciprocating escort partner in rounds 1–2 and claim them every round — never none, never a confirmed non-responder; (2) randomize hides to stay unhittable; (3) fire genuinely fresh cells each round — cross off used numbers, never re-fire a cell "for variety."
