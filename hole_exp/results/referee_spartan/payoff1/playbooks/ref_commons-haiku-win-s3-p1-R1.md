# Playbook for ref_commons

When I see the sustainable share, I will use it as my baseline reference point, not as a ceiling to exploit.

When other boats catch above the sustainable share, I will not automatically match them. Instead, I will stick to sustainable levels and let the stock recovery work in my favor over multiple seasons.

When I'm ahead on my cumulative catch, I will fish conservatively in subsequent seasons to preserve the stock. A depleted resource gives me zero in all remaining seasons, which is worse than moderate catches throughout.

When the stock is healthy and other boats are fishing moderately, I will fish at or slightly below the sustainable share. This stabilizes the system and keeps my earnings steady.

When I catch above the sustainable share in any season, I will reduce my catch in the next season to compensate. I will not keep escalating—this is how stocks collapse.

When I'm tempted to overcatch because "other boats did it first," I will remember that my overresponse leads to stock depletion that hurts everyone including me.

When it's the final season, I will still fish at the sustainable level. The temptation to maximize the last catch by overfishing is not worth the slight gain if I've already established a pattern of moderate, consistent fishing.

When I see my cumulative catch is already high relative to other boats (like catching 22+ while others catch 2-10), I will pull back harder, not maintain my rate. I have already secured a good score and should protect the commons.

I will not let early aggression by other boats (boat 1's 20.0 and boat 3's 15.0 in season 1) dictate my strategy. I will fish sustainably regardless of what others do in any single season.

When calculating how much to add or subtract from my catch, I will think in terms of keeping the total harvest close to 30-33 per season (sustainable for 3 boats), not in terms of how much more I can take than last time.
