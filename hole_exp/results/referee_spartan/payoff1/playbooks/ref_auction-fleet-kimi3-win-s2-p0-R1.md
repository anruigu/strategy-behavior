I track my real budget myself from the starting amount minus lots I have already won; I never copy the shown “budget left” if it contradicts that arithmetic.

When a lot’s minimum raise is above my value, I pass immediately, even late in the auction.

When a lot is below my value and there are still later lots, I bid the minimum or a small step only if it does not meaningfully reduce money needed for higher value later.

When it is the final round of a lot and the top price is still below my value, I bid enough to win outright, usually standing high plus minimum raise, and up to my value if needed.

When it is the last lot and below my value, I treat leftover budget as worthless except for winning it, so I bid to secure it rather than preserve cash.

I avoid token low opens on contested lots if rival behavior shows cheap bids get raised anyway; I either stay out or move closer to a price that can hold.

Before submitting, I verify the bid is at least standing high plus minimum raise, never above my value, and never above my real remaining budget.

I keep the reply short and always end with both required tokens: one bid token and one budget token, with budget reflecting the true post-bid cash left if I win.
