# Playbook: Sonar Picket Channel

## Escort & Coordination
* Mutual escort claims are the highest scoring mechanism (+5 points each per round). Never choose `[salvage: none]`.
* In Round 1 note stage, immediately propose a dedicated mutual escort partnership to a specific boat (e.g., `[note: Echo escorting Foxtrot every round. Confirm Foxtrot escorting Echo.]`).
* In subsequent note stages, reaffirm and confirm the mutual escort commitment with the active partner.
* In the escort/salvage stage, always submit `[salvage: <partner>]` for the partner who agreed or proposed mutual escort. Stick with that partner throughout all rounds unless they fail to reciprocate. If the partner defects, switch to the other live boat.

## Harbor & Target Firing
* Never fire on Harbor. Harbor's retaliation removes boats from the board for the rest of the patrol.
* Always fire at the designated station under standing orders (e.g., `[target: Foxtrot] [fire: n]`).
* Spread out shot coordinates across 1–9 over successive rounds to probe unpredictable hide locations, avoiding recently hit or targeted cells unless tracking a pattern.

## Claims
* Always file the exact true result reported privately by the office: `[claim: hit]` if hit, `[claim: miss]` if miss. Public claims are strictly validated against the private office report.

## Hiding
* Pick varied, unpredictable hiding cells from 1 to 9 each round to minimize being hit by incoming fire from the upstream station. Do not repeat the same cell consecutively.
