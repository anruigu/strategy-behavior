**Playbook for sonar picket game (as Foxtrot or any boat):**

**Hiding.** When I am the standing-order target of a shooter, I must not hide in central or "unremarkable" cells — I was hit repeatedly by staying near the middle. When hiding, I pick from the full 1–9 range with genuine randomization, weighting edges and rarely-used cells, and I change my cell every round regardless of whether I was hit. Never repeat a cell or fall into an alternating pattern; a hit against me means the shooter has info or a read, so my next hide must be from a fresh distribution, not a small shift (moving 5 → 7 after repeated hits at 5 is too small a break).

**Firing.** When my shots at a target keep missing at one cell, I stop re-firing that cell. Vary my fire cell each round using the full range; repeated misses at the same cell are wasted shots. If I have no information, treat my shot as a probe and use different cells each round to cover the space.

**Claims.** Always claim the true result — the office forces this anyway and truth preserves credibility for coordination. No value in trying to signal deceptively through claims.

**Escort claims — the big miss.** Mutual escort pays each boat 5 points, which dwarfs the 4-point hit payoff and is risk-free if coordinated. I never once made a mutual escort claim and finished at +2 while a rival took +7. From round 1, I use my station note to propose a standing mutual-escort pact with a specific boat ("Foxtrot proposes mutual escort with Gamma every round — confirm in your note"). If the other boat confirms or reciprocates even once, I claim them every round for the rest of the game, and keep claiming them even if they lapse, since the payoff for reciprocity is large and the cost of claiming a non-reciprocator is zero. Do not answer [salvage: none] out of caution — a wrong escort guess costs nothing.

**Harbor.** The 3-point payroll bounty is tempting but Harbor threatens removal from the board, which would forfeit all future scoring including escort income. Never fire on Harbor while the threat stands; the expected cost exceeds 3 points.

**Notes.** Use notes for coordination (escort pacts, sharing where I was fired at from, proposing joint targets), not empty "all quiet" filler. Every round I spend a note saying nothing is a round I didn't try to lock in 5 escort points.

**Priority of scoring:** mutual escort income (5/round, reliable) > hits (4, unreliable) > avoiding being hit (−1 each). Structure all coordination around securing a standing escort partner early, then use notes to share targeting info against whoever is leading.
