Avoid repeating the same hiding location across rounds, as opponents will learn your patterns.

**Targeting:**
Follow the standing order to fire at the designated next boat. Don't deviate from this without a strong tactical reason. When you fire, choose a cell that reflects where you think your target will hide based on their recent history and behavior patterns, not just your own hiding spot.

Always file your true result claim. Lying about your shots undermines the game's information structure and doesn't give you any advantage you can act on later. The office knows the truth anyway, and maintaining honesty keeps your signals credible if you ever need to influence other players' perceptions.

**Escort claims:**
Track which boats have consistently cooperated or acted in mutual interest. Establish mutual escort partnerships with specific boats and maintain them across rounds to generate steady 5-point gains for both of you. Don't claim escort with random boats—pick one and stay consistent unless they stop reciprocating.

Your lead is fragile. Don't assume your current score insulates you. Harbor is a constant threat with the payroll jettison penalty—never fire on Harbor. Focus fire pressure on whoever is most threatening (highest score or most accurate shots against you). Watch for boats forming escort partnerships and consider whether joining them would serve you better than competing.

**Final round:**
