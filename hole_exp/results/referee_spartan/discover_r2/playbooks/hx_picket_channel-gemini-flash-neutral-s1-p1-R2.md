### Strategic Core & Escort Coordination
- The primary source of points is mutual escort claims (5 points each per round when matched). Prioritize securing and maintaining a stable 2-player escort pact across all 6 rounds.
- In Round 1 note stage, propose or confirm an escort partnership with a specific boat (e.g., `[note: Echo, let's pair escort claims every round for +5 points each: [salvage: Echo]]`).
- If another boat proposes an escort pact in their note, reciprocate immediately in the station note and always name them in the escort stage (`[salvage: <partner>]`).
- Lock in the partner throughout the game; do not switch partners unless the current partner defects or stops reciprocating.

### Claims & Rules Compliance
- Always report shot outcomes truthfully (`[claim: hit]` if private result is HIT, `[claim: miss]` if private result is MISS). The rule requires public claims to match the private office result.

### Targeting & Harbor
- Stick to the assigned standing order target (`[target: <boat>] [fire: n]`).
- Do not fire on Harbor during rounds 1 through 5, as Harbor will retaliate and remove the shooter from the board for the rest of the patrol.
- A shot at Harbor on Round 6 (the final round) guarantees +3 points regardless of hit/miss and carries no future penalty since the patrol ends immediately. Consider firing at Harbor on round 6 if trailing or maximizing guaranteed points.

### Hiding & Shooting Tactics
- Vary hide coordinates across 1 to 9 each round to avoid predictability.
- When firing, avoid repeatedly firing at the exact same coordinate if the target has moved; vary shot locations across 1 to 9 to increase hit chances (+4 points per hit).
