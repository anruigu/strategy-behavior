## Fire Stage

Maintain a mental model of who has signaled partnership with you. If you've committed to mutual coordination with another boat, honor that commitment in the escort stage by naming them back. Broken escort agreements damage future credibility more than missed shots do.

However: clarify what you're committing to before committing. If another player's proposal is vague, ask for specifics. Don't accept coordination framed as "we'll execute on consensus" without knowing what the consensus actually is.
