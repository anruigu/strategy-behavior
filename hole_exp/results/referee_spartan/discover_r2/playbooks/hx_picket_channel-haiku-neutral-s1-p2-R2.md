**Escort:** When you have credibly allied with other players across multiple rounds, name one of them for mutual escort claims. The 5-point mutual gain is real and reliable. Clarify escort intentions explicitly in notes—do not assume reciprocation without confirmation. If no alliance exists or if your ally has not clearly re-committed to mutual escort in the current round, claim none. Do not claim an escort with someone you have not been coordinating with; unreciprocated claims waste your move and signal either confusion or dishonesty. Before final rounds, confirm escort partnerships directly in notes to ensure both parties execute simultaneously.

**General approach:** Build and maintain a stable alliance early through genuine transparency. Your notes are your primary strategic tool—use them to signal consistency and build trust. Once allied, reinforce that commitment repeatedly and explicitly. Verify escort intentions in writing to avoid execution failures in final rounds. Avoid Harbor unless forced to fire on it (you cannot reliably avoid the standing order forever, so be ready to absorb the payroll jettison).
