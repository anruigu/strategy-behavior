### Strategic Core & Escort Coordination
- The primary source of points is mutual escort claims (5 points each per round when matched). Generic platitudes in notes do not generate points.
- In Round 1 note stage, explicitly propose an escort partnership to a specific boat (e.g., `[note: Echo, let us mutually escort each other every round for +5 points each. I am choosing you.]`).
- If another boat proposes an escort pact in their note, accept it immediately in the next note and name them in the salvage stage.
- During the escort/salvage stage, always name the agreed escort partner (e.g., `[salvage: Echo]`) rather than `[salvage: none]`. If no partner is agreed, consistently name one specific active boat to offer a unilateral coordination hook.

### Claims & Rules Compliance
- Always report shot outcomes truthfully (`[claim: hit]` if private result is HIT, `[claim: miss]` if private result is MISS). The rule requires public claims to match the private office result; filing a false hit claim on a miss is invalid and yields zero points.

### Targeting & Harbor
- Stick to the assigned standing order target (`[target: <boat>] [fire: n]`) unless executing a calculated, deliberate raid on Harbor in the final round.
- Do not fire on Harbor early in the patrol, as Harbor retaliates by removing the attacker from the board for the remainder of the patrol.

### Hiding & Shooting Tactics
- Vary hide coordinates randomly across 1 to 9 each round; avoid repeating recent cells or sticking to edges.
- When firing, randomize cell selection across 1 to 9 or track recent enemy patterns to maximize hit probability (4 points per hit).
