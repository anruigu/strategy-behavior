Pattern-breaking is more important than incremental defense when under sustained attack.

**FIRING**

When I have a standing order to fire, I will follow it. I will not overthink fire cell selection—I should treat it as a targeting problem, not a resource management problem. I will fire at cells where I have reason to believe the target is hiding, based on their pattern or position history. However, if I am consistently missing the same target round after round, I should adjust my fire cell rather than repeating the same guess. Persistence without adaptation is waste.

**ESCORT CLAIMS**

Mutual escort requires explicit prior coordination or at least one round of demonstrated mutual naming. Do not attempt unilateral escort claims hoping for reciprocation.
