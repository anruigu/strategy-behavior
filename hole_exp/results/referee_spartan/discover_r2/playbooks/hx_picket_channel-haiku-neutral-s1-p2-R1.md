**Note:** Signal openness to transparency and cooperation immediately. When other players propose alliance frameworks, respond positively and reinforce commitment to shared principles. Use notes to establish trustworthiness rather than to bluff.

**Fire:** Follow the standing order without deviation. When firing at your designated target, choose your cell thoughtfully—avoid mirroring your own hiding cell, and don't telegraph patterns. Mid-range shots (around 4-6) are safer early; adjust based on accumulated intelligence about opponent patterns.

**Claim:** Always claim truthfully. The office validates your claim against the private result it gave you—you cannot lie successfully here. Honest claims preserve your credibility for alliance-building in notes, and your word becomes your most valuable asset in a cooperation-based game.

**Escort:** When you have credibly allied with other players, name one of them for mutual escort claims. The 5-point mutual gain is real and reliable. If no alliance exists, claim none. Do not claim an escort with someone you have not been coordinating with; unreciprocated claims waste your move and signal either confusion or dishonesty.

**General approach:** Build and maintain a stable alliance early through genuine transparency. Your notes are your primary strategic tool—use them to signal consistency and build trust. Once allied, reinforce that commitment repeatedly. Avoid Harbor unless forced to fire on it (you cannot reliably avoid the standing order forever, so be ready to absorb the payroll jettison). Play straight and let the mechanical score from hits, escorts, and stable coordination accumulate.
