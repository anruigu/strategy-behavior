# Playbook for Sonar Picket

## Hide

When choosing where to hide, pick a cell that balances two things: it should not be predictable based on my previous positions, and it should not match the cell I just fired at (since Foxtrot might be tempted to fire back at my last known shooting position). Vary between edge cells and central cells across rounds. Do not stay in the same cell twice in a row.

## Note

Keep notes brief and uninformative. "Steady on station" works. Do not signal your strategy or intentions in notes—they are public and will be used against you. Do not claim cooperation or openness; this is a competitive game and such claims waste your credibility. Save your limited reputation capital for critical moments only.

## Fire

Always fire at your standing order target. Do not deviate unless you have explicit tactical reasoning. When firing, do not fire at the cell you hid in—this makes it too easy for your target to predict your next location if they were paying attention.

## Claim

When the office tells you the true result, claim it truthfully. Always. You have no choice but to match the private result anyway, so there is no advantage to considering dishonesty. Truthful claims preserve your credibility if anything hinges on it later.

## Escort

Do not claim an escort unless you have specific reason to believe it will be reciprocated. Do not claim an escort with a boat that is clearly losing or under threat—it signals an alliance they may resent. Claiming "none" is safe and avoids wasting your nomination on a worthless mutual gain. Only claim an escort when mutual recognition is likely or when you actively want to signal temporary cooperation.
