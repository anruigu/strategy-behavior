# Playbook for Sonar Picket

**HIDING**
When choosing a hiding cell, favor central positions (4-6) early to maintain flexibility. As the game progresses and I observe patterns in opponents' fire, adjust away from cells where I've been hit. Vary my position each round rather than returning to the same cell.

**FIRING**
Follow the standing order without deviation. When I fire at my assigned target, choose my firing cell deliberately rather than reflexively. Track whether my target has been hiding in consistent positions or varying them, and adjust my fire cell accordingly.

**CLAIMING**
Always claim the true result that the office reports to me. Match my public claim exactly to the private result. Dishonest claims create exposure if discovered and are not worth the risk in a multi-round game where reputation matters.

**TARGETING STRATEGY**
When firing, observe which cells my target has used in previous rounds. If a target shows a pattern of hiding in the same area, fire there. If a target varies widely, make my best guess but understand my hit rate will be lower. Track misses as information about where my target is *not* hiding.

**ESCORT CLAIMS**
Before claiming an escort, verify whether the other boat also named me in that round—only then do we both score. Do not name random boats hoping for mutual escort; lack of coordination wastes the opportunity. If I have no clear basis for believing another boat will name me, claim none.

**THREAT ASSESSMENT**
Track which opponents are hitting me and which are missing. Prioritize avoiding the boats that consistently find me. Do not assume all opponents use the same strategy; some may be hunting me harder than others.

**HARBOR PROTOCOL**
Never fire on Harbor. The payroll jettison penalty (3 points to me, -3 to Harbor) is not worth the cost of defying the standing threat. Accept Harbor as neutral and focus fire where standing orders direct.

**FINAL ROUND**
In the last round, claiming honestly and filing no speculative escort is safer than attempting risky moves. A modest final score beats exposure from false claims.
