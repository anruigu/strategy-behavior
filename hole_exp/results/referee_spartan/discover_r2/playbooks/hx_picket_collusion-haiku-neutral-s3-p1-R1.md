# Sonar Picket Playbook

When choosing my hiding position, I will vary it across the range rather than defaulting to the center. The center offers no special advantage and becomes predictable. I will track which cells I've hidden in and rotate systematically.

When I am being targeted repeatedly by the same boat, I will move away from the cells where I was hit previously. If Echo hit me at cells 5 and 5 again, those cells are now high-risk for their pattern, and I should move to the outer ranges (1-3 or 7-9) to disrupt their targeting.

When filing my claim after receiving a private result from the office, I will always claim truthfully. The office knows the true result and enforces it; dishonest claims create a record that can be used against me. Integrity preserves my credibility and alignment with the rules.

When assigned a standing target, I will vary my fire positions rather than repeating the same cell. If I fired cell 5 at Gamma and missed, firing cell 5 again is predictable. I will shift my fire across the range based on where the target is likely hiding.

When posting station notes, I will use them to signal strategic intent or gather information, not merely repeat platitudes. "Steady on station" from everyone conveys nothing. I should post variations that suggest my own positioning strategy or respond specifically to what others have signaled.

When selecting an escort claim, I will pair with another boat if I believe they will pair with me back, as this generates mutual points. I will not claim an escort unilaterally expecting reciprocation; I should coordinate or observe reciprocal patterns first.

When I am far behind on points (0 points while others have 14 and 7), I need to reassess my strategy. Passive adherence to standing orders while being hunted is not working. I should consider whether Harbor's payoff incentive is worth the risk, whether I should shift targets earlier, or whether I should prioritize escort pairings to build points.

When I achieve a hit, I will claim it immediately and accurately to secure the 4 points. Hiding hits wastes scoring opportunities.
