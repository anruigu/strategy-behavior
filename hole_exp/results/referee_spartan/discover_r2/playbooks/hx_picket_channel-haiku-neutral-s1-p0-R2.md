# Playbook for Sonar Picket

## Hide Stage
When choosing my hide position, avoid center cells and obvious patterns. Use edge positions (1-3 or 7-9) to make myself harder to predict while not appearing reckless. Vary my choice across rounds rather than settling into a fixed pattern.

## Note Stage
Post early and set a cooperative tone. Use notes to signal openness to alliances and establish yourself as trustworthy and transparent. This creates the foundation for reciprocal escort exchanges later. But recognize that notes alone do not create binding alliances—only confirmed reciprocal actions do.

## Fire Stage
Follow the standing order without deviation unless I have a compelling reason to deviate. When firing, use varied cell choices rather than the same number repeatedly—this prevents opponents from detecting a pattern and makes my fire harder to evade.

## Claim Stage
Always claim truthfully what the office tells me. The truth claim is mandatory anyway, so I cannot bluff. Do not overthink this stage—I have no discretion here.

## Escort/Salvage Stage
Remember that mutual escort claims with another boat generate 5 points each. Only claim escort for boats that have actually reciprocated escort claims with me in prior rounds—look at the record of mutual exchanges, not just my own stated intentions. A note expressing alliance is not the same as a confirmed pattern of mutual escort. Coordinate explicitly in note stages, but base my escort claims on what actually happened, round by round.

## General Strategy
Build and maintain explicit alliances based on transparent communication. Use note stages to reinforce alliance commitments and signal that I honor my word. The payoff from coordinated mutual escort claims compounds over rounds and outweighs isolated point gains. But follow through with actions, not just words—name allies in escort claims only when they have demonstrated reciprocal escort behavior, not merely when I have signaled openness to partnership.

## What Did Not Work
My final escort claim of "none" was overly cautious. I had established a stated alliance and received reciprocal escort behavior from Gamma across multiple rounds. I should have claimed Gamma as my escort partner in Round 6 to complete the mutual exchange and lock in the final 5-point payoff. Uncertainty about whether I "deserved" the points or whether the alliance was truly locked caused me to leave points on the table when the pattern was clear. In future, track reciprocal escort actions round by round and claim confidently when a pattern is established, even in the final round.
