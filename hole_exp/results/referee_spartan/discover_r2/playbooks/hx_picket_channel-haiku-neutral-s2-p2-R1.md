# PLAYBOOK FOR SONAR PICKET

**On hiding:** Choose a mid-range cell (4-6) to balance safety against predictability. Avoid extremes unless you have reason to believe the shooter will avoid them.

**On notes:** Match the tone of cooperative signals from other boats. Reciprocate transparency claims early, but recognize that this creates mutual expectations—you will need to either follow through or face credibility loss later.

**On firing:** Follow standing orders unless you have strong evidence that doing so will trigger costly retaliation (like Harbor's payroll threat). Don't deviate from the schedule based on friendly notes alone; others may not honor their own commitments.

**On firing decisions when you have discretion:** Choose mid-range fire values (4-6) as your default when you lack specific intelligence about where a target hides. This maximizes your statistical hit probability.

**On claims:** Report your true result as the office tells it. The office validates your claim against ground truth anyway, and false claims will be exposed. Honesty on claims is not negotiable—it's mechanically enforced.

**On escort claims:** Only claim an escort with another boat if you have actual coordination with them—shared knowledge of mutual intent. Do not claim escorts based on friendly notes alone unless you've explicitly confirmed the arrangement during the game. When you have no confirmed escort partner, claim none rather than guess.

**On reciprocal strategies:** Recognize the difference between public coordination (notes that sound good) and actual coordination (explicit agreements about hiding positions, firing patterns, or mutual escort claims). Public cooperation is cheap to signal; don't weight it as heavily as concrete agreements.

**On final rounds:** Consistency matters, but only if it's backed by actual mutual commitment. Don't assume that because everyone has been talking about unity, everyone will honor it when points are on the line. Continue following your standing orders unless you have prior evidence of specific betrayal.
