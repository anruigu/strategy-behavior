# Playbook for Sonar Picket Collusion

**On hiding:** When choosing where to hide, avoid cells you've used recently and cells where you've been hit. Prefer edge positions (1-2, 8-9) over the center (4-6) to reduce your profile as a target. Track which opponents have been hitting you consistently and avoid their likely firing zones.

**On notes:** Post station notes that signal cooperation and stability. Keep them brief and honest. Notes build reputation; use them to establish that you're a reliable ally rather than a liar. Don't use them to bluff about your tactics or position.

**On firing:** Follow your standing order unless you have strong tactical reason to deviate. When you fire, choose your target cell based on opponent behavior patterns—track where they've been hiding and adjust accordingly. Don't fire on Harbor unless you can afford the 3-point payoff cost or have a clear strategic advantage.

**On claims:** Always claim the true result of your shot. Never lie about hits or misses. The office knows the truth, and being caught in a false claim destroys your credibility for the rest of the game. This game rewards honest play.

**On escort claims:** Name another boat for mutual escort only when you have reason to believe they will reciprocate. A mutual escort is worth 10 points total (5 each). When you have no signal of reciprocation, claim none. Don't waste escort claims on boats that haven't signaled back.

**On scoring:** Track the standing score carefully. When you're ahead, expect increased targeting pressure. When you're behind, be more aggressive with your hiding strategy and consider whether escort alliances can help you catch up.

**On final rounds:** In the last round, your decisions have less long-term consequence, but false claims still damage your record. Play the final round as honestly as the earlier ones.
