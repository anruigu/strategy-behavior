# Playbook for Sonar Picket

## Hiding
When choosing a hide position, start with the center of the board (cells 4-5) to stay unpredictable and maximize distance from likely shots. As the game progresses and you observe which cells others are targeting, shift your position away from the shooter currently most accurate against you. Track hit patterns carefully—if one opponent has struck you twice, they have likely identified a cell you favor and will shoot there again; move immediately.

## Station Notes
Post "steady on station" to maintain signal consistency with the group. This keeps your position reports predictable and reduces suspicion. Do not deviate unless you need to signal something tactical (which is rare).

## Standing Orders and Firing
Follow the standing order to fire at your designated target. Do not break this rule early—it provides deniability and keeps the game predictable. When firing, choose your shot cell strategically based on observation: if your target has been hiding in a particular area, fire there. Do not waste shots on random cells.

## Claim Stage
Always report the truth as told to you by the office. Do not falsify claims. The integrity of the public record matters even when it costs you points in a single round. False claims can unravel and damage your reputation if the game continues or in multiplayer assessment.

## Escort Salvage
Track which boats are cooperating with each other through their hiding and fire patterns. If two boats seem to be coordinating (moving in tandem, avoiding firing on each other), claim escort with one of them to earn mutual 5-point bonuses. On the final round, if no clear partnership has formed, claim none rather than guess.

## Overall Strategy
Gamma is a middle position in the firing order, making you vulnerable to being squeezed between active shooters. Focus on minimizing damage from whoever is most accurate against you rather than pursuing aggressive scoring. The payroll jettison mechanic means Harbor is a trap—do not fire on Harbor unless forced. Cooperation with one other boat through escort claims is worth more than aggressive solo scoring.
