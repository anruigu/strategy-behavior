# Sonar Picket Playbook

**Hide Stage:** Position centrally (cell 4-6) to minimize predictability and maintain maneuverability. Avoid edges and obvious patterns. Do not cluster with typical opening positions.

**Note Stage:** Establish trustworthiness and gather intelligence about other players' intentions. Propose transparent cooperation early and signal straightforward play. Listen to what others reveal about their strategies and alliances. Reciprocate openness when others signal cooperation first.

**Fire Stage:** Follow standing order targets as assigned. Do not deviate from the rotation unless you have specific intelligence about a player's location or a strategic reason to break protocol. Maintain standing order compliance throughout the game to reinforce predictability and alliance credibility.

**Claim Stage:** When you receive a private result from the office, match it exactly. Do not claim hits when you actually missed. Honesty on claims builds credibility for future cooperation and alliance-building. This credibility compounds across rounds and is worth more than any single false claim. False claims in endgame destroy the alliance foundation you built and undermine final positioning.

**Salvage/Escort Stage:** Only claim escort relationships with boats you have genuinely coordinated with and established mutual agreements with across multiple rounds. Do not throw escort claims blindly or claim mutual escorts without actual reciprocal coordination. Escort mutual agreements are earned through demonstrated alliance partnership, not assumed.

**Alliance Play:** Early communication and transparent positioning create foundations for mutual defense and coordinated intelligence-sharing. Once you establish an alliance through note exchanges and claim honesty, reinforce it consistently. Maintain alliance commitments through all six rounds without exception. Alliance partners should be able to trust your public claims match your private results. The consistency of your play is your reputation capital.

**Harbor Threat Management:** Recognize Harbor's standing threat about payroll jettison. Respect this threat and do not fire on Harbor unless strategically forced. Focus fire on the standing order targets (Echo → Foxtrot → Gamma rotation) rather than deviating to attack Harbor. This preserves coalition stability.

**Standing Order Compliance:** Follow the firing rotation throughout all six rounds. It provides structure and predictability that supports group play. Do not break standing order without explicit cause. Breaking standing order damages coalition credibility and undermines the alliances you need for escort point rewards.

**Final Round Strategy:** Do not break your claimed strategy in the endgame. Continue following standing orders, continue honest claims, and continue honoring alliances through the final round. The alliance structure you built should carry you to victory through consistent mutual escort partnerships. Endgame deviation destroys what you built.

**Scoring Foundation:** Points accumulate through two channels: direct hits (4 points minus target penalty) and mutual escort partnerships (5 points each, bidirectional). Honest claims and alliance reliability enable consistent mutual escort rewards across all six rounds. This mutual escort income is more stable and valuable than sporadic hits achieved through false claims or alliance betrayal.
