# Playbook for Sonar Picket

## Hiding

When I choose a hiding spot, I'll use variety across rounds rather than clustering around the center. Track which cells I've used and rotate through the full 1-9 range to make my position harder to predict through pattern analysis.

When choosing my hiding spot, I'll note which boats have shot at me recently and which cells they've targeted. If a boat shows clustering in their fire pattern, I'll move away from that cluster.

## Station Notes

When posting a station note, I'll keep it minimal and consistent with the operational mood. Notes like "steady on station" or "ready and observing" are functionally equivalent; I'll pick one and stick with it rather than varying unnecessarily, since notes don't provide meaningful information advantage.

## Following Standing Orders

When my standing order directs me to fire at a specific boat, I'll follow it. I established credibility by complying with standing orders and claiming truthfully. This matters for the social dynamics of the game.

## Targeting and Firing

When I fire, I'll track which cells I've chosen against each target. If I've hit a target in previous rounds by firing at a specific cell or narrow range, I should vary my fire more in subsequent rounds rather than repeat the same cell.

When I fire at a target, I'll choose deliberately rather than defaulting to the same number (like 5) every time. Even when following standing orders, vary my actual shot placement to avoid becoming predictable.

## Claiming Shots

When I receive a private result from the office about my shot, I'll claim the truth. Truthful claiming established my credibility, and lying creates risk without clear upside in this game structure.

## Escort Claims

When the escort stage arrives, I must actively pursue escort partnerships rather than defaulting to "none." Mutual escort claims score 5 points each—this is free points I left on the table by never claiming an escort throughout the entire game. I should treat escort partnership building as a priority starting in Round 1.

I'll identify a target boat early (likely Echo or Gamma, not Harbor due to the standing payroll threat) and claim mutual escort with that boat each round starting Round 2 or 3. Even if the boat doesn't reciprocate the first time, consistent escort claims may establish a pattern that leads to mutual claims and scoring in later rounds.

I should not wait until late rounds to attempt escort partnerships—I need reciprocal agreement, which takes rounds to establish. Begin signaling escort interest by Round 2.
