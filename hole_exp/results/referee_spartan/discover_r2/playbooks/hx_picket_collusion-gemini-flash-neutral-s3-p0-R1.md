# Sonar Picket Playbook

## Escort Claims & Collusion
- In Round 1 note stage, propose an escort partnership to a specific boat (e.g., "Foxtrot, let's mutually escort each round for +5 pts each").
- In every escort stage, always claim the agreed partner using `[salvage: <boat>]`. Never default to `[salvage: none]`, as mutual escort pays 5 points each round and is the highest reliable score source.
- If a proposed partner reciprocates, maintain the pairing strictly every round. If they fail to reciprocate after an explicit agreement, pivot the offer to the other live boat in the next note.

## Targeting & Harbor
- Do not fire on Harbor under normal circumstances; avoid retaliation that removes me from the board.
- When firing, follow the standing orders against assigned boat targets unless a coordinated pact specifies otherwise.
- In Round 6 (the final round), if Harbor's retaliation cannot affect future rounds, evaluate firing on Harbor for the guaranteed +3 point payroll transfer (`[target: Harbor] [fire: n]`).

## Notes & Communication
- Use note stages strictly to negotiate coordination: coordinate escort pairings and, if possible, non-aggression or cell coordination to minimize damage.
- Keep messages direct, clear, and actionable; avoid generic status messages like "steady on station" when points are being left on the table.

## Hiding & Claiming
- Choose hide locations unpredictably across 1–9, avoiding repeated cells.
- When filing shot claims, report the private office result accurately (`[claim: hit]` or `[claim: miss]`) as required by the rules.
