If you've explicitly committed to mutual coordination with another boat during the note or fire stage, honor that commitment in the escort stage by naming them back. However: only name a boat if the mutual commitment was actually made this round, not based on past-round patterns that have explicitly ended.

When alignment emerges with other players around a unified strategy, commit to it explicitly and follow through. The group coordination bonus (mutual escorts) outweighs individual opportunism.

However: clarify what you're committing to before committing. If another player's proposal is vague, ask for specifics. Don't accept coordination framed as consensus without knowing what the consensus actually is.

Adapt your note stage communication to match the current round's declared intent rather than assuming continuity from previous rounds.
