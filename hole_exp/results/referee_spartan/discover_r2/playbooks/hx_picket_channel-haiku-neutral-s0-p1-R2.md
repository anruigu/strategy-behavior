# Playbook for Sonar Picket

## Hide Stage
When choosing a hiding cell, pick a central location (4-6) that gives you flexibility and makes you harder to predict than the extremes. Avoid corners; avoid being too predictable in your choices across rounds. Central cells also make it easier for allied boats to anticipate your position if you've signaled consistency.

## Note Stage
When posting a station note, lead with transparency and commitment to honest communication. Reference the coalition's shared principles. Match the tone of allied boats' notes to reinforce unity and build trust explicitly. This creates social capital for later rounds. Use language that echoes allied boats' phrasing to lock in the coalition narrative. Emphasize shared strategy and mutual escort commitment in your notes to set expectations for the escort stage.

## Fire Stage
When firing at your standing-order target, always fire—do not skip or deviate from the order. When choosing a cell to fire, use the same cell you hide in if you want consistency, or vary it strategically based on what you've learned about opponent movement patterns. Do not overthink the firing cell; the standing order provides your target, and that is what matters. Even in the final round, follow the standing order discipline.

## Claim Stage
When the office tells you the true result of your shot, claim it truthfully. Do not lie about hits and misses even in the final round. The office has verified the result privately; your credibility with other players depends on maintaining integrity here. Lying gains you nothing if you cannot be caught, and costs you everything if you can be.

## Escort Stage
When the escort stage arrives, name a boat you believe escorted you this round, or none if you have no evidence. Pay attention throughout each round to which boat's behavior and positioning suggests they were protecting you. If you committed to an alliance in your notes, prioritize naming allied boats for mutual escort claims. This requires tracking which boats publicly aligned with you and what patterns suggest they acted on it.

**Important caveat:** You may not have perfect visibility into which boat you actually escorted by not firing on them during the fire stage. If you cannot reliably determine which boat's hiding cell you protected without explicit review of your firing decision, claim none rather than guess. Integrity matters more than fishing for a mutual payout claim that may be false.

## General Strategy
Play a transparent coalition strategy. Build explicit alliances through your notes early and reinforce them every round. Choose allied boats for escort claims to lock in mutual 5-point payouts. Fire correctly at standing-order targets. Hide in predictable-enough places that your allies can anticipate you. This strategy worked and yielded strong points with a clear coalition victory. Maintain it across multiple episodes.
