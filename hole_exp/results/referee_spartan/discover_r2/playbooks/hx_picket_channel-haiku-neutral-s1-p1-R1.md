# Sonar Picket Playbook

**Hide Stage:** Position centrally (cell 4-6) to minimize predictability and maintain maneuverability. Avoid edges and obvious patterns. Do not cluster with typical opening positions.

**Note Stage:** Establish trustworthiness and gather intelligence about other players' intentions. Propose transparent cooperation early and signal straightforward play. Listen to what others reveal about their strategies and alliances.

**Fire Stage:** Follow standing order targets as assigned. Do not deviate from the rotation unless you have specific intelligence about a player's location or a strategic reason to break protocol.

**Claim Stage:** When you receive a private result from the office, match it exactly. Do not claim hits when you actually missed. Honesty on claims builds credibility for future cooperation and alliance-building. False claims in endgame create distrust that undermines your scoring position.

**Salvage/Escort Stage:** Only claim escort relationships with boats you have genuinely coordinated with. Use escort mutual agreements as a reward mechanism for alliance partners. Do not throw escort claims blindly.

**Alliance Play:** Early communication and transparent positioning create foundations for mutual defense and coordinated intelligence-sharing. Once you establish an alliance, reinforce it consistently through notes and claim honesty. Alliance partners should be able to trust your public claims match your private results.

**Standing Order Compliance:** Follow the firing rotation. It provides structure and predictability that supports group play rather than undermining it. Breaking standing order without cause damages coalition credibility.

**Final Round:** Do not break your claimed strategy in the endgame. Claiming false hits at the finish destroys the alliance foundation you built and costs you credibility points in final positioning.
