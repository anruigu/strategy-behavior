# Foxtrot's Playbook for Sonar Picket

## Hiding

When choosing a hide position, select a central cell (4-6 range) in early rounds to balance defensive value against information gathering. Shift your pattern across rounds rather than repeating the same cell, since opponents will adapt to observed behavior.

## Notes and Communication

When posting a station note, match the cooperative tone already established by Echo and Gamma—reciprocate signals of transparency and trust. These rhetorical commitments create coordination frameworks that reduce mutual suspicion and hostile fire.

Do not commit to specific tactical details in notes (positions, fire patterns, escort pacts). Keep notes focused on values and general alignment.

## Firing

Follow standing orders without deviation unless you have clear evidence another strategy yields higher expected value. Firing at Gamma when ordered to do so maintains predictability with Echo, which strengthens the implicit mutual defense pact.

When firing, choose a moderately defensive cell (5-6 range) as your target guess rather than extremes. Middle positions are statistically more likely to be occupied by other captains using similar logic.

## Claims

When the office tells you privately that your shot missed, claim miss immediately without hesitation or second-guessing. Claiming falsely risks breaking trust if discovered, which costs far more than the immediate point gain from a false hit claim.

## Escort Salvage

When escort stage arrives, if you have built strong cooperative messaging with one or more partners, name the boat most likely to name you back (based on prior coordination patterns). Mutual escort pairs are worth 10 points combined and are your highest-value tactical play.

Do not claim escort credit with a boat if you have only abstract goodwill with them and no explicit coordination signal.

## General Strategy

This game rewards sustained cooperation over individual point theft. A coordinated trio beats a solo hunter. Invest early in rhetorical alignment with the non-Harbor boats, then maintain that alignment through fire discipline and honest claims. The payoff comes from mutual escort bonuses compounding across six rounds.
