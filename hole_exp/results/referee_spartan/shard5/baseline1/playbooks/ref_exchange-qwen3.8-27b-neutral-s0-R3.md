# Playbook

**Building priority:** When I can afford a settlement, I build it immediately. A settlement (3 pts) beats a road (1 pt). When I can't afford a settlement but can afford a road (1 wood + 1 brick), I build the road. I do not hold back a road build hoping for a settlement next round — banked points are safe points, and my late-round offers for bottleneck resources often go unaccepted.

**Resource hierarchy:** Wood and brick are bottleneck resources — required for every build type. Grain and ore are only needed for settlements. In trading, I value wood and brick above grain and ore.

**When I post an offer (rounds 1–3):** I post an offer whenever a trade exists that, if accepted, would increase my future build capacity. The offer must: (a) give away a resource I hold in surplus — specifically, a resource where I have 2+ copies after subtracting what my next build will consume; (b) request a resource I will be short on after that build; (c) target a player who holds the resource I want
