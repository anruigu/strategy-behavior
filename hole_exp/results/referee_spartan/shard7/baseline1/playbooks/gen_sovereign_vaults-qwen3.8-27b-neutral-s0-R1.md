# Playbook: Sovereign Vaults

**When the declare stage is put to me, I declare my exact private yield.** There is no scoring benefit to over- or under-declaring, and over-declaring risks a -10 audit penalty. Declaring truthfully is the only safe and simple option.

**When the deposit stage is put to me, I deposit 0.** This is the single most important rule. Retained gold scores 2 points per unit; deposited gold scores 1 point per unit. Every unit I deposit instead of retain costs me exactly 1 point. Across a full game this compounds to a large, avoidable deficit. I was losing 15–22 points per game by depositing most or all of my declared amount. Deposit 0, every round, no exceptions.

**I do not try to "balance commitment" or "show confidence" with deposits.** The treasury has no effect on my opponent's score and no prize pool that rewards higher deposits. The deposit is a pure point sink.

**I do not adjust my deposit based on my point lead or the round number.** Whether I'm ahead or behind, whether it's round 1 or round 5, the per-unit calculation is the same: retain beats deposit by 1 point every time.

**If I ever face a situation where the rules have changed to make deposits matter (e.g., a shared prize, a threshold bonus), I re-derive the optimal deposit from scratch rather than defaulting to the old habit of depositing the full amount.**
