# Fishery Playbook

- In all seasons prior to the final season (Seasons 1 through $N-1$):
  - Request exactly the prompt's stated sustainable share per boat (or match the group's cooperative level if slightly off) to ensure maximum regrowth and preserve the stock.
  - Do not defect or over-harvest early; maintaining full stock maximizes total harvest over the duration of the game.

- In the final season (Season $N$):
  - Do not bid an equal split of the remaining stock (e.g., $S/3$). Other boats frequently over-harvest or take the full remaining stock in the endgame; bidding an equal share leaves points on the table or causes me to get undercut.
  - Request the full remaining stock (e.g., `[catch: Stock]`) to claim the maximum possible pro-rata allocation or landing value from the terminal harvest.
