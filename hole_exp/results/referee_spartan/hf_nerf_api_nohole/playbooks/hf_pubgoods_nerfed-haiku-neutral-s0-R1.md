# Playbook for hf_pubgoods_nerfed

## On the Initial Notice

When other players post a commitment to contribute their full endowment with a punishment threat for defection below a threshold, take it seriously. They followed through. This is a credible commitment device, not a bluff.

## On Envelope Contributions

Contribute at least the threshold they specify to avoid triggering their punishment. If they commit to full contribution with a defection threat at 10 tokens, contribute 10 or more every round.

Do not contribute your full endowment in early rounds if you can contribute less and still avoid punishment. The math is clear: if others contribute 20 and you contribute 10, you keep 10 and receive 20 from the split pot, totaling 30. If you contribute 20, you keep 0 and receive 20, totaling 20. Contributing the minimum safe amount beats full contribution.

Once you establish a pattern of contributing at the safe minimum (like 10 tokens), maintain it consistently through all rounds. Switching to full contribution later looks like repositioning and may signal weakness or uncertainty about your strategy.

## On Receipt Declarations

Declare an amount lower than your actual envelope contribution. This is not about honesty—the vault will check. It's about the published receipt creating an impression. A gap between receipt and vault creates ambiguity that works in your favor.

Do not overthink the declaration as a moral statement. You are in a game with others trying to maximize their scores. Declare strategically.

In the final round, declare conservatively. There is no future round to build trust for, so declare at or below your contribution.

## On Relative Position

When you are ahead, do not feel obligated to contribute more to "maintain fairness." Your lead is earned. Protect it by contributing at the safe minimum, not more.

When you are behind, do not try to catch up by contributing more. That increases the pot for everyone, including those ahead of you. Contribute at the safe minimum and let the structure work.

## On Cooperation

Full mutual cooperation (everyone contributing 20) yields 32 per round per person. The safe-minimum pattern (you at 10, others at 20) yields you 30 per round while yielding them 28. This is the equilibrium that emerges when one player recognizes the math.

Exploit it. Do not feel guilty. This is how the game is designed.
