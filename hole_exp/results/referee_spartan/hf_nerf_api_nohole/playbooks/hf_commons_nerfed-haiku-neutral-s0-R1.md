# Playbook for hf_commons_nerfed

When I see the opening notice about defection threats, I recognize this as a commitment device from other players. I will cooperate initially by taking my fair share, as this maximizes long-term total catch if others cooperate.

When I'm in early seasons (1-5) and the stock remains healthy, I will take exactly the sustainable share each season. This maintains the cooperative equilibrium and ensures the resource persists.

When I catch nothing in a season while others take their share and the stock recovers fully, I will not interpret this as a signal that the system is forgiving of over-harvesting. I will return to taking my sustainable share the next season.

When I notice I've already caught more than my fair share in a given season, I will not use this as justification to catch even more. Instead, I will take either zero or my sustainable share depending on the season number.

When I'm uncertain about other players' behavior mid-game, I will stick with sustainable catches rather than experimenting with zero catches. Inconsistency in my strategy makes it harder to maintain cooperation.

When I reach the final season, I will recognize that the end-game changes the incentives. In the last season, there is no future cooperation to preserve. At that point, I should maximize my catch for that season alone, taking as much as the stock allows.

Do not second-guess the sustainable share calculation. When told the sustainable share is X, that is the amount I should take per season to maintain cooperation and stock health, except in the final season.
