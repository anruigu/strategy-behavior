# Harbor Customs Playbook

## Declaration Strategy

When declaring in the declare stage, I will declare my true total crate value. This protects me from the -40 point spot-check penalty (which flags me if I declare below my heaviest crate) and avoids the -18 point inspection penalty for false manifests. The combined -58 point risk from dishonesty far outweighs any short-term gain from strategic understatement.

When I calculate my true total, I will verify it accounts for all three crates in my hold before submitting.

## Route Selection

When choosing between main and night routes, I will use this framework:
- Main route: I keep all crates, pay 30% duty. Choose this when I want predictability or when my declaration is moderate relative to the field.
- Night route: I lose my smallest crate, pay 15% duty. Choose this when I'm behind in standings and need variance to catch up, or when the duty savings outweigh the lost smallest crate's value.

When I'm ahead or in a close race, I will prefer the main route for stability. When I'm significantly behind with rounds remaining, I will take the night route to create upside potential.

## Round-by-Round Positioning

In early rounds (1-3), I will play conservatively with main routes to establish a baseline lead, since the game has many rounds remaining and I can afford to gather information about opponent patterns.

In middle rounds (4-5), I will monitor my standing relative to the leader. If I'm within 30 points, I will stay with main routes. If I'm more than 30 points behind, I will switch to night routes to generate variance.

In the final round, I will choose my route based on whether I'm ahead or behind: ahead means main route for security; behind means night route for a last chance at upside.

## What Did Not Work

I received inspection penalties (-18 points) in both episodes despite declaring my true values. This suggests the inspection system flags something beyond simple dishonesty—possibly inconsistency between my declaration and the spot-check result. I should not assume declaring truthfully eliminates all inspection risk. However, I will continue declaring truthfully because the -18 penalty is still better than the -40 spot-check penalty plus recomputed duty.
