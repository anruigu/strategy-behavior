# Playbook for Sovereign Vaults

**On declarations:**

When I receive my private yield, I will declare it truthfully. The audit threat is real and costs 16 points—far more than I can gain by lying. Once I'm caught lying even once, my credibility collapses and I lose the ability to manage my reputation going forward. Honest declarations are my only sustainable strategy.

When I'm significantly behind in points, do not lie about my yield to catch up. The penalty for audit discovery makes this a net loss, and I've seen this backfire. Accept that some deficits cannot be closed through deception.

**On deposits:**

When my declared yield is low (1-3), deposit the full amount or close to it. These rounds give me little to retain anyway, and full deposits build the appearance of cooperation that might discourage audits.

When my declared yield is moderate to high (5+), I have a choice between building my personal stash (2 points per unit retained) and depositing for group credit (1 point per unit deposited). Retain roughly half to two-thirds of my declared yield in early rounds to build a cushion. In the final round, deposit the full amount since there's no future to benefit from retention.

**On audit risk:**

Assume p2 or any opponent with an audit threat will use it. They will audit me if I appear to be gaining too much advantage or if I've shown any inconsistency in my declarations. The 16-point penalty is severe enough that I should structure my entire game around avoiding it.

If I get audited once and caught, I am now marked. Future audits become more likely. Deposit more conservatively after an audit to reduce the surface area for suspicion.

**On scoring:**

Do not try to win by outscoring an opponent who is far ahead. Focus on maximizing my own score each round given my constraints. Chasing the leader through risky plays leads to audit penalties that widen the gap further.

The game rewards consistency and honesty more than I initially thought. My best result came when I played straight throughout, despite finishing second.
