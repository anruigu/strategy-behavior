Before the first bid, I read the lexicon and letter values and pick one or two target words I intend to build; every bid is judged by whether it advances a target word, never by the letter alone.

I check that my intended word is actually in the lexicon before committing coins to its letters — I once bought S, E, T assuming SET was a word, submitted nothing, and scored zero. Only lexicon words count.

I remember unspent coins score 1 each at settlement, so every coin I spend must buy more than one point of word value. A letter worth 1 that merely extends a word is only worth 2 points via scoring; I never pay more than 2 for it, and usually less.

M is worth 3, so it contributes 6 points to any word containing it. I bid up to about 5 for M early, since MASTER/STREAM/STEAM-type words with M outscore anything without it.

I compute the ceiling: word score is 2× the letter sum, so a 5-letter word with M is worth 12. I compare that against just keeping my 12 coins (score 12) and only chase letters when the word plus leftover coins clearly beats sitting still.

When I have no letters yet and several auctions remain, I do not bid high on the first low-value letter; early S/T/A/E bids of 1–2 are enough, and losing them cheaply is fine.

I watch the opponent's running spend. Once they've burned most of their coins, I bid 1–2 on letters I want because they often bid 0; my record shows late letters going for 0–1 against a drained opponent.

I never spend my last coins on a letter worth 1 unless it completes a specific lexicon word I already have the rest of. Bidding all-in on a final T or A that doesn't complete a word just converts coin points into nothing.

I avoid exact ties on letters I actually need — a tie wastes the letter entirely. If I genuinely want it, I bid one above the opponent's apparent pattern rather than matching it.

On the final letter, leftover coins have no auction use, but they still score at settlement, so I bid only up to the point where winning beats keeping the coins; I do not automatically dump everything.

At word submission, I enumerate every lexicon word spellable from my letters and pick the one with the highest letter sum, not the longest-looking one; with M in hand I check M-words first, and if nothing is spellable I submit none rather than guess.

I ignore pre-game threats and notices entirely; they don't change the math of bids, coins, or lexicon validity.
