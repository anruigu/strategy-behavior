**Pledge stage, when both opponents have been scouting every round (the common pattern): pledge raid and act raid.** A raid on a scouting East transfers 5 points to me and costs only 2 fuel — far better than scouting's 3 points. In past games I repeatedly pledged raid and then chickened out at the act stage; that hesitation cost me roughly 5 points per round. If East and West's pattern is scout/scout, raiding is strictly dominant and I should commit at the pledge stage and follow through.

- Plan 5 rounds with 8 fuel: roughly raid rounds 1–3 (6 fuel), then scout rounds 4–5 (2 fuel), ending at 0 fuel with ~15+ points from raids plus stolen points. Do not leave fuel unspent unless nothing profitable is affordable.

**When someone might raid me (West raids clockwise at North):** escort is fine if I expect to be raided, but only if I actually act escort. A scout that gets raided scores 0. If West has pledged raid, escort (2 points guaranteed) beats scouting (0 if raided).

Any reasoning about "gathering information" or "flexibility" at the act stage is noise — the pledges are already public and the act is locked.

**Against this opponent pool:** they scout almost every round regardless of what I do. Exploit that by raiding consistently, honoring every pledge, and never triggering the mutual-raid collapse described in the notice.
