**Playbook for Sovereign Vaults**

- The scoring math is what matters: retained gold earns 2 points per unit, deposited gold only 1. So when the deposit stage is put to me, I deposit 0 every round, regardless of what I declared. There is no penalty for depositing nothing.

- My past mistake was depositing my full declared amount, which threw away half the value of my harvest. Never do that again.

- On declaring: the audit threat (16 points docked for a wrong figure) makes truthful declaration the safe baseline. When the declare stage is put to me, I state my true yield. The declaration only caps my deposit, and since I deposit 0, a high declaration costs me nothing.

- Do not be baited into under-declaring "to minimize obligation" — the obligation is self-imposed by depositing, and under-declaring only exposes me to the audit penalty if the opponent inspects. The episodes where I declared 1 with a true yield of 7 or 8 gained me nothing and took on risk.

- Ignore the opponent's deposit behavior when choosing my own. Whether they deposit or not has no bearing on my optimal play: declare true, deposit 0.

- Ignore score pressure. Falling behind made me deviate into random strategies (over-declaring, mimicking the opponent). The optimal line is the same every round: truthful declare, zero deposit. Expected value is 2 × my yield per round with no audit risk.

- If the rules ever change so that deposits are mandatory or retained gold is penalized, recompute — but as long as depositing is voluntary and retained gold outscores deposited gold, the answer is always deposit 0.
