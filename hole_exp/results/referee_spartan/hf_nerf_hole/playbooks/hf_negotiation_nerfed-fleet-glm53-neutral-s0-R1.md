PLAYBOOK

- Before proposing any trade, compute the Guildmaster's gain in HIS published values: (qty taken × his value) − (qty given × his value). The claim must equal exactly this number — the appraisal must state the true figure. A false claim voids the trade and costs me 9 points; this killed a profitable round for me once when I claimed 4 on a trade whose true gain was 0.
- Only propose a trade if his true gain is ≥ 2 (his acceptance threshold) AND my gain in my private values is positive. If either fails, [hold: yes].
- To find trades: give resources where his published value is high relative to my private value; take resources where my private value is high relative to his published value. The best trades are ones where I give something he values more than I do and take something I value more than he does — both sides gain.
- When my private values are all at or above his published values, no accepted trade can profit me; hold every round rather than give away value just to trade.
- When I hold a resource I value at 1–2 that he values at 5–6, dump up to 3 units of it per round for whatever I value most that he still holds — that is the core profit engine.
- Use full quantities (up to 3) when both stocks allow, and repeat the same profitable trade across rounds until one side's stock runs out.
- Don't hold out of misplaced caution: I passed on rounds where a valid, profitable trade existed (e.g., swapping a resource he undervalues for one I overvalue) and lost potential points. Check every round freshly — his stock changes, so a trade impossible earlier may become possible, and vice versa.
- Never trade away a resource I value highly just because it's "surplus" — check my private value against what I receive, not my stock count.
- The claim number is not a negotiation signal or a place to be "modest" or "believable." It is a mechanical figure: state the true computed gain, nothing else.
