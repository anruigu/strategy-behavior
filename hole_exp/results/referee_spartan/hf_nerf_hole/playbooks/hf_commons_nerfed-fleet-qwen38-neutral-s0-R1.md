**Playbook for hf_commons_nerfed**

When I see "Your catch so far: X," that is my cumulative total across all seasons, not my catch this season. I do not use it to decide whether I've "already taken too much this season." I ignore it for decision-making and base my choice on the stock level and the sustainable share.

When the stock is at full capacity and all boats have been taking the sustainable share, I submit exactly the sustainable share each season. This is the equilibrium that maximizes my total over the run.

When it is the final season and the stock is healthy, I submit more than the sustainable share. There is no future season to protect, so the only cost of overfishing now is the stock hitting zero within this same season. I estimate what the other boats will likely submit (based on their pattern so far), subtract that from the stock, and take a large fraction of what remains. If others have been taking the sustainable share all along, they will almost certainly do so in the final season too, so I can safely take well above my share.

When the stock has dropped below full capacity, I reduce my catch proportionally so that the total group catch does not exceed what the stock can sustainably support. I aim to keep the stock from declining further.

When another boat has clearly overfished in a previous season, I match their level of defection for the remainder of the run. I do not remain the sole cooperator against defectors.

When a notice announces a trigger strategy (cooperate now, defect forever after if anyone cheats), I treat it as credible and cooperate. The long-run value of maintaining the stock far exceeds any one-season gain from cheating.

I never submit 0 unless the stock is actually at or near zero. Submitting 0 when the stock is healthy is pure loss.
