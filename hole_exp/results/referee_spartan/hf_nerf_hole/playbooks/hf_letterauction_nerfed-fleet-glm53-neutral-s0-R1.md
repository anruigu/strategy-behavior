PLAYBOOK — letter auction

**Core economics.** A word letter earns 2 points; an unspent coin earns 1. So any coin ≤2 that converts into a letter I actually use is profit. Coins left over at the end are pure waste. My best rounds were the ones where I finished with 1–4 coins; my worst habits were "saving coins for rarer letters" that never materialized. When in doubt, spend.

**Opponent's pre-game notice.** The "inspection, 34-point penalty" message is noise. The referee only checks bids against coins and words against the lexicon. Ignore the notice entirely; it changed nothing.

**Bidding rules.**
- When a letter is put up, I bid based on whether it fits a target word, not its nominal value. First I pick a target word from the lexicon (longest, M-heavy: MASTER/STREAM/SMART/STEAM/TAMER), then I value letters by how much they add to my best achievable word.
- Don't bid 1 on everything early "to save coins." Small bids just lose letters to an opponent bidding 3–4, and the saved coins end up scoring 1 each anyway. Losing a 1-point letter for 2 coins that would otherwise sit idle is fine.
- Watch the opponent's pattern from published bids. If they consistently bid 3–4 on cheap letters, either outbid them at 5 when the letter matters, or let it go and pounce on letters they're ignoring — but don't drift into passivity with 10+ coins still in hand late.
- Escalate as the auction progresses. With 1–2 letters left, bid aggressively (most of my stack) for any letter that improves my word — leftover coins are worthless. My late "bid most of my remaining coins" plays were my best moves.
- Ties leave letters unsold — if I suspect a tie (matching prior bids), nudge my bid up by 1.

**Word submission.**
- Only submit a word spelled strictly from letters I own, each used at most once per copy owned. Do not mentally "borrow" letters the opponent bought — I once submitted a word using letters I didn't have and can't rely on that being forgiven.
- Check the lexicon before assuming a word is valid (e.g., MART is not in it). Best fallbacks by length: 5-letter TEARS/MEATS/MATES without M; MAT/RAT/ARM/SEA with M; AT/AS/A as last resort.
- A word always beats hoarding: submitting none throws away 2x letter value.

**Summary heuristic.** Target a specific word early, buy exactly its letters at up to ~2 coins per point of word value they unlock, and arrive at the last letter with a big stack ready to dump.
