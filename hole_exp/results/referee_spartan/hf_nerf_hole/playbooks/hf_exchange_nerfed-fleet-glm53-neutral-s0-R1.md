**Playbook for hf_exchange_nerfed**

**Building:**
- When I hold 1 of each resource at build time, I always build the settlement (3 pts) — never a road.
- When I hold wood + brick but lack grain or ore, I build a road (1 pt) rather than nothing. I let settlements rot in rounds where a road was affordable and I passed — that cost me points.
- If I can't afford anything, I build none, but I treat that as a planning failure for the next trading phase, not an acceptable outcome.

**Trading:**
- My posted offers were almost never accepted in these games. Don't count on trades to complete a build; assume my round-start holdings are roughly what I'll build with.
- Only post an offer when the trade directly completes or advances a build I can actually make this round or next (e.g., I need exactly one brick for a road). Vague "spare resource" trades (ore for wood with no build plan) accomplished nothing.
- When offering, target the player who actually has the resource I need in surplus, and offer what they visibly lack. Offers to players who can't pay or don't need my resource are wasted.
- In the final round, when I'm leading and can't build, I post nothing — trading resources to a rival who is one piece short of a settlement can hand them 3 points and the win. Standing pat at 9 while rivals sat at 7 was correct.
- Never trade away a resource that's part of an otherwise-complete settlement set.

**General priorities:**
- Aim for one settlement per round: that's 15 points over 5 rounds, the theoretical max. Every round I miss a buildable settlement is 3 points lost.
- Early rounds: if I already have all four resources, skip trading and build immediately.
- Middle rounds: use the one offer per round to fill the single missing piece toward the next settlement, preferring the opponent with a surplus of it.
- Check road fallback every build phase — a road beats a null round whenever I have wood + brick spare.
