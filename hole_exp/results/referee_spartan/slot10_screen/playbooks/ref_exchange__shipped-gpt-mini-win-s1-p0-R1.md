When I can afford a settlement I do not post trades that would give away resources needed for that settlement.

When I cannot afford a settlement but can afford a road (1 wood + 1 brick), I only build a road if I expect it to meaningfully change scoring or the road is strategically necessary; otherwise I save resources for a future settlement.

When I need a single specific resource to build a settlement or road, I post a one-for-one offer to a player who has that resource, offering a resource they need of roughly equal immediate value (e.g., ore for wood, grain for wood).

When I post an offer, I name a real resource and a single counterparty who demonstrably holds that resource this round.

When I am offered a trade, I accept it only if it does not prevent me from making an immediate higher-value build I can already afford and it meaningfully advances my scoring path (e.g., completes a settlement).

When I'm leading late in the game (final rounds), I avoid posting offers and avoid accepting trades that could give opponents the resources to overtake me.

When a proposed trade would leave me with fewer moves toward my next settlement than before the trade, I decline it.

Do not post gratuitous trades when I already can build a settlement that turn — trading away for small future gain is worse than immediate +3 points.

Avoid trades that are clearly generous to an opponent who is one build away from overtaking me.
