When I'm dealt starting resources, if I can build a settlement immediately, build it unless doing so leaves me with zero brick and I expect brick will be scarce next rounds; otherwise prefer the settlement for immediate points.

When I can afford a settlement, choose it over a road unless I specifically need a single point to tie/win.

When I'm missing one resource for a desired build, post a 1-for-1 offer to the player who has that resource, offering a resource they need (prioritize giving wood before ore).

When another player posts an offer giving me brick for my ore, accept it if I can spare the ore and it enables me to build next turn—brick is high priority.

When no one currently has brick, post offers to trade ore for brick rather than grain or wood, because securing brick unlocks both roads and settlements.

When I lack brick and no one has it, avoid spending my ore on low-value trades that don't get me brick; preserve trading currency (ore) for the critical brick trade.

When I have spare wood and someone else has grain I need for a build, offer 1 wood for 1 grain—wood is easiest to spare early.

If I cannot build this turn and no useful trades are possible, do not force offers; conserve resources and wait for better trading opportunities.

Do not trade away all my ore if doing so prevents accepting good future offers or building an ore-reliant strategy.

If an offer will let me score immediately (settlement/road), prioritize accepting or making that trade even if it costs a resource I might otherwise prefer to keep.
