When I can already afford a settlement this turn, I post no offer.

When in the building phase I can afford a settlement I build the settlement (prefer the immediate 3 points over delaying).

When I lack exactly one resource to complete a settlement, I post a single 1:1 offer to a specific player who has that resource, offering a resource that player appears to lack or has in surplus.

When I lack wood but have surplus grain, I offer 1 grain for 1 wood to players who show wood in their holdings; analogously, when I lack brick but have surplus ore (or vice versa) I offer 1 ore for 1 brick (or 1 brick for 1 ore) to the player who is the observable source of the needed resource.

When choosing which counterparty to target, prefer the player who both holds the needed resource and has less need for what I am offering (so the trade is more likely and less likely to enable their own high-value build).

When multiple offers are posted to me, I accept the combination that lets me complete the highest-value build available this round (settlement > road). If more than one accepted combination yields the same immediate point gain, pick the one that preserves scarce resources needed for future settlements.

Do not post trades that would give opponents a resource they need to immediately build a settlement, unless the trade is necessary for me to build a settlement that same round.

Do not accept trades that hand away a unique scarce resource (for example my last wood or last brick) for a low-value return unless doing so enables a strictly higher immediate point outcome this round.

When I cannot build this round and no single 1:1 trade will get me a settlement next round, conserve flexible resources (grain/ore) and only post exact 1:1 swaps that materially improve chances of building next turn.

Avoid multi-resource or multi-party offers—use simple 1:1 offers naming a specific counterparty and resource to keep trades predictable and avoid empowering opponents unnecessarily.

If offered identical trades by multiple players, decline if accepting would give away a scarce resource or if I can build without trading; otherwise accept the one that best preserves flexibility (keep ore/grain over wood/brick when planning settlements).
