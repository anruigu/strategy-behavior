When I can afford a settlement, choose it over a road unless I specifically need a single point to tie/win.

When another player posts an offer giving me brick for my ore, accept it if I can spare the ore and it enables me to build on the upcoming build phase—brick is high priority.

When evaluating incoming offers, only accept trades that (a) enable an immediate build that increases my score that round, or (b) clearly set up a guaranteed build next round given known holdings; do not accept trades that leave me unable to build when the trade's goal was to enable building.

When no one currently has brick, post offers to trade ore for brick rather than grain or wood, because securing brick unlocks both roads and settlements; however, prefer to give wood before ore unless wood is scarce for me.

When I lack brick and no one has it, avoid spending my ore on low-value trades that don't get me brick; preserve ore as a trading currency for critical brick trades or future city upgrades.

When I have spare wood and someone else has grain I need for a build, offer 1 wood for 1 grain—wood is easiest to spare early.

If I cannot build this turn and no useful trades are possible, do not force offers; conserve resources and wait for better trading opportunities.

Do not trade away all my ore if doing so prevents accepting good future offers or building an ore-reliant strategy.

If an offer will let me score immediately (settlement/road), prioritize accepting or making that trade even if it costs a resource I might otherwise prefer to keep.

When posting offers, target the player who both (a) holds the needed resource and (b) lacks the resource I am offering; this increases acceptance chance.

When multiple players post identical offers to me, prefer accepting the one that uses a resource I can most spare and that leaves me best positioned to build in the same or next build phase.

Avoid accepting offers that exchange a resource I need for a guaranteed build (e.g., wood needed to finish a settlement) unless the incoming resource immediately completes that build.

If aiming for a city (ore+grain), do not accept trades that remove my only wood/brick if those are needed to secure a nearer settlement point; compare point gain timelines before accepting.

When in doubt between giving wood or ore in a 1-for-1 trade, give wood unless I foresee an ore-heavy build (city) within the next two turns.
