# Playbook for ref_exchange__shipped

**On Settlement vs. Road Priority**
When deciding what to build, always prioritize settlements (3 points) over roads (1 point) unless you're blocked by missing resources. The 3x point multiplier makes settlements vastly more efficient in a 5-round game. Build settlements whenever you have all four required resources.

**On Resource Trading to Enable Building**
When you identify that one resource blocks you from building a high-value structure (like needing just one wood for a settlement), actively seek trades to acquire it. Post offers that give away your abundant resources to get the missing piece. Don't hoard resources speculatively if they prevent immediate scoring.

**On Rejecting Unfavorable Trades**
When offered trades that don't move you closer to building, reject them. This includes trading away resources that are required components for your next settlement, even if the trade seems balanced. Grain and brick are particularly valuable as settlement components.

**On Resource Scarcity and Build Readiness**
Pay close attention to whether you actually have the resources to execute your intended trades and builds. In round 2, I thought I had 1 wood + 1 brick + 2 grain + 2 ore but my holdings were 0 wood + 1 brick + 2 grain + 2 ore. Verify holdings before committing to a trading strategy.

**On Late-Game Resource Drought**
By the final rounds, resources may dry up entirely despite earlier abundance. Don't assume that trading away or using resources early will be compensated by later yields. If you're holding only 1 ore in round 5 with no wood, brick, or grain, it's because earlier trades or builds exhausted your liquidity. Plan accordingly—prioritize building settlements as soon as possible rather than banking on future draws.

**On Endgame Realism**
In the final round, build any structure you have the resources for—but only if you actually have them. In round 5, I correctly recognized I had no valid builds with only 1 ore. Don't force a build that isn't possible. However, this situation should be avoided earlier: if you know you're in a 5-round game with 3 settlements as the goal, secure your second and third settlements by round 4 at the latest, not round 5.

**On Aggressive Early Settling**
Aim to build your first settlement in round 1 (maximizes points across 5 rounds) and your second by round 3. Each settlement postponed costs you points over the remaining turns. Trading to complete a settlement is usually worth it if you have even one abundant resource to give up.

**On Offensive Trading Against Leaders**
When one opponent has a significant point lead, consider whether you can trade with them to weaken their position while strengthening yours, but only if it clearly advances your own score this round. Don't sacrifice points just to hurt the leader.
