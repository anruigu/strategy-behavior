If I can currently build only a road but can plausibly reach a settlement next turn (through likely trades or minor yields), prefer to aim for the settlement rather than taking the road.

Never post an offer that spends resources I need to build in the current build phase.

When I have a single-resource surplus and need exactly one of some other resource to build next turn, post a 1-for-1 offer trading that surplus for the needed resource to a specific player who currently has that resource.

When posting an offer, always name a real counterparty who has shown the resource recently; avoid anonymous or open offers.

Prioritize trading away common excesses (wood, grain, ore) to obtain a missing common building resource (especially brick) rather than hoarding the excess.

If an offered trade would give an opponent the exact final resource to build immediately while only marginally helping me, refuse unless I gain at least equivalent immediate benefit.

Accept posted offers that give me a needed resource and only ask for something I can spare, provided acceptance does not enable an opponent to build immediately at my expense.

When multiple posted offers together would let me build next round, accept all net-beneficial offers that still leave me able to build (do not accept offers that leave me short in the build phase).

Avoid making any trade that would leave my recorded holdings negative at the end of the trading phase; do not accept trades that create or worsen negative balances.

When I need a specific scarce resource (e.g., ore to fix a negative), prioritize offering grain or another spare resource to a player who holds that scarce resource rather than offering resources that would enable them to build.

Do not rely on accumulating only wood (or any single resource) — secure brick (and other distinct build components) early through targeted trades so I can convert holdings into points.

Keep offers simple and 1-for-1 where possible; complex multi-resource trades are less likely to be accepted and harder to coordinate in short rounds.
