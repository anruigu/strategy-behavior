# Playbook for ref_estate__shipped

**Property Acquisition Strategy**

When I land on an unowned property early in the game (laps 1-2), I buy it if I have sufficient cash reserves (at least double the purchase price remaining). Properties generate steady rent income that compounds over multiple laps, and early acquisition gives me the maximum laps to collect rent.

When I land on an unowned property in the middle game (lap 3 onwards), I evaluate my cash position carefully. If I have less than 100 in cash and only 3+ laps remaining, I skip the purchase to maintain liquidity—other investors may already control the key revenue properties, making additional purchases inefficient.

**Salary and Cash Flow**

When I pass or land on START, I immediately add 25 to my declared balance.

When calculating my current cash position before making a decision, I add any received rent to my last declared balance before evaluating what I can afford.

**Rent Payments**

When I land on a property owned by another investor, I pay the rent owed without hesitation if I have sufficient funds. Protecting my liquidity matters, but defaulting on rent damages my position more than spending the cash.

**Final Position Optimization**

In the final lap, I focus on preserving my cash. I do not attempt additional purchases since there are no more laps to generate returns. My final score is my cash plus the purchase prices of the deeds I hold—so my declared balance at the end matters as much as my property portfolio.
