# Estate Game Playbook

When I land on an unowned property in early laps (laps 1-2), I will buy it if I have at least 80-100 cash remaining after purchase. Property ownership generates ongoing rent income and counts toward final score, making early acquisitions valuable.

When I land on an unowned property in mid-to-late laps (laps 3+), I will only buy if I have cash reserves of at least 60-80 after purchase. By this point, I need liquidity to handle rent payments without going broke.

When I land on an owned property and owe rent, I will pay immediately and declare my reduced balance accurately.

When I land on a property I own, I will do nothing—no payment is owed.

When I pass START, I will collect my salary and add it to my balance.

When deciding whether to buy a property, I will not overextend myself early just to acquire deeds. The game rewards both deed value AND cash on hand. A low cash position leaves me vulnerable to unexpected rent demands.

When I have less than 60 cash remaining and am past lap 2, I will not buy additional properties unless I am confident other players won't land on my properties before I can replenish my balance.

I will track the property holdings of other players so I can anticipate which properties are still available and which rent obligations might come my way.

I will aim to maintain a working cash reserve rather than depleting myself to own multiple properties early. My previous game ended with solid scoring (292), but I should have attempted to purchase more properties in mid-game when my balance allowed it, rather than being overly cautious.
