When I can afford a settlement, I build it immediately (use 1 wood, 1 brick, 1 grain, 1 ore) to maximize points now.

When I cannot build a settlement but can build a road with a single trade’s worth of resources, I prioritize acquiring 1 wood or 1 brick to make that road (1 wood + 1 brick → 1 point).

When I lack wood or brick that are necessary for the next settlement, I post offers asking for wood or brick, giving grain first, then ore if necessary (post: to Pk, give 1 grain, get 1 wood/brick).

When I have only one resource type blocking a build (e.g., missing wood), I accept an offered 1-for-1 if it completes an immediate build.

When an offered trade gives me a resource that will not enable a build this round and costs my last grain (or my last of any scarce build resource), I decline — do not trade away the flexible resource that blocks future builds unless the incoming resource lets me score immediately.

When I can spare grain and the incoming ore (or other resource) will enable an immediate or very likely next-round build, accept; otherwise prefer to keep grain for settlement costs and trading flexibility.

When I have no feasible build this round (no wood and no brick and no way to trade to get both), do not post trades that give opponents what they need for immediate points — avoid helping opponents complete builds unless it directly enables a higher-value return for me.

When offers are posted to me, accept multiple if each independently improves my ability to build this round; otherwise accept none.

Do not post any offer when I already have the exact resources to build the highest-scoring available structure (no trade if settlement is affordable).

If trading patterns in the game show that opponents are repeatedly exchanging grain for ore or wood for grain, exploit that by offering the resource they’re repeatedly giving up to get the resource I need.

If I end a midgame with several grains but no wood/brick, recognize that hoarding grain alone did not work — prioritize converting grain into missing building materials earlier.
