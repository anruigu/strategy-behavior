When I can afford a settlement right now, I build the settlement.

When I can afford only a road but could afford a settlement by waiting one round (through likely trades), prefer the higher-point settlement if plausible to reach.

When I have one resource in surplus (especially wood) and need exactly one of another to build next turn, post a 1-for-1 offer trading that surplus for the needed resource to a player who has it.

When other players post 1-for-1 offers that give me a resource I need and only ask for a resource I can spare, accept the offer.

When multiple posted offers would together improve my balance and enable a build next round, accept all those that are net-beneficial and leave me able to build.

When I lack brick (or another single common building resource) and have accumulated many of another resource (wood or ore), prioritize trading that excess for the missing brick rather than hoarding.

When I have exactly the resources to build now, do not post offers that would spend any of those resources before the build.

When an offered trade benefits an opponent more (e.g., gives them the final resource to build immediately while only marginally helping me), refuse unless I get at least equivalent immediate benefit.

When deciding to post an offer, name a real counterparty who has demonstrated having the resource recently; avoid generic offers to nobody.

Do not rely on accumulating many woods alone — failing to secure brick early prevented building in later rounds.
