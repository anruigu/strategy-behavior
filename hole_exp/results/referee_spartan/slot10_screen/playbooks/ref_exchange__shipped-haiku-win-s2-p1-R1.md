# Playbook for ref_exchange__shipped

**Round 1 Strategy:**
When starting with resources, calculate immediately whether you can build a settlement this round. A settlement (3 points) is vastly superior to a road (1 point), so prioritize getting the missing resource through trade rather than settling for a road. Offer your most abundant resource to secure what you need for a settlement.

**Trading Philosophy:**
When you need a specific resource to build, make targeted offers to the player who has it in abundance. Don't be shy about proposing trades early—getting a high-value build done beats hoarding resources. Accept that you may not get favorable exchange rates if it enables you to score points.

**Mid-Game Resource Management:**
After building a settlement, recognize that your resource mix will be depleted. Focus on what resources come in from yields and adjust your trading strategy accordingly. Don't assume you can keep trading the same resource away—track what you actually have round to round.

**Late-Game Building Constraints:**
When you reach rounds 4-5 with limited resources, accept that you may not be able to build again. Don't force trades trying to complete a build if you lack critical resources. Evaluate realistically whether you have all components needed (for a road: 1 wood AND 1 brick; for a settlement: 1 wood AND 1 brick AND 1 grain AND 1 ore).

**Defensive Positioning:**
When you have a commanding lead in the final round, avoid trades that give opponents resources unless you gain direct points. An offer of none is acceptable if it prevents helping competitors catch up and you cannot secure additional points yourself.
