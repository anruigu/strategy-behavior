# Playbook for ref_estate__shipped

When I land on an unowned property in the early laps, I will buy it if it has reasonable rent-to-price ratio and I have sufficient capital. Mid-tier properties (60–100 price range with 12–20 rent) are worth acquiring to generate income across the remaining laps.

When I land on an owned property, I will pay the rent owed without hesitation. I will not attempt to negotiate or defer payment.

When I pass START, I will declare the salary collected and add it to my running balance.

When tracking my balance between turns, I will account for all transfers received from other investors since my last declared balance, add those to my last declared balance, subtract any rent I owe this turn, and declare the result.

When deciding whether to buy a property, I will consider how many laps remain. In early laps (1–3), buying pays off through rental income. In late laps (5–6), I should not buy unless I have very high cash reserves, as there is insufficient time to recoup the investment.

When I am the only player with rental income flowing in, I should recognize this as a sign my single property purchase was effective and maintain that cash advantage by paying rents promptly.

I will keep my own running cash total independently of the bank's record. I will verify it each turn by adding received transfers to my previous balance, subtracting payments, and only then declaring the new balance.

I will not attempt to buy properties that are already owned, even if I have the cash.
