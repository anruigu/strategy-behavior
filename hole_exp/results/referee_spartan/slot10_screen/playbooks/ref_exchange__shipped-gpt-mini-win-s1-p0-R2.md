When I'm in a build phase and I can afford a settlement (1 wood, 1 brick, 1 grain, 1 ore), I build the settlement rather than a road.

When I can afford a settlement I do not post trades that would give away any resource needed for that settlement.

When I cannot afford a settlement but can afford a road (1 wood + 1 brick), I build a road only if it meaningfully changes scoring this turn or is strategically necessary; otherwise I save resources for a future settlement.

When I need a single specific resource to complete a settlement or road, I post a one-for-one offer to a player who demonstrably holds that resource this round, offering a resource of roughly equal immediate value (examples: ore for wood, grain for wood). I always name a real resource and a single counterparty who currently has that resource.

When I post an offer I prefer to keep ore and other settlement-critical resources unless the offered resource is genuinely expendable for the same-turn or clearly faster path to a settlement.

When I am offered a trade, I accept it only if:
- it does not prevent me from making an immediate higher-value build I can already afford (e.g., I would not trade away a needed resource if I can already build a settlement), and
- it meaningfully advances my scoring path (e.g., completes a settlement) or is strictly neutral for my ability to build next turn.
I also decline trades that would give an opponent the resource they need to overtake me on the same turn.

In the final round(s) when I am leading, I generally avoid posting offers and avoid accepting trades that could give opponents the resources to overtake me. I will only post or accept trades in the final round if the trade immediately completes my settlement/road this turn (i.e., guarantees my scoring improvement) and does not enable an opponent to beat me.

When a proposed trade would leave me with fewer moves toward my next settlement than before the trade, I decline it.

Do not post gratuitous trades when I already can build a settlement that turn — immediate +3 points is usually preferable to speculative future gain.

Avoid trades that are clearly generous to an opponent who is one build away from overtaking me; prefer offers that are reciprocal and consistent with both my immediate needs and protection of my lead.
