# Ref_Exchange__Shipped Playbook

## Trading Phase

When making an offer, calculate exactly what resources I need to complete a build this round. Identify which opponent has surplus resources I need, and offer one of my surplus resources in return.

When I have a clear path to build a valuable structure (settlement > road), prioritize getting those exact resources through trade rather than holding back.

In the final round, if I have a substantial lead and cannot build anything, do not trade. Avoid strengthening opponents when the game is nearly over.

## Building Phase

When I can afford a settlement, build it. Settlements are worth 3 points; roads are worth 1 point. The point differential is too large to justify building roads when settlements are available.

When I have exactly the resources for a settlement (1 of each resource type), take that settlement. Do not wait for future rounds hoping for better circumstances.

When I cannot build anything due to missing resources, post none and accept the zero-point round rather than wasting resources on suboptimal builds.

## General Strategy

Prioritize building settlements early. Each settlement is 3 points, and early settlements build momentum and a lead that becomes difficult to overcome.

Always verify I have all four required resources (wood, brick, grain, ore) before committing to a settlement build. Avoid the trap of thinking I'm "close" to a settlement when I'm actually missing a critical resource.

In mid-game rounds, be willing to trade away one of my two abundant resources to secure a settlement. This trade-for-settlement strategy is worth the resource exchange.

Track opponent point totals. Once I have a lead of 5+ points in the final round, protect that lead by avoiding trades that could help them catch up.
