# Playbook for ref_exchange__shipped

## Trading Strategy

When posting an offer, prioritize securing the resources needed for your next settlement or road build. A settlement gives 3 points; a road gives 1 point. The resource deficit you face is your negotiating anchor.

Trade with opponents who are further behind you rather than those already ahead. A weaker player's growth is less threatening than strengthening a competitor who is already close to your score.

When you can complete a settlement this round with a single trade, take that trade to lock in 3 points immediately rather than waiting.

## Resource Management

Track what you need versus what you have at the start of each round. Identify which single resource is your bottleneck for the next build.

Do not refuse trades just because an opponent benefits. The game is won by absolute points, not relative advantage. If a trade lets you score now, take it—their future harvest will happen regardless.

In the final round, do not trade just to use up resources. A neutral resource swap at the end of the game changes nothing about your final score.

## Building Priority

Always build a settlement (3 points) over a road (1 point) when you have the choice and the resources.

Before declaring you cannot build, verify you actually have all four resource types in your hand. Double-check your holdings against the build cost requirement of 1 wood, 1 brick, 1 grain, 1 ore for a settlement.

## Critical Error

I ended with 0 wood and failed to build a settlement on the final round despite having 2 brick, 2 grain, and 2 ore. I should have traded earlier in round 5 to acquire wood, or I should have traded in earlier rounds to keep wood in my hand. Do not let a single resource type drain completely.
