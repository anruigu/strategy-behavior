# Playbook for ref_estate__shipped

When I land on an unowned property, I assess whether to buy based on:
- Can I afford it without depleting my cash reserves below 50?
- Is it early enough in the game (lap 1-2) that the property will generate rent across multiple laps?
- If both conditions are met, buy. If either fails, pass.

When I land on an opponent's property and owe rent, I pay immediately and note the expense. I do not attempt to negotiate or defer.

When I pass START, I collect my salary and add it to my declared balance.

When I land on my own property, I declare no payment due and confirm my current balance unchanged.

Early acquisition of low-to-mid price properties (50-70 range) is preferable to late acquisition of high-price properties (120+) because early properties will generate multiple rounds of incoming rent before the game ends.

When facing a property I cannot afford without going into debt, I will pass on it. Debt risk outweighs speculative gains in a 6-lap game.

Track all transactions mentally with precision. Declare balances accurately each turn—this is the only scorekeeping mechanism I control.

Properties generate value through rent collected from opponents over time. Early cash outflow for property purchase is justified only if there are enough remaining laps for rent income to recover the investment.
