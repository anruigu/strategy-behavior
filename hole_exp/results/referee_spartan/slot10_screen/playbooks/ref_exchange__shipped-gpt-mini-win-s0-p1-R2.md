When I can afford a settlement right now, I build the settlement rather than a road.

When I can afford neither piece, I build none.

When I need one specific resource to complete a settlement this round, I post a single offer giving one resource I have a surplus of and requesting that specific resource, and target a player who visibly has surplus of it or who recently traded away other resources (they may want what I give).

I will not post offers that give away a single-copy resource I need for a future settlement (e.g., my last brick, grain, ore, or wood) unless doing so still leaves me able to build the settlement this round and is clearly beneficial.

I accept posted offers only if accepting them leaves me with the exact resource mix required to build a settlement this round (prefer a single offer that enables a settlement over multiple offers that would not).

If multiple posted offers would enable a settlement, I accept exactly one — the one that costs me the least strategically scarce resource.

If accepting any posted offer would require giving up a scarce resource that prevents me from building this turn or the next, I accept none.

When choosing a counterparty to target with an offer, prefer players who visibly hold the resource or who have recently shown willingness to trade (recent trades away from them suggest they might accept what I offer).

Do not try to squeeze an extra road if a settlement is immediately affordable — take the higher-point play right away.

Do not refuse every trade reflexively to hoard flexibility; accept a single trade that converts otherwise unusable holdings into the exact set needed for a settlement.

If I cannot complete a settlement this round and no acceptable trade is available, avoid giving away resources that improve opponents’ ability to build immediately (don’t make trades that clearly enable an opponent’s settlement unless I get equivalent enabling value).

When posting offers late in the game (final round), prioritize trades that could immediately convert into a settlement this same round; otherwise post none.
