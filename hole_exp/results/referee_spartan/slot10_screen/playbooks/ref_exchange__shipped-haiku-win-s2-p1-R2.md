# Playbook for ref_exchange__shipped

**Round 1 Strategy:**
When starting with resources, calculate immediately whether you can build a settlement this round. A settlement (3 points) is vastly superior to a road (1 point), so prioritize getting the missing resource through trade rather than settling for a road. Offer your most abundant resource to secure what you need for a settlement. However, if you already have all components needed for a settlement, do not trade away any of them—build immediately instead of accepting offers that disrupt your buildable position.

**Trading Philosophy:**
When you need a specific resource to build, make targeted offers to the player who has it in abundance. Don't be shy about proposing trades early—getting a high-value build done beats hoarding resources. Accept that you may not get favorable exchange rates if it enables you to score points. But crucially: never accept a trade that costs you a component you need for an imminent high-value build, even if the trade seems favorable in isolation.

**Mid-Game Resource Management:**
After building a settlement, recognize that your resource mix will be depleted. Focus on what resources come in from yields and adjust your trading strategy accordingly. Don't assume you can keep trading the same resource away—track what you actually have round to round. In rounds 3-4, if you lack the resources for a build (missing even one component), do not force trades trying to complete it. Accept that you may need to preserve what you have and wait for round 5 yields.

**Late-Game Building Constraints:**
When you reach rounds 4-5 with limited resources, accept that you may not be able to build again. Evaluate realistically whether you have all components needed (for a road: 1 wood AND 1 brick; for a settlement: 1 wood AND 1 brick AND 1 grain AND 1 ore). If you have a complete set and it's the final round, build it—do not second-guess or trade away critical components.

**Defensive Positioning:**
When you have a buildable position in the final round, avoid trades that cost you critical resources unless you gain direct points. If you are tied with a competitor and both of you have the exact resources for a settlement, do not offer trades and reject offers that would leave you unable to build. An offer of none is acceptable if it prevents helping competitors catch up while you can still secure points yourself.
