When posting an offer, assess whether the trade moves me closer to a complete building set rather than just improving one resource in isolation. Before posting any offer, identify which single resource is blocking me from building, and only trade away resources I have in surplus relative to that constraint.

When I have a choice between trading partners, favor the player who has the resource I'm missing, but only if the trade is genuinely balanced. Don't assume acceptance; consider whether they benefit enough to actually say yes.

When an offer is made to me, evaluate it against my immediate building goal, not just resource diversity. A trade that feels balanced may still move me away from my target.

## Building Phase

When I cannot build because I lack one resource type, do not build a road instead—one point is not worth the wasted wood and brick when I might have no way to recover them later.

Identify my single bottleneck resource early in the game. Track which other players have it. Plan my trades multiple rounds ahead to accumulate that resource, not just react round-to-round.

In the final round especially, do not assume I'll find a trading partner for a critical resource. By round 5, if I don't have brick, I almost certainly won't get it.

Settlements are worth 3 points; roads are worth 1. Direct all early trading toward settlement-building, not incremental roads.
