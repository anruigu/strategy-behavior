# Playbook for ref_exchange__shipped

**On opening rounds and resource positioning:**
When I have resources to build a settlement immediately, I build it rather than speculating on trades. Three points is a significant lead early on, and the certainty of those points outweighs the risk of failed trading strategies.

**On evaluating trades offered to me:**
When someone offers me a resource I'm short on for a resource I have in abundance, I accept it if it enables a build I couldn't otherwise make. A trade that converts 1 grain (abundant) into 1 wood (scarce) is worth accepting because it puts me in a position to build immediately.

**On final-round decision-making:**
In the final round, maximize my score by building whatever I can. A settlement is worth 3 points versus a road's 1 point. Even if I'm already leading, building in round 5 is my last chance to gain points. Calculate exactly what resources I need and commit to the highest-value build.

**On settlement vs. road choices:**
When I have the option to build either a road or a settlement with the same investment (1 wood + 1 brick), I build the settlement every time. The three points versus one point is a massive efficiency difference and should never be a difficult choice.

**On offer-posting strategy:**
When I'm in a position to build what I need without trading, I post no offer. This is especially true in later rounds where time is limited. Don't trade for the sake of trading—only trade when it enables a build I couldn't otherwise make or when it puts me in a materially better position. In the final round, posting no offer is safe if I'm already ahead and can't make a high-value build anyway.

**On tracking my own progress:**
Track my own builds explicitly each round: which round I built, what I built, and my points after each build. This prevents confusion about my actual score versus what I expected. I finished at 12 points, which likely means I built 4 settlements total (12 ÷ 3 = 4), not 3 as I initially thought.

**On resource deficits:**
Negative resource values indicate a problem. If I end a round with -1 ore, I either made an invalid trade or miscalculated. In round 5, having -1 ore prevented me from building anything. Avoid letting this happen by verifying resource balances before accepting trades and after builds.

**On opponent monitoring:**
Track what other players have at round start and what they're building. This helps me predict future trades and understand whether I'm winning or losing. Use opponent point totals to gauge whether I need aggressive building or can play conservatively.
