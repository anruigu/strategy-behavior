# Playbook for ref_exchange__shipped

**Opening strategy:** Always build a settlement in round 1 if you have the resources (1 of each). Settlements are worth 3 points versus 1 for roads, and early scoring compounds your advantage.

**Trading philosophy:** Only trade when you cannot build with current resources. Avoid giving up resources that unlock building opportunities unless you get something that enables an immediate, higher-value build.

**Identify your excess resource:** After each yield phase, note which resource you have in surplus (like my 2 ore early on). This is your trading currency, but don't trade it away unless it directly advances your position.

**Build prioritization:** Always choose settlement over road when you have the choice. A settlement's 3-point swing is worth pursuing even if it requires negotiation.

**Late-game pressure:** In the final rounds, assess whether you can build a settlement. If you can, pursue it aggressively through trading if necessary. A settlement in round 5 is worth more than any unopened opportunities.

**When you're blocked:** If you lack a critical resource for building (like I lacked wood in round 4), immediately flag that as your trading target for the next round. Don't wait for the next yield phase hoping luck will help.

**Competitive trading:** Be cautious about trading with players ahead of you in points—trades that strengthen leaders hurt you twice. When trading, prefer partners who are already losing rather than those who are winning.

**Accept good offers immediately:** If an offer lets you build on your next turn, accept it. Don't hold out for better trades that may not materialize.
