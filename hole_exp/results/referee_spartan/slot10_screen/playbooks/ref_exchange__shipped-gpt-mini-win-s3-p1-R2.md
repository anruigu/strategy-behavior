When I'm in the build phase and I can afford a settlement, I build the settlement (prefer 3 points now over a road's 1).

When I'm in the build phase and I cannot afford a settlement but can afford a road, I build the road only if the road is the best immediate-scoring option and I have no clear path to a settlement next turn.

When I'm allowed to post a trade, I post at most one 1-for-1 offer and name a specific counterparty who visibly has a surplus of the resource I need.

When I post a trade, I only offer a resource I can spare without preventing me from building this round (unless I have a clear plan and buffer). I will not trade away a resource that is uniquely required to build this turn.

When choosing trading partners, I prefer to deal with players who recently showed willingness to trade that resource (past trades) or who have visible stockpiles of it.

When an offer is posted to me, I accept it if and only if accepting enables an immediate build that increases my score, or it guarantees a higher-scoring build on the next turn without handing the opponent the win.

I decline offered trades that do not enable an immediate improvement for me and that could help the opponent score immediately or more than they help me.

When I cannot build this turn and no good trade is available (i.e., I would have to give up scarce resources or enable an opponent), I post no offer rather than making a risky or lopsided trade.

Do not accept trades that mainly benefit the opponent's ability to score immediately unless I get equal immediate benefit.

Do not make multi-resource or uneven offers unless I have a strategic reason and buffer; stick to simple 1-for-1s to keep options flexible.

If I can already afford to build this round, avoid posting offers that could be accepted and leave me short of that build.

When uncertain between building now or reserving resources for a higher future gain, prefer the guaranteed immediate points unless there is a clear, demonstrated path to a higher-scoring play next turn.
