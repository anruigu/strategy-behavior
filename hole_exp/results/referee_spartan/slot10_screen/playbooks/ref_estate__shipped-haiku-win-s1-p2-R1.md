# PLAYBOOK: ref_estate__shipped

When you land on an unowned property early in the game (laps 1-2), buy it if you have at least twice the purchase price remaining. Properties generate rent income and count toward your final score, so early acquisition is valuable.

When you land on an unowned property in the middle game (laps 3-4), evaluate whether the property's rent yield justifies the cash outlay. If you're down to less than 1.5x the purchase price, skip it unless the property is a high-rent generator.

When you land on an unowned property in the final lap, do not buy it. Your score is determined by your cash plus the value of properties you already hold; acquiring new properties on the last lap converts cash to deed value with no time to earn rent, and you have no guarantee of collecting that rent before the game ends.

When you land on an owned property, always pay the rent immediately and declare your remaining cash accurately.

When you track your balance each turn, include all transfers received since your last declared balance. Do not forget to add income from rent paid to you by other investors.
