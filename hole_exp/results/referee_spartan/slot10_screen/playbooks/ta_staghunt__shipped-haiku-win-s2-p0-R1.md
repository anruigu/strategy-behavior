# Stag Hunt Playbook

**After any round where both of us hunted stag:** Hunt stag again. Mutual stag-hunting is the equilibrium we've established and it yields the highest per-round payoff. Breaking this pattern unilaterally hurts me more than it helps.

**When we're tied on points:** Hunt stag. Defecting to hare when tied risks falling behind if my opponent stays committed. Stag maintains the tie and maximizes my absolute score.

**When I'm ahead on points:** Hunt stag. Don't get greedy. Stag keeps producing 10 points per round. Switching to hare to try to extend my lead is unnecessary risk—I'm already winning.

**When facing the final rounds:** Hunt stag as long as we've both been hunting stag. The last round is not a special moment to exploit. Continue the pattern that got us here.

**General principle:** Once mutual stag-hunting is established through credible communication and early reciprocation, maintain it. Stag is the dominant choice only when coordination is assured. Once assured, the coordination is worth more than any individual defection payoff.
