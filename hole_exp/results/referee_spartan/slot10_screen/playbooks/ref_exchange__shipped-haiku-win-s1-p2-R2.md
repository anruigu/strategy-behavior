# Playbook for ref_exchange__shipped

**Early rounds: prioritize high-value builds**
When I have the resources to build a settlement, build it immediately. Settlements are worth 3 points versus 1 for roads, and securing points early gives me a lead that compounds across rounds.

**Trading: be strategic about what you offer**
When I post an offer, ensure it genuinely helps me move toward a buildable state (having 1 of each resource for a settlement or 1 wood + 1 brick for a road). Do not make trades just to move resources around without a clear path to building.

**Trading: track what each opponent needs and protect critical resources**
Before posting an offer, check what resources each opponent currently holds and what they're likely to need. If I'm ahead or tied and an opponent is one resource away from a settlement, I should avoid giving them that resource unless I receive something equally valuable in return. Brick and ore tend to be scarcer—protect them.

**Trading: don't rely on acceptance of outbound offers**
When I post an offer, assume it may be rejected. Build my strategy around what I currently hold, not what I hope to receive. If an offer doesn't get accepted, I should still be able to explain why my original holdings were reasonable for the round.

**Recognize when you're in a weak position**
If I enter a late round with imbalanced holdings (e.g., 1 ore and nothing else), acknowledge that I've already made suboptimal trades earlier and cannot recover. Do not waste late-round efforts on trades that won't let me build. The time to fix resource imbalance is rounds 2–3, not round 5.

**Trading: consider blocking strategically but don't overcommit to it**
When tied or behind a competitor, I can consider trades that deny them resources. However, denying an opponent's build is only valuable if I can simultaneously improve my own position. Never make a trade purely to block if it leaves me unable to build.

**When you receive an offer**
Before declining an offer I cannot fulfill, double-check my holdings. More importantly, before reaching that point, never post an offer that requires resources from me that I don't actually have.

**Late game with insufficient resources**
When I have fewer than 4 resources in the final round and cannot build, accept this is the outcome of earlier decisions. Do not attempt trades that won't complete a buildable set.

**Resource balance across rounds**
After building a settlement or road, check my remaining holdings. If I'm left with one or two very abundant resources (like 3 grain) and missing critical ones (like brick), I'm in trouble for future rounds. Mid-game trades should aim to prevent this imbalance, not create it.
