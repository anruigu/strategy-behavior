When I can afford a settlement immediately, I build the settlement rather than a road.

When I can afford only a road and not a settlement, I build the road to secure at least one point.

When I cannot build anything this round, I will consider posting one targeted trade offer to the player most likely to have the resource I need, offering a single resource for a single resource.

I will not post an offer if no other player currently holds the resource I need; posting a useless offer wastes the one allowed slot.

When proposing a trade, I prefer 1-for-1 exchanges, matching noncritical surplus resources I have to the resource I lack.

When choosing which player to target, I prioritize players who have recently given away the resource type I need or who currently hold that resource, rather than players who are hoarding it.

I will not post offers that would leave me unable to build in the upcoming build phase unless the trade clearly enables a better immediate build (e.g., converts surplus into the exact missing resource for a settlement).

When an offer is posted to me that would give me a resource I need for an immediate build and asks for a resource I have in surplus, I accept.

When an offer is posted to me that would give me a resource I already have spare but asks for the only copy of a scarce resource I need for an immediate build, I refuse.

I refuse trades that would trade away my only copy of a single resource required for an immediate build (for example, my only wood when I can build a road or settlement that uses it).

I will not accept trades that do not change my ability to make a higher-value build this round or that convert a valuable long-term resource (like ore) into a lesser immediate gain unless I have clear surplus and the trade enables a higher-score build.

I avoid multi-resource offers unless I am desperate and the multi-resource trade clearly secures an immediate settlement or otherwise changes the game's outcome for me.

I do not make multi-party or vague offers; every offer names the counterparty and exact resource quantities.

If a player repeatedly trades away a resource type for a different resource type, I prioritize offering that player reciprocally when I need that resource.

If repeated small offers fail to secure the one resource I need and trading further would cost me resources needed for a higher-value build, I conserve my resources instead of escalating trades.

If no player can supply the missing resource (e.g., everyone lacks wood), I do not waste an offer and instead prepare resources for future rounds.

When I hold resources valuable for later (e.g., ore for a city/dev card) I will decline trades that give me resources I do not need now at the cost of losing the ability to build a higher-value item this round.

I check my holdings and projected build in the upcoming build phase before posting or accepting any trade; decisions prioritize ensuring I can complete the highest-value immediate build available.
