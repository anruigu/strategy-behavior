# Playbook for ref_exchange__shipped

## Trading Phase Strategy

When I post an offer, I should focus on completing resource sets that let me build immediately, not just improve flexibility. Target trades that get me to a settlement (1 of each resource) within one round if possible.

When I receive an offer, evaluate it against my immediate build potential. Don't accept trades just because they improve "balance"—accept only if they move me closer to a buildable state or if I'm ahead and can afford to help opponents less than they help me.

In the final round, don't trade away resources unless the trade directly enables a build I couldn't otherwise make. Resource conversion for its own sake wastes a turn.

## Building Phase Strategy

Always build a settlement when I can afford it (3 points is significantly better than a road's 1 point). Build roads only when I cannot build a settlement and building is still advantageous—in the final round, a road is better than nothing, but in earlier rounds, sometimes saving resources for better opportunities matters more.

## Resource Tracking and Scarcity

Pay close attention to which resources other players have and which are scarce across the table. If all players are low on wood, wood will be hard to trade for. Mid-game, I accumulated grain and brick but lost access to wood early. In endgames with low wood availability, I cannot build roads or settlements that require it.

Actively work to diversify my resource holdings in early-to-mid rounds so I'm not locked into a single resource type by the final rounds.

## Game Positioning

Watch what other players are building and their point totals. If I'm behind by more than I can realistically close, don't waste resources on marginal trades trying to catch up. If I'm competitive, identify which resources I need and pursue them directly.

Recognize when I'm in an endgame (final round especially) and stop speculating. Either I have the resources to build something that scores, or I don't. Accept that constraint rather than hoping for trades that probably won't happen.

By round 5, I had no wood and couldn't build anything despite having 4 brick and 5 grain—the only resource that mattered was the one I lacked.

Be more specific: only trade for resources I actually need to complete a build in the near term, and actively maintain some minimum of each critical resource (especially wood, needed for both roads and settlements). A surplus of one resource is only useful if I can trade it for what I actually lack.
