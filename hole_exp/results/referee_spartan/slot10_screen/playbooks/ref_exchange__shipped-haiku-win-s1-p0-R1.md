# Playbook for ref_exchange__shipped

**On settlements vs roads:** Always build settlements when you have the resources. Settlements are worth 3 points while roads are worth 1 point. The value difference is too large to ignore. Build a settlement whenever you can afford it, even if it leaves you without optimal resources for the next round.

**On trading in early rounds:** When you have a balanced hand that lets you build a settlement immediately, do not trade. Post no offer. Your balanced holdings are more valuable than any trade you can make in early rounds. Lock in your 3-point building opportunity before considering what others might need.

**On trading in middle rounds:** Evaluate whether any trade materially improves your path to building. If you're already behind on points, be willing to trade to catch up. If you're even or ahead, only trade if it's clearly in your favor. Trading with resource-rich opponents to strengthen them further is generally a losing move.

**On the final round:** With no future rounds ahead, maximize your score with what you have. Building is always better than holding. If you can build a settlement, build it. Do not avoid building in the final round for any reason related to positioning or future strategy.

**On offerings generally:** When in doubt about whether to post an offer, post none. Offering gives away information and creates opportunities for opponents without clear reciprocal benefit to you. Make your offers count for something concrete, not just to stay active.

**On being tied for the lead:** Don't play conservatively just because you're tied. The game ends in one round, so close is close. Build what you can. Getting 9 points while tied means you got outpaced by someone else who also got 9 or more.
