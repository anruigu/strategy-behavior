# Ref_Exchange__Shipped Playbook

## Trading Phase

When making an offer, calculate exactly what resources I need to complete a build this round. Identify which opponent has surplus resources I need, and offer one of my surplus resources in return.

When I have a clear path to build a valuable structure (settlement > road), prioritize getting those exact resources through trade rather than holding back.

Do not post offers unless I can realistically complete a settlement this round. Rejected offers waste no resources, but offering suboptimal or unfavorable terms signals weakness and may not even be accepted.

In the final round, if I'm tied or ahead, avoid trading to strengthen opponents. A trade that helps me reach a settlement is worth it; a trade that merely helps an opponent is not.

## Accept Phase

When I already have all resources needed for a settlement this round, reject all incoming offers. Accept trades only if they move me closer to a settlement I couldn't otherwise build.

## Building Phase

When I can afford a settlement, build it. Settlements are worth 3 points; roads are worth 1 point. The point differential is too large to justify building roads when settlements are available.

When I have exactly the resources for a settlement (1 of each resource type), take that settlement. Do not wait for future rounds hoping for better circumstances.

When I cannot build anything due to missing resources, post none and accept the zero-point round rather than wasting resources on suboptimal builds.

## General Strategy

Prioritize building settlements early. Each settlement is 3 points, and early settlements build momentum and a lead that becomes difficult to overcome.

Always verify I have all four required resources (wood, brick, grain, ore) before committing to a settlement build. Avoid the trap of thinking I'm "close" to a settlement when I'm actually missing a critical resource.

In mid-game rounds, be willing to trade away one of my two abundant resources to secure a settlement. This trade-for-settlement strategy is worth the resource exchange, but only if the trade actually secures a settlement I can build.

Track opponent point totals. In the final round, if I have a lead, protect it by avoiding trades that help opponents catch up—unless the trade is essential to building my own settlement and winning outright.

Be realistic about trade acceptance. If an opponent won't accept an offer (especially P1 with scarce ore), don't post it. Save posting offers for trades that are genuinely attractive to both sides.
