# Playbook for ref_exchange__shipped

**Opening strategy:** Always build a settlement in round 1 if you have the resources (1 of each). Settlements are worth 3 points versus 1 for roads, and early scoring compounds your advantage.

**Trading philosophy:** Only trade when you cannot build with current resources. Avoid giving up resources that unlock building opportunities unless you get something that enables an immediate, higher-value build.

**Identify your excess resource:** After each yield phase, note which resource you have in surplus. This is your trading currency, but don't trade it away unless it directly advances your position by unblocking a build.

**Build prioritization:** Always choose settlement over road when you have the choice. A settlement's 3-point swing is worth pursuing even if it requires negotiation.

**Late-game pressure:** In the final rounds, assess whether you can build a settlement. If you can, pursue it aggressively through trading if necessary. A settlement in round 4 or 5 is critical to maximizing end-game score.

**When you're blocked:** If you lack a critical resource for building, immediately flag that as your trading target for the next round. A one-resource gap (like needing ore to complete a settlement) is worth a direct trade if your partner has it.

**Trading partner selection:** When identifying a trade, check whether the partner has the resource you need AND has a surplus of it (more than they can spend on their own builds). Partners who are resource-constrained themselves cannot help you.

**Do not over-trade:** Offering multiple resources to pursue a single future build is a losing strategy. Each trade should have immediate payoff (enabling a build this round or next round at most).

**Competitive position matters less than your own builds:** Do not spend mental effort trying to block or weaken opponents. Focus entirely on whether a trade unblocks a settlement or road for you. A trade that "helps everyone" equally still helps you if it lets you build.

**Resource deficits are dangerous:** Ending a round with negative resources (like -1 ore) indicates overcommitment. In future rounds, account for your resource balance before committing to a settlement build.

**Accept offers only if they close a one-resource gap:** If an opponent offers you a resource that completes a settlement (and you would build it immediately), accept. Otherwise, decline or let it pass.

**Avoid trades that require future yields:** Only trade on the expectation that you can complete a build in the current building phase or the next round's building phase. Do not bank on multiple future yield phases to complete a trade chain.

**What did not work:** Trying to position myself strategically by offering trades to players in last place while weakening the leader was ineffective. Negotiations that don't directly unblock my builds waste the one offer I can post per round. Focus on your own path to points, not on the relative standings of others.
