When I start a round and my holdings already meet a settlement cost, I build the settlement immediately rather than trading for marginal upside.

When I need one resource to complete a settlement and another player has repeatedly traded that resource for ore, I will post an offer of 1 ore for 1 of that resource to the specific player who has shown that pattern.

When an offer to me gives the single resource I need to finish a settlement and I can spare the requested resource without dropping below my ore threshold for future city/dev plans (default threshold = 3 ore), I accept it.

When an incoming offer would drop my ore below my city/dev threshold and the gain is only one resource that does not immediately convert into a settlement or a higher-scoring build this round, I decline.

When I cannot build a settlement this turn but can build a road with available wood+brick, I build the road to take the guaranteed 1 point rather than holding resources idle (unless holding enables a settlement next turn with high probability).

When I have enough ore to consider city/dev plans, I prioritize keeping ore over taking small single-resource trades unless the trade directly enables a 3-point settlement that I can build that round.

When posting an offer, I name a specific counterparty who has previously shown a propensity to accept that swap pattern rather than broadcasting a generic offer.

When multiple offers are posted to me, I accept any that immediately yield a buildable settlement or that leave my strategic resource thresholds intact; otherwise I accept none.

Do not trade away the single resource that is the bottleneck for a settlement if I can instead post an offer to acquire that bottleneck from the player who trades it most often.

If I lack both wood and grain (or another pair) so that no build is possible this round, avoid desperate unfavorable trades just to change holdings; instead wait to convert into a road or settlement on a later round when feasible.

If two or more players post identical offers (e.g., 1 grain for 1 wood) and I only need one of those resources to complete a settlement, prefer accepting from the counterparty who leaves me with better remaining resource balance or who has historically provided favorable trades.

If accepting a trade would convert a current build opportunity (settlement) into a non-build round, prefer declining unless the trade creates a guaranteed higher-point outcome this round.
