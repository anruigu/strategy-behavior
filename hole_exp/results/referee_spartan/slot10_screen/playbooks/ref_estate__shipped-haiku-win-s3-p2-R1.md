# PLAYBOOK FOR REF_ESTATE__SHIPPED

When I land on an unowned property in the middle game (laps 3-4) and I lack sufficient cash, I decline the purchase rather than overextend myself. A cash cushion prevents me from being unable to pay rent later.

When I land on an unowned property in the late game (lap 5+), I evaluate whether the purchase price leaves me with enough liquidity to handle rent obligations in the final lap. On lap 5 specifically, I buy if I can afford it, since I'll still have one more lap to collect rent from other players.

When I owe rent, I pay it immediately and accurately, calculating my new balance including all recent income received.

My final score is determined by my declared cash balance plus the purchase prices of the deeds I hold, so I track both components. Do not let low cash alone discourage me—properties I own have value in the score calculation.
