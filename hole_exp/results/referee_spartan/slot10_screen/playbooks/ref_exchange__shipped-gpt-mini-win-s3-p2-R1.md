When I can only afford a road (1 wood + 1 brick) but could reach a settlement next round with one more resource, prefer saving resources for the settlement unless the extra point from the road will change the end-game outcome.

When I lack any resource needed to build this round, post one trade offer to get the single most blocking resource (the one that completes a settlement first, then a road). Name a counterparty who has that resource.

When posting an offer, give a resource I have in surplus (2+ of) or one that is least useful for current goals; ask only for the exact resource I need. Do not overtrade.

When an offer is posted to me, accept it if the incoming resource completes a build I can perform this round (settlement preferred over road), or if it advances me to a clear next-turn build priority; otherwise decline.

When multiple identical offers come in that each cost me the same resource (e.g., both want 1 wood for 1 grain), accept only as many as are necessary for my immediate build or plan — avoid turning all of one scarce resource into duplicates of another unless that other is what I need.

When I have 0 of a resource that blocks every feasible build (e.g., no brick), prioritize trading for that resource over hoarding potential city materials (grain/ore) unless a city is immediately reachable and more valuable.

When I can build nothing and a trade would convert a resource I need into one I don't (e.g., giving wood when I actually need brick), refuse or counteroffer for the specific resource I need.

When choosing between trading with opponents, prefer the counterparty who has less use for what I offer (more likely to accept) and who is not racing to the same immediate build unless the trade benefits me more than them.

When offered a straightforward 1:1 swap that gets me from “cannot build” to “can build a settlement,” accept immediately.

When I can complete a city (if that mechanic exists) in one trade and it yields more points than a settlement, prioritize that trade; but do not sacrifice the single blocking resource for a later goal if it prevents me from scoring now and the opponent can immediately use it to score.
