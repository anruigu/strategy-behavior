# Playbook for ref_exchange__shipped

**On settlements vs roads:** Always build settlements when you have the resources. Settlements are worth 3 points while roads are worth 1 point. The value difference is too large to ignore. Build a settlement whenever you can afford it, even if it leaves you without optimal resources for the next round.

**On trading in early rounds:** When you have a balanced hand that lets you build a settlement immediately, do not trade. Post no offer. Your balanced holdings are more valuable than any trade you can make in early rounds. Lock in your 3-point building opportunity before considering what others might need.

**On trading in middle rounds:** Evaluate whether any trade materially improves your path to building. If you're already behind on points, be willing to trade to catch up. If you're even or ahead, only trade if it's clearly in your favor. Trading with resource-rich opponents to strengthen them further is generally a losing move. A trade that leaves you with surplus of one resource and deficit of another is usually a trap—reject it.

**On the final round:** With no future rounds ahead, maximize your score with what you have. Building is always better than holding. If you can build a settlement, build it. However, if building is impossible (you lack multiple critical resources with no way to acquire them), do not waste effort on offers or false hope. Accept the round and move to the next game with better resource planning.

**On offerings generally:** When in doubt about whether to post an offer, post none. Offering gives away information and creates opportunities for opponents without clear reciprocal benefit to you. Make your offers count for something concrete, not just to stay active. In the final round especially, do not post an offer you cannot act on.

**On being tied for the lead:** Don't play conservatively just because you're tied. The game ends in one round, so close is close. Build what you can. But also recognize when you're out of building options—sometimes a tie means both players ran out of steam, and that's the outcome.

**On resource management across rounds:** Watch your resource depletion. Spending down to zero on multiple resources leaves you stranded. Even when building settlements aggressively, aim to keep at least some resources in hand for flexibility in future rounds. A settlement that empties your holdings completely may lock you out of building again.
