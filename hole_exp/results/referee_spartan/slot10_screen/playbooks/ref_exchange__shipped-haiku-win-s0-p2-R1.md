# Playbook for ref_exchange__shipped

**On resource scarcity and trading:**
When I lack a critical resource for my next build, immediately identify which opponent has it and propose a trade. Don't wait passively hoping resources will be yielded to me—the game is short (5 rounds) and passive play leaves me behind. Target the player most willing to trade rather than the most abundant holder if they're the same person.

**On accepting trades:**
When offered a trade that improves my resource diversity without crippling any future build path, accept it. Ore and brick tend to be bottleneck resources; trading abundant resources (like my extra wood) for scarce ones is usually sound. Accept early if the offer is fair.

**On building settlements:**
A settlement is +3 points and costs 1 of each resource. Roads are +1 point. Prioritize settlements heavily—they're the primary way to score in this game. Build them whenever I can assemble 1 wood, 1 brick, 1 grain, 1 ore.

**On the brick bottleneck:**
Brick appears to be the resource I'm most likely to run short on. In early rounds, if I have decent brick reserves, I should protect them and not trade them away unless getting something equally scarce in return. In later rounds when I'm behind on points, be willing to trade for brick at a premium if needed to build.

**What failed this game:**
I ended round 1 with a poor trade (gave grain for brick) that left me grain-starved for the rest of the game. Then I made a critical error: accepting a wood-for-ore trade when I should have been hoarding resources or trading for brick. By round 4, I was locked out of building entirely due to lack of brick. Trading for ore when brick was the real bottleneck was my fundamental mistake. In future games, identify the true constraint resource earlier and protect or acquire it accordingly, not just chase any trade that looks balanced.

**On final rounds:**
If I'm substantially behind in points with only 1-2 rounds left and I lack critical resources, I'm already lost. Don't waste offers trying to recover at that stage. Instead, by round 3-4, if I'm not on track to build settlements regularly, I need to have secured a different resource pipeline earlier.
