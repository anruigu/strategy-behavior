Your score is determined by your cash plus the value of properties you already hold; acquiring new properties on the last lap converts cash to deed value with no time to earn rent, and you have no guarantee of collecting that rent before the game ends.

When you land on an owned property, always pay the rent immediately and declare your remaining cash accurately.

When you track your balance each turn, include all transfers received since your last declared balance. Do not forget to add income from rent paid to you by other investors. Include salary collected when you pass START.
