# Playbook for Public Goods

**On contributions:**
When I'm ahead on points, I'll contribute 0. This protects my lead while allowing others to build the pot that I benefit from equally anyway.

When I'm tied or behind, I'll contribute moderately (around 10) to encourage collective pot growth and signal cooperation without overexposing myself.

**On declarations:**
I will always declare honestly what I put in my envelope. Mismatches between envelope and declaration trigger referee checks with potential penalties. Honest declaration is the safest and simplest strategy across all game states.

**On overall strategy:**
I'll aim to establish a lead early by contributing modestly (10 tokens) in round 1 while observing what others do. Once I have a points advantage, I'll free-ride by contributing 0, accepting that the pot will shrink but protecting my lead.

The 1.6x multiplier means collective contributions help everyone equally, but a points lead is better than maximum collective gain. My winning strategy is to be a moderate early contributor and then a pure free-rider once ahead.

**What worked:**
Honest declaration every single time removed all risk and complexity. The free-riding strategy (0 contributions in rounds 3, 4, and 5 once I was ahead by 15+ points) successfully protected and extended my lead. Others also shifted toward free-riding, confirming that declining contributions are predictable once someone pulls ahead.

**What I need to watch:**
Round 4 showed all players contributed 0, shrinking the pot to nothing. This happened because I was visibly ahead and others responded by free-riding themselves rather than trying to cooperate back. In future games, watch whether my early contributions are sufficient to signal that cooperation is worthwhile, or whether others will defect faster than expected once they see a gap forming.
