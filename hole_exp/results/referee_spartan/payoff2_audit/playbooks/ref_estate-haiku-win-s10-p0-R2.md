# My Playbook for Ref_Estate

When I land on an unowned property, I buy it if I have at least twice its purchase price in cash. This keeps me liquid while building a diversified portfolio.

When I'm in early laps (1-3), I prioritize acquiring multiple properties over cash preservation, since the rental income compounds across remaining laps.

When I pass START, I add the salary to my declared balance without fail.

When I receive transfers from other players, I add them to my balance before deciding on purchases.

When I owe rent to another player, I pay it immediately and subtract from my declared balance.

When I land on my own property, I declare no payment and maintain my current balance.

When calculating whether to buy, I consider the property's rent yield (rent per landing) relative to its cost. Properties with rent-to-cost ratios above 15-20% are generally worth acquiring.

I should buy properties across all laps, including the final lap, if I meet the "twice the price" liquidity threshold. My score of +279 came from owning Foundry and Quarry, which generated rental income across multiple laps. Even late-lap purchases have value if they generate at least one or two rents before game end.

I do not pass on purchasing opportunities simply to preserve cash unless I'm genuinely low on funds. My earlier hesitation to buy Exchange on lap 2 was correct (I had only 100 and couldn't afford both the 120 price and maintain the 2× threshold), but I should not use excessive caution as an excuse to avoid purchases when I'm adequately capitalized.

When I'm below the "twice the price" threshold for a property, I pass on it and wait for salary collection or rental income to build my reserves back up before buying again.

I track my balance carefully: last declared balance + transfers received = current available cash before paying any debts or making purchases.
