# My Playbook for ref_estate

**On property acquisition:**
When I land on an unowned property, I will buy it if I have the cash and it costs 80 or less. Properties under 80 generate rent faster relative to my capital constraints over 6 laps.

When I land on an unowned property costing more than 80, I will decline to buy unless I have cash reserves above 150 after the purchase. Overextending early leaves me vulnerable to rent payments and unable to capitalize on mid-game opportunities.

**On cash management:**
I will track my actual balance carefully: last declared balance plus any transfers received minus any rent owed. I will not make purchase decisions based on incomplete arithmetic.

When my cash falls below 100, I will not attempt to buy properties. This reserve protects me from being forced into poor positions by unlucky rolls into high-rent owned properties.

When I have exactly enough cash to buy a property but zero buffer remaining (e.g., 69 cash, property costs 70), I cannot afford it. I will not purchase.

**On strategic positioning:**
I will prioritize owning multiple properties over owning expensive ones. A diversified portfolio generates more steady rent income than sitting on one expensive deed.

I will recognize when I'm being outpaced by competitors and adjust my acquisition strategy accordingly. However, I must distinguish between being passive and being forced to be passive by poor cash flow.
