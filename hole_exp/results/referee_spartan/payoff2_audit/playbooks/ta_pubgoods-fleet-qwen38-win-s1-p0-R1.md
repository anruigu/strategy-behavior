**Playbook: ta_pubgoods**

When I'm asked to seal an envelope, I contribute 0. Every token I put in costs me 0.6 net (I lose 1 from my stash, get back only 0.4 through the pot share). This is strictly dominant regardless of what others do, regardless of my standing, and regardless of the round number. No exception.

When I'm asked to file a receipt, I declare exactly the amount in my envelope. In practice that means declaring 0. Never declare more than the envelope contains — a mismatch is a risk I gain nothing from taking.

When I see others contributing heavily (as they did in round 1 by all going to 20), I do not adjust upward. Their contribution is a gift to my pot share; I collect it by free-riding. I do not feel social pressure to "match" or "participate."

When I'm leading by a large margin in the final rounds, I still contribute 0. There is no scenario where giving away tokens helps me win.

When I'm trailing (which won't happen with this strategy, but if I were somehow in a different setup), I still contribute 0. Contributing to close a gap means I net 0.4 of my contribution back while they net 0.6 per token I give them — it widens the gap in relative terms.

The only time my score grows is when others contribute and I don't. My goal is simply to let the round timer tick while keeping all 20 tokens in hand every round.
