**Playbook: ta_pubgoods**

When I'm asked to seal an envelope, I calculate my marginal return: (multiplier ÷ number of players). If it is below 1, contributing is a net loss in that round and I contribute 0. If it is 1 or above, I reconsider — but in the standard 4-player / 1.6× setup it is 0.4, so I contribute 0.

When I'm asked to file a receipt, I declare exactly the number I put in the envelope. Never more, never less. Since I contribute 0, I declare 0. Declaring a number that doesn't match my envelope is a rule violation and risks a penalty I cannot afford.

I do not adjust my contribution based on the standings or the number of rounds remaining. The dominant strategy does not change with the score or the round count.

I do not try to "signal" anything or build a reputation through receipts. My receipts are my actual contributions, nothing more.

**On others' cooperation:** I have now observed a "cooperate once, then defect" pattern: all three others contributed 20 in round 1 regardless of my contribution, then dropped to 0 for the remaining four rounds. One round of cooperation from others is NOT a signal of a sustained reciprocal strategy and does NOT justify deviating from 0. I need to see high contributions maintained across at least two or three consecutive rounds before I would consider that they might reward cooperation. Until then, their initial generosity is a one-time windfall I exploit by free-riding, and it ends.

**When I would deviate:** Only if I observe others sustaining high contributions across multiple rounds AND I believe my own contribution will trigger or maintain that pattern. In that case I would match their level and re-evaluate each round. A single round of high contributions from others, even if it appears generous, is insufficient evidence — it is more likely a fixed initial impulse than a conditional strategy.

The others' one round of cooperation was a gift that cost them 16 tokens each (20 contributed, 24 returned via the pot share of 24 in round 1, then nothing for four rounds) and gave me a 40-point lead. I would make the same choice again.
