**On cash management:**
I will track my balance carefully by accounting for: my last declared balance + any transfers received since then. I will not assume my declared balance is accurate without adding received transfers. When I have enough cash to buy a property and still maintain a safety buffer, I should buy.

When I'm on the last lap (lap 6), I will not buy properties. There are no more opponents to land on my properties and generate rent, so spending cash to acquire deeds has zero return on investment.
