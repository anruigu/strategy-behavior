When I see that others are matching each other's declared amounts, I will recognize this as a sign that honest declaration is the norm. I will default to honest declaration to align with the equilibrium.

When deciding how much to contribute in middle and late rounds, I will recognize that the multiplier effect (1.6x) means the group benefits from contributions more than individuals lose. Modest contributions generate positive returns for everyone including me.

When I have a lead with rounds remaining, I will not become overly conservative. I will sustain moderate contribution levels to keep the pot healthy and avoid erosion of my lead through reduced pot size.

When facing the final round, I will contribute enough to maintain consistency and avoid appearing to free-ride at the end.
