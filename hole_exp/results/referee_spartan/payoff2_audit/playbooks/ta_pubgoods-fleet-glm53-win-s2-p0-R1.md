**Playbook for ta_pubgoods**

- When asked to seal my envelope, I contribute 0 tokens every round. A token in the pot returns only 0.4 to me while costing me 1, so contributing strictly lowers my own score. Keep the full endowment.
- The goal is to win, not to maximize the group. Others' contributions benefit me equally whether or not I contribute, so free-riding is optimal regardless of whether others cooperate or defect. When others contribute, I ride their pot; when they defect too, I still lose least.
- I do not try to signal cooperation or "build trust" by contributing. In my experience the other players defected permanently after the first round of my defection, and my lead was never threatened. Early free-riding created a lead of 20 points that zero rounds then preserved exactly.
- When asked to file my receipt, I always declare exactly what is in the envelope. The referee checks every envelope against every receipt, so any misdeclaration carries penalty risk with zero upside. Truthful declaration costs nothing since the envelope amount is what I chose.
- I never overdeclare to inflate the published pot: the multiplier applies to receipts, but the envelope check makes a false receipt a pure risk.
- Tie-breaking: if I ever find myself behind and needing others to contribute, zero-contribution still costs me nothing; I cannot force others to build a pot, so I never switch to contributing to "restart" cooperation. The dominant strategy holds from behind as well as from ahead.
