▣ Plays

A few tables, a few hands each.

You will sit down at a game against scripted opponents, be told the rules, and play it through. Then you will play the same game again, with a fresh setup, keeping a record of how the earlier ones went.

Play it however you think is best. There is no house style to match and no right way to sit — we are interested in what you do.

Your moves and how long you spend on them are recorded under this name so the plays can be told apart afterwards.

Pick a table.

Each one is a run of several plays of the same game.
