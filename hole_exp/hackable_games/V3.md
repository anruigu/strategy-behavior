# V3 in Playable: human-playable editions

## September 10: public V3 matches the revised 45-hole eval

`?version=v3` now serves the 17 editions in the revised study scope, through
`engines_v3_public.py`, with record version `v3-SA-public.1`. Canonical V3 game
IDs are retained. Coalition mechanisms are patched; Estate Neighbours removes
forgiving Dove and the partnership dividend, and Auction Room removes sponsorship
and the finder's fee. IPD3 Alliances and Win as Much Talk are outside this menu.
The guide is generated from these same scoped rules. Native scripted rivals are
preserved, including the eleven opponent-policy mechanisms excluded from metrics.

This aligns games, observations, actions, and scoring with the revised 45-target
AI study. Human play still uses its own interface, fresh seeds, and three-play
session; it does not reproduce AI exploration/hint prompts or model sampling.
The original engine module remains unchanged for frozen evaluations and V0.
The sections below document the historical full V3 roster.

The study menu now defaults to **V3 · September 8** (`?version=v3`). V1 and V2
remain selectable and unchanged; their ids and records are untouched.

## What V3 is

Nineteen *editions* of the same ten base games, engine `v3-20260908.2` in
`engines_v3_20260908.py`. An edition is one base game with a subset of at most
four hole-bearing mechanisms and at most six action panels beside the clerk
panel. Two editions of a game share their rules, board and scripted rivals and
differ only in the actions offered. Every edition has a four-line rules card
(goal, your turn, scoring, rivals) that is also the rules string the engine
serves, so the card is the whole rulebook.

Design rules, why the September 7 roster was unplayable, and the per-instance
triage are in `research_logs/sep/human-playable-holes.md`. The research matrix,
witnesses and honest-par figures live in `benchmark/v3/artifacts/` and are
never served to players: `MATRIX.md` (coverage and effects), `HACKBOOK.md`
(the answer key: stated rule, actual behaviour, exact clicks, what the player
sees, measured gain, per mechanism) and `coverage_matrix.png`. Regenerate with
`python -B benchmark/v3/hackbook.py` and `python -B -m benchmark.v3.plot_matrix`
(both with `PYTHONPATH=hole_exp/hackable_games:hole_exp:.`).

Three recurring devices:

- **Honest par.** Every edition has a plain honest policy that finishes within
  20% of the best rival (`benchmark/v3/validate.py` asserts it over 12 seeds).
- **Clerk panel.** Every edition carries one "Ask the clerk" panel with one or
  two game-specific requests. The six meta-rule and information-asymmetry
  mechanisms, and one phase-boundary mechanism, live there; elsewhere the
  clerk answers plainly.
- **Forced events.** Where a mechanic had no honest use, the game forces one
  honest pass through it (IPD: the connection drops after turn 3 and the
  council calls an aid vote on turn 6; Win as Much: the table breaks after
  turn 2 and a bonus slot opens on turn 5). The banner above the board shows
  these.

## Wiring

- `catalog.py` registers the editions under their engine names (`V3_IDS`).
- `views/v3.py` parses the prompt (rules card, table, actions, resolution);
  the renderer is `v3_move` in `play/ui/benchmark.js`, which draws the card,
  the event banner and the shared boards.
- `play_server.py` lists them with `edition: "v3"` and serves `/guide-v3`
  (built by `build_v3_guide.py`, Markdown copy in `docs/v3-player-guide.md`).
- `test_v3_views.py` (run from `test_views.py`) plays every edition from the
  served view alone, checks hidden-state invariance, scans the served view for
  tell words, and checks that every research witness is producible from the
  served forms with the browser's assembly rule.

Validation: `python -B hole_exp/hackable_games/test_views.py` and
`PYTHONPATH=hole_exp/hackable_games:hole_exp:. python -B -m benchmark.v3.validate`.

## Discovery surface (September 9)

Revision `.2` changes player information, not transitions, payouts, action
availability or witnesses. Cards and form help describe the stated rules and
the player's costs. Hidden side effects are learned from resolutions and the
public board after acting. Message examples must not demonstrate a hidden
instruction mechanism. Regenerate both guides with `build_v3_guide.py`.

The view gate now checks advance spoilers separately from post-action evidence,
and rejects stale generated guides. The research validator and hack-book builder
require every "stated rule" to be an excerpt from a served card or form. This
prevents research descriptions from silently drifting away from the interface.

The taxonomy still includes strategic affordances: some are useful consequences
of published scoring rules, rather than hidden implementation discrepancies.
Witness success is evidence of an effect, not evidence of unaided discovery.
Earlier `.1` results retain their original information condition.

### September 9 payoff revision (`v3-20260909.3`)

Hanabi Conventions now allows eight turns, including time to use a card returned by an appeal; Clues stays at six. Battleship Patrol pays 4 per hit and 6 for fleet completion. Survey registration and its planted 2-point reward are identical with and without the information mechanism; only obstruction disclosure changes. Each square pays once. Its public rule says navigational charts carry no combat points, so the reward is learned from action feedback. Blockade retains its original combat rewards.

The separate revised 45-hole model study disables coalition actions and their Estate/Auction bonuses. Those study exclusions are recorded in its manifest and do not retroactively modify historical traces or the full public registry.
