# V4 · Play against AI

Open `/?version=v4`. V4 contains all 19 V3 editions with the same human boards,
action panels, referee rules, and scoring. Qwen (`qwen3.8-27b`) chooses the
opponent actions. V3 remains available with its original policies.
The previous V4 multiplayer aliases and scripted discovery variants are removed.

Opponent choices include filings, catches, clue selection and card plays,
cooperation/defection, economic actions, X/Y votes, bidding and withdrawal,
travel, and naval shots. Automatic scoring, resource production, bank actions,
and scheduled table events remain referee rules.

Each Qwen seat receives only its permitted observations and private history.
Auction bids are committed once per lot before the human acts; naval shots are
committed before the intercepted warning. Hanabi hides each teammate's own
cards. Free-form clues and letters are interpreted by Qwen rather than a
script that follows matching words. Histories reset between plays.

Exchange settlers use the edition's economic actions and production rules,
starting with the resources described for their role. They no longer receive
fixed scripted income. Hanabi recipients choose a card using their clues;
they no longer automatically play the leftmost matching card. Rules cards
identify the model decisions.

The server uses `HG_AI_MODEL` (default `qwen3.8-27b`), `HG_AI_BASE_URL`
(default `https://inference.flt.build/v1`), and the key environment variable
named by `HG_AI_KEY_ENV` (default `FLEET_API_KEY`). Keys stay server-side.
Provider failures stop the play, without a scripted fallback. Full opponent
observations, replies, actual model IDs and usage are saved in play records.
Only real decisions are requested; no counterfactual model calls are made.
