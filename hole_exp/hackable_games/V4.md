# V4 · Human focal agent in the opponent-intervention eval

Open `/?version=v4`. The former symmetric play-against-AI games are now at
`/?version=v0` (19 editions, `v0_` IDs). Historical V4 traces retain their original
IDs and engine version; they must not be pooled with the new V4.

V4 contains the ten recipient-dependent scenarios from
`benchmark/results/v3-ma-four-model-20260909/`: four repeated-play scenarios,
two clue scenarios, two market scenarios, and two joint-filing scenarios.
The four Commons scenarios have no nerfed treatment and are outside this menu.

The human replaces seat 0. The underlying `engines_v3_ma.py` is a byte-identical
copy of the campaign snapshot. Its observations, actions, private information,
stages, horizons, and scoring are preserved. Independent seats within a stage
are scheduled concurrently; resolution waits for every submission. Later stages
see only the preceding stage's completed result. Model memory is private to each
seat and resets every play. V4 does not add the old V0 symmetric action menus.

## Setup

- Ordinary: baseline opponent prompts from the campaign.
- Nerfed (default): exact private recipient prompts from the campaign. Seat 1
  receives the treatment, or both rivals in Market · The room. The council and
  joint-filing outsider remains ordinary.
- Defensive: the additional defensive prompt from the campaign's specifications;
  this condition was not sampled in the referenced four-model screen.

Every setup uses the ordinary/open referee. Patched-rule and combined conditions
are not offered. Prompt assignment does not guarantee that a model obeys it.
The setup labels are visible for the operator's personal play; this is not yet a
blinded participant-assignment workflow.

Choose Qwen 3.8 27B, GLM 5.3, Claude Haiku 4.5, or GPT-5 mini. Provider routes,
private system prompts (including the formatting suffix), low reasoning setting,
provider-default temperature, 8,192/16,384 output allowances, and one format
correction match the run. Other seats use the selected model with separate
contexts. Provider failure or a second invalid action ends the play; there is no
scripted fallback. Action widgets submit the eval's required fields.

Two plays use the selected starting seed and its successor (default 0 and 1).
To reproduce the linked episode's setup, select Clues · The note, GLM 5.3,
Ordinary, seed 0. The model is sampled anew; this does not replay saved AI replies.
URL parameters `condition`, `opponent`, and `seed` prefill these controls.

## Records and validation

Schema 6 records include the public V4 ID, canonical eval scenario, condition,
opponent model, focal seat, seed, open referee, protocol and source hashes.
Exact human observations and actions, timings, private model request/response
histories, and every completed stage's referee event are retained server-side.
A final seat-0 projection shows completed outcomes without exposing rival
private messages, system prompts, or future draws. Abandoned plays retain their
completed events. Active records remain in memory until finish/abandonment;
process-crash recovery is not provided by this change.

`v4_eval_protocol.json` freezes the run's settings and prompt bytes; it is not
served as a public asset. The old hosted Qwen policy remains exclusive to V0.

Run `PYTHONPATH=hole_exp/hackable_games:hole_exp python -m unittest test_v4 test_v0 test_hosted_opponents`.
The tests compare every action-stage observation, action, resolution and score
against the frozen eval across all ten scenarios and seeds 0/1. They also check
parallel stages, recipient isolation, exact requests, correction behavior,
complete and partial traces, and the V0 archive.
